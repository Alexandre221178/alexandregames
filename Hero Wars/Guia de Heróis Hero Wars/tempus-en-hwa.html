Tempus Skills Breakdown

Tempus possesses a set of time-bending abilities that can disrupt enemy strategies while empowering his team. Here’s a breakdown of his core skills and how to use them effectively:

1. Chronostasis

Skill Description: Tempus stops time for all enemy Heroes, freezing their movement, skill usage, and evasion. During this time, enemy Armor and Magic Defense are reduced, and the cooldown of all their skills is halted. This ability also prolongs the duration of all Curses and Debuffs affecting the enemies.

Strategy: Chronostasis is a game-changing ability that can turn the tide of battle. By freezing enemy actions and defenses, Tempus creates a window of opportunity for his team’s mages to unleash their most powerful attacks without fear of retaliation. Use this skill at key moments, particularly when enemies are about to use high-impact abilities, to completely nullify their threat. Paired with heroes like Iris and Phobos, it allows you to exploit weakened enemies and maximize damage.

2. Withering

Skill Description: Tempus temporarily weakens distant enemies in front of him, reducing their Physical Attack and max Health.

Strategy: Withering is particularly effective against physical attackers and frontline enemies. This skill allows Tempus to mitigate the damage from opponents who rely on Physical Attack, while also reducing their maximum Health. Use this to soften up enemy tanks or damage dealers, giving your team a clear advantage. This is especially effective when Tempus is positioned against teams with strong physical damage output.

3. Temporal Anomaly

Skill Description: Tempus sends the enemy Hero with the highest Armor Penetration into the Temporal Anomaly, where they are slowed down, lose energy, and take continuous magic damage over time.

Strategy: Temporal Anomaly targets the enemy with the highest Armor Penetration, making it an ideal counter against opponents designed to shred through your team's defenses. By isolating and weakening this target, Tempus ensures that your team’s tanks and frontline heroes stay protected longer. Additionally, the magic damage over time can help wear down high-priority enemies.

4. Time Paradox

Skill Description: Tempus summons a giant clock in the center of the battlefield, creating an area of effect (AoE) that slows down enemies while speeding up allies within its range.

Strategy: Time Paradox is Tempus’ ultimate control ability. The AoE affects both enemies and allies, slowing enemies down while boosting the speed of Tempus' team. This can be used to control the pace of battle, allowing your allies to quickly outmaneuver and overpower opponents. In close matches, the ability to manipulate time for both teams can be the difference between victory and defeat. Utilize Time Paradox to keep enemies from reaching key objectives or to ensure your team executes attacks with deadly precision.

Tips for Playing Tempus

  1. Timing is Everything: Tempus excels at controlling the battlefield, so using his skills at the right moments is crucial. Don’t rush to use Chronostasis; wait until enemies are about to launch their attacks or when key debuffs are in place to maximize its effectiveness.
  2. Protect Your Mages: Tempus is most effective when paired with magic-based heroes. Use his time manipulation abilities to shield vulnerable mages from physical attacks and buy them time to unleash their most powerful spells.
  3. Target Key Opponents: Use Temporal Anomaly to neutralize high-priority targets like enemy heroes with strong Armor Penetration. By removing these threats from the battlefield temporarily, you give your team a significant advantage.
  4. Maximize AoE Effects: Time Paradox affects both your team and the enemy, so positioning is key. Make sure your team is within the clock’s range to benefit from the speed boost while trapping enemies in its slowing effect.
  5. Synergize with Debuff Heroes: Tempus shines when paired with heroes that apply debuffs and curses. Heroes like Iris and Phobos will see their debuffs extended and amplified by Chronostasis, allowing you to keep enemies weakened for longer.

Best Skin for Tempus Hero Wars Alliance

Tempus's skin evolution is crucial for maximizing his time-control support role. Each skin provides distinct advantages, from devastating magic attack to improved survivability and versatility in different battle scenarios.

Cybernetic Skin

Magic Attack +10,650

Evolution Priority: 1st – Very High – This skin provides the maximum direct boost to magic attack, making it equal in raw damage output to the Stellar Skin. The Cybernetic Skin amplifies Tempus's control abilities like Chronostasis and Temporal Anomaly, enabling him to deal devastating damage while controlling the battlefield. This should be your top priority for maximum offensive support potential.

Stellar Skin

Magic Attack +10,650

Evolution Priority: 1st – Very High – This skin provides a direct and substantial increase to magic attack, offering identical raw damage output to the Cybernetic Skin. Both skins are equally valuable for maximizing Tempus's offensive potential and support impact, especially when paired with burst mages like Iris or Polaris. Choose based on aesthetic preference between these top-tier options.

Default Skin

Intelligence +1,365

Evolution Priority: 2nd – High – Intelligence increases magic attack (+4,095), magic defense (+1,365), and contributes slightly to physical attack (+1,365). While it offers less raw damage than the Cybernetic or Stellar Skins, it improves both offensive and defensive aspects of Tempus's kit, making it a strong secondary choice. It enhances all abilities and helps sustain him longer in battles with magic-heavy opponents.

Lunar Skin

Health +106,645

Evolution Priority: 3rd – Medium-High – As a middle-line hero, Tempus can be exposed to AoE and burst damage. The health boost from this skin improves his survivability, ensuring he can consistently apply his crowd control and debuffs. Prioritize this skin if Tempus is being eliminated too early in fights.

Angel Skin+

Armor +21,300

Evolution Priority: 4th – Medium – Armor helps mitigate physical damage, which is useful against heroes like Dante or Keira. However, because Tempus isn't usually targeted by single-target physical attacks and already sits in the middle line, this skin is situational and offers the least value overall.

Tempus Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Tempus harnesses three powerful artifacts that enhance his magical damage, survivability, and team support capabilities. Each artifact provides distinct advantages that work together to maximize his effectiveness in time-control support strategies.

Chronowheel (Weapon)

Magic Attack +32,040

Evolution Priority: 1st – Very High – This is Tempus's primary artifact and should be your top priority. Chronowheel provides the highest direct magic attack bonus (+32,040), significantly amplifying the damage of his Chronostasis and Temporal Anomaly abilities. Additionally, it triggers with Tempus's ultimate ability, granting powerful stat bonuses to his entire team for 9 seconds, making it invaluable for coordinated offensive pushes and team synergies.

Tome of Arcane Knowledge (Book)

Magic Attack +10,680 / Health +53,394

Evolution Priority: 2nd – High – This artifact offers excellent balance between offensive and defensive capabilities. The magic attack boost (+10,680) strengthens his damage output, while the substantial health increase (+53,394) improves his survivability on the middle line. This combination ensures Tempus can maintain presence in prolonged battles while consistently applying his control abilities without being eliminated prematurely.

Ring of Intelligence (Ring)

Intelligence +3,990

Evolution Priority: 3rd – Medium – Intelligence provides versatile scaling, converting +3,990 Intelligence into magic attack (+11,970), magic defense (+3,990), and physical attack (+3,990). While it delivers less raw magic damage than Chronowheel or Tome of Arcane Knowledge, it improves overall sustainability and adds multi-stat benefits. Prioritize this artifact after the first two for complete optimization of Tempus's support toolkit.

Tempus's glyph evolution strategy focuses on maximizing his survivability and magical output to sustain his time-control support role. Each glyph provides distinct advantages that enhance his effectiveness in battle based on his defensive and offensive needs.

As a middle-line support hero, Tempus requires exceptional durability to maintain battlefield presence throughout prolonged battles. The substantial +122,800 health boost ensures he can consistently apply his crowd control abilities, absorb incoming AoE damage, and survive burst attacks without being eliminated prematurely. This is foundational to his effectiveness as a support unit.

Magic Attack

Level 80 Stats: Magic Attack +12,850

Evolution Priority: 1st – Very High – Magic Attack is equally critical for maximizing Tempus's offensive capability. The +12,850 bonus directly amplifies the damage of Chronostasis, Temporal Anomaly, and Time Paradox, transforming him from a pure control support into a formidable damage dealer. This allows him to capitalize on the windows he creates with his crowd control abilities, dealing significant damage while controlling the battlefield.

Magic Defense

Level 80 Stats: Magic Defense +12,850

Evolution Priority: 2nd – High – Magic Defense is your second priority, providing crucial mitigation against magical damage from enemy mages and burst heroes. The +12,850 bonus helps Tempus resist common magical threats in competitive teams. While his teammates like Morrigan provide healing and support, reducing incoming magic damage directly translates to lower healing requirements and longer sustained presence in combat.

Armor

Level 80 Stats: Armor +12,850

Evolution Priority: 3rd – Medium-High – Armor provides physical damage mitigation with +12,850, which is situationally valuable against physical-heavy teams. However, Tempus's position on the middle line means he's less frequently targeted by single-target physical attacks compared to frontline heroes. Prioritize this glyph after Health, Magic Attack, and Magic Defense, primarily for teams facing strong physical damage dealers like Dante or Keira.

Intelligence

Level 80 Stats: Intelligence +2,110

Scaling: +6,330 Magic Attack / +2,110 Magic Defense / +2,110 Physical Attack

Evolution Priority: 4th – Medium – Intelligence provides versatile multi-stat scaling, converting +2,110 Intelligence into offensive and defensive bonuses. While it offers decent scaling, its individual stat contributions are lower than dedicated glyph options. Prioritize Intelligence last, as the flat +12,850 Magic Attack glyph delivers superior offensive value, and dedicated defensive glyphs provide more focused protection.

Synergies

Tempus works exceptionally well with Way of Eternity heroes, but he is particularly potent when paired with:

  • Iris: Iris’ debuff abilities complement Tempus’ time manipulation. When Tempus uses his skill Chronostasis, it enhances the effects of Iris' debuffs by extending their duration.
  • Phobos: Phobos’ Curse ability, which lowers enemy effectiveness, becomes even more deadly when combined with Tempus' skills that slow and weaken enemies.
  • Xe'Sha and Celeste: These powerful mages benefit from Tempus' time manipulation abilities, allowing them to unleash devastating magic while enemies are slowed and unable to defend.
  • Morrigan – Supports Tempus with healing and damage buffs, helping keep him alive in the front lines.
  • Nebula – Enhances Tempus’s magic attack with her boosts, making him more lethal.
  • Dante – Protects Tempus from high burst damage while helping control enemy movement.

Tempus Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

  • Tempus has 2 talismans, and you must decide which one to equip before entering battle. Unlike skins, where you receive the bonus regardless of which one is active, only the stats from the currently equipped talisman are applied in combat. Choosing the right talisman can make a huge difference depending on the enemies you face and the role you want Tempus to play.

Talisman of Everlastingness

  • Main slot: Intelligence +2,000
  • Each Intelligence point grants:
    • +3 Magic Attack
    • +1 Magic Defense
    • +1 Physical Attack (if Intelligence is the hero’s main stat)
  • Total bonus from reroll slots: Armor +19,800 (sum of three slots: 3 × 6,600)
  • This talisman is ideal when you want to boost Tempus’s magical power and gain some physical resistance through additional armor. Excellent choice against physical damage teams.

Talisman of Everlastingness

  • Main slot: Toughness +4,000
  • Toughness effect:
    • Reduces all incoming damage when Tempus’s health is low
    • Applies to all types of damage (physical, magical, and pure), excluding health loss from allied skills
    • Formula: (Toughness / (Toughness + enemy’s main stat)) × 100%
  • Total bonus from reroll slots: Health +181,500 (sum of three slots: 3 × 60,500)
  • This talisman greatly enhances Tempus’s survivability in late-game moments or against high burst damage. A solid option for defensive strategies.

For players looking to master the art of battlefield control, Tempus is a must-have hero in Hero Wars Alliance.
