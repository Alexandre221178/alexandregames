O evento Fofinho apresenta marcos de missão em etapas que recompensam com Moedas de Valor e itens do evento. Aborde os marcos de forma constante em vez de tentar concluir tudo de uma vez — algumas metas são de longo prazo e valem mais quando planejadas ao longo de vários dias.

Antes de gastar em totens ou pacotes, verifique os custos oficiais no jogo para sua região. Considere estimativas iniciais como provisórias e evite gastar demais no primeiro dia: o evento costuma oferecer formas repetíveis de recuperar recursos.

Priorize o artefato que melhor apoia o papel principal de Fofinho e só então invista em artefatos secundários, após garantir fluxo estável de moedas e energia. Resetar capítulos regenera moedas da loja, portanto teste composições de equipe sem medo — você pode recuperar moedas ao refazer conteúdo.

Se pretende usar Fofinho de forma competitiva mais tarde, considere adiar upgrades permanentes do herói e foque nas metas do evento usando Moedas de Valor e impulsos temporários. O ascenso completo exige farm contínuo: Fofinho precisa de aproximadamente 630 Fragmentos para chegar ao rank máximo.

How to Prepare and What to Save for Fofinho’s Event

To maximize rewards during Fofinho’s event, plan your resources and timing in advance. The event mixes spending milestones, upgrades, and activity-based missions—prioritize currencies and items that unlock or accelerate progress.

  • Emeralds: Save Emeralds for tier purchases and bulk milestone spending. Time purchases to match milestone requirements to avoid leftover currency.
  • VIP & Bundles: Align bundle purchases with event windows so VIP progress and spending milestones complete efficiently.
  • Artifact & Glyph Materials: Keep artifact and glyph upgrade resources ready to reach required levels—these materials are typically slow to farm.
  • Skin Upgrade Materials: Reserve skin stones and upgrade items if missions ask for skin ranks or upgrades.
  • Energy & Campaign Farming: Hold back enough energy to grind campaign and event stages needed for battle-count missions and repeatable tasks.
  • Gold: Maintain a Gold reserve for promotions, evolutions, and skill upgrades. A large reserve (for example, 15–20 million Gold) helps if you plan major power increases during the event.

Final Thoughts

Fofinho’s event rewards steady preparation and targeted spending. Focus on the milestones that give the most progress per cost, use energy efficiently for repeatable missions, and concentrate upgrades on the hero(s) required by event tasks to maximize reward value.

Conclusion

Both free-to-play and paying players can complete many event goals with planning. Prioritize daily missions, save key currencies before the event, and follow this guide to get the most value from Fofinho’s event.
