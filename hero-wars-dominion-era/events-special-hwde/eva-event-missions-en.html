Eva's event combines the Freedom Protocol event quests with the Unleashing Power hero-development missions. Use the lists below to review all 16 mission chains, their milestones, and the available reward information.

If you intend to build Eva, prepare Emeralds, artifact materials, glyph resources, skin stones, and the Gold needed for her upgrades. Several objectives can be completed together when resources are saved in advance.

Prioritize upgrades that advance multiple missions at once. Eva's Gift of the Elements, evolution, rank, Artifacts, Glyphs, and ability levels each have their own milestone chain.

Freedom Protocol also tracks event battles, Emerald purchases and spending, Archdemon damage, Hero Stall purchases, VIP Points, Stall Coins, and Sapphire Medallions. Use the mission lists below to choose realistic targets before spending.

How to Prepare for Eva's Event Missions

Choose the milestones you can reach with your saved resources. Six Unleashing Power chains focus on Eva; New Edge accepts skin upgrades for any hero, and Call of the Stars tracks Ascension Spire glyph unlocks.

  • Emeralds: Keep purchase and spending objectives separate. Spending saved Emeralds advances Emerald Flow, but does not count as buying Emeralds for Emerald Echo.
  • Eva upgrades: Prepare resources for her Gift of the Elements, evolution, rank, Artifacts, Glyphs, and skills. Use the individual milestones above to plan each upgrade.
  • Skin Stones and Ascension materials: Save these for New Edge and Call of the Stars. Skin missions count upgrade actions rather than a target skin level.
  • Event battles and the Hero Stall: Track wins, pet purchases, and Stall Coin spending as you progress. Cute Helpers counts purchases across all playthroughs.
  • Sapphire Medallions: Plan shop purchases around the Sapphire Fever milestones.
  • Purchases: If you choose to spend money, check the Emerald and VIP points awarded by each offer against the relevant mission targets.

Review the rewards shown in the game before spending: Freedom Protocol reward amounts are unavailable in the supplied information, and the Unleashing Power amounts above are a September 8 reference.
