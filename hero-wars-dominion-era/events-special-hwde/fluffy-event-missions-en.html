The Fluffy event features staged mission milestones that reward Valor Coins and event items. Tackle milestones in a steady rhythm rather than trying to finish everything at once—some goals are long-term and reward more efficient payoffs if planned across multiple days.

Before spending on totems or bundles, check official in-game costs for your region. Treat early cost estimates as provisional and avoid overspending on the first day: the event usually offers repeatable ways to recoup resources.

Prioritize the relic that best supports Fluffy's primary role first, then invest in secondary relics once you secure stable coin and energy income. Resetting chapters regenerates stall coins, so test team compositions without fear — you can reclaim coins by re-running content.

If you plan to use Fluffy competitively later, consider delaying permanent hero-level upgrades and focus on event milestones using Valor Coins and temporary boosts. Full maxing requires a steady grind: Fluffy needs approximately 630 Soul Stones to reach max rank.

How to Prepare and What to Save for Fluffy’s Event

To maximize rewards during Fluffy’s event, plan your resources and timing in advance. The event mixes spending milestones, upgrades, and activity-based missions—prioritize currencies and items that unlock or accelerate progress.

  • Emeralds: Save Emeralds for tier purchases and bulk milestone spending. Time purchases to match milestone requirements to avoid leftover currency.
  • VIP & Bundles: Align bundle purchases with event windows so VIP progress and spending milestones complete efficiently.
  • Artifact & Glyph Materials: Keep artifact and glyph upgrade resources ready to reach required levels—these materials are typically slow to farm.
  • Skin Upgrade Materials: Reserve skin stones and upgrade items if missions ask for skin ranks or upgrades.
  • Energy & Campaign Farming: Hold back enough energy to grind campaign and event stages needed for battle-count missions and repeatable tasks.
  • Gold: Maintain a Gold reserve for promotions, evolutions, and skill upgrades. A large reserve (for example, 15–20 million Gold) helps if you plan major power increases during the event.

Final Thoughts

Fluffy’s event rewards steady preparation and targeted spending. Focus on the milestones that give the most progress per cost, use energy efficiently for repeatable missions, and concentrate upgrades on the hero(s) required by event tasks to maximize reward value.

Conclusion

Both free-to-play and paying players can complete many event goals with planning. Prioritize daily missions, save key currencies before the event, and follow this guide to get the most value from Fluffy’s event.
