Event Overview

The Tidus and Gelo event introduces a full set of missions designed to help you unlock, upgrade, and strengthen the new Fire Titans. Each task provides high–value rewards, essential resources, and opportunities to speed up your overall account growth.

This guide breaks down every mission with clear explanations and practical tips, helping you complete all tasks efficiently—maximizing rewards while avoiding wasted energy and resources.

⚔️ Understanding the Tidus and Gelo Event

The Tidus and Gelo event celebrates the arrival of Tidus and Gelo. Like other Titan introduction events, it offers generous rewards from fragments and totem resources to Emeralds, Gold, and Energy refills. But the real challenge lies in planning your spending and playtime to get the most from it.

🎯 Event Duration

The event typically lasts 7 days, giving players ample time to complete missions and unlock milestones. Since many missions require long-term resource use (Emeralds, VIP points, Titan upgrades), having a plan before the event starts is crucial.

🪶 How to Maximize Your Event Progress

1. Start With Daily Battles

Every day, you can earn progress by completing event battles. Focus on Tidus Titan missions to increase synergy points. Make sure to activate energy-efficient Titans for farming efficiency.

2. Save Emeralds Before the Event

One of the most common mistakes is spending Emeralds too early. Save at least 20,000 Emeralds before the event begins. You’ll need them for missions requiring spending or buying items, ensuring you complete multiple missions at once.

3. Focus on Tidus Upgrades

During the event, prioritize Titan Potions and Gold for Tidus. Their growth directly affects your progress in “Tidus Event” missions. Avoid spreading your resources across multiple Titans during this event window.

🏆 Final Thoughts

The Tidus event is one of the most rewarding Titan events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. Whether you’re a free-to-play player or someone looking to enhance your Earth Titan lineup, the event provides a fair balance of effort and reward. The key to success is strategic timing completing multiple quests simultaneously while managing your Emeralds and Titan resources.

Tidus brings a refreshing level of tactical depth to Titan battles. By mastering their event and learning to use them effectively, you’ll not only strengthen your Titans team but also become a far more versatile player in Guild Wars and PvP. Remember: every mission completed is a step toward building one of the strongest Titan duos in the game!

Prepare your Titans, plan your resources, and let the Tidus Event begin!
