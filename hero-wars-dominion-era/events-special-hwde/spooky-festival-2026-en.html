Relic priority

Upgrade the Tome of Greed first to improve coin income.

Before each upgrade, decide what you want to accomplish next. Keep a written budget so that optional purchases do not consume resources you intended to save for progression.

Hero Stall tips

  • Chapter resets restore Stall Coins.
  • Pin unaffordable cards for the next round.
  • Some heroes appear only in later chapter stages.
  • Violet 1/7 and 2/7 have equal power; tiers are violet, orange, and red.

When a battle stops your progress, review the team's roles before making another purchase. Change one part of your setup at a time so that you can identify which adjustment helps.

Quests and rewards

Quests cover battles, Stall purchases, and spending. Unleashing Power rewards Martha development, skin upgrades, and Ascension. The Battle Pass offers free and paid tracks.

Review the rewards you can reach with your existing inventory first. Choose hero upgrades that also fit your regular team's needs. Compare a purchase with the next useful milestone before spending.

Spending plan

  1. Choose one main goal and set a resource limit before starting.
  2. Review available quests before upgrading heroes or buying resources.
  3. Compare shop items against your account's current bottleneck.
  4. Reassess your remaining budget whenever progress becomes expensive.

The shop closes when the event ends. Make your final shopping decisions before the in-game countdown expires.
