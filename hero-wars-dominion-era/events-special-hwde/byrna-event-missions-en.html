The Byrna event features staged mission milestones that reward Valor Coins and event items. Plan your progress across the full event instead of trying to rush every mission on the first day, because several goals are designed to scale better when you prepare resources in advance.

If you intend to build Byrna seriously, this event is a good moment to combine unlock progress with efficient resource spending. Gold, Emeralds, artifact materials, glyph resources, skin stones, and VIP milestones all matter more when they overlap with event quests.

Prioritize upgrades that push multiple missions at once. For example, leveling Byrna, promoting her rank, raising artifact levels, and improving total skill levels can all be coordinated so one investment unlocks several rewards in the same session.

Since Byrna is a sustain-focused hero, players interested in long-fight teams should treat this event as both a resource event and a hero progression event. The better you prepare before the event begins, the easier it becomes to clear the highest-value mission tiers.

How to Prepare and What to Save for Byrna’s Event

To maximize rewards during Byrna’s event, organize your resources before the event starts. The best progress usually comes from stacking hero upgrades, spending milestones, and activity quests together instead of treating each mission separately.

  • Emeralds: Save Emeralds for both purchase and spending milestones so you can clear multiple event quests without waste.
  • Gold: Keep enough Gold for leveling Byrna, upgrading skills, promoting ranks, and handling sudden progression pushes during the event.
  • Artifact and Glyph Materials: Byrna missions are easier when you already have artifact resources and glyph runes stored, since those upgrades are expensive to rush at the last minute.
  • Skin Stones: If you plan to complete skin upgrade missions, holding skin stones before the event is much better than trying to farm them during the event window.
  • VIP and Bundles: Time your purchases carefully so bundle spending also helps complete VIP quests and spending milestones at the same time.
  • Energy and Daily Activity: Keep enough activity and battle resources ready to clear win-based missions consistently across the entire event.

Final Thoughts

Byrna’s event rewards preparation more than improvisation. Players who enter with saved currencies, planned upgrade targets, and a clear mission order will get far more value than players who spend everything without a route.

Conclusion

If you want to build Byrna efficiently, this guide gives you a clear mission framework to prepare around. Focus on overlapping objectives, save resources in advance, and use the event period to push both hero progress and overall account value.
