Somna's event combines the Nightmare Abode event quests with the Unleashing Power hero-development missions. These milestones award Valor Coins, Sapphire Medallions, and other event resources.

If you intend to build Somna, prepare Emeralds, artifact materials, glyph resources, skin stones, and the Gold needed for her upgrades. Several objectives can be completed together when resources are saved in advance.

Prioritize upgrades that advance multiple missions at once. Somna's Gift of the Elements, evolution, rank, Artifacts, Glyphs, and ability levels each have their own milestone chain.

Nightmare Abode also tracks event battles, Emerald purchases and spending, Archdemon damage, Hero Stall purchases, VIP Points, Stall Coins, and Sapphire Medallions. Use the mission lists below to choose realistic targets before spending.

How to Prepare and What to Save for Heroes’ Event

To maximize rewards during Heroes’ event, organize your resources before the event starts. The best progress usually comes from stacking hero upgrades, spending milestones, and activity quests together instead of treating each mission separately.

  • Emeralds: Save Emeralds for both purchase and spending milestones so you can clear multiple event quests without waste.
  • Gold: Keep enough Gold for leveling heroes, upgrading skills, promoting ranks, and handling sudden progression pushes during the event.
  • Artifact and Glyph Materials: Heroes’ event missions are easier when you already have artifact resources and glyph runes stored, since those upgrades are expensive to rush at the last minute.
  • Skin Stones: If you plan to complete skin upgrade missions, holding skin stones before the event is much better than trying to farm them during the event window.
  • VIP and Bundles: Time your purchases carefully so bundle spending also helps complete VIP quests and spending milestones at the same time.
  • Energy and Daily Activity: Keep enough activity and battle resources ready to clear win-based missions consistently across the entire event.

Final Thoughts

Heroes’ event rewards preparation more than improvisation. Players who enter with saved currencies, planned upgrade targets, and a clear mission order will get far more value than players who spend everything without a route.

Conclusion

If you want to build heroes efficiently, this guide gives you a clear mission framework to prepare around. Focus on overlapping objectives, save resources in advance, and use the event period to push both hero progress and overall account value.
