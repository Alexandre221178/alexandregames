Fluffy was an ordinary lamb until fate placed him on a sacrificial altar. When the Guardians interrupted the dark ritual, the demon Baalthazar attempted to possess Fluffy's body. However, the lamb's pure mind awakened and proved too innocent for Baalthazar to fully control. Now they are forced to coexist in one body, creating a unique hero who serves the Guardians — to Fluffy they are heroes, but to Baalthazar they are tools for revenge against the Archdemon.

As a Support Mage positioned on the Middle line with Intelligence as his primary attribute, Fluffy brings a fascinating duality to battle. His pure lamb consciousness provides healing and protective abilities, while Baalthazar's demonic influence unleashes devastating shadow magic. In this guide, you'll discover everything needed to navigate the Unleashing Power event with confidence — mission details, strategic insights, and preparation tips for both new and veteran players. Master this unique hero and watch your team's power reach new heights.

What to Expect From the Fluffy Event Unleashing Power

The Unleashing Power event brings a mix of daily missions, energy spending tasks, and hero-focused objectives. Players can earn Fluffy resources, magical items, summoning spheres, and more.

Below, we break down each mission group, explain how rewards scale, and provide efficient strategies to complete everything without overspending. Let the shadows guide your victory.

How the Unleashing Power Event Works

The Unleashing Power event centers on awakening Fluffy's dual nature and unlocking his combined abilities. This event introduces quest chains that focus on hero leveling, evolution, artifact strengthening, and Intelligence-based progression. Completing these missions rewards you with Hero Potions, Elemental Spheres, and event currency used to unlock and upgrade Fluffy.

Free-to-play players should prioritize completing all main event quests first, as these provide the highest-value evolution materials. The mission flow begins with simple tasks, then advances into more demanding goals like artifact upgrades and shadow-element progression. Each completed quest strengthens Fluffy and accelerates your overall event progress.

To evolve Fluffy efficiently, concentrate your resources solely on Fluffy during the event. Avoid spreading hero resources across other elements, and complete all "Growth Reward" missions to secure top-tier hero materials and additional summoning opportunities.

Fluffy Unleashing Power Missions

The event contains Fluffy-focused missions that revolve around awakening his dual nature, enhancing his Support and Intelligence abilities, and balancing both his lamb's purity and Baalthazar's demonic powers. Completing these tasks provides high-value rewards and speeds up your progression. Since many missions can be repeated, upgrading Fluffy early helps you reach higher reward tiers faster and maximize your gains throughout the event.

Convert Energy Coins and Altar of Fate

Free-to-play players should prioritize using the Energy Exchange inside the Altar of Fate to convert their Energy Coins into Valor Coins, allowing them to reach the total of 3,130 Soul Stones required for Fluffy 6★. This method is one of the safest and most reliable ways for F2P to secure her without depending too much on campaign boosters or harder event missions.

If you are not F2P, it is recommended to save the Energy Exchange for the final days of the event. This ensures you don't waste Energy Coins that might still be needed for Workshop upgrades, Altar pulls, or Chapter progression. Spend your Energy Coins efficiently use them mainly for Altar summons that target Fluffy Soul Stones, and upgrade your Workshop relics to reduce overall costs and maximize long-term value.

The Event Workshop: Fluffy Upgrade Priorities

The Event Workshop plays a key role in powering up Fluffy. Start by upgrading the Tome of Greed, which lowers costs and enhances rewards when spending Energy Crystals or improving relics dramatically increasing your efficiency. Next, focus on the Tome of Shadows to boost Fluffy Soul Stone gains over time. The Shadow Sigil comes last; while it improves battle performance, it is less essential than maintaining a consistent flow of Soul Stones and event currency during the event.

Strategy Tip: Maximize the Tome of Greed First

Always upgrade the Tome of Greed first. Even at low levels it reduces Energy Crystals costs, and at higher levels the bonus becomes incredible. Lower Altar pull costs and increased energy efficiency allow you to farm Fluffy Soul Stones to absolute star much faster, speeding up your overall event progression.

The Altar of Fate and Soul Stone Farming

The Altar of Fate is where most Valor Coins should be spent. Each 10x pull offers chances to obtain Fluffy's Soul Stones and other high-value rewards. Avoid single pulls unless you need a specific item. Batch openings are more efficient and statistically yield more Soul Stones.

Star Reinforcement Table

With the Tome of Greed and Grail of Generosity at level 6, expect approximately 35–40 Fluffy Soul Stones per 10x Altar summon. The Star Reinforcement table below shows a total requirement of 630 Soul Stones to reach 6★ for Fluffy, so you'll need roughly 16–18 10x summons depending on luck and Workshop bonuses.

Each 10x summon at Grail of Generosity level 6 costs about 22,000 Valor Coins, so plan for approximately 352,000–396,000 Valor Coins in total to complete Fluffy's evolution to 6★. Save Valor Coins in advance and prioritize Workshop relic upgrades to reduce overall costs.

Embers of the Past - Fluffy Event Chapters' Rewards

If you manage to spend Boosters to advance through the event chapters, you can collect all rewards but F2P players should focus only on Chapter I and Chapter II, since these do not require any Boosters to complete. This strategy allows you to secure the most valuable prize of the entire event: Fluffy at 6★.

Titan Soul Stones are far rarer and harder to obtain than the Campaign resources you would get from later chapters using Boosters. Even with perfect play, F2P players cannot realistically reach the final grand prize the Absolute Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere. Therefore, prioritize Chapters I and II to maximize value without wasting resources, and save your Boosters for future events or strategic Campaign progression.

Battle Pass: Is It Worth It?

The Battle Pass for Fluffy's event provides significant value for both F2P and paying players. The free tier alone offers substantial rewards including Soul Stones, Energy Crystals, and upgrade materials. However, the premium tier unlocks exponential benefits that can accelerate your progress dramatically.

Recommendation: For F2P players, focus on completing all free Battle Pass levels. Light spenders should consider the Premium tier as it provides nearly 40% of the Soul Stones needed for Fluffy 6★ at an excellent price point. The Elite tier is only recommended if you want to guarantee 6★ within the event duration.

What to Buy in the Event Shop

In the event shop, focus first on purchasing items that help you complete event missions efficiently. After that, prioritize Fluffy-specific artifacts and exclusive materials. These unique items are likely obtainable only during this event or through cash offers, making them highly valuable for players aiming to strengthen Fluffy and their shadow teams.

Shopping Priority List

  1. Fluffy Soul Stones - Always purchase first to progress toward 6★
  2. Shadow Essence - Required for star upgrades and skill enhancements
  3. Mystic Crystals - Needed for higher star upgrades (4★+)
  4. Dark Orbs - Essential for 5★ and 6★ evolution
  5. Energy Crystals - Use for Workshop upgrades and Altar pulls
  6. Exclusive Artifacts - Fluffy's weapon and accessory artifacts

Since Fluffy Soul Stones are earned through other missions, use your shop currency wisely on long-term upgrades. Prioritize Shadow Essence and Mystic Crystals, as they provide lasting progression benefits. Other hero Soul Stones are optional, and totems or upgrade materials should be chosen based on your current needs. Avoid consumables like energy potions and save your resources for the most impactful items.

Mistakes to Avoid to Ensure Fluffy 6⭐

Reaching 6★ with Fluffy requires careful resource management and strategic planning. Avoid these common pitfalls that can derail your progress:

Critical Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Spending Valor Coins on low-value items - Don't waste currency on consumables or temporary boosters
  • Upgrading Workshop relics in wrong order - Always prioritize Tome of Greed before Grail of Generosity
  • Using single Altar pulls - 10x pulls are statistically more efficient and cost-effective
  • Spreading resources across multiple heroes - Focus solely on Fluffy during the event
  • Ignoring free Battle Pass rewards - Complete all free tiers for substantial Soul Stone gains
  • Converting Energy Coins too early - Save Energy Exchange for final days unless you're F2P
  • Skipping daily event missions - These provide consistent progress and valuable materials
  • Purchasing premium Shop items before essentials - Get Soul Stones and upgrade materials first

Resource Management Tips:

Start preparing before the event begins by saving Gold for Shadow element artifacts and gathering upgrade materials. Plan your Valor Coin spending carefully — you'll need between 462,000 and 660,000 total for 6★ evolution. Focus on consistent daily progress rather than trying to rush through everything in the final days.

💡 Alexandre's Success Strategy

Getting a new hero like Fluffy to six stars as a free-to-play player requires patience and planning, but it's absolutely achievable with the right approach! The key is starting your preparation early and following a disciplined resource management strategy.

Save plenty of Gold before the event to upgrade Shadow element artifacts, collect Energy Crystals for Workshop upgrades, and hoard Valor Coins for Altar summons. Most importantly, resist the temptation to spend resources on other heroes during the event — Fluffy should be your sole focus. With dedication and smart planning, 6★ Fluffy is within reach for any player!

Free-to-Play Path to Six Stars

F2P players should complete all Stage 1 and 2 missions first, repeat Fluffy missions until fully completed, max the Tome of Greed and Tome of Shadows, and use Altar of Fate 10x pulls. Prioritize Fluffy Soul Stones above all other items and save extra energy for daily mission resets. With consistent dedication, six stars is absolutely achievable without spending.

Minimum Artifact and Equipment Requirements

To efficiently complete event missions and farming objectives, ensure your Fluffy meets these minimum requirements:

  • Artifacts: Minimum Level 90 for each of the 3 main artifacts (Weapon, Ring, and Book)
  • Skills: Minimum Level 80 for all abilities
  • Hero Level: Level 100
  • Equipment: All items at least Purple quality

These upgrades are sufficient to complete all event missions and unlock the ability to use Sapphire Medallions in the event shop to purchase additional Energy Crystals, accelerating your progress toward six stars.

Conclusion

The Fluffy event offers one of the best opportunities in Hero Wars: Dominion Era to unlock and evolve a powerful new shadow hero, but success depends heavily on preparation and resource management. By prioritizing Valor Coins, upgrading Tome of Greed before Grail of Generosity, and focusing on the essential missions if you are F2P, you can reliably secure Fluffy at 6★ — the most valuable reward for most players.

Premium purchases can help P2P or light spenders push faster toward their goals with Battle Pass tiers and exclusive shop items, but these are far less impactful than obtaining the hero herself through strategic farming. Stay patient, plan your currency savings ahead of time, and you will maximize your gains throughout the event while avoiding unnecessary waste of resources.
