Nightfall is the introductory event for Umbra and Caligo. New Titan events like this are among the most generous events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, with many free rewards from the shop and a totem of choice that may require up to $50.

Plan before spending resources. The event can be very rewarding, but reaching the final chapter without spending is not expected, and the last chapter's totem reward will probably require paid progress.

Disclaimer: Alexandre Games is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with Nexters in any way. This event guide was created from the current Umbra event information available before the end of the event and may change at any time depending on updates made by the developers.

Event Overview

The Umbra event is built around chapters, event battles, the Hero Stall, the Development Shop, Sapphire Medallions, and direct Umbra and Caligo upgrades. Use the optimizer before you commit major resources, especially Emeralds, relic levels, and artifact fragments.

Without spending, it is impossible to reach Chapter VII. Chapter resets regenerate Stall Coins, so you can test your own team compositions, but completing the event still takes time. Waiting for Nexters' gifts can improve your optimization, but do not leave the full event for the last day.

  • Always upgrade the first relic before investing in the third relic to earn many more coins.
  • Some heroes only become available on the later stages of a chapter.
  • The island contains Sapphire Medallions and Valor Coins.
  • Umbra and Caligo need 3,130 Soul Stones and 18,400 artifact fragments to be maxed.

Understanding the Umbra Event

The Nightfall event celebrates the arrival of Umbra and Caligo. The two major quest families are Spawn of the Dark, focused on event-wide progress, and Voice of the Night, focused on upgrading Umbra and Caligo directly.

Event Duration

The event is expected to run like other new Titan events, giving players several days to complete missions and unlock milestones. Since many missions require Emeralds, VIP points, Titan upgrades, chapter energy, and repeated battles, planning before the event starts is crucial.

How to Maximize Your Event Progress

1. Plan Before Spending

Consult the optimizer before using Emeralds, Energy Crystals, medallions, relic upgrades, or artifact fragments. The event rewards planning more than rushing.

2. Upgrade the First Relic Early

Upgrade the first relic before investing in the third relic. This gives significantly more coins and makes later decisions easier.

3. Use Chapter Resets Wisely

Stall Coins regenerate when you reset a chapter, so test team compositions instead of locking into the first setup that works.

4. Wait for Gifts, But Not Too Long

Nexters may grant gifts before the final day, and waiting can improve optimization. The full event takes time, so do not postpone everything until the last day.

Final Thoughts

The Umbra event is one of the most rewarding Titan introduction events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. The free shop rewards are strong, but the highest chapter rewards and totem path are built for players who plan spending carefully.

Umbra and Caligo require 3,130 Soul Stones and 18,400 artifact fragments to reach maximum investment. Track every chapter, shop purchase, medallion, and Altar of Fate pull before committing resources.

Prepare your Titans, plan your resources, and let the Nightfall event begin.
