Event Overview

The Lumira and Apollo event introduces a full set of missions designed to help you unlock, upgrade, and strengthen the new Light Titans. Each task provides high-value rewards, essential resources, and opportunities to speed up your overall account growth.

This guide breaks down every mission with clear explanations and practical tips, helping you complete all tasks efficiently — maximizing rewards while avoiding wasted energy and resources.

Understanding the Lumira and Apollo Event

The Lumira and Apollo event celebrates the arrival of the Light-element summoner Titan Lumira and her companion Apollo. Like other Titan introduction events, it offers generous rewards - from fragments and totem resources to Emeralds, Gold, and Energy refills. But the real challenge lies in planning your spending and playtime to get the most from it.

Event Duration

The event typically lasts 7 days, giving players ample time to complete missions and unlock milestones. Since many missions require long-term resource use (Emeralds, VIP points, Titan upgrades), having a plan before the event starts is crucial.

How to Maximize Your Event Progress

1. Start With Daily Battles

Every day, you can earn progress by completing event battles. Focus on Lumira Titan missions to increase synergy points.

2. Save Emeralds Before the Event

One of the most common mistakes is spending Emeralds too early. Save at least 20,000 Emeralds before the event begins.

3. Focus on Lumira Upgrades

During the event, prioritize Titan Potions and Gold for Lumira. Their growth directly affects your progress in "Lumira Event" missions.

Final Thoughts

The Lumira event is one of the most rewarding Titan events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. The key to success is strategic timing - completing multiple quests simultaneously while managing your Emeralds and Titan resources.

Lumira brings a refreshing level of tactical depth to Titan battles with her summoner role and ability to call Apollo into the fight. By mastering the event and learning to use them effectively, you will strengthen your Titans team and become a more versatile player in Guild Wars and PvP.

Prepare your Titans, plan your resources, and let the Lumira Principle of Light Event begin!
