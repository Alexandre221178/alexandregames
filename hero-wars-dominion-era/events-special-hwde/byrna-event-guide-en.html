Byrna is a Healer, Mage, and Grove Keeper whose main stat is Strength. That makes her very different from the usual fragile support archetype in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, because she combines sustain with real durability.

According to the game data, Byrna sits at position 42 and reaches 174,510 Power, 828,482 Health, 74,315 Magic Attack, and 42,409 Magic Penetration at max development. She is built for longer battles, where healing, damage sharing, and scaling support matter more than quick burst.

In this guide, the event structure remains similar, but the page is now correctly adapted to Byrna and the event The Ritual's Price. If you want to invest in a hero who protects the frontline while improving team sustain over time, this is the right event to prepare for.

What to Expect From The Ritual's Price

The Ritual's Price event keeps the familiar rhythm of daily missions, energy spending tasks, and hero-focused objectives. Players can earn Byrna resources, event currency, and upgrade materials that help push her toward 6 stars.

Below, we keep the same practical focus: how to manage the Workshop, how to use the Altar of Fate, and what to prioritize if you are free-to-play or only spending a little.

How The Ritual's Price Event Works

The The Ritual's Price event centers on building Byrna through hero leveling, evolution, artifact strengthening, and resource planning. Since Byrna's Soul Stones are temporarily unobtainable outside special opportunities, this event is one of the most efficient ways to develop her quickly.

Free-to-play players should prioritize the most efficient quest chains first. Byrna benefits heavily from upgrades that improve survivability and healing output, so levels, skills, artifacts, and glyphs all contribute to both event progress and long-term account value.

To evolve Byrna efficiently, concentrate your resources on Byrna during the event. Avoid spreading Gold, materials, and enhancement resources across multiple heroes if your main goal is to push her to 6 stars.

Byrna Mission Focus

Byrna's kit explains why this event is valuable. Roar of Nature damages all enemies and heals allies. Guardian Spirit protects the frontline by sharing incoming damage. Bear Cuddle spreads healing value through the team. Living Heart increases Byrna's Magic Attack whenever allies restore health, which means her support grows stronger over time.

Convert Energy Coins and Altar of Fate

Free-to-play players should prioritize using the Energy Exchange inside the Altar of Fate to convert Energy Coins into Valor Coins. This remains one of the safest ways to build toward Byrna 6 stars without relying too much on the most expensive parts of the event.

If you are not F2P, saving part of your Energy Exchange for the final days can still be the best approach. That gives you more flexibility to decide between extra Altar pulls, Workshop upgrades, or chapter progression once your totals are clear.

The Event Workshop: Byrna Upgrade Priorities

The Workshop is still the center of efficient progress. Start with the Tome of Greed to improve Valor Coin efficiency. After that, move into the Grail of Generosity to reduce conversion costs. The overall logic is the same: better efficiency first, cheaper Altar costs second.

Strategy Tip: Maximize the Tome of Greed First

Always upgrade the Tome of Greed first. Even at low levels it reduces Energy Crystal pressure, and at higher levels the coin efficiency becomes much stronger. Lower Altar costs and increased conversion value let you farm Byrna Soul Stones much faster.

Embers of the Past - Byrna Event Chapters' Rewards

If you manage to spend Boosters to advance through the event chapters, you can collect all rewards but F2P players should focus only on Chapter I and Chapter II, since these do not require any Boosters to complete. This strategy allows you to secure the most valuable prize of the entire event: Byrna at 6★.

Titan Soul Stones are far rarer and harder to obtain than the Campaign resources you would get from later chapters using Boosters. Even with perfect play, F2P players cannot realistically reach the final grand prize the Absolute Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere. Therefore, prioritize Chapters I and II to maximize value without wasting resources, and save your Boosters for future events or strategic Campaign progression.

Battle Pass: Is It Worth It?

The Battle Pass for Byrna's event provides strong value for both F2P and paying players. The free tier already helps with Soul Stones, Energy Crystals, and general upgrade materials. Premium value depends mostly on how close you want to get to 6 stars during the event itself.

Recommendation: F2P players should still complete all free Battle Pass levels. Light spenders can consider the Premium tier if they want to accelerate Byrna's evolution and reduce pressure on Altar pull luck.

What to Buy in the Event Shop

In the event shop, focus first on items that help you complete event missions efficiently. After that, prioritize Byrna-specific materials, Soul Stones, and anything that supports her artifacts, glyphs, and survivability.

Shopping Priority List

  1. Byrna Soul Stones - Always purchase first if you still need progress toward 6 stars
  2. Energy Crystals - Useful for Workshop efficiency and Altar value
  3. Artifact Materials - Especially for Talisman of the Orc Princess, Manuscript of the Void, and Ring of Strength
  4. Glyph Resources - Byrna scales well with Magic Attack, Magic Penetration, Armor, Magic Defense, and Strength glyphs
  5. Skin Materials - Helpful for boosting Strength or Armor through Byrna's skins
  6. Flexible Account Resources - Only after the Byrna-specific priorities are covered

Since Byrna Soul Stones are earned through the event itself, spend shop currency on upgrades that keep paying off after the event ends. Long-term value is more important than short-lived consumables.

Mistakes to Avoid to Ensure Byrna 6⭐

Reaching 6 stars with Byrna requires careful resource management and realistic planning. Avoid these common mistakes:

Critical Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Spending Valor Coins on low-value items - prioritize Soul Stone progress and core upgrades
  • Upgrading Workshop relics in the wrong order - Tome of Greed still comes before Grail of Generosity
  • Using single Altar pulls - 10x pulls remain more efficient
  • Spreading resources across multiple heroes - focus on Byrna during the event window
  • Ignoring free Battle Pass rewards - they meaningfully reduce pressure on the rest of the event
  • Underestimating Gold costs - Byrna still needs heavy Gold investment to reach full evolution
  • Skipping daily missions - steady daily progress matters more than a late panic push
  • Buying premium shop items before essentials - secure Byrna progress first, luxury second

Resource Management Tips:

Start preparing before the event begins by saving Gold, Energy Crystals, artifact materials, and general enhancement resources. Byrna scales well in long fights, so investing properly in her is not just event progress — it improves your roster after the event too.

💡 Alexandre's Success Strategy

Getting a new hero like Byrna to six stars as a free-to-play player requires patience and planning, but it is absolutely possible with the right approach.

Save plenty of Gold before the event, collect Energy Crystals for Workshop upgrades, and hoard Valor Coins for Altar summons. Most importantly, avoid spending your best resources on other heroes during the event. If Byrna is your target, make her the center of your plan.

Free-to-Play Path to Six Stars

F2P players should complete the earliest mission stages first, repeat Byrna-focused progress missions when available, max the Tome of Greed before pushing the Grail too hard, and use Altar of Fate 10x pulls. Prioritize Byrna Soul Stones above all other optional rewards.

Minimum Artifact and Equipment Requirements

To complete event missions more comfortably, aim for these minimum Byrna goals:

  • Artifacts: steady upgrades to the three main artifacts, with Ring of Strength and Manuscript of the Void as strong priorities
  • Skills: keep all four skills leveled, especially Roar of Nature and Guardian Spirit
  • Hero Level: raise Byrna consistently so healing and scaling stay relevant
  • Equipment: maintain rank progression so her stats and mission milestones move together

These upgrades are enough to keep event progress moving while also improving Byrna's real usefulness in Arena, Campaign, and long-form fights.

Conclusion

The Ritual's Price is one of the best opportunities in Hero Wars: Dominion Era to unlock and evolve Byrna, a healer who becomes more threatening the longer the fight goes. By prioritizing Valor Coins, respecting the Tome of Greed upgrade order, and focusing on Byrna-specific growth, you can make this event far more efficient.

Byrna is not just another support unit. Her combination of healing, damage sharing, and scaling Magic Attack gives her long-term account value. If you prepare resources in advance and stay disciplined during the event, pushing Byrna toward 6 stars is a realistic and worthwhile goal.
