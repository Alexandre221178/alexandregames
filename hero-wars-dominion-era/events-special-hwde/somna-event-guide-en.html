Somna's introductory event combines a new-Hero progression track, chapter battles, an event shop, the Altar of Fate, and a possible Totem reward. Events in this format can return excellent value, but the currencies are connected closely enough that spending without a route can block a later chapter or shop purchase.

Plan Your Final Goal Before Spending

The largest rewards sit behind several linked systems. Decide first whether your target is Somna progression, Chapter VI, the final chapter's Totem, or a Totem from the shop. Prices, free gifts, and live offers can change the exact route, so treat spending estimates as planning ranges rather than guarantees.

Somna Event Overview

Free Route

Complete every free mission, use the daily resets, and collect all gifts. The supplied event values indicate that Chapter VI may require about 50,000 Emeralds.

Totem Route

Free progress is not expected to reach Chapter VII or a shop Totem. The last chapter's Totem may require spending up to roughly $46, depending on gifts and offers.

Somna Goal

Somna requires 630 Soul Stones for Absolute Star. Do not confuse this normal Hero requirement with the much larger totals used by some older event guides.

A carefully chosen paid route may supply enough Sapphire Medallions for an additional Totem from the shop after the chapter reward. This is possible, not guaranteed: calculate against the live offers and your actual Medallion balance before buying.

What Kind of Hero Is Somna?

Somna is an Agility-based control Hero positioned at number 51. Her game plan is not raw burst damage: she spreads Sleepiness, reduces enemy Physical and Magic Attack, transforms heavily affected targets into sheep, and uses her ultimate to put the enemy team to sleep for up to 7 seconds.

  • Lullaby Veil: sleeps enemy Heroes until the duration ends or the damage threshold wakes them.
  • Dream Armor: protects the front ally and lets that ally apply Sleepiness with basic attacks.
  • Counting Sheep: damages the enemy center and transforms targets already carrying three Sleepiness stacks.
  • Domain of Tranquility: punishes enemies that damage Somna or adjacent allies by applying more Sleepiness.

At the supplied maximum values, Somna has 446,752 Health, 92,033 Physical Attack, and 173,612 Power. Fenris, Oliver, and Axel can patron her. Sebastian can block her sleep control; Khorus does not provide the same answer.

Best Relic Order: Tome of Greed First

Upgrade the Tome of Greed before investing deeply in the other event relics. Its purpose is economic: earlier upgrades increase the coin income generated by the actions you will repeat throughout the event. Delaying it means performing the same chapter and shop activity for less currency.

Recommended Sequence

  1. Raise the Tome of Greed as early as your planned route allows.
  2. Only then invest in the Grail or combat relics needed to clear the next stages.
  3. Stop upgrading a relic when its next level costs more than the extra progress it unlocks.

Relic levels also have an end-of-event conversion value, but that small return should not be the reason to over-upgrade. Their main value comes from what they unlock while the event is active.

Chapter Progress and Battle Resets

Resetting a chapter restores the Stall Coins used for its battles. This makes resets useful for testing team combinations instead of treating every unsuccessful attempt as a permanent loss. Some Heroes only become available on later stages within a chapter, so a team that fails early may become viable after the roster expands.

Do not rush every reset on day one. Waiting for later gifts gives you more information and can reduce the amount of Emeralds or money needed. At the same time, the chapters take time to play, so leaving the entire event until the final day creates a different risk: having enough resources but not enough time to use them.

How to Use Sapphire Medallions

Sapphire Medallions are the scarce shop currency and should be assigned only after your chapter plan is settled. A Totem is the long-term prize for accounts still building Elemental Spirits, while items such as Skin Stone Chests, Chaos Cores, Wish resources, Energy, Pet resources, and red equipment can be useful when a Totem is out of reach.

  • Reserve the Medallions required for your primary target before buying small materials.
  • Compare the value of a shop Totem with the final chapter's Absolute Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere.
  • Avoid buying convenience items early merely because they are affordable.
  • On the final day, convert the remaining balance deliberately instead of letting the game decide your priorities.

The Event Workshop: Upgrade Priorities

The Event Workshop plays a key role in powering up Heroes. Start by upgrading the Tome of Greed, which lowers costs and enhances rewards when spending Energy Crystals or improving relics dramatically increasing your efficiency. Next, focus on the Tome of Shadows to boost Hero Soul Stone gains over time. The Shadow Sigil comes last; while it improves battle performance, it is less essential than maintaining a consistent flow of Soul Stones and event currency during the event.

Convert Energy Coins and Altar of Fate

Free-to-play players should prioritize using the Energy Exchange inside the Altar of Fate to convert their Energy Coins into Valor Coins, allowing them to reach the total of 3,130 Soul Stones required for Heroes 6★. This method is one of the safest and most reliable ways for F2P to secure her without depending too much on campaign boosters or harder event missions.

If you are not F2P, it is recommended to save the Energy Exchange for the final days of the event. This ensures you don't waste Energy Coins that might still be needed for Workshop upgrades, Altar pulls, or Chapter progression. Spend your Energy Coins efficiently use them mainly for Altar summons that target Hero Soul Stones, and upgrade your Workshop relics to reduce overall costs and maximize long-term value.

Strategy Tip: Maximize the Tome of Greed First

Always upgrade the Tome of Greed first. Even at low levels it reduces Energy Crystals costs, and at higher levels the bonus becomes incredible. Lower Altar pull costs and increased energy efficiency allow you to farm Hero Soul Stones to absolute star much faster, speeding up your overall event progression.

The Altar of Fate and Soul Stone Farming

The Altar of Fate is where most Valor Coins should be spent. Each 10x pull offers chances to obtain Hero's Soul Stones and other high-value rewards. Avoid single pulls unless you need a specific item. Batch openings are more efficient and statistically yield more Soul Stones.

Star Reinforcement Table

With the Tome of Greed and Grail of Generosity at level 6, expect approximately 35–40 Hero Soul Stones per 10x Altar summon. The Star Reinforcement table below shows a total requirement of 630 Soul Stones to reach 6★ for Hero, so you'll need roughly 16–18 10x summons depending on luck and Workshop bonuses.

Each 10x summon at Grail of Generosity level 6 costs about 22,000 Valor Coins, so plan for approximately 352,000–396,000 Valor Coins in total to complete Hero's evolution to 6★. Save Valor Coins in advance and prioritize Workshop relic upgrades to reduce overall costs.

Battle Pass: Is It Worth It?

The Battle Pass for Hero's event provides significant value for both F2P and paying players. The free tier alone offers substantial rewards including Soul Stones, Energy Crystals, and upgrade materials. However, the premium tier unlocks exponential benefits that can accelerate your progress dramatically.

Recommendation: For F2P players, focus on completing all free Battle Pass levels. Light spenders should consider the Premium tier as it provides nearly 40% of the Soul Stones needed for Hero 6★ at an excellent price point. The Elite tier is only recommended if you want to guarantee 6★ within the event duration.

What to Buy in the Event Shop

In the event shop, focus first on purchasing items that help you complete event missions efficiently. After that, prioritize Hero-specific artifacts and exclusive materials. These unique items are likely obtainable only during this event or through cash offers, making them highly valuable for players aiming to strengthen Hero and their shadow teams.

Shopping Priority List

  1. Hero Soul Stones - Always purchase first to progress toward 6★
  2. Shadow Essence - Required for star upgrades and skill enhancements
  3. Mystic Crystals - Needed for higher star upgrades (4★+)
  4. Dark Orbs - Essential for 5★ and 6★ evolution
  5. Energy Crystals - Use for Workshop upgrades and Altar pulls
  6. Exclusive Artifacts - Hero's weapon and accessory artifacts

Since Hero Soul Stones are earned through other missions, use your shop currency wisely on long-term upgrades. Prioritize Shadow Essence and Mystic Crystals, as they provide lasting progression benefits. Other hero Soul Stones are optional, and totems or upgrade materials should be chosen based on your current needs. Avoid consumables like energy potions and save your resources for the most impactful items.

Mistakes to Avoid to Ensure Hero 6⭐

Reaching 6★ with Hero requires careful resource management and strategic planning. Avoid these common pitfalls that can derail your progress:

Critical Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Spending Valor Coins on low-value items - Don't waste currency on consumables or temporary boosters
  • Upgrading Workshop relics in wrong order - Always prioritize Tome of Greed before Grail of Generosity
  • Using single Altar pulls - 10x pulls are statistically more efficient and cost-effective
  • Spreading resources across multiple heroes - Focus solely on Hero during the event
  • Ignoring free Battle Pass rewards - Complete all free tiers for substantial Soul Stone gains
  • Converting Energy Coins too early - Save Energy Exchange for final days unless you're F2P
  • Skipping daily event missions - These provide consistent progress and valuable materials
  • Purchasing premium Shop items before essentials - Get Soul Stones and upgrade materials first

Resource Management Tips:

Start preparing before the event begins by saving Gold for Shadow element artifacts and gathering upgrade materials. Plan your Valor Coin spending carefully — you'll need between 462,000 and 660,000 total for 6★ evolution. Focus on consistent daily progress rather than trying to rush through everything in the final days.

Free-to-Play Path to Six Stars

F2P players should complete all Stage 1 and 2 missions first, repeat Hero missions until fully completed, max the Tome of Greed and Tome of Shadows, and use Altar of Fate 10x pulls. Prioritize Hero Soul Stones above all other items and save extra energy for daily mission resets. With consistent dedication, six stars is absolutely achievable without spending.

Minimum Artifact and Equipment Requirements

To efficiently complete event missions and farming objectives, ensure your Hero meets these minimum requirements:

  • Artifacts: Minimum Level 90 for each of the 3 main artifacts (Weapon, Ring, and Book)
  • Skills: Minimum Level 80 for all abilities
  • Hero Level: Level 100
  • Equipment: All items at least Purple quality

These upgrades are sufficient to complete all event missions and unlock the ability to use Sapphire Medallions in the event shop to purchase additional Energy Crystals, accelerating your progress toward six stars.

Mission and Resource Plan

Somna's Unleashing Power missions reward upgrades to her Gift of the Elements, evolution, rank, Artifacts, Glyphs, and abilities. The connected Nightmare Abode quests track battles, Emerald purchases and spending, Archdemon damage, Hero Stall purchases, VIP Points, Stall Coins, and Sapphire Medallions.

Use the dedicated Somna Event Missions Guide to check every threshold before committing resources. The efficient approach is to make one action advance more than one objective—for example, spending Emeralds only when the purchase also unlocks a chapter or supplies the exact materials needed for Somna.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting without a target: chapter, shop, and relic costs compete for the same limited resources.
  • Upgrading combat relics before the Tome of Greed: this lowers the coin return from later activity.
  • Assuming Chapter VII is free: the supplied values indicate that it requires paid progress.
  • Using Medallions on small items too early: doing so can remove the option to buy a Totem later.
  • Expecting Khorus to stop Somna: Sebastian is the relevant sleep counter in the supplied Hero data.
  • Waiting until the final hours: late gifts help planning, but chapter battles still require real play time.

Final Recommendation

Free players should aim for the highest chapter their saved Emeralds and mission resources can reach without damaging the rest of their account. Light spenders should price the complete route to a Totem before buying the first offer. In both cases, upgrade the Tome of Greed early, preserve Sapphire Medallions until the plan is certain, and leave enough time to finish the battles.

Somna herself is a specialized control Hero. Her value depends on whether your teams can exploit long sleep windows and stacked attack reduction, so obtaining her is only part of the decision—consider whether you also have the resources and allies needed to build a functional Somna team.
