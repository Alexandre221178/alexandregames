How to Plan Your Orm Event Missions

  • Set a resource budget: compare your inventory with the next milestone before starting an upgrade.
  • Track purchases and spending separately: record both to avoid confusing your progress between mission chains.
  • Keep two checklists: use the Orm development table for your Titan and the shop lists for your event run.
  • Review rewards in the game: reward amounts are not yet confirmed here. Check each task before committing resources.
