What to Expect From the Lumira Event Principle of Light

The Lumira Principle of Light event brings a mix of daily missions, energy spending tasks, and Titan-focused objectives. Players can earn Lumira and Apollo resources, elemental items, summoning spheres, and more.

Below, we break down each mission group, explain how rewards scale, and provide efficient strategies to complete everything without overspending. Let the light guide your victory.

How the Principle of Light Event Works

The Lumira Principle of Light event centers on the rise of Lumira, a Light-element summoner Titan who calls Apollo into battle. This event introduces quest chains that focus on Titan leveling, evolution, artifact strengthening, and progression. Completing these missions rewards you with Titan Potions, Elemental Spheres, and event currency used to unlock and upgrade Lumira and Apollo.

Free-to-play players should prioritize completing all main event quests first, as these provide the highest-value evolution materials. The mission flow begins with simple tasks, then advances into more demanding goals like artifact upgrades and progression. Each completed quest strengthens Lumira and accelerates your overall event progress.

To evolve Lumira efficiently, concentrate your resources solely on Lumira and Apollo during the event. Avoid spreading Titan resources across other elements, and complete all “Growth Reward” missions to secure top-tier Titan materials and additional summoning opportunities.

Lumira Principle of Light Missions

The event contains Lumira-focused missions that revolve around leveling Lumira and improving her overall battle strength. Completing these tasks provides high-value rewards and speeds up your progression. Since many missions can be repeated, upgrading Lumira early helps you reach higher reward tiers faster and maximize your gains throughout the event.

Convert Energy Coins and Altar of Fate

Free-to-play players should prioritize using the Energy Exchange inside the Altar of Fate to convert their Energy Coins into Valor Coins, allowing them to reach the total of 3,130 Soul Stones required for Lumira 6★. This method is one of the safest and most reliable ways for F2P to secure her without depending too much on campaign boosters or harder event missions.

If you are not F2P, it is recommended to save the Energy Exchange for the final days of the event. This ensures you don’t waste Energy Coins that might still be needed for Workshop upgrades, Altar pulls, or Chapter progression. Spend your Energy Coins efficiently — use them mainly for Altar summons that target Lumira Soul Stones, and upgrade your Workshop relics to reduce overall costs and maximize long-term value.

The Event Workshop: Lumira Upgrade Priorities

The Event Workshop plays a key role in powering up Lumira. Start by upgrading the Tome of Greed, which lowers costs and enhances rewards when spending Energy Crystals or improving relics dramatically increasing your efficiency. Next, focus on the Grail of Generosity to boost Lumira Soul Stone gains over time. The Amulet of Battle comes last; while it improves battle performance, it is less essential than maintaining a consistent flow of Soul Stones and event currency during the event.

Strategy Tip: Maximize the Tome of Greed First

Always upgrade the Tome of Greed first. Even at low levels it reduces Energy Crystals costs, and at higher levels the bonus becomes incredible. Lower Altar pull costs and increased energy efficiency allow you to farm Lumira Soul Stones to absolute star much faster, speeding up your overall event progression.

The Altar of Fate and Soul Stone Farming

The Altar of Fate is where most Valor Coins should be spent. Each 10x pull offers chances to obtain Lumira's Soul Stones and other high-value rewards. Avoid single pulls unless you need a specific item. Batch openings are more efficient and statistically yield more Soul Stones.

Altar of Fate and Titan Soul Stone Upgrades

When opening the Altar of Fate in 10x batches with Tome of Greed and Grail of Generosity at level 6, you can expect an average of 105 to 150 Lumira Soul Stones per 10x summon. To reach the total requirement of 3,130 Soul Stones needed to summon Lumira and evolve her to absolute star, you will need approximately 21 to 30 10x summons, depending on your luck and Workshop upgrades.

Each 10x summon at Grail of Generosity level 6 costs 22,000 Valor Coins, which means you will need between 462,000 and 660,000 Valor Coins in total to complete the evolution of Lumira to 6 stars. This makes it extremely important to save Valor Coins in advance for efficient progress.

Free-to-Play Path to Six Stars Example

F2P players should complete all Stage 1 and 2 missions first, repeat Lumira missions until fully completed, max the Tome of Greed, and use Altar of Fate 10x pulls. Prioritize Lumira Soul Stones above all other items and save extra energy for daily mission resets. With consistent dedication, six stars is absolutely achievable without spending.

Minimum Artifact and Skin Requirements

To efficiently complete event missions and farming objectives, ensure your Lumira meets these minimum requirements:

  • Artifacts: Minimum Level 110 for each of the 3 main artifacts (Crown, Weapon, and Seal)
  • Skin: Minimum Level 50
  • Titan Level: Level 100

These upgrades are sufficient to complete all event missions and unlock the ability to use Sapphire Medallions in the event shop to purchase additional Energy Crystals, accelerating your progress toward six stars.

Principle of Light - Lumira Event Chapters' Rewards

If you manage to spend Boosters to advance through the event chapters, you can collect all rewards but F2P players should focus only on Chapter I and Chapter II, since these do not require any Boosters to complete. This strategy allows you to secure the most valuable prize of the entire event: Lumira at 6★.

Titan Soul Stones are far rarer and harder to obtain than the Campaign resources you would get from later chapters using Boosters. Even with perfect play, F2P players cannot realistically reach the final grand prize the Absolute Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere. Therefore, prioritize Chapters I and II to maximize value without wasting resources, and save your Boosters for future events or strategic Campaign progression.

What to Buy in the Event Shop

In the event shop, focus first on purchasing items that help you complete event missions efficiently. After that, prioritize the new and exclusive artifacts — the Tome of Belav (weapon artifact) and the Light Spirit Totem. These unique items are likely obtainable only during this event or through cash offers, making them highly valuable for players aiming to strengthen Lumira and their Titan teams.

Since Lumira Soul Stones are earned through other missions, use your shop currency wisely on long-term upgrades. Prioritize pet energy and skin stones, as they provide lasting progression benefits. Other hero Soul Stones are optional, and totems or upgrade materials should be chosen based on your current needs. Avoid consumables like energy potions and save your resources for the most impactful items.

Mistakes to Avoid

Don’t waste currency on low-value consumables. Avoid prioritizing flashy items over Lumira Soul Stones if your goal is six stars. Skipping Workshop upgrades is also a mistake — upgrading tomes early maximizes long-term gains.

The Lumira Event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era rewards careful planning. Prioritize missions, max the Tome of Greed, and focus resources on Soul Stones. Use the Altar of Fate efficiently with 10x pulls. The event skin is optional; six stars on Lumira is the main goal. With patience, you will unlock her and strengthen your roster significantly.

End of Event — Currency Conversion Rates

When the Principle of Light event ends, any leftover currency is automatically converted into Adventure Chests. Here are the conversion rates so you can plan your spending before the event closes:

  • 1 Sapphire Medallion → 15 Adventure Chests
  • 2 Energy Crystals → 1 Adventure Chest
  • 750 Valor Coins → 1 Adventure Chest
  • 10 Buffs → 1 Adventure Chest

To avoid losing value, spend your Valor Coins and Energy Crystals before the event ends. Sapphire Medallions offer the best conversion rate, so holding onto extra medallions is not a bad option if you cannot use them effectively during the event.

Conclusion

The Lumira and Apollo event offers one of the best opportunities in Hero Wars: Dominion Era to unlock and evolve a powerful Light-element summoner Titan. Success depends heavily on preparation and resource management. By prioritizing Valor Coins, upgrading Tome of Greed before Grail of Generosity, and focusing on the early chapters if you are F2P, you can reliably secure Lumira at 6★ the most valuable reward for most players. Remember that stall coins regenerate when you reset a chapter, and the event island contains medallions and coins that can help you progress further.

Boosters can help P2P or light spenders push deeper into chapters for extra catalysts and medallions, but those rewards are far less impactful than obtaining Lumira herself. Stay patient, plan your currency savings ahead of time, and you will maximize your gains throughout the event while avoiding unnecessary waste of resources.
