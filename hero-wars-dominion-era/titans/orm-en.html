Orm is not a support Titan who waits quietly behind a tank. He chooses the ally standing at the front, binds that ally to the enemy in the center, and turns incoming pressure into a delayed punishment. The more aggressively your opponent attacks the protected target, the more dangerous the end of Ruinous Bond can become.

That makes Orm fascinating but demanding. His value depends on timing, formation, and keeping the linked ally alive long enough for the stored damage to return. In this guide, I will explain his formula in plain language, show where I would invest first, and build teams that give his ultimate enough time to decide the battle.

Who Is Orm?

Orm is a Distortion Support Titan introduced during Clash of Worlds in October 2026. He fights from battlefield position 12 and specializes in protecting the front ally while redirecting accumulated damage toward the enemy in the center. His basic attack deals 1,261,688 Physical Damage every three seconds, but Ruinous Bond—not his normal attack—is the reason to build him.

  • Role: Support
  • Element: Distortion
  • Battlefield position: #12
  • Basic attack: 1,261,688 Physical Damage every 3 seconds
  • Maximum Health: 8,662,432
  • Maximum Power: 234,203
  • Best synergy: Durable front Titans, Alecto, Valdur, and three-Titan Distortion cores

Orm is strongest when the opponent concentrates damage on your front line. Ruinous Bond lets the linked ally take only 20% of incoming damage for six seconds, then sends the remaining accumulated damage to the central enemy. He rewards a sturdy formation and careful manual timing more than a team built only for immediate burst.

Orm Overall Rating in the Meta

  • Control: ★★★☆☆ — Orm does not stun or displace enemies, but delayed reflected damage can alter how safely the central enemy attacks.
  • Damage: ★★★★☆ — Ruinous Bond has a listed 10,000% Physical Attack coefficient, although its real payoff depends on damage accumulated during the link.
  • Synergy: ★★★★★ — Front-line protection, a central target, and Distortion Totem access make his value rise sharply in a team built around his timing.
  • Independence: ★★☆☆☆ — Orm needs a front ally to protect, incoming enemy pressure to store, and enough survival time for the link to expire.

Alexandre's Tips

I see Orm as a tactical support, not a plug-and-play damage dealer. If your front Titan receives little damage, his ultimate loses much of its threat. In the correct formation, however, the enemy can end up feeding the attack that defeats its own center.

Orm Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Ruinous Bond protects your front line, stores redirected damage, and returns that pressure to the enemy center after six decisive seconds.

Skill 1: Ruinous Bond

In-game description: Links an ally at the front to the central enemy for 6 seconds. The linked ally takes only 20% of incoming damage. The remaining damage is accumulated and dealt to the linked enemy once the skill expires.

Skill explanation: Imagine that Orm places a protective cable between your front ally and the enemy in the center. During the next six seconds, the ally receives only one fifth of the damage that would normally land. The other four fifths are stored. When the timer ends, the stored pressure is released against the linked enemy. Use it when the opponent is about to attack your front line heavily, not during a quiet part of the fight.

Protection: (linked ally receives 20% of incoming damage; the remaining 80% is accumulated).

Listed damage formula: (10,000% × Orm's Physical Attack = 126,168,800 Physical Damage).

The displayed coefficient represents Ruinous Bond's listed damage scale. The actual result still depends on accumulated damage, defenses, elemental interactions, levels, and other combat modifiers.

PvP Evolution Priority: Very High — Correct timing can preserve the front line through an enemy burst and punish the central target afterward.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Low — Orm helps only in mixed rooms; prioritize Earth, Fire, Water, and healing first.

Main Totem: Distortion Spirit Totem

Planar Storm: Available with at least three Distortion Titans. Deadly shards cover the battlefield for 15 seconds. Once per second, the storm pushes a random enemy in a random direction. Movement inside the zone deals 1,067,141 damage based on distance traveled.

Orm helps complete the three-Distortion requirement while protecting the front ally during the storm. Repeated pushes disturb formations while Ruinous Bond is active.

Best Elemental Totem Fusion for Orm

Fusion adds a separate skill to the main Totem. Orm benefits most from Physical Attack, Distortion movement pressure, and extra sustain.

How Does Totem Fusion Work?

Choose Elemental or Primal, select a desired skill, and invest Catalysts. More Catalysts improve the chance of receiving the chosen skill at a higher rank. If two Totems have the same skill, only the higher-level version activates. Catalysts are consumed, but you may reject the result and restore the previous skill state.

1st - Main Sequence

Main Sequence permanently increases all allied Titans' Attack. At Rank I, it gives +146,250 Attack every 5 seconds.

This is my first choice because Ruinous Bond lists a 10,000% Physical Attack coefficient. The permanent team bonus remains useful even when the enemy feeds little damage into the bond.

2nd - Star Dust

Star Dust covers the field for 15 seconds, pushes a random Titan every second, and deals 828,750 movement damage according to distance traveled.

It fits Distortion naturally. Together with Planar Storm, forced movement creates pressure while Orm's linked ally receives only 20% of incoming damage.

3rd - The Depths Whisper

The ten-second whirlpool damages enemies and distributes healing among allies. Rank I lists 50,312 damage and total healing per activation.

Ruinous Bond protects but does not heal. The whirlpool can stabilize damaged Titans after the protection window ends.

Alexandre's Elemental ranking: Main Sequence → Star Dust → The Depths Whisper.

Best Primal Totem Fusion for Orm

Orm wants Energy, survival, and Attack. These Primal skills support those needs without depending on a shield his own kit does not provide.

1st - Primal Zeal

After a basic attack, the Titan can restore Energy. Rank I lists 40% maximum Energy with a 20% chance.

Orm attacks every three seconds, giving him frequent trigger opportunities. Earlier Energy means an earlier bond or a second activation.

2nd - Pulse of the Ancients

When an allied Titan uses an ability, that Titan is healed. Rank I lists 112,000 healing with a 21-second maximum activation frequency.

Pulse gives Orm recovery after Ruinous Bond and is especially helpful when the formation lacks healing.

3rd - Call of Elements

Attack rises according to missing Health. Rank I lists +2,100 Attack for every 21% maximum Health missing.

Orm often loses Health while waiting for the link to finish. This converts danger into extra Physical Attack, but ranks third because it matters only after he is wounded.

Alexandre's Primal ranking: Primal Zeal → Pulse of the Ancients → Call of Elements.

How to Counter Titan Orm - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Orm is most dangerous when you pour sustained damage into the ally protected by Ruinous Bond. Fire, Water, and Earth have the natural advantage over Distortion. The best counters weaken Orm's Attack, pressure him before he gains Energy, or survive the delayed return damage.

Angus - Earth

Angus: Earth has the natural advantage and Angus has the durability to survive delayed damage. His area pressure attacks Orm instead of focusing only on the protected ally.

Araji - Fire

Araji: Fire naturally counters Distortion. His goal is to maintain enough pressure to defeat Orm before repeated bonds stabilize the front line.

Mairi - Water

Mairi: Water has the elemental advantage, while Abyss Curse reduces all enemies' Attack by 40% for eight seconds. That directly weakens Orm's Physical Attack scaling.

Orm Guide Conclusion

Orm asks one decisive question: how much damage will the enemy commit to your front line during the next six seconds? Ruinous Bond reduces that ally's incoming damage to 20%, stores the remainder, and returns the accumulated pressure to the enemy center.

My recommended route is the Primordial Skin before Default for PvP, then Distorted Balance Seal, Crown of Distortion, and Zorval's Rite. Main Sequence is my preferred Elemental Fusion, while Primal Zeal leads the Primal ranking by helping Orm reach Ruinous Bond sooner.

Do not treat Orm as a universal Dungeon investment or automatic win. Build him when you want a tactical Distortion support and already have the front-line durability and structure needed to exploit his link.
