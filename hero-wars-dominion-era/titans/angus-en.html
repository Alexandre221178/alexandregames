Angus Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Understanding Angus's abilities is crucial to maximizing his offensive and defensive potential. Let's break down each skill and see how they dominate the battlefield.

Skill 1: Anger of the Earth

What it does: Angus plants himself into the ground and channels pure earth energy, unleashing it against ALL enemy Titans simultaneously. While channeling, he cannot move or perform any other action — he dedicates his entire being to this earth-shattering assault.

How it works: When activated, Angus deals 409,305 damage per second for 8 seconds to every enemy Titan at once. That's a potential total of 3,274,440 damage to EACH enemy simultaneously. The trade-off is that Angus himself is fully immobilized during the entire 8 seconds — he cannot move, dodge, or use basic attacks while channeling.

Why it matters in PvP and Dungeons: In competitive battles, hitting the entire enemy team for over 3.2 million total damage per target is absolutely devastating! This skill shines even brighter in dungeons — save Angus's charged ultimate for the next room where Water Titans wait, and watch the entire enemy Water team get decimated in seconds. Priority: Very High

Alexandre's Pro Tip: Angus deals massive AoE damage with his ultimate — he's not just a tank, he's also a powerful damage dealer! Keep in mind that Angus himself is immobilized during the 8-second channel, so position him well before activating. In dungeons, hold his ultimate charge until you enter an elemental room with Water Titans. The entire room can be wiped out in seconds. Even when Eden is not yet charged, Angus remains your primary Earth tank for dungeon runs.

Skill 2: Main Totem: Earth Spirit Totem

Why Earth Spirit Totem is essential for Angus: As an Earth Titan, Angus automatically synergizes with the Earth Spirit Totem when paired with at least 2 other Earth Titans. This totem doesn't just boost stats — it creates an active defensive mechanism that both protects Angus's entire team AND punishes attackers for daring to strike.

Totem Benefits:

  • Song of the Ancient Mountains: Activates when you have 3+ Earth Titans. Creates a crystal barrier that absorbs incoming damage and reflects double the amount back at attackers.
  • Damage Absorption: The barrier absorbs up to 7,659,015 damage before breaking.
  • Reflect Damage: Every hit absorbed is reflected back at the attacker for double the original damage.
  • Formula: (Barrier Absorption: 7,659,015 | Reflect: 2× absorbed damage)
  • Health Boost: Increases Health for all Earth Titans by +3,995,583, making Angus and his Earth allies dramatically tougher.

Priority: Very High - Essential for maximizing Angus's performance. The combination of massive health boost, enormous damage absorption, and devastating reflect creates a team composition that punishes any attacker who targets your Earth Titans.

Since Fire teams are the single biggest threat to Angus, this targeted protection directly addresses his greatest weakness. For a front-line tank like Angus who absorbs every hit in battle, this universal survivability boost is absolutely non-negotiable. Best Elemental Totem Fusion for Angus

Want to supercharge Angus's tanking and damage capabilities? Totem Fusion is your secret weapon! Think of it as adding special power-ups to your already formidable Angus. These fusions work alongside his Earth Spirit Totem to create devastating combos that can reshape entire PvP battles.

How Does Totem Fusion Work for Angus?

Imagine upgrading your armor and weapons simultaneously. That's exactly what Totem Fusion does for Angus! In the Fusion window, you choose between Elemental or Primordial Skills, select your desired ability, and invest Catalysts. The more Catalysts you invest, the better your chances for powerful high-level skills.

Important to know: If you already have a skill and fuse it again, your chances improve for a better version! When two totems have the same skill, only the higher-level one activates. Elemental Skills trigger globally with no cooldown, while Primordial Skills have individual cooldowns per Titan based on specific conditions.

Pro tip: If the fusion result doesn't suit Angus's playstyle, you can reject it and restore your totem to its previous state — no permanent changes!

🥇 1st Place: Murmur of the Crags

Murmur of the Crags (Rank I)

What it does: Creates a rocky barrier that protects ALL allies from attacks. The barrier absorbs 450,625 damage and reflects double the damage back at attackers!

Why it's PERFECT for Angus: Angus already benefits from the Earth Spirit Totem's Song of the Ancient Mountains which creates a massive defensive barrier. Murmur of the Crags doubles down on this defensive-reflect strategy! Together, they create a layered protection system: enemies burst through the Earth Spirit Totem barrier (getting reflected damage), then hit the Murmur of the Crags barrier (getting reflected again). Attackers effectively destroy themselves against Angus's team.

PvP Impact: This fusion is particularly devastating against burst damage opponents. When enemies unleash their ultimate abilities against your team, they trigger both reflect mechanics simultaneously, effectively dealing massive self-damage. Combined with Angus's incredible health pool, your team becomes an impenetrable fortress that punishes every attack. Priority: Very High

🥈 2nd Place: Ice Age

Ice Age (Rank I)

What it does: When your opponent's base totem activates, ALL their Titans freeze for 1 second and receive a fragility debuff that increases damage taken by 15.8% for 5 seconds.

Why it strengthens Angus: Angus's Anger of the Earth already deals massive AoE damage to all enemies simultaneously. Ice Age creates the perfect combo — when the enemy totem fires, all enemies freeze (interrupting their abilities) and become fragile. Then Angus's channeled ultimate hits already-frozen and fragile enemies for 15.8% more damage on top of his already massive output. The freeze also prevents enemies from moving away during the 8-second channel, maximizing total damage.

PvP Impact: This fusion dramatically amplifies Angus's already powerful ultimate. Landing Anger of the Earth on frozen, fragile enemies increases total damage significantly. The crowd control disruption also delays enemy responses, giving your team time to capitalize on the chaos. Priority: High

🥉 3rd Place: Last Flash

Last Flash (Rank I)

What it does: When a Titan's health drops to a critical threshold, they gain temporary invulnerability, preventing instant elimination during burst damage scenarios.

Why it protects Angus: While Angus is incredibly durable, he sits at the front line and will inevitably face focused fire from strong Fire Titan teams. Last Flash acts as a last resort survival mechanism — when enemies burst through his enormous health pool and bring him to critical health, this fusion grants precious seconds of invulnerability for your team to respond or for the battle to turn around.

PvP Impact: This fusion is most valuable in difficult guild wars matchups where the enemy specifically builds to counter Earth Titans with Fire element teams. The invulnerability window can be the difference between Angus surviving to use his ultimate or falling before activating it. Priority: Medium-High

Best Primal Totem Fusion for Angus

Now let's explore Primordial Fusions — triggered by specific conditions with individual cooldowns per Titan. For Angus the frontline tank, we want fusions that enhance his survivability and power throughout prolonged battles!

🥇 1st Place: Pulse of the Ancients

Pulse of the Ancients (Rank I)

What it does: Every time an allied Titan uses an ability, it heals for 112,000 health. Can activate no more than once per 21 seconds.

Why it's AMAZING for Angus: As a front-line tank constantly under fire, Angus needs every bit of healing he can get. Pulse of the Ancients rewards active gameplay by healing Angus every time any ally uses an ability. Since a team of 5 Titans uses abilities constantly throughout battle, this fusion triggers consistently at its maximum rate. It's like having a dedicated healer assigned specifically to keep Angus standing!

PvP Impact: This fusion dramatically extends Angus's survivability on the front line. With his already massive health pool (18.8M+) receiving consistent 112,000 HP healing every 21 seconds, Angus becomes extraordinarily difficult to eliminate through sustained damage. In extended battles, this can effectively heal Angus for over 500,000 additional HP — enough to survive an extra ultimate ability from opponents. Priority: Very High

🥈 2nd Place: Call of Elements

Call of Elements (Rank I)

What it does: Increases the Titan's attack power based on their missing health — the lower the health, the greater the attack bonus. This creates a powerful comeback mechanic that rewards Angus for surviving under pressure.

Why it's powerful for Angus: This fusion fits Angus's role perfectly! As a front-line tank, Angus constantly takes damage and his health naturally decreases during battle. Rather than this being purely a weakness, Call of Elements transforms Angus's survival into a power boost. When he's taken significant damage and his health drops, his attack increases dramatically, making Anger of the Earth even more devastating precisely when the battle is at its most critical.

PvP Impact: This fusion creates a dangerous pressure situation for opponents: either they focus fire on Angus (which makes him increasingly deadly as his health drops), or they ignore him and face his Anger of the Earth at full strength. It's a no-win scenario for the enemy. Combined with Pulse of Ancients healing him back up, the attack buff cycles repeatedly throughout battle. Priority: High

🥉 3rd Place: Triple Cycle

Triple Cycle (Rank I)

What it does: Creates a reactive cycle: If Angus gets a shield → he heals (50,400 HP/sec). If he gets healing → his attack increases (+5,600). If he gets a buff → he gains a shield (24,500 durability). Each effect lasts 2 seconds.

Why it's versatile for Angus: Since Angus receives the Earth Spirit Totem's health boost (a buff), Triple Cycle transforms that into a shield. That shield then triggers healing. That healing then boosts his attack. The whole cycle feeds into itself, continuously generating survival mechanics and damage boosts simultaneously. It's a self-reinforcing loop of power!

PvP Impact: Triple Cycle is especially valuable in fast-paced PvP where battles end quickly. The constant cycling between shields, healing, and attack buffs keeps Angus active and threatening from the very first second of battle. While it has lower raw numbers than Pulse of Ancients, the immediate triggering makes it excellent for short, intense confrontations. Priority: Medium-High

Angus Counters - Titans That Dominate Him

Every Titan has weaknesses, and understanding Angus's counters is crucial for both playing with him and against him. As an Earth Titan, Angus faces a fundamental disadvantage against Fire Titans due to the elemental triangle system in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

Why Fire Titans Counter Angus:

The elemental system works like rock-paper-scissors: Earth beats Water, Water beats Fire, Fire beats Earth. This means Fire Titans deal significantly increased damage to Angus while taking reduced damage from his attacks. Even with his massive health pool and defensive capabilities, Angus struggles against Fire-element opponents whose attacks carry an elemental multiplier against him. Note: Light and Darkness oppose each other and are neutral against Earth.

🔥 Top Fire Titan Counters:

Araji (Fire Titan)

Why Araji counters Angus: Araji's Fire element means every one of his attacks carries elemental advantage against Angus's Earth element. His powerful burst damage bursts through Angus's Earth shields — the Murmur of the Crags and Earth Spirit Totem barriers absorb some damage, but Araji's explosive firepower can shatter through both defensive layers rapidly. The elemental multiplier means Araji deals significantly more damage to Angus than his raw stats suggest, making confrontations one-sided in Fire's favor.

Counter Strategy: Use the Earth Spirit Totem's reflect mechanic to punish Araji's burst. When Araji unloads his full burst into the barrier and gets reflected double damage, it creates a window for Angus to activate Anger of the Earth and channel massive AoE damage against the weakened Fire team. Earth Crown artifact is essential in this matchup to reduce incoming Fire damage.

Asherona (Fire Titan)

Why Asherona counters Angus: Asherona's Fire elemental attacks carry amplified damage against Earth Titans. Combined with Pyro's supporting fire abilities, the duo creates sustained Fire pressure that chips through Angus's enormous health pool with elemental efficiency. Their coordinated attacks mean Angus faces amplified damage from multiple sources simultaneously, making it extremely difficult to outlast even with the Earth Spirit Totem's barrier and Pulse of the Ancients healing.

Counter Strategy: Prioritize Anger of the Earth early to channel massive AoE damage against the entire Fire team including Asherona and Pyro simultaneously. The sustained damage output buys crucial time for your team to respond before the elemental disadvantage becomes overwhelming. Position Angus to benefit from Earth Spirit Totem reflect, making Asherona's bursts partially self-damaging.

Ignis (Fire Titan)

Why Ignis counters Angus: Ignis's Fire element gives her consistent elemental advantage over Angus throughout the entire battle. Her attacks continuously deal amplified damage against Earth, slowly but relentlessly eroding Angus's health even through his defensive layers. Unlike burst damage counters, Ignis's sustained damage ensures Angus faces a constant uphill battle where the elemental disadvantage accumulates over time.

Counter Strategy: Focus on maximizing Angus's survivability in Ignis matchups — Champion Skin and Earth Crown artifact are critical investments. Time Anger of the Earth to hit Ignis alongside her allies when possible. Consider using Verdoc's totem-enabling ability to add a Water Titan to your composition, since Water counters Fire, providing a natural answer to Ignis's elemental advantage.

Moloch (Fire Titan)

Why Moloch counters Angus: Moloch is a particularly dangerous Fire counter because his stun abilities compound with the elemental advantage. When Moloch stuns Angus, he not only interrupts the timing of Anger of the Earth but also allows his entire Fire team to unload amplified elemental damage on the helpless, stunned Angus. The combination of crowd control plus Fire elemental multiplier creates devastating burst windows that can quickly eliminate even Angus's massive health pool.

Counter Strategy: Be prepared for Moloch's stun timing when playing Angus. Try to activate Anger of the Earth before Moloch's stun lands — getting the channel started first deals massive AoE damage to the Fire team and neutralizes the stun threat. Note that Angus is immobilized during the channel, so use it when he's already in a good position. The Earth Spirit Totem's reflect damage is especially punishing for Moloch's burst attacks if they land on the barrier during the channel.

Alexandre's Counter Tips:

  • Elemental Awareness: Always check enemy team composition before battle. If you see multiple Fire Titans (Araji, Asherona, Ignis, Moloch, Vulcan), prioritize leveling the Earth Crown artifact and Champion Skin to reduce incoming Fire damage.
  • Dungeon Strategy: In dungeons, save Angus's charged ultimate for rooms containing Water Titan enemies — Earth beats Water, so Anger of the Earth devastates Water rooms. In Fire Titan rooms, use the Earth Spirit Totem reflect to punish Fire bursts.
  • Team Synergy: Pair Angus with Verdoc who enables Earth Spirit Totem with only 2 Earth allies. This opens team slots for a Water Titan (which counters Fire!) to handle the Fire threat while your Earth core activates the totem.
  • Focus on Value: Even against Fire counters, Angus's role is to tank damage and buy time. The Earth Spirit Totem's double-reflect punishes Fire bursts. Keep Angus alive long enough to use his ultimate at least once per battle for maximum impact.
