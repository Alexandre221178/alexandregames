Nova Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Master Nova's devastating abilities that make her an unstoppable force in PvP battles. Each skill combines raw power with strategic effects for ultimate dominance.

North Wind (Primary Skill)

Skill Description: Nova launches a devastating projectile at the rearmost enemy lines, striking with precision and force. The attack causes (50% ATK) physical damage and stuns the affected targets for 4 seconds. This stunning effect prevents enemies from acting, making it perfect for shutting down enemy backline threats in PvP battles.

Damage Calculation: Damage = 50% × Your Current Attack Stat

Example with Nova's Stats: With Nova's base Attack of 1,286,286, North Wind deals: 50% × 1,286,286 = 643,143 damage per strike. This base damage increases proportionally with every attack stat boost from equipment, totems, and skin evolutions.

Why It Matters in PvP: The 4-second stun is absolutely critical in player-versus-player combat. It interrupts enemy skills, prevents counterattacks, and gives your team precious time to eliminate dangerous threats. The higher your attack stat, the more devastating the damage becomes, making gear optimization essential for competitive success.

Main Totem: Water Spirit Totem

Requirements: Available only when your team has at least 3 Water Titans on the battlefield. Nova pairs exceptionally well with other Water Titans, making this totem her natural choice when conditions are met.

Totem Ability - Blessing of the Deep: This totem grants Nova a powerful new battle skill that heals allied Water Titans while simultaneously damaging nearby enemies. The healing effect splits intelligently between any Titans in need of restoration, ensuring no ally is left behind. The skill operates continuously for 10 seconds, providing consistent support throughout extended battles.

Healing & Damage per Activation: + 487,634 Health Restoration to affected allies and + 321,456 Damage to nearby enemies.

Stat Bonuses: The Water Spirit Totem enhances all Water Titans in your team: Attack + 12,847 for increased offensive capability.

Why This Totem Matters for Nova in PvP: While Nova doesn't benefit from choosing this totem herself, it becomes essential when you have 3+ Water Titans because it synergizes perfectly with her skills. Blessing of the Deep turns your Water Titan team into a self-sustaining force that heals while dealing damage a devastating combination in competitive battles. The attack boost ensures Nova's already powerful North Wind skill becomes even more lethal. In PvP, this synergy transforms Nova from a standalone threat into a team multiplier that can systematically dominate opponents.

Understanding How Totem Fusion Works

Totem Fusion allows you to unlock special awakening skills for your totems, boosting their power in battle. In the Fusion window, you choose between Elemental or Primordial skills, select your desired ability, and invest Catalysts. The more Catalysts you use, the higher your chances of success and getting better skill levels. If two totems have the same skill, only the higher-level one activates in battle. Elemental Skills trigger globally with no cooldown, while Primordial Skills activate based on specific conditions. If a fusion doesn't suit your needs, you can reject it and restore your totem to its previous state.

🥇 RANK 1: Ice Age (Elemental Fusion)

1st Choice: Ice Age Elemental Fusion

Why Ice Age is Essential for Nova: When your opponent's main totem skill activates, Ice Age instantly freezes all enemy Titans for 1 second, completely disabling them. They also gain a fragility state that increases all damage they receive by 15.8% for 5 seconds.

Synergy with North Wind: This is where the magic happens! After Ice Age stuns enemies for 1 second, Nova can immediately unleash North Wind for a guaranteed additional 4-second stun. Your opponent's team will be locked down for 5 seconds total completely helpless. The increased fragility damage means Nova's physical attack (50% ATK) deals significantly more damage during this window.

PvP Impact: In competitive battles, crowd control is king. Ice Age + North Wind creates an unstoppable chain that eliminates threats before they can respond. This combination is particularly devastating against enemy teams trying to coordinate attacks.

🥈 RANK 2: Wrath of the Depths (Elemental Fusion)

2nd Choice: Wrath of the Depths Elemental Fusion

How Wrath of the Depths Works: At the moment an allied Titan dies, it transforms into stone, rises above the battlefield, and crashes directly into the center of your opponent's team. This crash deals massive area damage and stuns opponents for 1 second. This effect activates once per battle.

Strategic Synergy with Nova: While Wrath of the Depths activates when a Titan dies, Nova benefits indirectly by cleaning up weakened enemies. The area stun creates perfect positioning for Nova to follow up with North Wind against multiple enemies simultaneously. The initial area damage softens enemy defenses, making your subsequent attacks far more impactful.

PvP Advantage: This fusion turns death into opportunity. Even when you lose a Titan, you gain a final powerful strike that repositions enemies and creates a stun window. Nova can capitalize on the chaos to eliminate multiple threats quickly. It's exceptional for comebacks and turning desperate situations into victories.

🥉 RANK 3: Last Flash (Elemental Fusion)

3rd Choice: Last Flash Elemental Fusion

What Last Flash Does: When Nova should die, she instead enters a burning rage state instead of falling. Her speed increases by 15%, her attack power becomes significantly stronger by 16,100 damage, and she becomes invulnerable to all effects with health locked at 1. She remains in this state for 5 seconds before eventually dying. This effect activates once per battle.

Why It Matters for Nova: Last Flash gives Nova a second life. When enemies think they've eliminated her, she suddenly transforms into an unstoppable attacker. With increased speed and attack power, she can unleash multiple North Wind attacks while invulnerable. This forces enemies to completely reassess their strategy mid-battle.

PvP Significance: This fusion is a psychological game-changer. The mere possibility of Nova's return creates hesitation in your opponent. The extra damage and speed boost mean she can eliminate key targets even while enraged. It's perfect for clutch victories when the battle seems lost.

Best Primal Totem Fusion for Nova - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Elevate Nova's survivability and combat efficiency with the most powerful Primal totem fusions. Master the top 3 Primal skills that amplify her abilities and guarantee dominance in PvP!

🥇 RANK 1: Triple Cycle (Primal Fusion)

1st Choice: Triple Cycle Primal Fusion

How Triple Cycle Works: This exceptional Primal skill creates a three-stage cycle. When Nova receives a shield, she gets healed for 50,400 health per second. When she receives healing, her attack increases by 5,600 damage for 2 seconds. When she gets a buff effect, a protective shield worth 24,500 durability is applied. Only one effect triggers per activation, but the chain continues throughout the battle.

Synergy with Nova's North Wind: Triple Cycle transforms Nova into a self-sustaining machine. After using North Wind to stun enemies, Nova receives automatic buffs that enhance her next ability. The healing component keeps her at full health, the attack buff makes subsequent North Wind attacks even more devastating, and the shield ensures she survives focused enemy attacks. This creates an unstoppable cycle where each skill feeds into the next.

PvP Dominance: In competitive battles, Triple Cycle gives Nova unmatched longevity. She can sustain intense focus fire while continuously growing stronger. Enemies struggle to burst her down because she heals, shields, and buffs herself automatically. This makes her the ultimate sustained damage dealer that's nearly impossible to eliminate.

🥈 RANK 2: Pulse of the Ancients (Primal Fusion)

2nd Choice: Pulse of the Ancients Primal Fusion

What Pulse of the Ancients Does: Every time Nova uses an ability (like North Wind), she is healed for 112,000 health. This healing activates no more than once every 21 seconds, preventing overpowering regeneration while still providing consistent sustain.

Perfect Match with North Wind: Since North Wind is Nova's primary ability in battles, she gets rewarded with substantial healing every time she uses it. This means Nova becomes more dangerous as the battle progresses she damages enemies while simultaneously healing herself. The 21-second cooldown ensures balanced gameplay while still providing regular healing bursts that keep her combative.

Why It's Crucial for PvP: Pulse of the Ancients transforms Nova into a life-stealing attacker. Each North Wind strike damages enemies AND heals Nova, creating a one-sided exchange in her favor. Over a long battle, this accumulates to massive sustainability advantages. Opponents find themselves slowly ground down while Nova remains fresh.

🥉 RANK 3: Call of Elements (Primal Fusion)

3rd Choice: Call of Elements Primal Fusion

How Call of Elements Works: Nova's attack scales proportionally with her missing health. For every 21% health she's missing from her maximum, she gains an additional 2,100 attack bonus. This means the more damaged Nova becomes, the more dangerous her attacks grow.

Strategic Advantage with North Wind: Call of Elements rewards aggressive gameplay. Even when Nova takes damage, her North Wind becomes progressively stronger. At 50% health, she gains significant attack bonuses that make her North Wind stun even more devastating. This inverts the typical battle dynamic where damaged units are weaker Nova becomes a comeback threat that grows fiercer as the battle rages.

PvP Psychology: Call of Elements creates mind games in competitive battles. Opponents hesitate to commit to focused attacks because doing so only makes Nova more powerful. This psychological advantage, combined with increasing attack damage, allows Nova to secure victories from seemingly losing positions. It's the ultimate "never give up" ability.

Skins & Equipment Priority for Nova

👗 Default Skin (PRIORITY 1 - EVOLVE FIRST)

Nova's Nova Default Skin

Stat Gain: +76,740 Physical Attack

Resource Cost: FREE to Obtain + Titan Skin Stones to Evolve

Recommended Timeline: EARLY TO MID GAME

Why Evolve Default Skin First: While the Default Skin comes with Nova at no cost, you'll still need to invest Titan Skin Stones to evolve it from Level 0 to 60. However, this is your ONLY option early game you have no alternative. Since you must evolve some skin anyway, prioritize the Default Skin because you're not forced to spend additional currency (like cash) to obtain it first. Once at max level, the +76,740 attack bonus amplifies your North Wind damage formula (50% ATK), effectively increasing your base damage output by nearly 38,370 per strike. This makes the Default Skin evolution essential you'll need those Titan Skin Stones regardless, so use them here.

🌟 Champion Skin (PRIORITY 2 - LATE GAME / OPTIONAL)

Nova's Champion Skin

Stat Gain: +76,740 Physical Attack (Same as Default Skin)

Resource Cost: 10,000 Titan Skin Stones OR Cash

Recommended Timeline: LATE GAME OR CONSIDER SUPER TITANS

Strategic Recommendation: While the Champion Skin provides identical stat gains as the Default Skin, it requires 10,000 Titan Skin Stones to acquire a significant resource investment. Since both skins grant the same +76,740 attack bonus, prioritize this evolution only after maximizing your Default Skin and establishing stronger Titans in your roster. The resources spent on the Champion Skin are better allocated to evolving Super Titans or investing in other priority heroes. Pursue this skin in late game as a prestige upgrade rather than a necessity.

Nova Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Maximize Nova's attack potential by evolving artifacts strategically. Discover which artifacts provide the greatest advantages for her devastating North Wind ability in PvP and Dungeon content.

Siungur's Maelstrom (Weapon Artifact)

Stat Gain: +479,475 Elemental Damage

Effect: Increases damage dealt by attacks and skills to Water, Fire, and Earth Titans. Becomes less effective when attacking Titans of a higher level.

PvP Evolution Priority: VERY HIGH – Nova is an Energy/Radiant marksman whose North Wind (50% ATK) deals pure attack-based damage. While this artifact adds Elemental Damage rather than Physical Attack, the additional damage output helps against enemy Titans, especially Water, Fire, and Earth types. In PvP battles against these elements, Siungur's Maelstrom amplifies your overall damage window after North Wind stuns.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH – Dungeons often feature diverse Titan types. While effective against Water/Fire/Earth enemies, Siungur's Maelstrom's reduced effectiveness against higher-level Titans makes it less reliable for endgame dungeon progression where level scaling becomes critical.

Water Crown (Armor Artifact)

Stat Gain: +97,482 Elemental Armor

Effect: When assigned to the Defense Team, Elemental Armor protects the Titan from attacks by Water, Fire, and Earth Titans. Becomes less effective when defending against Titans of a higher level.

PvP Evolution Priority: MEDIUM – As a Marksman on the Middle Line, Nova's role is offensive damage output rather than defense. While Elemental Armor provides protection against specific elements, her survival depends more on stunning threats with North Wind than on defensive stats. This is a secondary priority artifact.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: MEDIUM – Dungeons are more forgiving than PvP regarding element matchups, and Nova's primary value is sustained damage output. Defensive stats help but don't directly amplify your damage-dealing capability. Invest in this artifact only after maximizing offensive options.

Attack Seal (Stat Artifact)

Stat Gains: +2,397,375 Health & +419,542.5 Physical Attack

Effect: Adds bonus stats to a Titan, providing both survivability and damage output.

PvP Evolution Priority: HIGH – The Attack Seal is crucial for Nova. The +419,542.5 Physical Attack directly amplifies North Wind's damage formula (50% ATK = 209,771 additional damage per strike). Additionally, the 2.4M Health bonus provides essential survivability, allowing Nova to survive initial enemy engagements and unleash multiple North Wind stuns. This balanced stat distribution makes it a core evolution priority.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: HIGH – In dungeons, sustained damage output is paramount, and the Attack Seal delivers both offensive scaling and defensive survivability. The Physical Attack bonus directly increases your wave-clearing speed, while Health protects against dungeon enemies. This is an essential artifact for consistent dungeon progression.

Nova Pros and Cons - Hero Wars: Dominion Era (Web & Facebook)

✅ Pros

  • Devastating Attack Stat: With 1,286,286 base Attack, Nova's North Wind deals 643,143 damage per strike (50% ATK), making her one of the highest single-target damage dealers in the game.
  • Crowd Control Mastery: The 4-second stun on North Wind is game-changing in PvP, disabling entire enemy teams and preventing critical counterattacks. Combined with Ice Age totem fusion, enemies can be locked down for 5 seconds total.
  • Excellent Health Pool: At 7,746,415 Health, Nova has impressive survivability for a marksman, allowing her to withstand focused enemy fire while dealing consistent damage.
  • Strong Totem Synergies: Nova synergizes perfectly with Water Titans (Water Spirit Totem) and has exceptional Elemental/Primal totem fusion options like Ice Age, Triple Cycle, and Pulse of the Ancients that amplify her effectiveness.
  • Flexible Positioning: As a Middle Line Marksman, Nova maintains optimal distance while still dealing maximum damage output, allowing strategic team composition flexibility.
  • Dual Sustain Options: With Primal fusions like Triple Cycle and Pulse of the Ancients, Nova can become self-sustaining, healing herself while dealing damage—creating a one-sided advantage in extended battles.
  • Comeback Mechanics: Last Flash provides a second life window, and Call of Elements actually increases her damage when wounded, giving Nova the ability to turn losing battles into victories through sheer persistence.

❌ Cons

  • High Resource Requirements: Nova requires significant investment in Titan Skin Stones to evolve her Default Skin (Level 0-60), and the Champion Skin demands 10,000 additional stones—making her expensive to fully optimize.
  • Artifact Dependency: While Attack Seal is excellent, the Attack Seal artifact itself requires substantial resources. Without proper artifacts, her damage ceiling is significantly reduced.
  • Elemental Weakness: As an Energy/Radiant Titan, Nova doesn't have elemental advantage against specific enemy types. Siungur's Maelstrom artifact is less effective against higher-level Titans, limiting scaling potential.
  • Cooldown Reliance: Some of Nova's most powerful effects (Wrath of the Depths, Last Flash) activate only once per battle, limiting their overall impact in extended campaigns or repeated dungeons.
  • Moderate Elemental Damage: With 709,635 Elemental Damage compared to her 1,286,286 Attack, Nova's elemental scaling is secondary. This makes her less effective in content that rewards balanced elemental damage.
  • Totem Requirement Limitations: Nova's Water Spirit Totem requires 3+ Water Titans on the team to activate, reducing flexibility in team composition and forcing thematic building rather than pure power optimization.
  • Scaling Against Overleveled Enemies: In dungeons with significantly higher-level Titans, Nova's effectiveness drops because her stuns, damage amplification, and artifact bonuses all become less reliable against the level gap.
  • Mid-to-Late Game Timing: Nova requires substantial resource investment to become truly dominant, making her less effective in early game compared to other readily available Titans. She's a long-term investment rather than an immediate powerhouse.

Conclusion

Nova stands as one of the most formidable Marksman Titans in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, delivering unmatched physical attack power and crowd control capabilities. Her North Wind skill, dealing 50% of her attack value as pure damage with a devastating 4-second stun, makes her a cornerstone of high-damage team compositions. When paired with the right synergies—such as Verdoc's crowd control amplification, Tenebris' burst damage, and Sigurd's protective shielding—Nova becomes virtually unstoppable in both PvP and Dungeon content.

To maximize Nova's potential, prioritize investing in her Default Skin first, as it provides essential attack bonuses without excessive resource requirements. Focus your artifact progression on Siungur's Maelstrom for exceptional PvP performance, while maintaining balanced elemental armor through Water Crown and supplementary stat artifacts. The combination of Ice Age (RANK 1) for quick freezes and Triple Cycle (RANK 1) for sustained healing creates a resilient foundation that allows Nova to maintain offensive pressure throughout extended battles.

Building optimal teams requires understanding Nova's role as a primary damage dealer who thrives in formations that protect her positioning while amplifying her attack output. Whether you're tackling challenging Dungeon raids or competing in intense PvP matches, Nova's versatility and raw power ensure she remains relevant across all game modes. With consistent resource investment, strategic artifact evolution, and proper team composition, Nova will lead your forces to victory and establish dominance in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.
