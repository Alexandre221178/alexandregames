If your team keeps collapsing right before the fight turns in your favor, Avalon may be the missing piece. This Earth support Titan protects the entire lineup with a massive shield, buying precious seconds for your damage dealers to finish the job in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

In this Avalon guide, you will understand why a simple shield can decide PvP matches, stabilize Dungeon runs, and make Earth teams feel much harder to break. We will cover his skill, best totem fusions, skins, artifacts, counters, and the team setup that gets the most value from his protection.

Who Is Avalon?

Avalon is an Earth Support Titan who fights from the back line and specializes in one job: keeping the whole team alive at the exact moment enemies try to burst them down. His signature value comes from a full-team shield that can completely change the rhythm of a battle.

  • Role: Support
  • Element: Earth
  • Position: Back Line
  • Synergy: Angus, Eden, Sylva, Verdoc and Phyto

He is obtained through the Circle of Summoning and shines most in Earth-heavy compositions, especially when you want a safer answer against Water teams. Avalon is not the Titan you pick for flashy damage. You pick him because he prevents your team from falling apart before your win condition activates.

✅ Pros

  • Full-Team Shield: Forest Embrace places a shield on all allies, which is one of the most reliable defensive tools for absorbing burst damage in PvP.
  • Excellent Earth Team Synergy: Avalon fits naturally with Angus, Eden, Sylva, and Verdoc, helping Earth teams stay stable while their control and damage tools build pressure.
  • Strong Against Water Rooms: As an Earth Titan, he is a practical choice when attacking Water-based Dungeon rooms and mixed rooms where survivability matters.
  • Low Complexity, High Impact: Avalon is easy to understand for beginners because his value is direct and visible: when the shield appears, your team gets more time to fight.
  • Great Totem Fusion Synergy: Defensive elemental and primal totem skills combine extremely well with Forest Embrace, making his protection scale much better in late game setups.

❌ Cons

  • Low Personal Damage Pressure: Avalon does not threaten enemy teams by himself, so he depends on allies to convert his protection into kills.
  • Vulnerable to Fire Matchups: Fire Titans naturally pressure Earth teams, which makes Avalon less comfortable when the enemy has strong Fire burst.
  • One-Dimensional Role: Most of his value is concentrated in shielding, so if the shield timing is poor, his impact drops sharply.
  • Can Be Outvalued in Fast Fights: In extremely aggressive lineups, supports that provide direct damage or faster energy can sometimes create more momentum than a pure defensive support.
  • Needs the Right Team Around Him: Avalon performs best when his allies are dangerous enough to capitalize on the extra survival window he creates.

Avalon Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn exactly how Avalon protects your team, why his shield matters in PvP and Dungeon, and which supporting totem effect makes his Earth lineup much harder to break.

Skill 1: Forest Embrace

What it does: Avalon casts a shield on all allied Titans. In simple terms, this means every ally gets a protective layer before their real Health starts taking damage. When your team would normally lose a fight to enemy burst, this shield often gives them just enough extra time to survive.

How it works: Forest Embrace is not a heal. It is temporary protection that absorbs incoming damage first. That distinction is important because shields are strongest when the enemy is trying to delete one or two Titans quickly. Instead of immediately losing Health, your team burns through the shield first, which slows down the enemy's win condition.

Formula: Shield Absorption = (1,968,825).

Why it matters in PvP: PvP fights are often decided by the first heavy damage window. Forest Embrace directly answers that problem. If the enemy lineup depends on early burst, Avalon can neutralize that timing and let your own damage dealers retaliate. Priority: Very High

Why it matters in Dungeon: In Dungeon rooms, especially Water or mixed rooms, the shield reduces pressure on the rest of your lineup and makes bad damage spikes easier to manage. Avalon becomes much more valuable when consistency matters more than speed.

Alexandre's Tip:

Avalon is strongest when the rest of your team already has real damage or control. His shield does not win the fight by itself, but it gives Angus, Eden, and Sylva the time they need to do it for him.

Skill 2 - Main Totem: Earth Spirit Totem

Why it fits Avalon: If your team has at least 3 Earth Titans on the battlefield, the Earth Spirit Totem activates automatically. That is exactly the kind of setup Avalon wants, because Earth teams usually aim to outlast the enemy and punish them during longer trades.

How Song of the Ancient Mountains works: The totem creates a crystal barrier that protects all allies from attacks. Every time the barrier absorbs damage, it loses durability, but it also reflects double the absorbed damage back to the attacking enemies. This creates a powerful defensive-offensive loop: your team becomes harder to kill while the enemy damages themselves by trying to break through.

Formula: Barrier Absorption = (7,659,015). Reflected Damage = (2x absorbed damage). Earth Titan Health Bonus = (+3,995,583).

PvP and Dungeon value: This totem is excellent for Avalon because it stacks defensive layers on top of Forest Embrace. First the team gets the totem barrier, then Avalon adds another shield window. Against teams that rely on brute force, this combination can feel oppressive. Priority: Very High

Totem synergy tip:

If Avalon is in a true Earth lineup, the Earth Spirit Totem is not optional. It turns every enemy burst attempt into a risky trade and makes Avalon's protection much harder to outpace.

Best Totem Elemental Fusion for Avalon

If the Earth Spirit Totem is already the foundation of your team, Elemental Fusion is how you make that foundation much harder to crack. For Avalon, the best elemental skills are the ones that create more protection, punish enemy aggression, or buy enough time for his shield cycle to matter.

How Does Totem Fusion Work?

In the Fusion window, you choose the skill type, select the desired skill, and invest Catalysts to improve your chances of getting that result at a better level. If your totem already has the same skill, repeating that fusion increases the odds of receiving a stronger version. If two totems end up with the same skill, only the higher-level one is active in battle. Elemental skills usually trigger globally and do not depend on one Titan pressing a button, so they are especially valuable for stable defensive teams like Avalon-based lineups.

1st - Murmur of the Crags

Elemental Totem: Murmur of the Crags

Murmur of the Crags is the best elemental fusion for Avalon because it reinforces the exact thing he already does well: protecting the whole team. It creates a rocky cascade that shields all allies, absorbs 450,625 damage, and reflects double the damage taken back to attackers.

This fusion is easy to understand in battle. The enemy tries to burst you, but now they must break one more barrier before reaching Health, and they get punished while doing it. Since Avalon already provides a team-wide shield, Murmur of the Crags creates layered protection that is much more frustrating to chew through in PvP and extremely useful in longer Dungeon fights.

Why rank 1: It is the most natural extension of Avalon's identity. More barrier uptime means more value from every second your Earth team stays alive.

2nd - Ice Age

Elemental Totem: Ice Age

Ice Age freezes all enemy Titans for 1 second when their base totem skill activates and applies Fragility, increasing all damage they receive by 15.8% for 5 seconds.

This is a superb second option because Avalon loves tempo swings. That one-second freeze interrupts the enemy plan, and the extra damage taken gives your lineup a clean retaliation window right after Forest Embrace keeps everyone alive. In simple terms: you survive the enemy push, then Ice Age helps your team hit back harder.

Why rank 2: It adds control and offensive payoff, but it is slightly less consistent than Murmur of the Crags for pure defensive value.

3rd - Last Flash

Elemental Totem: Last Flash

Last Flash triggers when the Titan should die. Instead, that Titan enters a burning rage state, gains 15% Speed, becomes invulnerable to effects, cannot drop below 1 Health, and receives +16,100 Attack for 5 seconds. It activates once per battle.

For Avalon, this is valuable because a support Titan that survives a little longer can still deliver one more protection window or simply avoid collapsing before the team stabilizes. It is not as directly synergistic as Murmur of the Crags, but it remains a strong emergency option in PvP.

Why rank 3: Excellent safety net, especially in close battles, but less team-focused than the top two choices.

Best Totem Primal Fusion for Avalon

Primal fusions trigger under specific conditions, so the best ones for Avalon are the skills that either reward shields directly or help him cast his protection more reliably throughout battle.

1st - Echo Aegis

Primal Totem: Echo Aegis

Echo Aegis is the best primal fusion for Avalon because it directly interacts with shields. When a shield on your team breaks, there is a 20% chance to apply another shield for 5 seconds with durability equal to 4.2% of maximum Titan Health.

This is almost tailor-made for Avalon. Forest Embrace already tells the enemy, “You must break this first.” Echo Aegis answers, “And sometimes you will have to break another one.” For players who want a simple explanation: it makes your team feel sticky and annoying to finish off.

Why rank 1: Best direct synergy with Avalon's identity and the most frustrating option for enemies trying to secure quick kills.

2nd - Pulse of the Ancients

Primal Totem: Pulse of the Ancients

Pulse of the Ancients heals a Titan for 112,000 Health every time it uses an ability, with activations limited to once every 21 seconds.

This helps Avalon stay on the field longer and remain available for the moments when your team needs his shield most. It is a simple, reliable choice that improves consistency in PvP and Dungeon without demanding complicated timing.

Why rank 2: Very stable sustain, but it is less explosive than Echo Aegis because it does not strengthen the shield mechanic itself.

3rd - Primal Zeal

Primal Totem: Primal Zeal

Primal Zeal gives the Titan a 20% chance to restore 40% of maximum energy when using the basic skill.

For Avalon, faster energy means faster access to Forest Embrace. Even if the proc is not guaranteed, an earlier shield can completely change the first major damage trade of a fight. In other words, Primal Zeal is about getting protection online before the enemy finishes their burst plan.

Why rank 3: High upside, but more random than the two safer options above.

Best Skin for Titan Avalon Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Avalon's skin priority should follow one rule: first keep him alive, then improve the stat that matters for the matchups he actually takes, and only after that invest in lower-impact offensive bonuses.

Default Skin

Health: +1,022,985

PvP Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Avalon's best skin for PvP because raw Health helps against every form of damage. A dead support provides no shield, so making Avalon harder to kill is the most efficient investment.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Very High – Dungeon runs reward consistency. More Health makes Avalon much safer across repeated rooms, especially when you need him to keep shielding in Water and mixed fights.

Champion Skin

Elemental Armor: +49,065

PvP Evolution Priority: High – Elemental Armor is especially useful when Avalon is used into Water opponents, which is one of his natural jobs as an Earth Titan. It is more situational than Health, but still very efficient in the right matchups.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: High – Avalon often attacks Water rooms in Dungeon, so this skin has better practical value here than it would on many other Titans. It is the second-best upgrade after the Default Skin.

Mechanical Skin

Elemental Damage: +230,160

PvP Evolution Priority: Medium – Avalon is not picked for damage, so this skin does not change his core performance as much as Health or Elemental Armor. It is a luxury upgrade after survivability is already in a good place.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Medium – The offensive value is welcome when pushing Water rooms faster, but it still comes after the two skins that more directly improve Avalon's real job.

Avalon Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Artifact upgrades for Avalon should favor survivability first, matchup stability second, and damage last. The goal is to keep Forest Embrace relevant for as long as possible in every fight.

Avalon Artifact: Andvari's Bulwark

Elemental Damage: +479,475

Increases damage dealt by attacks and skills to Water Titans. Becomes less effective when the attacking Titan deals damage to Titans of a higher level.

PVP Evolution Priority: Medium-High – This is a useful matchup artifact when Avalon is drafted into Water teams, but it still does not improve his main contribution as directly as survival artifacts do.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: High – Avalon commonly attacks Water rooms in Dungeon, so extra elemental damage has more practical value here and helps Earth teams close fights faster.

Avalon Artifact: Earth Crown

Elemental Armor: +97,482

Elemental Armor protects the Titan against the attacks of Water Titans. Becomes less effective when the defending Titan takes damage from Titans of a higher level.

PVP Evolution Priority: High – Earth Crown is very strong in the matchups where Avalon is supposed to work hardest. It helps him stay alive against Water pressure, which means more reliable shielding.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: High – Since Avalon is often sent into Water rooms, this defensive artifact performs exactly where you want him to be used. It is one of his most practical upgrades.

Avalon Artifact: Balance Seal

Health: +3,995,583

Attack: +299,673

PVP Evolution Priority: Very High – This is the best artifact for Avalon because it gives the most universally important thing for a support Titan: survival. The huge Health gain keeps him on the field longer, while the Attack bonus is a welcome extra for overall pressure.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Very High – Balance Seal remains the top priority in Dungeon because its Health bonus works in every room, every matchup, and every stage of the run. If you want one safe artifact investment for Avalon, this is it.

How to Counter Titan Avalon Hero Wars Dominion Era

The best way to counter Avalon is to attack the weakness of Earth teams: strong Fire pressure. Fire Titans force Avalon to spend his shield defensively and can still break through with sustained damage, especially if his allies cannot punish them fast enough.

Araji

Araji is one of the most dangerous counters to Avalon because raw Fire burst can tear through shields faster than standard Earth teams want to handle. Even when Forest Embrace buys time, Araji's damage output forces Avalon into a purely defensive posture.

Asherona and Pyro

Asherona and Pyro counter Avalon by combining Fire-element pressure with aggressive frontline tempo. They force Earth teams to react immediately, and if Avalon's shield is traded too early, the rest of the fight becomes much easier for the Fire side.

Ignis

Ignis counters Avalon indirectly but very effectively. His team-wide Attack buff helps Fire allies break through Forest Embrace faster, turning Avalon's shield from a battle-saving wall into only a short delay. If the Fire team bursts during that buff window, Avalon can struggle to keep pace.

The best Avalon lineup is the classic Earth core: sturdy frontline, disruptive control, and enough damage to capitalize on every second of protection he creates. This team is strong in PvP and also dependable for Dungeon battles against Water and mixed rooms.

Team Composition Strategy

Angus: Frontline wall that buys space for the rest of the Earth team to function safely.

Eden: Provides control and disruptive pressure, helping Avalon turn defense into tempo swings.

Sylva: Adds consistent ranged damage so the team can actually punish enemies during shield windows.

Avalon: Full-team shield support that stabilizes the lineup during the enemy's most dangerous burst phase.

Verdoc: Utility and pressure support that makes the Earth core more complete and harder to outtrade.

Conclusion

Avalon is one of the clearest examples of how a support Titan can decide battles without being the top damage dealer. His value comes from timing, stability, and giving stronger allies the chance to actually finish the fight. If your team already has good damage and control, Avalon can be the piece that makes everything hold together.

For progression, focus first on survivability: Default Skin, Balance Seal, Earth Crown, and defensive totem synergy. After that, refine him with Murmur of the Crags and Echo Aegis if your goal is maximum PvP annoyance and safer Dungeon runs. Built correctly, Avalon turns Earth teams into a durable wall that wastes enemy burst and punishes impatience.
