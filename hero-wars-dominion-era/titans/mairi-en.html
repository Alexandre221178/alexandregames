Who Is Mairi?

Mairi is the embodiment of the ocean's healing power, a mystical support Titan who channels the essence of the Glacial Tide element. Positioned at the back line, she specializes in keeping her allies alive through powerful heals and protective abilities.

  • Role: Support
  • Element: Water (Glacial Tide)
  • Position: Back Line
  • Power: 206,325
  • Attack: 1,045,101
  • Health: 10,961,947
  • Elemental Armor: 146,547
  • Elemental Damage: 479,475

What makes Mairi exceptional is her ability to sustain your team through extended battles. Her healing isn't just reactive it's strategic, allowing you to maintain momentum even when facing overwhelming odds.

With impressive health stats and solid elemental damage, Mairi proves that support Titans can be both defensive powerhouses and offensive contributors. Her Glacial Tide element gives her natural synergy with other Water-based Titans, creating devastating elemental combos.

Mairi Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Understanding Mairi's abilities is crucial to maximizing her support potential. Let's break down each skill in simple terms and see how they help win battles.

Skill 1: Abyss Curse

What it does: Mairi unleashes a dark curse from the ocean depths that weakens all enemy Titans. Imagine draining your opponents' strength, making their attacks less effective like fighting with weights on their arms.

How it works: When activated, this skill decreases the Attack of all enemies by 40% for 8 seconds. This means every hit they land during this time deals significantly less damage, giving your team a massive defensive advantage.

Why it matters in PvP: In competitive battles, reducing enemy damage output by 40% is game-changing! This skill can neutralize powerful attackers during crucial moments. Time it right when facing enemy burst damage or ultimate abilities, and watch their offensive crumble. Priority: High

Alexandre's Pro Tip: Mairi's ability provides a significant strategic advantage in PvP battles, because to reduce enemy attack by 40% you only need to have Mairi on your team, without needing to level her up or spend resources on artifacts and skins, being effective in both dungeons and guild wars.

Skill 2: Main Totem: Water Spirit Totem

Why Water Spirit Totem is essential for Mairi: As a Water Titan, Mairi automatically benefits from this totem when paired with at least 2 other Water Titans. This isn't just about picking the best option it's about natural synergy. The totem amplifies everything Mairi does best: healing and sustaining the team.

Totem Benefits:

  • Blessing of the Deep: Activates when you have 3+ Water Titans. Heals all allied Titans while dealing damage to nearby enemies for 10 seconds.
  • Enhanced Healing: The healing splits among injured Titans, perfectly complementing Mairi's healing skills.
  • Damage Formula: (Total Health Regen per activation: 595,687.5) divided among Titans needing healing.
  • Stat Boost: Increases all stats for your Water Titans, making Mairi's heals stronger and her barriers more durable.

Priority: Very High - Essential for maximizing Mairi's performance in PvP. The combination of her healing skills with the totem's area healing creates an incredibly resilient team.

Best Elemental Totem Fusion for Mairi

Want to supercharge Mairi's defensive prowess? Totem Fusion is your secret weapon! Think of it as adding special power-ups to your already strong Mairi. These fusions work alongside her Water Spirit Totem (which is always active in battle) to create devastating combos that can swing PvP battles in your favor.

How Does Totem Fusion Work for Mairi?

Imagine you're upgrading your favorite weapon in a game. That's exactly what Totem Fusion does for Mairi! In the Fusion window, you choose between Elemental or Primordial Skills, select your desired ability, and invest Catalysts (special resources). The more Catalysts you invest, the better your chances of getting powerful high-level skills.

Important to know: If you already have a skill and fuse it again, your chances improve for getting an even better version! When two totems have the same skill, only the higher-level one activates in battle. Elemental Skills trigger globally with no cooldown, while Primordial Skills have individual cooldowns per Titan based on specific conditions.

Pro tip: If the fusion result doesn't suit Mairi's playstyle, you can reject it and restore your totem to its previous state no permanent damage done!

🥇 1st Place: Ice Age

Ice Age (Rank I)

What it does: When your opponent's base totem activates, ALL their Titans freeze for 1 second and receive a fragility debuff that increases damage taken by 15.8% for 5 seconds.

Why it's PERFECT for Mairi: Remember Mairi's Abyss Curse that reduces enemy attack by 40%? Ice Age creates the ultimate combo! First, Ice Age freezes and weakens enemies (making them take more damage), then Mairi's curse ensures they can barely fight back. It's like hitting your opponents with a double knockout punch they're frozen, weakened, AND their attacks are neutered!

PvP Impact: This fusion turns Mairi from a defensive support into a battlefield controller. The 1-second freeze interrupts enemy combos, while the fragility effect means your team's attacks hit 15.8% harder. Combined with Mairi's 40% attack reduction, enemies become harmless punching bags. Priority: Very High

🥈 2nd Place: Murmur of the Crags

Murmur of the Crags (Rank I)

What it does: Creates a rocky barrier that protects ALL allies from attacks. The barrier absorbs 450,625 damage and reflects double the damage back at attackers!

Why it synergizes with Mairi: Mairi's job is keeping the team alive, and this fusion gives her an extra layer of protection. Think of it as giving your entire team a shield that not only blocks damage but punches back twice as hard! While Mairi weakens enemy attacks with Abyss Curse, this barrier ensures any damage that does get through hurts the attacker more than it hurts you.

PvP Impact: Perfect for surviving burst damage teams. When enemies unleash their ultimate abilities, this rocky shield absorbs the hit and reflects devastating damage back. Combined with Mairi reducing their attack power, you create an impenetrable fortress. Priority: High

🥉 3rd Place: The Depths Whisper

The Depths Whisper (Rank I)

What it does: Creates a healing whirlpool that restores health to allies and damages nearby enemies over 10 seconds. Each activation distributes 50,312 total health among Titans who need it most, while also dealing 50,312 damage to enemies.

Why it complements Mairi: This is Mairi's healing support on steroids! Since Mairi already focuses on sustaining the team, The Depths Whisper multiplies her healing potential. It's like having a second healer on your team one that also hurts enemies while healing allies. The heal distribution is smart too, automatically helping the most injured Titans.

PvP Impact: Excellent for prolonged battles where sustain wins games. The continuous healing over 10 seconds combined with Mairi's defensive buffs creates incredible staying power. Your team simply outlasts opponents through relentless healing. Priority: Medium-High

Best Primal Totem Fusion for Mairi

Now let's explore Primordial Fusions these are triggered by specific conditions and have individual cooldowns per Titan. For Mairi, we want fusions that activate frequently and enhance her support capabilities!

🥇 1st Place: Pulse of the Ancients

Pulse of the Ancients (Rank I)

What it does: Every time an allied Titan uses an ability, it heals for 112,000 health. Can activate no more than once per 21 seconds.

Why it's AMAZING for Mairi: Mairi constantly uses her Abyss Curse ability to debuff enemies. Pulse of the Ancients rewards this active playstyle by healing Mairi every time she or her allies cast abilities! It's like getting a free health potion every 21 seconds just for playing normally. Since support Titans like Mairi use abilities frequently, this triggers consistently throughout battle.

PvP Impact: This fusion dramatically increases Mairi's survivability. As a backline support, she's often targeted first, but Pulse keeps her alive through constant healing. The more active your team is with abilities, the more value you get. In extended fights, this can heal Mairi multiple times, making her nearly impossible to kill. Priority: Very High

🥈 2nd Place: Triple Cycle

Triple Cycle (Rank I)

What it does: Creates a reactive cycle: If Mairi gets a shield → she heals (50,400 HP/sec). If she gets healing → her attack increases (+5,600). If she gets a buff → she gains a shield (24,500 durability). Each effect lasts 2 seconds.

Why it's versatile for Mairi: This is the Swiss Army knife of fusions! Since Mairi receives healing from her Water Spirit Totem's Blessing of the Deep, Triple Cycle transforms that heal into an attack buff. When allies shield her, she gets additional healing. When she receives any buff, she gets a protective shield. It's a self-sustaining cycle of bonuses!

PvP Impact: Triple Cycle turns Mairi into a jack-of-all-trades. The constant cycling between healing, attack buffs, and shields makes her unpredictable and hard to counter. While her primary role is support, the attack buff lets her contribute more damage. Priority: High

🥉 3rd Place: Echo Aegis

Echo Aegis (Rank I)

What it does: When a shield on your team breaks, it immediately reapplies a new shield worth 4.2% of maximum Titan health for 5 seconds. Has a 20% activation chance.

Why it protects Mairi's team: This fusion is insurance for your shields! When Mairi's team receives shields from any source and they break under enemy fire, Echo Aegis has a chance to instantly create a replacement shield. It's like having backup shields ready to deploy. For a defensive-focused Titan like Mairi, this extends the team's tankiness.

PvP Impact: Especially effective against burst damage teams that try to quickly break through shields. The automatic shield replacement frustrates opponents and buys precious seconds for Mairi to support the team. While the 20% chance means it won't always trigger, when it does, it can be the difference between a dead Titan and a surviving one. Priority: Medium-High

Mairi Counters - Titans That Dominate Her

Every Titan has weaknesses, and understanding Mairi's counters is crucial for both playing with her and against her. As a Water Titan, Mairi faces a fundamental disadvantage against Earth Titans due to the elemental triangle system in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

Why Earth Titans Counter Mairi:

The elemental system works like rock-paper-scissors: Earth beats Water, Water beats Fire, Fire beats Earth. This means Earth Titans deal significantly increased damage to Mairi while taking reduced damage from her attacks. Even with her massive health pool and defensive capabilities, Mairi struggles against Earth-element opponents.

🌳 Top Earth Titan Counters:

Angus (Earth Titan)

Why Angus dominates Mairi: As a powerful Earth tank, Angus naturally takes reduced damage from Mairi's attacks while dealing amplified damage back. His high durability combined with the elemental advantage makes him extremely difficult for Mairi to handle. Even with Abyss Curse reducing his attack by 40%, the elemental bonus often compensates, allowing Angus to pressure Mairi effectively.

Counter Strategy: Avoid direct confrontations. Focus on supporting your team's non-Water Titans to take down Angus, as Mairi's debuffs will still weaken him for your allies.

Avalon (Earth Titan)

Why Avalon dominates Mairi: Avalon's Earth element gives him the same fundamental advantage against Mairi. His abilities deal bonus damage to Water Titans while Mairi's attacks are weakened against him. This elemental matchup can quickly turn deadly for Mairi if she becomes Avalon's primary target.

Counter Strategy: Position Mairi carefully and rely on your Earth or Fire Titans to engage Avalon. Mairi should focus on keeping the team alive rather than trying to damage Avalon directly.

Sylva (Earth Titan)

Why Sylva dominates Mairi: Sylva's Earth affinity makes her a natural predator of Water Titans like Mairi. The elemental advantage ensures Sylva's attacks hit harder while taking less damage from Mairi's counterattacks. In prolonged battles, this advantage compounds, making it nearly impossible for Mairi to survive focused attention from Sylva.

Counter Strategy: Use Mairi's mobility and positioning to avoid Sylva's range. Let your team's damage dealers handle Sylva while Mairi focuses on support duties from a safe distance.

Verdoc (Earth Titan)

Why Verdoc dominates Mairi: Verdoc's Earth element provides the same counter advantage. His abilities are amplified against Water targets like Mairi, while Mairi's healing and debuffs do little to stop Verdoc's assault. The elemental mismatch means Verdoc can often eliminate Mairi before she provides significant value to her team.

Counter Strategy: Prioritize defensive positioning and rely on Mairi's team-wide debuffs to weaken Verdoc for your allies. Don't try to out-trade him directly focus on survival and support.

Eden (Earth Super Titan)

Why Eden dominates Mairi: Eden is the ultimate nightmare for Mairi. As a Super Titan with Earth element, Eden combines overwhelming power with the elemental advantage against Water Titans. Eden's abilities deal devastating damage to Mairi while simultaneously shrugging off her attacks. Even Mairi's Abyss Curse struggles to neutralize Eden's threat level.

Counter Strategy: When facing Eden, Mairi's best role is pure support keep distance, apply Abyss Curse to reduce Eden's damage output for the team, and focus entirely on keeping allies alive. Never engage Eden directly. Consider team compositions that include Fire or neutral-element Titans to handle Eden while Mairi provides backline support.

Alexandre's Counter Tips:

  • Elemental Awareness: Always check enemy team composition before battle. If you see multiple Earth Titans, Mairi may not be the optimal choice.
  • Team Synergy: Pair Mairi with Fire or neutral-element Titans who can handle Earth threats while she focuses on support.
  • Positioning Matters: Keep Mairi in the backline away from Earth Titans. Her debuffs work globally, so she doesn't need to be close to be effective.
  • Focus on Value: Even against counters, Mairi's 40% attack reduction affects the entire enemy team. Focus on maximizing this utility rather than trying to win the elemental matchup.

Team Composition Strategy

His Darkness element provides balance to the team, avoiding elemental counters. Tenebris's incredible durability synergizes perfectly with Mairi's 40% attack reduction on enemies, making him an unstoppable fortress that demolishes opposing tanks while remaining nearly unkillable.

Hyperion (Water Super Titan - Damage Dealer & Healer): Powerful Super Titan who combines devastating Water damage with team-healing capabilities. Benefits enormously from Mairi's support while also providing sustain. While Mairi weakens enemies with Abyss Curse, Hyperion capitalizes on the debuffed opponents to deal massive damage while keeping the team healthy. His Water element synergizes with Mairi's for stronger Water Totem activation.

Mairi (Water Support): The heart of this composition. Her Abyss Curse reduces all enemy attacks by 40%, while Water Spirit Totem heals the entire team. Mairi's debuffs make everyone on her team tankier and safer, allowing aggressive plays.

Nova (Support/Damage - Stun Power): Versatile support Titan who provides crowd control through powerful stun abilities, disrupting enemy formations and buying crucial time. Delivers additional damage alongside control utility.

Sigurd (Water Tank - Invincible Shield): Resilient Water tank with signature 5-second invincibility shield that makes him temporarily immune to all damage. Provides critical frontline alongside Tenebris damage. His Water element synergizes with Mairi's and Hyperion's for optimal Water Totem activation. Why This Team Works:
