Alecto does something most marksmen cannot: she disappears from danger, breaches the enemy back line, and unloads five strikes while completely invulnerable. That combination immediately makes her one of the most exciting new tactical pieces in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

Her damage is easy to understand but difficult to defend against. The real challenge is building enough Physical Attack to make every hit count, protecting her between casts, and choosing teammates who convert her back-line pressure into a complete victory.

Who Is Alecto?

Alecto is a Distortion marksman introduced during Fall of the Veil in August 2026. She fights from battlefield position 6 and is designed to punish the farthest enemy instead of spending the entire battle shooting into the opposing tank.

  • Role: Marksman
  • Element: Distortion
  • Battlefield position: #6
  • Basic attack: 1,144,764 Physical Damage every 4 seconds
  • Obtention: Circle of Summoning, not yet added at publication
  • Best synergy: Valdur, Distortion cores, and teams that protect her between Phase Breach casts

What makes Alecto special is the target choice. Phase Breach moves her behind the farthest enemy, so a fragile support or ranged damage dealer can suddenly become the center of the fight. She is invulnerable during the five-hit sequence, then returns to her original position. That safety is powerful, but it lasts only while the skill is active.

A New Elemental Cycle: How Distortion Changes Titan Battles

Distortion expands the familiar elemental balance instead of joining the original Earth-Water-Fire triangle. Distortion Titans have an elemental advantage over Light and Darkness Titans, while Fire, Water, and Earth Titans are the natural counters to Distortion.

Alecto Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Phase Breach combines back-line access, five-hit burst, area pressure, and temporary invulnerability. Understanding its timing explains Alecto's real value.

Skill 1: Phase Breach

In-game description: Alecto moves behind the farthest enemy and hits them five times. She is invulnerable while the skill is active. Every hit also damages nearby enemies, and Alecto returns to her initial position when the sequence ends.

Skill explanation: Think of Phase Breach as a protected back-line raid. Alecto does not remain behind the enemy forever: she appears there, completes all five hits without taking damage, then returns. The primary target receives 686,858 Physical Damage per hit, while nearby enemies receive 343,429 Physical Damage per hit.

Formula for each primary hit: (60% Physical Attack). Formula for each nearby-enemy hit: (30% Physical Attack).

Across all five hits, the listed values total 3,434,290 damage to the main target and up to 1,717,145 damage to each nearby enemy, before considering the opponent's defenses and combat modifiers.

PvP importance: The farthest enemy is often a support or marksman that expects the frontline to protect it. Alecto forces that unit to absorb direct burst. Her invulnerability can also make enemy attacks miss their intended damage window, but opponents can still punish her after she returns.

Dungeon importance: Phase Breach is valuable for removing a dangerous rear enemy and spreading damage across clustered rooms. Because Dungeon survival carries strategic weight, do not confuse temporary invulnerability with permanent sustain: Alecto still benefits from healing, shields, and attack reduction between casts.

Elemental Spirit of Distortion

Alecto's fourth Titan Artifact is the Elemental Spirit of Distortion. At maximum development it supplies +299,673 Physical Attack per Titan and 20,377 Power per Titan, directly strengthening both formulas in Phase Breach.

Accuracy note: the supplied Alecto data confirms this artifact and its stats but does not provide a separate active Distortion Totem battle-skill description. For that reason, this guide does not invent an unconfirmed Totem ability or activation rule.

Best Elemental Totem Fusion Skills for Alecto

The six newer Fusion Skills change Alecto's ranking completely. Permanent Attack scaling and enemy grouping now offer more value than the original survival-focused choices.

How Does Totem Fusion Work?

In the Fusion window, choose Elemental or Primordial, select the desired skill, and decide how many Catalysts to invest. More Catalysts improve the displayed chance of receiving that skill and a better rank. If the Totem already has your selected skill, the odds of receiving it again and at a higher level increase. When two Totems carry the same skill, only the higher-level version activates.

Elemental Skills normally trigger globally without cooldown unless their description says otherwise. Fusion consumes the Catalysts when it begins. You may reject an unwanted result and restore the previous Totem state, but the consumed Catalysts are not returned. The main Totem is already present in battle, so it is not ranked as a Fusion Skill.

1st - Main Sequence

In-game effect: every five seconds, Main Sequence increases the Attack of all allied Titans until the end of battle. At the supplied values, each activation grants +146,250 Attack, depending on Totem power.

Why it is now Alecto's best Elemental Fusion: Phase Breach calculates every primary hit from (60% Physical Attack) and every nearby hit from (30% Physical Attack). A single +146,250 Attack increment therefore adds 87,750 to each primary hit and 43,875 to each splash hit. Across all five hits, that is up to +438,750 primary damage and +219,375 damage to each nearby enemy, before defenses and other modifiers.

The effect also strengthens Alecto's basic attacks and continues gaining value in a long battle. It is universal, requires no stun, shield, death, or opposing Totem activation, and works in PvP attack, PvP defense, and Dungeon rooms.

2nd - Gravitational Anomaly

In-game effect: conjures an anomaly in the center of the enemy team, drags enemies inward, and damages them for five seconds. Damage is strongest at the epicenter, reaching 1,669,078 at the supplied Totem power and skill rank.

Why it fits Alecto: Phase Breach deals half of its main-hit damage to enemies standing near Alecto's chosen target. Gravitational Anomaly compresses the enemy formation, making it more likely that multiple opponents remain inside that splash area. It also disrupts positioning while Alecto is safely invulnerable during her own movement and attack sequence.

This is the strongest option when you want Alecto's single-target raid to become team-wide pressure. It is especially attractive against spread-out formations that normally prevent her nearby damage from reaching several Titans.

3rd - Nova Flare

In-game effect: creates a Nova Flare that concentrates for five seconds. For two seconds after activation, allied Titans cannot fall below 1 Health. The Flare then explodes and deals 5,449,641 damage to all enemies, depending on Totem power and skill rank.

Why it fits Alecto: Alecto is already invulnerable during Phase Breach, but she is exposed after returning to her original position. Nova Flare can preserve her during that vulnerable interval while also protecting the other four Titans. Its enormous team-wide explosion can soften every opponent before Alecto selects the farthest survivor for her next five-hit sequence.

It ranks below Gravitational Anomaly only because its protection can overlap with Alecto's own invulnerability and it does not directly improve her formulas. For overall team survival and raw area damage, Nova Flare can still be the better practical choice.

Best Primal Totem Fusion Skills for Alecto

Primal Fusion should help Alecto cast more often, survive after returning to position, or turn lost Health into enough Attack to finish a priority target.

1st - Primal Zeal

When the Titan uses its basic skill, Primal Zeal has a chance to restore Energy. At Rank I, the supplied values are 40% of maximum Energy and a 20% activation chance, with scaling based on Totem power and skill rank.

Phase Breach is Alecto's entire battle identity, so another cast is usually more valuable than a small passive effect. More casts mean more protected time, more back-line pressure, and another five-hit damage sequence.

2nd - Pulse of the Ancients

Every time an allied Titan uses an ability, that Titan is healed. At Rank I, the listed healing is 112,000 with an activation no more frequent than once every 21 seconds; both values depend on Totem development.

This is reliable sustain with no shield requirement. Alecto can receive healing after using Phase Breach, helping her survive the exposed interval before she has enough Energy to disappear again.

3rd - Call of Elements

Call of Elements increases a Titan's Attack in proportion to missing Health. At Rank I, the supplied value is +2,100 Attack for every 21% of maximum Health missing, with scaling based on Totem power and skill rank.

Alecto often enters a dangerous low-Health state between Phase Breach casts. This Fusion turns part of that danger into stronger five-hit damage. It is more aggressive than Pulse of the Ancients, but it does not actually keep her alive.

How to Counter Titan Alecto - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

The expanded Elemental Cycle gives a clear starting point: Fire, Water, and Earth are all natural counters to Distortion. The strongest answers combine that elemental advantage with area damage, Attack reduction, control, or sustained pressure that catches Alecto outside Phase Breach. The examples below represent all three counter elements and are listed alphabetically.

Angus - Earth

Angus: Earth has a natural advantage over Distortion. Anger of the Earth also damages the entire opposing team over eight seconds. Alecto avoids damage while Phase Breach is active, but the continuing area effect can catch her after she returns to her initial position. Angus's durability gives his team time to survive the five-hit burst and maintain pressure.

Araji - Fire

Araji: Fire naturally counters Distortion, and Araji contributes the sustained offensive pressure needed to punish Alecto between her invulnerable sequences. He does not cancel Phase Breach while it is active; the goal is to survive the five strikes and make the exposed interval after Alecto returns as dangerous as possible.

Mairi - Water

Mairi: Water already has the natural elemental advantage, and Abyss Curse reduces the Attack of all enemies by 40% for eight seconds. Because both Phase Breach formulas use Alecto's Physical Attack, a curse applied before the ultimate directly weakens every primary and nearby hit.

Alecto Guide Conclusion

Alecto is a focused answer to teams that feel too comfortable behind a durable frontline. Phase Breach reaches the farthest opponent, delivers five hits with substantial splash damage, and protects Alecto from all damage during the sequence. The new Elemental Cycle makes her role even clearer: she is built to pressure Light and Darkness, while Fire, Water, and Earth demand more careful protection and timing.

My upgrade route is straightforward: maximize the Default Skin before the Primordial Skin, begin artifacts with the Distorted Attack Seal, follow with Zorval's Wing, and leave the Crown of Distortion for third unless survival is already your main problem. With the expanded Elemental Fusion list, Main Sequence is now Alecto's best universal choice, followed by Gravitational Anomaly and Nova Flare. Primal Zeal remains the leading Primal option.

The updated battle notes expand Valdur and Alecto to eight distinct lineups built around Fire, Water, Earth, Light, Darkness, and Distortion partners. Several teams recorded 10-0 results against the listed Rigel-Solaris defenses, but each score belongs only to that exact matchup; Totem development, Fusion skills, and battle timing can still change the result.
