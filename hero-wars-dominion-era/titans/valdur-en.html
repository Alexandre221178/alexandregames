Valdur does not enter the battlefield alone. He tears at the fabric of reality, calls Echo to his side, and fills the enemy formation with lesser images that become more dangerous when they die. That reversal is what makes his design so fascinating: destroying his summons can be exactly what he wants you to do.

This guide explains the timing behind Echoclasm, the real value of each image, and why Valdur can create both explosive damage and unpredictable battlefield disruption. If you enjoy Titans that reward planning instead of simple brute force, Valdur deserves your attention.

Who Are Valdur and Echo?

Valdur and Echo are a Distortion Summoner pair introduced during Judgment Day in August 2026. Valdur fights from battlefield position 25 and uses Echoclasm to create one lesser image for every living enemy Titan. Instead of treating those images as permanent allies, his strategy turns their deaths into area damage and displacement.

  • Role: Summoner
  • Element: Distortion
  • Battlefield position: #25
  • Basic attack: 1,261,688 Physical Damage every 3.8 seconds
  • Maximum Health: 8,662,432
  • Obtention: Circle of Summoning, not yet added at publication
  • Best synergy: Alecto, Distortion teams, and allies that capitalize on enemy displacement

Distortion gives Valdur a natural offensive advantage against Light and Darkness, while Fire, Water, and Earth are the dangerous matchups. His images soften that weakness by crowding the field and resisting 30% of area-of-effect damage, but their real purpose is not merely survival: every death threatens nearby enemies with a powerful explosion.

Valdur and Echo Overall Rating in the Meta

  • Control: ★★★★☆ — Each dying image pushes the nearest enemy in a random direction, repeatedly disturbing positioning when several summons expire together.
  • Damage: ★★★★★ — Every image can explode for 1,135,519 area damage, and a five-enemy formation allows Valdur to summon five separate threats.
  • Synergy: ★★★★★ — Valdur and Echo count as two Distortion Titans, generate 20% more Totem Energy, and create space for Alecto or other damage dealers.
  • Independence: ★★★★☆ — Echoclasm supplies its own summons, damage, and disruption, although the best results still depend on favorable image placement and a team that exploits the chaos.

Alexandre's Tips

Valdur is one of the most complete tactical Summoners in the current Titan meta. His damage ceiling is enormous, but what separates him from a simple damage dealer is the ability to make every destroyed image disrupt the enemy formation.

Valdur and Echo Skills Guide - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn how Valdur summons durable lesser images, turns their deaths into explosive control, and accelerates the Distortion Totem in PvP battles.

Skill 1: Echoclasm

In-game description: Summons lesser images onto the battlefield, one for each living enemy Titan. Images have 692,995 Health and receive 30% less damage from area-of-effect attacks. After death, an image deals 1,135,519 damage to enemy Titans around it and pushes the closest enemy Titan in a random direction. If new images are summoned while living images remain, the old images die and are replaced.

Skill explanation: Think of Echoclasm as placing one explosive decoy for every enemy still alive. Against five living opponents, Valdur creates five images. Enemies can destroy them, but every destroyed image explodes around its position and moves the closest enemy unpredictably. If Valdur casts again before the images die, the surviving old images are deliberately destroyed first, so their explosions and pushes activate before the replacements arrive.

Image Health formula: (8% × Valdur's maximum Health = 692,995 Health per image).

Death explosion formula: (90% × Valdur's Physical Attack = 1,135,519 Physical Damage per image).

Five-image listed total: (5 × 1,135,519 = 5,677,595 Physical Damage) across all explosions before defenses and combat modifiers; overlapping blast areas can hit the same enemy more than once.

PvP Evolution Priority: Very High – Five-versus-five PvP gives Echoclasm its maximum opening image count. The combination of repeated area explosions, random displacement, and forced detonation on recast makes it Valdur's defining offensive and control tool on both attack and defense.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Low – Echoclasm remains dangerous in mixed rooms, but Distortion cannot enter the recurring Earth, Fire, or Water elemental rooms. Dungeon resources should first support elemental Titans and healers that preserve damaged teams between battles.

Skill 2: Spiritual Bond: Distortion

In-game description: Valdur and Echo perpetually balance on the border of existence, using the thinness of reality wherever they find it. For Totem activation, they count as two Distortion Titans and generate 20% more Energy for Distortion Totem activation.

Skill explanation: Valdur occupies one team slot but contributes two Distortion bodies to the Totem requirement. The additional Energy generation also moves the Totem closer to activation. This makes Valdur the natural foundation of a Distortion core and gives mixed teams more freedom in their remaining four positions.

Totem contribution formula: (Valdur and Echo = 2 Distortion Titans; Distortion Totem Energy generated = normal value × 120%).

PvP Evolution Priority: Very High – Efficient Totem activation is crucial in short PvP battles, and counting as two Distortion Titans allows Valdur to anchor both pure and mixed compositions.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Low – The passive helps only in mixed Dungeon rooms. It cannot replace the elemental Titans and sustain tools required for the far more frequent Earth, Fire, and Water battles.

Skill 3 - Main Totem: Planar Storm - Elemental Spirit of Distortion

Artifact stats: The Elemental Spirit of Distortion adds +299,673 Physical Attack per Titan at the listed maximum development, with 20,377 Power per Titan. That Attack bonus directly strengthens Valdur's basic attacks and the 90% Physical Attack formula behind every lesser-image explosion.

In-game description: Planar Storm covers the battlefield in deadly shards for 15 seconds. Once per second, a shock occurs within the zone and pushes a random enemy Titan in a random direction.

Movement damage: Any movement by an enemy Titan inside the effect zone deals damage based on the distance traveled. The listed damage value is 1,067,141.

Activation requirement: Planar Storm is available only if your team has at least 3 Distortion Titans on the battlefield.

Totem explanation: Planar Storm creates a damaging displacement zone for 15 seconds, with its own shock pushing one random enemy every second. Valdur has exceptional synergy with it because Echoclasm's lesser-image explosions also push enemies in random directions. Those forced movements can deal additional distance-based damage while the enemies remain inside the storm. Spiritual Bond makes Valdur and Echo count as two Distortion Titans, so they need only one additional Distortion Titan on the battlefield to satisfy the three-Titan activation requirement.

PvP Evolution Priority: Very High – Every point of Physical Attack is multiplied through Echoclasm's 90% formula and can apply across several image explosions.

Dungeon Evolution Priority: Low – The bonus can help Valdur in mixed rooms, but the Totem has no role in the elemental-only Earth, Fire, and Water rooms that define consistent Dungeon progress.

Alexandre's tip: Do not measure Echoclasm only by the first set of summons. The most dangerous moment may be the second cast, because every surviving old image dies at once before the new group appears. That forced replacement can create a sudden chain of explosions and pushes that completely changes the battlefield.

Best Elemental Totem Fusion Skills for Valdur and Echo

Valdur rewards Fusion Skills that turn movement into damage, increase Physical Attack, or group enemies so several image explosions overlap on the same targets.

How Does Totem Fusion Work?

In the Fusion window, choose Elemental or Primordial, select the skill you want, and decide how many Catalysts to invest. More Catalysts increase the displayed chance of receiving that skill and obtaining it at a better rank. Selecting a skill already present on the Totem improves the odds of receiving it again and at a higher level.

If two Totems carry the same Fusion Skill, only the higher-level version activates. Elemental Skills normally trigger globally without cooldown unless their description says otherwise, while Primordial Skills use their own conditions and per-Titan cooldowns. Fusion consumes the Catalysts when it begins; rejecting the result restores the previous Totem state but does not restore the Catalysts. The main Distortion Totem is already part of battle and is therefore not ranked below.

1st - Star Dust

In-game effect: covers the battlefield with a 15-second magical storm. Energy pushes a random Titan every second, and movement during the effect deals up to 828,750 damage depending on distance traveled.

Why it is Valdur's best Elemental Fusion: Echoclasm already pushes the closest enemy in a random direction whenever an image dies. Star Dust turns that built-in displacement into another source of damage while adding its own repeated pushes. One image death can therefore produce an explosion, reposition an enemy, and make that movement hurt.

PvP priority: Very High – Five-enemy battles maximize Valdur's image count and create the highest number of possible push interactions. Dungeon priority: Low – Powerful in mixed rooms, but unavailable throughout elemental-only Dungeon battles.

2nd - Main Sequence

In-game effect: increases the Attack of every allied Titan once every five seconds until the battle ends. Each listed activation grants +146,250 Attack, depending on Totem power.

How it scales Echoclasm: Valdur's image explosion uses (90% Physical Attack). One Main Sequence increment therefore adds 131,625 damage per image, or up to 658,125 listed damage across five explosions, before defenses and modifiers. The recurring buff also improves Valdur's 1,261,688 basic attack.

PvP priority: Very High – Universal, reliable, and excellent on defense where the battle may last through several increments. Dungeon priority: Low – Strong inside a mixed room, but Valdur's restricted access limits its overall Dungeon value.

3rd - Gravitational Anomaly

In-game effect: creates an anomaly in the center of the enemy team for five seconds, dragging enemies inward and dealing up to 1,669,078 damage at the epicenter.

Why it works with Valdur: pulling opponents together places more targets inside each lesser image's death explosion. Echoclasm can then punish the clustered group before its random pushes scatter them again. This Fusion offers less permanent scaling than Main Sequence, but its burst setup can be devastating.

PvP priority: High – Best when the enemy formation can be compressed into overlapping explosions. Dungeon priority: Low – Useful only in mixed rooms and provides no carryover healing.

Alexandre's Elemental ranking: Star Dust for the unique push interaction, Main Sequence for universal scaling, and Gravitational Anomaly for grouped explosion damage. Dark Matter becomes a specialized alternative when Valdur fights beside a reliable stunner such as Moloch.

Best Primal Totem Fusion Skills for Valdur and Echo

Primal Fusion should help Valdur recast Echoclasm, preserve his Health, or turn damage received into stronger image explosions in difficult battles.

1st - Primal Zeal

When the Titan uses its basic skill, Primal Zeal has a chance to restore part of its Energy. At Rank I, the listed values are 40% of maximum Energy and a 20% activation chance, scaling with Totem development.

This is especially dangerous on Valdur because a faster second Echoclasm does more than summon another wave. Every old image still alive is destroyed first, immediately activating its explosion and push before the new images arrive. More Energy can therefore become both burst damage and a complete battlefield reset.

2nd - Call of Elements

Call of Elements increases the Titan's Attack in proportion to missing Health. At Rank I, the listed value is +2,100 Attack for every 21% of maximum Health missing, with scaling based on Totem power and skill rank.

Valdur becomes more dangerous as he is worn down because every Attack increase feeds the 90% Physical Attack image-explosion formula. This is the aggressive choice for teams that can prevent his death without immediately restoring all missing Health.

3rd - Pulse of the Ancients

Every time an allied Titan uses an ability, that Titan is healed. At Rank I, the listed healing is 112,000 and can activate no more than once every 21 seconds, with both values depending on Totem development.

Pulse does not strengthen the images directly, but it gives Valdur another chance to survive until Echoclasm is ready. Choose it over Call of Elements when stability matters more than maximizing a low-Health damage window.

Alexandre's Primal ranking: Primal Zeal → Call of Elements → Pulse of the Ancients. In a fragile defense, Pulse can move above Call of Elements because a living Valdur is always more valuable than an Attack bonus on a Titan who dies before recasting.

How to Counter Titan Valdur and Echo - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Fire, Water, and Earth naturally counter Distortion, but raw elemental advantage is not enough. Killing lesser images triggers their explosions, so the safest counters reduce Valdur's Physical Attack, survive the resulting area damage, or eliminate him before a second Echoclasm replaces every remaining summon.

Angus - Earth

Angus: Earth has a natural advantage over Distortion, and Angus combines that edge with the durability needed to endure multiple image explosions. Anger of the Earth damages the entire opposing formation over eight seconds, pressuring Valdur and his summons simultaneously. The images will still explode when destroyed, so Angus's value comes from surviving that retaliation while continuing to damage Valdur himself.

Araji - Fire

Araji: Fire naturally counters Distortion, and Araji supplies the sustained offensive pressure needed to defeat Valdur before repeated casts fill the battlefield again. His goal is not to clear images carelessly; it is to keep enough damage on Valdur that the Summoner cannot reach another forced-replacement explosion cycle.

Mairi - Water

Mairi: Water already has the natural elemental advantage, while Abyss Curse reduces the Attack of all enemies by 40% for eight seconds. Because each image explosion uses 90% of Valdur's Physical Attack, a well-timed curse directly weakens the most dangerous part of Echoclasm and makes clearing the summons much safer.

Valdur and Echo Guide Conclusion

Valdur is at his best when the battlefield becomes crowded and unpredictable. Echoclasm creates one image for every living enemy, protects those summons from part of the incoming area damage, and turns each death into a 1,135,519-damage explosion plus random displacement. A second cast can be even more dangerous than the first because all surviving images are destroyed before their replacements arrive.

My upgrade route is straightforward: maximize the Default Skin before the Primordial Skin, then develop the Seal of Calling before Zorval's Reins and the Crown of Distortion. Star Dust is the most exciting Elemental Fusion because it converts Echoclasm's pushes into movement damage; Main Sequence is the safest universal alternative, while Primal Zeal leads the Primal ranking by accelerating the forced-replacement cycle.

The updated battle notes expand Valdur and Alecto to eight distinct lineups built around Fire, Water, Earth, Light, Darkness, and Distortion partners. Several teams recorded 10-0 results against the listed Rigel-Solaris defenses, but each score belongs only to that exact matchup; Totem development, Fusion skills, and battle timing can still change the result.
