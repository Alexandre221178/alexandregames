Avalon Titan Brawls are built around one core rule: Avalon must be on both attack and defense. That changes the pace of every match, because the Titan that normally acts as a backline shield support suddenly becomes the center of the whole event.

Based on my current tests, the most reliable Avalon Brawl lineup I found for both offense and defense is Brustar, Rigel, Avalon, Solaris, and Tenebris. I am also tracking a second solid option with Eden, Solaris, Verdoc, Avalon, and Rigel for players who want a more Earth-focused alternative. The first team uses a double front line to protect the back row, while the second one leans more into control and durability.

Best Avalon Brawl Team from My Current Tests

Brustar Rigel Avalon Solaris Tenebris
Second Avalon Brawl Team Option
Eden Solaris Verdoc Avalon Rigel

Why This Team Works

Brustar + Rigel as tanks: this double frontline gives your backline more time to function and makes it harder for enemy burst to reach Avalon, Solaris, and Tenebris too early.

Brustar as a counter piece: Brustar is especially useful here to help answer enemy Tenebris and Araji, which are two of the most dangerous threats in fast brawl battles.

Avalon as the stabilizer: Avalon keeps the whole team alive with Forest Embrace, a full-team shield that buys the exact window your damage dealers need.

Solaris + Tenebris as the core pressure: these two provide most of the damage and support value, while the front line and Avalon make sure they stay on the field long enough to swing the match.

Why the Second Team Can Work

Eden adds control: Eden helps disrupt the enemy front line and gives this setup a stronger control angle than the main team.

Verdoc supports the Earth core: Verdoc improves the value of an Earth-heavy lineup and helps the team stay stable through longer fights.

Solaris + Avalon keep the team balanced: Solaris still provides high-value support and pressure, while Avalon keeps the whole lineup protected long enough for Eden and Rigel to do their job.

Avalon's Skills and Protection Value

Forest Embrace

Avalon casts a shield on all allies, absorbing up to 1,968,825 damage. In Avalon Brawls, this is the most important skill in the match because every team is forced to run Avalon, so shield timing often determines which side keeps its damage dealers alive longer.

When your lineup includes Solaris and Tenebris, the shield becomes even more valuable. Those Titans need a few extra seconds to deliver their full impact, and Avalon provides exactly that window. Instead of trying to win instantly, this team survives the first exchange and takes over the fight after the enemy loses momentum.

Earth Spirit Totem Synergy

Avalon is an Earth Titan, so she naturally fits compositions that want extra durability and longer battles. Even in a mixed-element lineup like Brustar, Rigel, Avalon, Solaris, Tenebris, Avalon still adds Earth support utility while making your formation more stable against burst-heavy teams.

How Avalon Titan Brawl Really Works

Unlike regular Titan battles, the Avalon Brawl event enforces one rule above all others: Avalon must be present on both your attack and defense teams. That means every lineup is built around one guaranteed support Titan, and the battle often comes down to which four companions make better use of Avalon's shield.

Key Mechanics:

  • Mandatory Avalon: every team must include Avalon.
  • 5 Daily Lives: you get 5 attempts per day.
  • Brawl Coins: victories reward coins used in the event shop.
  • Shield Meta: since Avalon is always present, high-pressure damage dealers and sturdy front lines become more valuable than usual.

Because Avalon is already giving the entire team protection, aggressive but stable mixed teams tend to perform well. That is why the double tank setup with Brustar and Rigel feels so effective in this brawl: it protects your damage core long enough for Solaris and Tenebris to take control.

Best Attack Strategies

Use the Double Frontline to Reach the Mid Fight

On attack, the goal is not to explode instantly. The goal is to survive the first enemy wave and let your backline win the longer exchange. Brustar and Rigel give you exactly that structure, keeping Avalon and your super titans protected while the fight develops.

Let Brustar Absorb Key Counters

Brustar is especially important when facing teams built around Tenebris or Araji. Those Titans can snowball very quickly in Brawls, so using Brustar as one of your tanks makes the matchup much safer and reduces the chance of losing before Avalon and Solaris stabilize the battle.

Play Around Solaris and Tenebris

Your attack wins by creating a safe environment for Solaris and Tenebris. Avalon shields the team, the tanks hold the line, and your backline converts that safety into damage and utility. If the fight lasts long enough for your support and damage core to cycle properly, the matchup usually becomes favorable.

Best Defense Strategies

Mirror the Same Reliable Core

My current best defense is the same lineup I use on attack: Brustar, Rigel, Avalon, Solaris, Tenebris. In many brawls, the strongest defense is not something fancy, but the most stable composition that punishes careless attackers.

Force the Enemy Into a Slow Fight

This team is difficult to burst through because it layers protection in several ways: two tanks in front, Avalon shielding the whole team, and a backline that keeps generating value while the enemy struggles to break formation. That makes it strong on defense, where many players prefer faster and riskier offensive teams.

Protect the Damage Core at All Costs

On defense, your job is to keep Solaris and Tenebris alive long enough to decide the match. If Brustar and Rigel can delay the enemy and Avalon lands Forest Embrace on time, your defensive setup becomes much harder to crack than it first appears.

Avalon Brawls Conclusion

Right now, the strongest Avalon Brawl lineup from my tests is Brustar, Rigel, Avalon, Solaris, and Tenebris. It combines stability, counterplay against dangerous threats like Araji and Tenebris, and enough backline pressure to work on both offense and defense. A second team I would keep in mind is Eden, Solaris, Verdoc, Avalon, and Rigel, especially if you want a sturdier alternative built around more Earth synergy.

Avalon is the piece that makes the whole structure work. Without her shield, the team is just a sturdy formation. With Forest Embrace, it becomes a lineup that can absorb the first hit, hold position, and let Solaris plus Tenebris decide the battle.

As more matchups appear after the event begins, this page can be expanded with new counters and alternative teams. For now, this is the cleanest and most reliable starting point for players who want a strong Avalon Brawl setup immediately.
