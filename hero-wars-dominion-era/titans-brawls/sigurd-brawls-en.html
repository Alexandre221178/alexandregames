In the Sigurd Titan Brawls event, every battle features Sigurd on both offense and defense turning each matchup into a strategic puzzle. This guide was crafted to spark your curiosity and excitement, helping you navigate the chaos with confidence.

Sigurd, the mighty Water Tank, is famous for his powerful shield that grants five seconds of invincibility. Mastering its timing especially in manual mode can completely change the outcome of a fight. Let’s dive into how to build the strongest teams for both attack and defense in this unique event.

Note: The Sigurd Brawl runs alongside the Guardian of Balance event. Complete Guardian of Balance missions for Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins, while Titan Brawl: Sigurd gives you 5 daily lives to earn even more coins through battles.

How Sigurd Titan Brawl Really Works

Although your calendar only shows Sigurd Titan Brawl, the event actually runs in parallel with Guardian of Balance. Together, they form a unified tournament that maximizes your total rewards.

In Guardian of Balance, you complete missions to earn Event Points, which can be exchanged for Brawl Coins—the main currency needed for Sigurd Brawl purchases.

Meanwhile, Sigurd Titan Brawl grants you 5 lives per day to battle other players, requiring Sigurd in both offense and defense. Winning and defending grants additional Brawl Coins, making both tracks essential.

Titan Brawl Shop Tip for Beginners

The Titan Brawl Shop is one of your most valuable resources for powering up your Titan team. For Beginner and Intermediate players, your priority should be focused on two key areas: acquiring the rarest resources and securing Soul Stones for the best Titans.

1. Titan Potion (Top Priority): The most critical purchase for players who haven't maxed their Titans is the Titan Potion. Leveling up Titans is extremely resource-intensive, and Titan Potions are notoriously difficult to acquire in bulk. While you get a small amount daily from the Dungeon, the shop provides a necessary boost. Furthermore, leveling is crucial because some Titan abilities and effects are less effective or completely fail if the enemy Titan is a higher level than yours. Prioritizing Potions ensures your Water team, led by Sigurd, remains competitively leveled.

2. Hyperion Soul Stones: Once you have enough Titan Potions, focus your remaining currency on Hyperion Soul Stones. Hyperion is one of the essential Super Titans. While non-Super Titans (like Sigurd, Angus, Moloch, etc.) can drop Soul Stones daily in the Dungeon, Super Titan Soul Stones are only reliably acquired through rare events or Summoning Spheres. Investing in Hyperion here is a significant shortcut to obtaining one of the game’s strongest damage dealers.

3. Other Items: Other items in the shop, such as equipment for heroes, can often be acquired more easily through daily missions. While valuable, they should generally take a backseat to the Titan Potion and Super Titan Soul Stones until your core Titan team is maxed out.

How to Use Sigurd in PvP?

As a tank, Sigurd specializes in absorbing incoming damage and protecting his team. His invincibility shield is one of the strongest defensive skills in the entire Titan roster.

Whether used offensively or defensively, Sigurd provides unparalleled value in the Titan Brawl thanks to his durability and powerful presence in the front line.

Defense Strategy for the Sigurd Brawl

Water Titans are naturally weak against Earth Titans, and with the recent addition of Verdock, full Water defensive teams have become even easier to counter. To avoid giving attackers a straightforward advantage, the best strategy is to mix Sigurd with Fire Titans or other off-element picks. This creates unpredictable defenses, reduces elemental vulnerability, and forces opponents to rethink their usual counter lineups.

By diversifying your defensive lineup, you break the traditional elemental triangle and make it harder for attackers to rely on simple Earth-based strategies. Mixed-element defenses also increase the chances that your backline survives longer, allowing Sigurd’s shield window to disrupt the enemy’s timing and potentially turn the battle in your favor.

Attack Strategy for the Sigurd Brawl

If the enemy defense stacks multiple Water Titans alongside Sigurd, they will typically run at least three Water units to activate the Water Totem. In these situations, your priority should be to counter with Earth Titans Verdoc, Angus, and Eden who naturally exploit Water’s elemental weakness. Fill the remaining two slots according to the opponent’s composition to maximize pressure and survivability.

For example, if the enemy is running a setup like Sigurd, Hyperion, Nova, Brusta, and Tenebris, a strong counter would be Angus, Eden, Verdoc, Avalon, and Solaris. This lineup applies overwhelming Earth damage while maintaining support and control tools needed to break through Sigurd’s frontline and neutralize their Water-based synergy.

Best Offensive Strategies for Beating Sigurd Teams

Beating Sigurd-based teams requires exploiting their reliance on Water synergy and predictable shield timing. Prioritize Earth Titans whenever the enemy runs three or more Water units to activate their totem, and adjust your last two slots according to the opponent’s backline threats. By combining strong Earth damage with disruptive control, you can break through Sigurd’s defenses before his invincibility window can swing the battle.

Additional Tips for Sigurd Brawls

  • Adaptability is Key: Regularly update your team compositions based on the current meta and popular defensive setups you encounter.
  • Utilize Buffs and Debuffs: Heroes that can apply buffs or debuffs to enemy Water Titans can significantly sway the battle in your favor.
  • Practice Timing: Understanding when Sigurd’s shield will activate allows you to time your attacks and abilities for maximum impact.
  • Scout Opponents: Before engaging in a brawl, take note of common defensive strategies used by top players to better prepare your offensive lineup.

Sigurd Brawls Conclusion

The Sigurd Titan Brawl challenges players to think beyond standard elemental matchups, rewarding creativity and flexibility. By understanding how Water synergies work and how Sigurd’s shield affects battle timing, you gain a significant edge in both offense and defense.

Mixing elements on defense forces your opponents into harder decisions, preventing easy Earth counters and giving Sigurd more time to protect your team. Diversified lineups are the key to creating unpredictable and resilient defensive setups.

On offense, leaning into Earth Titans and adapting your composition to the enemy’s backline gives you the power to break even the toughest Water formations. With the right strategy and team structure, you can consistently outperform opponents and rise in the Sigurd Brawl rankings.
