In the Nova Titan Brawls event, every battle features Nova on both offense and defense, turning each matchup into a strategic puzzle. This guide was crafted to spark your curiosity and excitement, helping you navigate the chaos with confidence.

Nova, the versatile Marksman, positions herself at the middle line and unleashes devastating long-range attacks. Her North Wind skill launches devastating projectiles that stun enemies, making her a powerful addition to any lineup. Let's dive into how to build the strongest teams for both attack and defense in this unique event.

Note: The Nova Brawl runs alongside the Guardians of Balance event. Complete Guardians of Balance missions for Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins, while Titan Brawl: Nova gives you 5 daily lives to earn even more coins through battles.

Nova's Skills and Abilities

North Wind

Nova launches a projectile at the rearmost enemy lines, causing 643,143 damage (depends on attack) and stunning the affected targets for 4 seconds. This skill is incredibly valuable for disrupting enemy positioning and eliminating threats from the back.

Blessing of the Deep (Water Spirit Totem)

Nova's secondary ability enhances her Water affinity, providing additional support to Water-aligned teams. When combined with other Water Titans, this ability activates the Water Spirit Totem, granting team-wide benefits that amplify the effectiveness of your entire lineup.

How Nova Titan Brawl Really Works

Although your calendar only shows Nova Titan Brawl, the event actually runs in parallel with Guardians of Balance. Together, they form a unified tournament that maximizes your total rewards.

In Guardians of Balance, you complete missions to earn Event Points, which can be exchanged for Brawl Coins—the main currency needed for Nova Brawl purchases.

Meanwhile, Nova Titan Brawl grants you 5 lives per day to battle other players, requiring Nova in both offense and defense. Winning and defending grants additional Brawl Coins, making both tracks essential.

Titan Brawl Shop Tip for Beginners

The Titan Brawl Shop is one of your most valuable resources for powering up your Titan team. For Beginner and Intermediate players, your priority should be focused on two key areas: acquiring the rarest resources and securing Soul Stones for the best Titans.

1. Titan Potion (Top Priority): The most critical purchase for players who haven't maxed their Titans is the Titan Potion. Leveling up Titans is extremely resource-intensive, and Titan Potions are notoriously difficult to acquire in bulk. While you get a small amount daily from the Dungeon, the shop provides a necessary boost. Prioritizing Potions ensures your team, led by Nova, remains competitively leveled.

2. Hyperion Soul Stones: Once you have enough Titan Potions, focus your remaining currency on Hyperion Soul Stones. Hyperion is one of the essential Super Titans. While non-Super Titans (like Nova, Angus, Moloch, etc.) can drop Soul Stones daily in the Dungeon, Super Titan Soul Stones are only reliably acquired through rare events or Summoning Spheres. Investing in Hyperion here is a significant shortcut to obtaining one of the game's strongest damage dealers.

3. Other Items: Other items in the shop, such as equipment for heroes, can often be acquired more easily through daily missions. While valuable, they should generally take a backseat to the Titan Potion and Super Titan Soul Stones until your core Titan team is maxed out.

How to Use Nova in PvP?

As a Marksman, Nova specializes in dealing consistent ranged damage from the middle line. Her North Wind skill provides excellent crowd control through stunning, making her invaluable for both offensive and defensive compositions.

Whether used offensively or defensively, Nova provides excellent utility and damage output in the Titan Brawl thanks to her positioning and powerful abilities.

Defense Strategy for the Nova Brawl

Water Titans are naturally weak against Earth Titans. To avoid giving attackers a straightforward advantage, the best strategy is to mix Nova with Fire Titans or other off-element picks. This creates unpredictable defenses, reduces elemental vulnerability, and forces opponents to rethink their usual counter lineups.

By diversifying your defensive lineup, you break the traditional elemental triangle and make it harder for attackers to rely on simple Earth-based strategies. Mixed-element defenses also increase the chances that your backline survives longer, allowing Nova's ranged attacks to disrupt the enemy's timing and potentially turn the battle in your favor.

Attack Strategy for the Nova Brawl

If the enemy defense stacks multiple Water Titans alongside Nova, they will typically run at least three Water units to activate the Water Totem. In these situations, your priority should be to counter with Earth Titans Verdoc, Angus, and Eden who naturally exploit Water's elemental weakness. Fill the remaining two slots according to the opponent's composition to maximize pressure and survivability.

For example, if the enemy is running a setup like Nova, Sigurd, Hyperion, Brusta, and Tenebris, a strong counter would be Angus, Eden, Verdoc, Avalon, and Solaris. This lineup applies overwhelming Earth damage while maintaining support and control tools needed to break through their frontline and neutralize their Water-based synergy.

Best Offensive Strategies for Beating Nova Teams

Beating Nova-based teams requires exploiting their reliance on Water synergy and disrupting her ranged attacks. Prioritize Earth Titans whenever the enemy runs three or more Water units to activate their totem, and adjust your last two slots according to the opponent's backline threats. By combining strong Earth damage with disruptive control, you can break through Nova's defenses and neutralize her crowd control abilities before they can swing the battle.
