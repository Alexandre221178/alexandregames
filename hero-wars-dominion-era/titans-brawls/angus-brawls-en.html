Angus Titan Brawls are built around one core rule: Angus must be on both attack and defense. That changes the dynamics of every match, because the Titan that normally acts as an Earth frontline tank and AoE damage dealer suddenly becomes the mandatory anchor of the whole event.

Based on my current tests, the most reliable Angus Brawl lineup I found for both offense and defense is Angus, Eden, Verdoc, Nova, and Tidus. I am also tracking a second solid option with Angus, Brustar, Rigel, Solaris, and Tenebris for players who want a more mixed-element alternative. The first team maximizes Earth synergy and the Earth Spirit Totem, while the second one leans more into a stable double frontline with strong backline pressure.

Why This Team Works

Angus as the Earth tank anchor: Angus sits on the front line absorbing punishment while building up his Anger of the Earth ultimate. Once charged, he channels 8 seconds of devastating AoE damage against every enemy simultaneously — making him both the shield and the hammer of this team.

Eden as the Earth super titan: Eden synergizes directly with Angus's Earth element and contributes to activating the Earth Spirit Totem at full power. Together they form a double-Earth core that few brawl lineups can match in raw staying power.

Verdoc as the totem enabler: Verdoc unlocks the Earth Spirit Totem with just 2 Earth allies. With Angus, Eden, and Verdoc on the field, the totem runs at maximum strength — the crystal barrier absorbs up to 7.6 million damage and reflects double back at attackers.

Nova + Tidus as the support backbone: Nova provides crowd control to create windows for Angus's ultimate, while Tidus provides consistent healing that keeps the frontline standing through tough exchanges.

Why the Second Team Can Work

Brustar + Rigel as a double frontline with Angus: This triple frontline setup gives your backline maximum protection and makes it very hard for enemy burst to reach Solaris and Tenebris before they deliver full value.

Angus handles ranged AoE: Even without full Earth synergy in this lineup, Angus still delivers his massive Anger of the Earth AoE every time it charges — keeping pressure on every enemy regardless of their position.

Solaris + Tenebris as the core pressure: these two provide most of the damage and support value from the backline, while the triple frontline and Angus buy them the time they need to swing the match.

Angus's Skills and AoE Value in Brawls

Anger of the Earth

Angus channels pure earth energy and unleashes it against all enemy Titans simultaneously, dealing 409,305 damage per second for 8 seconds to every opponent at once. That is a potential total of over 3.2 million damage to each enemy. While channeling, Angus is fully immobilized — but in Brawls, where every team is forced to run him, that trade-off becomes the defining mechanic of every match.

When your lineup includes Eden and Verdoc activating the Earth Spirit Totem, Anger of the Earth becomes even more effective. The crystal barrier absorbs early burst and reflects double damage back, buying Angus the seconds he needs to reach his full channel. Three million damage hitting every enemy while they are already weakened by reflect damage consistently decides brawl outcomes.

Earth Spirit Totem Synergy

Angus is an Earth Titan, so he naturally enables the Earth Spirit Totem when paired with at least 2 other Earth allies. With Angus, Eden, and Verdoc in the same lineup, the totem activates Song of the Ancient Mountains — the crystal barrier absorbs up to 7,659,015 damage and reflects double the absorbed amount back at every attacker. In Brawls where Angus is mandatory, building the rest of your team around Earth synergy to maximize totem uptime is the most reliable path to consistent victories.

How Angus Titan Brawl Really Works

Unlike regular Titan battles, the Angus Brawl event enforces a single rule above all others: Angus must be present on both your attack and defense teams. That means every lineup is built around one guaranteed front-line tank, and the battle often comes down to which four companions make better use of Angus's AoE ultimate and Earth totem.

Key Mechanics:

  • Mandatory Angus: every team must include Angus on both attack and defense.
  • 5 Daily Lives: you get 5 attempts per day.
  • Brawl Coins: victories reward coins used in the event shop.
  • AoE Meta: since Angus is always present, teams that can survive long enough to charge and release Anger of the Earth gain a massive structural advantage over lineups built only for early burst.

Because Angus is already giving the entire team a front-line tank and a powerful AoE threat, Earth-heavy compositions that activate the totem barrier tend to perform the best. That is why the triple-Earth core of Angus, Eden, and Verdoc feels so reliable in this brawl: it protects Nova and Tidus behind a wall of crystals until Anger of the Earth decides the fight.

Best Attack Strategies

Let Angus Absorb the First Wave Before Using His Ultimate

On attack, the goal is not to burst the enemy down instantly. The goal is to let the Earth Spirit Totem absorb and reflect the opening hits, then release Anger of the Earth the moment it is charged for maximum AoE impact. Eden and Verdoc keep the totem active throughout the fight, creating a structure where the enemy team hurts itself just by attacking.

Use Nova to Set Up Angus's Channel

Angus is immobilized for the full 8 seconds of his channel, so the best windows to activate it are right after Nova lands a stun. When enemies are crowd-controlled, they cannot disrupt the channel and they cannot reposition — every second of Anger of the Earth lands on a standing, helpless target. That combination consistently produces the highest single-activation damage totals in the brawl.

Play Around the Earth Spirit Totem Reflect

Your attack wins by letting the enemy burn through the barrier and take reflected double damage before Angus even activates his ultimate. If the fight lasts long enough for the first barrier to break and Angus to channel once, the accumulated reflect damage plus AoE output usually makes the matchup very one-sided in your favor.

Best Defense Strategies

Mirror the Same Earth Core

My current best defense is the same composition I use on attack: Angus, Eden, Verdoc, Nova, and Tidus. In many brawls, the strongest defense is not a tricky formation, but the most stable one that punishes attackers who try to rush through the Earth Spirit Totem barrier.

Force the Enemy Into a Reflect Loop

This team is particularly hard to burst through on defense because it layers protection in several ways: Angus absorbs hits at the front, the Earth Spirit Totem creates a crystal barrier that reflects double damage, and Tidus heals the team between exchanges. Attackers who commit full burst into the barrier effectively deal a large portion of damage to themselves before a single Anger of the Earth fires.

Protect the AoE Channel at All Costs

On defense, Angus's 8-second channel is your most dangerous weapon. If the Earth frontline can delay the enemy long enough for Anger of the Earth to complete even once, the damage output typically decides the match. Keep Angus alive behind the totem barrier and let the reflect mechanic soften attackers before the channel lands.

Angus Brawl Counters and Threats

Because Angus is an Earth Titan, the main threat in every brawl matchup is enemy Fire Titans. Fire beats Earth in the elemental triangle, so any team that runs heavy Fire in the opponent's 4 companion slots has a structural advantage over Angus's Earth core.

Key Fire Threats to Watch:

  • Araji: Araji's burst can shatter through the Earth Spirit Totem barrier faster than most other Titans. The reflect helps, but Araji's explosive output can still break through both layers rapidly. Prioritize Earth Crown artifact to reduce incoming Fire damage in these matchups.
  • Ignis: Ignis deals sustained Fire damage that accumulates a large elemental disadvantage over longer fights. Time Anger of the Earth against Ignis and her allies together to prevent the sustained damage from becoming overwhelming.
  • Moloch: Moloch's stun compounds the Fire threat by interrupting Anger of the Earth before the channel completes. Try to activate the channel before Moloch's stun lands — getting it started forces him to either wait or lose to the ongoing damage.
  • Asherona: Asherona and Pyro together provide concentrated Fire pressure from multiple sources. The Earth Spirit Totem reflect is most punishing here since the coordinated burst hits the barrier all at once.

Counter Tip: When facing Fire-heavy Angus Brawl compositions, the Earth Spirit Totem's reflect mechanic is your best tool. Enemies who dump burst into the barrier receive double the absorbed damage back. Combining that reflect window with a well-timed Anger of the Earth usually recovers the elemental disadvantage and turns the fight around.

Angus Brawls Conclusion

Right now, the strongest Angus Brawl lineup from my tests is Angus, Eden, Verdoc, Nova, and Tidus. It combines Earth totem activation, AoE channel pressure from Anger of the Earth, crowd control windows from Nova, and reliable healing from Tidus — covering both offense and defense with the same structure. A second team I would keep in mind is Angus, Brustar, Rigel, Solaris, and Tenebris, especially if you want a more mixed-element option with a stronger double frontline and proven backline pair.

Angus is the piece that makes the whole structure work. Without his AoE channel, the team is just a durable Earth formation. With Anger of the Earth landing once per fight on every enemy simultaneously, it becomes a lineup that consistently closes out matches before the opponent recovers.

As more matchups appear after the event begins, this page can be expanded with new counters and alternative teams. For now, this is the cleanest and most reliable starting point for players who want a strong Angus Brawl setup immediately.
