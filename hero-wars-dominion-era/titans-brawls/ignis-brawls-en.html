In the Ignis Titan Brawls event, every battle features Ignis on both offense and defense, demanding sharp strategic thinking to come out on top. This guide was created to help you build the strongest teams and maximize your win rate in every encounter.

Ignis, the blazing support Titan, fights at the back line and empowers allies with her fire-fueled abilities. Her Burning Blood skill boosts the Attack of all allied Titans, turning your entire team into a relentless offensive force. With the arrival of the Summoner Titans — Asherona and Araji — the best Ignis Brawl teams have changed significantly, centering around explosive Fire synergy.

Best Teams for Ignis Brawl – New Summoner Era

Note: Ignis is mandatory on both attack and defense in this brawl.

Team 1: Ignis, Tenebris, Araji, Asherona, Moloch (Best all-round team)

Team 2: Ignis, Tenebris, Araji, Asherona, Brustar (Strong alternative with 2 totems)

Team 3: Ignis, Araji, Verdoc, Asherona, Moloch (Strong alternative with Verdoc)

Note: The Ignis Brawl runs alongside the Guardians of Balance event. Complete Guardians of Balance missions for Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins, while Titan Brawl: Ignis gives you 5 daily lives to earn even more coins through battles.

Ignis's Skills and Abilities

Burning Blood

Ignis ignites the fighting spirit of all allies, increasing the Attack of every allied Titan by 567,126 (scales with Ignis's Attack) for 6 seconds. This ability is the cornerstone of her role: by dramatically boosting team-wide Attack, Burning Blood turns every other Titan on your team into a far more dangerous threat. When timed with heavy hitters like Araji or Asherona, the result is devastating burst damage.

Age of Flames (Fire Spirit Totem)

Ignis's secondary ability channels her firepower into the Fire Spirit Totem, granting powerful Fire-type bonuses to the entire allied team. When paired with at least 2 other Fire Titans, the Fire Spirit Totem activates, dealing area flame damage to enemies while enhancing all Fire Titan abilities. This totem synergy is what makes Fire teams so dangerous — especially when Asherona is present, who counts as two Fire Titans for energy generation purposes.

Key Allies: Why Araji and Asherona Transform Ignis Teams

Araji – The Fire Sharpshooter

Araji's Incinerating Ray shoots a focused stream of fire at the nearest enemy for 7 seconds, dealing 1,308,574 damage per second (scales with Attack). This makes Araji one of the highest single-target DPS Titans in the game. Her second skill, Herald of Flame, speeds up all allies by 30% for 8 seconds, accelerating skill activation across the board.

When Ignis activates Burning Blood and boosts all allies' Attack, Araji's Incinerating Ray becomes even more lethal. The combination of a massive per-second damage output plus the 30% speed boost makes Araji-Ignis one of the strongest duos in the Brawl.

Asherona – The Pyromancer Summoner

Asherona's core ability — Pyromania — summons Pyro, a fiery elemental that deals massive area damage the moment he appears. When Pyro lands, he instantly hits all nearby enemies with an explosion based on Asherona's Attack power, making it a powerful opener, especially in PvP where early pressure matters.

After spawning, Pyro continuously applies Smoldering Brand every 10 seconds to enemies close to him. This mark behaves like a delayed fire trap: when another Titan hits a branded enemy, the mark explodes and deals additional area damage. These chained explosions make Asherona extremely strong in PvP, where several Titans attack different targets simultaneously.

Pyro's survivability scales with Asherona — his Health is a percentage of her maximum Health. He cannot be targeted directly, but whenever Asherona takes damage, Pyro mirrors that loss. Protecting Asherona keeps Pyro active longer. This ability is one of the strongest area-damage tools among all Titans due to its repeated detonations.

Spiritual Bond: Fire

This ability merges Asherona's and Pyro's elemental energy, causing them to count as two Fire Titans for Fire Totem activation. Think of it as a totem accelerator: it helps your Fire Totem fill its energy bar much faster in PvP. Since the Fire Totem deals global area damage and burns enemies when activated, triggering it before your opponent gives a massive advantage. Because of this, Asherona is not only a damage dealer — she speeds up your entire Fire team's elemental power, making her an essential piece of the modern Ignis Brawl lineup.

How Ignis Titan Brawl Really Works

Unlike regular Titan battles, the Ignis Brawl event enforces a key rule: Ignis must be present on both your attack and defense teams. This creates a fascinating meta where every team composition revolves around amplifying Ignis's offensive buffs while picking the right four companions to dominate the opposition.

Key Mechanics:

  • Mandatory Ignis: Every team must include Ignis — no exceptions.
  • 5 Daily Lives: You get 5 attempts per day to battle other players.
  • Brawl Coins: Win battles to earn coins that can be exchanged for valuable rewards.
  • Summoner Meta: With the arrival of Asherona (and Araji), Fire teams got a massive upgrade. Classic teams are still valid, but Summoner teams dominate the top tier.

The key to success lies in building around Ignis's Burning Blood: time it perfectly with Araji's Incinerating Ray and Asherona's Pyro to unleash devastating burst sequences that opponents cannot survive.

Best Attack Strategies

Fire Summoner Burst (Top Meta)

Stack Ignis + Asherona + Araji + Moloch + Tenebris for maximum burst damage. Ignis's Burning Blood fires up Araji's Incinerating Ray and amplifies Asherona's Pyro explosion. Moloch provides frontline protection, while Tenebris adds control. This combo is nearly unstoppable when executed correctly.

Fire Element Synergy

Three or more Fire Titans (Ignis counts as one, Asherona counts as two via Spiritual Bond) activate the Fire Spirit Totem early, dealing global fire damage before the enemy can respond. Prioritize reaching the Totem activation threshold as quickly as possible to apply pressure from turn one.

Timing Burning Blood

Ignis's Burning Blood lasts only 6 seconds — use it precisely when your heavy hitters are ready to unleash their ultimates. Coordinating it with Araji's Incinerating Ray or Asherona's Pyro landing moment multiplies your burst potential enormously.

Best Defense Strategies

Fortified Fire Frontline

Since attackers must also run Ignis, they'll be looking to mirror your setup. Place a strong tank like Moloch at the frontline to absorb punishment while Ignis empowers your backline attackers with Burning Blood. Moloch's survivability buys Ignis the time needed to cast her buff reliably.

Asherona's Pyro as Ambush Defender

Pyro's Smoldering Brand creates a passive threat zone for attackers who advance too quickly. Even on defense, a well-positioned Asherona paired with Araji can melt through attacking teams that underestimate the deferred explosion mechanics of Smoldering Brand.

Counter-Element Flexibility

While Fire teams are dominant, mixing in Tenebris (Dark) gives your defense tools to neutralize Earth or Light counters. Don't over-commit to a single element on defense — a mixed lineup keeps opponents guessing.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Neglecting Ignis's Development

Since Ignis is mandatory, every opponent runs her too — so a higher-level Ignis is a direct competitive advantage. Upgrading her Attack directly increases the Burning Blood buff value, making your entire team hit harder. Don't treat her as a throwaway slot.

Ignoring the Fire Totem Threshold

The Fire Spirit Totem requires multiple Fire Titans to activate. With Asherona counting as two, reaching the threshold is easier than ever — but only if you build with Fire synergy in mind. Mixing too many off-element Titans delays or prevents Totem activation entirely.

Poor Timing of Burning Blood

Ignis's Burning Blood lasts only 6 seconds. Using it before your heavy hitters are primed to attack wastes most of the buff window. Learn to synchronize it with Araji's Incinerating Ray activation for maximum burst efficiency.

Conclusion

The Ignis Titan Brawl is one of the most exciting events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, especially since the arrival of Summoner Titans dramatically shifted the meta. With Asherona and Araji joining the fray, Fire teams now have an unprecedented explosive potential that rewards players who understand burst timing and Totem mechanics.

Whether you are running the new Summoner meta or relying on classic Ignis lineups, the key principles remain the same: time Burning Blood correctly, protect your backline, and use elemental synergies to outpace the opposition. Keep experimenting, study opponent patterns, and adapt your lineup accordingly.

Good luck in the Brawl — and may Ignis's flames lead you to victory!
