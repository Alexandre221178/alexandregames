Hero Wars: Dominion Era Calendar

Special Reward Events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Demon Dolls

Get a demon doll for every 35 energy spent in the campaign. Open the Demon Dolls you defeat and get unexpected prizes.

Average rewards for 100 initial openings:

Emerald Waterfalls

Spend and buy emeralds to receive rewards. The more emeralds you spend or buy, the better the rewards you get!

How to find the Emerald Waterfalls menu: Go to "Special Event" and look in the left menu until you find it.

Secret Wealth

Complete tasks to receive chests with random rewards. The more tasks you complete, the better the rewards you get!

Secret Wealth A shop that appears a few days a month. You can pay with emeralds and pet potions. The offers are random and change three times a day. It is the best source of choice crystals and insignias for ascension, and the only use of pet potions once you leveled up your pets. Those pet potions can notably be traded for orange artifacts, titan artifact spheres and titan summoning spheres.

Where to find the Secret Wealth shop: It appears a few days a month in the "Merchant" menu.

Piggy Bank

A piggy bank that reimburses up to 20% of the emeralds you spent during the event, after it concludes. Reoccurs a few days a month. A great opportunity to level up your totem.

Emeralds Spent Cashback Percentage
0 - 4,999 5%
5,000 - 19,999 10%
20,000 - 39,999 15%
40,000+ 20%

Emerald Sale

During special events, you can buy emeralds with a x4 or x5 bonus multiplier. This is the best time to buy emeralds if you plan to spend them on events like the Emerald Waterfalls or other in-game purchases.

Fluffy's Preorder

Before Fluffy's main event begins, players can preorder Fluffy Soul Stones packages. This gives you a head start in collecting the resources needed to unlock and upgrade Fluffy when the event officially launches.

Tip: Check the preorder offers carefully to maximize value before the event starts.

Fluffy's Event

The Fluffy Event allows players to earn Fluffy Soul Stones through missions, chests, and special event activities. Complete event quests to collect enough stones to unlock Fluffy or upgrade her star level.

Strategy: Focus on completing daily missions and event milestones to maximize your Fluffy Soul Stone rewards. F2P players can reach up to 6★ by completing all available tasks!

Time to Love Event

The Time to Love Event is a special event where players can participate in various activities to earn unique rewards box with heros soul stones. Engage in event-specific missions and challenges to collect exclusive items and bonuses.

Tip: You can choose the soul stones inside the chests as your rewards during the Time to Love Event make sure to verify Alexandre Games Hero Wars Tier List before make yous choose.

Wind from the West Event

"Wind from the West" is a limited-time event featuring wind-themed missions and challenges. Complete event quests, and open event chests to obtain exclusive rewards such as hero soul stones and Lunar Skin.

Tip: When choosing soul stones from event chests, consult the Alexandre Games Hero Wars Tier List to prioritize the best heroes for your roster.
