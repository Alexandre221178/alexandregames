During Cyber Con, players can participate in a series of themed missions that reward Cyber Crystals, Energy, Gold, Hero resources, and fragments for exclusive Cyber Chests. These missions refresh daily, allowing both casual and competitive players to progress steadily throughout the event.

This event also introduces limited-time Cyber Skins, available through special bundles or event chests. These skins not only give your heroes a futuristic look but also provide powerful stat boosts, making them highly valuable for long-term account progression.

What Is the Cyber Con Event?

Cyber Con is a special annual event that takes place at the end of November and lasts for six full days. During this time, the Dominion is transformed into a neon-soaked cyberworld filled with exclusive missions, limited-time rewards, and futuristic cosmetics.

Players can complete event tasks to unlock valuable resources, earn cyber-themed bonuses, and collect unique Cyber Skins released only during this celebration. Whether you're here for power, style, or pure chaos, Cyber Con is one of the most rewarding events of the year.

Cyber Con - Forgotten Treasury

The Forgotten Treasury is a special screen where players can open value cells to claim rewards. Your main objective is to uncover the Grand Prizes hidden among the cells. Each attempt requires a Chip Key — simply click a locked cell to use one.

Even though the game lets you preview all cells temporarily, once the Treasury is closed, the cell positions are shuffled. Because of that, it is impossible to memorize where the Grand Prizes are located, making every attempt a new challenge.

After finding at least one Grand Prize, you gain access to the Refresh button, which rerolls all prizes and starts a new layout of the Treasury with fresh rewards.

Event Conclusion

Cyber Con is a high-value event packed with rewarding quests, Chip Keys, and opportunities to unlock exclusive prizes inside the Forgotten Treasury. By completing daily missions, managing your resources, and refreshing wisely after securing Grand Prizes, players can significantly boost their progress. Whether you're aiming for rare materials, currency, or collectibles, this event offers excellent returns for both free-to-play and paying players.
