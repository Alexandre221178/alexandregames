The Importance of Event Resources & How to Maximize Your Gains

Winterfest is one of the most profitable events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, offering multiple categories of high-value resources that accelerate both Titan progression and Hero development. Understanding the purpose of each reward and how to efficiently earn them is essential to getting the maximum return on your daily activity.

Resources such as Titan Resource Chests, Hero Resource Chests, Wish Crystals, and Winterfest Baubles all play a critical role in strengthening your account and improving long-term growth. Below is a quick explanation of why each one matters:

  • Titan Resource Chest: Allows you to choose rewards such as Gold, Titan Potions, Titan Skin Stones, Essence of the Elements, Elemental Tournament Coins, Artifact Seal Chests, and Gleaming Seal Chests. These items directly boost Titan levels, skins, and artifacts—core components for Dungeon power and Guild Wars.
  • Hero Resource Chest: Provides flexible hero progression materials like Bottled Energy, Gold, EXP Potions, Skin Stone Chests, Chaos Cores, Rare Enchantment Runes, Wish Insignias, and Absolute Artifact Chests. These accelerate your hero upgrades across every system: skins, glyphs, ascension, and artifacts.
  • Wish Crystals: Can be exchanged for Random Crystals, Vibrant Crystals, or Radiant Crystals. These crystals are extremely important because they are used to unlock and level Ascension Skill Nodes, making them one of the most impactful progression resources in the game.
  • Winterfest Baubles: Earned by completing event quests and used to decorate your guild’s Winterfest Tree. Higher tree levels provide bonus boxes, festive buffs, and a large number of rewards shared with all guild members.

Tips to Improve Your Event Performance

  • Do all daily quests: Many missions are repeatable every day and represent the bulk of Baubles and Crystals gained.
  • Spend Emeralds strategically: Only invest where you get multiple mission progressions at once (Energy bundles, Outland refreshes, Artifact Chest x10, etc.).
  • Pre-save resources: Hoarding Energy Bottles, Outland Openings, Titan Potions, and Emeralds before the event massively increases total rewards.
  • Multiply your gains with synergy: Some missions overlap—example: buying Emeralds → energy bundles → more quests completed.
  • Never claim rewards out of order: Always check if a claim will complete multiple tiers at once before collecting.
  • Coordinate with your guild: More Bauble donations raise the Winterfest Tree level faster, improving everyone’s rewards.
  • Save Wish Crystals until the end: You may receive more from late-event missions, allowing for bigger exchange batches.
