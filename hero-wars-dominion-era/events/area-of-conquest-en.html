The Area of Conquest has arrived in Hero Wars: Dominion Era! For four intense days, guilds will face off in thrilling cross-server PvP battles, conquer strategic settlements, and gather valuable resources.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to make the most of this competitive event: from requirements and mechanics to strategies and rewards. Whether you’re a guild leader or a new recruit, your choices can decide victory or defeat.

What Makes the Area of Conquest Special?

Unlike other events, this one puts your guild’s coordination to the test. With four guilds of similar power matched together, every strategy counts. Conquering and holding settlements rewards your team, but only the most united guilds can secure victory.

Prepare your heroes, rally your guildmates, and dive into the battle for dominance. Will your guild rise as conquerors, or fall behind in the fight for resources?

General Information – Understanding the Basics

The Area of Conquest can feel a bit overwhelming at first, but don’t worry I’ll explain it in a simple way so that even if you don’t have much RPG experience, you’ll be able to follow along and enjoy the event.

The main goal is to help your guild collect something called Superior Coins. Think of them as special points that your whole guild earns together. When enough coins are gathered, your guild’s Castle Level goes up. Every time the Castle Level increases, all guild members receive rewards. That means your effort helps not only you but your entire team!

How do you get these coins? By capturing settlements on the map, winning battles, and opening treasure chests. Sometimes there’s also a smaller event inside the Area of Conquest that gives extra Superior Coins.

To keep things fair, the system puts four guilds of similar strength on each map. Only guilds created before the event starts can join. So you’re always competing against groups that are not too much stronger or weaker than yours.

Capturing Settlements and Chests

The map is made up of tiles. To start earning coins from a settlement, your guild needs to capture its tile. Once it’s yours, the settlement produces coins for your guild every minute. Some settlements give a small amount, while others give a lot more so it’s worth going after the most valuable ones.

Treasure Chests work a little differently. When your hero stands on a chest tile, you open it, and the rewards go to the entire guild. After being collected, chests will appear again in random places, so it’s a good idea to keep an eye on the map.

Moving and Action Points (AP)

Every move or action you take on the map uses something called Action Points, or AP for short. Think of AP as your energy. Without it, your hero can’t move or fight. The good news is that AP slowly recovers over time, so even if you run out, you don’t have to wait too long to play again.

If you don’t have enough AP right now, you can still plan your hero’s path across the map. Your hero will automatically continue moving when you get your AP back. And if you change your mind, you can cancel the path at any time by clicking on the tile where you want to stop.

Battles on the Map

When it comes to fighting, things work a little differently than in normal battles. Your heroes don’t automatically heal between fights, so if they lose health, they’ll stay weak until you replace them with a fresh team or until the battle ends.

To start a battle, your hero needs to be on a tile right next to an enemy. Then you can confirm the attack, and the fight will begin. If your team loses, your heroes are teleported back to the guild’s castle. If you win, you’ll capture the enemy’s settlement and begin earning Superior Coins from it instead.

Remember: even if you’re new, your effort matters! Every capture and every chest opened helps your guild progress toward better rewards. It’s all about teamwork.

Road to the Top – Seasonal Rewards Progression

Leveling up your rewards in the Area of Conquest event is simple and exciting! Complete seasonal quests to earn season experience, which increases your reward level. At each level, you can claim exclusive rewards, including Superior Coins, Health Potions, Speed Boosters, and Hero Upgrade Chests. The more quests you complete, the higher your reward level, so make sure to participate daily and maximize your gains!

Battle for Territories – Guild Ranking Rewards

In the Area of Conquest event, your guild’s ranking determines the rewards every member receives. Gather resources and earn Superior Coins on the map to climb the rankings! Remember: only Coins collected on the map count towards your guild’s position.

The higher your guild ranks, the better the rewards. Top guilds can earn Chest of Patterns, which contains war flag patterns. You can choose the specific reward when opening the chest. Additionally, Golden Thread is awarded, which is used to upgrade war flag patterns. Obtain Golden Thread by unraveling patterns and maximize your guild’s strategic advantage!

Conclusion – Master the Area of Conquest

The Area of Conquest event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era is a unique opportunity for both new and experienced players to earn incredible rewards while enjoying exciting cross-server PvP battles. By completing missions, gathering Superior Coins, and coordinating with your guildmates, you can level up your Castle, claim exclusive seasonal rewards, and unlock powerful items like Health Potions, Speed Boosters, Hero Upgrade Chests, and war flag patterns.

Remember to participate daily, manage your Action Points wisely, and focus on capturing settlements to maximize your coin earnings. The guild ranking system encourages teamwork, and higher-ranking guilds receive the best rewards, including Chests of Patterns and Golden Thread to upgrade your war flags. No matter your experience level, strategic planning and persistence will help you climb the ranks and fully enjoy everything the event has to offer.

Stay active, support your guild, and aim for the top – your efforts in the Area of Conquest will be rewarded generously!
