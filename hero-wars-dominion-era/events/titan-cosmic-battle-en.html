The Titan Cosmic Battle is a cross-server PvP event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era where players battle each other using their Titans at current power. Instead of the regular Titan Battles, this event puts your whole Titan roster to the test in league-based matchmaking across multiple servers.

Adapted from the Heroes' Cosmic Battle, this event replaces heroes with Titans. You start with 5 attacks per day and recover 1 attack per hour (up to 5 maximum), so logging in frequently is key. Certain Titans are banned each round following a community vote, keeping the meta fresh every time the event runs.

How Titan Cosmic Battle Works

General Rules

Titan Cosmic Battle is a cross-server event where players compete against each other using Titans at their current power level. Players advance to higher-tier leagues by earning points — the higher you climb, the greater the victory rewards and the final event reward.

Banned Titans

You can pick any of your Titans except those voted out by the community on the day before the event starts. Banned Titans display a lock icon on their avatars and cannot be used for the entire duration of the event. The same Titans cannot be banned in consecutive events, which keeps the meta rotating every month.

Joining the Event

To participate, press the Defense button and assemble your defense team. Once your defense is set, you officially join the event and other similarly-ranked players can attack you. Matchmaking then becomes available.

Attacking

Press Find to get matched against an opponent of similar ranking. Press Attack to assemble your attack team and enter the battlefield. You start with 5 attacks and recover 1 attack per hour (capped at 5). Log in frequently to maximize your battles throughout the day.

Win Streak

Consecutive wins earn you an additional prize. The more wins you rack up, the higher your win-streak level — and the more valuable the bonus reward. Your win streak resets when you lose a battle.

Retrying a Battle

If you lose a defensive battle, press Retry to make a comeback. Retrying will not end your win streak or drop your ranking.

Strategic Tip

In theory, losing all matches on day one and then securing a ×4 win-streak bonus the next day sounds efficient. However, for strong players targeting the highest division (which requires around 35 wins, plus inevitable losses), this approach wastes too much climbing time. If you're aiming for the top, play consistently from the start rather than intentionally losing.

Rewards

All battle rewards and the final ranking rewards include Cosmic Battle Coins that can be spent in the event shop. The top 50 players receive extra coins as a bonus at the end of the event.

Titan Cosmic Battle Event Shop

Beginners should focus on collecting Cosmic Battle Coins to exchange for valuable items in the event shop.

Prioritize items that will help you progress your main titan team in the event and strengthen your team. The hardest items are Titans Potion, Titan Skin Stone and Essence of the Elements.

Conclusion

Titan Cosmic Battle is one of the most exciting cross-server competitions in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. By assembling a strong defense team, managing your attacks throughout the day, completing all available missions, and keeping your win streak alive, you can climb the leagues and earn a wealth of Cosmic Battle Coins to spend in the event shop. Stay active, plan your battles wisely, and enjoy the cosmic rivalry!
