The Emerald City event is one of the most rewarding limited-time challenges in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. It mixes strategy, resource management, and a bit of luck—inviting players to push forward for bigger prizes. If you’ve ever wondered how far your emeralds can take you, this guide will show you every advantage hidden inside the event.

During Emerald City, quests refresh the moment you complete them, letting you chain rewards quickly. By spending emeralds, you earn Emerald Experience, a special event currency that fills your event pass. The more Emerald Experience you collect, the higher you climb—and the better the rewards become.

There is also a paid track available for players who want an extra boost, offering premium rewards on top of the free ones. Whether you’re a casual player or a heavy spender, this event offers valuable opportunities for progression.

Emerald City Quests Overview

Quests refresh immediately after completion, allowing you to chain rewards throughout the event. Simply press the button to begin each task. Almost all quests involve spending emeralds in specific game features, which then reward you with Emerald Experience — the key resource for leveling your event pass.

Atrium Summons (Emeralds)

Perform summons in the Atrium using emeralds to earn fast Emerald Experience. This is one of the most efficient ways to progress early in the event.

  • Reward: x10 → 600 Emerald Experience

Costs:

  • Summon x1 — 200 Emeralds
  • Summon x10 — 1500 Emeralds

Opening Outland Chests

Opening Outland Chests during the event provides solid value, especially because early openings cost fewer emeralds.

  • Reward: x10 → 1200 Emerald Experience

Costs:

  • Open Outland Chest x1 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x2 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x3 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x4 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x5 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x6 — 90 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x7 — 200 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x8 — 200 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x9 — 200 Emeralds
  • Open Outland Chest x10 — 200 Emeralds

Tip: Open 6 Outland Chests per day. Each costs only 90 emeralds. After the sixth chest, the cost spikes to 200 emeralds — making additional openings inefficient.

Astral Seer (Emerald Use)

Using the Astral Seer is another important quest that rewards a high amount of Emerald Experience.

  • Reward: x10 → 1200 Emerald Experience

Costs:

  • Open Astral Seer x1 — 350 Emeralds
  • Open Astral Seer x10 — 3000 Emeralds

Opening an Artifact Chest

Artifact Chests are a good option if you want to improve both heroes and event progress at the same time.

Opening Titan Artifact Spheres

Titan players benefit greatly from this quest, as it contributes to Titan progress while boosting event levels.

Costs:

Circle of Titan Summoning

Another Titan-oriented quest focused on summoning in the Circle of Summoning using emeralds.

Pet Summoning

Pet Summoning quests provide some of the highest Emerald Experience rewards in the event. Great for players focused on pet teams or long-term account power.

  • Reward: x10 → 1000 Emerald Experience

Costs:

  • Pet Summoning x10 — 2500 Emeralds

Strategies for F2P, Low Spenders, and VIP Players

The Emerald City event rewards every type of player, but your approach should change depending on your spending level. Below are optimized strategies for F2P, low spenders, and VIP players to help you maximize Emerald Experience and get the most value out of the event.

F2P Strategy (Free-to-Play)

F2P players must focus on efficiency above all. Since emeralds are limited, avoid high-cost tasks and prioritize activities that give the best experience-per-emerald ratio.

  • Open exactly 6 Outland Chests per day. The first six cost 90 emeralds each; beyond that, the price jumps to 200, which is not efficient.
  • Avoid Astral Seer x1 openings. The cost (350 emeralds) is too high for the reward. Save for x10 openings if possible.
  • Skip Pet Summons and Titan Sphere 100x. These are expensive and only worthwhile for players with larger budgets.
  • Save emeralds before the event. The event lasts a few days; spending everything on day one is not required.
  • Focus on Artifact Chest x10 if you are close to upgrading a key hero artifact.

F2P players can comfortably reach a good portion of the free event pass, especially if they follow the daily Outland strategy. It may take discipline, but the rewards add up quickly.

Important Notice: The current Emerald City event offers poor value for most players. The rewards are not very attractive for the amount of emeralds required, and the $29 paid pass provides limited return. Spend only what you feel comfortable with, and consider saving your emeralds for future events with better offers and higher efficiency.

Low Spender Strategy

Low spenders have more flexibility and can aim to complete a larger percentage of the event pass. The goal is to combine efficient emerald spending with one or two premium tasks.

  • Activate the paid track if the rewards match your goals (skins, resources, hero upgrades, or exclusive items).
  • Perform Atrium Summons x10 for better value than individual pulls.
  • Use Astral Seer x10 at least once. This gives excellent Emerald Experience and helps progress the event significantly.
  • Consider Pet Summoning x10 if you already invest in pets — this task gives one of the highest experience rewards.
  • Continue the F2P Outland strategy for optimal daily value.

Low spenders can usually finish the entire free track and reach a large part of the paid track without overspending, especially if they distribute their emerald use across the event days.

VIP / Big Spender Strategy

VIP players can complete the event quickly and take advantage of the best-value multipacks. The key here is not scarcity, but maximizing overall account growth during the event.

  • Buy the paid pass early to claim bonus rewards as you progress.
  • Use Astral Seer x10 multiple times. This is one of the strongest tasks for Emerald Experience generation.
  • Summon Pets x10 consistently. Pet progress is slow without emeralds, so VIP players benefit greatly from this.
  • Open Titan Artifact Sphere x100 if you focus on Titan improvements.
  • Do Atrium Summons x10 until you reach your desired Soul Stone or hero progress thresholds.
  • Finish Outland every day (full 10 chests). While only the first six are cheap, VIP players can afford the extra chests for bonus progress.

VIP players can realistically finish the event in 1–2 days if desired, but spreading the spending across all event days ensures consistent value and avoids missing any daily resets.

Conclusion

Emerald City Conclusion - Bad Event or Not!

The Emerald City event is currently very expensive for F2P and even for regular players, especially considering that the rewards are not particularly attractive. The paid pass costs around $29 and offers limited return for its price. If you choose to spend emeralds, do so carefully—only invest what you can afford and wait for better-value offers that often appear in future events.
