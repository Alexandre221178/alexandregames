The Hero Event Awakening in Hero Wars: Dominion Era is one of the best opportunities to strengthen a newly released hero while collecting a huge amount of valuable rewards. These introductory hero events are considered among the most generous events in the game, offering players access to powerful resources, exclusive shop rewards, and even the chance to obtain a Totem Sphere of their choice with relatively low spending compared to other events.

Convert Energy Coins and Altar of Fate

Free-to-play players should prioritize using the Energy Exchange inside the Altar of Fate to convert Energy Coins into Valor Coins. This remains one of the safest ways to build toward the event hero 6 stars without relying too much on the most expensive parts of the event.

The Event Workshop: Upgrade Priorities

The Workshop is still the center of efficient progress. Start with the Tome of Greed to improve Valor Coin efficiency. After that, move into the Grail of Generosity to reduce conversion costs. The overall logic is the same: better efficiency first, cheaper Altar costs second.

If you are not F2P, saving part of your Energy Exchange for the final days can still be the best approach. That gives you more flexibility to decide between extra Altar pulls, Workshop upgrades, or chapter progression once your totals are clear.

Strategy Tip: Maximize the Tome of Greed First

Always upgrade the Tome of Greed first. Even at low levels it reduces Energy Crystal pressure, and at higher levels the coin efficiency becomes much stronger. Lower Altar costs and increased conversion value let you farm Heroes' Soul Stones much faster.

The Altar of Fate is where most Valor Coins should be spent. Each 10x pull offers chances to obtain Heroes' Soul Stones and other high-value rewards. Avoid single pulls unless you need a very specific result. Batch openings remain more efficient.

Embers of the Past - Heroes Event Chapters' Rewards

If you manage to spend Boosters to advance through the event chapters, you can collect all rewards but F2P players should focus only on Chapter I and Chapter II, since these do not require any Boosters to complete. This strategy allows you to secure the most valuable prize of the entire event: Heroes at 6★.

Titan Soul Stones are far rarer and harder to obtain than the Campaign resources you would get from later chapters using Boosters. Even with perfect play, F2P players cannot realistically reach the final grand prize the Absolute Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere. Therefore, prioritize Chapters I and II to maximize value without wasting resources, and save your Boosters for future events or strategic Campaign progression.

Battle Pass: Is It Worth It?

The Battle Pass Heroes' event provides strong value for both F2P and paying players. The free tier already helps with Soul Stones, Energy Crystals, and general upgrade materials. Premium value depends mostly on how close you want to get to 6 stars during the event itself.

Recommendation: F2P players should still complete all free Battle Pass levels. Light spenders can consider the Premium tier if they want to accelerate Heroes' evolution and reduce pressure on Altar pull luck.

What to Buy in the Event Shop

In the event shop, focus first on items that help you complete event missions efficiently. After that, prioritize Heroes-specific materials, Soul Stones, and anything that supports their artifacts, glyphs, and survivability.

Shopping Priority List

  1. Elemental Spirit Summoning Sphere – Top priority for summoning powerful Titan Totems.
  2. Primal and Elemental Catalysts – Essential resources for upgrading Titan Totems.
  3. Gift of the Dominion – This red sphere is always one of the best purchases. It is mainly used to promote heroes to higher red ranks, especially if your main heroes are not fully upgraded yet.
  4. Legendary Gift of the Dominion – This orange sphere is useful for upgrading heroes at Orange rank progression.
  5. Glyph Resources – Glyphs are important for completing event quests and improving overall hero strength. Prioritize Magic Attack, Magic Penetration, Armor, Magic Defense, and Strength glyphs.
  6. Skin Materials – Helpful for boosting Strength and Armor through Heroes’ skins.
  7. Flexible Account Resources – Only purchase additional resources after securing the main event priorities and upgrading the featured hero.

Since Hero Soul Stones are earned directly through the event itself, it is usually better to spend shop currency on long-term progression resources that continue to provide value even after the event ends.

Mistakes to Avoid to Ensure your Hero Reaches 6⭐

Reaching 6 stars with your hero requires careful resource management and realistic planning. Avoid these common mistakes:

Critical Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Spending Valor Coins on low-value items - prioritize Soul Stone progress and core upgrades
  • Upgrading Workshop relics in the wrong order - Tome of Greed still comes before Grail of Generosity
  • Using single Altar pulls - 10x pulls remain more efficient
  • Spreading resources across multiple heroes - focus on the event hero during the event window
  • Ignoring free Battle Pass rewards - they meaningfully reduce pressure on the rest of the event
  • Underestimating Gold costs - the event hero still needs heavy Gold investment to reach full evolution
  • Skipping daily missions - steady daily progress matters more than a late panic push
  • Buying premium shop items before essentials - secure the event hero's progress first, luxury second

Resource Management Tips:

Start preparing before the event begins by saving Gold, Energy Crystals, artifact materials, and general enhancement resources. The event hero scales well in long fights, so investing properly in them is not just event progress — it improves your roster after the event too.

💡 Alexandre's Success Strategy

Getting a new hero like the event hero to six stars as a free-to-play player requires patience and planning, but it is absolutely possible with the right approach.

Save plenty of Gold before the event, collect Energy Crystals for Workshop upgrades, and hoard Valor Coins for Altar summons. Most importantly, avoid spending your best resources on other heroes during the event. If the event hero is your target, make them the center of your plan

Free-to-Play Path to Six Stars

F2P players should complete the earliest mission stages first, repeat hero-focused progress missions when available, max the Tome of Greed before pushing the Grail too hard, and use Altar of Fate 10x pulls. Prioritize Heroes Soul Stones above all other optional rewards.

Minimum Artifact and Equipment Requirements

To complete event missions more comfortably, aim for these minimum hero goals:

  • Artifacts: steady upgrades to the three main artifacts, with Ring of Strength and Manuscript of the Void as strong priorities
  • Skills: keep all four skills leveled, especially Roar of Nature and Guardian Spirit
  • Hero Level: raise the event hero consistently so healing and scaling stay relevant
  • Equipment: maintain rank progression so their stats and mission milestones move together

These upgrades are enough to keep event progress moving while also improving the event hero's real usefulness in Arena, Campaign, and long-form fights.

Conclusion

The Awakening Hero Event is one of the best opportunities in Hero Wars: Dominion Era to unlock and evolve the event hero, a healer who becomes more threatening the longer the fight goes. By prioritizing Valor Coins, respecting the Tome of Greed upgrade order, and focusing on the event hero's growth, you can make this event far more efficient.

The event hero is not just another support unit. Their combination of healing, damage sharing, and scaling Magic Attack gives them long-term account value. If you prepare resources in advance and stay disciplined during the event, pushing the event hero toward 6 stars is a realistic and worthwhile goal.
