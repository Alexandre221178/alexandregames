Fate's Wish is a limited-time event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era built around two reward systems: the Wheel of Wishes and the Vault of Fate. You spin the wheel with Wish Coins, collect rewards, and use Seals of Fate to play Amira's risk-versus-guarantee game for even better prizes.

This guide explains how the event works, how the Seal of Fate guarantee works, what happens to unused resources, and every quest chain with complete reward tables and total gains.

Basics

You can access Fate's Wish from the main game screen while the event is active. Inside the event, the Wheel of Wishes lets you spend Wish Coins for a chance to win valuable rewards. Use the Rewards icon on the left-side menu to view the complete reward list and the chance of obtaining each item.

Wish Coins come from two main sources: completing event quests and purchasing special offers. The event also has a second resource, the Seal of Fate, which can be won from the Wheel of Wishes or earned from event quests.

A Seal of Fate lets you enter the Vault of Fate and play Amira's game. Amira offers two choices: a visible reward on the left, or the hidden Treasure of Fate on the right. The visible reward is the safe option. The Treasure of Fate is the risk: it can be better than the visible reward, but it can also be worse.

In the Vault of Fate, use the list button at the bottom of the screen to view all available Vault rewards and their drop chances.

Seal of Fate Guarantee

The Wheel of Wishes has a pity rule for Seals of Fate. If you do not receive a Seal of Fate within 19 consecutive spins, the 20th spin is guaranteed to contain one. Receiving a Seal of Fate at any point resets the guarantee counter.

If you complete every quest chain listed below, you can earn up to 108 Wish Coins from quests. If all of those coins are used on the wheel, the guarantee alone gives at least 5 Seals of Fate from spins, plus 3 Seals of Fate directly from quests. Random Seal drops can increase that number.

Best Strategy for Fate's Wish

For free-to-play players, the safest approach is to complete the login chain, save Energy before the event when possible, and use Pet Summoning only if you already have enough resources prepared. The VIP and Emerald chains are useful, but they should not force you to spend more than you normally would.

The most important detail is the Vault of Fate. Playing with Amira counts for the A Guide to the World of Magic chain, but the Victory Over Fate chain specifically requires opening the hidden Treasure of Fate. If you always take the visible reward, you can miss the Treasure chain progress.

Choose the visible reward when it is something you really need or when it is already high value. Choose the Treasure of Fate when you are chasing the Treasure quest chain or when the visible reward is not important enough to protect.

⚠️ Important Vault tip: when Amira offers the Vault of Fate choice, my recommendation is to choose the hidden Treasure of Fate on the right. The chance to get an important item can compensate for losing the visible reward, especially because it also progresses the Victory Over Fate quest chain. I learned this the hard way while creating this guide: I lost a Gift of Dominion after making the wrong choice.

Final Thoughts

Fate's Wish is a strong event because it combines guaranteed quest rewards with random wheel rewards and the Vault of Fate's extra prize layer. The best rewards are locked behind heavy activity or spending, but even moderate participation can bring Wish Coins, Skin Stone Chests, Artifact Chests, and useful resource chests.

The big takeaway is simple: use Wish Coins before the event ends unless you prefer the automatic conversion, track your Seal of Fate guarantee, and in most cases prioritize the hidden Treasure of Fate when Amira gives you the Vault choice.
