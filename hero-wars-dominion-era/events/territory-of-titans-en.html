Territory of Titans is a special titan-focused variant of the classic Area of Conquest event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. For four days, guilds battle on a cross-server map to capture settlements, collect Superior Coins, and earn exclusive titan resources.

This guide covers everything you need to know: participation requirements, chest mechanics, end conversion tips, all event quests with their rewards, ranking prizes, and the Road to the Top pass. Whether you lead your guild or just joined, this guide will help you make the most of every minute.

What is the Territory of Titans?

Territory of Titans (also known as Titan AoC) is a guild event built on the same map-based conquest mechanics as the regular Area of Conquest, but with a strong focus on titan resources. Titan Upgrade Chests, Titan Potions, Essence of the Elements, and Primal/Elemental Catalysts are among the key prizes.

To participate, you must have joined your guild at most three hours before the start of the event. Players who join the guild after that cutoff will not be eligible.

General Information – Understanding the Basics

The Territory of Titans shares the same core mechanics as the Area of Conquest. The main goal is to help your guild collect Superior Coins — special points that your whole guild earns together. When enough coins are gathered, your guild's Castle Level goes up. Every time the Castle Level increases, all guild members receive rewards.

Coins are earned by capturing settlements on the map, winning battles, and opening treasure chests. Four guilds of similar strength compete on each map, so coordination within your guild is essential.

The titan twist is that many missions and rewards are tied to titan-specific resources: Titan Upgrade Chests, Titan Potions, Essence of the Elements, and Primal/Elemental Catalysts are the standout prizes of this event.

Chests

Treasure Chests in Territory of Titans reward 100 Superior Coins per guild member, although only the first 100 guild members count for the guild's ranking score. The remaining members' contributions still serve to upgrade the castle and feed into the end conversion.

Chests appear 15 minutes after the start of the event, and then every four hours after that. They do not spawn in exactly the same position each time, but tend to appear in similar areas of the map.

End Conversion

At the end of the event, unspent Superior Coins can be converted into other rewards. The exact conversion rates are still to be confirmed.

Tip: Once you realize your guild will not be able to upgrade the castle any further before the event ends, it is better to keep your remaining coins for the end conversion rather than spending them on castle upgrades. The guild management may want to call for this when appropriate.

Moving and Action Points (AP)

Every move or action on the map uses Action Points (AP). AP recovers over time, so running out is only a temporary setback. You can pre-plan your hero's path and they will automatically continue moving when AP is restored. You can cancel the path at any time by clicking on the tile where you want to stop.

Battles on the Map

Heroes do not automatically heal between battles. If they lose health, they stay weakened until you switch in a fresh team or the battle ends. To start a fight, your hero must be on a tile adjacent to an enemy, then confirm the attack.

If your team loses, your heroes are teleported back to the guild's castle. A win grants you the enemy's settlement and starts generating Superior Coins for your guild from that tile.

Conclusion – Master the Territory of Titans

The Territory of Titans event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era is a fantastic opportunity for guilds to strengthen their titan rosters while enjoying the intense cross-server PvP of the Area of Conquest format. By completing all 8 quest tracks, earning Superior Coins on the map, and coordinating daily summons, you can collect Titan Upgrade Chests, Titan Potions, Essence of the Elements, and valuable Primal/Elemental Catalysts.

Remember that chests appear 15 minutes after the event starts and every 4 hours after that — don't miss them. Once your guild can no longer upgrade the castle, hold onto your coins for the end conversion. The ranking system rewards teamwork: the guilds that capture and hold the most settlements reach the top and earn the best prizes, including the exclusive Clan Frame for the #1 guild.

Stay active, manage your Action Points wisely, and keep your guild coordinated — your efforts in the Territory of Titans will pay off in titan power!
