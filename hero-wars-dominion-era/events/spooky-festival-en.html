Every year at the end of October, Hero Wars: Dominion Era celebrates Halloween with a 7-day special event called Spooky Festival.

During this time, players can earn Ghost Energy, Spooky Festival Coins, and exclusive Halloween Avatars by completing event missions and spending resources.

🕸️ Spooky Festival Event Overview

  • Event Duration: 7 Days
  • Event Month: October
  • Main Rewards: Ghost Energy, Spooky Festival Coins, Avatars
  • Special Currencies: Ghost Energy (used in event activities) and Spooky Festival Coins (used in the Halloween shop)

🧙‍♀️ Spooky Festival Cauldron

The Spooky Festival Cauldron is where you spend your Ghost Energy collected during the event. Each spin of the cauldron gives a random reward from the list below, and you can keep spinning as long as you have energy available.

Rewards from the Spooky Festival Cauldron
Reward Quantity Type
Great Enchantment Rune x5 Resource for equipment enchantment
Chaos Core x3 Artifact resource used to evolve Heroes
Small Skin Stone Chest x5 Contains Skin Stones for hero skins
Absolute Artifact Chest x1 Opens to get any artifact fragment of your choice
🎁 Grand Prize: Spooky Festival Coin x1 Used in the event shop for exclusive rewards

💡 Tip: The Cauldron is the main way to convert your activity into rewards during the event. Make sure to use all your Ghost Energy before the event ends so you don’t miss out on valuable resources and the chance to get extra Spooky Festival Coins!

Spooky Festival Merchant

The Spooky Festival Store is where you can spend your Spooky Festival Coins, obtained from completing event missions and as Grand Prizes in the Cauldron. Inside the store, players can purchase a variety of valuable items and exclusive limited-time skins.

Highlights from the Spooky Festival Store

💡 Alexandre’s Note

Demonic Skins can sometimes be obtained outside of the event or through special offers, but for F2P players, they must be purchased with Skin Stones, which means a significant investment of resources. Therefore, it’s best to prioritize these skins for your main team heroes or meta heroes you plan to upgrade in the future. Once you’ve secured the most important skins, focus on buying items that make your heroes stronger overall, such as artifact and chaos upgrade materials.

🔥 Furnace of Souls

During the Spooky Festival event, the Furnace of Souls is the main battle activity where you face a different horde of enemies each day. Each day comes with unique buffs that change daily, making every fight feel different and requiring strategic team adjustments.

💡 On the event screen, you can check the Damage Ranking — it shows the top-performing players and their best teams, which can help you plan your lineup and improve your score. The maximum score per battle is 160 points, and you’ll need to reach a total of 250 points to collect all the available rewards.

💡 Alexandre’s Note: The top-performing teams in the Furnace of Souls usually rely on the powerful Orion + Augustus combo, thanks to their incredibly high Pure Damage output. However, Augustus is a very rare hero — not everyone has access to him. To use any hero in this event, you must own them (even at the minimum level), since the event temporarily upgrades them to max level for battle. If you don’t have Augustus, great alternatives are Heidi or Iris, who are much easier to obtain and can also deal strong Pure Damage, making them excellent backup options for F2P players.

🎯 Tip: Focus on maximizing your daily damage output. Using heroes with strong area attacks and support that boost survivability will help you achieve higher scores more consistently. Remember, the higher your score, the more Ghost Energy you’ll earn — which can be spent later in the Spooky Festival Cauldron for exclusive rewards!

💡 Spooky Festival Conclusion

The Spooky Festival is one of the best seasonal events in Hero Wars: Dominion Era to stock up on Ghost Energy for event activities and obtain exclusive Halloween-themed avatarsand Demonic Skins. Even free-to-play players can progress well by logging in daily, completing the Furnace of Souls battles, and spending energy strategically. Remember to save some Emeralds beforehand — the Emerald missions give a great boost of Ghost Energy and Coins!

Whether you’re a new player or a veteran Guardian, the Spooky Festival offers plenty of exciting tasks and spooky rewards. Make sure to complete as many quests as possible and enjoy this haunted celebration in Dominion!

💡 Conclusão do Festival Assustador

O Festival Assustador é um dos melhores eventos sazonais de Hero Wars: Dominion Era para acumular Energia Fantasma usada nas atividades do evento e obter avatares exclusivos de Halloween e Skins Demoníacas. Mesmo os jogadores free-to-play podem progredir bem ao entrar diariamente, completar as batalhas da Fornalha das Almas e gastar energia de forma estratégica. Lembre-se de guardar algumas Esmeraldas com antecedência — as missões com Esmeraldas oferecem um ótimo impulso de Energia Fantasma e Moedas!

Seja você um novo jogador ou um Guardião veterano, o Festival Assustador traz diversas tarefas empolgantes e recompensas aterrorizantes. Certifique-se de completar o máximo de missões possível e aproveite esta celebração assombrada em Dominion!
