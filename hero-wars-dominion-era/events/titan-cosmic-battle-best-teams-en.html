Titan Cosmic Battle changes every run because the banned Titans reshape the strongest team options. This page focuses on the June 2026 edition of Titan Cosmic Battle in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, with quick team recommendations and practical synergy notes for players who want a clean starting point.

Use these teams as a base, then adapt them to your account. In this event your Titans fight with their real current power, so a slightly less popular lineup with stronger artifacts, skins, and totem support can outperform a copied meta team with weak investment.

The following Titans are out of the June 2026 Titan Cosmic Battle event and cannot be used in battle teams:

Table: Titans out of Titan Cosmic Battle in June 2026

June 2026 Umbra, Lumira, Solaris Light and Dark lineups lose important flexibility, so mixed elemental teams and surviving super Titans become more valuable.

Best Titan Cosmic Battle Teams for June

These lineups are the best starting points for the June 2026 Titan Cosmic Battle meta. Test them on attack and defense, then adjust based on your strongest Titan investments and the totem you can activate reliably.

Table: Recommended Titan Cosmic Battle teams for June 2026
Priority Team Why It Works
1 Hyperion, Eden, Verdoc, Tidus, Sigurd Balanced control, sustain, and durability. Hyperion and Sigurd stabilize the fight while Eden, Verdoc, and Tidus pressure the enemy team.
2 Hyperion, Araji, Tidus, Asherona, Moloch A more aggressive option with strong Fire pressure. It is useful when your Araji and Moloch are highly invested and you need faster wins.
3 Tenebris, Eden, Verdoc, Tidus, Sigurd A strong alternative if your Tenebris is one of your best Titans. Sigurd helps buy time while the damage and control pieces build momentum.

Beginner Team Building Advice

If you are a beginner, focus on choosing your strongest Titans first, but do not ignore synergy. The most important detail is usually the totem requirement, because activating the correct totem can decide close battles.

Table: Totem activation reminders for Titan Cosmic Battle
Element Normal Requirement Summoner Note
Light or Dark 2 Titans of the same element activate the Light or Dark totem. Use this to build compact teams when key Light or Dark Titans are not banned.
Water, Earth, or Fire 3 Titans of the same element are normally required to activate the totem. With Summoner Titans, it is possible to activate the totem with only 2 Titans, because Summoners count as 2 Titans in battle.

Practical rule: if the perfect meta team makes you lose your best totem or forces you to use weak Titans, choose the stronger account-based team instead.

How to Choose Between These Teams

  • Start with power: If one of your recommended Titans is far behind, replace it with a stronger Titan that keeps your totem active.
  • Protect your win streak: In Cosmic Battle, consistent wins are better than risky teams that only work against perfect targets.
  • Watch defense results: A team that wins on attack may still be easy to counter on defense. Test both roles.
  • Respect the bans: Umbra, Lumira, and Solaris are unavailable in this June 2026 edition, so do not build your plan around them.
  • Keep options ready: Prepare at least two teams so you can adapt to enemy defenses and league pressure.

Quick Conclusion

For June 2026, the safest Titan Cosmic Battle direction is to build around your strongest available super Titans, keep totem activation in mind, and avoid forcing banned Light or Dark strategies. Hyperion, Eden, Verdoc, Tidus, Sigurd is the most balanced recommendation, while Araji-based and Tenebris-based versions can be stronger if your account is invested in those Titans.
