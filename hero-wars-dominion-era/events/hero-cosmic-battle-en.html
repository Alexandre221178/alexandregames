Heroes' Cosmic Battle is a cross-server PvP event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era for Web and Facebook. Unlike Hero Brawls, your Heroes fight with their real current power, so upgrades, artifacts, skins, glyphs, ascension, pets, and team synergy all matter.

The event is linked to The Path of a Warrior missions. Battles give points and Cosmic Battle Coins, while the mission chain adds extra Cosmic Battle Chests and coins that can be spent in the event shop.

How Heroes' Cosmic Battle Works

Run-Up Vote

Before the main event begins, players can vote for Heroes they do not want to see in Cosmic Battle. Each player can choose up to 7 Heroes. When the voting period ends, the 10 most-voted Heroes are banned from this event.

Banned Heroes

Banned Heroes cannot be used in attack or defense teams during Heroes' Cosmic Battle, but they remain available in all other game modes. If there is a tie around the last ban position, the game resolves it randomly. The same forbidden Heroes are not meant to repeat from the previous Cosmic Battle, keeping the event meta from becoming too predictable.

Joining the Main Event

To enter the cross-server arena, set a defense team on the event screen. Your Heroes enter battle with their current power, so strengthening your roster before and during the event can directly improve your results.

Attacks and Recovery

You begin with 5 attacks. One attack is restored every hour, and up to 5 attacks can be stored at once. Log in regularly so you do not waste recovery time while sitting at the cap.

Points, Rankings, and Leagues

Winning battles gives points and Cosmic Battle Coins. Points move you through the event rankings and leagues, and higher positions usually mean better coin income and stronger final rewards.

Win Streak Strategy

Successive victories increase your bonus rewards. Some players try to lose early and then climb later with a bigger streak bonus, but this is risky for strong accounts with limited play time. If your goal is a high league, start climbing early because reaching the top can require around 35 wins plus time to recover from inevitable losses.

Practical Tip

Build a defense that is annoying to defeat, not just a team with high raw power. Timeout threats, control, sustain, and smart pet choices can matter more than placing your five strongest Heroes together.

The Path of a Warrior Event Missions

The full mission chain can reward up to 93 Cosmic Battle Chests and 274,275 Cosmic Battle Coins. Quest names and values may be adjusted by Nexters in future reruns, so always confirm the live event screen before spending resources.

Preparation Checklist

  • Save Energy: Keep energy bottles and daily energy rewards for the event if you want to push Rigorous Training.
  • Save Outland: Do not open Outland chests right before the event if you can wait.
  • Save Artifact Keys: Artifact Chest openings are part of the mission chain.
  • Prepare Pets: Pet Summoning rewards coins and chests, and pets can decide close Cosmic Battle fights.
  • Review Bans: Build several team options in case your usual Heroes are forbidden.

Conclusion

Heroes' Cosmic Battle rewards active players who manage attacks, build flexible teams, and prepare resources for The Path of a Warrior missions. Set a reliable defense early, spend attacks before they cap, watch the banned Hero list, and use your Cosmic Battle Coins on upgrades that strengthen your best teams.
