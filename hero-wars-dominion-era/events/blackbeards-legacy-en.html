Blackbeard's Legacy is a seasonal event in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. Complete permanent and daily quest chains to earn Pirate Keys, then use those keys to open the Buccaneer Stash for resources and exclusive cosmetics.

This guide lists every 2026 quest threshold and reward, explains the daily reset chains, calculates the maximum Pirate Key total, and shows every reward amount currently listed for the Buccaneer Stash.

How the 1,186 total is calculated: the three non-daily chains award 731 Pirate Keys. The five daily chains award up to 65 Keys per day, or 455 across seven daily resets. Therefore, 731 + 455 = 1,186 Pirate Keys. This maximum requires completing every tier, including 180,000 Emerald spending, 9,000 VIP points, 4,000 Energy per day, 45 Tower chests per day, and 100 Summoning Circle summons per day.

How Blackbeard's Legacy Works

The event is divided into three event-long quest chains and five chains marked as daily. Event-long progress accumulates until the event ends. Daily progress resets with the game day, so unfinished daily tiers do not carry over to the next reset.

Every listed quest tier awards Pirate Keys. One Pirate Key opens the Buccaneer Stash once. The event interface also lists an alternative opening price of 196 Emeralds, but using Keys earned from quests is the more economical route for most players.

The login chain also grants player avatar #1780 and avatar frame #256. The Emerald-spending chain grants player avatar #1781, while player avatar #1354 appears among the Buccaneer Stash rewards.

Best Blackbeard's Legacy Strategy

  1. Never miss a login. Long Voyage is the easiest source of 35 Pirate Keys and contains two exclusive cosmetic milestones.
  2. Finish the low-cost daily objectives first. Normal daily quests, Arena/Grand Arena fights, and the Tower provide up to 24 Keys per day before counting Energy or Summoning Circle spending.
  3. Set a sensible Energy stopping point. The chain gives only one extra Key at each step. Spending heavily just to reach 4,000 Energy is poor value unless another event or your normal farming plan also rewards that Energy.
  4. Save Summoning Spheres before the event. Devil of the Seas provides 32 daily Keys, but 100 summons every day is extremely resource-intensive. Claim only the tiers that match resources you already planned to use.
  5. Do not chase VIP or Emerald milestones for Pirate Keys alone. The largest Key totals sit behind 9,000 VIP points and 180,000 Emeralds. Treat those tiers as bonuses for planned spending, not as a reason to overspend.
  6. Prefer earned Pirate Keys over the 196-Emerald opening. Because drop rates are not listed, buying Stash openings directly with Emeralds carries uncertain value.

Practical free-to-play target: collect all login Keys, complete as many normal daily quests and Arena fights as possible, open the Tower chests you can afford, and use naturally regenerated Energy. This captures the accessible portion of the event without sacrificing large resource reserves for one additional Key per tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Pirate Keys can you earn?

The theoretical maximum from all listed quest chains is 1,186 Pirate Keys across seven days: 731 from event-long chains and 455 from daily chains. Most players will earn much less because the maximum includes very high VIP, Emerald, Energy, Tower, and Summoning Circle requirements.

How much does one Buccaneer Stash cost?

One opening costs 1 Pirate Key. The event data also lists an alternative price of 196 Emeralds.

Do daily quest thresholds carry over?

No. Chains labeled daily reset each game day. Claim each completed daily tier before the reset and begin again the following day.

Does the total at the top of a quest chain award extra Keys?

No. The chain total is the sum of the individual quest rewards shown in that chain.

Final Thoughts

Blackbeard's Legacy offers a large number of Buccaneer Stash openings, but its headline maximum is heavily weighted toward paid and resource-intensive milestones. The best approach is to secure the free login rewards, complete efficient daily tiers, and avoid spending Emeralds or saved resources only to gain one more Pirate Key.

The Buccaneer Stash contains a broad mix of Hero, Pet, Titan, Artifact, and currency rewards plus an exclusive avatar. Since the available data does not show drop rates, open it for the overall resource mix rather than assuming any particular rare reward is guaranteed.
