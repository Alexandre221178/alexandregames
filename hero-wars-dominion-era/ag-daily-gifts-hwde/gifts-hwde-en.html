Don't Miss Your Daily Gifts!

Looking for today's free rewards in Hero Wars: Dominion Era? The Alexandre Games blog helps Web and Facebook players quickly find and claim their daily gifts — including Energy, Platinum Boxes, and many more surprises that refresh every day!

Check your in-game mailbox to collect your reward. If it doesn't show up right away, restarting the game usually helps. Remember, daily rewards are only available for a limited time — make sure to claim them before they expire!
