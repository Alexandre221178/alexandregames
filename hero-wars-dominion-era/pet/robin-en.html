Robin gives Energy to slow Heroes and impedes fast opponents in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. He steals enemy Energy, supports an ally who has gained less Energy, and reduces the attacks of opponents who receive bonus Energy.

His patronage skill, Fair Wind, supplies one hero with bonus Energy every five seconds. Below you will find his stats, all three skills, eligible heroes, and Alexandre's suggestions for using him.

Robin's Active Pet Skills

1. On Borrowed Wings

In-game description: Steals up to 90.62 Energy from each enemy Hero and transfers it to an ally who received the least amount of Energy in the last 15 seconds. If an enemy is not a Hero, steals 110.6 Energy from them.

White skill · Periodic · 15-second cooldown

Skill Explanation: Robin takes Energy from the enemy team and passes it to the ally who gained the least Energy over the previous 15 seconds. This helps that ally work toward their next ultimate while delaying enemy ultimates. The recipient is selected by recent Energy gain, so it may change during the battle.

  • Energy stolen from each Hero: 90.62 (0.45% Skill Power + 35).
  • Energy stolen from a non-Hero: 110.62 (0.45% Skill Power + 55), displayed as 110.6.

2. Counterflow

In-game description: Passive skill. When an opponent receives bonus Energy, Robin decreases their Physical and Magic Attack by 31,000 for 5 seconds.

Green skill · Passive

Skill Explanation: An enemy who receives bonus Energy temporarily loses both Physical Attack and Magic Attack. The trigger is bonus Energy, so the value of this passive depends on the opponent's abilities and team. Alexandre highlights Orion, Jorgen, Andvari, Alvanor, Tristan, and Polaris as matchups to consider.

Attack reduction formula: 250% Skill Power + 100. At 12,360 Skill Power, both attacks are reduced by 31,000.

3. Fair Wind

In-game description: Gives the owner 75.01 bonus Energy once every 5 seconds.

Violet skill · Patronage · Every 5 seconds

Skill Explanation: The hero under Robin's patronage receives a regular Energy bonus, helping them activate their ultimate more frequently. Alexandre recommends trying it on healers and heroes whose ultimates provide shields or control. Against Satori, remember that this bonus Energy can expose the owner to his marks.

Bonus Energy formula: 0.452% Patronage Power + 25.

Why do some descriptions use 0.45%?

The available descriptions contain both coefficients. At 11,064 Patronage Power, 0.452% gives 75.01 Energy after rounding; 0.45% gives 74.79. This guide uses the formula that matches the listed 75.01 value.

Alexandre's Tips: Who Benefits from Robin?

Alexandre's experience: “My Byrna became much stronger after I started using her with Robin.” His suggestions focus on heroes who benefit from activating their ultimate more frequently.

Best Patronage Choices: Alexandre's Recommendations

Byrna

Alexandre reports a strong improvement with Robin and recommends trying the extra Energy on this healer.

Guus

Another healer Alexandre suggests for more frequent ultimate use.

Nebula

Extra Energy can help her reach an ultimate that otherwise takes time to activate.

Fafnir

Astaroth

Consider them when you want more frequent shield support from their ultimates.

Somna

Alexandre recommends her for more frequent sleep effects: her ultimate puts enemies to sleep for 7 seconds.

Pairing Robin with Satori

Alexandre sees a useful pairing against enemies who gain bonus Energy: Robin reduces their attacks while Satori punishes them with marks. This is a team idea to try against the relevant matchups.

Facing an Enemy Satori

Fair Wind's bonus Energy can also expose Robin's owner to Satori's marks. Alexandre suggests adding Nebula to clear marks or Sebastian for protection against debuffs when considering this matchup.

Strengths and Limitations

  • Helps an ally with low recent Energy gain through On Borrowed Wings.
  • Provides recurring Energy, Health, and Magic Defense through patronage.
  • Counterflow depends on opponents receiving bonus Energy.
  • Fair Wind's bonus Energy can become a drawback against Satori.

Is Robin a Good Fit for Your Team?

Consider Robin when your team needs more frequent ultimates or when bonus Energy is central to the enemy's strategy. Alexandre's first choices to try include Byrna, Guus, Nebula, and Somna. Check the Satori matchup before assigning Fair Wind to a hero.
