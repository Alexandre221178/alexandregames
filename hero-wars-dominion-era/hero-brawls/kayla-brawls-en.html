Who is Kayla?

Role: Warrior — Fights at the front line • Attribute: Agility

What am I? A good-for-nothing daughter? A butcher to my own people? The last of the ashen elves, or a monster stinking with the filthy demon blood that flows in my veins? I have no answer, but I know Aidan and I have embarked on the path to bring Light and Hope to the Dominion! We’ll be walking down that path until I’m consumed by the fiery frenzy once more...

Strengths & Weaknesses

Strengths:

  • Distracts enemies in the back line.
  • Can kill some important back line heroes (ex. Thea, Jet, Sebastian).
  • In battle, Kayla thrives when surrounded by allies who can amplify her chaos-driven energy. She shines brightest in teams that balance aggression and defense, turning the tide with her explosive reactions and unpredictable combos.

Kayla Brawls Battles is a limited 3-day event where you can farm soul stones by battling in the special brawl arena. You can only fight 5v5 and both attack and defense teams are required to include Aidan. The remaining four heroes can be chosen freely, allowing dozens of strategic combinations.

Also, by completing missions, you earn Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins — the exclusive currency used to purchase rewards, hero stones, and upgrade resources.

Best Brawls Teams for Kayla - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Best Team #1

This team focuses on maximizing Kayla's burn damage while Sebastian protects the team against negative effects and Augustus teams. Lyria provides lifesteal for Kayla, and Aidan offers protection and synergy amplifying Kayla's, making her a constant threat on the battlefield.

Team Strategy Notes:

  • Aidan's Bonds of Flame – Establishes a mutual bond with the farthest ally, or with Kayla if she is on the allied team. The bound ally becomes the target of Phoenix's Embrace. The heroes share health, passing any healing or damage taken to each other. They also gain a bonus that scales with their Armor and Magic Defense. The bond starts at the beginning of battle and is severed on death.

Daily Brawl Rewards Explained

Each day, you can earn a solid amount of Brawl Coins through both offensive victories and successful defensive battles. Every win grants 300 Brawl Coins and 3,000 Gold, up to a limit of 22 victories per day. In addition, each successful defense awards 300 Brawl Coins, counted up to 5 defensive wins daily. When combined, this allows you to earn a maximum of 15,100 Brawl Coins per day if you reach all available limits.

Brawl Shop Recommendations

If Kayla isn't unlocked yet, your top priority in the Brawl Shop should be purchasing Kayla Skin Stones — this is the fastest and most cost-efficient way to improve his combat potential. Your second priority should be Artifact Chests and Chaos Cores, as these resources are always in high demand and cannot be reliably farmed. Avoid buying equipment pieces, since they can be farmed in Campaign missions and regular Events at a much better value.

Conclusion

Kayla Brawl is one of the most rewarding modes both for team-building strategy and daily resource income. By focusing on the right purchases in the Brawl Shop, such as Kayla Skin Stones, Artifact Chests, and Chaos Cores, players maximize long-term progress while avoiding waste on farmable equipment.

Consistent participation, strong offensive teams, and reliable defensive setups ensure you reach the daily coin cap, accelerating your growth every day. Master your synergy teams, optimize your pet choices, and the Brawl will quickly become one of the best sources of value in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.
