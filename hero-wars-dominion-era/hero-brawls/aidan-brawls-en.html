Who Is Aidan

Aidan is a back line support hero bound by flame and fate to his sister Kayla. As a member of the Fire Heralds, he brings powerful healing and searing magic to the battlefield. His origin is marked by tragedy, but his resolve to protect allies remains unwavering.

Class: Healer/Mage
Position: Back Line
Main Stat: Intelligence

Aidan excels as an Intelligence-based healer and damage dealer, offering steady healing waves and protective shields from the back line while applying persistent burn damage to enemies.

What Is the Aidan Brawls Battles Event?

The Aidan Brawls Battles is a limited 3-day event where you can farm Aidan soul stones by battling in the special brawl arena. You can only fight 5v5 and both attack and defense teams are required to include Aidan. The remaining four heroes can be chosen freely, allowing dozens of strategic combinations.

Also, by completing missions, you earn Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins — the exclusive currency used to purchase rewards, hero stones, and upgrade resources.

Aidan Brawl Best Teams - Hero Wars: (Web & Facebook)

Aidan performs exceptionally well in Brawl battles thanks to his strong synergy with his sister Kayla, forming one of the most powerful defensive duos in the game. Because of their shared health mechanic through the Bonds of Flame skill, they can sustain each other while providing constant healing, shields, and burn damage to enemies.

Aidan also pairs extremely well with heroes who benefit from sustained healing and shields, such as Dante, who becomes nearly unkillable with proper support. Together with heroes like Nebula, Sebastian, or Mushy, Aidan creates resilient team setups that excel in prolonged battles and can withstand heavy burst damage.

Extra Tips

There are no teams with a 100% win rate. Many popular influencers claim otherwise just to get clicks, but the truth is very different. They achieve easier results because they've been playing for many years, and older servers are far less competitive. Veteran players already have plenty of resources and are not worried about winning every battle just to earn a few Brawl Coins, so their defensive teams are usually weaker.

You, on the other hand, as a newer player on a fresh and competitive server, will naturally face stronger opponents — the same way I do on my new Hero Wars: Dominion Era account. So don't get frustrated. Use the tips carefully, and if you face back-line assassins like Jhu, Kayla, or Cleaver, try positioning Aidan with strong front-line protection using tanks like Corvus, Dante, or Electra to absorb damage while Aidan heals the team.

Daily Brawl Rewards Explained

Each day, you can earn a solid amount of Brawl Coins through both offensive victories and successful defensive battles. Every win grants 300 Brawl Coins and 3,000 Gold, up to a limit of 22 victories per day. In addition, each successful defense awards 300 Brawl Coins, counted up to 5 defensive wins daily. When combined, this allows you to earn a maximum of 15,100 Brawl Coins per day if you reach all available limits.

Brawl Shop Recommendations

If Aidan isn't unlocked yet, your top priority in the Brawl Shop should be purchasing Aidan Skin Stones — this is the fastest and most cost-efficient way to improve his combat potential. Your second priority should be Artifact Chests and Chaos Cores, as these resources are always in high demand and cannot be reliably farmed. Avoid buying equipment pieces, since they can be farmed in Campaign missions and regular Events at a much better value.
