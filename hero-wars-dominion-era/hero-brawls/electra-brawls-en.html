Electra is a mysterious warrior born from chaos itself. Once a lifeless creation, she now channels the energy of countless souls into raw power, making her one of the most unpredictable heroes in Dominion. Her blend of resilience, agility, and burst potential makes her both a formidable tank and a tactical choice for creative players.

  • Main Role: Tank
  • Additional Role: Control / Support
  • Position: Fights at the Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Patronage: Axel, Biscuit, Merlin, Oliver

In battle, Electra thrives when surrounded by allies who can amplify her chaos-driven energy. She shines brightest in teams that balance aggression and defense, turning the tide with her explosive reactions and unpredictable combos.

Brawls Battles is a limited 3-day event where you can farm soul stones by battling in the special brawl arena. You can only fight 5v5 and both attack and defense teams are required to include Aidan. The remaining four heroes can be chosen freely, allowing dozens of strategic combinations.

Also, by completing missions, you earn Event Points that convert into Brawl Coins — the exclusive currency used to purchase rewards, hero stones, and upgrade resources.

Daily Brawl Rewards Explained

Each day, you can earn a solid amount of Brawl Coins through both offensive victories and successful defensive battles. Every win grants 300 Brawl Coins and 3,000 Gold, up to a limit of 22 victories per day. In addition, each successful defense awards 300 Brawl Coins, counted up to 5 defensive wins daily. When combined, this allows you to earn a maximum of 15,100 Brawl Coins per day if you reach all available limits.

Brawl Shop Recommendations

If Electra isn't unlocked yet, your top priority in the Brawl Shop should be purchasing Electra Skin Stones — this is the fastest and most cost-efficient way to improve his combat potential. Your second priority should be Artifact Chests and Chaos Cores, as these resources are always in high demand and cannot be reliably farmed. Avoid buying equipment pieces, since they can be farmed in Campaign missions and regular Events at a much better value.

Conclusion

Electra Brawl is one of the most rewarding modes both for team-building strategy and daily resource income. By focusing on the right purchases in the Brawl Shop, such as Electra Skin Stones, Artifact Chests, and Chaos Cores, players maximize long-term progress while avoiding waste on farmable equipment.

Consistent participation, strong offensive teams, and reliable defensive setups ensure you reach the daily coin cap, accelerating your growth every day. Master your synergy teams, optimize your pet choices, and the Brawl will quickly become one of the best sources of value in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.
