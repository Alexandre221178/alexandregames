Who Is Galahad in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Galahad is a Strength-based Tank who epitomizes courage and unwavering determination. Sturdy as Kharunian steel and knowing no fear or doubt, he moves only forward into battle. His confidence in himself and his allies has kept this great warrior strong for many years, making him one of the most reliable front-line defenders in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

What makes Galahad truly unique is his 45% Vampirism—a game-changing stat that allows him to recover health based on the damage he deals. The more enemies he strikes, the more he heals himself, creating a self-sustaining tank who becomes harder to kill the longer he fights. This vampirism synergizes perfectly with his high physical attack, turning his offensive capabilities into defensive strength.

  • Main Role: Tank / Warrior
  • Faction: Blessed
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Position: Fights at the front line
  • Key Strengths: Great survivability, High physical damage, 45% Vampirism for self-healing

Galahad Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Exceptional Self-Sustain: With 45% Vampirism, Galahad heals himself constantly through combat, making him one of the most self-sufficient tanks who doesn't rely on external healers.
  • High Physical Damage Output: Unlike purely defensive tanks, Galahad deals substantial physical damage with all his skills, making him a legitimate offensive threat.
  • Control Immunity: Unstoppable Charge breaks all crowd control effects, allowing him to escape stuns, freezes, and silences that would neutralize other tanks.
  • Area-of-Effect Ultimate: Iron Skies hits all enemies simultaneously, providing excellent team fight damage and massive healing through Vampirism when hitting multiple targets.
  • Pure Damage Penetration: Pride of Harun adds pure damage that ignores all armor and magic defense, ensuring he remains threatening even against heavily armored enemies.
  • Backline Disruption: His mobility and aggressive playstyle allow him to dive enemy backlines and pressure vulnerable damage dealers and supports.
  • Strong Early-to-Mid Game: Galahad performs exceptionally well before enemies acquire strong anti-healing capabilities, making him dominant in early progression.
  • Low Gear Dependency: Unlike some tanks requiring specific artifacts or extensive support, Galahad functions effectively with basic upgrades thanks to his innate Vampirism.

❌ Cons

  • Anti-Healing Vulnerability: Galahad's entire survivability depends on his Vampirism. Heroes like Celeste, Biscuit, or War Flag of Decline completely shut down his self-healing, making him vulnerable.
  • Energy Dependency: His most powerful ability (Iron Skies) requires full energy. Energy-denial heroes like Jorgen can prevent him from using his ultimate, drastically reducing his effectiveness.
  • Dodge Weakness: Against high-dodge heroes (Aurora, Dante, Heidi, Yasmine, Qing Mao), Galahad struggles because missed attacks don't trigger Vampirism healing.
  • No Team Support: Unlike utility tanks (Astaroth's resurrection, Cleaver's displacement), Galahad provides no buffs, shields, or support to teammates—he's purely selfish.
  • Control Vulnerable Window: While Unstoppable Charge breaks control, he's still vulnerable to being controlled initially. Heavy crowd control can lock him down before he breaks free.
  • Magic Defense Scaling: Without investing in magic defense (skins, glyphs, artifacts), Galahad can be quickly melted by magic-heavy teams despite high health.
  • Countered by Pure Damage: While he deals pure damage, he's also vulnerable to it. Heroes like Iris who expose his Soul can bypass all his defensive investments.
  • Late Game Scaling: As the meta shifts toward anti-healing and control-heavy compositions in late game, Galahad becomes less dominant compared to utility tanks or more specialized options.

Verdict: Galahad excels as a self-sufficient, aggressive tank who dominates through raw survivability and damage. However, his reliance on Vampirism makes him vulnerable to specific counters. He's best used in compositions that can protect him from anti-healing or when facing teams lacking hard counters. Pair him with cleanser supports or energy generators to maximize his potential while mitigating his weaknesses.

Galahad Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Understanding skill priorities helps you maximize Galahad's potential efficiently, especially when resources are limited early in the game.

1. Iron Skies (Ultimate)

Galahad calls down swords of justice from above, striking all enemies on the battlefield. This is his ultimate ability, triggered when his energy bar fills completely. The swords rain down dealing physical damage to multiple enemies simultaneously, making it excellent for damaging entire enemy teams.

Damage Formula: 28,628 (30% physical attack + 60 × level)

💉 Vampirism Synergy: Since this skill hits multiple enemies, it triggers massive healing through Galahad's 45% Vampirism. The more enemies you hit, the more health you recover—making this ultimate both an offensive and defensive tool.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Galahad's ultimate ability and your primary source of area damage. Every upgrade increases the damage significantly, affecting all enemies hit. Since it's your ultimate, it charges throughout battle and can turn fights around. Prioritize this skill first as it provides the best return on investment for both damage output and team fight impact.

1A. Iron Retribution - Ascension Skill

After unlocking Ascension V, Iron Skies gains a powerful additional effect. Now, when the swords strike enemies who have less than 30% Health remaining, those enemies receive the Retaliation debuff (maximum 5 enemies per cast). This enhancement transforms your ultimate from pure damage into a finisher that can trigger multiple counter-attacks against weakened foes.

Effect: Applies Retaliation to enemies below 30% Health (max 5 targets)

Ascension Note: This upgrade makes Iron Skies even more valuable, but remember that Ascension takes considerable time to unlock. Even without it, the base skill deserves top priority.

2. Unstoppable Charge

Galahad charges forward fearlessly, breaking through any control effects (stuns, freezes, silences) that are affecting him. As he dashes, he deals physical damage to all nearby enemies in his path. This skill has a 15-second cooldown, meaning Galahad can use it multiple times during a battle. It's essential for both mobility and survivability, allowing him to escape dangerous situations or dive into enemy formations.

Damage Formula: 41,214 (50% physical attack + 50 × level)

Cooldown: 15 seconds

💉 Vampirism Synergy: Each enemy hit during the charge provides healing through 45% Vampirism. With frequent activations (every 15s), this skill constantly sustains Galahad during prolonged fights.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill keeps Galahad alive and fighting by removing crowd control effects that would otherwise neutralize him. The damage is respectable and it activates frequently due to the short cooldown. Upgrade this second to improve both survivability and sustained damage. The control-break mechanic is invaluable against teams with heavy crowd control.

2A. Relentless Charge - Ascension Skill

Upon reaching Ascension II, Unstoppable Charge gains an automatic trigger: whenever the closest enemy activates their ultimate ability, Galahad immediately charges at them, bypassing his normal ability queue. This can only occur once every 7 seconds. Additionally, the first enemy hit by this reactive charge loses 30% of their energy, potentially delaying or preventing their next ultimate.

Effect: Auto-triggers when nearest enemy uses ultimate (once per 7s) + Target loses 30% energy

Ascension Note: This enhancement adds significant strategic value by interrupting enemy ultimates and controlling energy. However, since Ascension II comes much earlier than V, you might have this sooner, making the skill even more worthy of investment.

3. Retaliation (Periodic)

Galahad performs a special counter-attack, automatically striking the enemy with the lowest current health. This is a periodic skill that activates automatically every 20.5 seconds throughout the battle. It's Galahad's way of finishing off weakened enemies, dealing substantial physical damage. The skill smartly targets whoever is closest to death, making it excellent for securing eliminations.

Damage Formula: 61,042 (80% physical attack + 50 × level)

Cooldown: 20.5 seconds

💉 Vampirism Synergy: With the highest damage multiplier (80% physical attack), this skill provides Galahad's largest single-target heal through Vampirism. Each Retaliation strike can recover substantial health, making it crucial for sustained survivability.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill has the highest base damage of all Galahad's abilities and automatically targets the most vulnerable enemy. It's extremely efficient for finishing off opponents that your team has weakened. Upgrade this alongside Unstoppable Charge, as the high damage multiplier means each level provides significant damage increases. This skill has no Ascension upgrade, so what you invest goes directly into proven, reliable damage.

4. Pride of Harun (Periodic)

This skill has both passive and active components. Passive: Galahad's Iron Skies ultimate now deals an additional 17,135 pure damage with every cast. Active: Every 18 seconds, Galahad enters a heroic state for 10 seconds, during which ALL his skills and basic attacks deal extra pure damage. Pure damage ignores both armor and magic defense, making it extremely valuable.

💉 Vampirism Synergy: Since Vampirism works with all damage dealt by abilities (physical, magic, AND pure), this extra pure damage also heals Galahad through his 45% Vampirism! This makes Pride of Harun significantly more valuable for sustained survivability—you're healing from both physical AND pure damage portions simultaneously.

Passive Bonus Formula: 17,135 pure damage added to Iron Skies (20% physical attack + 25 × level)

Active Buff: All skills/attacks +17,135 pure damage for 10 seconds

Cooldown: 18 seconds

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – While pure damage is excellent and this skill enhances your ultimate permanently, it should be upgraded after the first three skills. The passive bonus only affects Iron Skies (which you're already upgrading), and the active buff, while strong, has less immediate impact than your other abilities. However, it's still worth upgrading steadily—just prioritize your ultimate, charge, and retaliation first. This skill scales well but benefits most when your other skills are already strong.

Summary: Focus on Iron Skies first for maximum area damage, then split resources between Unstoppable Charge (survivability) and Retaliation (single-target damage), and finally upgrade Pride of Harun to amplify everything else. This approach ensures Galahad remains effective throughout all stages of the game.

How to Counter Galahad - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

While Galahad is a formidable tank with high survivability and self-healing, several heroes can effectively counter him by exploiting his weaknesses.

Key Weaknesses:

  • Healing Dependency: Galahad relies heavily on his 45% Vampirism to sustain himself. Anti-healing effects drastically reduce his survivability.
  • Energy Reliance: His ultimate Iron Skies is his main area damage and healing source. Preventing energy gain weakens him significantly.
  • Control Vulnerability: While Unstoppable Charge breaks control, heavy crowd control can still lock him down and prevent healing.
  • Dodge-Based Heroes: Galahad is less effective against heroes with high dodge (Aurora, Dante, Heidi, Yasmine, Qing Mao) since missed attacks don't trigger vampirism.

Best Counters:

Celeste

Celeste is one of the hardest counters to Galahad. Her Cursed Flame ability blocks all healing received by enemies, converting 42% of the blocked healing into magic damage dealt to them. Since Galahad depends entirely on his Vampirism for sustain, Celeste's anti-heal completely shuts down his self-healing, turning his strength into a weakness. Every time Galahad would heal, he takes magic damage instead.

Iris

Iris is a devastating counter to tanks like Galahad through her Expose Soul ability. She exposes an unprotected part of the Soul in the nearest enemy (typically the front-line tank) for 13 seconds. All damage dealt to this exposed Soul is transferred to its owner as pure damage. This is exceptionally effective against Galahad because pure damage ignores all his armor and magic defense investments, bypassing his primary defensive stats. While the Soul is exposed, Galahad takes massive additional pure damage from every attack his team receives, effectively multiplying the damage he suffers. Since Galahad positions himself on the front line, he's almost always Iris's primary target, making this ability a hard counter to his tankiness.

Jorgen

Jorgen hard-counters Galahad by preventing energy gain through his Torment of Powerlessness ability. This skull summon deals magic damage to front-line heroes (including Galahad) and prevents affected enemies from gaining energy for 9 seconds. Without energy, Galahad cannot activate his ultimate Iron Skies, which is his primary source of area damage and massive vampirism healing. By constantly applying energy block, Jorgen effectively neutralizes Galahad's most impactful ability, significantly reducing his threat level and sustainability. This makes Jorgen one of the most reliable counters in any team composition.

Biscuit (Pet)

Biscuit is one of the most effective pet counters to Galahad, functioning both as a main battle pet and through patronage effects. His ultimate ability Icy Storm summons a storm under enemies that deals magic damage and, most critically, prevents enemies from receiving ANY healing from anyone but themselves for 5 seconds. This completely shuts down external healing support while also blocking Galahad's Vampirism since it comes from dealing damage to others, not self-healing. Additionally, Biscuit's Mighty Snowball deals magic damage and applies a slow effect, reducing Galahad's mobility. When used as a patronage pet on an ally, Biscuit's attacks also reduce healing received by enemies. The combination of direct healing prevention (Icy Storm) and patronage anti-healing makes Biscuit exceptionally valuable against healing-dependent tanks like Galahad.

Counter Strategy Summary: To effectively counter Galahad, prioritize anti-healing abilities (Celeste, Biscuit), energy denial (Jorgen). Combining multiple counter mechanics (e.g., Celeste + Jorgen) makes Galahad nearly useless in battle. Avoid relying solely on damage without anti-healing, as Galahad's Vampirism will keep him alive through sustained attacks.

Combined with his tankiness and self-sustain, this flag makes Galahad nearly unkillable in prolonged fights. In prolonged battles, this extra 10% healing can be the difference between surviving and falling. The War Flag of Recovery synergizes perfectly with Galahad's kit and can be combined with War Flag of Vanguard for maximum healing output.

Galahad and Team Benefit: This War Flag directly accelerates Galahad's ultimate ability activation. Iron Skies also repositions him to the enemy backline, allowing him to disrupt enemy formations and target vulnerable damage dealers. Faster energy gain = more ultimates = more damage = more healing = better survivability and more impact in battle. This flag is especially powerful in longer fights where ultimate abilities are used multiple times. Additionally, if your team has other Tank heroes (like Cleaver, Aurora, or Chabba), they also benefit from the increased energy gain, making this flag valuable for tank-heavy or double-tank compositions. The War Flag of Fervor synergizes excellently with Galahad's aggressive playstyle and ensures he can use his most powerful ability as often as possible.

Recommended War Flag Priority for Galahad: War Flag of Vanguard (constant healing for front tank) > War Flag of Recovery (amplifies all healing sources) > War Flag of Fervor (faster ultimates for more damage/healing). All three flags complement Galahad's self-sustaining tank role perfectly. Choose based on your team composition and battle needs—Vanguard for pure Galahad survivability, Recovery for team-wide healing boost, or Fervor for ultimate-focused aggressive play.

Conclusion: Is Galahad Worth It?

Galahad excels as a self-sufficient tank who dominates early-to-mid game content through his unique 45% Vampirism mechanic. He's an excellent choice for players building their first serious team composition, especially if you lack strong healers or prefer aggressive playstyles. His low gear dependency means he functions effectively even with basic investments, making him accessible for newer players while scaling well into late game when properly developed. However, be aware of his critical weaknesses: anti-healing heroes (Celeste, Biscuit), energy denial (Jorgen), and high-dodge enemies can completely neutralize his effectiveness. Galahad works best in physical damage compositions alongside heroes like Lyria, Sebastian, and other damage dealers who benefit from his front-line disruption.

If you're deciding whether to invest in Galahad, consider your current roster and the meta you're facing. He's particularly valuable if you're encountering balanced teams without dedicated counters, and he pairs excellently with War Flag of Vanguard for maximum survivability. However, if your server meta heavily features Celeste, Helios, or Jorgen, you might want to prioritize tanks with more utility like Astaroth (resurrection), Aurora (dodge/control), or Cleaver (displacement). Ultimately, Galahad rewards aggressive players who understand positioning and timing—when played correctly with proper support, he becomes a relentless force that pressures enemies while refusing to die. Invest in his defensive stats first (Health, Armor, Magic Defense), then enhance his damage output, and you'll have a versatile tank who serves you well across multiple game modes.
