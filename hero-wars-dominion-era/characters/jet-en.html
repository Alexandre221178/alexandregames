Jet isn’t your average goblin he’s a mad scientist with a heart for healing and a mind for destruction! Whether he’s boosting your team’s critical hits or shutting down enemy heals, Jet can turn the tide of any battle with his unique mix of support and sabotage.

In this complete guide, we’ll explore Jet’s best synergies, stats, and skill strategies to help you decide when and how to unleash his full potential in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

Who Is Jet?

Jet is a back-line Healer and Support hero who relies on Intelligence as his main stat. Known as the insect exterminator from Tornville, he uses his scientific experiments to both harm enemies and empower allies.

  • Class: Healer / Support
  • Position: Back Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence

He excels at boosting your team’s critical hits rate, making him invaluable for physical teams that depend on burst damage. At the same time, he weakens enemy defenses and stops them from recovering health a powerful combination for long battles.

Jet works best alongside heroes like Dante, K’arkh, Yasmine, Ishmael, Keira, and Jhu, forming deadly synergies that mix offense with survivability. Let’s dive deeper into how to master his skills and build the perfect Jet team.

Jet Pros and Cons - Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Strong backline support: heals and buffs the ally with the highest Physical Attack.
  • Increases critical hit chance for all allies through Inordinate Fury.
  • Reduces enemy healing and lowers Armor of the highest-Armor target with Acid Shot.
  • Excellent synergy with Fighters and Marksmen (Dante, K’arkh, Yasmine, Ishmael, Keira, Jhu).
  • High Intelligence scaling boosts Magic Attack, enhancing both heals and debuffs.

❌ Cons

  • Vulnerable to burst damage due to being a backline support with moderate health.
  • Relies heavily on allies’ positioning and stats to maximize healing and buffs.
  • Passive critical hit bonus (Inordinate Fury) only affects physical damage allies; mages’ skills do not benefit fully.
  • Skills can be interrupted, reducing effectiveness in intense PvP or burst scenarios.

Jet Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn which Jet skills to upgrade first in Hero Wars: Dominion Era! Understand how each skill works and why some upgrades matter more than others.

Elixir of Vigor

Jet channels a life-giving elixir toward the ally with the highest Physical Attack. While the effect is active, that ally gains increased Physical Attack, 30% faster attack speed, and regenerates Health every second until Jet’s energy runs out or he’s interrupted.

Formula: (20% Magic Attack + Ivl * 50) Physical Attack boost.
Health regen per second: (25% Magic Attack + Ivl * 50).

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Jet’s signature skill and his main source of team support. It can dramatically increase your main damage dealer’s performance, especially in physical teams like Dante, Yasmine, or K’arkh compositions.

Acid Shot

Jet fires acid at the enemy with the highest Armor, reducing their Armor for 8 seconds. This helps your damage dealers hit harder, especially against tough tanks.

Formula: (10% Magic Attack + Ivl * 25) Armor Reduction.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – The effect is strong in physical teams, but less impactful if your main heroes deal magic or pure damage. Upgrade after his main and passive abilities.

Potion of Fatigue

Jet throws a poisoned flask that prevents nearby enemies from regenerating Health for 8 seconds. While useful, this effect has limited value in endgame fights where most opponents rely on shield or burst mechanics instead of healing.

Evolution Priority: Medium – The healing block can help against specific heroes like Maya or Celeste, but it’s situational. Upgrade last, since the impact is smaller in high-level fights where healing is less dominant.

Inordinate Fury

While Jet is alive, all allies gain an increased chance to land critical hits. This passive effect makes the entire team more dangerous, especially heroes who rely on critical damage.

Formula: (2.5% Magic Attack + Ivl * 15 + 100) Critical Hit Chance bonus.

Evolution Priority: High – This passive runs constantly, boosting all allies without requiring activation. It’s particularly effective in teams with critical-based damage dealers, multiplying Jet’s usefulness throughout the battle.

💡 Important Note

When the Inordinate Fury arrow appears above your allies’ heads during battle, it means they are receiving a critical hit chance bonus. However, this bonus only works for physical attack heroes. Magic-based heroes do not benefit from this effect unless they have physical basic attacks. In that case, they can land critical hits with their basic attacks, but not with their skills.

Jet Glyph Evolution Priority

Upgrade Jet’s glyphs smartly this guide keeps the game order and ranks each glyph by real battlefield impact so you know what to evolve first.

1st Glyph - Magic Attack

This glyph raises Jet’s Magic Attack, which directly increases the chance of critical hit from allies, the power of his heals, buffs, and debuffs the single most effective stat for Jet.

Stats gain: +6,500 Magic Attack

Evolution Priority: Very High – Max this glyph first. Because most of Jet’s skills scale with Magic Attack, boosting this stat yields the largest and most consistent improvement to his Elixir healing, Inordinate Fury scaling, Acid Shot potency, and overall team impact.

2nd Glyph - Health

This glyph increases Jet’s total Health, improving survivability so he can keep casting and sustaining allies in long fights.

Stats gain: +62,200 Health

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Upgrade this after Magic Attack (or alongside it if you face heavy burst). Extra Health helps Jet survive longer to maintain his buffs and heals, which is crucial in prolonged or high-damage encounters.

3rd Glyph - Magic Defense

This glyph raises Jet’s Magic Defense, reducing incoming magic damage from enemy mages and AoE casters.

Stats gain: +6,500 Magic Defense

Evolution Priority: Medium – Useful when your opponents rely on magic damage. It’s a situational defensive upgrade; prioritize it after you’ve secured Magic Attack and a base of survivability (Health).

4th Glyph - Dodge

This glyph adds Dodge, giving Jet a chance to avoid physical attacks. Dodge is less reliable because it’s chance-based and situational.

Stats gain: +1,995 Dodge

Evolution Priority: Low – Upgrade this last. Dodge can help occasionally, but it’s inconsistent and provides less predictable value than raw Magic Attack, Intelligence, or large Health pools.

5th Glyph - Intelligence

This glyph increases Jet’s Intelligence (his main stat), which converts into multiple benefits: Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and a small Physical Attack bump.

Stats gain: Intelligence +1,135

  • - Magic Attack from Intelligence: +3,405
  • - Magic Defense from Intelligence: +1,135
  • - Physical Attack from Intelligence: +1,135

Evolution Priority: High – Even though it’s listed last visually in the game UI, Intelligence is an important early-to-mid upgrade because it provides a balanced boost to Jet’s primary scaling (Magic Attack) plus defensive value. Treat it as the second-most important glyph after Magic Attack when planning evolution order.

How to Counter Jet - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn which heroes are effective against Jet in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. These counters exploit his weaknesses, especially his reliance on Intelligence and support buffs.

Amira

Why Amira counters Jet: Amira exploits Jet’s high Intelligence and the critical hit buffs he gives allies. Her Art of Deceit boosts healing on Intelligence-based enemies but simultaneously reduces their Magic Attack and deals damage, directly weakening Jet. Her Desperate Fury ensures that Jet’s agility allies miss critical hits, neutralizing the bonus from Inordinate Fury.

Cornelius

Why Cornelius counters Jet: Cornelius targets enemies with the highest Intelligence using Heavy Wisdom, dealing massive damage proportional to Intelligence. This directly punishes Jet’s high stat scaling, reducing his survivability and effectiveness in prolonged battles.

Helios

Why Helios counters Jet: Helios’ Flaming Retribution punishes critical hits from enemies while Solar Wind is active. Since Jet buffs his allies’ critical hit chance, Helios reflects that aggression back onto Jet and his team, mitigating the impact of Jet’s passive and offensive support.

Kayla

Why Kayla counters Jet: Kayla’s Possessed by Fire allows her to leap behind Jet and self-ignite, dealing sustained damage while renewing Phoenix Glyphs. Jet is a backline support and vulnerable to heroes who can bypass his front line, making Kayla’s mobility and damage over time highly effective against him.

Conclusion

Jet is a versatile and powerful backline support, excelling at healing, buffing physical allies, and disrupting enemy defenses. His skills, when paired with the right heroes, pets, and artifacts, can turn the tide of battle by maximizing team survivability and critical damage potential.
