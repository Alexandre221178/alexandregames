Astrid and Lucas are not a conventional Marksman. Astrid does not gain Energy at all: every attack fills a red Rage bar until Lucas charges into the front line, absorbs enemy hits without taking normal damage, and turns the fight into a frantic hunt. That single rule makes the duo genuinely different from every other hero in Hero Wars: Dominion Era.

Their reworked kit finally makes the partnership feel complete. Lucas now threatens groups with stronger area attacks, Astrid supports both of them through Rampant Nature, and the shared Hunter's Mark opens an exciting connection with Adam and other Hunters. In my view, the duo is at its best when you build around this rhythm instead of treating Astrid as just another back-line shooter.

Who Are Astrid and Lucas in Hero Wars: Dominion Era?

Astrid is an Agility Marksman who fights beside Lucas, her trusted pet. Astrid fires from the back while Lucas changes the shape of the battlefield: at full Rage, he runs forward, protects allies by drawing attacks, and mauls nearby enemies. Their Hunter identity matters more after Ascension, because marked enemies can feed Astrid more Rage whenever allied Hunters damage them.

  • Main Role: Marksman
  • Additional Roles: Warrior, Tank, Hunter
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Position: #57, back line
  • How to Get Soul Stones: Campaign, Soul Chests, Soul Atrium
  • Pet Patronage: Fenris, Mara, Vex
  • Best Synergy: Adam and Hunter teams

Astrid's Unique Rage Mechanic

Astrid gains 17% Rage with every attack instead of collecting Energy. At 100%, her ultimate becomes available and releases Lucas. This means Energy-control heroes such as Satori and Jorgen cannot delay her resource bar in the usual way. Watch the red bar under her health: her timing depends on attacks, not on damage received or Energy manipulation.

Astrid and Lucas Pros and Cons - Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Unique Rage resource ignores normal Energy-control strategies.
  • Lucas protects allies by moving to the front and taking enemy hits.
  • Strong area pressure and ramping damage while Lucas is enraged.
  • Hunter's Mark enables repeatable stuns and synergy with Adam.
  • Ascension can give every allied Hunter 30% damage reduction.
  • Very high Physical Attack and Armor Penetration at maximum investment.

❌ Cons

  • Slow 5-second basic attack makes Rage timing vulnerable to interruptions.
  • Lucas loses 7% Rage whenever an enemy hits him, shortening his active window.
  • Full team value depends on late Ascension upgrades and Hunter allies.
  • Control is conditional: enemies must carry Hunter's Mark when Lucas attacks.
  • Low Magic Defense leaves Astrid exposed to strong magic burst.
  • No built-in healing, dodge, vampirism, or critical-hit chance.

Astrid and Lucas Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

For beginners, Rampant Nature gives the most immediate improvement to the duo's complete rotation. After that, invest in Lucas's damage and Astrid's mark reliability; the Ascension bonuses are powerful, but they arrive much later.

1 - Go for it, Lucas! (White/Ultimate)

In-game description: Each time Astrid attacks, she gains 17% Rage. Once her Rage reaches 100%, Astrid can use her first skill: her pet takes on its Rage form and rushes to the front row, taking hits instead of other characters. Attacks cannot damage the pet, but each enemy hit reduces its Rage by 7%. While it has Rage remaining, the pet attacks the nearest enemies. Once its Rage is depleted, it returns to its passive form.

Skill Explanation: Astrid needs roughly six uninterrupted basic attacks to fill the bar from zero. Activating the skill sends Lucas forward, where he cannot lose health from ordinary attacks; hits instead consume Rage and shorten his stay. Lucas then deals area physical damage of 55,367 per attack, so Physical Attack boosts both Astrid's shots and his assault.

Formula - Physical Damage: 55,367 (35% Physical Attack + 135 × level)

Evolution Priority: High - 2nd – This is the core transformation and a major damage source. Rampant Nature comes first because its speed boost helps Astrid reach full Rage sooner and improves both partners.

Skill Animation Info

  • Rage gained per Astrid attack: 17%
  • Rage lost per enemy hit on Lucas: 7%
  • Area: Enemies nearest to Lucas
  • Keywords: Rage, front row, area physical damage, damage interception

2 - Rampant Nature (Green)

In-game description: Astrid increases attack speed and skill use speed for 9s. If her pet is in its Rage form, this effect applies to both of them. Both also gain this effect each time the pet takes on its Rage form.

Skill Explanation: The updated skill benefits Astrid and Lucas together. Faster attacks mean quicker Rage generation for Astrid and more Lucas attacks before enemies drain his bar. At maximum level, their attack and skill speed rises by 159.8% for nine seconds, making this the engine that connects every other ability.

Formula - Speed Increase: 159.8% (0.46 × level + 100)

Evolution Priority: Very High - 1st – Level this first. It accelerates Rage generation, basic attacks, skill use, and Lucas's entire active window, giving a beginner the broadest return for every resource spent.

  • Initial Cooldown: 1 second
  • Cooldown: 20 seconds
  • Duration: 9 seconds
  • Keywords: Attack speed, skill speed, Rage form

2 - Hardened Temper - Ascension II Skill

In-game description: While Rampant Nature is active, Astrid takes 10%, 20%, and finally 30% less damage. At grade 3, the 30% damage reduction also applies to all Hunters on the team.

Skill Explanation: Unlocking Ascension II turns the speed window into a defensive window. The early grades protect Astrid herself; grade 3 is the real prize, extending 30% damage reduction to every Hunter while Rampant Nature is active. It is excellent, but beginners should remember that its team-wide value requires both late investment and the right allies.

3 - Predator's Charge (Blue/Passive)

In-game description: Passive skill. As long as Astrid's pet is in Rage form, each consecutive attack deals increasing damage.

Skill Explanation: Every uninterrupted Lucas attack becomes stronger than the last. At level 130, each consecutive hit adds 7% damage. Rampant Nature is crucial here because more attacks during the same Rage window mean more stacks and a much stronger end to Lucas's charge.

Formula - Damage Increase per Attack: 7% (0.05 × level + 0.50)

Evolution Priority: High - 3rd – This is Lucas's scaling damage passive. Upgrade it after the first two skills because it only contributes while Lucas is already in Rage form.

  • Keywords: Passive, Rage form, consecutive attacks, stacking damage

4 - Hunter's Mark (Violet/Passive)

In-game description: Passive skill. Each of the Hero's attacks applies Hunter's Mark to the target for 4 seconds. If the pet attacks a marked target, it briefly stuns them. Chance to stun is lowered if the target's level is above 130.

Skill Explanation: Astrid marks what she shoots, and Lucas turns those marks into short stuns. His area attacks can stun every marked enemy they hit, not only the primary target. The reworked mark is no longer treated as a negative effect, so Sebastian and similar cleansing abilities cannot block or remove it. It is also the same Hunter's Mark used by Adam.

Evolution Priority: Medium High - 4th – Keep it leveled so the stun chance remains reliable against enemies near Astrid's level. Damage skills come first for beginners, but neglecting this passive removes much of her control value.

  • Duration: 4 seconds
  • Keywords: Hunter's Mark, stun, unremovable mark, Hunter synergy

4 - The Thrill of the Hunt - Ascension V Skill

In-game description: Astrid gains 100, 200, 300, and finally 500 Rage at the start of battle. At grade 3, whenever a target affected by Hunter's Mark takes damage from any Hunter, Astrid gains an additional 30 Rage.

Skill Explanation: Starting Rage releases Lucas earlier, before the enemy has fully established its rotation. Grade 3 changes the whole team plan: each Hunter hit on a marked target grants 30 additional Rage. Adam is especially interesting because both heroes share the same mark. This is a late-game unlock, so it should strengthen an already functional build rather than distract a beginner from the four basic skills.

Best Pet Patronage for Astrid and Lucas

Astrid can receive patronage from Fenris, Mara, or Vex. The best choice depends on whether you need raw physical pressure, longer control, or more focused damage.

1 - Fenris: Best General Patronage

Fenris strengthens the physical-damage plan and helps Astrid pressure armored targets. This is my first choice when the team needs Astrid and Lucas to deal consistent damage rather than extend control.

Patronage Priority: Very High

2 - Mara: Best Control Patronage

Mara complements Hunter's Mark when your strategy depends on Lucas keeping marked targets stunned. Choose her when controlling the enemy rotation matters more than adding raw physical pressure.

Patronage Priority: High

3 - Vex: Offensive Alternative

Vex suits compositions built to concentrate damage on the enemy Astrid is already marking. It is a strong alternative when your lineup is designed to focus and eliminate one target quickly.

Patronage Priority: Medium High

Best Synergy Heroes for Astrid and Lucas

Adam is the clearest partner because he uses the same Hunter's Mark. Once The Thrill of the Hunt reaches grade 3, his damage against marked targets helps generate 30 Rage for Astrid, bringing Lucas back into action sooner.

The key is not simply adding damage dealers. Astrid wants Hunters who can repeatedly touch marked targets during her four-second mark window. At maximum Hardened Temper, those allies also receive 30% damage reduction during Rampant Nature, so the same team loop improves offense and survival together.

Conclusion

Astrid and Lucas reward patience more than most Marksmen. The early build can feel slow because Astrid's five-second basic attack delays Rage, but Rampant Nature changes that rhythm dramatically. Prioritize speed, Armor Penetration, and Physical Attack, then let Ascension turn the duo into the center of a Hunter composition.

What makes them worth building is not only damage. Lucas temporarily creates a front line, marked targets become vulnerable to stuns, and Energy counters cannot interfere with Astrid's red Rage bar. If you enjoy heroes with a distinctive mechanic and a team that grows stronger through coordination, this duo offers one of the most interesting playstyles in Dominion Era.
