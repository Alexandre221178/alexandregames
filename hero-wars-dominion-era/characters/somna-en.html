Somna does not enter a fight to top the damage chart. She enters to steal time. One ultimate can put an entire enemy team to sleep, and every Sleepiness stack makes the next exchange safer for your side.

In my tests, that control translated surprisingly well into both physical and magic teams. Somna gave Adam the long battle he wants, while Fluffy, Cascade and Krista gained a support who could interrupt the enemy before their pressure collapsed.

Who Is Somna in Hero Wars: Dominion Era?

Somna is a middle-line Agility control hero built around Sleep and Sleepiness. Her ultimate can pause all five opponents, while the rest of her kit reduces enemy Physical and Magic Attack, reinforces the ally in front, and turns heavily affected enemies into fleeing sheep.

My view is simple: Somna is at her best when the team has a reason to buy time. She does not finish opponents herself; she creates the silence between their attacks so Adam, Fluffy, Cascade, Krista or another win condition can take over.

  • Main Role: Control
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Position: Fights at the middle line
  • Patronage: Fenris, Oliver, Axel
  • Soul Stones: Temporarily unobtainable

Somna Overall Rating in the Meta

  • Control: ★★★★★
  • Damage: ★★☆☆☆
  • Synergy: ★★★★★
  • Independence: ★★☆☆☆

That low Independence score is not a criticism; it explains how to use her. Somna is an amplifier for a plan. Give her a team that profits from extra seconds and her value rises sharply.

Somna Pros and Cons - Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Teamwide control: Lullaby Veil can put the entire enemy formation to sleep for 7 seconds.
  • Excellent tempo support: Sleep and sheep transformations create time for scaling allies to become dangerous.
  • Reduces both damage types: Sleepiness lowers Physical and Magic Attack, so it is useful beyond physical matchups.
  • Protects the frontline: Dream Armor gives a large Armor boost to the ally in front of Somna.
  • Flexible team fit: My tests produced good results in physical and magic compositions.

❌ Cons

  • Low personal damage: Somna controls the battle but normally needs allies to secure kills.
  • Sleep can break early: Opponents wake after receiving 107,633 damage, so uncontrolled area damage can shorten the pause.
  • Sebastian is a direct counter: His protection can block the control effect that defines Somna's ultimate.
  • Level checks matter: Her control chances are reduced against enemies above the listed skill level.
  • Limited availability: Her Soul Stones are temporarily unobtainable.

Somna Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Somna's skills should be leveled for reliable control first, then frontline protection. Her Physical Attack scaling improves effects, but control remains the real prize.

1st Skill - Lullaby Veil

In-game description: Puts enemy Heroes to sleep for 7s. Sleeping opponents cannot act but can wake up prematurely if they receive 107,633 damage. Sleep chance is lowered if the target's level is above 130.

Skill Explanation: Somna pauses the whole enemy team. Each sleeping hero stays inactive until the 7 seconds end or incoming damage reaches the threshold. The threshold is not damage dealt by Somna; it is how much damage the sleeping target can take before waking.

Formula - Damage Threshold: 107,633 (100% Physical Attack + 120 × level)

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Somna's identity and strongest opening for the team. Keep it leveled so its control check remains as reliable as possible.

  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Keywords: Ultimate, Sleep, Control

2nd Skill - Dream Armor

In-game description: Blesses the ally in the front for 10s. The blessing increases their Armor by 34,110 and adds the Sleepiness effect to their basic attacks. Each Sleepiness effect on an opponent decreases their Physical and Magic Attack by 26,908. The effect lasts for 10s and can be applied up to 3 times. Sleepiness chance is lowered if the target's level is above 130.

Skill Explanation: Somna protects the ally directly ahead of her and turns that ally's basic attacks into debuffs. At three stacks, the enemy can lose up to 80,724 Physical and Magic Attack, which is why this skill can quietly decide a long fight.

Formula - Armor Increase: 34,110 (30% Physical Attack + 50 × level)

Formula - Attack Decrease per Stack: 26,908 (25% Physical Attack + 30 × level)

Evolution Priority: High – It adds defense and offensive suppression in one cast, but it supports Somna's control plan rather than starting it.

  • Initial Cooldown: 10 seconds
  • Cooldown: 16 seconds
  • Duration: 10 seconds
  • Maximum Sleepiness Stacks: 3

3rd Skill - Counting Sheep

In-game description: Curses an area in the center of the enemy team. Opponents in the area receive 31,510 damage, and 1 Sleepiness effect is applied to them. If an affected enemy already has 3 Sleepiness effects, they turn into a sheep for 5s. They cannot attack and try to run for their lives. Sleepiness chance is lowered if the target's level is above 130.

Skill Explanation: This is Somna's repeating control loop. The 31,510 physical damage is modest, but the real reward is turning a fully stacked enemy into a harmless sheep for 5 seconds.

Formula - Physical Damage: 31,510 (30% Physical Attack + 30 × level)

Evolution Priority: Very High – Repeated sheep transformations are Somna's best control after her ultimate, and skill level affects whether Sleepiness lands.

  • Initial Cooldown: 5 seconds
  • Cooldown: 13 seconds
  • Sheep Duration: 5 seconds
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Sleepiness, Control

4th Skill - Domain of Tranquility

In-game description: Passive skill. When an opponent deals damage to Somna or adjacent allies, 1 Sleepiness effect is applied to the enemy who dealt the damage. If the damage was dealt by an ultimate ability, 2 Sleepiness effects are applied. This effect can trigger no more often than once every 1s. Sleepiness chance is lowered if the target's level is above 130.

Skill Explanation: Enemies help Somna build her control simply by attacking her area. Ultimate damage is punished harder with two stacks, creating a fast path toward Counting Sheep's transformation.

Evolution Priority: High – It accelerates Somna's whole Sleepiness engine, especially against area and ultimate damage, but it still needs her active control skills to cash in those stacks.

  • Cooldown: 1 second per trigger
  • Keywords: Passive, Sleepiness, Control

Best Patronage for Somna

Only Fenris, Oliver and Axel can patron Somna. Fenris maximizes her Physical Attack scaling, while Oliver and Axel trade some offense for survival.

1st Place:

Fenris

Fenris is my first choice when Somna is safe enough to play aggressively. His Physical Attack raises all of her formulas, Armor Penetration improves Counting Sheep's damage, and Blind Rage can add a 2-second Blind to her basic attacks.

2nd Place:

Oliver

Oliver is the best sustain option and worked well in the tested magic composition. Health, Armor and repeated healing help Somna survive long enough to cycle Sleepiness and sheep transformations again.

3rd Place:

Axel

Axel is the burst-protection choice. Defensive Blub prevents one hit from removing too much of Somna's maximum Health, although his Magic Attack patronage stat does little for her Physical Attack formulas.

How to Counter Somna Hero Wars Dominion Era

Somna's threat is concentrated in control. The cleanest answer is a hero who prevents that control before her allies can exploit the free time.

Sebastian

Sebastian is Somna's direct counter because his control protection can block Sleep and prevent her ultimate from freezing the entire team. Without that long pause, Somna has much less time to build Sleepiness into sheep transformations. Khorus should not be treated as the same answer here: Somna's Sleep is specifically not blocked by Khorus.

Alexandre's Tip: Why Doesn't Khorus Block Somna's Sleep?

Because Khorus's skill simply was not programmed to block the Sleep effect.

The Indomitable, Khorus's passive skill, blocks only the following control effects:

  • Silence
  • Charm
  • Blind
  • Stun
  • Mind Control

Sleep is not included in that official list. In practice, this means:

  • ✅ Keira's Silence → Khorus can block it.
  • ✅ Arachne's Stun → Khorus can block it.
  • ✅ Phobos's Mind Control → Khorus can block it.
  • ❌ Somna's Sleep → Khorus does not block it.

Final Thoughts on Somna - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Somna is the kind of hero whose best work happens between the obvious moments. She interrupts an ultimate, weakens an attacker, protects the ally in front and suddenly your damage dealer has survived long enough to win.

From my tests, I would not lock her into one archetype. She was convincing beside Adam in a physical long-fight core and equally interesting as control support for Fluffy, Cascade and Krista. If you enjoy teams that win through timing and pressure instead of one explosive opening, Somna deserves your attention.
