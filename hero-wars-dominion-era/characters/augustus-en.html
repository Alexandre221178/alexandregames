Augustus is one of those heroes who immediately captures players’ curiosity a calm, sky-touched ambassador who speaks softly but strikes with storms. In this guide, you’ll learn how to unlock his true destructive potential, from his thunder bursts to his synergy with Orion, the mighty pet Khorus, and even the newest frontline protector, Electra. Who Is Augustus in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Augustus is a powerful back-line mage whose thunder magic deals massive burst damage while offering excellent synergy with energy-boosting and magic penetration allies.

  • Main Role: Mage
  • Additional: Burst Damage, Magic Synergy
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Position: Fights at the Back Line

Despite his serene personality, Augustus becomes a terrifying force when paired with heroes who enhance magic attack or accelerate skill activation. His lightning abilities scale extremely well, making him a top-tier option for magic-centric teams.

He excels in fast-building combos, especially when supported by Orion’s rapid energy cycling and strong magic penetration, Khorus’s magic amplification from shield effects, and Electra’s reliable front-line protection with her magic attack weapon artifact and powerful damage absorption that help stabilize long battles.

✅ Pros

  • Excellent Pure damage scaling, especially when combined with Albus or magic-penetration teams.
  • Strong area damage thanks to Lightning Rod and damage transfer effects.
  • Synergizes extremely well with Orion due to rapid ultimate activation and Magic Penetration artifact boosts.
  • High impact in long fights where his damage amplification ramps up significantly.
  • Useful against magic-tanky teams because part of his damage bypasses Magic Defense.

❌ Cons

  • Relies heavily on Magic Attack gear and artifact evolution to reach full potential.
  • Not very durable without support; vulnerable to early burst unless paired with Axel or strong frontline.
  • Damage transfer effect decreases sharply with distance, making positioning important.
  • Struggles against high-dodge teams without sufficient accuracy support.
  • Requires proper team setup to shine performs poorly in random or unoptimized lineups.

Augustus Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Master Augustus: learn his lightning formulas, exact damage numbers and the optimal skill upgrade order to quickly maximize burst and team synergy.

1 - Lightning Rod

Lightning Rod hits the enemy with the highest Health and deals heavy magic damage. Simple explanation for beginners: it takes a percentage of the target’s health (very strong vs tanks) but is capped by Augustus’s magic-scaling maximum.

Formula Target Health: 40% Health

Formula Max Magic Damage: 428,286 (max: 300% Magic Attack + (Level 130) × 150)

How it works in plain terms: if the target has very high HP, Lightning Rod will deal 40% of that HP but if that would exceed Augustus’s cap, the game uses the capped value shown above.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Augustus’s main burst nuke; upgrade first to maximize his ability to remove or cripple high-HP frontliners immediately.

2 - Path of Least Resistance

This passive converts Augustus’s basic attacks into steady 83,081 magic damage and forces him to hit the enemy with the lowest Magic Defense. For a new player: Augustus will always try to burn the enemy that is easiest for magic to hurt, so he finishes supports and fragile mages quickly.

Formula Magic Damage: 83,081 (50% Magic Attack + (Level 130) × 115)

Why this matters: it keeps Augustus dealing damage consistently between his big hits. Because Silence doesn't stop it, this skill is very reliable.

Evolution Priority: High – Boost this early after Lightning Rod to increase Augustus’s sustained DPS and kill priority targets faster.

3 - Grounded

Whenever Augustus deals damage with Lightning Rod or his basic attack, all nearby enemies take a portion of 53.5% that hit. Think of it as a "splash" effect: the main target takes full damage and nearby enemies suffer part of it as well.

Total Damage: 53.5% of the triggering damage

Formula Damage Transfer: 53.5% ((Level 110) × 0.35 + 15%)

Simple example: if Augustus hits someone for 100,000, Grounded can transfer up to 53,500 to nearby enemies (scaled by distance). It’s powerful when enemies are grouped.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Excellent for AoE and teamfights, but less urgent than his direct single-target nuke and core attack boost.

4 - Superconductivity

Superconductivity applies a stacking debuff that reduces an enemy’s Magic Defense for 6s each time Augustus hits. When Magic Defense is fully penetrated, extra magic damage converts into pure damage which ignores resistances.

Formula Magic Defense Reduction: -3993.93 (1.5% Magic Attack + (Level 90) × 15 + 600)

Plainly: every hit weakens the enemy against magic. Over time this makes Augustus and all magic allies hit much harder. It’s an indirect but team-amplifying effect.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Great in long fights and magic comps, but relies on other skills landing first, so upgrade after direct-damage abilities.

Note: This skill has exceptional synergy with Orion. Orion is the fastest ultimate attacker in Hero Wars and one of the few heroes who rapidly gains Energy, allowing him to trigger his ultimate repeatedly. Every time Orion casts his ultimate, he also activates his weapon artifact, granting Magic Penetration to all allies. This penetration significantly amplifies the effectiveness of Augustus’s ability, letting Augustus convert even more damage into pure damage against enemies.

    Best Patronage for Augustus

    Augustus benefits the most from pets that amplify his Magic Attack, boost his Pure damage output, or increase the frequency and survival of his powerful area-damage skills.

    1st Place:

    Albus

    Albus is Augustus’s strongest patron due to his ability to increase all Pure damage dealt by the master. Augustus’s skill kit naturally converts a portion of his damage into Pure damage, so Albus directly amplifies his most dangerous output. Combined with high Magic Attack scaling, Albus produces the highest damage spikes in long fights and boss encounters.

    2nd Place:

    Merlin

    Merlin is the best alternative when focusing on magic burst speed and penetration. His patronage grants Magic Penetration and accelerates the cast speed of magic-damage skills, letting Augustus activate his abilities faster and break through enemy defenses more reliably. Excellent in magic-offense teams with Orion or Krista/Lars.

    3rd Place:

    Axel

    Axel is the best defensive option. His damage-splitting passive prevents Augustus from being one-shot and increases his survival in burst-heavy matchups. Since Augustus needs time on the battlefield to ramp his damage and pure-damage effects, Axel’s protection significantly improves his consistency against strong physical or magic assassins.

    4th Place:

    Biscuit

    Biscuit works well in anti-healing compositions. Augustus deals constant multi-target magic damage, so reducing enemy healing every time he hits is useful against heroes like Martha, Celeste, or Aurora teams with heavy regeneration. However, Biscuit adds no pure damage scaling, so he ranks lower for overall damage potential.

    5th Place:

    Khorus

    Khorus provides shielding based on magic damage dealt, which can help Augustus survive physical bursts. However, the shield synergy works better on heroes with extremely high hit frequency like Orion. Since Augustus focuses on large, slower damage instances rather than rapid ticks, he does not fully benefit from Khorus’s aura, placing it as the weakest patronage choice for him.

    Augustus Best War Flag Hero Wars

    Discover the best War Flags for Augustus in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, focusing only on the flags that truly enhance his energy flow, skill uptime, and teamwide magic synergy.

    War Flag of Readiness

    The War Flag of Readiness is the strongest option for Augustus because he always stands in the back line, guaranteeing he receives its bonus Energy at the start of the fight and every 20 seconds. This accelerates his first skill use and increases his overall impact throughout the battle.

    Augustus and Team Benefit: Earlier Energy allows Augustus to activate his supportive and damage-enhancing skills much sooner, improving team burst potential and perfectly synergizing with mage-heavy compositions especially teams using Orion for continuous magic penetration activation.

    War Flag of Pet Strength

    The War Flag of Pet Strength boosts Pet Skill Power by 10%, directly increasing the efficiency of Augustus’s patron pet in battle. Because Augustus frequently supports magic-heavy teams, the enhanced pet skill boosts group damage or protection depending on the chosen patron.

    Augustus and Team Benefit: Stronger pet skills improve both offensive and defensive plays, making this flag especially good when running offensive pets like Mara or defensive pets like Axel.

    War Flag of Frost

    War Flag of Frost automatically reduces enemy skill levels every 18 seconds. This provides valuable disruption against enemy supports, healers, and control heroes giving Augustus’s team an easier time surviving and bursting down opponents.

    Augustus and Team Benefit: Lower enemy skill levels directly reduce enemy healing, control duration, and damage, making this flag powerful for control-based or mage-heavy Augustus teams that aim to outlast and overwhelm the enemy.

    How to Counter Augustus – Hero Wars: Dominion Era

    Augustus relies heavily on Magic Attack, Intelligence scaling, and teamwide magical amplification. To counter him effectively, you must target his weaknesses: his high Intelligence, his dependence on uninterrupted skill casts, and his reliance on magic-damage–based team compositions. The counters below exploit these weaknesses directly through anti-magic mechanics, Intelligence destruction, and magic-damage shutdown.

    Cornelius

    Why Cornelius Counters Augustus: Cornelius is one of the hardest counters to Augustus because his ultimate targets the enemy with the highest Intelligence usually Augustus. The monolith deals damage based on the target’s Intelligence, allowing Cornelius to bypass Augustus's defenses and eliminate him quickly. Additionally, Cornelius reduces the Magic Attack of the strongest mage on the enemy team, weakening Augustus’s teamwide magic amplification. His magic defense shield further reduces Augustus’s damage output.

    Folio

    Why Folio Counters Augustus: Folio directly attacks Augustus’s main stat by stealing Intelligence from the enemy with the highest Intelligence for 15 seconds. Since Augustus heavily depends on Intelligence for damage, survivability, and skill scaling, this theft greatly reduces his impact. If the Intelligence steal fails or is blocked, Folio instead deals burst magic damage that scales with his own Magic Attack often enough to break Augustus’s sustain. Folio’s skill cycle weakens Augustus’s magical support for the entire enemy team.

    Isaac

    Why Isaac Counters Augustus: Isaac is the strongest anti-magic hero in the game. Tes’Lin's Magic Power Capacitor absorbs a portion of all incoming magic damage and converts it into Charge instead of Energy, which denies Augustus’s team the Energy they expect to gain from magical hits. This shuts down mage-based teams and prevents Augustus’s allies from triggering their ultimates on time. When Isaac reaches full charge, he releases a teamwide silence that prevents Augustus from casting his skills, stopping his support and magic amplification entirely.

    Rufus

    Why Rufus Counters Augustus: Rufus is one of the strongest defensive counters to Augustus because he cannot be killed by Magic Damage or Pure Damage two of Augustus’s main damage types. His skill Rakashi's Barrier covers the entire team with a shield that absorbs all incoming magic damage, massively reducing Augustus’s burst and sustained damage. Since Augustus relies heavily on magic pressure and pure damage amplification for his team, Rufus completely neutralizes his offensive impact, making Augustus nearly useless against teams protected by Rufus.

    Augustus Guide Conclusion

    Augustus stands out as a powerful backline support capable of amplifying magic damage, reinforcing shields, and enabling devastating burst combos when paired with the right heroes. His ability to enhance team synergy makes him a valuable addition to many modern Dominion Era strategies. When properly equipped with the best War Flags and supported by strong magic allies, Augustus becomes a core piece in high-level battles.

    Building teams around Augustus requires understanding his strengths and identifying the perfect partners heroes like Orion, Aidan, and Nebula elevate his full potential. With the proper composition and tactical choices, Augustus can significantly shift the outcome of battles, offering both utility and explosive power. Mastering his setups ensures consistent performance in Guild Wars, Adventures, and Arena.
