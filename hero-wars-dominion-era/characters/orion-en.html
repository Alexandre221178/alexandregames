Who Is Orion in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Orion is a pure back-line mage built around burst magic damage, repeated casts, and punishing enemies before they can set up. He does not need to stand in the front to control the pace of battle because his energy engine and repeated missiles do the work for him.

  • Main Role: Mage
  • Additional Role: Burst Damage / Control
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Position: Fights at the Back Line (Position 47)
  • Patronage: Merlin, Axel, Khorus, Mara, Biscuit

Orion Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Web and Facebook

Pros

  • Excellent burst mage with repeated casts thanks to his passive energy gain.
  • Very strong synergy with heroes and pets that improve magic damage, slows, or survival.
  • Can pressure both PvP and PvE because his damage starts fast and scales well with upgrades.
  • His weapon artifact improves the whole team's magic damage output, not only his own.

Cons

  • Back-line survivability is average, so he can fall quickly to burst or anti-mage counters.
  • He performs much better with support than alone, especially against magic-resistant enemies.
  • Control is useful but not fully reliable against overleveled enemies because level checks matter.
  • If his first casts are delayed, his team can lose the tempo advantage that makes him dangerous.

Orion Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Orion should first improve the skills that make him cast faster and burst harder, then invest in control tools that become even better when ascension upgrades are unlocked later.

1 - Total Destruction

Total Destruction is Orion's ultimate burst tool. He fires 6 rockets at the enemies with the highest health, and each rocket deals 62,098 magic damage. This is easy to understand in battle: Orion aims at the healthiest targets so he can quickly break the enemy core instead of wasting his burst on almost dead heroes.

Formula - Magic Damage: 62,098 (45% Magic Attack + 50 x Level)

Ascension V - Tactical Destruction: When an enemy is stunned or slowed, they receive a mark. When Orion uses this skill, he launches an extra rocket at every marked target and each extra rocket deals 41,866 magic damage. Because Ascension V comes late, the base skill is already worth leveling, and ascension simply pushes it even higher.

Formula - Ascension Bonus Damage: 41,866 (30% Magic Attack + 4,800)

Evolution Priority: High – This is Orion's main burst button, so upgrading it keeps his kill pressure relevant from the early game to endgame. It becomes even better once your account eventually unlocks the ascension extra rockets.

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Magic damage, burst, marked targets
  • Area: Enemies with the highest health
  • Hits: 6 rockets, plus extra ascension rockets on marked targets

2 - Magnetic Field

Magnetic Field explodes on the enemy back line, deals 50,721 magic damage in an area, and slows enemies for 4 seconds. In simple terms, this skill helps Orion touch the heroes hiding behind the tank line and makes them easier to finish because the slow can also prepare targets for his ascension ultimate.

Formula - Magic Damage: 50,721 (40% Magic Attack + 10 x Level)

Evolution Priority: Medium – The slow and back-line reach are valuable, but Orion can already do his main job without maxing this first. Upgrade it after the skills that directly define his burst rhythm.

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 14.5 seconds
  • Keywords: Magic damage, slow, back-line pressure
  • Duration: 4 seconds
  • Area: Enemy back line area

3 - Antimatter Blast

Antimatter Blast fires a missile at the nearest enemy, deals 106,642 magic damage, and stuns that target for 4 seconds. For players who want the easy explanation: this is Orion's control shot. It locks down the closest threat while still hitting hard enough to matter.

Formula - Magic Damage: 106,642 (80% Magic Attack + 60 x Level)

Ascension II - Antimatter Chain: After the first hit, the projectile bounces to the 2 nearest enemies. Each bounce deals 53,321 magic damage and stuns for 1 second. Because this upgrade unlocks earlier than Ascension V, it is a meaningful future boost, but the base single-target control is already the main reason to level the skill.

Formula - Ascension Bounce Damage: 53,321 (40% Magic Attack + 3,900)

Evolution Priority: Medium High – This skill adds strong control and a heavy burst hit, so it deserves good investment. It stays below Orion's top priorities because his energy engine and main ultimate usually decide more fights first.

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 5 seconds
  • Cooldown: 20 seconds
  • Keywords: Magic damage, stun, chain projectile
  • Duration: 4 seconds base stun, 1 second ascension bounce stun
  • Range: Nearest enemy
  • Hits: 1 base hit, plus 2 ascension bounces

4 - Full Charge

Full Charge is the passive that makes Orion feel fast. Every attack gives him 550 extra energy, which means he reaches Total Destruction much earlier and cycles back to it faster than many other mages. If you ever wondered why Orion seems to cast nonstop, this is the answer.

Formula - Extra Energy: 550 (5 x Level - 100)

Evolution Priority: Very High – For beginners this is the upgrade that changes Orion the most because more energy means more ultimates, more artifact activations, and more total pressure. Level it as early as possible.

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Passive, energy gain, tempo acceleration

Best Patronage for Orion

Orion wants pets that either amplify his spell damage directly or make sure he lives long enough to spam the ultimates that define his best matches.

1st Place:

Merlin

Merlin is Orion's best patronage because the pet gives Magic Attack, Magic Penetration, and Tempus Magica. That combination improves Orion's main damage numbers and speeds up the magic skills that matter most in his kit.

2nd Place:

Axel

Axel is the safest defensive option. Orion is a fragile back-line mage, and Axel's damage cap prevents sudden burst kills while still giving useful Magic Attack and Armor support.

3rd Place:

Khorus

Khorus shines in magic-heavy lineups. Orion deals repeated magic damage, so Khorus can convert part of that into shielding and help intelligence teams survive long enough to win extended fights.

4th Place:

Mara

Mara is more specialized. She increases control duration, so she helps Orion's Antimatter Blast feel more annoying, but she does less for his raw damage output than Merlin or for his safety than Axel.

5th Place:

Biscuit

Biscuit is the situational anti-heal pick. He can be useful into sustain teams, but he does not improve Orion's core burst pattern as much as the higher-ranked patrons do.

How to Counter Orion Hero Wars Dominion Era

Cornelius

Cornelius defeats Orion because he targets high-Intelligence heroes directly. Orion's massive Intelligence stat makes him one of the easiest victims for Cornelius to delete before the mage engine gets rolling.

Isaac

Isaac is one of Orion's hardest counters because he punishes repeated magic damage. Orion feeds Isaac's anti-mage tools and can lose all his tempo if Isaac starts converting that spell pressure into an advantage.

Lian

Lian punishes Orion's area pressure because her control can interrupt aggressive casters that keep hitting into her reactions. Orion can accidentally trigger the punishment he does not want.

Rufus

Rufus blocks magic-focused win conditions, and that is exactly what Orion depends on. When Rufus absorbs or survives the magic burst, Orion can spend too much of the fight hitting into a bad target.

Satori

Satori punishes heroes that gain energy quickly, and Orion does that constantly because of Full Charge. The faster Orion plays, the easier he can become for Satori to punish with Fox Fire marks.

Ziri

Ziri can absorb Orion's first pressure window and drag the fight longer than he wants. Once she forces his burst into her durability, the enemy back line gets more time to survive and answer back.

Orion Best War Flag Hero Wars

Orion performs best with War Flags that accelerate his first ultimate from the back line, strengthen his pet-based support, or create even better conditions for his control and burst sequence.

War Flag of Readiness

Readiness is excellent for Orion because he fights in the back line and is often the rearmost hero in his teams. The extra Energy helps him reach Total Destruction earlier, which means earlier burst, earlier weapon artifact activation, and faster pressure on the enemy core.

Orion and Team Benefit: Faster first ultimate and better early tempo for magic burst teams.

War Flag of Frost

Frost is one of Orion's smartest secondary flags because the slow effect helps create marked targets for Tactical Destruction after Ascension V. Even before that, lowering enemy skill levels and slowing the fight down makes it easier for Orion to keep control of the tempo.

Orion and Team Benefit: Better setup for slow-based synergy and safer windows for repeated missile pressure.

War Flag of Pet Strength

Pet Strength is very good when Orion is paired with Merlin, Axel, or Khorus. It increases pet skill power by 10%, which improves the patronage value that often decides whether Orion survives burst or pushes enough extra magic pressure to win.

Orion and Team Benefit: Stronger patronage effects for burst, shielding, or survivability depending on the chosen pet.

Orion Hero Wars: Dominion Era Guide Conclusion

Orion remains one of the cleanest examples of a tempo mage in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. When his energy engine, magic scaling, and protection are all working together, he can decide fights before the enemy lineup fully stabilizes.

If you invest in his core damage upgrades first, support him with the right pet, and choose war flags that accelerate his first cast, Orion stays a very dangerous pick in the current meta.
