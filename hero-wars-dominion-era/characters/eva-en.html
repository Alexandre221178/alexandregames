Eva caught my attention because her sniper rifle does more than hit hard. Hunter's Mark turns an enemy damage dealer into a target your team can pressure while slowing their actions and cutting the Energy they gain from taking damage. That combination makes her an interesting choice when you want physical damage with meaningful support.

In my battles against teams built around Cascade, Augustus and Orion, I tested Eva with Astrid and Adam, then tried pairing her with each of them separately. Those combinations won demanding fights and gave me confidence in developing her. Here, I will explain what makes her kit work, which skills I would level first, and how I would spend her Skin Stones.

Who Is Eva in Hero Wars: Dominion Era?

Eva is an Agility Marksman with an additional Support role and the Hunter special. Her kit combines physical shots, damage reduction across the enemy team, and a passive that weakens enemies affected by Hunter's Mark. I see her as a damage dealer who helps her allies survive long enough to finish the fight.

  • Main Role: Marksman
  • Additional Role: Support
  • Special: Hunter
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Patronage: Fenris, Biscuit, Vex

Basic Attack: 96,348 physical damage (100% Physical Attack), with a listed cooldown of 3.1 seconds. Her three active skills also scale with Physical Attack, so improving that stat benefits several parts of her kit.

Eva Overall Rating in the Meta

These are my practical ratings for Dominion Era, based on her kit and the battles described above. They reflect my assessment, rather than a measured win rate or a guarantee against every team.

  • Control: ★★★★☆
  • Damage: ★★★★☆
  • Synergy: ★★★★★
  • Independence: ★★☆☆☆

Her slow and Energy suppression can disrupt an enemy's rhythm, while her ultimate supplies strong physical pressure. Synergy is her standout quality: Astrid and Adam impressed me in testing, and Jorgen is another synergy option noted for her. The low Independence score reflects her need for protection and a team that can capitalize on marked targets.

Eva Pros and Cons - Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Pressure on dangerous enemies: Threat Assessment selects an unmarked target by its recent damage, rather than simply firing at the nearest opponent.
  • Team protection: Suppressive Shot reduces the damage all enemy Heroes deal by 30% for 10 seconds at the listed maximum.
  • Disruptive marks: Suppression Protocol slows marked enemies by 30% and reduces their Energy gain from taking damage or dodging by 85%.
  • Reliable ultimate targeting: Ballistic Verdict cannot be dodged and ignores the first interruption during its charge.
  • Promising physical combinations: Eva worked well with Astrid and Adam in my tests against several strong magic teams.

❌ Cons

  • Needs time: Her ultimate charges for two seconds. Ignoring the first interruption is limited protection, not blanket immunity.
  • Mark dependence: Her passive and the ultimate's collateral damage require Hunter's Mark to be active.
  • Gaps in coverage: Threat Assessment has a 16-second cooldown for a 10-second mark; Suppressive Shot has an 18-second cooldown for a 10-second debuff.
  • No healing in her listed kit: She needs allies to help her survive sustained pressure.
  • Energy suppression has limits: It affects Energy gained from taking damage or dodging attacks, rather than every source of Energy.

Eva Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn how Eva's skills work, keep their in-game order, and see which upgrades best improve her damage, team protection, and Hunter's Mark control.

My upgrade order: Suppression Protocol > Suppressive Shot > Ballistic Verdict > Threat Assessment. This is a spending order once all four are unlocked; level the available skills as you progress. The cards below stay in their in-game order.

Reading the formulas: Physical Attack is Eva's offensive stat; level is the level used by the skill formula. For example, 204% means multiplying Physical Attack by 2.04. At 96,348 Physical Attack and level 130, Ballistic Verdict gives 268,049.92, displayed as 268,050. The listed damage values are before enemy mitigation.

1. Ballistic Verdict

In-game description: Eva charges the sniper rifle for 2s, ignoring the first interruption to this ability. The shot is then fired at the nearest enemy, dealing 268,050 damage. This attack cannot be dodged. If there are any Heroes on the battlefield affected by Hunter's Mark, each of them also takes 162,800 collateral damage.

Skill Explanation: Eva spends two seconds preparing a shot at the nearest enemy, dealing 268,050 physical damage. Each Hero affected by Hunter's Mark also takes 162,800 collateral physical damage. Think of the mark as a way to extend the shot's pressure beyond its nearest target. The attack cannot be dodged, but ignoring the first interruption does not mean the whole charge is immune to disruption.

Formula - Target physical damage: 268,050 (204% Physical Attack + 550 * level)

Formula - Collateral physical damage: 162,800 (115% Physical Attack + 400 * level)

Skill Animation Info

  • Charge Duration: 2 seconds
  • Target: Nearest enemy; collateral damage to Heroes with Hunter's Mark

Evolution Priority: High - 3rd – This is her main burst tool, and leveling it increases both the primary and collateral damage. I put it after her two scaling support effects because those help the whole team gain time. Give it extra attention if Eva is your main source of damage.

2. Threat Assessment

In-game description: Fires at the target without Hunter's Mark that has dealt the most damage over the last 10 seconds, dealing 105,969 damage. The target is affected by Hunter's Mark for 10s.

Skill Explanation: Eva looks at damage dealt during the previous 10 seconds, chooses the highest-damage target among enemies without Hunter's Mark, and hits it for 105,969 physical damage. She then marks it for 10 seconds. This connects her kit: the target becomes eligible for the ultimate's collateral damage and, after the fourth skill is unlocked, the slow and Energy suppression.

Formula - Physical damage: 105,969 (83% Physical Attack + 200 * level)

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 11 seconds
  • Cooldown: 16 seconds
  • Hunter's Mark Duration: 10 seconds
  • Damage Assessment Window: Previous 10 seconds

Evolution Priority: Medium - 4th – Unlock it as soon as it becomes available: the mark is essential. Its listed level scaling increases the shot damage, however, rather than extending the 10-second mark. When Gold is tight, that makes additional levels less urgent than improving her passive, team protection, or ultimate.

3. Suppressive Shot

In-game description: Fires through all enemy Heroes, dealing 80,153 damage and reducing the damage they deal by 30% for 10s.

Skill Explanation: The shot passes through all enemy Heroes, dealing 80,153 physical damage to each and reducing the damage they deal by 30% for 10 seconds. In simple terms, a damage amount of 100 becomes 70 when considering this reduction alone. That protection is why I value the skill so highly when the enemy threatens several allies at once.

Formula - Physical damage: 80,153 (67% Physical Attack + 120 * level)

Formula - Damage reduction: 30% (0.15 * level + 10.5)

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 8 seconds
  • Cooldown: 18 seconds
  • Debuff Duration: 10 seconds
  • Area: All enemy Heroes

Evolution Priority: Very High - 2nd – Leveling this skill improves both its physical damage and its damage reduction. Protecting the team helps Eva and her allies stay alive through the next exchange, so I prioritize it ahead of extra burst damage.

4. Suppression Protocol

In-game description: Enemies that receive Hunter's Mark are slowed by 30% and gain 85% less Energy from taking damage or dodging attacks for the duration of Hunter's Mark.

Skill Explanation: This passive deals no direct damage. When an enemy receives Hunter's Mark, it applies a 30% slow and 85% less Energy gained from taking damage or dodging attacks for the mark's duration. If one of those sources would normally grant 100 Energy, only 15 remains after this reduction alone. It does not remove stored Energy or prevent every way an enemy can gain it.

Formula - Slow: 30% (0.15 * level + 10.5)

Formula - Energy gain reduction: 85% (0.5 * level + 20)

Skill Animation Info

  • Type: Passive
  • Duration: For the duration of Hunter's Mark

Evolution Priority: Very High - 1st – Both the slow and Energy suppression improve with level. Once unlocked, this is my first priority because it makes every active mark more disruptive and helps your team pressure enemies without feeding as much Energy through damage.

Best Skin for Eva in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Compare Eva's skins in their in-game order, see their stat gains and Skin Stone costs, and choose between stronger shots and better survivability.

My Eva skin priority: Default Skin first for a balanced damage build, then Paragon Skin for survivability when available. If Eva falls before she can use her skills, prioritize Health for that matchup. All unlocked skins' bonuses stack regardless of the appearance you select.

1. Default Skin

Stats gain: Agility +1,365

  • Physical Attack from Agility: +4,095
  • Armor from Agility: +1,365

Each Agility point grants two Physical Attack and one Armor, plus one additional Physical Attack because Agility is Eva's main stat. That gives her three Physical Attack per point and improves her basic attack and all three damaging skills.

Total Agility Skin Stones for max level: 30,825.

Evolution Priority: High - 1st – This is my first choice for Eva's damage development. It supports every Physical Attack formula in her kit, adds Armor, and costs fewer Skin Stones to maximize than Paragon. It does not increase her slow, damage reduction, or Energy suppression percentages, which scale with skill level.

2. Paragon Skin

Stats gain: Health +106,645

More Health gives Eva a larger survival buffer while she waits for her shots and marks. It does not directly increase her damage formulas, but surviving another cycle can matter more than a stronger opening shot.

Total Agility Skin Stones for max level: 55,410.

Evolution Priority: Medium - 2nd – Build this after Default Skin when Eva already survives comfortably. Raise its priority if she dies before applying her debuffs or completing her ultimate; her team support depends on staying alive.

Eva Glyph Evolution Priority

Plan Eva's glyph upgrades with stat gains and practical priorities, balancing stronger physical attacks with the defenses she needs to stay alive.

My usual upgrade order: Physical Attack > Agility > Health > Magic Defense > Armor. The cards follow the in-game order. Move defensive glyphs ahead when a specific enemy damage type is ending the fight before Eva can contribute.

1. Physical Attack Glyph

Stats gain: Physical Attack +4,340

Evolution Priority: Very High - 1st – This directly improves her basic attack and the damage of Ballistic Verdict, Threat Assessment and Suppressive Shot. It is the clearest first investment when she already survives.

2. Armor Glyph

Stats gain: Armor +6,500

Evolution Priority: Medium - 5th – Useful against physical pressure, but it does not strengthen her skills. I put it after Magic Defense for the magic opponents discussed in this guide; reverse those two when physical attackers are the problem.

3. Health Glyph

Stats gain: Health +62,200

Evolution Priority: High - 3rd – A larger Health pool gives Eva more time to complete her charge and reach another debuff cycle. Upgrade this sooner if she dies early, especially when one defense stat would not cover all incoming damage.

4. Magic Defense Glyph

Stats gain: Magic Defense +6,500

Evolution Priority: Medium High - 4th – This helps Eva survive the magic damage in the Cascade, Augustus and Orion matchups from my tests. Its value depends on the enemy; it does not improve her physical damage or protect against pure damage.

5. Agility Glyph

Stats gain: Agility +1,135

  • Physical Attack from Agility: +3,405
  • Armor from Agility: +1,135

Each point gives Eva two Physical Attack, one Armor, and one extra Physical Attack because Agility is her main stat.

Evolution Priority: High - 2nd – Agility improves damage and Armor together. Physical Attack remains my first damage glyph because its direct gain is larger than the Physical Attack derived from this glyph, but Agility is a strong next step.

Eva Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Compare Eva's weapon, book and ring in their in-game order, with maximum stat gains and upgrade priorities for her physical damage and team support.

My artifact priority: Weapon > Book > Ring for a physical team built around Eva. Their upgrade materials differ, so develop them together when resources allow. The weapon provides a temporary team buff; the book and ring improve Eva herself.

1. Weapon - AMR-X0 "Annihilator"

Team buff: Armor Penetration +50,190

Using Ballistic Verdict can trigger the weapon artifact's Armor Penetration buff for the whole team for nine seconds. Activation reliability depends on the artifact's evolution. This is a temporary combat bonus, not another permanent addition to Eva's maximum-stat table.

Evolution Priority: Very High - 1st – My first artifact target for the Eva, Astrid and Adam core. Armor Penetration helps their physical hits get through enemy Armor during the buff window. If your team already covers the enemy Armor comfortably, additional penetration has less value and her personal damage upgrades become more attractive.

2. Book - Alchemist's Folio

Physical Attack +5,577
Armor Penetration +16,731

The book supplies persistent offensive stats. Physical Attack increases Eva's skill formulas, while Armor Penetration helps those physical hits outside the weapon's temporary buff.

Evolution Priority: High - 2nd – It combines two useful offensive stats and makes Eva less dependent on an active team artifact buff. I value this consistent pressure before the ring when enemy Armor is still limiting her damage.

3. Ring - Ring of Agility

Stats gain: Agility +6,249

  • Physical Attack from Agility: +18,747
  • Armor from Agility: +6,249

For Eva, each Agility point becomes three Physical Attack and one Armor. This is a substantial personal damage upgrade, with some protection against physical hits.

Evolution Priority: Medium High - 3rd – The ring improves all her damaging skills but supplies no Armor Penetration. Third place means it follows the team buff and her book in my default plan; prioritize its materials when penetration is already sufficient or Eva needs more personal damage.

Best Pet Patronage for Eva

Choose Eva's patron from Vex, Fenris and Biscuit, comparing offensive stats, added control and healing reduction to find the best fit for your team.

My ranking: Vex > Fenris > Biscuit for the physical teams below. Patronage stats unlock at blue rank and the bonus skill at violet rank. A patron can help its assigned Hero without being your active team pet. See the official pet guide for those unlock rules.

1st Place: Vex

Vex

Patronage bonus stats: Physical Attack and Armor Penetration; values depend on Patronage Power.

Deep Wounds: Eva's physical damage attacks apply up to five Deep Wounds stacks, which expire after eight seconds. At the supplied maximum, each stack adds 5,036 damage to subsequent incoming physical attacks from allies; five stacks represent a listed bonus of 25,180 before relevant battle interactions.

Why I rank Vex here: This is my first choice because Eva can help set up targets for the other physical attackers. Her all-enemy shot also gives her a way to reach several targets. Deep Wounds is a separate effect from Hunter's Mark; it does not extend the mark or increase her Energy suppression percentage.

The stack behavior is also described in the official Vex skill reference.

2nd Place: Fenris

Fenris

Patronage bonus stats: Physical Attack and Armor Penetration; values depend on Patronage Power.

Blind Rage: Basic attacks have a listed 40% chance to blind an enemy for two seconds at maximum patronage. The chance follows the supplied formula: 0.32% Patronage Power + 4.905.

Why I rank Fenris here: Fenris supports Eva's physical damage while adding control through her basic attacks. I choose him when that blind is useful or Vex is assigned elsewhere. In the recommended teams, Fenris is usually reserved for Astrid or Somna, which also makes Vex a practical choice for Eva.

3rd Place: Biscuit

Biscuit

Patronage bonus stats: Magic Attack and Armor; values depend on Patronage Power.

Stayin' Chill: The owner's attacks reduce enemy healing for five seconds. The listed maximum reduction is 30.005%, using 0.23% Patronage Power + 5.

Why I rank Biscuit here: Biscuit is my situational option against healing-heavy teams. Healing reduction can help damage stick, but his Magic Attack bonus does not scale Eva's physical shots. Use him to solve that matchup rather than as the default damage patron.

How to Counter Eva in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

These are matchup options inferred from the heroes' skill interactions, listed alphabetically. My Eva tests described earlier do not establish win rates against these counters. Look at the whole opposing team before choosing one.

Corvus

Corvus's Altar of Souls retaliates with pure damage when enemies damage his allies. Eva's Suppressive Shot hits every enemy Hero, and her ultimate can damage additional marked targets, giving the altar opportunities to punish her while it is active. That is a reason to test Corvus against her area pressure, especially if she lacks enough healing or protection. Official Corvus skills.

Lian

Lian's passive can charm the hero who damages her. Eva cannot avoid hitting Lian with an all-enemy Suppressive Shot, so that interaction can disrupt Eva's follow-up actions. Charm can end early when the affected hero takes damage, and Eva's allies may provide cleansing or protection, so this is a control option to test rather than a guaranteed shutdown. Official Lian skills.

Eva Best War Flags - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Pick a War Flag for Eva's whole team, starting with frontline sustain and adjusting for enemy burst damage or battles where your healing needs help.

War Flag of Vanguard

Every four seconds, it heals the front Hero for 1.6% of maximum Health, plus 0.3% per inserted red pattern.

Eva and Team Benefit: This is my default and the flag selected for all three lineups below. Sustaining whoever occupies the front helps preserve the formation while Eva repeats her damage and debuff cycle. The heal targets the front Hero; it is not a direct heal for Eva.

War Flag of Bastion

At the start, allies gain Armor based on 30% of the front Hero's Armor. This falls toward 5% over 20 seconds; each red pattern extends the effect by three seconds.

Eva and Team Benefit: A matchup alternative when early physical damage is breaking the team before it stabilizes. Its shared Armor can protect Eva as well as the front, but it is not my answer to heavy magic or pure damage.

War Flag of Recovery

Increases all healing by 10%.

Eva and Team Benefit: Consider it when Guus's healing needs to sustain several allies rather than when the front Hero alone is under pressure. It reinforces existing healing; it does not give Eva a healing skill or increase shield values.

Patterns: I would start by comparing Physical Attack and Armor Penetration, then use Magic Defense against the magic matchups discussed here or Armor against physical pressure. These are team-building choices; compare replays to see whether damage or survival is holding you back. Flag mechanics are documented in the official War Flags guide.

Final Thoughts: Is Eva Worth Building?

Eva earned my attention through the way her damage and support worked together with Astrid and Adam. The most convincing reason to build her is that combination: her shots pressure enemies while her debuffs give the rest of the team more room to act.

I would begin with her skill levels, Default Skin and offensive glyphs, while adding enough Health and defenses to keep her alive. Develop her weapon artifact for the physical team, use Vex as the starting patron, and test the lineup that fits your existing roster. If a replay shows Eva dying before her second useful action, fix that survival problem before spending everything on larger damage numbers.

My preference is to build around the Eva, Astrid and Adam core first, then compare the Somna variations when control matters more. Let the actual opponent and the battle replay decide which version deserves your next investment.
