Who Is Fluffy in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Fluffy is an unlikely enigma: outwardly adorable, inwardly bound to an ancient Archdemon. This guide explores the lore, skills and practical ways to use Fluffy in your teams.

  • Main Role: Mage
  • Additional Role: Support
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Position: Fights at the Not Defined Yet

Pros and Cons — Hero Wars: Dominion Era

✅ Pros

  • Excellent vs. auto-attack teams: The ultimate grants immunity to basic attacks and reflection, neutralizing physical attackers.
  • Team resurrection: To Hell and Back revives allies with bonuses, turning defeats into opportunities.
  • Ultimate interruption: Usurpation can cancel enemy ultimates—great against ultimate-reliant compositions.
  • Good late-game scaling: Mark of Death and artifacts increase damage and penetration in the late game.
  • Synergy with flags/artifacts: Benefits greatly from Recovery, Readiness and defensive artifacts to mitigate self-damage.

❌ Cons

  • Risk of self-damage: Some counter mechanics (e.g., Usurpation when triggered) inflict self-damage; ensure sufficient HP and sustain.
  • Vulnerable to magic bursts: Heavy magic damage can outpace heals and bypass the ultimate's protection.
  • Ability/CD dependency: Usurpation has a cooldown and a chance to cancel—not always reliable.
  • Positioning is critical: Needs proper placement to take advantage of Readiness and resurrection windows.
  • Counters: Silences, dispels and skill-level reduction greatly weaken Fluffy.

Fluffy Skills Guide – Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Fluffy is not your average support. His kit blends demonic protection, resurrection, and devastating counterplay. Master each ability to turn Baalthazar's wrath into your greatest asset.

Deal with the Devil (Ultimate)

Deal with the Devil envelops all allied Heroes for 12 seconds. While active, allies are immune to damage from enemy basic attacks and reflect it back to attackers.

This ultimate is a double-edged sword: it grants incredible protection against auto-attack heavy teams. Time it wisely to maximize reflections and avoid exposing allies to heavy burst damage.

To Hell and Back

Baalthazar protects his flock from death for 10 seconds. All allies who die during this time will be resurrected when the effect ends. They restore some Health, and their physical and magic attack are increased until the end of the battle.

This is essentially a team-wide "second chance" mechanic. Timing is crucial: activate it before a massive enemy burst or ultimate combo. Resurrected allies come back stronger, turning potential defeats into aggressive counter-attacks.

Mark of Death

Fluffy puts Marks of Death on himself, increasing his magic armor penetration. When Fluffy dies, the Marks explode, dealing damage to enemy Heroes for each Mark. Up to 10 Marks can be active at once.

This passive turns Fluffy's death into a weapon. The more Marks accumulated, the bigger the explosion. It synergizes with risky plays: sometimes letting Fluffy fall at the right moment can wipe out weakened enemies. The magic penetration bonus also makes Fluffy's other damage more effective.

Usurpation

When an enemy uses an ultimate ability, Fluffy cancels it and deals damage to them. At the same time, Fluffy takes damage based on his maximum Health. This ability can trigger once every 20 seconds. The chance to cancel an ultimate ability depends on the target's level.

This is Fluffy's most disruptive skill. Canceling enemy ultimates can completely swing a fight. However, the self-damage and 20-second cooldown mean you can't rely on it constantly. Best against teams with devastating single-target or AoE ultimates.

Formula: (Damage to enemy: 50% magic attack + 150 * level + 100) = 77,076; Self-damage: 20% of max Health (scaling formula: 20% (-0.15 * level + 39.5)).

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrading improves the cancel chance and damage dealt. Essential against ultimate-reliant enemies. Upgrade alongside or after To Hell and Back.

Fluffy – Skill Upgrade Priority

See the recommended order to upgrade Fluffy's skills to make the most of his kit:

  1. Deal with the Devil (Ultimate) – Basic-attack immunity and reflect; core of the kit.
  2. To Hell and Back – Area resurrection with an attack buff; decides long fights.
  3. Usurpation – Cancels enemy ultimates; crucial against burst compositions.
  4. Mark of Death – Magic penetration and explosion on death; powerful but situational.

Fluffy Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Fluffy’s artifacts reinforce her risky demon-support kit in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, adding teamwide magic defense, raw health and scaling damage.

Baalthazar's Skull (Weapon Artifact)

Stats gain: Magic Defense +50,190

When Fluffy uses her ultimate (Shieldbreaker skill trigger), Baalthazar's Skull activates and grants bonus stats to the whole team for 9 seconds. This teamwide magic defense buff stacks perfectly with Deal with the Devil, covering a weakness in her kit: she already shuts down basic attacks, and the artifact helps your team survive enemy magic bursts and self-damage mechanics.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This artifact directly protects the entire team at the exact moment Fluffy is most vulnerable. Because it is teamwide, always-on when the ultimate fires and shores up a key defensive layer, it offers the highest impact for both PvP and PvE.

Tome of Arcane Knowledge (Book Artifact)

Stats gain: Health +83,649; Magic Attack +16,731

The book artifact is Fluffy’s personal power and safety layer. Extra Health helps her survive self-damage from triggers (for example, Usurpation) and enemy burst damage, while bonus Magic Attack amplifies all her skills, including Deal with the Devil damage, resurrection buffs and Mark of Death explosions. It does not directly help the whole team like the weapon, but it makes Fluffy much harder to burst down.

Evolution Priority: High – Strong mix of offense and survivability on Fluffy herself. Upgrading this artifact greatly improves her consistency in long fights, but it is slightly less valuable than Baalthazar's Skull because its effect is not teamwide.

Ring of Intelligence (Stat Artifact)

Stats gain: Intelligence +6,249

  • Magic Attack from Intelligence: +18,747
  • Magic Defense from Intelligence: +6,249
  • Physical Attack from Intelligence: +6,249

The Intelligence ring scales all of Fluffy’s numbers: more Magic Attack for damage and stronger buffs, extra Magic Defense for her own safety, and a bit of Physical Attack thanks to Intelligence being her main stat. However, this artifact only benefits Fluffy and offers no extra team utility or special effect.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Excellent long-term scaling but less impactful per level than the weapon and book. Level it after Baalthazar's Skull and the Tome, once you are comfortable with Fluffy’s survivability and team protection.

Fluffy Best War Flag Hero Wars

Top War Flags to amplify Fluffy's survivability and team resurrection, prioritizing increased healing, early energy gain, and enemy skill suppression.

War Flag of Recovery:

Increases all healing by 10% — ideal when Fluffy runs Deal with the Devil and team resurrections.

Fluffy and Team Benefit: Recovery directly boosts the healing portion of To Hell and Back and helps allies survive self-damage mechanics and heavy burst, making resurrection safer and more reliable for the team.

War Flag of Readiness:

Gives energy to the rearmost Hero at battle start and periodically — scales with red patterns.

Fluffy and Team Benefit: If Fluffy is placed in the rear, Readiness lets her cast ultimate and resurrection much earlier, securing defensive windows and catching enemies off-guard; excellent for tempo control.

War Flag of Decline:

Decreases enemy team healing by 10%.

Fluffy and Team Benefit: Decline is a strong counter when opponents rely on heavy healing; it prevents enemies from easily negating Fluffy's damage and reduces the effectiveness of heals that would undo your resurrections.

War Flag of Frost:

Every 18 seconds reduces enemy skill levels by 2 for 8 seconds.

Fluffy and Team Benefit: Frost helps neutralize skill-reliant enemy teams (lowering ultimates and skill potency), making Fluffy's cancels and resurrections more impactful by reducing incoming burst and control.

Conclusion

Fluffy is a specialist support/mage that shines against auto-attack teams thanks to Deal with the Devil and can turn fights around with To Hell and Back. Prioritize survivability (Health/Armor glyphs and defensive skins), level the ultimate and resurrection first, and use artifacts like Baalthazar's Skull to protect the team. Beware heavy magic burst and crowd-control—proper timing and positioning are essential. In short: great in long fights and physical metas when built and played carefully.
