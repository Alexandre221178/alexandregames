Who Is Electra?

Electra is a mysterious warrior born from chaos itself. Once a lifeless creation, she now channels the energy of countless souls into raw power, making her one of the most unpredictable heroes in Dominion. Her blend of resilience, agility, and burst potential makes her both a formidable tank and a tactical choice for creative players.

  • Main Role: Tank
  • Additional Role: Control / Support
  • Position: Fights at the Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength

In battle, Electra thrives when surrounded by allies who can amplify her chaos-driven energy. She shines brightest in teams that balance aggression and defense, turning the tide with her explosive reactions and unpredictable combos.

Let’s dive deeper into her skills, strategies, and best team synergies to unleash her full potential.

Electra Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Electra is a powerful protector who turns her own health and defense into strength, shielding allies and striking enemies with life-draining magic damage.

Embrace of Pain

Electra creates a magic shield that absorbs (18% Health + 600) damage for 10 seconds. While the shield is active, she cannot move or act but becomes immune to control effects. When the shield expires, it explodes and deals area damage based on how much it absorbed: (0.050% Magic Attack + 10.1) of the total absorbed damage.

Formula: (18% Health + 600) / Damage: (0.050% Magic Attack + 10.1).

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Electra’s most important skill because it provides both defense and offense. The shield prevents her from dying quickly and deals strong damage when it explodes, making it essential for both survivability and team protection.

Spark of Life

Deals (50% Magic Attack + 300) damage to nearby enemies and restores her own Health equal to (0.010% Health + 52) of the damage dealt. This gives Electra a constant source of healing in combat, helping her stay alive while damaging opponents.

Formula: Damage (50% Magic Attack + 300) / Vampirism: (0.010% Health + 52).

Evolution Priority: High – It provides damage and self-healing, allowing Electra to sustain herself in battle. Although not as impactful as her shield, it’s still crucial for her long-term survival and consistent output.

Oath of Protection

For 5 seconds, Electra redirects a portion of her allies’ incoming damage to herself for any ally whose current Health is lower than hers. During “Embrace of Pain,” this effect is automatically applied. The amount redirected depends on her skill level.

Note: While Electra is using her ultimate Embrace of Pain, she automatically triggers Oath of Protection again. For 5 seconds, all allies whose current Health is lower than Electra's gain a protective effect that redirects the damage they receive back to her. This means that during Embrace of Pain, Electra is effectively protecting the entire team a second time, doubling the value of her defensive utility.

Formula: Redirects % of damage from allies to Electra.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Useful in team fights to protect allies, but it can cause Electra to take too much damage if not timed correctly. Important for tank-style builds but secondary to her shield and main damage skill.

Immortal Shell

At the start of battle, Electra converts all her Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Strength. Then, for every 100 points lost from these stats, her Strength increases by (0.0050% Health + 11). Once converted, her defensive stats can’t change during the fight.

Formula: Strength = (0.0050% Health + 11) per 100 lost defense points.

Evolution Priority: Medium – This passive gives a solid Strength boost that improves her survivability and shield strength, but its impact is less direct than her active skills. Upgrade after her main abilities are strong.

Choose the skin that best converts into usable Strength or raw survivability for Electra because her Immortal Shell changes defenses into Strength at battle start.

  • - Physical Attack from Strength: +1,365

Because Strength directly raises Health, it enlarges the Embrace of Pain shield (18% Health + 600) and improves lifesteal scaling from Spark of Life. This skin is a reliable all-round choice for survivability and damage scaling.

Demonic Skin (Magic Defense)

After conversion, the extra Strength increases Electra’s Health, enlarges her Embrace of Pain shield, improves her Spark of Life healing, and helps her absorb redirected damage from Oath of Protection. In practice, this is one of her best skins for raw survivability scaling.

Total of Skin Stones for max level: 55,410

Electra Glyph Evolution Priority

Electra’s glyph priorities focus on maximizing survivability and shield power. Since several of her abilities scale directly with Health and Strength, while Immortal Shell converts Armor into Strength, defensive glyphs gain significantly more long-term value.

1st Glyph – Magic Attack

Stats gain: Magic Attack +6,500

Evolution Priority: Medium – Boosts damage for Embrace of Pain and Spark of Life, but Electra functions primarily as a survivability-based tank, not a burst mage. Useful, but not a core priority early on.

2nd Glyph – Armor

Stats gain: Armor +6,500

Evolution Priority: High – Immortal Shell converts all Armor into Strength at the start of battle, giving Electra more Health and damage. Even though Armor becomes zero in-battle, its Strength conversion greatly improves shielding (Embrace of Pain) and survivability.

3rd Glyph – Health

Stats gain: Health +62,200

Evolution Priority: Very High – Electra’s strongest glyph. Health increases Embrace of Pain’s shield (18% Health), Spark of Life healing, her ability to tank redirected damage from Oath of Protection, and also synergizes with Immortal Shell’s Strength scaling.

4th Glyph – Magic Penetration

Stats gain: Magic Penetration +6,500

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Improves damage on both offensive abilities, helping Electra deal meaningful magic damage. Useful, but still secondary to survivability glyphs.

5th Glyph – Strength

Stats gain: Strength +1,135

- Health from Strength: +45,400

- Physical Attack from Strength: +1,135

Evolution Priority: Very High – One of Electra’s most impactful glyphs. Strength increases her max Health substantially, boosting Embrace of Pain and Spark of Life, while helping her tank redirected damage. Works perfectly with Immortal Shell’s defense-to-Strength conversion.

Electra Artifact Evolution Priority

Learn the real Hero Wars: Dominion Era electra artifact priority order to maximize her magic burst, defense scaling, and overall survivability in battles.

Cage of Pain Artifact:

Stats Gain: Magic Attack: +50,190

Evolution Priority: Very High – This artifact is triggered with her ultimate skill, Shieldbreaker, boosting the entire team’s stats for 9 seconds. Since Electra’s damage output and shield explosion both scale with Magic Attack, evolving this artifact gives her the largest improvement to offensive and team-support potential.

Defender’s Covenant Artifact:

Stats Gain: Armor: +12,546
Magic Defense: +12,546

Evolution Priority: High – This artifact provides Armor and Magic Defense, both of which are converted into Health through Electra’s passive skills. Because her survivability depends on the total amount of health gained from these defenses, evolving this artifact significantly improves her durability in prolonged fights.

Ring of Strength Artifact:

Stats Gain: Strength: +6,249

  • Health from Strength: +249,960
  • Physical Attack from Strength: +6,249

Evolution Priority: Medium – Strength increases her Health and slightly her Physical Attack, but since Electra primarily deals Magic Damage and scales better with Magic Attack and Defense-to-Health conversion, this artifact provides the least direct combat improvement compared to the other two.

Best Patronage for Electra

Electra benefits most from pets that enhance her survivability, boost Health, or protect her from burst damage, supporting her role as a redirect tank and shield-based frontline.

1st Place:

Axel

Axel is Electra’s best pet. His patronage ability prevents her from taking fatal damage by limiting the maximum damage she can receive from a single hit. This works perfectly with Oath of Protection and Embrace of Pain, allowing Electra to safely absorb redirected damage from allies while building larger shields. His Magic Attack + Armor stats also convert into Strength through Immortal Shell, improving Electra’s Health and shield scaling.

2nd Place:

Oliver

Oliver is a strong defensive option for Electra. His Health and Armor bonuses are converted into Strength at the start of battle via Immortal Shell, giving Electra more maximum Health and shield power. His automatic healing when Electra drops below 50% HP greatly increases her sustain during redirection-heavy fights.

3rd Place:

Merlin

Merlin enhances Electra’s Magic Attack and Magic Penetration, improving the damage of Spark of Life and the explosion from Embrace of Pain. While this increases her offensive output, Electra’s main role is tanking and protecting teammates, so Merlin is helpful but not optimal for her primary function.

4th Place:

Biscuit

Biscuit provides Magic Attack and Armor, which do convert into Strength through Immortal Shell, but his anti-heal effect does not synergize well with Electra’s defensive role. Biscuit is more situational and not ideal as her main patronage.

How to Counter Electra – Hero Wars Dominion Era

Electra is a high-risk, high-survivability damage redirector whose kit revolves around absorbing allies’ damage (Oath of Protection), sustaining herself through lifesteal (Spark of Life), and creating a massive control/mitigation window with Embrace of Pain and Immortal Shell. These strengths can be shut down by heroes who suppress healing, disrupt energy gain, or prevent her from cycling her defensive abilities. Below are the best counters and why they work.

Biscuit

Biscuit shuts down Electra by heavily reducing her self-healing and lifesteal during Embrace of Pain. Since Electra redirects damage from allies and relies on Spark of Life to recover, Biscuit’s Icy Storm and healing-reduction patronage drastically weaken her sustain, making both Electra and her protected allies easier to eliminate.

Celeste

Celeste counteracts Electra by blocking all external healing when in Dark Form. Since Electra takes redirected damage from allies through Oath of Protection and relies on constant healing to survive, Celeste’s Cursed Flame prevents recovery at critical moments. Her Light Form also provides strong cleansing and burst healing to allies Electra tries to pressure.

Isaac

Isaac disrupts Electra’s entire kit by silencing her with Tes’Lin’s Overdrive. This prevents her from activating Embrace of Pain, which is essential for her damage reduction and lifesteal loop. His MP-300 Capacitor also charges rapidly from magic damage, allowing frequent silences and reducing Electra’s ability to cycle skills and protect allies.

Electra Best War Flag Hero Wars

Electra benefits most from War Flags that enhance her ability to cycle her ultimate faster and protect allies through Oath of Protection. Below are the War Flags that provide the strongest synergy with her kit.

War Flag of Fervor:

Electra and Team Benefit: This is Electra’s best War Flag. As a tank whose defensive power depends on using her ultimate, increasing Tank Energy Gain by 10% dramatically improves her performance. Faster ultimates mean more frequent activation of Embrace of Pain and therefore more uptime on Oath of Protection, redirecting damage from allies and keeping the entire team alive longer. This War Flag enhances Electra’s role as a high-frequency, team-protection tank.

War Flag of Pet Strength:

Electra and Team Benefit: This is a strong secondary option when using Axel as Electra’s patron. Increasing Pet Skill Power by 10% boosts Axel’s protective bubble, helping Electra absorb large hits more safely. It is especially effective against burst-heavy teams, but still not as universally strong as the increased ultimate frequency provided by Fervor.

War Flag of Decline:

Electra and Team Benefit: A situational but useful choice against healing-heavy opponents. Since Electra’s team relies heavily on redirecting and sustaining damage, reducing enemy healing can help secure kills faster. Still, it is less impactful than Fervor and Pet Strength for Electra specifically.

War Flag of Recovery:

Increases all healing by 10%.

Electra and Team Benefit: This flag synergizes perfectly with Electra’s Spark of Life lifesteal-based regeneration. Since she absorbs damage from allies, increasing her healing directly boosts her ability to stay alive while tanking redirected damage. It also benefits healer-support teammates who stabilize Electra during long engagements.

War Flag of Fervor:

Increases Energy Gain for Tanks by 10%.

Electra and Team Benefit: Because Electra functions as a frontline tank-damage hybrid, faster energy gain helps her cast Embrace of Pain sooner and more often. This massively increases her survivability window and boosts the damage-reflection pressure applied to enemies. The flag is especially strong in teams that rely on her ultimate cycle to maintain control over the fight.

Conclusion – Electra Guide Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Electra is not just another tank she’s a force of chaos forged into perfection. Her ability to convert pain into power makes her one of the most unique heroes in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. With Embrace of Pain, she can withstand deadly blows and unleash devastating pure damage, while her Oath of Protection keeps allies safe by sharing their suffering.

Building her correctly means focusing on her survivability and scaling Strength. Prioritize her Weapon Artifact for maximum team support, enhance her Book Artifact for stronger defenses, and complete her setup with Strength-based boosts for extra durability and offense.

Electra shines in teams that balance aggression and sustain pairing her with strong healers or support heroes can turn any battle in your favor. Whether you admire her for her resilience, her beauty, or her deadly efficiency, one thing is certain: once Electra rises from the shadows, she never fades again.
