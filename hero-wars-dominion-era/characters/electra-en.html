Who Is Electra?

Electra is a mysterious warrior born from chaos itself. Once a lifeless creation, she now channels the energy of countless souls into raw power, making her one of the most unpredictable heroes in Dominion. Her blend of resilience, agility, and burst potential makes her both a formidable tank and a tactical choice for creative players.

  • Main Role: Tank
  • Additional Role: Control / Support
  • Position: Fights at the Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength

In battle, Electra thrives when surrounded by allies who can amplify her chaos-driven energy. She shines brightest in teams that balance aggression and defense, turning the tide with her explosive reactions and unpredictable combos.

Electra Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Electra is a powerful protector who turns her own health and defense into strength, shielding allies and striking enemies with life-draining magic damage.

1- Embrace of Pain (Ultimate)

Electra creates a shield that absorbs 246821 (18% Health + Level x 500 + 100) damage for 10 seconds. While the shield is active, she cannot move or act but becomes immune to control effects. When the shield expires, it explodes and deals area damage based on how much it absorbed: 51,4% (0.050% Magic Attack + 10.1) of the total absorbed damage.

Formula shield to herself that absorbs: (18% Health + Level x 500 + 100)

Formula Damage: 51,4% (0.050% Magic Attack + level x 0.1 + 10).

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Electra’s most important skill because it provides both defense and offense. The shield prevents her from dying quickly and deals strong damage when it explodes, making it essential for both survivability and team protection.

Spark of Life

Deals 49.509(50% Magic Attack + level x 200 +100) damage to nearby enemies and restores her own Health equal to 410 % (0.010% Health + level x 2 + 50) of the damage dealt. This gives Electra a constant source of healing in combat, helping her stay alive while damaging opponents.

Formula - Damage: 44900 (50% Magic Attack + level x 200 +100)

Formula - Vampirism: 410%(0.010% Health + level x 2 + 50).

Evolution Priority: Medium – It provides damage and self-healing, allowing Electra to sustain herself in battle. Although not as impactful as her shield, it’s still crucial for her long-term survival and consistent output.

Oath of Protection

For 5 seconds, Electra redirects a portion of her allies’ incoming damage to herself for any ally whose current Health is lower than hers. During “Embrace of Pain,” this effect is automatically applied. The amount redirected depends on her skill level.

Note: While Electra is using her ultimate Embrace of Pain, she automatically triggers Oath of Protection again. For 5 seconds, all allies whose current Health is lower than Electra's gain a protective effect that redirects 40% (Level x 0.15 + 23.5)% the damage they receive back to her. This means that during Embrace of Pain, Electra is effectively protecting the entire team a second time, doubling the value of her defensive utility.

Formula: Redirects 40% (Level x 0.15 + 23.5)% of damage from allies to Electra.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Useful in team fights to protect allies, but it can cause Electra to take too much damage if not timed correctly. Important for tank-style builds but secondary to her shield and main damage skill.

Immortal Shell

At the start of battle, Electra converts all her Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Strength. Then, for every 100 points lost from these stats, her Strength increases by 75 (0.0050% Health + level x 0.15 + 11). Once converted, her defensive stats can’t change during the fight.

Formula: Strength = 75 (0.0050% Health + level x 0.15 + 11) per 100 lost defense points.

Evolution Priority: High – This passive gives a solid Strength boost that improves her survivability and shield strength, but its impact is less direct than her Ultimate skill. Upgrade after her main skill.

Electra – Immortal Shell Conversion at Battle Start

At max level, Electra begins the battle with the following base stats before Immortal Shell activates:

  • Intelligence: 2200
  • Agility: 1677
  • Strength: 11031
  • Health: 611,542
  • Physical attack: 15,464
  • Magic attack: 37,599
  • Armor: 19,557
  • Magic defense: 23,510
  • Magic penetration: 11,728

When Immortal Shell triggers, Electra converts all Armor + Magic Defense + Dodge into Strength. At this level, the total converted amount is:

19,557 (Armor) + 23,510 (Magic Defense) = 43,067 total points converted

For every 100 points lost, Electra gains 55.08 Strength.

Final Strength gained:
(43,067 ÷ 100) × 55.08 = 23,721 Strength

Since Electra is a Strength-based hero, each point of Strength increases her Health by +40.

Total Health gained:
23,721 × 40 = +948,840 Health

Final Health after transformation:
611,542 (base) + 948,840 = 1,560,382 Health

This massive conversion explains why Electra becomes much tankier the moment the battle starts, even before using Embrace of Pain or Oath of Protection.

Best Patronage for Electra

Electra benefits most from pets that enhance her survivability, boost Health, or protect her from burst damage, supporting her role as a redirect tank and shield-based frontline.

1st Place:

Axel

Axel is Electra’s best pet. His patronage ability prevents her from taking fatal damage by limiting the maximum damage she can receive from a single hit. This works perfectly with Oath of Protection and Embrace of Pain, allowing Electra to safely absorb redirected damage from allies while building larger shields. His Magic Attack + Armor stats also convert into Strength through Immortal Shell, improving Electra’s Health and shield scaling.

2nd Place:

Oliver

Oliver is a strong defensive option for Electra. His Health and Armor bonuses are converted into Strength at the start of battle via Immortal Shell, giving Electra more maximum Health and shield power. His automatic healing when Electra drops below 50% HP greatly increases her sustain during redirection-heavy fights.

3rd Place:

Merlin

Merlin enhances Electra’s Magic Attack and Magic Penetration, improving the damage of Spark of Life and the explosion from Embrace of Pain. While this increases her offensive output, Electra’s main role is tanking and protecting teammates, so Merlin is helpful but not optimal for her primary function.

4th Place:

Biscuit

Biscuit provides Magic Attack and Armor, which do convert into Strength through Immortal Shell, but his anti-heal effect does not synergize well with Electra’s defensive role. Biscuit is more situational and not ideal as her main patronage.

How to Counter Electra – Hero Wars Dominion Era

Electra is a high-risk, high-survivability damage redirector whose kit revolves around absorbing allies’ damage (Oath of Protection), sustaining herself through lifesteal (Spark of Life), and creating a massive control/mitigation window with Embrace of Pain and Immortal Shell. These strengths can be shut down by heroes who suppress healing, disrupt energy gain, or prevent her from cycling her defensive abilities. Below are the best counters and why they work.

Biscuit

Biscuit shuts down Electra by heavily reducing her self-healing and lifesteal during Embrace of Pain. Since Electra redirects damage from allies and relies on Spark of Life to recover, Biscuit’s Icy Storm and healing-reduction patronage drastically weaken her sustain, making both Electra and her protected allies easier to eliminate.

Celeste

Celeste counteracts Electra by blocking all external healing when in Dark Form. Since Electra takes redirected damage from allies through Oath of Protection and relies on constant healing to survive, Celeste’s Cursed Flame prevents recovery at critical moments. Her Light Form also provides strong cleansing and burst healing to allies Electra tries to pressure.

Isaac

Isaac disrupts Electra’s entire kit by silencing her with Tes’Lin’s Overdrive. This prevents her from activating Embrace of Pain, which is essential for her damage reduction and lifesteal loop. His MP-300 Capacitor also charges rapidly from magic damage, allowing frequent silences and reducing Electra’s ability to cycle skills and protect allies.

Electra Best War Flag Hero Wars

Electra benefits most from War Flags that increase her survivability, ultimate frequency, and overall damage mitigation for the team. Because she relies heavily on Health scaling and frequent ultimates, the War Flag choices below offer the strongest synergy with her kit.

War Flag of Vanguard:

Electra and Team Benefit: This is Electra’s best War Flag. Vanguard heals the front Hero every 4 seconds for a percentage of their Max Health — exactly the stat Electra scales from. Since her shield, vampirism, and damage redirection all depend heavily on high Health, constant %HP regeneration dramatically increases her durability during Embrace of Pain and while absorbing redirected damage from Oath of Protection. Vanguard provides unmatched sustain and is now the top choice for keeping Electra alive in long fights.

War Flag of Fervor:

Electra and Team Benefit: Now the second-best option. Increasing Tank Energy Gain by 10% allows Electra to cast her ultimate more frequently, boosting uptime on both Embrace of Pain and Oath of Protection. Fervor remains excellent, especially in high-burst matchups, but it cannot match Vanguard’s constant survivability boost.

War Flag of Pet Strength:

Electra and Team Benefit: A solid third-place option when Electra uses Axel as her patron. Increasing pet Skill Power by 10% boosts Axel’s damage cap bubble, improving Electra’s ability to absorb lethal hits. Good for anti-burst strategies, but overall less impactful than Vanguard and Fervor.

War Flag of Decline:

Electra and Team Benefit: A niche but useful option when facing heavy-healing enemy teams. Reducing their overall healing by 10% helps secure kills faster, especially when Electra's team lacks anti-heal support. Should only be used in specific matchups.

Conclusion – Electra Guide Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Electra is not just another tank she’s a force of chaos forged into perfection. Her ability to convert pain into power makes her one of the most unique heroes in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. With Embrace of Pain, she can withstand deadly blows and unleash devastating pure damage, while her Oath of Protection keeps allies safe by sharing their suffering.

Building her correctly means focusing on her survivability and scaling Strength. Prioritize her Weapon Artifact for maximum team support, enhance her Book Artifact for stronger defenses, and complete her setup with Strength-based boosts for extra durability and offense.

Electra shines in teams that balance aggression and sustain pairing her with strong healers or support heroes can turn any battle in your favor. Whether you admire her for her resilience, her beauty, or her deadly efficiency, one thing is certain: once Electra rises from the shadows, she never fades again.
