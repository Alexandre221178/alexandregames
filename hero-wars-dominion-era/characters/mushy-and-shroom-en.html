Mushy & Shroom are one of the most fascinating tanks in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, born from the ancient magic of the forest. As sentient fungi who evolved through pure woodland energy, they fight not as one but as many. Their gameplay revolves around creating additional copies that join the battle once they receive enough healing, opening up a unique strategy that rewards sustain-heavy teams.

If you love heroes that feel alive with personality, lore, and clever mechanics, Mushy & Shroom will instantly stand out. Their synergy with healers like Martha, Aidan, and Maya allows them to multiply their frontline presence, while their silence ability helps shut down dangerous mages and magic-damage heroes. They are both charming and powerful making them a favorite among players who enjoy creative and tactical team builds.

Who Is Mushy & Shroom in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Mushy & Shroom are frontline tanks empowered by ancient forest magic. They summon additional copies, scale extremely well with heal-over-time effects, and silence enemies to counter magic attackers.

  • Main Role: Tank / Summoner
  • Additional: Control (Silence), Sustain
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Position: Fights at the Front Line

Mushy & Shroom Pros and Cons – Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Excellent sustain thanks to constant healing and regeneration from the Shrooms.
  • High battlefield control by spawning multiple Mushrooms that distract enemies and absorb damage.
  • Very strong in long battles, especially against teams that cannot quickly burst them down.
  • Scales extremely well with Healing buffs, defensive emblems, and support Heroes like Martha or Alvanor.
  • Can overwhelm the enemy frontline by creating additional units that block skills and basic attacks.
  • Great synergy with defensive Earth teams and any lineup focused on survivability.

❌ Cons

  • Highly vulnerable to burst damage teams capable of deleting Mushy before healing ramps up.
  • Weak against heroes who deal high AoE damage that can wipe out spawned Shrooms instantly.
  • Low offensive pressure; relies heavily on allies to deal real damage.
  • Struggles against anti-summon mechanics and heroes who gain advantages from killing summons.
  • Can be countered by Rufus, Iris, or other heroes who exploit low defenses and multi-hit setups.
  • Performance drops significantly without the right pet patronage (Axel or Oliver).

Mushy & Shroom Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Mushy & Shroom rely heavily on healing synergy and duplicate spawning, making their skills scale differently than traditional tanks. This guide explains each skill simply and shows which ones matter the most.

Perfect Copy

Perfect Copy creates an inactive Shroom with missing health. Once fully healed, it activates and becomes a full clone able to attack and use all learned skills. Up to 3 active Shrooms can exist, greatly increasing team pressure and battlefield control.

Formula (Health Scaling): 545,230 (50% Health + Lvl * 100)

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Mushy & Shroom’s strongest ability because all their gameplay revolves around healing the clones to activate them. More levels = healthier clones = faster activation = more battlefield presence.

Autonomous Copy

Ascension Skill: Unlocked at Ascension V.

This new ascension effect keeps Shroom's copies active even after Mushy & Shroom die, which greatly improves their value in longer fights and prevents all generated pressure from disappearing when the main hero falls.

Effect: Shroom's copies stay active after Mushy and Shroom's death

Wild Growth

Wild Growth is a passive skill that heals inactive Shrooms and Spore Mushrooms automatically. This healing gets stronger with each active clone, making the entire kit ramp up extremely quickly. More healing = faster clone activation = more silence and explosions later.

Formula (Healing per second): 2.75% + 7,610 (8% Magic Attack + Lvl * 20)

Evolution Priority: High – This passive accelerates everything Mushy & Shroom do. More healing means faster clone activations, more explosions, and stronger damage scaling later.

Mycelium Connection

Ascension Skill: Unlocked at Ascension II.

When a Spore Mushroom or a Shroom copy fully restores its health for the first time, Mushy & Shroom are healed. This adds an extra sustain loop on top of the hero's normal healing engine.

Formula (Healing): From Shroom: 130,250 (200% Magic Attack + 5,000)
From Spore Mushroom: 13,025 (20% Magic Attack + 500)

Branching Mycelium

Branching Mycelium spawns 3 Spore Mushrooms that become active after being fully healed. Once activated, they run toward the enemy and explode, dealing magic damage and applying silence for 4 seconds.

Formula (Explosion Damage): 52,938 (70% Magic Attack + Lvl * 70)

Formula (Spore Starting Health): 170,314 (16% Health)

Cooldown: 11s

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – The silence and AoE magic damage are extremely strong, but the value depends on how fast the spores are healed. Still very important for control and burst.

Will-o'-the-Wisp

Will-o’-the-Wisp deals magic damage to the nearest enemy. The damage increases based on healing received since the last cast: 50% of Mushy’s healing and 150% of allied healing is turned into additional damage.

Formula (Base Magic Damage): 63,100 (80% Magic Attack + Lvl * 100)

Formula (Max Extra Damage): 208,235 (12% Health + Lvl * 600 + 2,500)

Cooldown: 18s

Evolution Priority: Medium – The scaling damage is strong but inconsistent, because it depends on external healing. Good skill, but not as essential as clones, passives, and silence.

Best Patronage for Mushy & Shroom

This guide lists the best pets for Mushy & Shroom in Hero Wars: Dominion Era, ranked from most useful to least useful based on in-battle benefits and patronage synergy for tanking and survival.

1st Place:

Axel

Axel is the best pet for Mushy & Shroom because his patronage skill greatly increases survivability by redirecting heavy damage and capping how much damage the hero can take from a single attack. Mushy & Shroom rely heavily on staying alive to spawn mushrooms and sustain healing, so Axel prevents burst deaths from heroes like Dante, Ishmael, and K’arkh. The bonus Magic Attack also improves their damage output, while Armor contributes to tanking.

2nd Place:

Oliver

Oliver is a strong secondary option focused entirely on tank sustain. His passive healing when dropping below 50% HP helps Mushy & Shroom recover during long fights, and his patronage bonuses (Health and Armor) further strengthen their durability. However, because Oliver offers no team protection and lacks burst-damage mitigation, he ranks slightly below Axel for front-line survival.

How to Counter Mushy & Shroom Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Arachne

Arachne counters Mushy & Shroom by stunning both the hero and their mushrooms, preventing healing pulses and interrupting their defensive setup. Her burst pure damage bypasses their Magic Defense scaling.

Iris

Iris is one of the hardest counters because Expose Soul transfers Mushy & Shroom’s massive health pool into pure damage. Their mushrooms also trigger extra bite damage, accelerating their death.

Rufus

Rufus is a soft counter because Mushy & Shroom deal heavy magic and pure damage. If their killing blow is magical, Rufus resurrects with restored health, fully denying their finisher. This buys time for his team to overpower the mushrooms.

Mushy & Shroom Best War Flag Hero Wars

Mushy & Shroom benefit greatly from War Flags that enhance healing, survivability, and tank energy flow, boosting their frontline sustain and team utility.

War Flag of Recovery:

This War Flag increases all healing by 10%, directly boosting Shroom’s healing output and increasing Mushy’s survivability during long fights.

Mushy & Shroom and Team Benefit: Stronger healing keeps the frontline stable and allows their mushroom duplication to function longer before collapsing.

War Flag of Vanguard:

Heals the front hero every 4 seconds based on Max Health. Mushy’s naturally high durability synergizes perfectly with HP-based passive healing.

Mushy & Shroom and Team Benefit: Extra sustain ensures Mushy survives burst phases while Shrooms continue applying healing and pressure.

War Flag of Fervor:

Boosts tank energy gain by 10%, allowing Mushy to activate skills more frequently, especially the defensive and healing-related abilities.

Mushy & Shroom and Team Benefit: Faster skill activations increase protection, control, and overall team longevity.
