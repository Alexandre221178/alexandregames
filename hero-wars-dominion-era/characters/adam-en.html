Who Is Adam in Hero Wars Alliance?

Adam is a sharpshooter from the frontier wastelands, renowned for his precision shooting and his ability to mark targets with deadly accuracy. Armed with his signature Starfang Adaptive Pistol, he hunts down the strongest enemy on the battlefield and systematically disassembles their defenses with each shot. While only obtainable through special events, Adam's impact in endgame battles is significant.

Adam Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Adam's upgrades should first improve the damage and mark-stacking patterns that define his whole kit, then improve the supporting effects that make his Hunter's Mark pressure harder to trade into.

1 - Bounty Lock (White/Ultimate)

In-game description: Fires a tracking round at the enemy Hero without Hunter's Mark closest to the center of the enemy team. The shot applies Hunter's Mark and deals 257,905 damage. If the target dies while affected by Hunter's Mark, the ability is immediately reset.

Skill Explanation: Bounty Lock is Adam's primary burst tool and the source of his Hunter's Mark mechanic. He fires a precision shot at the enemy closest to the center without a mark, dealing heavy physical damage and applying Hunter's Mark. The mark feeds directly into Adaptive Caliber to start stacking further bonuses, and if the marked target dies the skill resets instantly, letting Adam chain marks back-to-back.

Evolution Priority: Very High – First skill to upgrade because it is Adam's main damage trigger and the starting point of his entire Hunter's Mark scaling loop. Every other skill in his kit depends on marks being applied quickly and repeatedly.

2 - Fan the Hammer (Green)

In-game description: Fires a series of 3 shots at the nearest enemy, dealing 78,990 damage with each shot. If there are enemies on the battlefield with Hunter's Mark, the shots ricochet to them, dealing 46,045 damage. Cooldown: 7s

Skill Explanation: Fan the Hammer adds AOE spread to Adam's otherwise single-target kit. He rapidly fires 3 shots at the nearest enemy, and each shot ricochets to every marked target on the field. The more marks Bounty Lock has applied, the more enemies take simultaneous ricochet hits, turning Fan the Hammer into a multi-target pressure tool that punishes grouped or marked teams.

Evolution Priority: High – Second skill to upgrade because its ricochet damage scales directly with how many Hunter's Marks are active. Once Bounty Lock is leveled, Fan the Hammer multiplies the payoff of every mark on the board.

3 - Adaptive Caliber (Violet/Passive)

In-game description: Passive skill. When Adam deals physical damage and does not fully penetrate the target's armor, his Armor Penetration increases by 3,533. If Adam fully penetrates the target's armor, his Physical Attack increases by 4,707. Both effects stack and last until the end of the battle.

Skill Explanation: Adaptive Caliber is Adam's passive scaling engine. It reacts to every hit he lands: if the target still has armor to spare, Adam gains armor penetration to eventually cut through it; once he fully pierces armor, he instead gains raw physical attack. Both bonuses are permanent for the duration of the battle, meaning Adam grows exponentially stronger the longer a fight goes on.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Third to upgrade because higher levels increase the Physical Attack and Armor Penetration gained per stack, directly amplifying the snowball potential that makes Adam dangerous in prolonged fights.

4 - Reactive Screen (Blue)

In-game description: Once every 10s, when Adam takes damage from enemies, a smoke screen is deployed beneath him for 5s. While inside the smoke screen, Adam gains 13,436 additional dodge and can evade all types of damage, not just physical. Cooldown: 10s

Skill Explanation: Reactive Screen is Adam's only defensive tool and it activates automatically the moment he takes damage. A smoke screen appears under him for 5 seconds, boosting his dodge and allowing him to evade both physical and magic damage during that window. Because it triggers on taking a hit rather than on a timer, it reliably kicks in during the moments Adam needs it most.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Last to upgrade because survivability only matters once Adam already threatens enough damage to be worth protecting. Higher levels increase the dodge bonus, which has diminishing returns compared to leveling the offensive skills first.

Best Patronage for Adam

Adam benefits from several pets through the patronage system. His primary synergies are with Fenris (1st choice) for Physical Attack boosts, and Cain (2nd choice) for armor penetration support.

1st Place: Fenris

Fenris increases Adam's Physical Attack, directly boosting Bounty Lock damage and Hunter's Mark stacking. This is Adam's best pet choice for maximizing offensive output.

2nd Place: Cain

Cain provides armor penetration support that synergizes with Adam's Adaptive Caliber passive, helping his attacks cut through high-armor defenders more effectively.

3rd Place: Biscuit

Biscuit offers utility that complements Adam's kit, providing additional support in battles where sustain and healing reduction are crucial.

4th Place: Vex

Vex provides additional utility that can help Adam in specific scenarios, offering support in battles where crowd control and debuffs are important.

Adam's Best Counter

Adam's main counter is Fluffy. Fluffy's 4th skill cancels an enemy's ultimate ability the moment it is used, dealing 7,654 damage and triggering once every 20 seconds. Because Adam's entire damage pattern depends on Bounty Lock applying Hunter's Mark as often as possible, each cancel removes a mark application and blocks a potential skill reset — shutting down his stacking loop for up to 20 seconds at a time.

Best Synergy Heroes for Adam

Adam thrives alongside heroes who amplify physical attack, provide armor reduction, or protect him long enough to stack Hunter's Mark bonuses. Here are the top synergy heroes:

Electra's 2nd skill deals damage to nearby enemies and converts 399% of that damage into healing for the team. This sustained recovery keeps Adam's composition alive long enough for Bounty Lock to reset multiple times and for Hunter's Mark stacks to fully accumulate.

Fafnir's Runic Blade enchants the physical attack ally he is shielding, adding 34,164 extra physical damage to every attack for 8 seconds. When Fafnir bonds to Adam, every Bounty Lock hit and every Fan the Hammer ricochet during that window gains that flat damage bonus, directly amplifying Adam's burst window.

Iris's 1st skill, Expose Soul, exposes the nearest enemy for 13 seconds. All damage taken by the exposed Soul is transferred to its owner as pure damage, bypassing resistances. When Adam uses Bounty Lock on an exposed target, the hit is converted to pure damage, significantly amplifying his single-target burst against that priority enemy.

Lyria's 2nd skill bonds to the ally with the highest physical attack — Adam — giving both heroes a vampirism bonus that sustains them through long fights. Her passive 4th skill reduces all enemies' dodge by 19,978 and adds extra physical damage to attacks, making Adam's shots land more reliably and hit harder on every marked target.

Polaris's Northern Lights reduces the level of enemy reactive and chance-based skills by 10, weakening proc-based defensive abilities that would otherwise interrupt Adam's damage. Her aura also extends allied control effects by 5%, making any CC on the team last longer and giving Adam a wider window to stack Hunter's Marks uncontested.

Adam Best War Flag Hero Wars

Adam benefits most from flags that keep his frontline alive long enough for Hunter's Mark to stack, or that cut enemy healing in sustain matchups.

War Flag of Vanguard:

Once every 4 seconds, heals the front hero on your team for 1.6% of their max health, plus an additional 0.3% for each red pattern inserted.

Adam and Team Benefit: Adam fights in a mid-line position and needs his frontline to survive long enough to accumulate Hunter's Mark stacks. Vanguard keeps Fafnir or any tank in Adam's best teams alive, which directly extends the window where Adam can build damage and deal with priority targets uninterrupted.

War Flag of Decline:

Decreases the enemy team's Healing by 10% for the duration of battle.

Adam and Team Benefit: Decline is Adam's best choice against sustain and healing teams. Adam's entire damage model depends on stacking enough marks to burst priority targets before they recover. Reducing enemy healing by 10% makes that burst window more reliable and prevents opponents from outlasting his mark-building phase.

Conclusion

Adam is a powerful and precise marksman who excels at eliminating the most dangerous enemy on the field. His Hunter's Mark mechanic creates a rewarding stack-based playstyle that, when supported by the right team, results in exponentially increasing damage output over the course of a battle. His core artifacts, the Starfang Adaptive Pistol and Ring of Agility, directly enhance his strengths, while his best skins provide additional stat scaling.

While Adam is currently unobtainable through standard means, players lucky enough to have him should invest heavily in his White and Green skills first, build around physical attack synergy heroes like Electra, Fafnir, and Lyria, and always watch out for Fluffy on the opposing team. With the right setup, Adam can be a devastating force in Hero Wars: Dominion Era battles.
