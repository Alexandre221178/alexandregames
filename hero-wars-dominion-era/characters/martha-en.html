When your team is close to falling apart, Martha can turn a cup of tea into a second chance. In Hero Wars: Dominion Era, she combines group healing, personal survivability, and a team speed boost that helps both physical and magic damage dealers. I like her most when a team needs time to recover and keep fighting.

The real question is where to invest first. My approach is to build her healing foundation, then improve the stats that keep her alive. Here, I explain her skills, both Ascension upgrades, skins, glyphs, artifacts, pets, and team choices, with the formulas behind those priorities so you can adapt the build to your own battles.

Who Is Martha in Hero Wars: Dominion Era?

Martha is an Intelligence-based healer who fights at the back line. Her story begins with a remarkable tea from the woods of Zarakkar, guarded by the Foremother, the ancient ancestor of all tortoises. That gift of longevity shapes her entire kit: she heals herself through basic attacks and supports her allies with a healing totem.

  • Main Role: Healer
  • Additional Role: Support
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Position: Back line; battlefield position #75
  • Patronage: Oliver, Axel, Robin
  • Soul Stones: Soul Chests, Soul Atrium

Her healing comes from two different stats. Magic Attack improves Tea Party, while Health improves Secret of Longevity and Healing Brew. This is why I give Health such a strong place in her skin priorities.

Martha Overall Rating in the Meta

These are my role-based assessments of her kit, rather than measured win rates. Her value comes from enabling allies and sustaining a fight.

  • Control: ★☆☆☆☆ — Her listed skills provide no direct crowd control.
  • Damage: ★☆☆☆☆ — Her basic attack is secondary to healing and support.
  • Synergy: ★★★★★ — Group healing and speed support many different damage dealers.
  • Independence: ★★★☆☆ — She can sustain herself, but needs allies to turn that survival into a victory.

Player rating: 4.8/5 in the supplied hero data. For me, her appeal is the combination of a durable back line and healing that can stabilize the whole team.

Martha Pros and Cons — Hero Wars: Web & Facebook

✅ Pros

  • Strong group healing: Tea Party restores allies' health, and Healing Brew adds extra healing for the ally with the least health.
  • A durable back line: Her Health pool and self-healing help her withstand pressure from Jhu, Kayla, Jorgen, and Luther. She can also endure pressure from Cleaver, although his displacement remains a problem.
  • Useful across damage types: Her team speed boost supports both physical and magic damage compositions.
  • A defensive and boss-fight option: Her sustained healing makes her a useful choice for defense teams and against Osh in Asgard.

❌ Cons

  • Healing disruption: Celeste, Jet, Yasmine, and the pet Biscuit can reduce the effectiveness of her healing.
  • A vulnerable totem: Area damage can destroy it, interrupting both group healing and Healing Brew. Ascension II improves its durability at grade 3, but does not make it indestructible.
  • Position matters: Cleaver can pull her forward and expose the back line. Shorter projectile travel also reduces the healing from Secret of Longevity.

Martha Skills Upgrade Priority — Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Learn how Martha's healing and speed skills work, which upgrades I prioritize first, and how Ascension improves her support as your account grows.

My beginner upgrade order: Tea Party → Healing Brew → Foremother's Oath → Secret of Longevity, as each skill becomes available. If Martha herself is dying first, move Secret of Longevity up. The cards below stay in their in-game order.

Formula examples use skill level 130, 922,177 Health, and 121,512 Magic Attack. Base skill priorities stand on their own; the later Ascension unlocks are additional investments.

1. Foremother's Oath

In-game description: Increases all allies' speed for 6s.

Skill Explanation: This is a team speed buff, not a damaging attack. At level 130, it increases allies' speed up to 250% for 6 seconds. I value it because it helps the whole team act faster, regardless of whether your main damage dealer uses physical or magic damage. That speed value should not be read as a guaranteed 250% increase in total team damage.

Formula: Increases allies' speed up to 250% (1 * level + 120)

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 6 seconds

Evolution Priority: Medium High — 3rd — I build the healing skills first, then strengthen the team speed boost. It becomes especially valuable once the team can survive long enough to use those faster actions.

2. Secret of Longevity

In-game description: Martha's basic attacks heal her when a projectile hits an enemy. The farther the projectile travels, the more health it will restore.

Skill Explanation: Martha heals herself when her basic-attack projectile reaches an enemy. A longer journey gives a larger heal, up to 273,322 Health per projectile with these stats. The attack itself deals physical damage; the passive adds healing for Martha. This explains why her back-line position matters and why being pulled forward can weaken her recovery.

Formula: One projectile can restore up to 273,322 (24% health + 400 * level) health

Evolution Priority: Medium — 4th — This directly helps Martha rather than healing the whole team. Upgrade it sooner when back-line pressure is killing her; otherwise, establish her team healing first.

3. Tea Party

In-game description: Conjures a totem in front of the character that restores allies' health. Enemies can't target the totem, but it can take AoE damage.

Skill Explanation: The totem is the foundation of Martha's group healing. Its listed healing value is 105,407 for each ally, with an 8-second duration. This is a healing effect, not magic damage, even though it scales with Magic Attack. Enemies cannot select the totem as a target, but area attacks can still hit and destroy it. When that happens, your team loses the healing it was relying on.

Formula: Health regen for each ally: 105,407 (60% magic attack + 250 * level) for 8s

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 15 seconds
  • Duration: 8 seconds

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st — This heals the whole team and enables Healing Brew. It earns first priority even before Ascension is available, because the base totem is already central to her job.

Ascension II: Invigorating Herbs

In-game description — Grade 3: While the Totem is active, it increases the speed of all allies by 20% (20). The Totem can take up to 3 (3) hits before it is destroyed.

Skill Explanation: Before Ascension, Tea Party provides healing. Invigorating Herbs adds a team speed bonus while the totem is active: 10% at grade 0, 15% at grade 1, and 20% at grades 2 and 3. Grade 3 also lets the totem take up to 3 hits before destruction. I see that final step as valuable protection for the source of both of her totem-based heals.

4. Healing Brew

In-game description: While the totem is active, the ally with the least health now receives additional healing.

Skill Explanation: While Tea Party's totem is active, the ally with the least health receives an extra 180,327 Health per second for 8 seconds. This is targeted healing, not damage. It helps the ally in greatest need while Tea Party supports everyone. The extra healing depends on the totem remaining active, so it cannot replace Tea Party.

Formula: Health regen per second: 180,327 (15% health + 400 * level - 10,000) for 8s

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 8 seconds

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd — Once unlocked, this strengthens her ability to save an injured ally. I place it after Tea Party because it only works while that skill's totem is active.

Ascension V: Turtle's Patronage

In-game description — Grade 3: While Martha has more than 50% (50) Health, 20% (20) of damage received by the two nearest allies is transferred to her.

Skill Explanation: Healing Brew already helps an injured ally; Turtle's Patronage adds damage sharing. While Martha is above 50% Health, she takes 10%, 15%, or 20% of the damage received by the nearest ally, depending on grade. At grade 3, the 20% transfer covers the two nearest allies. Damage is transferred to Martha, so her own survival still matters. At 50% Health or below, the stated activation condition is no longer met.

Best Skin for Martha — Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Choose Martha's skins around the healing you need most: Health strengthens two skills, Armor helps her survive, and Magic Attack improves Tea Party.

My Martha skin priority: Solar or Celestial → the other Health skin → Winter → Default → Masquerade. Solar and Celestial provide exactly the same bonus and have the same leveling cost, so start with whichever you have. Move Winter ahead if physical damage is killing Martha; move Masquerade up if she already survives comfortably and your main problem is group healing.

All skin bonuses stack regardless of the appearance you equip. You do not need to wear a Health skin to receive its upgraded Health bonus. The cards below keep their original order.

Default Skin

Stats gain: Intelligence +1,365

Each Intelligence point gives 3 Magic Attack and 1 Magic Defense, plus 1 Physical Attack because Intelligence is Martha's main stat.

  • Magic Attack from Intelligence: +4,095
  • Magic Defense from Intelligence: +1,365
  • Physical Attack from Intelligence: +1,365

The extra Magic Attack adds 2,457 to Tea Party's formula: 60% × 4,095. Physical Attack is a secondary benefit for a hero whose main job is healing.

Evolution Priority: Medium — after Health and Armor — Intelligence gives a useful mix of healing and magic defense at a lower total stone cost. I put it after the defensive core; a smaller budget can justify working on this cheaper skin earlier.

Total Intelligence Skin Stones for maximum level: 30,825.

Winter Skin

Stats gain: Armor +10,665

This is the defensive choice against physical damage. The benefit is keeping Martha alive so her healing and speed support can continue.

Evolution Priority: High — after both Health skins — Armor helps Martha withstand physical pressure, but does not directly increase her healing formulas. Move this skin ahead of the second Health skin if physical attackers are the reason she dies.

Total Intelligence Skin Stones for maximum level: 58,412.

Evolution Priority: Very High — joint 1st with Celestial — Health improves Martha's survival and two healing skills at once. It is my first general-purpose investment, with the same priority as Celestial.

Evolution Priority: Medium — situational healing upgrade — This directly improves group healing, but does not raise the Health-based healing of Secret of Longevity or Healing Brew. Move it ahead of defensive skins when Martha is safe and Tea Party needs more healing output.

Total Intelligence Skin Stones for maximum level: 55,410.

This bonus adds approximately 25,595 to Secret of Longevity's maximum self-heal (24% × 106,645) and 15,997 per second to Healing Brew (15% × 106,645), before temporary effects. It also gives Martha more Health to absorb damage when Turtle's Patronage is unlocked.

Martha Glyph Evolution Priority

Build Martha's glyphs around reliable healing and survival, then adjust the defensive upgrades to the enemies that put the most pressure on your team.

My general glyph order: Health → Armor → Intelligence → Magic Attack → Magic Defense. Move Magic Defense ahead of Armor against magic pressure, or Magic Attack ahead of defensive upgrades when Martha already survives comfortably. The cards keep their in-game order.

1. Magic Attack Glyph

Stats gain: Magic Attack +6,500

The +6,500 Magic Attack adds 3,900 to Tea Party's healing formula (60% × 6,500). It improves healing across the team, but not the two Health-based heals.

Evolution Priority: Medium High — 4th — I prioritize keeping Martha alive first. Move this glyph up when she is safe and the whole team needs stronger totem healing.

2. Health Glyph

Stats gain: Health +62,200

The +62,200 Health adds 14,928 to Secret of Longevity's maximum heal and 9,330 per second to Healing Brew. It also provides a larger pool for damage transferred by Turtle's Patronage.

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st — This single upgrade improves survival and two healing skills, making it my strongest general starting point.

3. Magic Defense Glyph

Stats gain: Magic Defense +6,500

Magic Defense reduces incoming magic damage. It does not directly increase Martha's healing.

Evolution Priority: Medium — 5th by default — Keep it later when physical damage is the main threat, but move it ahead of Armor against magic damage teams.

4. Armor Glyph

Stats gain: Armor +6,500

Armor helps Martha survive physical attacks aimed at the back line. Unlike Health, it does not directly increase a healing formula.

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd — A healer who dies cannot sustain the team. I build this early when physical pressure threatens Martha before she can recover.

5. Intelligence Glyph

Stats gain: Intelligence +1,135

+3,405 Magic Attack, +1,135 Magic Defense, and +1,135 Physical Attack come from this main-stat bonus. The Magic Attack adds 2,043 to Tea Party's healing formula.

Evolution Priority: Medium High — 3rd — I value its mixture of healing and magic defense after the Health and Armor foundation. Pure healing output favors the Magic Attack glyph instead.

Martha Artifact Evolution Priority — Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Choose Martha's artifacts by their effect on survival: the book strengthens healing, the teapot supplies Armor, and the ring improves her main stat.

My artifact order: Tome of Arcane Knowledge → Zarakkari Teapot → Ring of Intelligence. Move the weapon ahead when the team urgently needs protection from physical damage. Weapon, book, and ring use different leveling materials, so this order is most useful when deciding where shared resources should go.

Weapon: Zarakkari Teapot

Stats gain: Armor +50,190

When Foremother's Oath activates the weapon artifact, the team receives its Armor bonus for 9 seconds. The displayed maximum bonus is +50,190 Armor. This is a temporary team buff, not permanent Armor added to Martha.

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd — This protects the entire team against physical damage. It can become my first priority in that matchup, and Robin's extra Energy helps Martha reach the ultimate that triggers it.

Book: Tome of Arcane Knowledge

Stats gain: Health +83,649

Stats gain: Magic Attack +16,731

The book adds approximately 20,076 to Secret of Longevity's maximum healing, 12,547 per second to Healing Brew, and 10,039 to Tea Party's formula. These increases come from 24% and 15% of its Health, and 60% of its Magic Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st — It strengthens all three healing skills while making Martha harder to kill. That broad benefit is why I start here for a general build.

Ring: Ring of Intelligence

Stats gain: Intelligence +6,249

The Intelligence provides +18,747 Magic Attack, +6,249 Magic Defense, and +6,249 Physical Attack. It adds approximately 11,248 to Tea Party's formula (60% × 18,747).

Evolution Priority: Medium High — 3rd — This gives the largest direct Tea Party increase of the two permanent artifacts, but the book also improves Health-based healing and the weapon protects allies. Raise the ring earlier if stronger group healing is your main need.

Best Patronage for Martha

Pick Martha's patron by the problem you need to solve: Robin supports Energy gain, Oliver adds durability, and Axel helps her withstand powerful hits.

My general ranking: Robin → Oliver → Axel. These are all three compatible patrons. This order assumes Martha survives long enough to support the team; against lethal burst damage, Axel can become the best choice.

1st Place — Robin

Robin

Fair Wind gives Martha about 75.01 bonus Energy every 5 seconds at the listed maximum patronage power. Robin also supplies +99,576 Health and +9,958 Magic Defense. I put him first because the Health supports her two Health-based heals, while Energy helps her use Foremother's Oath and its Armor artifact buff more often. Her ultimate grants speed; it does not directly summon Tea Party's totem.

2nd Place — Oliver

Oliver

Oliver supplies Health and Armor, a useful combination when Martha needs to survive physical pressure. Eternal Bloom provides patronage healing, with a listed maximum value of 11,064 every 0.5 seconds when its effect is active. I favor him when Martha's own durability is the problem, particularly as Turtle's Patronage adds transferred damage. Oliver skill reference.

3rd Place — Axel

Axel

Axel supplies Magic Attack and Armor. His Defensive Blub patronage limits damage from an individual attack to roughly 45% of the owner's maximum Health at maximum patronage power. Choose him earlier if large individual hits kill Martha before she can recover. The Magic Attack also improves Tea Party. This protection is not the team damage-sharing shield from Axel's active skill. Axel skill reference.

Patronage and the main pet are separate choices. Robin can patron Martha while Axel is selected as the main pet for the team. Only the selected main pet supplies its active battle skills; assigning a patron does not give Martha those active skills.

How to Counter Martha in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

The most effective approach is to interrupt the healing your opponent needs: reduce healing received, destroy the totem, or disrupt Martha's position.

The options below are in alphabetical order. Their value depends on which allies their skills actually reach; adding one counter is not a guaranteed victory.

Biscuit

Icy Storm prevents affected enemies from receiving healing from other characters for 5 seconds, which interrupts Martha's healing of her teammates. Self-healing is an exception. Biscuit's patronage separately makes its owner's attacks reduce the target's healing received for 5 seconds. This is useful against a team relying on Martha to recover between attacks. Biscuit skill reference.

Celeste

Cursed Flame blocks healing on affected enemies, and Limbo converts a portion of that blocked healing into magic damage. Instead of trying to outdamage every heal, Celeste creates a window in which Martha cannot restore the affected ally normally. Position and flame coverage matter. Celeste skill reference.

Cleaver

Rusty Hook pulls a distant enemy toward Cleaver. When Martha is the target, she loses her protected back-line position and faces nearby threats. The shorter travel distance of her attacks also reduces the self-healing described by Secret of Longevity. Her high Health does not prevent displacement. Cleaver skill reference.

Jet

Potion of Fatigue stops nearby affected enemies from regenerating Health for 8 seconds. Jet can therefore deny Martha's healing to the allies he hits without needing to reach Martha herself. The effect is limited to affected targets, rather than automatically shutting down the entire team. Jet skill reference.

Yasmine

Unknown Toxin blocks part of each heal received by enemies affected by Yasmine's poison. This makes it harder for Martha to rescue a poisoned ally. It is partial healing suppression, not a universal healing ban, and Yasmine must first apply her poison. Yasmine skill reference.

Watch the totem, too. Area attacks can destroy it even though enemies cannot target it directly. With Invigorating Herbs at grade 3 it can take up to three hits, so one area hit should not be assumed to remove a fully upgraded totem. Losing the totem interrupts both Tea Party and Healing Brew.

Martha Best War Flags — Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Choose a War Flag that solves the team's problem: Recovery strengthens healing, Readiness helps the rear hero, and Bastion protects against attacks.

War Flag of Recovery

My first choice for sustained healing.

Effect: Increases all healing by 10%.

Martha and Team Benefit: This directly supports Martha's healing role. I start here when the team survives the opening and needs stronger recovery throughout the battle. A healing bonus does not remove an enemy healing-block effect.

War Flag of Readiness

For more frequent rear-line ultimates.

Effect: Gives 100 Energy to the rearmost hero at the start and every 20 seconds. Each red pattern adds 20 Energy.

Martha and Team Benefit: Martha is a back-line hero, but she only receives the Energy if she is actually the rearmost hero in that lineup. When she is, this helps her activate Foremother's Oath and the Armor artifact buff. If another hero stands behind her, that hero receives the benefit instead.

War Flag of Bastion

For surviving early physical pressure.

Effect: At the start, adds Armor equal to 30% of the front hero's Armor; this declines to 5% over 20 seconds. Each red pattern extends the effect by 3 seconds.

Martha and Team Benefit: Choose it when physical damage overwhelms the team before Martha can stabilize the fight. Its strength depends on the front hero's Armor, so it is a team-building decision rather than a bonus based on Martha's own Armor.

Is Martha Worth Building?

I recommend Martha when your team needs sustained healing and a speed boost that supports its damage dealers. Her best investment path starts with a simple question: is she dying, or is the team failing to recover?

If Martha is dying, prioritize Health and the relevant defense, and reconsider her patron. If she survives but allies keep falling, improve Tea Party, Healing Brew, and the stats behind those formulas. Build the base kit first, then use Invigorating Herbs to improve the totem and Turtle's Patronage to protect nearby allies.

For a general build, my starting point is Health-focused upgrades, the Tome of Arcane Knowledge, Robin patronage, and the War Flag of Recovery. Adjust those choices to the opponent. A durable Martha with a protected totem can keep a team fighting; a bigger healing number alone will not solve displacement, a destroyed totem, or healing suppression.
