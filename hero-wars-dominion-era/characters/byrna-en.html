Byrna Guide – Skills, Artifacts, and Best Synergies for Hero Wars Dominion Era

Byrna is a new support hero introduced in Hero Wars Dominion Era. She brings a unique combination of healing, scaling magic attack, and team survivability. Even though she is classified as a support hero, Byrna has mechanics that allow her to grow stronger during battle while helping her team survive longer fights.

This is a preliminary guide based on the currently known abilities and mechanics. Since Byrna is still new, some strategies may evolve as players test her in Arena, Grand Arena, and Guild Wars.

Who is Byrna?

Byrna is a half-orc hero blessed by the guardian spirit Ursa. Instead of using raw brute force, she channels the spirit of the bear to protect and heal her allies. Her abilities revolve around spreading healing effects across the team while gradually increasing her own Magic Attack during the battle.

This combination makes her especially powerful in long fights, where she can continuously scale and strengthen her abilities.

Byrna's Artifacts

Orc Princess Talisman

Main stat: Magic Penetration.

This artifact improves Byrna’s ability to deal magic damage through her abilities. Since some of her skills deal magic damage to enemies, Magic Penetration helps them remain effective even against high Magic Defense targets.

Manuscript of the Void

Stats: Magic Attack and Magic Penetration.

This artifact is extremely important for Byrna because many of her abilities scale with Magic Attack. Increasing this stat directly improves both her healing output and her damage potential.

Ring of Strength

Main stat: Strength.

Strength increases Byrna’s health pool, allowing her to survive longer in battle. Since she is a support hero who needs time to scale her power, survivability is important.

Byrna Skills

Roar of Nature

Byrna calls upon the guardian spirit to damage all enemies while healing her allies. The healing from this ability is then converted into maximum health increase for allies until the end of the battle.

This effect makes Byrna extremely valuable in prolonged fights. Each activation effectively increases the durability of the entire team, making them progressively harder to defeat.

Teams that rely on surviving burst damage can greatly benefit from this ability.

Guardian Spirit

Byrna summons a guardian spirit that connects to the front-line tank. If no tank is available, it connects to a random ally.

The protected hero only receives part of the incoming damage, while the rest is redirected to the spirit. The spirit has its own health pool and absorbs damage until it is destroyed.

While the spirit is active, Byrna’s attacks no longer deal direct damage. Instead, she commands the spirit to attack enemies.

This mechanic provides additional survivability for the front-line hero while also allowing Byrna to contribute extra damage through the spirit.

Bear Cuddle

Byrna applies a healing effect over time to the front-line ally, typically the tank.

While this effect is active, any healing received by that hero spreads to the rest of the team, restoring a portion of the same healing to other allies.

This mechanic creates powerful synergy with heroes that generate frequent healing, such as tanks with vampirism or heroes that can heal themselves during combat.

In many situations, the tank becomes a continuous healing source for the entire team.

Living Heart

Each time an ally restores health, Byrna gains additional Magic Attack for the rest of the battle.

This passive ability is what allows Byrna to scale during combat. The more healing events occur, the stronger Byrna becomes.

Since her Magic Attack increases over time, both her damage and healing abilities become stronger as the fight continues.

How Byrna Scales During Battle

One of Byrna’s most interesting mechanics is the interaction between Bear Cuddle and Living Heart.

When the tank receives healing:

  • the tank restores health
  • Bear Cuddle spreads part of that healing to other allies
  • each healing event activates Living Heart

This means a single healing event can trigger multiple Magic Attack increases for Byrna.

Over time, this creates a scaling effect where Byrna becomes increasingly powerful throughout the battle.

Best Tank Synergies

Byrna works best with tanks that either heal themselves or frequently trigger healing effects.

Electra

Electra benefits from Byrna because her abilities allow her to restore health through damage. When Electra heals herself, Bear Cuddle spreads part of that healing to the rest of the team.

Additionally, Byrna’s health-increase effect makes Electra even harder to kill in extended fights.

Galahad

Galahad can use vampirism to restore health while dealing damage. With Bear Cuddle active, his lifesteal healing can spread to other allies, improving the team’s sustain.

Cleaver

Cleaver’s high health pool and durability make him a good target for Byrna’s healing effects. When he receives healing, part of it can support the entire team.

Julius

Julius is a durable tank with shields and survivability mechanics. Byrna can improve his sustainability in longer fights while increasing overall team durability.

Best Team Strategies for Byrna

Byrna performs best in teams designed for longer battles.

She is particularly effective in:

  • magic damage teams
  • sustain compositions
  • defensive Arena teams
  • teams that generate frequent healing

These types of teams allow her passive ability to scale her Magic Attack and strengthen the entire team over time.

Is Byrna Good?

Although she is still new and strategies are evolving, Byrna appears to be a strong support hero with excellent scaling potential.

Her combination of healing distribution, maximum health increase, and Magic Attack scaling makes her especially dangerous in extended fights.

As players continue testing her in competitive modes, Byrna may become an important hero in defensive and sustain-based team compositions.

Final Thoughts

Byrna introduces a unique mechanic to Hero Wars Dominion Era: healing that spreads across the team while simultaneously increasing her own power.

If used in the right team composition, she can significantly increase survivability and create a powerful scaling effect that grows stronger the longer the battle lasts.

Since this guide is based on early information, expect strategies and optimal team compositions to evolve as more players experiment with Byrna in real battles.
