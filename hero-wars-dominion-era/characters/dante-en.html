In Hero Wars: Dominion Era, Dante emerges as one of the most versatile and deadly marksmen in the game. With his agility-based attacks and mid-line positioning, Dante combines speed and power to strike down enemies before they even reach your tanks.

Whether you're a beginner or a veteran player, understanding how to maximize Dante’s potential can give you a powerful edge in both PvE and PvP battles.

Who Is Dante in Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Dante is an Agility-based marksman who fights from the middle line and wins fights by mixing strong spear damage with one of the most annoying Dodge engines in the game. He is simple to understand on paper, but in practice he becomes much more oppressive when his Dodge loops start protecting him and feeding extra spear pressure.

  • Main Role: Marksman
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Position: Fights at the Middle Line (Position 29)
  • Patronage: Fenris, Cain
  • Soul Stones: Tower Shop, Soul Chests, Soul Atrium
  • Best Synergy: Lyria, Nebula, Aidan

What makes Dante special is that he does not rely on raw stats alone. He pressures multiple enemies with spectral spears, buffs team Dodge, punishes heavy hits with automatic counterattacks, and lowers enemy main stats at the same time. That package gives him damage, disruption, and survivability without needing to play like a standard backline glass cannon.

Pros and Cons of Dante Based on His Skills

Dante excels in dodge-based mechanics and burst physical damage, making him a powerful and tricky hero in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. Below are his main strengths and weaknesses.

Pros:

  • Strong Dodge Synergy: Dante’s Retribution skill triggers extra attacks when he dodges, increasing his damage output significantly.
  • High Burst Damage: His main skill, Instrument of Fate, deals heavy physical damage and disrupts enemies by pushing them back.
  • Debuff Capabilities: With Shackles of Weakness, Dante reduces enemies’ main stats, weakening their performance in battle.
  • Team Support: His Foresight skill boosts dodge for all allies, improving team survivability.

Cons:

  • Dependent on Dodge: Dante’s damage and utility heavily rely on successfully dodging attacks, making him less effective against accuracy or control-heavy enemies.
  • Squishy Without Proper Builds: Without sufficient armor or health, Dante can be fragile and prone to burst damage.
  • Situational Debuff: The stat reduction from Shackles of Weakness is useful but depends on landing hits from his spears, which can be interrupted.

Dante Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars: Dominion Era

Dante’s upgrades should first improve the damage and Dodge patterns that define his whole kit, then improve the supporting effects that make his spear pressure harder to trade into.

1 - Instrument of Fate (Ultimate)

Instrument of Fate is Dante’s main burst tool. He throws 4 spectral spears at multiple enemies, dealing 210,193 physical damage and pushing targets back. This gives him real frontline disruption while also pressuring several enemies at once, which is why the skill stays central in both PvP and PvE.

Formula - Physical Damage: 210,193 (220% Physical Attack + 100 x Level)

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is the first skill to care about because it is Dante’s clearest source of burst, positioning pressure, and artifact activation tempo.

Skill Info

  • Keywords: physical damage, knockback, multi-target pressure
  • Area: multiple enemies

2 - Foresight

Foresight gives all allies 16,524 Dodge for 5 seconds. This is the skill that turns Dante from a selfish damage dealer into a real dodge-core enabler, because it helps the whole team survive physical pressure while also making Dante lineups much more frustrating to pin down.

Formula - Bonus Dodge: 16,524 (18% Physical Attack + 3 x Level)

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade this early because Dante teams become much more stable when the dodge window is large enough to create missed attacks and energy swings.

Skill Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 5 seconds
  • Cooldown: 18 seconds
  • Keywords: team dodge, physical mitigation, dodge synergy

3 - Shackles of Weakness

Shackles of Weakness adds a debuff layer to Dante’s spear hits. Whenever a spectral spear from Instrument of Fate or Retribution lands, Dante reduces the enemy’s main stat by 7,022 for 5 seconds. That lowers enemy damage, defense scaling, or healing scaling depending on who gets hit, which gives Dante more value than a pure damage marksman in longer fights.

Formula - Main Stat Reduction: 7,022 (7% Physical Attack + 8 x Level - 292)

Evolution Priority: Medium High – This is worth leveling before Retribution in most builds because the stat reduction applies more consistently across fights and improves Dante’s overall pressure profile.

Skill Info

  • Duration: 5 seconds
  • Keywords: main stat reduction, spear synergy, debuff pressure

4 - Retribution

Retribution is Dante’s automatic punish when the enemy plays into his Dodge pattern. After dodging damage greater than 5% of his total health, he throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy for 111,596 physical damage. The effect is powerful in the right matchup, but it is more conditional than the rest of his kit because it depends on successful dodges and meaningful incoming hits.

Formula - Physical Damage: 111,596 (110% Physical Attack + 100 x Level)

Evolution Priority: Medium – This skill is still good, but it becomes best when Dante already has enough Dodge support and survivability to trigger it often.

Skill Info

  • Cooldown: 1 second
  • Trigger: dodge of damage above 5% of Dante’s total health
  • Keywords: counterattack, dodge trigger, single-target damage

Best Patronage for Dante

Dante benefits most from pets that boost dodge, physical attack, and energy gain, increasing his damage and survivability.

1st Place: Cain

Cain greatly boosts Dante's survivability and skill uptime. His Dodge bonus fits perfectly with Dante’s Retribution skill and lets him trigger more spectral spears. Cain also restores Energy when Dante dodges, helping him use his ultimate more often.

2nd Place: Fenris

Fenris increases Dante’s Physical Attack and Armor Penetration, improving overall damage output. His blinding effect on basic attacks can reduce incoming damage in prolonged fights, making him a strong offensive support option when Cain isn’t available.

How to Counter Dante in Hero Wars

Dante can be a dangerous opponent, but he has clear weaknesses you can exploit with the right heroes and tactics. Because his playstyle depends on dodging physical hits, crowd control abilities and magic damage are particularly effective.

  • Magic-Based Heroes: Since Dante’s Dodge doesn’t work against magical or pure damage, heroes like Phobos, Celeste, and Iris can bypass his defenses and deal serious damage. Iris, in particular, can punish Dante with pure damage that ignores armor and dodge completely.
  • Control Effects: Heroes with stun, charm, or silence skills can interrupt Dante’s spear attacks. Lian is a perfect counter each time Dante attacks her, he risks being charmed, disrupting his flow and making him a liability to his team.
  • High Dodge Heroes: Although Dante excels at dodging, he struggles against other heroes with similar agility. Aurora, Heidi, Yasmine, and Qing Mao can often evade his attacks, turning his strength into a disadvantage. Heidi, in particular, can blind and poison him while dodging his spears with ease.
  • Positioning Exploits: Dante often stands near the front lines, making him vulnerable to area-of-effect control or focused burst damage. Proper positioning and timing can neutralize his impact early in the fight.

Countering Dante requires leveraging heroes who deal magic damage and can interrupt his actions through crowd control. Avoid relying heavily on physical damage or heroes who can’t mitigate his Dodge advantage.

Dante Best War Flag Hero Wars

Dante performs best with War Flags that either strengthen his tempo windows, improve the value of his patron pet, or make it easier for his dodge-based team to survive long enough for spear pressure to take over.

War Flag of Pet Strength

Dante and Team Benefit: Dante often relies heavily on Cain or Fenris to push either his dodge engine or his physical damage profile. War Flag of Pet Strength increases the value of that patron support and fits naturally into Dante comps that are already built around pet synergy.

War Flag of Frost

Dante and Team Benefit: Frost helps Dante teams survive cleaner against enemy bursts and support rotations by lowering enemy skill levels. That gives Dante more time to cycle Dodge buffs, ultimates, and spear pressure without losing momentum too early.

War Flag of Decline

Dante and Team Benefit: Decline is a strong situational option when Dante is trying to break sustain teams. His spear pressure is much more dangerous when enemy healing is reduced and targets cannot comfortably recover between his damage windows.

Dante Guide Conclusion

Dante continues to be a great hero in Hero Wars: Dominion Era as a dependable and versatile hero. His ability to increase Dodge for all allies offers strong defensive synergy, especially when paired with heroes like Aurora. Positioned on the front line, his passive skill weakens the main stat of enemies, making it easier for his team to land critical hits. To unlock his full potential, Dante thrives alongside damage-boosting allies such as Jet, Nebula, Sebastian, and Fafnir, which secures his role in many top-tier lineups.

Even with the arrival of Lyria who reduces enemy Dodge Dante remains strong. His passive skill can counteract Lyria by lowering her main stat, helping to maintain his effectiveness even when he fails to dodge attacks. In fact, Dante is likely to remain in top-tier teams, often fighting alongside Lyria as an ally rather than as an opponent.
