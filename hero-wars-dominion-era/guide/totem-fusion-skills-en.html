What Is Totem Fusion?

Totem Fusion is the system that lets you add extra skills to Elemental Spirit Totems in Hero Wars: Dominion Era. These skills sit on top of the basic Totem ability and give your Titan team extra control, burst damage, shields, healing, energy, or attack scaling.

Elemental Skills are the more explosive Fusion Skills. They can trigger crowd control, damage, defensive barriers, anti-death windows, or extra effects tied to enemy actions.

Primal Skills are passive support skills. They usually react to things your Titans already do, such as receiving a shield, being healed, using a skill, or fighting while low on Health.

The updated pool has 12 Elemental Skills and 5 Primal Skills. Because duplicate skills do not stack cleanly, the best choice depends on your element, your first Totem activation, and whether your team wins through burst, control, sustain, or long fights.

Totem Skills - How Does Fusion Work?

Elemental Spirit Fusion

Fusion Skills are created and upgraded with Catalysts. If you fuse a skill your Totem already has, your odds of seeing that same skill again improve, and the result has a better chance to appear at a higher rank.

You can refuse the crafted result before accepting it. Once you accept a Fusion Skill, treat it as part of that Totem build and plan around it.

If two Totems bring the same Fusion Skill, the bonuses do not stack as two separate effects. In the best case, the strongest version applies or the duration is refreshed, so avoid copying the same skill across multiple Totems unless you have a very specific reason.

Elemental Skills usually decide the tempo of the fight. Primal Skills usually make your Titans harder to kill, faster to charge, or more dangerous as the battle continues.

Elemental Catalyst vs Primal Catalyst - What Is the Difference?

Totem Fusion uses two Catalyst types. The one you need depends on the skill type you are trying to fuse.

Elemental Catalysts are used for Elemental Skills. These skills are usually active battle effects such as control, burst damage, barriers, or trigger-based punishment.

Primal Catalysts are used for Primal Skills. These skills are passive support effects that react to Titan conditions like healing, shields, Energy, missing Health, and ability usage.

Type Used For Skill Effect
Elemental Catalyst
Elemental Skills Control, burst damage, barriers, anti-death windows, and battlefield effects
Primal Catalyst
Primal Skills Passive effects tied to healing, shields, Energy, missing Health, or skill usage

How to Get Elemental Catalysts

Elemental Catalysts are mainly tied to Totem Fragment conversion and Totem Fusion rewards. If you have extra Totem Fragments, exchanging them through the Fusion interface is the direct way to push Elemental Skill rolls and upgrades.

Tip: Save your fragments when you are chasing a specific high-impact skill, because using Catalysts in larger batches improves your odds of landing a useful result.

How to Get Primal Catalysts

One of the most valuable ways to obtain Primal Catalysts is by deconstructing a Legendary Gift of Dominion. This item is usually used to upgrade all equipment of a hero to Orange rank, so converting it should be a deliberate choice.

If you do not urgently need a Legendary Gift of Dominion for hero gear, turning it into Primal Catalysts can be worth it for Titan-focused accounts. One Legendary Gift gives 135 Primal Catalyst units.

Tip: Dominion Gifts and Catalysts are rare resources. Spend them around the Totem team you actually use in Guild War, Clash of Worlds, and daily Titan PvP.

Complete Updated Totem Fusion Skill List

All values in this list are shown for Totem level 130, Totem rank 6, and Skill rank 6. Effects that say they depend on Totem power or skill rank can scale differently on lower builds.

Elemental Skills

Last Flash

Totem Skill In-game description: When a Titan should die, it goes into a state of burning rage instead. Its speed increases by 15%. While enraged, the power of the Titan's attack increases, and it becomes invulnerable to all effects, while its Health cannot drop lower than 1. The Titan dies once the effect ends. Activates once per battle.

Totem Skill Explanation: Last Flash is a clutch survival skill. It is strongest on Titans that can keep stunning, damaging, or charging the Totem after they would normally be dead, such as Moloch, Angus, or a Nova tank setup.

Max-rank values: Attack increase: 112,125; duration: 20 seconds; cooldown: 300s.

Star Dust

Totem Skill In-game description: The battlefield is covered by a magical storm, leaving a distortion behind. The effect is active for 15 sec., and the energy periodically pushes a random Titan. While the effect lasts, any movement by a Titan on the battlefield deals damage to them depending on the distance traveled.

Totem Skill Explanation: Star Dust punishes movement, so it becomes much better when Titans are pushed, pulled, or forced to reposition. It is a matchup skill rather than a universal best pick.

Max-rank values: Damage from movement: 828,750; push frequency: every 1 sec.

Ice Age

Totem Skill In-game description: Once the base Totem skill of your opponent activates, all of their Titans are frozen for 1 sec., losing their ability to act. They also have the fragility effect applied to them which increases all damage received.

Totem Skill Explanation: Ice Age is still one of the safest PvP picks. It interrupts the enemy Totem timing and adds a long fragility window, making your follow-up damage much more threatening.

Max-rank values: Fragility damage bonus: 55%; fragility duration: 15 sec.

Wrath of the Depths

Totem Skill In-game description: At the moment of its death, an allied Titan turns to stone, rises high above the battlefield, and then falls right in the center of your opponent's team. The stone deals area damage and stuns enemies. Activates once per battle.

Totem Skill Explanation: Wrath of the Depths can steal momentum after a Titan dies, but it is reactive. It is useful when your frontline is expected to fall, yet weaker than skills that create pressure before you lose a Titan.

Max-rank values: Damage: 2,193,750; stun duration: 4 sec.; cooldown: 300s.

Gravitational Anomaly

Totem Skill In-game description: Conjures an anomaly in the center of the enemy team. It drags in enemies and damages them for 5 sec. The further the Titans are from its epicenter, the less damage they receive.

Totem Skill Explanation: Gravitational Anomaly is best when your team benefits from grouping enemies. It can help AoE damage land better, but its value depends on enemy positioning and whether your team can punish the pull quickly.

Max-rank values: Damage in the epicenter: 1,669,078.

Nova Flare

Totem Skill In-game description: Creates a Nova flare on the battlefield. It concentrates for 5 sec. For 2 sec. after activation, the Health of allied Titans can't fall lower than 1. After this, the Flare blows up and damages all enemies.

Totem Skill Explanation: Nova Flare combines a short anti-death window with heavy teamwide damage. It is one of the best universal skills because it can save your team during a lethal burst and then punish the enemy side.

Max-rank values: Explosion damage: 5,449,641.

Flame Dance

Totem Skill In-game description: Summons a blazing vortex that sweeps through the enemy team and damages nearby opponents.

Totem Skill Explanation: Flame Dance is simple and aggressive. It shines on Fire and Dark plans that want to delete enemies before they stabilize, but it offers less control than Ice Age or Dreamveil.

Max-rank values: Damage to opponents upon every activation: 973,294.

The Depths Whisper

Totem Skill In-game description: Creates a whirlpool that restores Health to allied Titans and damages nearby enemies over 10 sec. Each time it activates, the restored Health is distributed among Titans in need of healing.

Totem Skill Explanation: The Depths Whisper is a sustain skill. It is better in longer fights and on teams that can survive long enough to receive the full healing value.

Max-rank values: Damage and total Health restoration upon every activation: 486,647.

Murmur of the Crags

Totem Skill In-game description: Creates a rocky cascade on the battlefield that protects all allies from any attacks. It loses durability when damaged but reflects double the damage taken back at the attackers.

Totem Skill Explanation: Murmur of the Crags is one of the strongest defensive skills. It buys time for slow teams, protects key ultimates, and punishes enemies that keep attacking into the barrier.

Max-rank values: Damage absorbed by the barrier: 5,654,756.

Dark Matter

Totem Skill In-game description: Every time an enemy receives a stun effect, they are struck by a comet. It deals damage and increases vulnerability to elemental damage by 75%.

Totem Skill Explanation: Dark Matter is excellent when your team already applies stuns. Moloch, Nova, and several Light control setups can turn this into repeated extra damage plus a huge elemental damage window.

Max-rank values: Comet damage: 2,437,500; vulnerability duration: 21 sec.

Main Sequence

Totem Skill In-game description: Every few seconds, increases the attack stat of all allied Titans until the end of battle.

Totem Skill Explanation: Main Sequence rewards teams that can stretch the fight. Water and Earth teams often make the best use of it because they can survive while the attack bonuses build up.

Max-rank values: Bonus attack: 146,250; activation frequency: once every 5 seconds.

Dreamveil

Totem Skill In-game description: Once the base Totem skill of your opponent activates, it applies a sleep effect to all enemy Titans. Affected Titans fall asleep and remain inactive until they wake up. The effect is deactivated when the timer runs out or a Titan receives enough damage.

Totem Skill Explanation: Dreamveil is a powerful control skill because it can pause the entire enemy team after their Totem activation. It is best when your team can choose when to burst, because excessive damage can wake sleeping Titans early.

Max-rank values: Duration: 7.75 seconds; damage until woken up: 2,925,000; cooldown: 10s.

Primal Skills

Pulse of the Ancients

Totem Skill In-game description: Every time an allied Titan uses an ability, it is healed.

Totem Skill Explanation: Pulse of the Ancients is reliable sustain for teams that use abilities often. It helps stabilize mid-fight, though the heal may not always land on the Titan under the most pressure.

Max-rank values: Healing: 780,000; activation: no more than once per 8.5 seconds.

Primal Zeal

Totem Skill In-game description: When using a basic skill, the Titan has a chance to restore a part of its Energy.

Totem Skill Explanation: Primal Zeal is stronger after the update because the activation chance is high. It adds randomness, but that randomness can win fights by speeding up key Titan skills or Totem timing.

Max-rank values: Amount of Energy: 255; activation chance: 70%; cooldown: 5s.

Echo Aegis

Totem Skill In-game description: When a shield applied to a Titan on your team breaks, another shield will be applied to it for 5 sec.

Totem Skill Explanation: Echo Aegis is excellent on teams that already create shields. It is especially attractive with Earth-style protection and any build that wants to layer defensive windows.

Max-rank values: Shield durability: 23.5% of maximum Titan Health; activation chance: 120%; cooldown: 5s. Chance above 100% is converted into shield durability.

Triple Cycle

Totem Skill In-game description: If a Titan gets shield applied to it, it is healed. If a Titan gets healed, its attack becomes more powerful. If Titan's attack gets buffed, shield is applied to it. Only one effect is triggered per activation.

Totem Skill Explanation: Triple Cycle is the best all-around Primal Skill because it turns common support effects into a loop of healing, attack, and shielding. It fits many teams without needing manual timing.

Max-rank values: Healing: 39,000 Health per second; attack buff: 170,625; shield durability: 351,000; duration of any effect: 12 sec.; cooldown: 10s.

Call of Elements

Totem Skill In-game description: Titan's attack is increased proportionally to its missing Health.

Totem Skill Explanation: Call of Elements is a risky damage scaler. It can swing close fights when a Titan survives at low Health, but it is less reliable than shields, healing, or Energy acceleration.

Max-rank values: Attack buff: 14,625 for every missing 11% Health.

Two-Totem Team Tips

In two-Totem teams, one Totem will usually activate before the other based on your Titans' positions and how quickly each side gains Energy.

As a rule, place your most important active Elemental Skill on the Totem that activates first. Put passive or slower value skills on the second Totem, especially Primal Skills that keep working throughout the fight.

Do not stack duplicate Fusion Skills on both Totems unless you are comfortable with the waste. Copies of the same skill do not stack as separate bonuses; at best, the stronger effect wins or the duration is refreshed.

Totem Fusion FAQ

Q: Can I fuse Skills into a Totem below level 130?
A: Yes. The values in this guide use level 130, rank 6, and skill rank 6 for comparison, but Fusion can be used before that.

Q: Can Totems below Absolute Star use Fusion?
A: Yes. Even a lower-star Totem can benefit from Fusion if you have the required Catalysts.

Q: Which Elemental Skill should I chase first?
A: For general PvP, start with Ice Age, Dreamveil, Nova Flare, or Last Flash. For element-specific builds, Murmur of the Crags, Flame Dance, Dark Matter, or Main Sequence can be better.

Q: Which Primal Skill is the safest investment?
A: Triple Cycle is the safest overall pick. Primal Zeal is also very strong after the update because of its high Energy restore chance.

Q: Do two copies of the same Fusion Skill stack?
A: No. Duplicates do not stack as separate full bonuses. In most cases, you should diversify your Totem skills.

Q: Can I refuse a bad Fusion result?
A: Yes. You can reject the crafted result before accepting it and keep your previous Totem state.

Conclusion

Totem Fusion is now much deeper than the original six-skill version. The update added more control, more burst windows, more scaling, and more team-specific decisions, so the best answer is no longer one universal skill for every account.

For most PvP players, the safest Elemental priorities are Ice Age, Dreamveil, Nova Flare, and Last Flash. For Primal Skills, Triple Cycle is the strongest overall choice, with Primal Zeal and Echo Aegis close behind depending on your team.

Use the tier list for general priority, then use the element table to choose the skill that actually matches your Titans. That is where Totem Fusion starts becoming powerful instead of just expensive.
