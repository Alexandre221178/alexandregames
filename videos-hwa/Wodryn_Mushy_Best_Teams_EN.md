# Wodryn and Mushy — My PvP Team Choices

## Roteiro EN-US

885 palavras na narração. Foco: escolhas de times, uso em batalha e opinião do Alexandre sobre PvP.

The Wodryn and Mushy team I would build first is not the same team I would pick for every attack.

Hey guys, Alexandre here from Alexandre Games.

Today, I want to show you the teams I would use in PvP, why I like them, and which one I would choose for my own account.

Let's start with what these heroes bring.

Wodryn helps protect allies behind him. He can clear harmful effects, give critical hit chance, and help the team take less damage.

Mushy brings another body to the front line, but she is more than a tank. Give her time and healing, and her mushrooms can turn the fight around.

Healing helps her mushrooms become active. It also raises the damage of one of her skills.

That is why I like pairing them with healers. The goal is to stay alive long enough to fight back.

My first team is Guus, Byrna, Yasmine, Mushy, and Wodryn.

This is my favorite starting point for a player who already has these heroes built.

Yasmine gives me physical damage. Mushy adds magic damage and more pressure as the battle goes on. I am not asking Yasmine to do all the work alone.

Wodryn's protection also fits Yasmine because her critical hit chance helps her benefit from his damage reduction.

Guus and Byrna support the team with healing, which matters for Mushy's mushrooms as well.

Against Cleaver, Byrna, Kendle, Dorian, and Aidan, this team was very strong. It also gave me good results against Julius, Byrna, Crow, Guus, and Peech.

Those were different defenses with different Relic investment, so I would not treat them as equal tests.

What stood out was that Mushy could help finish fights even when Yasmine did not survive.

For attack, I would keep this team ready against those tested defenses. For defense, this is the first team from this group I would try.

That is my choice to test, though. My attack wins do not prove how well it will hold on defense.

My second team is Eva, Guus, Byrna, Mushy, and Wodryn.

I would choose this version if Eva is already one of my main heroes.

It had some excellent runs against both defenses, including before I unlocked Wodryn's Relic. But it also had weaker runs as I changed his build.

So I would use it for a planned attack rather than trust it against every opponent.

The main idea is still the same: keep the team alive, let Mushy's mushrooms join the fight, and give the damage dealers time to work.

If I already had Eva built and Yasmine was weak, I would try this before spending resources on Yasmine.

That is how I choose teams on my account. A good option with heroes I own can be more useful than a perfect team I cannot build yet.

If you enjoy seeing which teams I actually test, subscribe for more. Now, here is another option.

Miu, Crow, Byrna, Mushy, and Wodryn.

I like this team as another attack choice against the Cleaver and Kendle defense.

Miu also worked well with Mushy in my separate tests. Mushy benefited from her buff and brought more damage while still helping at the front.

This version also lets me use Guus elsewhere, which matters when I need several teams for Grand Arena.

But I would not put it above the Yasmine team for general use. My results against the Julius defense were less complete.

I would build this version if Miu and Crow were already strong on my account. I would not start both from zero just for this matchup.

Now, Mushy also gave me a strong option without Wodryn.

That team is Guus, Byrna, Tristan, Mushy, and Luther.

This is one I would keep for attacking Chaos.

In my Arena tests, it beat Chaos teams with golden Relics and more than one hundred fifty thousand extra power.

It also worked against a Chaos team with Electra.

Luther jumps into the enemy team, while Mushy helps hold our front line. This gives me a different way to approach the fight.

For Arena or a planned Guild War attack, I would consider this team when I see a similar defense. I would still check the exact enemy lineup.

There is one more Wodryn option worth testing if you already use Kayla and Aidan.

Aidan, Peech, Guus, Kayla, and Wodryn surprised me against the Julius defense. In one set, it won all ten fights.

If Kayla and Aidan are already part of your main team, try this before building Mushy from scratch.

So, what would I actually build?

For using Wodryn and Mushy together, my first choice is the Yasmine team. Eva is my alternative if she is already stronger on my account.

For attacking Chaos with Mushy, I would also keep the Luther and Tristan team ready.

If I were free to play or a low spender, I would build one of these teams well before starting another.

Which would you trust on defense: the Yasmine version or the Eva version? Tell me who you face most often.
