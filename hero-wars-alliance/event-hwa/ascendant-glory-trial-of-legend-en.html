Trial of Legends looks intimidating because its final milestones ask for enormous stockpiles: 750,000 Rune Stones, 250,000 Titan Potions, 100 Heroic Chests, and 200,000 Emeralds. I see those numbers differently. They are not a shopping list. They are a map showing exactly where each type of account should stop.

Unlike Rising Legend's daily-reset chains, these missions accumulate across the full event. That gives us control. Ascendant Glory begins on Thursday, so we can place every expensive resource on its strongest Guild VS day and still finish on Wednesday.

The Safest Strategy

Claim the natural milestones first: seven logins, normal Guild Activity, free Heroic Chests, regular Expeditions, and Titan Potions you already intended to use. Then evaluate Rune Stones and Emeralds. A player who stops at a profitable middle tier has played the event better than a player who reaches the final tier and regrets it for three months.

3. Value Exchange

There is no direct Guild VS mission for spending Emeralds. If you spend them, attach that spend to something useful: Energy for Wednesday, Outland Chests for the repeatable Spark chain, or a resource your account already intended to buy.

A Ceiling Is Not a Goal

The 200,000-Emerald tier exists for players already spending at that level. It is not efficient to create 200,000 Emeralds of demand just to receive 42,400 Ascension Crests.

4. Fellow in Arms

Guild Activity comes from spending Energy and from exchanging surplus equipment in the Guild Forge. Advanced players can convert excess gear for up to 2,000 Guild Activity per day, so use that daily limit instead of trying to repair the entire 45,000-point ladder at the end.

Best Energy Day: Wednesday

Energy contributes to Guild Activity on every event day, but Wednesday also awards 100 Guild VS points per 3 Energy. Exchange spare gear daily, then make Wednesday the largest Energy push.

5. Runic Might

This is the mission most players should decide before opening the event. Guus also has a glyph-level chain in Spark of Glory, so Thursday can advance three goals at once: Runic Might, Awakened Glyphs, and Guild VS.

Best Day: Thursday

Guild VS awards 100 points per 20 Rune Stones and 50,000 points per 100 Rune Spheres. Upgrade Guus first if you are pursuing Twilight Skin+, then stop when the next milestone becomes harmful to your wider roster.

6. Beyond Horizon

Start Valkyrie's Expeditions throughout the event. If Ascendant Glory becomes active before the Thursday server reset, delaying the previous day's available Expedition starts until the event opens can create extra progress.

Best Days: Friday and Tuesday

Each Expedition started earns 5,000 Guild VS points on these days. Friday is Event Day 2, so enter the event with Expedition slots ready instead of already occupied by long timers.

7. On a Titanic Scale

Titan Potions raise Titan levels. Spend them on Titans that improve Dungeon survival or your war lineup; an event is a good reason to perform a planned upgrade, not a good reason to scatter levels across Titans you never use.

Best Days: Saturday and Tuesday

Both days award 150 Guild VS points per 100 Titan Potions. Saturday gives an early push and Tuesday is the final matching day before the event closes.

8. Heroic Discovery

Free Heroic Chests make the first milestones accessible, but the upper half is an Emerald sink. Open saved or planned chests on a matching Guild VS day and ignore the emotional pull of an unfinished progress bar.

Best Days: Thursday and Monday

Each Heroic Chest awards 4,000 Guild VS points on both days. Thursday is available immediately; Monday is the second chance after four event days of free openings.

My Trial of Legends Priority Order

  1. Claim all seven logins and natural Guild Activity.
  2. Spend Rune Stones on Thursday only after deciding how far Guus should grow.
  3. Start Expeditions on Friday and Tuesday; use Titan Potions on Saturday and Tuesday.
  4. Open planned Heroic Chests on Thursday or Monday.
  5. Treat VIP and Emerald milestones as bonuses from planned spending, never as reasons to create spending.

Turn Trial Progress into More Crests

Every completed Trial of Legends milestone advances its meta-chain in Spark of Glory. When the Crests arrive, compare Guus, Twilight Skin+, and Relic Shards in the Champion's Gallery Shop Guide.
