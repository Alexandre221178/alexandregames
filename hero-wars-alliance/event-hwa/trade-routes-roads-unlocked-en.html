Roads Unlocked Final Checklist

  • Log in on all seven event days to complete Day After Day.
  • Remember that Generous Soul, Hidden Reserves, On a Titanic Scale, Beyond Horizon, Tower Trophy, and Payback Time reset daily.
  • Finish each resource-spending action before the daily reset; progress does not carry into the next daily ladder.
  • For Payback Time, start the boss rally yourself so the victory counts toward the Rally Leader objective.
