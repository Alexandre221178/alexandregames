6. Titanic Contribution

Objective: Collect Titanite in the Guild Dungeon every day before the event ends.

Guild VS Strategy

Strategy: This Guild VS task lasts 7 days, so plan your Dungeon runs instead of trying to finish everything on the first day.

  • New players usually collect around 75–300 Titanite per day, for a total of approximately 525–2,100 Titanite during the event.
  • Veteran players with stronger Titans can often exceed these values.
  • Don't sacrifice your long-term Dungeon progression just to reach one more reward tier. Preserving your Titans is usually more valuable than forcing extra Titanite today.

VIP Tip: Players with VIP Level 7+ can purchase 75 Titanite per day from the VIP Shop for 300 Emeralds. Over the full 7-day event, this provides an additional 525 Titanite, which can be enough to unlock higher reward milestones.

8. Payback Time

Objective: Defeat Realm bosses as the Rally Leader every day before the event ends. These are Realm rally bosses, not Hydra and not ordinary map monsters.

Guild VS Strategy

Target Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday, the Guild VS days with a matching Defeat Boss action. Use the Research Speedups on Wednesday or Saturday and spend the Rune Stones/Spheres on Thursday for additional Guild VS progress.

Roads Unlocked Final Checklist

  • Log in all seven days and never sacrifice sustainable Dungeon progress for one Titanite tier.
  • Spend Energy Wednesday, Skin Stones Tuesday/Friday, and Titan Potions Tuesday/Saturday.
  • Choose between Thursday's summon discount and Monday/Friday's exact Guild VS score according to your budget.
  • Run Realm boss rallies Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday; do not confuse them with Hydra attacks.
  • Check the live VIP Points counter before making a paid push, because that is the only Roads Unlocked ladder still missing verified thresholds from the supplied snapshot.
