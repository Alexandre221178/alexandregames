Prices, quantities, purchase limits, and the full shop inventory should be checked in the event shop. The featured lineup does not specify those values.

Byrna’s Crimson Grove Skin and Skin+

Crimson Grove Skin and Skin+ are available through special offers in the event shop. Review each offer separately to confirm the cost, what it unlocks, and whether you need to own the base skin before purchasing the upgrade.

If Byrna is part of the team you are building, compare the offer with your remaining upgrade budget. Include the resources needed to level the skin when deciding how much to spend. See the Byrna guide for her build.

Phobos and Dorian Relic Fragments

The featured relic offers are for Phobos and Dorian. Count the fragments you already own, then check how far a shop purchase moves you toward the next unlock or upgrade. Focus on the hero you use before splitting your budget across both.

Review the Relic guide and your current in-game relic requirements before buying.

Eva and Crow Artifacts

Eva and Crow Artifacts are also featured in this rotation. Check the exact artifact, fragment quantity, price, and purchase limit on each offer. Buy toward a planned upgrade for the hero you are actively developing.

Read the Eva guide and Crow guide to compare artifact priorities.

Choose Purchases Around Your Team

Start with a specific upgrade target. For a Byrna team, review the Crimson Grove offers first. For a team relying on Phobos or Dorian, compare the relic offers. If Eva or Crow needs artifact progress, choose the offer that completes a useful upgrade. Keep enough budget to finish the target you chose.

Check the remaining shop items against that plan before spending the balance. Availability alone is not a reason to buy every offer.

Plan Your Ascension Crest Budget

Use the updated Rising Legend, Trial of Legends, and Spark of Glory mission tables to estimate the Crests you can earn. Count milestones you can realistically complete and rewards you have already claimed.

Spark of Glory’s Mojo skins and Crow talisman are mission rewards. Keep those rewards separate from the Crimson Grove special offers and other purchases in Champion’s Gallery.
