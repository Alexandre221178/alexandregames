Relic Season Event - Complete Guide

The Relic Season Event is a limited-time event in Hero Wars Alliance that runs for 7 days, bringing exciting new content for your heroes. During this period, players have the opportunity to acquire powerful Legendary Ability Relics that enhance their heroes' abilities and combat effectiveness. This guide will help you understand the event mechanics, rewards, and strategies to maximize your gains during the Relic Season.

Event Overview

During the Relic Season, the main focus is on obtaining Legendary Ability Relics for your heroes. These relics are exclusive items that directly boost hero abilities and play a crucial role in late-game progression. The event includes a Battle Pass system, allowing players to unlock additional rewards and progression milestones. By completing event missions throughout the 7-day period, players can earn relics as rewards.

Battle Pass & Rewards

The Relic Season Event features a purchasable Battle Pass that provides enhanced rewards and exclusive benefits. Players who invest in the Battle Pass will unlock premium tiers with superior relic drops and additional materials. Completing daily and weekly missions directly contributes to your progress, allowing you to claim relics and other valuable items.

P2W Nature & F2P Considerations

It's important to note that the Relic Season Event is primarily a Pay-to-Win (P2W) event. Free-to-Play (F2P) players will receive limited rewards and minimal relic drops from event missions, with no guaranteed hero relics. This creates a significant advantage for paying players who can purchase the Battle Pass and access premium reward pools. However, F2P players shouldn't despair – Legendary Ability Relics will become available through future free events, though obtaining them this way may take considerable time and patience.

Conclusion

The Relic Season Event is a comprehensive 7-day event that offers players multiple ways to earn Relic Season Points. With a maximum of 3575 points available, completing all challenges can provide substantial rewards. However, keep in mind that this is primarily a P2W event, and free-to-play players should focus on achievable goals rather than chasing all milestones.

Whether you purchase the Battle Pass or not, participating in daily logins, expeditions, and tower activities will help you accumulate valuable points. Remember that the rewards obtained through this event can boost your heroes' power significantly, so prioritize this event if you're looking to strengthen your roster. Plan your time wisely and enjoy the rewards!
