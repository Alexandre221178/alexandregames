Unstable Equilibrium is Ancient Awakening's week-long cumulative branch. It contains 91 reward stages, but the main event's meta-chain ends at 80, letting you skip eleven of the most expensive stages while still claiming the final meta reward.

Cumulative, Not Daily

Every Unstable Equilibrium ladder keeps progress through Sunday. Do not spend an important resource early merely to claim a small stage: wait for the matching Guild VS day, use free attempts as they refresh, and decide which eleven high-cost stages you will skip.

Alexandre's 80-Quest Strategy

  1. Secure all seven login stages.
  2. Use planned research speed-ups on Wednesday.
  3. Use Arena and Grand Arena free attempts throughout the week.
  4. Open saved Elemental Spheres Monday or Friday and spend saved Rune Stones Saturday.
  5. Count completed stages before spending Emeralds or chasing extreme VIP, Sphere, and Rune Stone tiers.
  6. Skip the eleven stages with the worst cost for your account.

Unstable Equilibrium FAQ

When should I open Elemental Spheres?

Monday and Friday both award 1,200 Guild VS points per opening. Choose the event milestone that fits your stockpile.

When should I use extra Arena attempts?

Arena and Grand Arena both score Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Use free attempts daily and plan extra attempts for those matching days. Rune Stones score Saturday.

Which stages are best to skip?

That depends on your inventory, but the final VIP, Emerald, Elemental Sphere, and Rune Stone tiers are usually the most expensive candidates. Count stages before spending.
