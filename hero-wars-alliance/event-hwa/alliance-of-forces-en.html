Rune Sphere is the essential resource used to upgrade Class skills in Hero Wars Alliance. The Alliance of Forces event is one of the most important opportunities to collect Rune Spheres because it combines daily login rewards, spending milestones, Energy tasks, and Tower progression in a single event cycle.

This event becomes especially valuable once you start working on Glyph Class Skills for your strongest heroes. If you want to strengthen Tanks, Warriors, Marksmen, Mages, Healers, Support, or Control heroes through Class Glyph progression, Alliance of Forces is one of the best events to prepare for in advance.

Strategic Guide for Alliance of Forces

1. Understanding Glyph Class Skills

Glyph Class Skills become available when heroes of the same class reach level 40 in all of their current Glyphs. These class-based bonuses apply to categories such as Tank, Warrior, Marksman, Mage, Healer, Support, and Control. Once unlocked, the Class skill strengthens every hero that belongs to that class, making Rune Spheres a key long-term progression resource.

2. Why Rune Spheres Matter

Unlike Rune Stones, which are used for standard Glyph upgrades, Rune Spheres are tied to Class Glyph progression. That makes Alliance of Forces one of the most important events for accounts that are already moving beyond basic Glyph development and into class-wide upgrades for their strongest teams.

3. Best Way to Prepare for the Event

The most efficient preparation is to save actions that overlap with the event objectives. Holding back some Tower progress, planning Energy usage, and deciding in advance whether you want to spend Rune Spheres during the event can make a big difference. The better your preparation, the easier it is to collect every available milestone.

4. Focus on Your Main Team First

Rune Spheres are difficult to obtain in large quantities, so they should usually be invested into the classes that matter most for your account. If your core Arena and Guild War teams rely heavily on Tanks and Marksmen, for example, improving those classes first will often bring more practical value than spreading resources across every class equally.

5. Combine Daily Progress with Milestone Planning

Alliance of Forces rewards players who combine routine activity with smart planning. Daily login rewards are simple, but the larger value comes from aligning Energy spending, Tower chests, and Rune Sphere investment with the milestone tables. Thinking ahead lets you turn normal account progress into a much stronger event result.

Analysis of Class Glyph Progression: Astaroth Example

Astaroth is a good example of why Rune Spheres matter so much in Hero Wars Alliance. As a Tank hero, he contributes to the Tank Class Glyph once his personal Glyph progression reaches the required point. After all of his current Glyphs are fully developed to the needed stage, the Tank Class skill becomes available and begins to benefit every Tank hero tied to that class.

From there, Rune Spheres become the resource that pushes class progression further. Each investment helps strengthen the shared Class Glyph, which improves the passive value that Tank heroes receive in battle. This is why Alliance of Forces is so valuable: it gives players a focused way to collect the resource needed for this higher level of progression.

For players building durable frontlines, the impact can be significant. Stronger Tank Class progression can improve the stability of core heroes used in Arena, Grand Arena, Guild War, and PvE modes. In practice, that means Rune Spheres earned during Alliance of Forces can translate directly into better survivability for one of the most important roles in the game.

Conclusion

Alliance of Forces is one of the best events in Hero Wars Alliance for players who want to strengthen Class Glyph progression and collect more Rune Spheres efficiently. By preparing your Tower timing, planning your Energy use, and deciding in advance how many Rune Spheres you want to invest during the event, you can get much more value from every day of the event and accelerate the growth of your most important hero classes.
