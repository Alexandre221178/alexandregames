Daily Reset Warning

Only Day After Day is cumulative. The VIP, Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Summoning Circle, Tower, Artifact Chest, and Realm boss ladders reset daily. The main Ancient Awakening meta therefore counts up to 391 possible stage completions across the week.

A best day is when you should make the largest push, not permission to ignore a worthwhile daily-reset stage on other days.

Best Daily Priority

  1. Claim the easy login and low-cost stages.
  2. Earn normal Guild Activity and use the Guild Equipment Forge only for gear you need.
  3. Take all free Tower opportunities.
  4. Make resource-heavy pushes on the exact Guild VS day.
  5. Stop before a milestone costs more than its rewards are worth.

Firstborn Force FAQ

Should I save every resource for its best day?

No. These ladders reset daily. Complete affordable stages every day, then make the biggest push on the matching Guild VS day.

Does Monday's Summoning Sphere task match Summoning Titans?

No. Firstborn Force requires summons in the Summoning Circle. The exact Guild VS match is Friday.

Do Hydra battles count for Payback Time?

No. Payback Time requires Realm boss defeats as Raid Leader; Guild VS matches that boss action on Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday.
