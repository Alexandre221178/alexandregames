Power up your Heroes with Skin Stones, send Expeditions, and spend Energy to collect Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, random red gear recipe fragments, and other valuable resources during Rune Storm.

Realm players can push even further by defeating rally bosses, while Dungeon progress adds another path through Titanite rewards. If you plan well, Rune Storm overlaps nicely with the Season event, letting you progress both at the same time instead of wasting Energy and Emeralds on separate grinds.

Important Realm Requirement Before You Start

  • You must unlock Realm Mode at player level 80 to participate in the Realm-based Payback Time boss quests.
  • Inside Realm, coordinate with your guild for boss rallies and do not confuse rally bosses with regular map monsters.
  • If your Realm is still underdeveloped, read the Realm Mode Guide first, because several Rune Storm missions depend on it.

Best Rune Storm Strategy Before the Event Starts

  • Save Emeralds for Energy refills if possible. That lets you progress Wise Exchange and Energy Burst at the same time.
  • Prepare Skin Stones in advance. Price of Honor is one of the best moments to level hero skins because you also gain Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, and red fragment progress.
  • Queue Valkyrie Expeditions daily. Beyond Horizon resets every day, so missed attempts are gone forever.
  • Push Titanite daily. Titan Forge asks for up to 800 Titanite during the event, and the VIP shop can help with 75 Titanite per day for 300 Emeralds if needed.
  • Do not confuse monsters with bosses. Payback Time counts Realm bosses defeated through guild rallies, not regular map monsters.

Strategy: If reaching 800 Titanite over the 3-day event is difficult, remember that the VIP shop can sell 75 Titanite per day for 300 Emeralds. Beginners often collect around 75 to 150 Titanite per day, while active Dungeon pushes can reach 200 to 300 or more.

Final Advice for Rune Storm

Rune Storm looks expensive at first, but the event becomes much more manageable when you stack goals. Spend Emeralds mostly on Energy, use Skin Stones only when the account value is real, and treat Realm preparation as part of the event instead of a separate chore.

Low spenders can get close to half of a 300-fragment red item target across this event and the next cycle. That is normal. Hero Wars rewards patience, so build your heroes for the long term instead of chasing every reward tier in one weekend.
