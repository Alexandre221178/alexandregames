Rising Legend is where Ascendant Glory quietly tests your discipline. Five chains reset every day, so waiting for the perfect Guild VS overlap can save resources in one place and lose an entire day's milestones in another. The winning plan is not “save everything.” It is complete affordable daily tiers, then make the largest push on the matching Guild VS day.

Read This Before Spending

Daily reset: Generous Soul, Power Core, Summoning Titans, Hidden Reserves, and Payback Time. Week-long progress: Day After Day, Taming Hydra, and Trial of Strength. If a daily chain is affordable, claim its realistic tiers before reset; only the size of the push should wait for its best Guild VS day.

2. Generous Soul

This VIP ladder resets daily. Guild VS rewards VIP Points on every active day, but that does not make a purchase automatically valuable. Spend only when the purchase itself was already part of your plan.

My Guild VS Strategy

Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday all score 100 Guild VS points per VIP Point. Sunday scores nothing, so it is the worst day for a flexible purchase.

3. Power Core

Chaos Cores are used when awakening and evolving Hero artifacts. They disappear quickly, and Ascendant Glory has another artifact chain waiting in Spark of Glory. This is why I would not spend them casually on Thursday just to clear a notification.

Best Day: Saturday

Guild VS awards 3,000 points per Chaos Core on Saturday. Complete only a low daily tier before then if the reward matters, then make Saturday your largest Power Core day.

This daily ladder counts summons completed in the Summoning Circle. Monday's Guild VS task to open a Summoning Sphere is not the same wording and should not be treated as an exact match.

Best Day: Friday

5. Hidden Reserves

Fairy Dust levels Titan artifacts and is mainly earned through Hydra activity and Elemental Spheres. It has two exact Guild VS overlaps during this event, which makes patience especially rewarding.

Best Days: Friday and Wednesday

Guild VS awards 50 points per 25 Fairy Dust on both days. Friday is useful for early progress; Wednesday is the final chance to finish the ladder before Ascendant Glory closes.

6. Taming Hydra

This cumulative chain counts Hydra battles or donating three Horns of Summoning to the Fairies. There is no exact Hydra task in Guild VS, so follow your guild's Hydra schedule instead of forcing a false overlap with Realm bosses.

Alexandre's Tip

Advanced players can check the Titan Soul Shop when they need more Horns of Summoning, but exchange-currency value still matters. A late event tier is not worth weakening the Titan plan that earns those resources in the first place.

7. Trial of Strength

Arena fights are cumulative, so this is one of the easiest chains to synchronize. Five of the seven Ascendant Glory days include the same Arena action in Guild VS.

Best Days: Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Monday, Tuesday

Each Arena fight scores 6,000 Guild VS points on those days. Sunday and Wednesday do not have the matching task, so use them only if you still need the event milestone or your daily ranking battles.

8. Payback Time

This daily chain requires defeating Realm bosses as a Rally Leader. Do not confuse it with Hydra battles or ordinary map monsters.

Best Days: Saturday, Monday, Tuesday

Guild VS awards 850 points per boss on these days. Saturday is the first strong overlap, while Monday and Tuesday give two more chances before the event ends.

My Rising Legend Priority Order

  1. Never miss Day After Day.
  2. Use Friday for the largest Summoning Circle and Fairy Dust push.
  3. Use Saturday for Chaos Cores and Realm bosses.
  4. Use Monday and Tuesday for more Realm bosses, then Wednesday for the final Fairy Dust push.
  5. Complete affordable low daily tiers on off-days, but do not burn rare resources merely because progress resets.

Where the Completed Missions Go Next

Every Rising Legend milestone also advances the large meta-chain inside Spark of Glory. After collecting the Crests, use the Champion's Gallery Shop Guide before spending them.
