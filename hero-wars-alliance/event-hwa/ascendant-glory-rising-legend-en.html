Where Completed Missions Go Next

Every claimed Rising Legend milestone advances the Rising Legend mission-count ladder in Spark of Glory. Rising Legend offers up to 301 completions, while that meta ladder ends at 250, so you can miss 51 daily milestones and still finish it.
