Rising Legend contains eight mission chains: six reset daily, while Day After Day and Taming Hydra accumulate over the seven-day event. Complete affordable milestones before each server reset: unfinished daily progress disappears. Match daily actions and saved rewards to Guild Versus when possible.

Daily resets take priority: six chains reset every day. Day After Day and Taming Hydra accumulate across the event. Milestone requirements are cumulative within each chain; do not add the thresholds together. Saving all daily actions for one Guild Versus day will not recover missed daily rewards.

These are base Guild Versus points. Direct mission totals apply to one daily chain; reward claims and reward use are separate actions. Chaos Cores and Fairy Dust have no direct matching task. Sunday has no active Guild Versus tasks, but daily Rising Legend missions still reset.

Use saved reward resources on matching days when practical. The equipment recipes can help complete an item; equipping that item can score Thursday according to its rarity. Opening artifact material chests is not the Guild Versus Artifact Chest action. Never delay a daily reward claim if doing so would lose it at reset.

1. Day After Day

Event-wide mission: progress accumulates during the event; complete this chain once.

Log in once on each of the seven event days. Login itself has no Guild Versus match; the Hero Soul Stones from the first milestone can score on Tuesday when received, if claiming then does not block progress.

6. Taming Hydra

Event-wide mission: progress accumulates during the event; complete this chain once.

Complete up to 21 Hydra battles or give Horns of Summoning to the Fairies during the event, following the in-game counter. Use available daily attacks across the seven days. This chain has no daily reset and no direct Guild Versus match; Hydra is not the separate Boss task.
