📿 Shards of the Past – Complete Event Guide

Shards of the Past is a 3-day special event in Hero Wars Alliance that runs alongside the Keepers' Shop (Talisman Shop), where you can spend Talisman Coins to purchase powerful Talismans. The event features 8 quest tracks covering daily logins, Emerald spending, Grand Arena battles, Hydra raids, Rune Stone spending, Guild Activity Points, and Realm monster hunts. The featured reward is a Talisman unlocked by spending 6,000 Emeralds in the Wise Exchange quest.

⚠️ Important: To participate in the Shards of the Past event, you need to unlock Realm Mode at level 80 and be in a guild. Check out our Realm Mode Guide to get started.

Event Highlights

  • Talisman – Featured reward: earn 1 Talisman by spending 6,000 Emeralds in the Wise Exchange quest
  • Talisman Coins – Earned through multiple quests; spend them in the Keepers' Shop for more Talismans
  • Relic Shards – Available across multiple quests
  • Chaos Cores – Earned by spending Emeralds, gaining Guild Activity Points, and fighting Realm monsters
  • Rune Spheres – Available in Grand Arena, Hydra, United Front, and Keepers of the Border quests

🏪 Keepers' Shop – Talisman Shop Strategy

The Keepers' Shop runs alongside this event and lets you purchase Talismans using Talisman Coins. However, Talisman Coins earned through event quests alone are quite limited. The best way to earn more is by opening Reward Boxes:

  • 1x Reward Box = 200 Emeralds
  • 10x Reward Box = 1,800 Emeralds
  • Reward Boxes drop: Skin Stones, Bronze Chests, Gold, and Talisman Coins

💡 Tip: Opening 3×10 Reward Boxes costs 5,400 Emeralds. Combined with daily Emerald spending in event quests, you can easily reach the 6,000 Emerald threshold to receive the Talisman from Wise Exchange — all within 3 event days. Before investing in Talismans, check our Talisman Guide to see which ones are truly worth it for your team.

🔑 Best Emerald spending strategy: Use Emeralds in Outland 3 (50+ tries) or open 1×10 (1,800/day), use Tower tips from Alexandre Games and buy extra Energy there — this way you earn event Season Pass rewards too.

🛒 The Keepers' Shop – Your Talisman Store

The Keepers' Shop runs alongside Shards of the Past and lets you spend Talisman Coins to purchase Talismans directly — including VIP talismans from previous events. Even if you don't spend Emeralds, consistently completing the event quests will build up your coin balance over time.

Talisman prices in the shop vary by rarity and power:

  • 3,000 coins – For basic or older talismans
  • 8,000 coins – For stronger or faction-specific talismans
  • 60,000 coins – For meta-defining, premium talismans

The shop selection rotates with each event — you may not see the same talismans again for months. Check our Talisman Guide before spending coins to make sure you're buying the ones that truly benefit your team.

🎁 What's Inside the Reward Box?

Completing all Shards of the Past quests gives you a solid amount of Talisman Coins, but usually not enough to afford the premium talismans in the Keepers' Shop. That's where the Reward Box comes in — open boxes with Emeralds for a chance at bonus Talisman Coins and other valuable items.

Here's what you can get from the Reward Box:

  • Talisman Coin x100 – 7.27%
  • Gold x100,000 – 7.27%
  • Spark of Power x200 – 6.69%
  • Artifact Coin x200 – 6.69%
  • Skin Stones (Intelligence, Strength, Agility) x100 – 5.23%
  • Artifact Chests (Essence, Scroll, Metal) – 3.49%
  • Talisman Coin x200 or x500 – 2.91%
  • Rune Stone x300 – 2.91%
  • Talisman Coin x2,000 – 0.29% (guaranteed after 173 tries)
  • Emeralds x200 – 0.29% (guaranteed after 173 tries)

Cost:

  • 1 box – 200 Emeralds
  • 10 boxes – 1,800 Emeralds (10% discount)

Opening 3×10 boxes costs 5,400 Emeralds. Combined with your normal Emerald spending during the event, you'll reach the 6,000 Emerald threshold in the Wise Exchange quest and unlock the featured Talisman. If you're going for high-volume openings, the guaranteed rewards kick in on the 173rd box.

📦 Key Items to Collect

  • Meta Cube: Used to reroll Talisman stats — essential for optimizing your hero builds.
  • Talisman Coin: Your currency for the Keepers' Shop. Earned through quests, Reward Boxes, and event milestones.
  • Mastery Crystal: Required to level up your Talismans after equipping them.

Efficiently managing your Emeralds and completing as many quests as possible will determine how much value you get out of Shards of the Past.

🎉 Final Thoughts: Is It Worth It?

Shards of the Past is one of the best events for Realm players to stock up on Talisman Coins while also earning Relic Shards, Chaos Cores, Rune Spheres, and large resource packs. Even free-to-play players can walk away with a Talisman by hitting the 6,000 Emerald milestone — which is very achievable if you plan your spending across the 3 event days.

For free-to-play players: log in every day, complete all 8 quests, and target the 6k Emerald milestone in Wise Exchange. Your Talisman Coins from United Front, Trial of Strength, and Keepers of the Border will add up for future shop purchases.

For competitive and spender players: push for higher Emerald milestones in Wise Exchange, open Reward Boxes in batches of 10, and use VIP Point rewards from Mark of Honor for maximum speedup gains. The higher Rune Stone tiers in Price of Power also pay off well in Mastery Crystals and Meta Cubes.

Whichever path you choose — plan ahead, use the Keepers' Shop wisely, and happy hunting!
