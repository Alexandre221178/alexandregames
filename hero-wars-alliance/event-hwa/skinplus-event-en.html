The Skin+ event in Hero Wars Alliance is a thrilling opportunity for players to enhance their heroes like never before. Over the course of 3 days, you'll gain access to exclusive skins and the highly coveted Skin+ which offers double the stat bonus of a regular skin!

To unlock a Skin+, you must first purchase the featured skin using real money. Only then will the Skin+ version be made available in the event shop, where you can use event coins to buy it. This limited-time event is your chance to make your strongest heroes even more powerful.

What Is the Skin+ System and Why Does It Matter?

Skin+ is an upgraded version of normal skins, offering 2x the stats of standard ones. These bonuses can dramatically improve your heroes' performance in battle, making them more competitive in both PvP and PvE modes.

But remember Skin+ isn’t free. It requires a two-step process: purchase the regular event skin with real money, and then spend your event coins to get the unlocked Skin+ version in the event shop.

Why You Should Not Wait to Get the Skin+ Later

I get this question a lot — “Alexandre, can’t I just wait and get the Skin+ later for free?” — and technically yes… but the long-term reality is much slower and more frustrating than most players expect. Let me break it down clearly so you can decide what makes the most sense for your account.

The base skin is not too bad to wait for. After a few months it usually starts appearing in free Outland Chests, and later it becomes available through Skin Certificates. That is a normal free-to-play timeline.

The Skin+ is completely different. Once the event ends, the most reliable way to obtain a random Skin+ is through the Heroic Chest — and this is where the grind becomes painful:

  • You need to open 400 Heroic Chests to guarantee a random Skin+ opportunity — and it may not even be the skin+ you need.
  • The main reward is Cleaver at 6 stars. If you already maxed him, you receive Cleaver Soul Stones instead.
  • You get 1 free chest daily, plus 1 more from watching an ad.
  • Even if you open both every day without missing, reaching 400 takes at least 200 days.
  • Miss a day, forget a timer, or get unlucky — and the wait stretches even further.
  • Realistically, for most players this can easily become many months or over a year for a random Skin+.

That is why Skin+ events are usually the best opportunity. You get the exact hero you want, immediately, with no RNG and no waiting.

Warning: if you wait for the Skin+ to become free, you may end up waiting a very long time — and that is not even guaranteed to give you the skin you want.

Complete Event Missions to Earn Skin+ Coins

During the Skin+ event in Hero Wars Alliance, you’ll find a variety of missions that reward you with Charisma Coin. These coins can be used in the event shop to purchase the powerful Skin+ or other valuable items like skin stones, season points, and more. Don’t miss the chance to maximize your rewards every task brings you closer to unlocking exclusive content!
