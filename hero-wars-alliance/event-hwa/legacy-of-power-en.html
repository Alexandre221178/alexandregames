Legacy of Power Event Overview

Legacy of Power has three login milestones. Check the current in-game dates; the Monday-to-Wednesday plan below applies when the event overlaps those days. Its eight quest chains cover daily logins, VIP Points, Emeralds, Summoning Circle summons, Titan Skin Stones, Hydra battles, Arena fights, and Mastery Crystals.

The event rewards Adventure Coins and Energy, Titan Potions, Meta Cubes, Elemental Spheres, Hero and Titan Soul Stones, Fairy Dust, artifact resources, Skin Stone Chests, Emerald Wrath and Stormfang recipes, Rune Spheres, and more.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Monday: open Summoning Spheres, spend Titan Skin Stones, and use Titan Potions. Elemental Sphere rewards also score when opened.
  • Tuesday: fight in the Arena and receive Hero Soul Stone rewards when possible.
  • Wednesday: use earned Artifact Chest Keys to open Artifact Chests, and finish intended event milestones before the deadline.
  • Other weekdays: Mastery Crystals and equipment score Thursday, Elemental Spheres and Artifact Chests score Friday, and Rune resources score Saturday. Use saved rewards later if the event has already ended.

Alexandre's Plan for a Monday-to-Wednesday Event

Monday — Summons and Titan Upgrades

Use saved Summoning Spheres and Titan Skin Stones while both events are active. At the final event milestones, 250 sphere openings give 375,000 Guild Versus points and 35,000 Titan Skin Stones give 23,330. Use Titan Potions at 150 points per 100 and open earned Elemental Spheres at 1,200 points each. Titan Power increases can also score separately.

Tuesday — Arena and Hero Soul Stones

Prioritize available Arena fights today: each gives 6,000 Guild Versus points. Claim Hero Soul Stone rewards today when possible, at 500 points each. Only delay claims if the game allows it without losing rewards or blocking a repeatable walkthrough. Hero Skin Stones obtained from reward chests can also score when spent today.

Wednesday — Artifact Chests and Event Completion

Use earned Artifact Chest Keys today: opening 50 chests gives 37,500 Guild Versus points. Complete intended Legacy of Power milestones before the event closes, including Mastery Crystals if Thursday falls outside the event. Keep earned equipment recipes for Thursday upgrades when practical, and save Rune resources for Saturday. Fairy Dust has no direct Guild Versus match.

Match the current event dates

Titan Skin Stones now match Monday; Mastery Crystals match Thursday. Do not wait until Thursday if the event ends Wednesday. Hydra battles and giving Horns to the Fairies have no direct Guild Versus task and are not the separate Boss mission. Use daily Hydra and Arena attempts throughout the event when needed to reach your intended milestones.

6. Taming Hydra — Battle Any Hydra

Repeatable after a complete walkthrough. Milestones are now 3, 6, 9, and 12. Advance by battling any Hydra or giving Horns of Summoning to the Fairies, following the in-game counter. There is no direct Hydra or Horn task in Guild Versus; do not equate Hydra battles with its separate Boss task.

Advanced Strategy: Golden Horns + Monday Guild Versus

For veteran accounts, Monday is the best day to turn accumulated Titan resources into progress. A Golden Horn of Summoning costs 15,000 Titan Potions in the Titan Soul Shop. If the purchase advances Monday's Use Titan Potions counter, 15,000 potions would yield 22,500 Guild Versus points; check the counter before repeating purchases. Giving the Horn to the Fairies advances Taming Hydra according to its in-game counter.

  • Wait for Monday's task to become active: Guild Versus awards 150 points for every 100 Titan Potions used.
  • Buy Golden Horns of Summoning: each purchase uses 15,000 Titan Potions and creates a second route through the repeatable Taming Hydra chain.
  • Use the Titan Soul Shop economy: advanced players can also buy Titan Potions with accumulated Titan Soul Coins.
  • Keep running the Dungeon daily: rebuild your Titan Potion and Titan Soul Coin reserves after the event and prepare for the next month's rotation.

My example: I entered this event with more than 1 million Titan Potions and about 3 million Titan Soul Coins. This strategy finally gives those veteran reserves a meaningful use.

Alexandre's opinion: this is a great move by the developers. It gives long-time players a new reason to keep completing the Dungeon every day instead of letting Titan resources accumulate without a clear purpose.

Final Recommendation

For a Monday-to-Wednesday event, prioritize Summoning Spheres and Titan Skin Stones on Monday, Arena and Hero Soul Stone claims on Tuesday, and earned Artifact Chest Keys on Wednesday. Keep all intended event progress within the event deadline. Repeating Wise Exchange or Taming Hydra adds another set of rewards; choose milestones that fit your available resources.
