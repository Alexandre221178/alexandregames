Legacy of Power Event Overview

Legacy of Power is a three-day Hero Wars Alliance event that begins on Monday and ends on Wednesday, alongside Seer's Game and Titan Rift. Its eight quest chains cover daily logins, VIP Points, Emeralds, Summoning Circle summons, Titan Skin Stones, Hydra battles, Arena fights, and Mastery Crystals.

The event rewards Adventure Coins and Energy, Titan Potions, Meta Cubes, Elemental Spheres, Hero and Titan Soul Stones, Fairy Dust, artifact resources, Skin Stone Chests, Red equipment fragments, and more.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Monday: open saved Summoning Spheres, fight in the Arena, and open any Elemental Spheres you earn.
  • Tuesday: finish remaining Arena fights and use Titan Potions earned from the event.
  • Wednesday: claim saved Hero Soul Stone rewards, spend earned Fairy Dust, and equip a completed Red item if possible.

Alexandre's Three-Day Plan

Monday — Summoning Sphere Day

Wait until Legacy of Power and Guild Versus are both live, then use saved Summoning Spheres. The 250-summon maximum can produce 375,000 Guild Versus points while completing Power Unleashed. Do Monday's Arena fights and open Elemental Spheres obtained early enough from the event. VIP Points also score today, but a planned purchase can be moved to Tuesday or Wednesday if your guild needs a later daily chest.

Tuesday — Arena and Titan Potion Day

Finish as many of the 30 Arena fights as your normal attempts allow; Arena gives the same 6,000 points per fight on Monday and Tuesday. Use Titan Potions already claimed from Legacy of Power on Tuesday, because Guild Versus awards 150 points per 100 potions used. Do not buy unnecessary Arena attempts only to force the final event milestone.

Wednesday — Save and Convert Rewards

If the game safely lets you leave quest rewards unclaimed, collect Hero Soul Stones on Wednesday for 500 points each. Spend the Fairy Dust earned during the event at 50 points per 25 Dust. Red equipment fragments can be especially valuable if they complete an item: equipping one Red item on Wednesday is worth 1,250,000 points.

No direct Guild Versus match during these three days

Titan Skin Stones match Guild Versus on Thursday and Mastery Crystals match on Saturday, after Legacy of Power has ended. Complete Price of Power and Cycle of Success before Wednesday's deadline, but do not describe them as double progress. Hydra battles are also not treated as the JSON's “Defeat Boss” task because the data does not confirm that equivalence.

Final Recommendation

The strongest free overlap is Monday's Summoning Circle plus Arena fights on Monday and Tuesday. Treat VIP and Emerald milestones as optional spending, not goals that must be completed. On Wednesday, convert the rewards you already earned into Guild Versus points, and make sure every Legacy of Power quest you want is complete before the event closes.
