🌌 Abyss Splendor – Complete Event Guide

Abyss Splendor is a special event in Hero Wars Alliance where players explore the Outland and the Tower to collect Titanite and earn valuable rewards including Skin Stone Chests and Rune Spheres. Realm players benefit from additional resources and speedups by training troops and gathering food.

Event Highlights

  • Skin Stone Chests – Both Small and Large variants available across all quests
  • Rune Spheres – Obtainable through Tower, Energy, and Titan Forge quests
  • 🏰 Army Training and Realm Resources – Food, Wood, Stone, Iron, and various Speedups

1. Dominion Calls

Objective: Log in during the event to earn Skin Stone Chests, Season Points, and Army Training Manuals.

Strategy: Consistency is key. Log in each of the 3 event days to collect all rewards. Even a brief login counts.

You can complete these quests during the 3 days of the event.
