Caravan Hall is the Trade Routes shop for the October 26–November 1, 2026 rotation. Spend Spooky Coins on Yasmine Relic Shards, 11 Talismans, Realm resources, and hero equipment.

This rotation’s shop

The featured Relic belongs to Yasmine. Every listed Talisman costs 60,000 Spooky Coins, is limited to one purchase, and requires team level 120. Choose a permanent upgrade for a hero you actively use before buying ordinary resources.

Plan a 50-shard target

Six purchases at 7,500 coins give 30 shards for 45,000 coins. Two purchases at 12,500 add 10 shards for 25,000 coins. Two purchases at 17,500 add the final 10 for 35,000 coins. That is 50 shards for 105,000 Spooky Coins.

The marginal price rises from 1,500 to 2,500 and then 3,500 coins per shard. Check how many shards you already own and stop when you reach your chosen upgrade. Compare the next Relic purchase with a Talisman for your main team.

Shop Talismans and mission rewards differ

Grand Caravan awards Oya’s Talisman of Warrioress at 25,000 VIP Points and Mojo’s Talisman of Shaman at 100,000 Emeralds. The Mojo item sold here is Talisman of Spirits, with Intelligence and Magic Attack. Check the item name before planning your spending.

Plan your Spooky Coins

Review your available mission rewards before committing to a shop target. Start with the milestones you can complete using resources already saved on your account.

  • Roads Unlocked: daily and event-wide mission rewards.
  • Grand Caravan: spending, chest, Arena, and troop-loss milestones.
  • Bountiful Roads: repeatable speed-up rewards and linked quest counters.

End-of-event currency conversion

The listed conversion exchanges 700 Spooky Coins for one Nature Chest. It is scheduled for November 2, 2026 at 23:00 (São Paulo), equivalent to November 3 at 02:00 UTC. Make your intended shop purchases before the in-game shop countdown ends.

Caravan Hall buying checklist

  1. Choose a relevant Talisman or a specific Yasmine Relic upgrade.
  2. Count your existing shards and available Spooky Coins.
  3. Use the cheaper Relic offers before the higher-priced tier.
  4. Confirm team level 120 before budgeting for a Talisman.
  5. Buy ordinary resources and equipment after reviewing your permanent-upgrade target.
