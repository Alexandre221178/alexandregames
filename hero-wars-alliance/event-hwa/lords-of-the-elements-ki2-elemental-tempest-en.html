Lords of the Elements: Elemental Tempest Event Overview

Elemental Tempest runs from Thursday through Sunday. Its eight chains cover daily logins, VIP Points, Emeralds, Titanite, Elemental Spheres, Artifact Coins, Arena fights, and Grand Arena fights.

Saturday matches spending claimed Rune rewards; Arena and Grand Arena score Thursday and Saturday. Elemental Spheres score Friday, while VIP Points score Thursday through Saturday. Sunday is for event-only completion.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Thursday: Use claimed Mastery Crystals and equip useful completed items. Arena and Grand Arena both award 6,000 points per fight.
  • Friday: Open planned Elemental Spheres for 1,200 points each. Continue daily event progress; Arena does not score Guild Versus today.
  • Saturday: Spend claimed Rune Stone rewards in batches of 20 for 100 points and Rune Spheres in batches of 100 for 50,000 points. Arena and Grand Arena also score today.
  • Sunday: Complete the fourth login, remaining Titanite and affordable event milestones. Guild Versus is inactive.

Titanite + Wise Exchange Strategy for VIP 7 Players

The 800-Titanite target averages 200 Titanite per day. Players with VIP 7 can buy 75 Titanite in the VIP Shop for 300 Emeralds each day.

  • Four purchases provide 300 Titanite and spend 1,200 Emeralds.
  • The purchases advance both Titanic Contribution and Wise Exchange.
  • You still need 500 Titanite from the Guild Dungeon to reach 800.
  • The 1,200 Emerald spend clears the 500-Emerald Wise Exchange milestone but remains 300 short of the 1,500 milestone.

Buy only when the Dungeon progress and rewards justify the Emerald cost. Strong Titan accounts should prioritize the free Dungeon route.

Important limits

Opening 500 Elemental Spheres, fighting 60 times in each Arena mode, spending 60,000 Artifact Coins, and earning 20,000 VIP Points are premium-scale goals. Stop at the last milestone that improves your account efficiently; completing every row is not required for a successful event.
