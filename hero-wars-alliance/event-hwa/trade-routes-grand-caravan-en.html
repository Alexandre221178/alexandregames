Grand Caravan Final Priority

  1. Claim all seven logins.
  2. Use Emeralds on Energy or discounts that advance a second mission.
  3. Spend Skin Stones and Artifact Coins only on core heroes.
  4. Use saved Artifact Chest keys before paying Emeralds.
  5. Complete the 30-fight Arena cycle and repeat only if your daily attempts allow it.
  6. Treat the 15,000-VIP and 100,000-Emerald Talismans as premium targets, not requirements.
