Grand Caravan runs from October 26 through November 1, 2026. Its seven mission chains reward logins, VIP Points, Emerald spending, Artifact Coins, Artifact Chests, Arena fights, and troop losses. Progress is event-wide: the chains do not reset daily or repeat after completion.

1. Day After Day

Objective: Log in on all seven event days to collect Adventure Energy and Season Points.

Guild VS Strategy

Log in every day to keep all seven rewards. Logging in itself has no direct Guild Versus scoring task. The reward is Adventure Energy, not the normal Energy used by Wednesday?s Energy-spending task. Plan Realm boss activity for Monday, Wednesday or Friday when each boss victory gives 850 Guild Versus points, while keeping affordable daily Roads Unlocked rally milestones on other days. Do not delay the daily login to wait for a scoring day.

2. Generous Soul

Objective: Score VIP Points during the event. Progress is cumulative across the event and does not reset daily. The final milestone is 35,000 VIP Points; 25,000 VIP Points award Oya’s Talisman of Warrioress. Make purchases only when they fit your budget.

Guild VS Strategy

VIP Points give the same 100 Guild Versus points per VIP Point on every active day, Monday through Saturday. Grand Caravan VIP progress is event-wide, so there is no single best VIP weekday. Keep planned purchases within your budget and consider the separate Roads Unlocked daily VIP milestones. If available before Tuesday’s reset, Crow and Eva Soul Stone rewards can be claimed Tuesday for 500 points per Hero Soul Stone; never miss a claim deadline to wait for that day.

3. Wise Exchange

Objective: Spend Emeralds during the event, up to 200,000. The 100,000-Emerald milestone awards Mojo’s Talisman of Shaman. Prefer spending that supports your regular account goals.

Guild VS Strategy

Emerald spending has no direct Guild Versus task: choose the action the Emeralds buy. Wednesday is the best match for planned Energy refills if you spend the Energy that day: 100 points per 3 Energy, plus Bountiful Roads Energy of Evolution progress. If already planning paid Artifact Chests, open them Wednesday or Friday for 750 points each while advancing Sealed Away. Prefer saved keys and do not spend extra Emeralds only to reach another milestone.

4. Artifact Empowering

Objective: Spend Artifact Coins during the event, up to 60,000. Prioritize heroes in your main teams.

Guild VS Strategy

Use Artifact Coins on useful artifact upgrades Thursday to score the hero power actually gained at 30 Guild Versus points per power. There is no direct score per Artifact Coin spent. Chaos Cores consumed Thursday give a separate 3,000 points each. Save Rune Stones and Rune Spheres from these rewards for Saturday: 100 points per 20 Rune Stones and 50,000 per 100 Rune Spheres.

5. Sealed Away

Objective: Open Artifact Chests during the event. Use saved keys first; the final milestone is 125 chests.

Guild VS Strategy

Open Artifact Chests Wednesday or Friday: both days give 750 Guild Versus points per chest. Completing all 125 openings on these days adds 93,750 points; split them only if it helps your guild or fits a confirmed in-game discount. Wednesday also lets you spend the Mastery Crystals from the first reward on Thursday for 200 points per 15 crystals. Use saved keys before paying Emeralds.

6. Trial of Strength

Objective: Fight in the Arena during the event. Complete all eight milestones with 30 Arena fights.

Guild VS Strategy

Use Arena attempts Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday for 6,000 Guild Versus points per fight while advancing this mission. Continue ordinary attempts on other days if needed to reach 30 fights before the event ends. Grand Arena has its own Guild Versus task but does not count as an Arena fight for Trial of Strength. Claiming Pallant Soul Stones does not score the Tuesday Hero Soul Stone task.

7. Tactical Losses

Objective: Lose troops at or above the required level during the event. Use the version shown in your game: level 9+, level 8+, or level 6+. These are alternative versions, not three ladders to add together. Progress does not reset daily, and this chain does not repeat after completion.

Troop level 9+ version

Guild VS Strategy

Align already-planned qualifying PvP activity with Saturday: Guild Versus awards 18 points per level 9 troop lost, 16 per level 8 and 8 per level 6. Only losses battling another player count for that Guild Versus task; boss or monster losses do not. Follow your event’s level 9+, 8+ or 6+ version and do not add the three ladders together. Training and healing speed-ups used Saturday give 200 points per minute and also advance Bountiful Roads No Time to Wait. Avoid unnecessary losses.

Simple spending order

Tuesday: use Arena attempts and claim ready Hero Soul Stone rewards. Wednesday: spend saved Energy and open Artifact Chests. Thursday: invest Artifact Coins in useful upgrades and spend Mastery Crystals. Friday: use the second Artifact Chest day and open earned Elemental Spheres. Saturday: spend Rune Stones and Rune Spheres, use Arena attempts, and align already-planned qualifying PvP activity with Tactical Losses.

Keep logins and ordinary progress going every day. VIP Points have the same Guild Versus rate on every active day, so let your budget and the daily Roads Unlocked milestones guide planned purchases.

Check the action that actually scores

  • All seven Grand Caravan mission chains track progress across the event. VIP Points and Tactical Losses do not reset daily and no Grand Caravan chain repeats after completion.
  • Emerald spending and Artifact Coin spending have no direct Guild Versus task. Score through the action purchased or the hero power actually gained on the appropriate day.
  • Artifact Chests score equally on Wednesday and Friday. There is no need to split the Grand Caravan ladder between those days unless doing so helps your guild or a confirmed in-game discount.
  • Roads Unlocked VIP quests reset daily. Balance any planned purchases with those daily milestones rather than moving every purchase to one day; do not buy solely for Guild Versus points.
  • Tactical Losses has alternative level 9+, 8+ and 6+ versions. Use the version shown in your game. Only losses battling another player count for Saturday Guild Versus troop-loss points.
  • Claiming rewards is not always the scoring action. Hero Soul Stones score when obtained on Tuesday, while Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, Mastery Crystals and Elemental Spheres score when spent or opened on their matching day.
  • Sunday has no Guild Versus tasks. Finish any remaining Grand Caravan objectives before the event ends, but do flexible spending on a matching active day when possible.
  • Keep daily logins and normal Arena progress. Do not delay a claim beyond its deadline, waste resources, or force unnecessary troop losses for an extra milestone.

For speed-up timing and the linked completion counters, see the Bountiful Roads Guild Versus spending plan.

Grand Caravan Final Priority

  1. Claim all seven login rewards.
  2. Plan Emerald and Artifact Coin spending around your main teams.
  3. Use saved Artifact Chest keys before paying Emeralds.
  4. Complete 30 Arena fights during the event.
  5. Follow the troop-level version shown in your game; do not add the three Tactical Losses tables together.
  6. Treat Oya’s Talisman of Warrioress at 25,000 VIP Points and Mojo’s Talisman of Shaman at 100,000 Emeralds as optional spending targets.
  7. All seven chains track progress across the event and can be completed once.
