Rewards Change Between Cycles

This reward list includes Hero Soul Stones, Unborn Stars, Skin Soul Stones, Harmony Gloves — Recipe, and Talisman of Stars. Check the in-game reward identity if a later cycle changes the featured items.

Gear & Glory Progress Planner

All three Gear & Glory chains are cumulative. A completed milestone in a linked event advances its matching counter; spending a resource is not itself a completed quest. Review the in-game counter as you complete and claim milestones.

Gear & Glory Final Checklist

  • Log in on all seven event days.
  • Complete affordable milestones in the seven daily Defiant Edge chains before reset; its login chain remains cumulative.
  • Track the cumulative targets of 420 Defiant Edge quests and 70 Unbroken Bond quests.
  • Use Guild VS overlaps for planned spending without sacrificing daily Defiant Edge rewards.
  • Claim completed milestones and check the matching Gear & Glory counter.
  • The final rewards include Harmony Gloves — Recipe at 420 Defiant Edge quests and Talisman of Stars at 70 Unbroken Bond quests.
