Rewards Change Between Cycles

The supplied season includes rotating Hero Soul Stones, Heartbreaker fragments, Viper Kiss—Recipe, Trickster Gloves – Recipe, Skin Soul Stones, and Talisman of the Acrobat. Future cycles may rotate premium rewards while keeping similar meta-quest structure.

Gear & Glory Final Checklist

  • Log in on all seven event days.
  • Complete affordable Defiant Edge stages before their daily reset.
  • Track cumulative Unbroken Bond milestones throughout the week.
  • Claim every completed milestone because each one advances the corresponding Gear & Glory meta-chain.
  • Check the live Heartbreaker, recipe, Skin Soul Stone, and Talisman rewards in case a future cycle rotates them.

Next Step: Spend Your Balance Sigils

After completing the event missions, use the Sanctum of Balance Shop Guide to compare Relic Shards, Soul Stones, Artifact Fragments, and rotating rewards before the shop closes.
