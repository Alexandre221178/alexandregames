The Featured Hero Rotates

The supplied season features Tristan, his Soul Stones, Artifact, unique red item, Solar Skin+, Dark Depths Skin, and Talisman of Blessing. Future cycles can replace Tristan and those rewards. Read every Tristan milestone below as “the current featured Hero” when applying the strategy to another month.

Prepare Before the Event

If the featured Hero is already maxed, many threshold quests may be immediately complete; otherwise save EXP Potions, equipment, Rune Stones, Sparks of Power, Artifact resources, and Chaos Cores. Never invest heavily in a Hero you do not plan to use only for a small Sigil return.

Gear & Glory Final Checklist

  • Confirm that the month's featured Hero belongs in your account plan before using rare resources.
  • Spend Sparks Monday, Rune Stones and flexible Hero upgrades Thursday, and equip items Wednesday or Friday.
  • Evolve Artifacts Tuesday/Wednesday for fragment scoring or Saturday for Chaos Core scoring.
  • Complete affordable Defiant Edge stages every day because they reset and drive the 450-quest meta-chain.
  • Check the live featured skins, Talisman, red item, and Hero names; the Tristan lineup is one season example.

Next Step: Spend Your Balance Sigils
