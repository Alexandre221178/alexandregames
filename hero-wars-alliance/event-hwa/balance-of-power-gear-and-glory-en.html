Rewards Change Between Cycles

This cycle includes Luther Soul Stones, Hammer of Righteous Judgment fragments, Luther's Cybernetic Skin, Yasmine's Demonic Skin, Phobos' Dark Depths Skin, Rune Stones, Viper Kiss recipe fragments, Fanatic's Gloves — Recipe, and Luther's Talisman of Purification. Event quests and rewards may change between cycles.

Gear & Glory Progress Planner

All three Gear & Glory chains are cumulative. A completed milestone in a linked event advances its matching counter; spending a resource is not itself a completed quest. Review the in-game counter as you complete and claim milestones.

Gear & Glory Final Checklist

  • Log in on all seven event days.
  • Complete affordable milestones in the six daily Defiant Edge chains before reset; its login chain remains cumulative.
  • Track the cumulative targets of 300 Defiant Edge quests and 75 Unbroken Bond quests.
  • Use Guild VS overlaps for planned spending without sacrificing daily Defiant Edge rewards.
  • Claim completed milestones and check the matching Gear & Glory counter.
  • The final rewards include Fanatic's Gloves — Recipe at 300 Defiant Edge quests and Talisman of Purification at 75 Unbroken Bond quests.
