Spark of Glory is the event that asks the uncomfortable but useful question: do you actually want to build Guus, or do you only want the new Twilight Skin+ because it is new? The missions reward nearly every part of his development, so a committed Guus player can create excellent value. A player without Guus in the main team can just as easily turn curiosity into an expensive side project.

This season's path offers enough Ascension Crests to reach the 90,000-Crest base Twilight Skin requirement without completing every extreme milestone. That is the important number. The final tiers are there for large accounts; the smart route is to combine natural Guus development with the Rising Legend and Trial of Legends meta-rewards.

The Featured Hero Can Change

Spark of Glory is a Skin+ event format. This supplied season features Guus and Twilight Skin+, but future versions can rotate the Hero, Skin+, Soul Stones, and shop rewards. The durable strategy is to match the mission action to Guild VS, then decide whether the featured Hero belongs in your real teams.

My Guus Decision

If Guus is in your main lineup, Spark of Glory is one of the rare events where investing in the featured Hero and earning the event currency are the same action. If he is not, prioritize free and low-cost milestones, unlock or evolve him through Champion's Gallery if needed, and do not force level 400 glyphs or level 100 artifacts merely to make the event screen look complete.

1. Secrets of Outland

Open 150 Outland Chests to finish one cycle. The entire chain becomes available again after a complete walkthrough, making it the only explicitly repeatable Spark of Glory chain.

Repeatable Does Not Mean Free

Use daily free openings first. Additional cycles can create Crests quickly, but the Emerald price rises while the reward per cycle remains 5,850 Crests and one random Red Equipment 1 fragment.

2. Soaring Might

Promote Guus through Hero ranks. Equipment is the expensive part of this chain, so Friday and Wednesday are more valuable than Thursday even though all rank gains increase Hero Power.

Best Days: Friday and Wednesday

Guild VS directly rewards every item equipped on those days, with much larger points for violet, orange, and red gear. Farm missing equipment through Energy and avoid buying it from Champion's Gallery unless time truly matters more than Crests.

3. Awakened Glyphs

Increase the combined level of Guus's glyphs. This chain consumes Rune Stones and, at advanced levels, Rune Spheres. Thursday is the cleanest triple overlap in the entire event.

Best Day: Thursday

Runic Might rewards Rune Stones, Spark of Glory rewards Guus glyph levels, and Guild VS rewards Rune Stone spending, Rune Sphere spending, and Hero Power gained. Decide your stopping point before you begin because this is where a satisfying combo can become overinvestment.

4. Gift of the Elements

Raise Guus's Gift of the Elements to level 30. Sparks of Power are easier to replace when your Dungeon and Titan development are healthy, which is another reason not to separate Hero event strategy from Titan strategy.

Best Day: Monday

Guild VS awards 30 points per 15 Sparks of Power spent. Thursday would score the resulting Hero Power, but Monday directly rewards the scarce resource being consumed.

5. Arcane Knowledge

Awaken or evolve any Guus artifact up to 18 times. These upgrades can consume Hero artifact fragments and Chaos Cores, and Guild VS rewards those materials on different days.

Choose the Resource You Want to Score

Saturday awards 3,000 points per Chaos Core. Tuesday and Wednesday award 1,200 points per Hero artifact fragment. If an evolution consumes both at once, choose the day that rewards your more valuable contribution; I prefer Saturday when Chaos Cores are the limiting resource.

6. Blacksmith Mastery

Raise all three Guus artifacts to the listed levels. Balance Essence, Scroll, and Metal resources across the three slots; one artifact at level 100 does not satisfy a milestone that asks for all artifacts.

Best Indirect Day: Thursday

Guild VS does not have a direct task for artifact levels, but every level increases Guus's power, and Thursday rewards Increase Hero Power. Arcane Knowledge is a different chain: artifact levels do not replace artifact awakening or evolution.

7. Complete Rising Legend Missions

Every claimed Rising Legend milestone advances this chain. Because several Rising ladders reset daily, you can reach a high mission count through affordable early tiers without completing every expensive daily ladder.

The Hidden Value

This meta-chain is why a small daily action can be worth more than it first appears: it gives the Rising reward, advances this Spark milestone, and may also score Guild VS. Count completed tiers, not only resources spent.

8. Complete Trial of Legends Missions

Trial of Legends has 75 listed milestones across its eight cumulative chains, and this meta ladder rewards every one of them. The final Skin Certificate appears at 45 completed missions, well before the 75-mission ceiling.

Build the Count Efficiently

Logins, Guild Activity, lower Rune Stone tiers, Expeditions, Titan Potions, and free Heroic Chests can create many completions without touching the highest VIP or Emerald tiers. Use the mission count to find a reachable reward, not to justify a costly chain.

My Spark of Glory Priority Order

  1. Collect natural Rising and Trial meta progress before forcing Guus upgrades.
  2. Use Thursday for Guus glyphs and Runic Might.
  3. Use Friday and Wednesday for Guus equipment ranks.
  4. Use Saturday for artifact evolution and Monday for Gift of the Elements.
  5. Stop when you have the Ascension Crests needed for your chosen shop priority.

Should the Crests Become Twilight Skin+?

Twilight Skin+ improves Guus's Critical Hit Chance and makes Honkageddon more reliable, but its increased event price changes the value calculation. Read the Guus Guide and my Champion's Gallery Shop Priorities before committing the Crests.
