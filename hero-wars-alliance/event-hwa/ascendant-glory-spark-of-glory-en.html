These are base rates. The two mission-count ladders have no direct Guild Versus task: the underlying actions earn points and advance their original events. Their completed missions then advance Spark of Glory. Do not postpone Rising Legend daily missions merely to wait for a matching weekday.

Hero Soul Stone points depend on receiving the rewards Tuesday; later rewards can only use a later Tuesday if the game allows the claim to wait safely. Spending Hero Skin Stones is a separate action from opening chests. Adventure Energy is not the regular Energy used by Wednesday's Guild Versus task. Sunday has no active Guild Versus tasks.

Alexandre's Outland Chest Strategy

Discounted Outland Chests offer several rewards alongside event progress: Skin Stones, possible skins and Skin Certificates, and Outland Shop Coins. They are a useful option when these rewards fit your account's needs. Friday is the Guild Versus match, at 2,000 points per chest. If a 55% discount is active then, 100 chests priced at 7,500 Emeralds normally would cost 3,375.

Cost estimates use 33.75 Emeralds per chest; actual spending depends on available bundles, discounts, and free openings. Crest totals use complete 150-chest cycles plus milestones reached in the remaining partial cycle, rather than multiplying the first 100-chest reward.

For a 300,000-Crest Skin+ target, Outland missions alone reach the target at 6,600 chests, awarding 301,400 Crests. Subtract Crests already available and rewards from the other event chains before planning more openings.

The supplied plan also mentions another Skin+ at 80,000 Emeralds spent. That reward is not shown in the supplied Trial of Legends Emerald-spending table, so confirm the separate offer and its conditions in the game. At the assumed price, 2,370 chests cost 79,987.50 Emeralds, which is below 80,000; 24 batches of 100 would cost 81,000.

4. Rising Legend

Complete missions in Rising Legend during the event. The updated schedule allows up to 322 milestone completions, while this ladder ends at 300, leaving a margin of 22. Six Rising Legend chains reset daily, so complete intended daily milestones before reset. This Spark of Glory ladder accumulates across the event and does not repeat.
