1. Secrets of Outland

Open Outland Chests during the event. This chain can be completed again after a full walkthrough, so each 150-chest completion starts a new cycle.

Use the 55% Outland Discount

The best time to open large batches is during the Friday, Saturday, and Sunday discount. Opening 100 chests normally costs 7,500 Emeralds; with the 55% discount, the same 100 chests cost 3,375 Emeralds. Outland chests also provide Skin Stones, skins, Skin Certificates, and Outland Shop Coins.

Alexandre's Extra Information: Outland Chest Strategy

When planning how many chests to open, remember that Outland Chests also provide Skin Stones, skins, Skin Certificates, and Outland Shop Coins. This makes discounted Outland Chests one of the best ways to spend Emeralds during the event and one of the strongest long-term investments in the game.

The new event Skin+ requires approximately 300,000 Ascension Crests. In addition, reaching the 80,000-Emerald milestone in the Emerald-spending mission already guarantees another Skin+, giving you an extra reward while working toward the event target.
