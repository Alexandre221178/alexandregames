Spark of Glory lists four detailed mission chains: seven logins, the cumulative Rising Legend and Trial of Legends mission-count ladders, and repeatable Realm troop training with four level variants. The tables below show every listed milestone and reward, totals for each chain, and combined totals with one selected training cycle.

These are base rates. The two mission-count ladders have no direct Guild Versus task: the underlying actions earn points and advance their original events. Their completed missions then advance Spark of Glory. Do not postpone Rising Legend daily missions merely to wait for a matching weekday.

Hero Soul Stone points depend on receiving the rewards Tuesday; later rewards can only use a later Tuesday if the game allows the claim to wait safely. Spending Hero Skin Stones is a separate action from opening chests. Adventure Energy is not the regular Energy used by Wednesday's Guild Versus task. Sunday has no active Guild Versus tasks.

Outland Mission Availability

The event description mentions opening Outland Chests, but the current mission tables do not include an Outland reward ladder. Check the event in game for availability. Outland rewards are excluded from the totals below.

Planning Outland Openings

Friday awards 2,000 Guild Versus points per Outland Chest. Check active discounts and your account?s needs before spending Emeralds. Until an Outland mission ladder is available in game, plan openings around chest rewards and Guild Versus points; no Spark of Glory Crest return is included here.

The extra reward at 270 missions has no available name or icon.

Plan Before Spending Ascension Crests

The three common chains award 196,850 Crests; one complete troop-training cycle brings the total to 203,950 Crests. Count rewards already earned from the other events as well, then compare your remaining target with the rewards in the Champion's Gallery Shop Guide.
