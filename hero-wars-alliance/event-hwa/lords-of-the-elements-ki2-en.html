Lords of the Elements KI 2 Event Overview

Lords of the Elements KI 2, also presented as Elemental Tempest, runs from Thursday through Sunday. Its eight chains cover daily logins, VIP Points, Emeralds, Titanite, Elemental Spheres, Rune Stones, Arena fights, and Grand Arena fights.

In Alexandre's opinion, Thursday is the decisive Guild Versus day because Rune Stones and Grand Arena match that day's tasks. Arena and VIP score from Thursday through Saturday; save Sunday for unfinished KI 2 missions because it no longer awards Guild Versus points.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Thursday: spend Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, and Titan Skin Stones; begin Arena and complete Grand Arena fights.
  • Friday: continue Arena progress and use Soul Reaper or Undying Gladiator fragments if they complete a Red item.
  • Saturday: finish Arena and convert earned Titan Potions plus Mastery Crystals into Guild Versus points.
  • Sunday: finish Titanite, Elemental Spheres, logins, and remaining KI 2 milestones without expecting Guild Versus points.

Alexandre's Four-Day Plan

Thursday — Rune and Arena Day

Wait until both events are live. Every 20 Rune Stones spent gives 100 Guild Versus points; the 450,000 maximum is theoretically worth 2,250,000 points. Grand Arena is exclusive to Thursday during this four-day window, and regular Arena also scores. Use already claimed Rune Spheres and Titan Skin Stones after checking your Guild's daily chest progress.

Friday — Arena and Red Equipment

Continue Arena with the attempts that make sense for your budget. If the Soul Reaper or Undying Gladiator fragments earned from KI 2 complete an item, Friday is a good day to equip it for 1,250,000 Guild Versus points. Do not count fragments as a complete item until the final equipment can actually be equipped.

Saturday — Final Battles and Resource Conversion

Finish the Arena chain only if the extra attempts are worth their Emerald cost. Use the 6,500 Titan Potions and 800 Mastery Crystals available from a full listed completion for up to 20,350 combined Guild Versus points in whole batches.

Sunday — Finish KI 2 Without Guild Versus

Guild Versus is unavailable Sunday. Complete the fourth login, remaining Titanite, Elemental Sphere openings, Arena or Grand Arena milestones that were unsafe to force earlier, and any final KI 2 reward claims. Sunday actions do not earn Guild Versus points.

Titanite + Wise Exchange Strategy for VIP 7 Players

The 800-Titanite target averages 200 Titanite per day. Players with VIP 7 can buy 75 Titanite in the VIP Shop for 300 Emeralds each day.

  • Four purchases provide 300 Titanite and spend 1,200 Emeralds.
  • The purchases advance both Titanic Contribution and Wise Exchange.
  • You still need 500 Titanite from the Guild Dungeon to reach 800.
  • The 1,200 Emerald spend clears the 500-Emerald Wise Exchange milestone but remains 300 short of the 1,500 milestone.

Buy only when the Dungeon progress and rewards justify the Emerald cost. Strong Titan accounts should prioritize the free Dungeon route.

Important limits

Opening 500 Elemental Spheres, fighting 80 times in each Arena mode, spending 450,000 Rune Stones, and earning 20,000 VIP Points are premium-scale goals. Stop at the last milestone that improves your account efficiently; completing every row is not required for a successful event.

Final Recommendation

For most players, Thursday Rune spending and normal Arena attempts from Thursday through Saturday deliver the best overlap. Treat high VIP, Emerald, Elemental Sphere, Rune Stone, and paid Arena milestones as optional. Use Sunday to finish KI 2 only; Guild Versus no longer scores then.
