Lords of the Elements: United by Power Event Overview

United by Power runs from Thursday through Sunday. Its eight chains cover logins, Hydra activity, Eternal Marks, Heroic Chests, Talisman rerolls, Expeditions, and quest completions from Titan's Roar and Elemental Tempest.

Heroic Chests score Friday, Expeditions score Thursday through Saturday, Mastery Crystals score Thursday, and Rune spending scores Saturday. Titan Skin Stones and Titan Potions score Monday, outside the event window.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Thursday: Begin the two quest-completion chains. Use already claimed Mastery Crystals and equip useful completed items. Start available Expeditions for 5,000 points each.
  • Friday: Open planned Heroic Chests for 4,000 points each and start available Expeditions. Continue Hydra and event-shop progress at a sensible pace.
  • Saturday: Spend claimed Rune Stones and Rune Spheres in complete scoring batches. Start available Expeditions and finish affordable quest-completion milestones.
  • Sunday: Finish logins, Hydra, Talismans, Expeditions, and both quest-completion chains. Guild Versus is inactive.

Golden Horns and the Weekly Rotation

Use Golden Horns only when they help your Hydra event objectives. The supplied Guild Versus rotation places Titan Potion spending on Monday, after this event ends. Do not count a Saturday Potion-spending bonus or delay event Hydra progress until Monday.

Hydra is not treated as the Guild Versus Boss task

Do not assume a Hydra battle completes the Guild Versus Defeat Boss task. That task occurs Friday during the event; Hydra credit must be verified in-game. Titan Potion spending scores Monday, outside this event window.
