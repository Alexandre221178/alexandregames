Ancient Awakening is a seven-day, Monday-to-Sunday event group built around Pallant, Elemental Coins, Titan materials, and two linked quest branches. The best plan is to complete daily opportunities without waste, then move flexible actions to the exact day when Guild VS rewards the same action.

Three Different Reset Rules

  • Main event: most ladders are cumulative; Heroic Discovery and Secrets of Outland repeat after a full walkthrough.
  • Firstborn Force: login is cumulative, while the other seven ladders reset daily.
  • Unstable Equilibrium: all eight ladders are cumulative across the week.

Always claim a completed stage before its applicable reset.

Alexandre's Quick Strategy

  • Before Monday: save Elemental Spheres, Artifact Chest Keys, Fairy Dust, Rune Stones, Summoning Circle summons, and flexible Heroic Chest openings.
  • Monday: open the largest Elemental Sphere batch and any planned Heroic Chests.
  • Tuesday: start Expeditions and open saved Artifact Chests.
  • Thursday: make the Rune Stone and Grand Arena push.
  • Friday: make the biggest Summoning Circle and Fairy Dust push.
  • Saturday: use extra Tower chests and Realm boss rallies.
  • Sunday: claim final logins and finish only targets that cannot be moved.

Do Not Mix Up These Objectives

Hydra battles are not Realm boss defeats. Taming Hydra has no exact Guild VS overlap. Firstborn Force's Payback Time does match Guild VS Defeat Boss on Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday. Likewise, a Friday Summoning Circle summon is not Monday's separate Summoning Sphere action.

The Two Linked Quest Branches

Firstborn Force

A daily-reset branch covering VIP Points, Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Summoning Circle summons, Tower chests, Artifact Chests, and Realm bosses. It supplies many small stage completions to the main 370-quest meta ladder.

Unstable Equilibrium

A cumulative branch covering VIP Points, Emeralds, Titanite, Elemental Spheres, Rune Stones, Arena, and Grand Arena. It contains 90 possible stages, so ten can be skipped while still reaching the 80-quest meta reward.

Pallant Event Shop

Spend the Elemental Coins earned throughout all three quest pages. Exclusive Pallant fragments and high-value Titan upgrades should be planned before common materials.

Spend Elemental Coins Only After Planning

The Trade Route ladder rewards spending, but it does not make every purchase efficient. Use the Pallant Ancient Awakening Shop Guide, secure exclusive priorities first, and calculate your expected final coins before filling the lower-value tiers.

Ancient Awakening FAQ

Which missions reset every day?

Inside Firstborn Force, Generous Soul, United Front, Hidden Reserves, Summoning Titans, Tower Trophy, Valkyrie's Artifacts, and Payback Time reset daily. Day After Day is cumulative.

Which main-event missions repeat?

Heroic Discovery restarts after 100 Heroic Chests and Secrets of Outland restarts after 150 Outland chests.

Can I finish the Unstable Equilibrium meta without maxing every ladder?

Yes. The branch has 90 possible stage completions and the main meta ends at 80, so you can skip ten stages.

Should I save flexible spending for Sunday?

No. Guild VS is inactive Sunday. Use Sunday only for final event progress that cannot be moved.
