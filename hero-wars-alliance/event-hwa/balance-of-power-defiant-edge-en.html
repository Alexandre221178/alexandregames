Defiant Edge is the daily-reset branch of Balance of Power. It awards Balance Sigils, rotating Soul Stones, Artifact materials, red-item fragments, Adventure resources, and other account materials for normal Hero, Titan, Arena, and Realm activity.

Daily Reset Rule

The VIP, Energy, Titan Potion, Hero Skin Stone, Summoning Circle, Arena, and Realm boss ladders reset every day. Their milestones can therefore be completed up to seven times and feed the Gear & Glory “complete Defiant Edge quests” meta-chain. The login ladder is cumulative across the week.

Defiant Edge Final Checklist

  • Claim all seven logins.
  • Complete realistic daily tiers before reset; do not plan these ladders as week-long totals.
  • Make the largest Energy push Wednesday, Skin Stone and summon push Friday, and Titan Potion or boss push Saturday.
  • Count the completed milestones toward Gear & Glory's Defiant Edge meta-chain.
  • Treat the named Soul Stones and Fragment of Red Equipment 1 (Random) as the supplied season's rotating rewards.
