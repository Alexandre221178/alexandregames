Daily Reset Rule

Generous Soul, Energy of Evolution, Hidden Reserves, Power Balance, Summoning Titans, Trial of Strength, and Payback Time reset every day. Each of these seven chains can be completed up to seven times during the event. Day After Day is the only cumulative chain. Do not skip affordable daily rewards just to wait for a Guild VS overlap. Sunday has no active Guild VS tasks, but Defiant Edge daily rewards remain available while the event is running.

Defiant Edge Best-Day Planner

Complete affordable daily milestones every day; use matching Guild VS weekdays for optional extra spending. These are weekdays in the Guild VS schedule, not Defiant Edge day numbers. Use the overlaps while both events are active.

Defiant Edge Final Checklist

  • Claim all seven login rewards and review all seven daily chains before each reset.
  • Plan optional Energy spending for Wednesday, Titan Potions for Tuesday or Saturday, and Rune Stones for Thursday.
  • Prioritize Summoning Circle summons on Friday; Monday also scores Summoning Spheres when you open them.
  • Arena overlaps on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. Bosses overlap equally on Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday.
  • Count completed Defiant Edge milestones toward Gear & Glory. Keep Titan Soul Stone reward slots separate when different Titans are shown in-game.
