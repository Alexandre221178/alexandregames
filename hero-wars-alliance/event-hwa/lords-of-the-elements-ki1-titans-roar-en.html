Lords of the Elements: Titan's Roar Event Overview

Lords of the Elements: Titan's Roar is a four-day Hero Wars Alliance event that starts on Thursday and ends on Sunday. Its eight mission chains cover logins, VIP Points, Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Summoning Circle summons, Tower chests, Hero Skin Stones, and boss victories as Raid leader.

Alexandre recommends using Thursday, Friday, and Saturday for missions that also score in Guild Versus. Save Sunday for unfinished Titan's Roar objectives and reward collection, because Guild Versus scoring is no longer available that day.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Thursday: Use already claimed Mastery Crystals in batches of 15 for 200 Guild Versus points. Useful Hero upgrades can score through Hero Power and equipment tasks. Open Tower chests and complete normal daily progress.
  • Friday: Complete Payback Time as Raid leader: up to 10 qualifying boss victories can add 8,500 Guild Versus points. Open Tower chests. Heroic and Artifact Chests from the other event chains also match Friday.
  • Saturday: Spend claimed Rune Stones in batches of 20 for 100 points and Rune Spheres in batches of 100 for 50,000 points. The event supplies 60 Rune Spheres across one completion of its listed milestones; add saved stock if useful. Open Tower chests.
  • Sunday: Claim the fourth login and finish affordable Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Hero Skin, summoning, Tower, and raid milestones before the event ends. Guild Versus is inactive.

Alexandre's Four-Day Plan

Thursday — Hero Development

Use already claimed Mastery Crystals in batches of 15 for 200 Guild Versus points. Useful Hero upgrades can score through Hero Power and equipment tasks. Open Tower chests and complete normal daily progress.

Friday — Raid Bosses

Complete Payback Time as Raid leader: up to 10 qualifying boss victories can add 8,500 Guild Versus points. Open Tower chests. Heroic and Artifact Chests from the other event chains also match Friday.

Saturday — Runes

Spend claimed Rune Stones in batches of 20 for 100 points and Rune Spheres in batches of 100 for 50,000 points. The event supplies 60 Rune Spheres across one completion of its listed milestones; add saved stock if useful. Open Tower chests.

Sunday — Event Completion

Claim the fourth login and finish affordable Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Hero Skin, summoning, Tower, and raid milestones before the event ends. Guild Versus is inactive.

Advanced Strategy: Finish United Front with the Guild Forge

Advanced accounts often have excess equipment that can be exchanged for Guild Activity in the Guild Forge. The exchange limit is 2,000 Activity per day.

  • Four-day maximum: 2,000 × 4 = 8,000 Activity, already above the 5,000 event target.
  • Simple route: exchange 2,000 on Thursday, 2,000 on Friday, and 1,000 on Saturday.
  • Late-start warning: if the event begins near your daily reset, make Thursday's exchange immediately so the first 2,000 opportunity is not lost.
  • Energy is the backup: Energy still creates Guild Activity, but there is no matching Spend Energy task during the event's active Guild Versus days.

This route protects Energy for other events while converting veteran equipment reserves into every United Front milestone.

Coordinate large upgrades with your Guild

Guild Versus chests reset daily, and the ninth daily chest requires 4,500,000 Guild points. Before a large Hero upgrade, equipping an item on Thursday, or making a planned VIP purchase, check which day's chest your Guild still needs. A large action made after the final chest has already opened cannot help the next day.

Convenience tip for high spenders

If you plan to open all remaining 33 chests, compare the total cost of opening them individually with the game's batch-opening option. In the current event, both methods cost around 2,320 Emeralds, but future events or special offers may differ.

Tip: Always check the price shown in-game before spending Emeralds, as batch offers can occasionally provide better value or save time by opening multiple chests at once.
