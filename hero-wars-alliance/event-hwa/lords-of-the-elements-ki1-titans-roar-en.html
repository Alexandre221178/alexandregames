Lords of the Elements: Titan's Roar Event Overview

Lords of the Elements: Titan's Roar is a four-day Hero Wars Alliance event that starts on Thursday and ends on Sunday. Its eight mission chains cover logins, VIP Points, Guild Activity, Fairy Dust, Summoning Circle summons, Tower chests, Hero Skin Stones, and Hero Soul Stones.

Alexandre recommends using Thursday, Friday, and Saturday for missions that also score in Guild Versus. Save Sunday for unfinished Titan's Roar objectives and reward collection, because Guild Versus scoring is no longer available that day.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Thursday: claim early rewards, then spend Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, and Titan Skin Stones while Thursday's tasks are active.
  • Friday: spend Fairy Dust, complete up to 125 Summoning Circle summons, and spend up to 20,000 Hero Skin Stones.
  • Saturday: open Tower chests and use earned Titan Potions plus Mastery Crystals.
  • Sunday: finish Guild Activity, Hero Soul Stones, logins, and any remaining Titan's Roar milestones; Guild Versus is unavailable.

Alexandre's Four-Day Plan

Thursday — Start and Convert Early Rewards

Wait until Titan's Roar and Guild Versus are both live. Claim the early login and practical mission rewards, then spend useful Rune Stones in batches of 20 and Titan Skin Stones in batches of 15. Titan's Roar offers 60 Rune Spheres in total; combine any already claimed with saved stock only if you can reach a complete 100-Sphere batch. Do not open saved Summoning Spheres yet.

Friday — Fairy Dust, Summons, and Hero Skins

This is the strongest direct-overlap day. Spend Fairy Dust on useful Titan Artifacts for up to 40,000 Guild Versus points, open up to 125 saved Summoning Spheres for 187,500 points, and spend up to 20,000 Hero Skin Stones for 933,100 points. Friday is also the only Titan's Roar day that matches the Red-item task.

Saturday — Tower and Resource Conversion

Repeat your chosen Tower chest target on Saturday because Tower Trophy resets daily; every chest opened that day also awards 4,000 Guild Versus points. Use earned Titan Potions and Mastery Crystals after Saturday's tasks activate; the full listed totals can add up to 5,900 combined points in whole batches.

Sunday — Finish Titan's Roar Without Guild Versus

Guild Versus is unavailable on Sunday. Complete the fourth login, finish Guild Activity, collect remaining Hero Soul Stones, and close any event milestones that were unsafe to delay. Do not save a Guild Versus action for Sunday expecting points.

Advanced Strategy: Finish United Front with the Guild Forge

Advanced accounts often have excess equipment that can be exchanged for Guild Activity in the Guild Forge. The exchange limit is 2,000 Activity per day.

  • Four-day maximum: 2,000 × 4 = 8,000 Activity, already above the 7,500 event target.
  • Simple route: exchange 2,000 on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, then 1,500 on Sunday.
  • Late-start warning: if the event begins near your daily reset, make Thursday's exchange immediately so the first 2,000 opportunity is not lost.
  • Energy is the backup: Energy still creates Guild Activity, but there is no matching Spend Energy task during the event's active Guild Versus days.

This route protects Energy for other events while converting veteran equipment reserves into every United Front milestone.

Coordinate large upgrades with your Guild

Guild Versus chests reset daily, and the ninth daily chest requires 4,500,000 Guild points. Before using 20,000 Hero Skin Stones, equipping a Red item, or making a planned VIP purchase, check which day's chest your Guild still needs. A large action made after the final chest has already opened cannot help the next day.

Final Recommendation

The best free value comes from four disciplined choices: convert early Rune and Titan rewards Thursday, save Fairy Dust, Summoning Spheres, and useful Hero Skin upgrades for Friday, open available Tower chests and use Titan resources Saturday, then finish Titan's Roar on Sunday without expecting Guild Versus points. Treat VIP as optional and never sacrifice long-term development only for event points.
