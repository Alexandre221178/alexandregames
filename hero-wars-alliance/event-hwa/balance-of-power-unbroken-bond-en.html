Unbroken Bond is the cumulative resource branch of Balance of Power. Its event-long quests track logins, VIP Points, Emerald spending, Titanite, Skin Stones, Elemental Spheres, Tower chests, and Grand Arena fights. All eight chains accumulate progress throughout the event and award Balance Sigils plus the materials listed below.

Rewards Change Between Cycles

The supplied season includes rotating Hero and Titan Soul Stones, Undying Gladiator's Armor — Recipe, Artifact resources, and Adventure materials. Future Balance of Power cycles may rotate the characters and premium rewards while keeping similar mission types.

Unbroken Bond Final Checklist

  • Complete all seven logins and continue sustainable Dungeon, Tower, and Grand Arena activity each day.
  • Plan Elemental Sphere openings for Monday and optional Grand Arena fights for Thursday.
  • Spend Hero Skin Stones Tuesday or Friday after checking the event mission eligibility. Titan Skin Stones have a separate Thursday Guild VS task.
  • Prioritize optional Tower chests Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Saturday.
  • Time Emerald spending around the action it buys; spending Emeralds alone has no direct Guild VS task.
  • All eight Unbroken Bond chains are cumulative. Completed milestones also contribute to Gear & Glory.
