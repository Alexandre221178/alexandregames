Rune Stone is the main resource used to upgrade Glyphs in Hero Wars Alliance. During the Runic Millstone event, you can collect a large amount of Rune Stones through daily login rewards, milestone missions, Emerald spending tasks, Expedition progress, and selected Realm objectives.

This event is especially valuable if you are still strengthening the base Glyphs of your main heroes. Tanks, damage dealers, and support heroes all benefit from stronger Glyph levels, so Runic Millstone is one of the best opportunities to stock up on Rune Stones while also picking up Season Points and other useful rewards.

Strategic Guide for Runic Millstone

1. Understanding the Value of Rune Stones

Rune Stones are the main currency used to improve standard Glyph levels in Hero Wars Alliance. That makes Runic Millstone one of the most practical events for strengthening the base power of your heroes. If your core team still depends on stronger Armor, Magic Defense, Health, Strength, Agility, Intelligence, or offensive Glyph values, this event has strong long-term value.

2. Prioritize Daily Tasks

The best value in Runic Millstone comes from tasks you can repeat or prepare in advance. Daily login rewards and Expedition progress are easy sources of Rune Stones, so make sure you complete them every day of the event instead of leaving everything for the last moment.

3. Plan Your Emerald Spending Carefully

The Value Exchange table offers a large amount of Rune Stones across multiple Emerald milestones. If you are already planning to spend Emeralds on another feature, try to align that spending with Runic Millstone. This way, one decision contributes to both your immediate account goals and your event progression at the same time.

4. Focus on Your Core Heroes First

Rune Stones are usually most effective when invested in the heroes you rely on most in Arena, Grand Arena, Guild War, and campaign progression. Strengthening the Glyphs of your main tank, primary damage dealer, and most used support heroes often gives a better return than distributing resources evenly across your full roster.

5. Turn Event Rewards into Long-Term Progress

Runic Millstone is not only a short event for quick rewards. It is also one of the best moments to push important Glyph breakpoints on heroes that define your main teams. The extra Rune Stones, Season Points, speedups, and support resources can all contribute to stronger long-term progression when used with a plan.

Analysis of Glyph Progression: Astaroth Example

Astaroth is a strong example of why Runic Millstone matters so much for account development. As one of the most reliable frontline heroes in Hero Wars Alliance, he benefits greatly from improved Glyph levels because those upgrades directly reinforce his durability and his ability to protect the team.

His Glyph set includes Magic Attack, Armor, Health, Magic Defense, and Strength. Even though not every stat has equal impact in battle, the overall improvement in survivability and role consistency becomes much more noticeable as these Glyphs rise. This is exactly the kind of progress Runic Millstone helps accelerate through its large Rune Stone rewards.

For players who use Astaroth in Arena, Grand Arena, Guild War, or campaign teams, extra Rune Stones earned during this event can strengthen one of the most important defensive heroes in the game. The same logic also applies to other core heroes in your lineup: investing event rewards into the heroes you actually use is what turns Runic Millstone into meaningful account power.

Conclusion

Runic Millstone is one of the most efficient events in Hero Wars Alliance for players who want to improve Glyph levels and increase the overall strength of their main heroes. By preparing daily objectives in advance, timing Expedition progress well, planning Emerald spending carefully, and investing Rune Stones into your most important heroes first, you can turn this event into a major boost for both immediate performance and long-term progression.
