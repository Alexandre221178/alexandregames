Ascendant Glory is not a normal resource event with a skin placed on top. It is a seven-day decision: how far are you truly willing to build the featured Hero? This season that Hero is Kendle, the reward currency is Ascension Crests, and the premium prize is her Inner Demons Skin+.

My advice is to resist the urge to click every red notification. The event now begins on Monday and ends on Sunday, aligning exactly with one complete Guild VS cycle. That means nearly every expensive mission has a better day. When you line them up correctly, the same Rune Stone, Chaos Core, Outland Chest, or artifact fragment can advance Ascendant Glory and score for your guild at the same time.

My Short Version

Kendle Soul Stones are not sold in Champion's Gallery, although some come from other event missions. Collect those mission rewards first. F2P players should evaluate the 90,000-Crest base Inner Demons Skin; Kendle mains and whales must decide whether the full 390,000-Crest base skin + Skin+ path is worth more than the shop's artifacts, Soul Stones, and Skin Stones.

What Each Event Really Does

Rising Legend

The daily engine. Day After Day is cumulative, while Generous Soul, Power Core, Hidden Reserves, Taming Hydra, Trial of Strength, and Payback Time reset every day. Missing a reset means losing potential milestones forever.

Open Rising Legend Guide

Trial of Legends

The seven-day account-resource test. Its cumulative chains ask for VIP Points, Emeralds, Guild Activity, Rune Stones, Expeditions, Tower Chests, and Heroic Chests. None of its chains reset or repeat.

Open Trial of Legends Guide

Spark of Glory

The meta-reward path. It pays additional Ascension Crests for completed Rising Legend and Trial of Legends missions, while Secrets of Outland is repeatable. This is where progress in the other events multiplies.

Open Spark of Glory Guide

Everything on the Ascendant Glory Screen

The event hub separates earning from spending. Complete Quests opens the three mission branches; Champion's Gallery is the Crest shop; New Skin+ contains the featured upgrade path; and the Reward Cascade, Arcane Gifts, and New Skin tiles hold additional event offers or rewards. Check every tile before spending because the Skin+ requires its base skin first.

The 390,000-Crest Decision

The base Inner Demons Skin costs 90,000 Ascension Crests. Only after securing it can you buy Kendle's Inner Demons Skin+ for another 300,000 Crests. The complete path therefore costs 390,000 Ascension Crests.

My rule is simple. If Kendle is central to your Arena, Grand Arena, or Guild team, the Skin+ deserves serious consideration because its Crushing bonus improves her finishing pressure. If Kendle is outside your main teams, the same Crests may build more account value through Crow and Kendle book artifacts, available Hero Soul Stones, and Skin Stones.

Best Plan by Account Stage

F2P / New Player

Collect Kendle Soul Stones from event missions because they are not sold in Champion's Gallery. Target the 90,000-Crest base skin, then preserve the remaining Crests for rare books, useful Soul Stones, and Skin Stones.

Low Spender

Reach 390,000 only if you already saved the Emeralds, Runes, Chaos Cores, and Outland budget assumed above. Otherwise, secure the base skin and compare Kendle's Skin+ with Aidan's 80,000-Emerald Blazing Skin+ path.

Whale / Kendle Main

Complete both meta ladders and use discounted Outland Chests to exceed the Skin+ target, then prioritize Crow's and Kendle's book artifacts, scarce Soul Stones, and Skin Stones.

Do Not Chase Every Final Tier

Ascendant Glory is designed to make partial progress feel incomplete. Do not let that feeling decide for you. A 750,000 Rune Stone target, 200,000 Emerald target, 35,000 VIP target, or 100 Heroic Chest target is not a recommendation. It is a ceiling. Stop when the next reward costs more than it improves your account.

Ascendant Glory Final Checklist

  • Open Monday with Heroic Chests, Realm bosses, Arena battles, and affordable VIP tiers.
  • Use Tuesday for the first Expedition, Realm boss, and Arena overlap.
  • Keep Energy and Fairy Dust available for Wednesday.
  • Save Rune Stones and Rune Spheres for Thursday.
  • Use Friday for Expeditions, Fairy Dust, Arena, and discounted Outland Chests.
  • Use Saturday for Chaos Cores, Realm bosses, Arena, and discounted Outland Chests.
  • Reserve Sunday for final claims and unavoidable daily tiers; avoid major flexible spending.
  • Read the Champion's Gallery priorities before spending Ascension Crests.
