Bountiful Roads is the Talisman progression and meta-reward branch of Trade Routes. This rotation rewards logins, Talisman rerolls, Mastery Crystal spending, repeatable Energy spending, and completed quest steps from Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan.

Trade Routes Guides

Alexandre's Best Bountiful Roads Combo

Reroll a useful Talisman to earn Mastery Crystals, invest those Crystals back into a core hero, and stack the repeatable Energy chain with Roads Unlocked. Let the two completion counters advance through efficient play; the premium target is 68 Grand Caravan quests for Julius's Winter Skin and Winter Skin+.

Objective: Log in on all seven event days for Adventure Coins.

6. Complete Grand Caravan Quests

Objective: Complete individual Grand Caravan quest steps; this meta-counter advances automatically up to 68.

Strategy

Treat this as a passive meta-chain. Plan Emerald, VIP, chest, Skin Stone, Artifact Coin, and Arena objectives in the Grand Caravan guide instead of overspending for one extra completion.

Bountiful Roads Final Checklist

  • Claim all seven login rewards.
  • Reroll only Talismans used by your core teams.
  • Spend the rewarded Mastery Crystals on a planned Talisman milestone.
  • Stack Energy of Evolution with Roads Unlocked and other Energy objectives.
  • Treat both Complete Quests chains as passive meta-rewards.
  • Use 68 Grand Caravan completions as the premium goal for Julius's Winter Skin and Winter Skin+.
