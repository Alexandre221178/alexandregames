Bountiful Roads is the Talisman progression and meta-reward branch of Trade Routes. This rotation rewards logins, Talisman rerolls, repeatable speed-up use, repeatable Energy spending, and completed quest steps from Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan.

Alexandre's Best Bountiful Roads Combo

Reroll a useful Talisman to earn Mastery Crystals, use speed-ups on planned upgrades, and complete repeatable Energy milestones. Advance the two completion counters through planned Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan quests.

1. Day After Day

Objective: Log in on all seven event days for Adventure Coins.

Strategy

Claim every daily login. Missing one day permanently loses that milestone because this chain does not repeat.

Guild VS Strategy

Log in daily and claim every Adventure Coin reward. Logging in and obtaining Adventure Coins have no direct Guild Versus scoring task, so there is no better weekday for this chain. Plan the use of your other event rewards around their own scoring actions. Do not skip a login to wait for a spending day.

2. Transforming Talisman

Objective: Reroll Talisman slots up to 500 times to earn Mastery Crystals and supporting resources.

Strategy

Reroll only a Talisman you genuinely use. The reward ladder is large, but poor rerolls on an inactive hero are still wasted Meta Cubes.

Guild VS Strategy

Rerolls and Meta Cube spending have no direct Guild Versus task. Reroll only a useful Talisman, then save the Mastery Crystals you earn for Thursday: 200 points per 15 crystals spent. Save the Rune Spheres from the first milestone for Saturday: 50,000 points per 100 spheres spent. You may reroll earlier in the week to have these rewards ready; claiming a resource is different from spending it.

3. No Time to Wait

Objective: Use any speed-ups for up to 2,460 minutes per cycle to earn Spooky Coins and resource packs. This chain repeats after a full walkthrough and does not reset daily.

Strategy

Use saved speed-ups on construction, research, or training you already planned. Each full cycle ends at 2,460 minutes, after which the chain can be repeated.

Guild VS Strategy

Use construction speed-ups Tuesday, research speed-ups Wednesday, training speed-ups Friday or Saturday, and healing speed-ups Saturday. Each matching Guild Versus task gives 200 points per minute. Friday can also score troops trained, while Saturday fits needed healing after planned PvP activity. This Bountiful Roads chain repeats after 2,460 minutes; it does not reset daily. Use general speed-ups on the matching activity and check that the in-game Guild Versus counter advances.

4. Energy of Evolution

Objective: Spend up to 4,000 Energy per full cycle. This reward ladder repeats after a complete walkthrough.

Strategy

Use saved Energy and planned refills when they also advance another active Energy objective. Each full cycle returns 3,300 Rune Stones.

Guild VS Strategy

Save flexible Energy spending for Wednesday: Guild Versus gives 100 points per 3 Energy spent. Energy of Evolution also advances and repeats after each 4,000-Energy cycle. If using planned Emerald refills, those Emeralds additionally advance Grand Caravan Wise Exchange. Claim the Rune Stones from completed cycles and save their spending for Saturday, when 20 Rune Stones give 100 points. Do not let normal Energy regeneration or an event deadline go to waste while waiting.

5. Complete Roads Unlocked Quests

Objective: Complete individual Roads Unlocked quest steps; this meta-counter advances automatically up to 270.

Strategy

Do not spend only to move this counter. Complete the efficient daily and event-long milestones in Roads Unlocked, then collect these meta-rewards passively.

Guild VS Strategy

Build this counter through affordable Roads Unlocked daily milestones every day; do not sacrifice daily rewards to concentrate everything on one Guild Versus day. Tuesday favors planned Hero Skin Stone spending at 700 points per 15 stones. Tower chests and Expeditions have the same rates Monday through Saturday: 4,000 per chest and 5,000 per Expedition. If already unlocked in time, claim Hero Soul Stone meta-rewards Tuesday for 500 points per stone; spend earned Rune Stones Saturday. The completion counter itself has no direct Guild Versus task.

6. Complete Grand Caravan Quests

Objective: Complete individual Grand Caravan quest steps; this meta-counter advances automatically up to 72.

Strategy

Treat this as a passive meta-chain. Plan Emerald, event-wide VIP, chest, Artifact Coin, Arena, and troop-loss objectives in the Grand Caravan guide instead of overspending for one extra completion.

Guild VS Strategy

Advance the underlying quests on their matching Guild Versus days: Energy Wednesday, Artifact Chests Wednesday or Friday, useful artifact upgrades Thursday, Arena Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday, and already-planned qualifying PvP losses Saturday. VIP Points score the same 100 points each on all active days. This counter goes to 72 Grand Caravan quest steps and does not score Guild Versus directly. Claim unlocked Crow or Yasmine Soul Stones Tuesday only if you can do so before the claim deadline; open earned Elemental Spheres Monday or Friday.

A practical weekly plan

Tuesday: construction speed-ups and planned Hero Skin Stone spending. Wednesday: research speed-ups, saved Energy and Artifact Chests. Thursday: spend Mastery Crystals, upgrade artifacts, spend Relic Shards and equip useful gear. Friday: train troops and open Artifact Chests or Elemental Spheres. Saturday: spend Rune Spheres and Rune Stones; use needed training or healing speed-ups.

Keep daily rewards and scoring actions separate

  • No Time to Wait (speed-ups) and Energy of Evolution repeat after a full cycle; they do not reset daily. Schedule saved resources on the matching Guild Versus day.
  • Talisman rerolls spend Meta Cubes. The current Guild Versus tasks do not award points directly for rerolls or Meta Cubes; Mastery Crystals earned from rerolls can be saved for Thursday.
  • Roads Unlocked has six daily chains plus an event-wide login chain. Complete affordable daily milestones every day instead of saving every resource for one preferred weekday.
  • Grand Caravan objectives are event-wide, including VIP Points and troop losses. Its chains do not reset daily or repeat.
  • Receiving a resource and spending it are different actions. Claim Mastery Crystals, Rune Stones and Rune Spheres before their preferred spending day; spend them on that day.
  • Sunday has no Guild Versus tasks. Finish remaining event goals then if needed, but save flexible spending for an active scoring day.
  • Prefer upgrades your account needs. Do not waste resources, force troop losses, or exceed a planned budget just to reach an extra milestone.

Bountiful Roads Final Checklist

  • Claim all seven login rewards.
  • Reroll only Talismans used by your core teams.
  • Use speed-ups on planned upgrades to advance the repeatable No Time to Wait chain.
  • Stack Energy of Evolution with other active Energy objectives.
  • Treat both Complete Quests chains as passive meta-rewards.
  • Collect Grand Caravan completion rewards up to 72 quests without overspending for the counter.
