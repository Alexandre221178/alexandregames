Bountiful Roads is the Talisman progression and meta-reward branch of Trade Routes. It pays you for rerolling Talisman slots, spending Mastery Crystals, fighting Hydra, winning with a Talisman hero, and completing quest steps from Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan.

Alexandre's Best Bountiful Roads Combo

Reroll a useful Talisman early enough to earn Mastery Crystals, save the Crystals for Saturday, then spend them when Guild VS tracks Mastery Crystal use. Run Arena Monday–Saturday with a Talisman hero so every win can count for Bountiful Roads, Grand Caravan, and Guild VS.

1. Day After Day

Objective: Log in on all seven event days for Adventure Coins and Season Points.

Guild VS Strategy

There is no spending overlap. Claim Sunday too, because Trade Routes remains active even though Guild VS does not.

Objective: Spend up to 15,000 Mastery Crystals to earn Meta Cubes and Season Points.

Guild VS Strategy

4. Taming Hydra

Objective: Battle Hydra up to 21 times, with the Horn alternative shown by the live quest when available.

Guild VS Strategy

Hydra attacks do not count as the Guild VS Defeat Boss action; those are separate modes. Use your three normal Hydra attacks every day: 3 attacks × 7 days reaches 21 without spending a Golden Horn.

5. Trial for the Keepers

Objective: Win up to 35 Arena battles while using at least one hero with a Talisman equipped.

Guild VS Strategy

Fight Monday–Saturday. Guild VS gives 6,000 points per Arena fight, Grand Caravan counts the fight, and this mission counts the win with a Talisman hero. One successful battle can advance all three.

7. Complete Grand Caravan Quests

Objective: Complete individual Grand Caravan quest steps; this meta-counter advances automatically.

Guild VS Strategy

Let the counter grow through efficient actions instead of buying weak milestones. Tower on Mon/Tue/Wed/Sat, Expeditions Tue/Fri, free Artifact keys Tuesday, paid Artifact opens Friday, Outland Thu–Sat, and Arena Mon–Sat.

Bountiful Roads Final Checklist

  • Complete three normal Hydra attacks every day to reach 21 without Golden Horn spending.
  • Reroll only a Talisman with real value to your core teams.
  • Save Mastery Crystals for Saturday's exact Guild VS overlap.
  • Use a Talisman hero in every Arena team Monday–Saturday.
  • Treat both “Complete Quests” lines as passive meta-rewards; never spend solely to move them by one step.
