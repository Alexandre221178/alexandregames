Titan Rift Event Overview

Titan Rift features three login milestones. Check the current in-game dates; the Monday–Wednesday plan below applies when the event overlaps those days. Complete eight quest chains by logging in, spending Energy, earning VIP Points, collecting Titanite, opening Elemental Spheres and Artifact Chests, starting Expeditions, and defeating Bosses as the Raid Leader.

The rewards include Titan and Hero Soul Stones, Titan Potions, Meta Cubes, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Fairy Dust, Adventure resources, artifact materials, Emerald Wrath recipes, Stormfang recipes, and other useful items.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Every event day: complete your intended daily VIP, Titanite, Elemental Sphere, Expedition, and Raid Leader Boss milestones before reset.
  • Monday: Elemental Spheres, Bosses, and using Titan Potions match Guild Versus.
  • Tuesday: Expeditions and VIP Points still match; Hero Soul Stones score when received.
  • Wednesday: use saved Energy and open Artifact Chests; Bosses also match.
  • Other weekdays: Friday also matches spheres, chests, and Bosses. Saved equipment can score Thursday; saved Rune resources can score Saturday.

Alexandre's Plan for a Monday–Wednesday Event

Monday — Spheres, Bosses, and Titan Upgrades

Open up to 50 Elemental Spheres for the daily Awakening chain and 60,000 Guild Versus points. Lead Boss raids and use earned Titan Potions: every 100 potions gives 150 points, and increases in Titan Power can also score. Start Expeditions and complete the other daily chains you intend to pursue before reset.

Tuesday — Daily Missions and Hero Soul Stones

Repeat the daily Titan Rift missions. Expeditions and VIP Points earn the same Guild Versus rates as Monday and Wednesday. Hero Soul Stones received today earn 500 points each; only postpone a reward claim if the game allows it without losing the reward or blocking a daily reset or repeatable chain. Save Artifact Chests for Wednesday when practical.

Wednesday — Energy, Artifact Chests, and Bosses

Spend saved Energy and open saved Artifact Chests while both events are active. A 10,000-Energy walkthrough yields 333,300 Guild Versus points; a 125-chest walkthrough yields 93,750. Both chains can repeat after completion. Lead Boss raids and finish the final daily missions before the event ends. Save earned Rune resources for Saturday and equip completed items on Thursday when practical; Fairy Dust has no direct Guild Versus match.

Daily resets take priority

Generous Soul, Titanic Contribution, Awakening, Beyond Horizon, and Payback Time reset daily. Do not save all three days' actions for one weekday and expect to recover missed daily rewards. Boss victories only advance Payback Time when you are the Raid Leader. If the event overlaps Friday, that day also matches Elemental Spheres, Artifact Chests, and Bosses.

Daily reset. The final milestone is 4,500 VIP Points each day. Guild Versus awards 100 points per VIP Point on every active day, Monday through Saturday, so those days are tied. Match any planned purchases to the current daily chain; progress does not carry over to the next day.

4. Titanic Contribution — Collect Titanite

Daily reset. Collect up to 350 Titanite in the Guild Dungeon each day. There is no direct Titanite task in Guild Versus. Follow Alexandre Games' Dungeon guide to improve your daily collection. Final Recommendation

For most players, the efficient stopping points are the resources already saved before the event. Do not spend heavily only to force the 10,000 Energy, 4,500 VIP Point, 50 Elemental Sphere, or 125 Artifact Chest milestones. The double-event timing makes saved resources much more valuable, but it does not automatically make expensive final milestones efficient.
