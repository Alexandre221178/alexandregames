Titan Rift Event Overview

Titan Rift is a three-day Hero Wars Alliance event that begins on Monday and ends on Wednesday. Complete eight quest chains by logging in, spending Energy, earning VIP Points, collecting Titanite, opening Elemental Spheres and Artifact Chests, starting Expeditions, and defeating Bosses as the Rally Leader.

The rewards include Titan and Hero Soul Stones, Titan Potions, Meta Cubes, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Fairy Dust, Adventure resources, artifact materials, Retribution Chestplate fragments, Tiara of Wisdom fragments, and other useful items.

Best Days at a Glance

  • Monday: open all saved Elemental Spheres, lead Boss rallies, and earn planned VIP Points.
  • Tuesday: open Artifact Chests, start Expeditions, lead Boss rallies, and use Titan Potions received from Titan Rift.
  • Wednesday: spend most of your Energy, claim Hero Soul Stones, spend earned Fairy Dust, and equip a completed Red item if possible.

Alexandre's Three-Day Plan

Monday — Elemental Sphere Day

After the event begins, log in and open up to 125 saved Elemental Spheres. Each sphere advances Awakening and is worth 1,200 Guild Versus points on Monday. Lead available Boss rallies and do any planned VIP purchase only after confirming both events are active. Reaching 4,500 VIP Points awards 35 more Elemental Spheres to open on Monday, while the five Artifact Chest Keys from the 2,600 milestone should be saved for Tuesday.

Tuesday — Artifact Chest and Expedition Day

Leave Expeditions unstarted until Tuesday's Guild Versus tasks appear, then start six. Open saved Artifact Chests on Tuesday, not Monday: 100 chests can add 75,000 Guild Versus points while completing Sealed Away. Use Titan Potions claimed from Titan Rift upgrades on this day for another linked task. If the game safely lets you leave quest rewards unclaimed, hold the Sealed Away Hero Soul Stones for Wednesday.

Wednesday — Energy and Reward Conversion Day

Wednesday is the strongest day for Energy: every 3 Energy spent gives 100 Guild Versus points. Save Bottled Energy, free Energy and refreshes for this final day. Claim Hero Soul Stones on Wednesday when possible, spend the Fairy Dust you earned, and equip a Red item only if the event fragments complete one.

Important: do not over-delay Boss missions

Boss victories only count for Titan Rift when you are the Rally Leader. Monday and Tuesday both have the Guild Versus Boss task, so split the ten victories according to available attempts and your guild's schedule. Do not wait until the final hours and risk missing the ten-win milestone.

Final Recommendation

For most players, the efficient stopping points are the resources already saved before the event. Do not spend heavily only to force the 10,000 Energy, 4,500 VIP Point, 125 Elemental Sphere, or 100 Artifact Chest milestones. The double-event timing makes saved resources much more valuable, but it does not automatically make expensive final milestones efficient.
