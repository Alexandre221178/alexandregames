Potentials use base Guild Versus rates and complete groups of each requirement. They assume every listed action or reward is available on its matching day; they are not a guaranteed combined total. Daily Guild Versus chest requirements can be reached before these maximum point totals. Sunday has no Guild Versus tasks.

Plan resource timing: use Titan Potions Monday, spend saved Energy for Guild Activity Wednesday, open saved Heroic Chests Friday, and spend Rune Stones Saturday. Start Expeditions throughout the event. Log in every day and finish all intended event milestones before the event deadline.

Use rewards when available: Titan Skin Stones score Monday, Hero Soul Stones when received Tuesday, Mastery Crystals Thursday, and Elemental Spheres Monday or Friday. Recipes may help complete equipment that scores Thursday according to rarity. Rewards earned after their matching weekday can be saved for the next week; they cannot contribute retroactively. Energy and Adventure Energy are different resources.

Turn Trial Progress into More Crests

Every completed milestone also advances the Trial of Legends mission-count ladder in Spark of Glory. There are 84 available Trial milestones, while the Spark of Glory meta ladder ends at 75 completed missions, so you can leave nine Trial milestones unfinished and still complete that meta ladder.
