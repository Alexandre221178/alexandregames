Trade Routes is a seven-day, Monday-to-Sunday event group built around Talismans. Its three linked events award Exchange Sigils, Adventure resources, Rune resources, Soul Stones, and other materials that can be converted into meaningful account progress through the Caravan Hall.

Alexandre's Quick Strategy

  1. Monday to Wednesday: use stored free resources on the exact Guild VS actions and build Trade Routes progress without chasing discounts.
  2. Thursday to Saturday: make planned Emerald purchases and paid openings during the discounts. Saturday is the strongest day for spending Relic Shards and Mastery Crystals.
  3. Sunday: Guild VS is inactive. Use the Elemental Sphere and Heroic Chest discounts only when the savings are worth giving up Guild VS points.

How the Trade Routes Event Group Works

1. Complete Quests

Progress through Roads Unlocked, Grand Caravan, and Bountiful Roads. Many actions advance two or three quest chains at once.

2. Earn Exchange Sigils

Collect the event currency from missions and Reward Boxes. Do not spend it until you have checked the full Caravan Hall lineup.

3. Buy Lasting Upgrades

Prioritize a useful Talisman or featured Relic Shards for heroes you actually build, then use leftover currency on real bottlenecks.

The Trade Routes Event Group cycle highlights Exclusive Heroes' Relic Shards. Talismans, UniversalRelic Shards, skins, and other rotating rewards may change when Trade Routes returns.

Trade Routes Reward Box

The Reward Box is the fastest paid route to more Exchange Sigils. It can also contain Talismans, Skin Stones, Artifact resource boxes, Sparks of Power, 100,000-Gold bundles, and other useful rewards. The result of each opening is random, so budget for the guaranteed event-currency progress rather than assuming a rare drop.

Best way to use Reward Boxes

Buy the 10-pack only when it reaches a planned Value Exchange milestone. Then route the rewards into Guild VS: Sparks of Power on Monday, Skin Stones on Tuesday or Friday, Artifact resources on Tuesday or Wednesday, and Relic-related upgrades on Saturday.

Caravan Hall Shop Priority

The Caravan Hall is the real prize. A stockpile around 60,000 Exchange Sigils can buy a premium featured item such as a strong Talisman or highlighted Relic Shards, depending on the current lineup and price. Compare permanent account value before spending on generic resources.

  1. Core-team Talisman: buy it first only when the hero is in your main teams and the Talisman's stats improve a real matchup.
  2. Featured Relic Shards: excellent long-term value. If you can immediately spend at least 10 shards, do it on Saturday for Guild VS.
  3. Hard bottlenecks: Rune Spheres, red gear fragments, and scarce progression materials can be worthwhile after the exclusive items.
  4. Generic resources: buy Gold or common materials only with leftovers; they are normally easier to replace.

Do not spend Sigils too early

First calculate the currency still available from logins, repeatable missions, and Bountiful Roads meta-quests. A small early purchase can leave you just short of a 60,000-Sigil premium item.

The Three Trade Routes Events

Roads Unlocked

Rewards Exchange Sigils, Rune Stones, Rune Spheres, Soul Stones, and Adventure resources through Energy, Skin Stone, Summoning, Titan Potion, Titanite, VIP, and Realm boss tasks.

Grand Caravan

The main Emerald-spending event, with Artemis and Miu Talismans, Drayne's Rebirth Skin, Talisman resources, and quest lines for Tower, Artifact, Outland, Expeditions, Arena, and VIP.

Bountiful Roads

The Talisman progression finale: reroll slots, spend Mastery Crystals, fight Hydra, win Arena battles with a Talisman-equipped hero, and collect meta-rewards from the first two events.

Trade Routes FAQ

What should I buy first in Caravan Hall?

A Talisman for a core hero or featured Relic Shards usually provide the best permanent value. Wait until you know your final Exchange Sigil total.

Is the 10x Reward Box option better?

Yes. Ten single openings cost 2,000 Emeralds, while the 10x option costs 1,800, saving 200 Emeralds or 10%.

What is the best day to spend resources?

Wednesday is best for Energy stacking; Saturday is best for premium progression because Relic Shards and Mastery Crystals both have direct Guild VS tasks.

Should I save everything for Sunday discounts?

No. Guild VS is inactive Sunday. Use Sunday only for its exclusive Heroic and Elemental discounts or to finish unavoidable Trade Routes milestones.
