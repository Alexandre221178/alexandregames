Trade Routes is a seven-day, Monday-to-Sunday event group built around Talismans. Its three linked events award Exchange Sigils, Adventure resources, Rune resources, Soul Stones, and other materials that can be converted into meaningful account progress through the Caravan Hall.

Alexandre's Quick Strategy

  1. Read the current quest guides first: thresholds, repeatable missions, and premium rewards can change between rotations.
  2. Stack progress: spend a resource only when the same action advances multiple Trade Routes missions, another active event, or a useful discount.
  3. Choose one permanent target: calculate your final Exchange Sigils before deciding between a Talisman and the featured Relic.
  4. Use generic resources last: equipment and common materials are usually easier to replace than seasonal Talismans and Relic Shards.

How the Trade Routes Event Group Works

1. Complete Quests

Progress through Roads Unlocked, Grand Caravan, and Bountiful Roads. Many actions advance two or three quest chains at once.

2. Earn Exchange Sigils

Collect the event currency from missions and Reward Boxes. Do not spend it until you have checked the full Caravan Hall lineup.

3. Buy Lasting Upgrades

Prioritize a useful Talisman or featured Relic Shards for heroes you actually build, then use leftover currency on real bottlenecks.

The featured hero Relic, Talismans, skins, mission thresholds, and other rewards may change whenever Trade Routes returns. Use this page for the permanent structure and the linked guides for the current rotation.

Trade Routes Reward Box

The Reward Box is the fastest paid route to more Exchange Sigils. It can also contain Talismans, Skin Stones, Artifact resource boxes, Sparks of Power, 100,000-Gold bundles, and other useful rewards. The result of each opening is random, so budget for the guaranteed event-currency progress rather than assuming a rare drop.

Buy the 10-pack only when it reaches a planned Value Exchange milestone or closes a realistic Exchange Sigil gap. Do not chase several premium shop items at once.

Caravan Hall Shop Priority

The Caravan Hall is the real prize. Talismans commonly cost 60,000 Exchange Sigils, while fully unlocking a featured Level 1 Relic can require considerably more. Compare the complete cost and permanent account value before spending on generic resources.

  1. Core-team Talisman: buy it first only when the hero is in your main teams and the Talisman's stats improve a real matchup.
  2. Featured Relic Shards: excellent long-term value only when you can reach the required unlock and meet the hero's star requirement.
  3. Hard bottlenecks: Rune Spheres, red gear fragments, and scarce progression materials can be worthwhile after the exclusive items.
  4. Generic resources: buy Gold or common materials only with leftovers; they are normally easier to replace.

Open the current Caravan Hall shop guide for the featured Relic, exact prices, every Talisman, and Alexandre's seasonal verdict. Use the complete Talisman guide to compare both Talismans for a hero.

Do not spend Sigils too early

First calculate the currency still available from all three events and Reward Boxes. A small early purchase can leave you just short of the permanent upgrade you actually wanted.

The Three Events and Caravan Hall

Roads Unlocked

The broad resource-progression branch. Its objectives commonly use Energy, Titan resources, Expeditions, Tower activity, summons, and other recurring account actions. Check the current guide for exact daily resets.

Grand Caravan

The spending and investment branch. It commonly includes Emeralds, VIP Points, chests, Arena battles, and rotating account resources, with premium skins or Talismans at high milestones.

Bountiful Roads

The Talisman-progression and meta-reward branch. It commonly tracks Talisman upgrades, battles with equipped heroes, and completion across the other Trade Routes events.

Caravan Hall

The seasonal shop where Exchange Sigils become Talismans, featured Relic Shards, equipment, and other resources. Prices, purchase limits, and featured heroes rotate.

Trade Routes FAQ

What should I buy first in Caravan Hall?

A Talisman for a core hero or featured Relic Shards usually provide the best permanent value. Wait until you know your final Exchange Sigil total.

Is the 10x Reward Box option better?

Yes. Ten single openings cost 2,000 Emeralds, while the 10x option costs 1,800, saving 200 Emeralds or 10%.

What changes when Trade Routes returns?

The featured Relic, Talismans, mission thresholds, bonus rewards, offers, and discounts can rotate. The three-event structure and Caravan Hall currency loop usually remain familiar.

When should I spend resources?

Spend when one action advances multiple active missions or overlaps with a useful discount. Check the current quest guides and calendar before committing rare resources.
