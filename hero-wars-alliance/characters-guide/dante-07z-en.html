Understanding Dante's Skills: Evolution Priority & Combat Impact

Relics empower heroes by granting them new abilities while boosting existing ones. Beyond skill improvements, Relics also enhance two secondary stats of each hero. The stats boosted depend on how that particular hero's skills scale: Dante scales with: Physical Attack and Dodge.

1st – Instrument of Fate

Dante's signature ability throws 4 spectral spears at multiple enemies, dealing Physical Damage (95% Physical Attack + 10150) and knocking targets back. This skill is your primary source of area control and consistent damage output.

The knockback effect is particularly valuable because it interrupts enemy channeling abilities, repositions dangerous backline threats, and synergizes perfectly with Dante's passive Retribution by creating dodge opportunities. Each spear hit also triggers Shackles of Weakness, reducing enemy main stats.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill directly scales your primary damage output and control effectiveness. Every skill level increases both the base damage (10150) and the Physical Attack scaling (95%), making it essential for Dante to remain relevant in high-power matches. The frequent multi-target application makes this skill extremely cost-efficient for evolution.

1st – Enhanced Legendary Relic: Instrument of Fate

The Legendary enhancement adds a powerful self-buff: whenever Dante knocks an enemy back (which happens frequently with his kit), he gains Armor Penetration (7.5% Physical Attack + 6000) for 6 seconds.

This transforms Dante from a pure controller into a scaling damage threat. Since he knocks back enemies with multiple skills (Instrument of Fate, Retribution, Piercing Throw), he maintains near-permanent uptime on this buff during combat. The Armor Penetration allows him to cut through tanky frontlines and deal meaningful damage even against high-armor compositions.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Dante's most impactful Legendary upgrade. The constant Armor Penetration buff dramatically increases his effective DPS against all targets, especially armored tanks. This skill single-handedly elevates Dante from "good control hero" to "meta damage dealer." Prioritize maxing this immediately after unlocking Legendary status.

  • Formula: Armor Penetration Bonus: 7.5% Physical Attack + 6000 (7.5% × 122223 + 6000 = 15,166.73)

2nd – Foresight

Dante grants all allies a Dodge bonus (10% Physical Attack + 3000) for 7 seconds. This team-wide buff activates every 14 seconds with an initial cooldown of 4 seconds.

While this might seem like a simple defensive buff, it's actually the cornerstone of Dante's entire combat strategy. Here's why: every dodge by Dante or an ally triggers his Retribution skill, which throws a spectral spear dealing damage and knocking back enemies. More dodge = more Retribution procs = more damage and more control.

This creates a beautiful synergy loop: Foresight increases dodge → more dodges trigger Retribution → Retribution creates more knockback opportunities → knockback maintains Armor Penetration buff from Legendary Instrument of Fate. Additionally, higher team dodge means better survivability for your entire team, allowing longer fights where Dante's sustained damage output shines.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Don't be fooled by this being a "support" skill. Foresight is absolutely critical to Dante's damage output and team utility. Upgrading this skill increases both the base dodge (3000) and the scaling (10% Physical Attack), which directly translates to more Retribution procs and better team survival. This should be your second priority after the Legendary Instrument of Fate.

Skill Animation Info

  • Dodge Bonus Formula: (10% Physical Attack + 3000)
  • Initial Cooldown: 4 seconds
  • Cooldown: 14 seconds
  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Keywords: Team Buff, Dodge, Support
  • Animation delay: 0.5 seconds
  • Area: All allies

3rd – Retribution

This is Dante's passive counterattack mechanic. When Dante or any ally dodges an attack, he automatically throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing Physical Damage (90% Physical Attack + 4150) and knocking them back. The cooldown is only 0.1 seconds, meaning this can proc rapidly in combat.

Think of Retribution as Dante's "dodge-to-damage converter". In teams with high dodge (especially when Foresight is active), this skill can trigger dozens of times per fight, making it one of Dante's highest total damage sources despite being a passive. Each proc also knocks back enemies, creating constant interruption and repositioning.

The real power comes from the synergy: Foresight increases dodge → more dodges → more Retribution procs → more knockbacks → more Armor Penetration uptime from Legendary Instrument of Fate → higher damage per Retribution hit. It's a self-amplifying damage cycle.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Despite being a passive skill, Retribution often accounts for 30-40% of Dante's total damage output in real fights. Every skill level increases the base damage (4150) and scaling (90%), making this evolution extremely cost-efficient. Because this skill procs so frequently, even small increases per hit translate to massive total damage gains. Prioritize this equally with Foresight.

Skill Animation Info

  • Physical Damage Formula: (90% Physical Attack + 4150)
  • Cooldown: 0.1 seconds
  • Keywords: Passive, Counterattack, Dodge-triggered, Knockback
  • Animation delay: 0.72 seconds
  • Range: Nearest enemy
  • Hits: 1 spear per dodge

4th – Shackles of Weakness

Whenever a spectral spear hits an enemy (from any of Dante's skills), he reduces that target's main stat (6% Physical Attack + 2400) for 7 seconds. This debuff applies to the enemy's primary attribute (Strength for tanks, Agility for agility heroes, Intelligence for mages).

This passive debuff is constantly active in combat because Dante throws spears so frequently (Instrument of Fate, Retribution, Piercing Throw). Against enemy damage dealers, this reduces their attack power. Against tanks, it lowers their health pool. The 7-second duration ensures near-permanent uptime on priority targets.

Evolution Priority: Medium – While this skill provides valuable utility by weakening enemies, it doesn't directly increase Dante's damage output or team survivability as dramatically as his other skills. The main stat reduction does help your team deal with threats more easily, but the impact is more subtle compared to the raw damage gains from upgrading Retribution or the dodge/survival gains from Foresight. Evolve this skill after your core damage and utility skills are maxed.

4th – Enhanced Legendary Relic: Shackles of Weakness

The Legendary version extends the main stat reduction duration from 7 seconds to 16 seconds. This more than doubles the debuff duration, making it significantly easier to maintain permanent debuffs on multiple enemies simultaneously.

With the extended duration, enemies hit by any of Dante's frequent spear attacks remain weakened for much longer, allowing your entire team to benefit from reduced enemy effectiveness throughout prolonged fights. This is particularly valuable in drawn-out battles where you're facing multiple strong enemies.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – The Legendary upgrade significantly improves the utility of this skill by ensuring near-permanent debuff uptime on enemies. While still not as impactful as the raw damage increases from other skills, the extended duration makes this much more valuable for team-wide damage output and control. Prioritize this after your core damage skills (Instrument of Fate Legendary, Retribution, Foresight) but before the utility-only skills.

Skill Animation Info

  • Main Stat Reduction Formula: (6% Physical Attack + 2400)
  • Duration (Base): 7 seconds
  • Duration (Legendary): 16 seconds
  • Keywords: Passive, Debuff, Main Stat Reduction
  • Area: Single target per spear hit

5th – Piercing Throw (Legendary Only)

This Legendary-exclusive skill unlocks at Legendary status and adds another powerful tool to Dante's arsenal. Every 9 seconds, he throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing massive Physical Damage (225% Physical Attack) and knocking them back.

Notice the scaling: 225% Physical Attack with no base damage component. This means Piercing Throw scales purely with your Physical Attack stat, making it incredibly powerful at high investment levels. At max gear, this single spear can hit for over 250,000 damage every 9 seconds – that's equivalent to a mini-ultimate on a short cooldown.

The knockback also maintains Dante's Armor Penetration buff from Legendary Instrument of Fate, and the spear hit triggers Shackles of Weakness. This skill transforms Dante's sustained damage output from "good" to "exceptional" in extended fights.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is pure, high-scaling burst damage on a short cooldown. Since it scales at 225% Physical Attack, every point of Physical Attack you gain translates to massive damage increases. After unlocking Legendary status, prioritize maxing this skill immediately. The 9-second cooldown means it contributes significantly to your total DPS, and the knockback utility adds constant control pressure.

Skill Animation Info

  • Physical Damage Formula: (225% Physical Attack)
  • Cooldown: 9 seconds
  • Keywords: Legendary Skill, High Damage, Knockback, Pure Scaling
  • Range: Nearest enemy
  • Hits: 1 powerful spear

6th – Shifting Focus (Legendary Only)

This adaptive passive skill unlocks at Legendary status and changes Dante's playstyle based on positioning. When enemies are near Dante, he gains bonus Dodge (25% Physical Attack). When no enemies are nearby, he instead gains bonus Physical Attack (25% Physical Attack).

This skill makes Dante incredibly versatile. In the early fight when enemies are close, the massive dodge bonus (25% of your Physical Attack translates to roughly 30,000+ dodge at high investment) dramatically increases survival and Retribution proc rate. As enemies get knocked back or defeated, Dante switches to offense mode, gaining raw Physical Attack that amplifies all his damage output.

The beauty of this skill is that it's always active and always beneficial. You're never wasting the buff – it adapts to the combat situation automatically. At high investment, the 25% scaling means you're getting 30,000+ in either dodge or Physical Attack constantly throughout the fight.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill provides substantial stat bonuses that directly improve either Dante's survivability (via dodge → more Retribution procs → more damage) or raw damage output (via Physical Attack). The 25% scaling is extremely generous, making this skill cost-efficient to evolve. However, since it's a passive stat bonus rather than a direct damage multiplier, it ranks slightly below the core damage skills like Piercing Throw and Retribution. Prioritize this after maxing your primary damage sources.

Skill Animation Info

  • Dodge Bonus (Enemies Near): (25% Physical Attack)
  • Physical Attack Bonus (Enemies Far): (25% Physical Attack)
  • Keywords: Legendary Skill, Passive, Adaptive, Position-based
  • Range: Automatically toggles based on enemy proximity

Final Evolution Priority Summary

To maximize Dante's effectiveness in combat, follow this skill upgrade priority order:

  1. 1st – Instrument of Fate (Legendary) – Constant Armor Penetration buff dramatically increases damage against all targets
  2. 2nd – Retribution – Passive skill that procs constantly, often 30-40% of total damage
  3. 3rd – Foresight – Team dodge buff that triggers more Retribution procs and improves team survival
  4. 4th – Piercing Throw – High-scaling burst damage on short cooldown (Legendary only)
  5. 5th – Instrument of Fate (Base) – Your primary active damage skill, essential for control and damage
  6. 6th – Shifting Focus – Adaptive stat bonuses provide constant value (Legendary only)
  7. 7th – Shackles of Weakness (Legendary) – Extended debuff duration improves team-wide effectiveness
  8. 8th – Shackles of Weakness (Base) – Utility debuff, lowest direct combat impact

Remember: Dante's power comes from the synergy between his skills. Prioritize the skills that create the damage feedback loop: Dodge → Retribution procs → Knockback → Armor Penetration → Higher damage → Repeat. Focus your evolution resources on skills 1-4 first to unlock Dante's full potential!
