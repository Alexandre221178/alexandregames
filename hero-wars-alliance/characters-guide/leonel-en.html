Leonel is a dual-blade fighter who arrived in Hero Wars Alliance alongside the brand-new Realm game mode — available once you reach level 80. He's not just another warrior; Leonel is a Critical Damage specialist who punishes enemies by draining their Energy with every critical strike. If you've ever wanted a hero that turns crits into a devastating weapon, Leonel is the one you've been waiting for.

In this guide, we'll cover everything you need to know about Leonel: his skill upgrade priorities, best artifacts, glyph order, skin strategy, counters, and the top team compositions. Whether you're a beginner collecting his Soul Stones through Realm missions or an advanced player looking for the ideal build, this guide has you covered.

Who Is Leonel in Hero Wars Alliance?

Leonel is a Front Line Warrior built around Critical Damage and Energy denial. He wields two blades and excels at punishing enemies who rely on shields or abilities by burning through their Energy reserves. As the first Realm-exclusive hero, his Soul Stones can only be obtained by completing Realm missions starting at level 80.

  • Main Class: Warrior
  • Realm Class: Infantry
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Faction: Way of Nature
  • How to get Soul Stones: Only Realm (available at level 80+)
  • Synergy: Yasmine, Oya, Alvanor, DarkStar

Leonel's kit revolves around landing critical hits to activate his Mark of Fate, which burns enemy Energy at an alarming rate. Pair him with allies who boost Critical Hit Chance and you'll see enemy ultimates shut down entirely.

Leonel Pros & Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Exceptional Critical Damage output with up to 300% crit multiplier from Royal Roar.
  • Powerful Energy denial through Mark of Fate — burns 75 Energy per second, crippling enemy ultimates.
  • Massive bonus damage against shielded enemies via Predator Jump (300% Phys. Atk. + 90,000).
  • Ultimate skill (Blades of Shadow) makes him untargetable during the animation, granting survivability.
  • Strong synergy with crit-focused allies like Yasmine and Oya.

❌ Cons

  • Soul Stones only available through Realm mode — very slow to star up for most players.
  • Heavily reliant on landing critical hits — if crits are suppressed, Mark of Fate becomes useless.
  • Front Line position makes him vulnerable to burst damage and crowd control.
  • Mark of Fate effectiveness is reduced against enemies with levels higher than 120.

Leonel Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Leonel's dual-blade kit revolves around critical strikes and energy drain. Understanding each skill's impact will help you decide which ones to level first for maximum battlefield dominance.

1st – Blades of Shadow (Ultimate)

Leonel's signature move — he vanishes into the shadows and unleashes a devastating series of strikes from behind enemy lines. What makes this skill truly special is that Leonel cannot be targeted by any enemy attacks or abilities while it's active. The first strike deals (75% Phys. Atk. + 36,000), and each subsequent hit deals (50% Phys. Atk. + 18,000) — with each hit dealing more damage than the last. This means the longer the skill runs, the more devastating it becomes. This skill cannot be copied by other heroes.

  • Formula - First Hit: Physical Damage: (75% Phys. Atk. + 36,000)
  • Formula - Additional Hits: Physical Damage per hit: (50% Phys. Atk. + 18,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Can't be copied, Untargetable
  • Level: Max level

Evolution Priority: High – This is Leonel's ultimate and his main burst window. The invulnerability during the animation gives him incredible survivability, and the escalating damage per hit makes it a fight-turning ability. Prioritize leveling this skill for maximum burst potential.

2nd – Predator Jump

Leonel leaps toward the nearest enemy and delivers two powerful strikes. Against normal enemies, each hit deals (Phys. Atk. + 30,000). But here's where it gets exciting — against enemies with shields, the damage triples to (300% Phys. Atk. + 90,000) per hit! On top of that, shielded enemies also receive the Mark of Fate (if the passive has been learned), which starts draining their Energy. This makes Leonel a natural counter to any shield-dependent hero.

  • Formula - Normal Hit: Physical Damage per hit: (100% Phys. Atk. + 30,000)
  • Formula - Against Shielded Enemies: Physical Damage per hit: (300% Phys. Atk. + 90,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Hits: 2 strikes
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Anti-Shield, Mark of Fate (if learned)

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Leonel's core DPS ability and his main synergy trigger. The triple damage against shielded enemies is massive, and the automatic Mark of Fate application ties directly into his energy-drain strategy. Level this first for consistent damage output and shield-busting power.

3rd – Royal Roar

Royal Roar is a powerful buff that increases the Critical Hit Chance of Leonel and the ally with the highest Physical Attack. Normally, the boost is (30% Phys. Atk. + 6,000). But if Leonel himself has the highest Physical Attack on the team, the effect concentrates entirely on him — increasing his Crit Hit Chance by an even greater (75% Phys. Atk. + 12,000) and raising his Critical Damage to a devastating 300%. This self-buff transforms Leonel into a crit machine that activates Mark of Fate on nearly every attack.

  • Formula - Shared Buff: Crit Hit Chance increase: (30% Phys. Atk. + 6,000)
  • Formula - Solo Buff (Leonel only): Crit Hit Chance increase: (75% Phys. Atk. + 12,000)
  • Critical Damage (Solo): 300%

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Buff, Crit Hit Chance, Critical Damage

Evolution Priority: Very High – This skill is the engine that drives Leonel's entire kit. Without high Crit Hit Chance, Mark of Fate rarely triggers. The 300% Critical Damage multiplier when buffing himself alone is one of the highest in the game. Level this alongside Predator Jump to unlock Leonel's full crit-based destruction.

Alexandre Tips: If you want Leonel to receive the full solo buff from Royal Roar (75% Phys. Atk. + 12,000 Crit Chance and 300% Crit Damage), make sure he has the highest Physical Attack on your team. Avoid pairing him with other high Physical Attack heroes if you want to maximize his self-buff. However, the shared buff is also excellent for boosting allies like Yasmine.

4th – Mark of Fate

Mark of Fate is Leonel's passive ability and the key to his energy-denial strategy. Every time Leonel lands a critical attack, the enemy receives a mark that burns 75 Energy per second. The higher Leonel's Critical Damage multiplier, the stronger the drain effect. Even better — the mark can stack multiple times, meaning enemies hit repeatedly by crits will lose Energy at an alarming rate, preventing them from ever using their ultimate abilities. This is what makes Leonel such a feared hero in PvP.

  • Energy Burn: 75 Energy per second
  • Trigger: Critical attacks
  • Stacking: Multiple marks can be applied simultaneously
  • Scaling: Effect strength increases with Critical Damage multiplier

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Mark, Energy Drain, Passive
  • Level: Max level
  • Note: The chance to apply the mark is reduced if the enemy's level is higher than 120.

Evolution Priority: High – Mark of Fate is what separates Leonel from other physical damage dealers. The energy burn completely shuts down enemy teams that rely on ultimates to win fights. While it's a passive skill that depends on crits to trigger, once Royal Roar is active, this passive fires constantly. It's incredibly powerful in long fights and PvP engagements.

Leonel Realm Skills - Hero Wars Alliance

Leonel brings exclusive Realm Skills that boost your infantry troops in the new Realm game mode. These skills don't apply to regular PvP or campaign battles — they only work in Realm.

1st – Double Blade

Leonel combines beastly rage and human cunning, inspiring the infantry under his command. This skill increases your infantry's Critical Hit Chance by a percentage that scales with the skill level. In large-scale Realm battles where hundreds of troops clash, even a small crit chance boost can dramatically increase your army's total damage output — especially since Leonel's entire identity revolves around critical strikes.

  • Effect: Increases Infantry Crit Chance by % (scales with skill level)
  • Type: Passive buff for all infantry troops
  • Keywords: Critical Hit Chance, Infantry, Buff

Upgrade Priority: Very High – Upgrade this first. The Crit Chance increase benefits all of your infantry attacks universally, making it the most consistent and impactful Realm skill. More crits mean more damage in every situation, regardless of the enemy type you're facing.

2nd – Predator's Shadow

The infantry that follows Leonel tries to mimic his predatory grace, gaining a tactical advantage in battle. Every second infantry attack deals additional bonus damage to Spearmen and Marksmen. The bonus damage percentage scales with the skill level, making Leonel's infantry squad increasingly effective against these common enemy types in Realm.

  • Effect: Every 2nd attack: +% dmg to Spearmen, +% dmg to Marksmen (scales with level)
  • Type: Situational passive buff for infantry troops
  • Keywords: Bonus Damage, Spearmen, Marksmen, Infantry

Upgrade Priority: High – Upgrade after Double Blade. While the bonus damage against Spearmen and Marksmen is valuable, it's situational — it only works against those specific enemy types. Double Blade offers a universal benefit, so this should be your second Realm priority.

Leonel Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Upgrading Leonel's artifacts in the right order is essential for maximizing his crit-based damage and energy denial potential in battle.

1st – Crest of Goldmane (Weapon Artifact)

Stat Bonus: Armor Penetration +32,040
Effect: When Leonel uses his ultimate skill (Blades of Shadow), it triggers an effect that gives bonus stats to the whole team for 9 seconds. The massive Armor Penetration stat ensures Leonel's physical damage cuts through enemy defenses, making every hit from Blades of Shadow and Predator Jump significantly more effective.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Max this artifact first. Armor Penetration is the stat that directly converts Leonel's high raw damage into actual damage dealt to enemies. Without it, armored tanks will absorb his hits. The team-wide buff on ultimate activation adds even more value.

2nd – Book Artifact

Stat Bonus: Armor Penetration +10,680 | Crit Hit Chance +2,967

Effect: This artifact boosts two of Leonel's most critical stats. Additional Armor Penetration stacks with the weapon artifact for even more damage, while the Crit Hit Chance directly increases how often Leonel triggers Mark of Fate. More crits means more energy drain on enemies.

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade after the weapon. The combination of Armor Penetration and Crit Hit Chance makes this artifact essential for Leonel's damage and energy-denial loop.

3rd – Ring of Agility (Ring Artifact)

Stat Bonus: Agility +3,990
Physical Attack from Agility: 11,970
Armor from Agility: 3,990

Effect: Boosts Leonel's main stat, increasing all skill damage and armor. Since Agility is his main stat, each point grants 3 Physical Attack and 1 Armor. This scales every formula in his kit — Blades of Shadow, Predator Jump, and Royal Roar all benefit from higher Physical Attack.

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade alongside the book artifact. The Physical Attack scaling feeds directly into all four of Leonel's skills, making this a well-rounded investment for both damage and survivability.

Leonel Glyph Evolution Priority

Upgrading Leonel's glyphs in the right order is key to maximizing his critical damage output and energy denial across all game modes.

1st – Physical Attack Glyph

Physical Attack is the backbone of Leonel's entire kit. Every single one of his skills scales with Physical Attack — from Blades of Shadow's burst damage to Royal Roar's crit buff calculation to Predator Jump's devastating shield-busting hits. Increasing this stat boosts everything at once.

Level 80 Stats: +8,340 Physical Attack

Evolution Priority: Very High – Max this glyph first. It's the single most impactful stat for Leonel's damage across all skills.

2nd – Health Glyph

As a Front Line warrior, Leonel absorbs a lot of damage. Health keeps him alive long enough to stack Mark of Fate on multiple enemies and land more critical strikes. However, since his real value comes from dealing damage rather than tanking, this is less critical than offensive stats.

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health

Evolution Priority: Low – Upgrade last. Leonel's survivability comes more from Blades of Shadow invulnerability and team support than raw HP. Focus on offensive glyphs first.

3rd – Crit Hit Chance Glyph

Critical Hit Chance is absolutely vital for Leonel because it directly controls how often Mark of Fate triggers. More crits = more energy drain on enemies = fewer enemy ultimates. It also synergizes with Royal Roar's 300% Critical Damage multiplier, making each crit hit incredibly powerful.

Level 80 Stats: +4,170 Crit Hit Chance

Evolution Priority: Very High – Upgrade immediately after Physical Attack. This stat is the trigger for Leonel's entire energy-denial strategy and his massive crit damage output.

4th – Armor Penetration Glyph

Armor Penetration ensures that Leonel's physical damage actually reaches the enemy instead of being absorbed by their armor. Without sufficient Armor Penetration, even high raw damage numbers get drastically reduced against tanky Front Line heroes.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Armor Penetration

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade after Phys. Attack and Crit. This stat converts your damage potential into real damage dealt, especially important against armored targets in PvP.

5th – Agility Glyph

Agility is Leonel's main stat, and each point grants 2 Physical Attack + 1 Armor + 1 extra Physical Attack (main stat bonus). This glyph gives a well-rounded boost to both offense and defense simultaneously.

Level 80 Stats: +2,110 Agility

  • Physical Attack from Agility: 6,330
  • Armor from Agility: 2,110

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade alongside Armor Penetration. The triple Physical Attack scaling makes this glyph a strong overall investment for both damage and survivability.

How to Counter Leonel in Hero Wars Alliance

Leonel's crit-based energy drain can be devastating, but these heroes have the tools to shut him down effectively.

Iris

Iris is one of the strongest counters to Leonel. Her passive skill Mysterious Pact makes her permanently immune to debuffs, and every time an enemy weakens an ally or applies a mark — exactly what Leonel's Mark of Fate does — Iris retaliates by reducing that enemy's Physical and Magic Attack by (10% Mag. Atk. + 4,800) for 7 seconds. Since Leonel constantly applies marks through critical hits, Iris will continuously lower his Physical Attack, directly weakening every skill in his kit. This creates a devastating feedback loop: the more Leonel crits, the weaker he becomes. With a max-level reduction of 16,333 on both Physical and Magic Attack, Iris effectively cripples Leonel's damage output and makes his Mark of Fate stacking work against him.

Leonel Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Leonel shines brightest when paired with allies who amplify his Critical Hit Chance and reward his aggressive playstyle.

Oya

Oya is Leonel's best partner in crime. She heals allies whenever they land a critical hit or break an enemy's shield — both of which Leonel does constantly. Even better, Oya increases the Crit Hit Chance of allies when they receive healing, creating a self-reinforcing loop: Leonel crits, Oya heals him, his crit chance goes up, he crits more, triggering more Mark of Fate stacks and more healing. This synergy turns Leonel into a self-sustaining damage machine that gets stronger the longer the fight goes on.

Yasmine

Yasmine is a fellow Nature assassin with exceptionally high Critical Hit Damage. She naturally synergizes with Leonel because both heroes thrive on crits. When Royal Roar's shared buff targets Yasmine (if she has the highest Physical Attack), her already lethal crit damage becomes even more devastating. Together, they form a dual-assassin core that overwhelms enemies with relentless critical strikes and energy drain from both sides of the battlefield.

Conclusion – Leonel Guide (Hero Wars Alliance)

Leonel is a unique and powerful addition to Hero Wars Alliance, bringing a fresh playstyle centered on Critical Damage and Energy denial. As the first Realm-exclusive hero, he rewards patient players who invest in collecting his Soul Stones through Realm missions. His kit — combining invulnerable burst damage from Blades of Shadow, shield-crushing power from Predator Jump, self-buffing crit amplification from Royal Roar, and relentless energy drain from Mark of Fate — makes him a terrifying presence in both PvP and long battles.

To get the most out of Leonel: prioritize Predator Jump and Royal Roar for skill upgrades, max your Physical Attack and Crit Hit Chance glyphs first, invest in the Crest of Goldmane weapon artifact, and pair him with Oya and Yasmine for devastating synergy. With the right build and team, Leonel can single-handedly shut down enemy ultimates and carry your team to victory.
