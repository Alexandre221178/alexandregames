Who Is Crow in Hero Wars Alliance?

Crow is a Way of Honor Control hero positioned in the Middle Line. His job is not to be a simple damage dealer; his job is to break the enemy's rhythm with Silence and then make every repeated Silence hurt.

  • Main Class: Control
  • Role: Control
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Faction: Way of Honor
  • Realm Buff: Spearmen attack/defense +120%
  • How to get Soul Stones: Current data does not list a permanent Soul Stone source.
  • Synergy: Polaris, Phobos, Byrna, Aidan, Isaac, Judge, Julius, Iris, Tempus, Electra, Miu, Folio, Cascade, Satori, Kayla
  • Hydra Tier List: Not rated in the current data.

Crow is especially dangerous against Intelligence heroes. Silent Judgment and Holy Grenade both pressure mage-style targets, while Witch Hunt keeps checking the enemy with the highest Intelligence every 12 seconds.

The scary part is not only the first Silence. The scary part is what happens after it. Swift Retribution adds extra hits against Silenced enemies, Final Verdict punishes reapplications, and Confinement can stop enemies from applying Debuffs or Curses while at least one enemy is Silenced.

How Silence Works in Hero Wars Alliance

The Silence effect looks like a mask with a crossed-out mouth. For Crow, this detail matters a lot because his best battles are built around keeping enemies Silenced long enough for his follow-up skills to punish them.

A Silenced hero can only deal physical damage and use skills that scale with Physical Attack. That means they cannot cast skills that do not deal damage, deal magic damage, deal pure damage, or scale with Magic Attack.

For example, Cornelius' ultimate deals physical damage, but it scales with Magic Attack. Because the skill depends on Magic Attack, Cornelius cannot use it while Silenced. This is why Crow is so dangerous against mage teams: he does not only stop magic damage, he can also interrupt physical-looking skills when their formula depends on Magic Attack.

Crow Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

Pros

  • Excellent anti-mage pressure because several skills target or punish Intelligence heroes.
  • Silence chain can become oppressive after Legendary Relic upgrades.
  • All damage formulas scale with Physical Attack, making his offensive build easy to understand.
  • Confinement can block Debuffs and Curses while Crow keeps at least one enemy Silenced.
  • Does not need off-meta Silence helpers to work, because his own kit already creates strong Silence uptime.

Cons

  • Needs Legendary Relic investment to reach his strongest control package.
  • Most of his value depends on keeping Silence active at the right time.
  • Armor protects him, but heavy burst can still punish his Middle Line position.
  • Teams with cleanse, shields, or fast physical pressure can reduce his control window.

Crow Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Crow currently has one listed talisman: Talisman of Retribution. That makes the choice simple, but it is still important to understand why it fits him. Skins keep their bonuses after you level them, but a talisman only gives the bonus of the talisman equipped in battle.

1 - Talisman of Retribution

Main Stat Slot: Agility +2,000. Because Agility is Crow's main stat, this gives Physical Attack +6,000 and Armor +2,000.

Reroll Slots: Armor +6,600 in each slot, for a combined reroll bonus of Armor +19,800.

Crow Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Crow's relic upgrades turn Silence into a battle plan: lock mages, spread control, punish repeats, and block Debuffs while Silence is active.

1st - Silent Judgment (Base Skill)

In-game description: Crow attacks enemies in the front, applying Silence for 8.0 seconds to all Intelligence-main-stat enemies.

Skill Explanation: This is Crow's main control button before relic upgrades. It attacks enemies in the front and punishes Intelligence-main-stat enemies with an 8-second Silence, which is already enough to disrupt many mage-heavy teams.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage: 228,026 (200% Physical Attack + 9,000 = 109,513 x 2 + 9,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Silence Duration: 8 seconds
  • Targeting: Enemies in the front; applies Silence to all enemies whose main stat is Intelligence.
  • Keywords: Control, Silence, Anti-Mage

Evolution Priority: Very High - Level this early because Crow's entire control loop starts here. More reliable Silence means more value from his later punish effects.

1st Legendary - Silent Judgment (Relic Enhancement: Level 10)

In-game description: Silent Judgment now applies Silence to all enemies for 8.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: This relic enhancement changes the scale of Crow's ultimate. Instead of mainly punishing Intelligence-main-stat enemies, Silent Judgment becomes a team-wide Silence tool, giving Final Verdict, Swift Retribution, Witch Hunt, and Confinement far more room to work.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Legendary Enhancement: Silence applies to all enemies for 8 seconds

Skill Animation Info

  • Silence Duration: 8 seconds
  • Targeting: All enemies
  • Keywords: Control, Silence

Evolution Priority: Very High - This is one of Crow's most important relic upgrades because it turns his main control skill into an all-enemy Silence effect.

2nd - Holy Grenade (Base Skill)

In-game description: Throws a grenade at the enemy with the highest Intelligence, applying Silence for 5.0 seconds. The target and nearby enemies take physical area damage.

Skill Explanation: Holy Grenade is Crow's targeted mage punishment. It searches for the enemy with the highest Intelligence, applies Silence, and deals area physical damage to the target and nearby enemies.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Area Physical Damage: 118,513 (100% Physical Attack + 9,000 = 109,513 + 9,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Targeting: Enemy with the highest Intelligence.
  • Silence Duration: 5 seconds
  • Keywords: Control, Silence, Anti-Mage, Area Damage

Evolution Priority: Very High - This skill is essential because it targets the kind of enemy Crow wants to shut down most: high-Intelligence threats.

2nd Legendary - Holy Grenade (Relic Enhancement: Level 20)

In-game description: Grenade shards deal physical damage to all Silenced enemies and enemies around them. Silence duration is increased by 2.0 seconds for all affected targets.

Skill Explanation: This relic upgrade rewards Crow for keeping Silence active. Once enemies are already Silenced, Holy Grenade adds shard damage around them and extends the Silence window, which helps Crow's control chain continue.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Grenade Shards Physical Damage: 91,134.75 (75% Physical Attack + 9,000 = 109,513 x 0.75 + 9,000)
  • Formula 2: Silence Duration Bonus: +2 seconds

Skill Animation Info

  • Targeting: All Silenced enemies and enemies around them.
  • Silence Duration Bonus: +2 seconds for all affected targets
  • Keywords: Control, Silence, Area Damage

Evolution Priority: Very High - The extra shard damage and longer Silence duration are exactly what Crow wants in longer fights.

3rd - Swift Retribution

In-game description: For 12.0 seconds, Crow's skills recharge 30% faster, and his basic attacks deal an additional hit to all enemies affected by Silence.

Skill Explanation: This skill turns Silence into tempo. The faster recharge helps Crow cycle back into control, and the extra hit makes every Silenced target more expensive for the enemy team to ignore.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Additional Physical Hit: 121,513 (100% Physical Attack + 12,000 = 109,513 + 12,000)
  • Recharge Speed Increase: 30%

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 12 seconds
  • Keywords: Buff, Silence Punish, Cooldown Reduction

Evolution Priority: Medium High - It is strong, but it depends on Silence being active. Level it after the main Silence tools so this extra damage has targets to hit.

4th - Final Verdict

In-game description: When Crow reapplies Silence to an enemy that already has the same active effect, the enemy takes physical damage. The effect's duration is increased by the duration of the longest Silence already affecting the enemy.

Skill Explanation: Final Verdict is the reason repeated Silence becomes so scary. Crow does not only refresh control; he also turns the overlap into damage and longer Silence duration.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage: 185,269.5 (150% Physical Attack + 21,000 = 109,513 x 1.5 + 21,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Trigger: Crow applies Silence to an enemy who already has Silence active.
  • Keywords: Silence Punish, Duration Extension

Evolution Priority: High - This passive gives Crow his best long-fight punishment. It is strongest after Silent Judgment, Holy Grenade, and Witch Hunt are already feeding repeated Silence windows.

5th - Witch Hunt (New Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 1)

In-game description: Every 12.0 seconds, Crow attacks the enemy with the highest Intelligence. If the target is Silenced, Crow spreads the effect to all nearby enemies for the remaining duration. Otherwise, he applies Silence for 5.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: Witch Hunt is a beautiful fit for Crow because it keeps hunting the enemy who usually matters most against him: the highest Intelligence target. If Silence is already there, it spreads. If it is not, Crow applies it and starts the loop again.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Retribution

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage: 164,269.5 (150% Physical Attack = 109,513 x 1.5)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 12 seconds
  • Targeting: Enemy with the highest Intelligence
  • Silence Duration: 5 seconds if the target is not already Silenced
  • Keywords: Control, Silence, Anti-Mage

Evolution Priority: Very High - This new skill gives Crow constant anti-mage pressure. Unlocking it early makes his Silence cycle more reliable even before the battle becomes messy.

6th - Confinement (New Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 30)

In-game description: While at least one enemy is Silenced, enemies cannot apply Debuffs or Curses. The skill remains active while Crow is alive.

Skill Explanation: Confinement changes Crow from a Silence specialist into a team protector against Debuff and Curse pressure. The condition is simple: keep at least one enemy Silenced and keep Crow alive.

Skill Animation Info

  • Active Condition: At least one enemy is Silenced.
  • Prevented Effects: Debuffs, Curses
  • Active While: Crow is alive
  • Keywords: Silence, Debuff Block, Curse Block

Evolution Priority: Very High - This is late, but extremely valuable. In Debuff or Curse-heavy matchups, Confinement can make Crow feel like a control hero and a defensive answer at the same time.

How to Counter Crow Hero Wars Alliance

Celeste

Celeste can help teams recover from control pressure and punish slower fights. Against Crow, the goal is to reduce the value of his Silence windows before Final Verdict and Confinement take over.

Nebula

Nebula can clean all kind of negative effects removing silence from two nearby allies.

Julius

Julius shields help absorb Crow's physical damage while his team tries to survive the first Silence wave. Shielded teams can sometimes buy enough time to answer Crow's control.

Yasmine

Yasmine can pressure vulnerable targets quickly. Against Crow, her value is speed: force damage before Crow stacks long Silence windows and blocks Debuffs or Curses with Confinement.

Crow Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Cascade

Cascade can add Silence against Intelligence-based enemies and reduce defenses in certain matchups. That makes it easier for Crow to trigger Swift Retribution, Final Verdict, Witch Hunt, and Confinement.

Fafnir

Fafnir prioritizes Agility-main-stat allies with Runic Shield and can boost physical attack damage. Crow appreciates both the protection and the extra pressure on his physical formulas.

Keira

Keira can add more Silence uptime, which gives Crow more targets for Final Verdict and Swift Retribution. This is optional rather than required, because Crow already has strong Silence pressure himself.

Phobos

Phobos fits Crow's anti-mage identity well. Together, they make high-Intelligence enemies feel trapped between Silence, control, and focused pressure.

Polaris

Polaris adds more control value to teams that already want to slow the fight down. Crow supplies Silence, while Polaris helps the team keep enemies from playing cleanly.

Thea

Thea can apply Silence with Vow of Silence, giving Crow another way to keep his Silence punish effects active. This is more of an optional synergy than a core meta requirement.

Crow Guide Conclusion

Crow is strongest when you stop thinking of him as only an anti-mage hero and start seeing him as a Silence engine. Silent Judgment starts the control, Holy Grenade targets the highest Intelligence enemy, Witch Hunt keeps checking the same weakness, and Final Verdict punishes every repeated Silence.

For investment, I would prioritize the Talisman of Retribution, Justice Skin, Physical Attack and Armor Penetration glyphs, Holy Grenade artifact, Laws Codex, and the Legendary Relic upgrades for Silent Judgment, Holy Grenade, Witch Hunt, and Confinement. If Crow stays alive, the enemy team can spend a shocking amount of the fight unable to use the tools they brought.
