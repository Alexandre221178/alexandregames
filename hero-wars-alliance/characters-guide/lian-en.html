Welcome, Champion! Ever dreamed of a hero who can charm the entire enemy team into a deep slumber, neutralizing even the most powerful opponents before they can act? Meet Lian, the enchanting queen of crowd control in Hero Wars Alliance. This mesmerizing sorceress wields hypnotic magic that puts enemies to sleep, deals devastating Pure damage, and punishes anyone who dares to attack her with instant charm effects.

But Lian's true power goes far beyond simple sleep spells. With Legendary Relics, she gains the terrifying Enthrall skill that repeatedly charms central enemies, and Heartbreak that deals bonus Pure damage to every charmed target when they're attacked. If you want a hero who shuts down entire teams and turns control into unstoppable offensive pressure, dive into this complete guide!

Who Is Lian in Hero Wars Alliance?

Lian is a powerful Control / Mage hero from the Way of Mystery faction, standing in the Back Line as the ultimate crowd control specialist. She enchants enemies with hypnotic charm, putting them to sleep and dealing Pure damage to those who resist. Her Intelligence-based kit makes her the perfect cornerstone for any CC-heavy team composition.

  • Main Class: Control
  • Main Role: Crowd Control / Sleep / Pure Damage
  • Position: Back Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Mystery
  • How to get Soul Stones: Special Events, Heroic Chest, Grand Arena Shop
  • Synergy: Satori, Amira, Folio, Miu
  • Hydra Tier List: B

Lian's primary function is to lock down the entire enemy team with sleep through her Enchantment ultimate, while Conciliation punishes any enemy that tries to damage her. Her unique mechanic of charming enemies into sleep — where Lian's own attacks don't wake them — creates devastating windows for her team to deal uncontested damage.

As an Intelligence hero, every point of Intelligence grants her three points of Magic Attack, one point of Magic Defense, and one extra point of Physical Attack. Investing in her main stat boosts virtually every skill in her arsenal, making her scale exceptionally well into the late game.

Class Skill (Control): Upon completion of a control effect, Lian slows the same target by 30% and reduces its total physical damage for 4.0 seconds. The damage reduction equals 1% of her higher attack stat + 9,000 (up to 10,383 with Talisman of Charm). The effect cannot trigger on a single target more often than once every 10 seconds.

Lian Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Team-Wide Sleep: Enchantment charms ALL enemies, putting them to sleep for 7.0 seconds — one of the strongest crowd control ultimates in the game.
  • Pure Damage Output: Hypnotic Ball deals Pure damage that bypasses both Armor and Magic Defense, making Lian effective against any defensive setup.
  • Self-Defense via Charm: Conciliation automatically charms enemies that attempt to damage Lian, giving her incredible survivability for a Back Line hero.
  • Legendary Power Spike: Enthrall adds repeating AoE charm every 14 seconds, and Heartbreak deals bonus Pure damage to all charmed enemies — creating a devastating CC-damage loop.
  • Enhanced Sleep Persistence: The Legendary Enchantment gives charmed enemies a 50% chance to stay asleep even when attacked, allowing your team to damage sleeping enemies without waking them.

❌ Cons

  • Vulnerable to CC Cleanse: Sebastian cleanses all control effects and grants CC immunity, directly shutting down Lian's entire kit in one skill cast.
  • High Intelligence Target: Cornelius specifically targets the enemy with the highest Intelligence — Lian's 17,537 Intelligence makes her a prime target for devastating monolith strikes.
  • Sleep Break Risk: Without the Legendary enhancement, sleeping enemies wake up when damaged, which can reduce the effective duration of her crowd control significantly.
  • Level-Dependent Control: Enchantment and Conciliation have reduced effectiveness against enemies above level 120, weakening her control in late-game PvP against over-leveled opponents.

Lian Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Choosing the right talisman can be the difference between victory and defeat. Unlike skins, which always contribute their stat bonus regardless of which one you equip, talismans only give their bonus when actively equipped. Lian has two talismans — Talisman of Charm and Talisman of Cuteness — and understanding when to use each is key to maximizing her impact in every battle scenario.

Talisman of Charm

The Talisman of Charm is Lian's best overall talisman for most battle situations. Its main stat grants +2,000 Intelligence, which translates into a massive multi-stat boost because Intelligence is Lian's primary stat. Each Intelligence point grants three points of Magic Attack, one point of Magic Defense, and one extra point of Physical Attack. That means this talisman alone provides +6,000 Magic Attack, +2,000 Magic Defense, and +2,000 Physical Attack just from the main stat.

The three reroll slots all roll Magic Attack, reaching up to +6,600 per slot. Treat these three slots as one combined reroll: the only thing that matters is the total Magic Attack bonus of +19,800. For a Control hero like Lian whose damage skills all scale with Magic Attack, this is an enormous offensive boost that amplifies her Pure damage, magic damage, and even her Class Skill's damage reduction.

Why is this the best pick? Lian's Hypnotic Ball formula (50% Mag.atk. + 6,000), Wandering Lights (30% Mag.atk. + 3,600), Enthrall (40% Mag.atk.), and Heartbreak (15% Mag.atk.) all scale with Magic Attack. The combined +25,800 MA boost from this talisman (6,000 from INT + 19,800 from rerolls) directly increases every damage skill's effectiveness.

Talisman of Cuteness

The Talisman of Cuteness takes a defensive approach. Its main stat grants +12,000 Armor, which significantly improves Lian's physical damage resistance. While this stat does not directly improve her damage or control formulas, it can help her survive longer against physical damage teams.

The three reroll slots offer Health, up to +60,500 per slot, totaling +181,500 Health. This gives Lian a much larger health pool (1,001,636 total vs. 820,136 with Charm), dramatically improving her survivability.

Use the Talisman of Cuteness in niche scenarios where you face heavy physical burst teams that threaten to kill Lian before she can cast her Enchantment ultimate. In most standard battles, however, the Talisman of Charm is the superior choice due to the massive Intelligence and Magic Attack bonuses that power her entire skill set.

Lian Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Relics empower heroes by granting them new abilities while boosting existing ones. Beyond skill improvements, Relics also enhance two secondary stats of each hero. The stats boosted depend on how that particular hero's skills formula scale. Here is how to prioritize Lian's skills to turn the Enchantress Queen into an unstoppable control force!

1st – Enchantment

This is Lian's Ultimate Ability and the cornerstone of her entire kit. She charms all enemies, putting them to sleep for 7.0 seconds. Sleeping enemies wake up when damaged — but here's the crucial detail: Lian's own attacks do not wake enemies. This means she can continue dealing damage while the entire enemy team sleeps, creating an enormous window for your team to set up their skills. The chance to charm is lowered if the target's level is above 120.

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 7.0 seconds
  • Animation delay: 1.3 seconds
  • Keywords: Stun, Control, Sleep
  • Note: Chance to charm is lowered if the target's level is above 120

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Lian's signature ability and the most powerful team-wide sleep in the game. Maximizing it through relics ensures consistent charm application even against higher-level enemies. Always prioritize this skill first.


1st – Enchantment (Enhanced) Legendary Relic

The Legendary enhancement transforms Enchantment from powerful to devastating. Charmed enemies now have a 50% chance not to wake up when attacked! This means your entire team can start dealing damage to sleeping enemies without reliably waking them. Each time this effect triggers, the chance decreases by 10% — so the first hit has 50%, the second 40%, and so on. This creates a window of 3-5 uncontested attacks on sleeping targets, which is game-changing in both PvP and PvE.

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial chance not to wake: 50%
  • Chance reduction per trigger: 10%
  • Keywords: Enhanced Sleep, Control

Evolution Priority: Very High – This enhancement is what makes Lian truly elite. The ability to damage sleeping enemies without waking them turns her from a setup hero into a complete win condition. Combined with Heartbreak's passive Pure damage, charmed enemies take devastating punishment while asleep.

2nd – Hypnotic Ball

Lian launches a mesmerizing projectile that explodes and deals Pure damage 5 times to each of the front-line enemy heroes. Pure damage bypasses both Armor and Magic Defense, making this skill devastating against even the tankiest opponents. The damage per hit is calculated as 75,139 (50% Mag.atk. + 6,000), with a total of 375,693 Pure damage across all 5 hits.

Formula with Talisman of Charm

  • Damage per hit: Pure Damage: 75,139 (50% × 138,277 + 6,000)
  • Total damage (5 hits): Pure Damage: 375,693

Formula with Talisman of Cuteness

  • Damage per hit: Pure Damage: 62,239 (50% × 112,477 + 6,000)
  • Total damage (5 hits): Pure Damage: 311,193

Evolution Priority: High – This is Lian's primary damage skill. The Pure damage type makes it universally effective, and the 5-hit mechanic means front-line enemies take massive punishment. Upgrade after Enchantment and Conciliation.

3rd – Wandering Lights

Lian creates 5 magical spheres and launches them at the nearest enemy, dealing magic damage. This skill provides consistent magic pressure on a single target, with a cooldown of 18.0 seconds. The damage per sphere is 45,083 (30% Mag.atk. + 3,600). While not as impactful as Hypnotic Ball's Pure damage, the concentrated single-target focus can help eliminate priority threats.

Formula with Talisman of Charm

  • Damage per sphere: Magic Damage: 45,083 (30% × 138,277 + 3,600)

Formula with Talisman of Cuteness

  • Damage per sphere: Magic Damage: 37,343 (30% × 112,477 + 3,600)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 18.0 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.73 seconds
  • Projectiles: 5 spheres
  • Keywords: Magic Damage

Evolution Priority: High – Solid supplemental damage that helps Lian contribute to single-target elimination. The 5 spheres concentrated on one enemy can finish off weakened targets. Upgrade alongside Hypnotic Ball.


3rd – Wandering Lights (Enhanced) Legendary Relic

Available at Legendary Relic level 20. The Legendary enhancement converts Wandering Lights into Pure Damage — bypassing both Armor and Magic Defense — and increases the damage formula to 40% Mag.atk. + 600 per sphere. Each sphere deals 55,911 Pure Damage with the Talisman of Charm, for a total of 279,555 Pure Damage across all 5 spheres. Pure Damage ignores all damage reduction, making every hit hurt regardless of the enemy's Armor or Magic Defense.

Formula with Talisman of Charm

  • Damage per sphere: Pure Damage: 55,911 (40% × 138,277 + 600)
  • Total damage (5 spheres): Pure Damage: 279,555

Formula with Talisman of Cuteness

Evolution Priority: Medium High – This enhancement improves a useful damage skill, but Lian's biggest battle impact still comes from Enchantment, Conciliation, and her legendary control loop with Enthrall and Heartbreak. Upgrade it after the more decisive control relics are secured.

4th – Conciliation

Lian's ultimate self-defense tool. Enemies that attempt to deal damage to Lian are automatically charmed for 4.0 seconds. This passive ability is incredibly powerful for a Back Line hero — any enemy that targets Lian gets put to sleep immediately, effectively protecting her from assassins, AoE damage, and focused fire. The charm chance is lowered if the attacker's level is above 120.

5th – Enthrall (New Skill) Legendary Relic

A powerful new skill granted by the Legendary Relic! Every 14.0 seconds, Lian charms the central enemy and nearby heroes. Here's the key mechanic: enemies that are already charmed take Pure damage equal to 55,311 (40% Mag.atk.), while enemies that are not charmed fall asleep for 4 seconds. This creates a devastating cycle — Lian puts enemies to sleep with Enchantment, then Enthrall punishes the sleeping ones with Pure damage while charming any that woke up.

Formula with Talisman of Charm

  • Damage to charmed enemies: Pure Damage: 55,311 (40% × 138,277)
  • Sleep duration (non-charmed): Control: 4.0 seconds

Formula with Talisman of Cuteness

  • Damage to charmed enemies: Pure Damage: 44,991 (40% × 112,477)
  • Sleep duration (non-charmed): Control: 4.0 seconds

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 14.0 seconds
  • Duration: 4.0 seconds (sleep)
  • Keywords: Sleep, Control, Pure Damage

Evolution Priority: High – Enthrall creates a perpetual CC-damage loop that keeps enemies locked down between Enchantment casts. The 14-second cooldown ensures near-constant charm uptime on central enemies. A must-have for maximizing Lian's legendary potential.

6th – Heartbreak (New Skill) Legendary Relic

The ultimate damage amplifier for Lian's control kit! When charmed enemies are attacked, Lian deals additional Pure damage equal to 20,742 (15% Mag.atk.). This passive triggers on every single attack against a charmed enemy — so if your entire team is hitting sleeping targets (especially with the Legendary Enchantment keeping them asleep), Heartbreak procs repeatedly for massive cumulative damage. It transforms Lian from a pure controller into a control-damage hybrid.

Formula with Talisman of Charm

  • Bonus damage per attack: Pure Damage: 20,742 (15% × 138,277)

Formula with Talisman of Cuteness

  • Bonus damage per attack: Pure Damage: 16,872 (15% × 112,477)

Evolution Priority: High – While the per-hit damage seems modest at 20,742, the cumulative effect is enormous when your entire team attacks charmed enemies. With 5 heroes attacking, that's 100,000+ bonus Pure damage per round. Upgrade after Enthrall to complete the CC-damage loop.

Best Skin for Lian Hero Wars Alliance

Lian's value comes from keeping enemies asleep — the longer she survives, the more time her team has to deal uncontested damage. Prioritize the skins that boost her survivability and control uptime first, then her damage scaling stats.

Default Skin

The Default Skin boosts Lian's primary attribute: Intelligence. Since Intelligence is her main stat, every point grants three points of Magic Attack, one point of Magic Defense, and one extra point of Physical Attack — amplifying virtually every skill she has while also improving her Magic Defense for better survivability.

  • Intelligence: +1,365
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +4,095
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +1,365
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +1,365

Evolution Priority: Very High – Intelligence is the most well-rounded stat for Lian, boosting both her offensive formulas and her Magic Defense simultaneously. A stronger Lian means more reliable Enchantment casts and longer control uptime. Always prioritize this skin first.

Romantic Skin

The Romantic Skin provides a direct Magic Attack bonus, which scales all of Lian's damage skill formulas — Hypnotic Ball, Wandering Lights, Enthrall, and Heartbreak. While Lian's primary role is crowd control support, higher Magic Attack also increases the punishment enemies receive through Heartbreak when charmed.

  • Magic Attack: +10,650

Evolution Priority: Very High – Even though Lian shines most as a support keeping enemies asleep, +10,650 Magic Attack amplifies the damage her team inflicts via Heartbreak procs on charmed targets. This skin also boosts her Class Skill damage reduction cap, indirectly improving survivability.

Beach Skin

The Beach Skin grants a massive Health boost. For a Back Line support hero whose entire value depends on staying alive to keep enemies enchanted, Health is the single most important defensive stat.

  • Health: +106,645

Evolution Priority: Very High – A dead Lian means zero crowd control, zero Heartbreak procs, and your team loses its strongest support tool. This massive Health bonus ensures she survives long enough to cast multiple Enchantments, keeping the entire enemy team locked down throughout the fight. Upgrade alongside Default and Romantic skins.

Demonic Skin

The Demonic Skin provides Magic Defense, reducing damage taken from enemy mages. Since Lian's high Intelligence makes her a prime target for Cornelius (who targets highest INT), Magic Defense is essential for her survival against the most common threat she faces.

  • Magic Defense: +10,650

Evolution Priority: High – Lian is frequently targeted by Cornelius's monolith and other magic-based threats. Extra Magic Defense directly increases her chances of surviving long enough to cast Enchantment. Upgrade right after the three core skins above.

Solar Skin

The Solar Skin provides Armor, boosting Lian's physical damage resistance. As a Back Line hero she is somewhat protected from direct physical attacks, but AoE physical skills and assassins can still reach her.

  • Armor: +10,650

Evolution Priority: Medium High – While less critical than Magic Defense for a Back Line mage, Armor still contributes to Lian's overall survivability against physical burst and AoE. Upgrade after Demonic Skin.

Lunar Skin

The Lunar Skin provides Crushing, which increases all outgoing damage (Physical, Magical, and Pure) dealt to enemies below 50% Health. The bonus does not apply to health loss from allied skills. While Crushing works with all of Lian's damage types, her main role is crowd control support rather than finishing off low-health targets.

  • Crushing: +5,920
  • Activation formula: (Crushing / (Crushing + target's main stat)) × 100%

Evolution Priority: Medium – Crushing can boost Lian's Pure and Magic damage against weakened enemies, but her value comes from keeping enemies asleep, not from finishing them off. Your damage dealers benefit more from Crushing than Lian does. Upgrade after all defensive skins.

Astral Skin / Astral Skin+

The Astral Skin provides Magic Reflection — a stat that gives Lian a chance to completely dodge an incoming magical attack and reflect 20% of the damage back to the attacker. When Magic Reflection triggers, Lian takes no damage from that hit. Acquiring the Skin+ upgrade doubles the stat value from +2,960 to +5,920, using the same skin stone investment.

  • Magic Reflection: +2,960 (base) / +5,920 (Skin+)
  • Only works against magical damage; physical and Pure damage cannot be reflected.
  • Can activate while Lian is Stunned, but does not activate while she is Silenced.
  • Activation chance: (Magic Reflection / (Magic Reflection + enemy's main stat)) × 100%
  • Enemy's main stat means Strength, Agility, or Intelligence, depending on the attacker.

The base Astral Skin can be obtained for free in the future, but the Skin+ version is time-gated: it is only available during the Skin+ Event, and after the event it should only return through the Heroic Chest — which can take months for low spenders or F2P players. Even though the price is not the most affordable, it is still worth buying during the event if Lian is one of your main heroes, because you must first own the base Astral Skin to unlock the Skin+ purchase with Skin+ Event Coins.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Magic Reflection is a niche defensive stat that does not scale Lian's control or damage formulas. However, it adds a layer of magic damage avoidance that complements her survivability, especially valuable against mage-heavy teams. The Skin+ upgrade to +5,920 makes the stat more meaningful, but invest in core skins (Default, Romantic, Beach, Demonic) first.

Lian Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Artifacts are core to Lian's power. Since Lian's main role is keeping the entire enemy team enchanted, prioritize the artifacts that help her survive and maintain crowd control uptime, alongside the ones that amplify her team's damage during the sleep window.

1st - Weapon Artifact: Idol of Charm

When Lian uses her Enchantment (Ultimate) skill in combat, this artifact triggers an effect that gives a Magic Attack bonus to the whole team for 9 seconds. With a 100% activation chance, this is a guaranteed team-wide offensive boost every time Lian puts the entire enemy team to sleep — the perfect setup for your damage dealers to capitalize on the CC window.

  • Activation Chance: 100%
  • Magic Attack bonus (team): +32,040
  • Duration: 9 seconds

Evolution Priority: Very High – This artifact defines Lian's support identity. A guaranteed +32,040 Magic Attack for the entire team during the sleep window lets your damage dealers punish helpless enemies. The longer Lian survives and casts Enchantment, the more value this artifact provides. Always upgrade first.

2nd - Book Artifact: Tome of Arcane Knowledge

The Book artifact provides a mix of Magic Attack and Health. For a support hero like Lian, the +53,394 Health is critical — it directly extends her survival time, which means more Enchantment casts and more Heartbreak procs over the course of the fight. The Magic Attack bonus also scales her Class Skill damage reduction cap.

  • Magic Attack: +10,680
  • Health: +53,394

Evolution Priority: Very High – The Health component alone makes this a top priority for a hero whose entire value depends on staying alive. The +10,680 MA is a welcome bonus that improves Heartbreak damage and her Class Skill cap. Upgrade alongside the Weapon artifact.

3rd - Ring Artifact: Ring of Intelligence

The Ring directly boosts Lian's primary stat, Intelligence. Since each Intelligence point grants Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and Physical Attack, it is the most well-rounded artifact for strengthening both her offense and her defensive resilience — especially the +3,990 Magic Defense that helps her survive Cornelius and other magic threats.

  • Intelligence: +3,990
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +11,970
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +3,990
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +3,990

Evolution Priority: High – The Ring provides a balanced boost of offense and defense through Intelligence. The +3,990 Magic Defense from Intelligence helps Lian survive against Cornelius, while the Magic Attack scales her Heartbreak passive. Upgrade after Weapon and Book artifacts.

Lian Glyph Evolution Priority

Glyphs provide raw stat power that keeps Lian alive and effective. Since her primary role is crowd control support — keeping enemies enchanted and asleep — survivability glyphs are the top priority, followed by the stats that scale her damage formulas.

1st Glyph - Health

Health is the single most important glyph for Lian. Her entire value depends on surviving long enough to cast Enchantment and keep the enemy team locked down. If she falls before casting her ultimate, the team loses its most powerful crowd control, Heartbreak damage amplification, and the Idol of Charm team buff. A massive Health pool ensures she stays alive to deliver multiple sleep cycles throughout the fight.

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) – A dead Lian provides zero crowd control. No other glyph comes close to the survival impact of +122,800 Health for a hero whose entire kit revolves around staying alive and casting Enchantment repeatedly.

2nd Glyph - Magic Attack

Magic Attack scales every damage skill in Lian's kit — Hypnotic Ball (50% MA), Wandering Lights (30% MA), Enthrall (40% MA), and Heartbreak (15% MA). It also increases her Class Skill's damage reduction cap, which indirectly helps survivability. While Lian is a support-focused hero, higher Magic Attack makes the punishment enemies receive through Heartbreak significantly stronger.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High (2nd Overall) – The primary scaling stat for all damage formulas and the Class Skill cap. Upgrade after Health to ensure Lian both survives and deals meaningful damage while enemies sleep.

3rd Glyph - Magic Defense

Magic Defense reduces the damage Lian takes from enemy mages and magic-based skills. Since Lian's high Intelligence makes her a prime target for Cornelius (who targets highest INT), Magic Defense is crucial for surviving his devastating monolith strikes and other magic threats.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Defense.

Evolution Priority: High (3rd Overall) – Essential protection against the anti-INT heroes that specifically target Lian. Upgrade this after Health and Magic Attack for balanced survivability.

4th Glyph - Armor

Armor reduces physical damage taken by Lian. While she is positioned in the Back Line and somewhat protected from direct physical attacks, AoE physical skills and assassin heroes can still reach her. Every defensive layer counts for a hero whose survival equals team-wide crowd control.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Armor.

Evolution Priority: Medium High (4th Overall) – A solid defensive glyph that complements Health and Magic Defense. Upgrade this after Magic Defense to round out Lian's physical damage resistance.

5th Glyph - Intelligence

Intelligence is Lian's main stat. Leveling this glyph provides a multi-faceted boost — every point grants Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and Physical Attack. It offers a balanced mix of offense and defense, though each individual stat gain is lower than the dedicated glyphs above.

  • Intelligence: +2,110
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +6,330
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +2,110
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +2,110

Evolution Priority: Medium (5th Overall) – Intelligence provides a well-rounded stat boost, but the dedicated Health, Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and Armor glyphs give higher individual values. The +2,110 extra Magic Defense from Intelligence is a nice survivability bonus. Level it last among glyphs.

How to Counter Lian in Hero Wars Alliance

Lian's crowd control dominance can be neutralized by heroes that cleanse effects or target her high Intelligence. Here are the heroes that can shut down her enchanting power.

Nebula

Nebula is the good counter to Lian. Her Equilibrium skill cleanses all control effects from two allies.

Cornelius

Cornelius directly targets the enemy with the highest Intelligence — and Lian, with her 17,537 Intelligence (Talisman of Charm), is almost always that target. Cornelius smashes a monolith down dealing damage proportional to the target's Intelligence. Since Lian stacks massive Intelligence through skins, glyphs, and artifacts, Cornelius's damage against her is devastating. One well-timed monolith hit can eliminate Lian before she casts Enchantment.

Lian Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Lian's charm-based crowd control creates devastating synergies with heroes that capitalize on controlled enemies or benefit from extended fight setups. These are the heroes that work best with her enchanting kit.

Satori

Satori places Fox Fire marks on enemies that detonate when the target gains or spends energy. Lian's Enchantment puts the entire team to sleep, and when enemies wake up and immediately use skills (spending energy), all of Satori's marks explode simultaneously for devastating damage. The CC-into-burst timing is exceptional — Lian controls the pace while Satori delivers the lethal follow-up.

Amira

Combined with Heartbreak's bonus Pure damage on charmed enemies, the Lian + Amira combination creates relentless multi-layered damage against helpless targets.

Folio

Folio benefits from enemy crowd control, gaining advantages when enemies are affected by CC effects. Lian's team-wide sleep from Enchantment triggers Folio's synergy mechanics consistently, allowing him to maximize his damage or utility output while enemies are helplessly charmed. The two create a feedback loop where Lian's control enables Folio's power spikes.

Miu

Miu creates dream orbs that synergize with sleep-based control heroes. Lian's charm effects perfectly set up Miu's dream mechanics — sleeping enemies are more vulnerable to Miu's orb detonations. Together, they create a dream team that combines crowd control with burst damage, leaving enemies trapped in an inescapable cycle of sleep and destruction.

Conclusion

Lian stands as one of the most powerful and dominant Control heroes in Hero Wars Alliance. Her unique combination of team-wide sleep, reactive charm defense, Pure damage that bypasses all defenses, and legendary skills that create an unbreakable CC-damage loop makes her the ultimate crowd control specialist.

Her Legendary Relics transform her from a strong controller into an essential strategic weapon. The enhanced Enchantment allowing enemies to stay asleep when attacked, Enthrall's repeating AoE charm every 14 seconds, and Heartbreak's passive Pure damage on every hit against charmed enemies create layers of control and damage that compound throughout the entire battle.

Focus on the Talisman of Charm for most battles, prioritize Enchantment and Conciliation for skill upgrades, and invest in Intelligence and Magic Attack through skins, artifacts, and glyphs. With the right build, Lian will enchant your enemies into submission and turn every battle into a one-sided slumber party.
