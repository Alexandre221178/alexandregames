The mysterious warlock Iris has returned stronger than ever! With her latest revamp in Hero Wars Alliance, she brings devastating new abilities that reshape the battlefield. Her enhanced Relic amplifies her curse-based damage, making her one of the most dangerous mages in Eternity’s ranks.

Once feared for her curse magic, Iris now channels dark energy more effectively than ever weakening enemies and empowering her allies. Whether in PvP or Hydra fights, her explosive Intelligence-based damage and synergy with heroes like Dante, Octavia, and Tempus make her a must-have in any top-tier team.

Who Is Iris in Hero Wars Alliance?

Iris is a daring warlock who uses her arcane power to drain life from enemies and enhance the curses cast by her allies. As part of the Eternity faction, she excels in teams that rely on magical synergy and burst damage.

  • Main Role: Explosive Damage Mage
  • Glyph Class: Mage
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Faction: Eternity
  • How to get Soul Stones: Grand Arena Shop, Heroic Chest, Events
  • Synergy: Dante, Octavia, Tempus, Faceless, Phobos
  • Hydra Tier List: A

Iris’s revamp focuses on refining her curse mechanics and boosting her raw magical potential. Her Legendary Relic Skills significantly increases the impact of her debuffs, turning her into a nightmare for enemy tanks and healers alike.

With her new abilities, Iris not only strengthens the Eternity faction but also reshapes team compositions centered on magical burst damage. If you enjoy tactical gameplay and controlling the pace of battle, Iris’s new version is a thrilling hero to master.

Iris Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Mobile

✅ Pros

  • Extremely high magic burst damage, capable of deleting tanks and frontliners with her soul explosion mechanic.
  • Excellent synergy with magic-based teams, especially those that lower enemy magic defense.
  • Versatile scaling — benefits greatly from Intelligence and Magic Penetration, making her easy to build offensively.
  • The new Legendary Relic abilities enhance her survivability and consistency, allowing her to stay effective even in longer battles.
  • Effective counter to heroes with high armor and shield.
  • Both Talismans provide flexible options: more defense for durability or more penetration for aggression.

❌ Cons

  • Very fragile — easily eliminated by burst damage or high physical DPS heroes.
  • Heavily reliant on energy generation and team protection to unleash her ultimate effectively.
  • Requires careful Talisman selection: choosing the wrong one can significantly reduce her battle performance.
  • Struggles against magic-immune or high magic defense teams if not properly supported.
  • Positioning in the middle line makes her vulnerable to area-of-effect skills and assassins like Yasmin.

1st – Soul Burning

Explanation: This is Iris’s ultimate and the most important ability to level up. When activated, she unleashes devastating magic energy that damages all enemies based on her Magic Attack and the number of Pure Soul charges she has collected. After the explosion, it executes enemies whose health is too low and even prevents resurrection. Formula: 90906 ( 40% Magic Attack + 12000 ) base damage, plus 10463 (5% Magic Attack + 600 ) per Pure Soul charge. Execution threshold: 90906 (40% Magic Attack + 12000 ). The ability becomes extremely powerful in long fights, where Iris can stack many charges before detonating them all at once.

Skill Info

Explanation: Iris summons her familiar, Bitey, who weakens enemies by lowering their Magic Defense. Formula: ( 20% Magic Attack + 25 × 3000). Discover which Iris skins give the greatest impact in battle! Learn how each bonus affects her magic damage, survivability, and Relic synergy.

Default Skin (Intelligence +1,365)

The Default Skin boosts Intelligence, which increases all of Iris’s core stats:

  • Magic Attack: +4,095
  • Magic Defense: +1,365
  • Physical Attack: +1,365

Since all her skills scale with Magic Attack, this skin provides a strong general power boost to her overall kit, including her Soul Burning and Inner Fire Relic effects.

Evolution Priority: High – Solid early investment for increasing Iris’s total Magic Attack and scaling efficiency.

Cybernetic Skin (Armor +10,650)

This skin grants a large boost to Armor, giving Iris better resistance to physical damage. While useful for keeping her alive longer, it does not directly enhance her offensive abilities or Relic-based damage scaling.

Evolution Priority: Low – Defensive value only; doesn’t improve her main Magic or Debuff abilities.

Beach Skin (Magic Attack +10,650)

Increases Magic Attack directly, improving the base power of all her abilities — especially Soul Burning and Inner Fire, which rely heavily on Magic scaling. It’s one of the best early offensive skins for Iris, offering a direct power spike for both PvP and PvE.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Essential for maximizing all of Iris’s Magic-based skills and Relic damage.

Stellar Skin+ (Magic Attack +21,300)

The upgraded version of the Stellar Skin doubles the Magic Attack bonus, making it Iris’s most powerful offensive skin. It drastically improves her burst potential in Soul Burning and enhances every Magic-scaling Legendary Relic effect she has.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Her best damage-focused skin and top priority for any offensive build.

Masquerade Skin (Health +106,645)

This skin increases Health, giving Iris greater survivability against burst teams. It’s helpful for players who use her in the front line or against strong area-damage enemies, ensuring she survives long enough to trigger Soul Burning.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – A defensive choice that keeps Iris alive longer to unleash her full combo.

Celestial Skin (Magic Critical Hit Chance +2,960)

Boosts Magic Critical Hit Chance, giving Iris a chance to deal extra burst damage with abilities like Inner Fire and Soul Burning. While not consistent, it adds explosive potential, particularly in long fights where she casts multiple times.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Adds random burst value, but less reliable than flat Magic Attack bonuses.

Winter Skin (Crushing +5920)

Crushing: The Ultimate Finisher

If there's one stat that stands out as a true game-changer, it's Crushing. This stat increases all outgoing damage to opponents with low health, making it the ultimate tool for finishing off enemies. Whether it's physical, magical, or pure damage, Crushing ensures your heroes capitalize on weakened opponents with devastating efficiency.

How Does Crushing Work?

Crushing applies to enemies whose health is below 50%. The formula for the damage increase is as follows:

Damage Increase (%) = (Crushing / (Crushing + Target's Main Stat)) × 100%

For instance, if your hero has 5,920 Crushing and the enemy Kayla has 2,486 Intelligence (their main stat), your hero will deal approximately 70.4% more damage to them when their health drops below 50%.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Essential for maximizing burst damage on low-health enemies and securing finishing blows in critical moments.

Iris Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Iris heavily relies on Magic Attack and timing her ultimate skill. Her artifacts amplify her burst potential, magic power, and durability — but one stands out as essential to evolve first.

1st - Weapon Artifact: Tome of Enslaved Souls

This artifact is Iris’s strongest source of team-wide Magic Attack. It activates together with her ultimate skill, greatly amplifying her burst damage and that of all allied mages. Since her ultimate triggers her main source of damage through Bitey and the soul explosion, maximizing this artifact ensures peak performance in every battle.

Evolution Priority: High – Core artifact for Iris’s overall DPS, must be evolved first.

2nd - Book Artifact: Tome of Arcane Knowledge

The Book Artifact provides both Magic Attack and Health, reinforcing Iris’s survivability while slightly boosting her sustained damage. It doesn’t influence her ultimate directly but is very useful in long fights or against teams with magic reflect or AoE damage.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Secondary priority for extra durability and balanced stats.

3rd - Ring Artifact: Intelligence

The Ring Artifact increases Iris’s Intelligence, which scales into Magic Attack and Magic Defense. While it provides consistent stat growth, its impact in battle is smaller compared to her weapon and book effects, making it the last priority to evolve.

Evolution Priority: Low – Passive stat increase only, less impact in real combat.

Which Talisman is Best for Iris?

Between both talismans, Talisman of Erudition is the best choice for most battles. Its high Magic Penetration greatly boosts Iris’s burst damage, especially against teams with strong magic defense. The Armor bonus also adds a small layer of protection against physical teams. Meanwhile, the Talisman of Collector is more defensive and should be reserved for countering heavy magic compositions or survival-focused strategies.

How to Counter Iris - Hero Wars Alliance

Corvus

Why Corvus defeats Iris: Corvus’ Altar of Souls punishes Iris for her continuous damage output. When Iris uses her abilities that transfer soul energy or deal damage over time, each tick triggers the altar, reflecting powerful counter-damage to her entire team. His altar scales with health, allowing Corvus to survive and destroy Iris-focused magic or burst compositions efficiently.

Heidi

Why Heidi Can Defeat Iris: Heidi becomes an effective counter to Iris only when she is part of teams that include Iris + Dante or Iris + Dante + Octavia together. Dante grants physical dodge bonuses to all allies, while Octavia further enhances dodge, provides an additional buff, and increases the chance to dodge magic damage.

Heidi can prevent Iris and her allies from dodging her attacks and toxins, while gaining enough time to retaliate with her pure damage abilities, which completely ignore dodge chances.

Octavia

Why Octavia defeats Iris: Octavia’s ultimate, Distortion of Fate, creates a magic mirror that reflects part of the incoming damage back to enemies. This is particularly effective against Iris’ soul energy burst and her Dark Ritual, which deals heavy area damage. When Iris strikes Octavia’s team, the reflected energy bounces back, often leading Iris to damage herself or her allies severely. The reflection effect lasts long enough to neutralize Iris’ main offensive window.

Iris Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Dante

Why Dante synergizes with Iris: Dante’s spears apply debuffs that trigger Iris’ Inner Fire ability, causing her to ignite enemies whenever they are affected by a debuff. This makes Dante an ideal partner, as his frequent debuff application ensures that Iris continuously burns multiple targets, amplifying her overall damage output and pressure during fights.

Nebula

Why Nebula synergizes with Iris: Nebula’s passive aura significantly increases the Magic Attack of nearby allies — boosting the hero in front and behind her. When placed correctly, Iris benefits from Nebula’s huge attack enhancement, allowing her Dark Ritual and Inner Fire abilities to deal devastating bursts of magic damage, especially in mid-game and late-game battles.

Octavia

Why Octavia synergizes with Iris: Octavia combines perfectly with Iris and Dante, forming a top-tier evasion-based trio. While Dante grants physical evasion to all allies, Octavia enhances both physical and magical evasion, protecting the entire team from diverse damage types. This defensive synergy allows Iris to stay alive longer, continuously burning enemies with Inner Fire while Octavia and Dante control incoming damage through dodges and reflections.

Iris Best Teams for Hero Wars Alliance

Octavia, Iris, Nebula, Dante, Electra

The team of Octavia, Iris, Nebula, Dante, and Electra creates a strong balance between offense and survivability. Electra works as a front-line tank, absorbing heavy hits and protecting Iris from burst counters, while Nebula amplifies her magic power. Supported by Dante’s debuffs and Octavia’s dual evasion boosts, Iris can safely unleash devastating Inner Fire damage throughout the battle.
