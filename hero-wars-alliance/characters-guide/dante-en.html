Which Talisman Should You Choose?

Summary:
Talisman of Soul increases Agility, which boosts both Physical Attack, Armor, and Crit Hit Chance. Talisman of Retaliation gives a much higher flat Physical Attack and a big Dodge bonus.

  • If you want more consistent damage and survivability (especially versus crit-based teams), Talisman of Soul is a solid, balanced pick. The extra Crit Hit Chance means you have a higher chance to deal double damage with critical hits, which can be game-changing if Dante is built for crits and your team provides crit buffs.
  • If you want to maximize Dante’s burst damage and counterattack potential (especially in dodge-based teams), Talisman of Retaliation is the best choice. The extra Physical Attack increases all skill damage (including criticals), and the Dodge bonus triggers more Retribution attacks, making Dante much more dangerous and harder to defeat.

Critical Hit vs. Consistency:
Critical hits deal double damage in Hero Wars Alliance. If your Dante has high crit multipliers, lots of crit buffs, and you want to gamble for big hits, the Talisman of Soul can outperform in short fights or against low-dodge teams. However, most battles and team compositions favor consistency and survivability—where Dodge and Physical Attack matter more for Dante’s kit and synergy.

Alexandre Tips: For most players, Talisman of Retaliation is the best talisman for Dante in Hero Wars. It provides the highest boost to his core skills: Retribution, Foresight, and Instrument of Fate, and makes Dante a top threat in both offense and defense. Use the Talisman of Soul only if you specifically want more Crit Hit Chance or are facing teams where burst damage is more important than overall consistency and control.

Dante Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Dante is a Middle Line Marksman who controls the battlefield with spectral spears. His kit revolves around knockbacks, dodge mechanics, and debuffing enemies. Understanding which skills to prioritize will help you maximize his devastating potential in battles.

1st – Instrument of Fate

This is Dante's primary damage skill where he launches 4 spectral spears at multiple enemies, dealing physical damage while pushing them back. The knockback effect is crucial because it not only disrupts enemy positioning but also synergizes perfectly with his legendary enhancement. The base damage is calculated as (95% Physical Attack + 10,150), making it a reliable source of consistent damage throughout the battle.

  • Formula: Physical Damage: 126,362 (95% × 122,223 + 10,150)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Physical Damage: 128,162 (95% × 124,223 + 10,150)

Skill Animation Info

  • Animation delay: 0.88 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

Evolution Priority: High – This skill becomes significantly more powerful with its legendary enhancement, which adds armor penetration each time you knock back enemies. Since Dante frequently uses knockback effects, this creates a snowball effect where his damage output increases dramatically over time.


1st – Instrument of Fate (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 10

The legendary enhancement transforms this skill into a game-changer. Whenever Dante knocks an enemy back (which happens frequently with his kit), he gains bonus armor penetration for 6 seconds. The armor penetration bonus is (7.5% Physical Attack + 6,000). This stacking effect means that in prolonged fights, Dante becomes increasingly deadly as he accumulates armor penetration, allowing him to cut through even the tankiest enemies.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Armor Penetration Bonus: 15,167 (7.5% × 122,223 + 6,000)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Armor Penetration Bonus: 15,317 (7.5% × 124,223 + 6,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 6 seconds
  • Keywords: Armor Penetration Increase

Evolution Priority: Very High – This enhancement is absolutely essential for Dante's late-game viability. The armor penetration stacking makes him a threat against high-armor teams and significantly boosts his overall DPS output in any extended engagement.

2nd – Foresight

Foresight is a powerful team support skill that increases all allies' Dodge for 7 seconds. The dodge bonus is (10% Physical Attack + 3,000). This skill is particularly valuable because dodge is central to Dante's entire kit – when allies dodge, it triggers his Retribution skill. Think of this as a skill that not only protects your team but also creates more opportunities for Dante to counterattack.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Dodge Bonus: 15,222 (10% × 122,223 + 3,000)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Dodge Bonus: 15,422 (10% × 124,223 + 3,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 4 seconds
  • Cooldown: 14 seconds
  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.5 seconds
  • Keywords: Buff

Evolution Priority: High – This skill deserves high priority because it enables Dante's reactive playstyle. More dodges mean more Retribution procs, which translates to more damage and control. It's especially effective when paired with other dodge-based heroes in your team composition.

Alexandre Tips: When you see the yellow flame on Dante or his allies, it means they are receiving Dante's dodge bonus even if they don't have Dodge as a stat. This interaction is stronger with Octavia, who amplifies the effect and provides additional magic attack dodge capability. Note: Dodge only applies against physical attacks.

3rd – Retribution

Retribution is Dante's reactive counterattack skill. Whenever Dante or any ally dodges damage, he automatically throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing (90% Physical Attack + 4,150) damage and knocking them back. This skill has an incredibly short 0.1-second cooldown, meaning in dodge-heavy compositions, Dante can trigger this multiple times in rapid succession, creating a relentless barrage of counterattacks.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Physical Damage: 114,151 (90% × 122,223 + 4,150)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Physical Damage: 115,951 (90% × 124,223 + 4,150)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 0.1 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.72 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is arguably Dante's most important skill because it's the foundation of his damage output. Combined with high dodge rates from Foresight and his 6th skill, Retribution can proc continuously, making Dante deal massive amounts of damage while simultaneously controlling enemy positions with constant knockbacks.

4th – Shackles of Weakness

Every time one of Dante's spectral spears hits an enemy, this skill reduces that target's main stat for 7 seconds. The reduction is (6% Physical Attack + 2,400). This debuff is particularly effective against enemy damage dealers, as it weakens their primary stat (Strength for warriors, Agility for marksmen, Intelligence for mages), reducing their overall effectiveness in battle.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 9,733 (6% × 122,223 + 2,400)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 9,853 (6% × 124,223 + 2,400)

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Keywords: Debuff

Evolution Priority: Medium – While useful for reducing enemy damage output, this skill is less impactful than Dante's direct damage and control abilities. It provides utility but doesn't directly increase your team's damage or survival as much as his other skills.


4th – Shackles of Weakness (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 20

The legendary enhancement dramatically extends the duration of the main stat reduction from 7 seconds to a whopping 16 seconds. This means that once an enemy is hit by Dante's spears, they remain weakened for much longer, significantly reducing their combat effectiveness throughout extended battles. The longer duration makes it much easier to maintain the debuff on multiple enemies simultaneously.

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 16 seconds
  • Keywords: Debuff

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – The extended duration makes this enhancement more valuable than the base skill. In longer fights, keeping enemies weakened for 16 seconds instead of 7 provides substantial value, especially against teams with multiple damage dealers. It's worth investing in after you've maxed out your damage-focused skills.

One of Dante's most striking characteristics is his ability to reduce the main attributes of enemies. This ability has a significant impact on the battlefield, weakening opponents and making them vulnerable to attacks from your team.

Alexandre Tips: The main stat reduction from Shackles of Weakness is particularly effective against different hero types:

  • Tanks: Reducing their health, making them less resistant to direct attacks.
  • Mages: Decreasing their magic defense and magic attack, making them more susceptible to magical damage.
  • Agility Heroes: Decreasing their physical attack and armor, making them less effective in direct combat.

This adaptability makes Dante a valuable component in a variety of battle situations.


5th – Piercing Throw (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

Piercing Throw is a brand new skill that unlocks with Dante's Legendary Relic. Every 9 seconds, Dante automatically throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing massive damage of (225% Physical Attack) with a knockback effect. This skill essentially adds another layer of consistent damage and control to Dante's already impressive kit, activating regularly throughout the fight without requiring any trigger condition.

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Physical Damage: 279,502 (225% × 124,223)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 9 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

Evolution Priority: Very High – This new skill is incredibly powerful because it scales with 225% of Physical Attack, making it one of Dante's hardest-hitting abilities. The automatic nature means it provides guaranteed damage output every 9 seconds, and the knockback adds yet another control element. This skill significantly boosts Dante's overall DPS and should be prioritized heavily.


6th – Shifting Focus (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

Shifting Focus is Dante's ultimate legendary skill that adapts to the battle situation. When enemies are close to Dante, he gains (25% Physical Attack) bonus Dodge. When no enemies are nearby, he instead gains (25% Physical Attack) bonus Physical Attack. This adaptive mechanic makes Dante incredibly versatile – he becomes evasive when threatened and aggressive when safe, maximizing both his survivability and damage output.

The bonus Dodge when enemies are near synergizes perfectly with Retribution, creating even more counterattack opportunities. The bonus Physical Attack when enemies are far away allows Dante to deal even more devastating damage safely from range. This dual-mode adaptability makes Dante extremely effective in virtually any combat scenario.

Spring Skin (Health)

Stat Bonus: Health +10,650

This skin is a great pick if you want to maximize both Dante’s damage and his team’s survivability through enhanced control effects.

Dante Artifact Evolution Priority – Hero Wars Alliance

Upgrading Dante’s artifacts is essential for maximizing his damage, control, and survivability. Here’s the recommended order, based on real impact for DPS, team synergy, and scaling in long battles.

1st – Spear of Fate (Weapon Artifact)

Stat Bonus: Dodge +8,898
Effect: When Dante uses his ultimate skill, all allies receive a Dodge buff for 9 seconds. This directly boosts Retribution triggers and team survivability, making it the most impactful artifact for both offense and defense.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Max this artifact first. The Dodge buff is crucial for Dante’s kit and team synergy, especially in PvP and long fights.

2nd – Book of Illusions (Book Artifact)

Stat Bonus: Dodge +2,967, Health +53,394

Effect: Increases Dante’s own survivability and Dodge, making him harder to kill and more likely to trigger Retribution. The health bonus is especially useful in long battles and against burst teams. Note: This artifact only boosts Dante himself, not the whole team, and does not increase the Dodge buff for allies.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Upgrade after weapon and ring. The extra Dodge and Health are valuable for Dante’s survival, but team-wide impact is lower than the ring.

3rd – Ring of Agility (Ring Artifact)

Stat Bonus: Agility +3,990
Physical Attack from Agility: 11,970
Armor from Agility: 3,990

Effect: Boosts Dante’s main stat, increasing all skill damage and armor. Most importantly, Physical Attack directly increases the Dodge bonus Dante gives to the whole team via his ultimate skill (Foresight). This means more survivability and more Retribution triggers for everyone. Prioritize this artifact after the weapon for maximum team impact and DPS scaling.

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade after the weapon. The Agility/Physical Attack bonus is crucial for both Dante’s damage and the Dodge buff he provides to allies.

Conclusion – Dante Legendary Relic Guide (Hero Wars Alliance)

Dante stands out as one of the most versatile and powerful heroes in Hero Wars Alliance, especially with his Legendary Relic upgrades. By prioritizing his Physical Attack and Agility stats, evolving the right skills and glyphs, and choosing the best artifacts and skins, you can maximize both his damage and survivability. Focus on upgrading his Retribution and Shifting Focus skills first, invest in Dodge for more counterattacks, and don’t overlook the synergy between his artifacts and team buffs. With the right build, Dante can dominate both PvP and PvE battles, controlling the field and carrying your team to victory.
