Who Is Dante in Hero Wars Alliance?

Dante is a Middle Line Marksman with a kit built around physical attacks, knockbacks and dodge-triggered counterattacks.

  • Main Class: Marksman
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Faction: Way of Eternity
  • How to get Soul Stones: In-game sources (events, tower shop and special offers) — check the in-game calendar and shop.
  • Synergy: Works best with dodge-focused allies and teams that amplify physical DPS and crowd control.
  • Hydra Tier List: See the site's Hydra Tier List page for seasonal rankings.

Dante pros & cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • High sustained physical DPS that scales well with Physical Attack.
  • Strong battlefield control thanks to frequent knockbacks.
  • Legendary relics add powerful effects (armor penetration, new skills).

❌ Cons

Talisman Guide – Hero Wars Alliance

Choosing the right talisman for Dante can make a huge difference in battle. Unlike skins (where you get all bonuses), only the equipped talisman grants its bonus. Below, we break down both options, show the total reroll slot value (all 3 slots combined), and explain which talisman is best for maximizing Dante’s skills and overall performance.

Talisman of Soul

  • Main Stat slot: Agility +2,000
  • Each Agility point grants:
    • 2 points to Physical Attack
    • 1 point to Armor
    • 1 extra point to Physical Attack if Agility is the hero's main stat (Dante: Yes)
  • Physical Attack from Agility: 6,000
  • Armor from Agility: 2,000
  • Reroll Slots (all 3): Crit Hit Chance +6,600 (2,200 × 3)

Which Talisman Should You Choose?

Summary:
Talisman of Soul gives more Agility (which boosts both Physical Attack and Armor, plus Crit Hit Chance), while Talisman of Retaliation gives a much higher flat Physical Attack and a big Dodge bonus.

  • If you want more consistent damage and survivability (especially versus crit-based teams), Talisman of Soul is a solid, balanced pick.
  • If you want to maximize Dante’s burst damage and counterattack potential (especially in dodge-based teams), Talisman of Retaliation is the best choice. The extra Physical Attack and Dodge directly boost his Retribution and Foresight skills, making him much more dangerous in both offense and defense.
Recommendation: For most players, Talisman of Retaliation is the best for battle, as it gives the highest boost to Dante’s most important skills (Retribution, Foresight, and Instrument of Fate). Only use Talisman of Soul if you need more Crit Hit Chance or are facing teams where survivability is more important than burst damage.

Dante Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Dante is a Middle Line Marksman who controls the battlefield with spectral spears. His kit revolves around knockbacks, dodge mechanics, and debuffing enemies. Understanding which skills to prioritize will help you maximize his devastating potential in battles.

1st – Instrument of Fate

This is Dante's primary damage skill where he launches 4 spectral spears at multiple enemies, dealing physical damage while pushing them back. The knockback effect is crucial because it not only disrupts enemy positioning but also synergizes perfectly with his legendary enhancement. The base damage is calculated as 92,085 (95% Physical Attack + 10,150), making it a reliable source of consistent damage throughout the battle.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill becomes significantly more powerful with its legendary enhancement, which adds armor penetration each time you knock back enemies. Since Dante frequently uses knockback effects, this creates a snowball effect where his damage output increases dramatically over time.

1st – Instrument of Fate (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 10

The legendary enhancement transforms this skill into a game-changer. Whenever Dante knocks an enemy back (which happens frequently with his kit), he gains bonus armor penetration for 6 seconds. The armor penetration bonus is 14,691 (7.5% Physical Attack + 6,000). This stacking effect means that in prolonged fights, Dante becomes increasingly deadly as he accumulates armor penetration, allowing him to cut through even the tankiest enemies.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This enhancement is absolutely essential for Dante's late-game viability. The armor penetration stacking makes him a threat against high-armor teams and significantly boosts his overall DPS output in any extended engagement.

2nd – Foresight

Foresight is a powerful team support skill that increases all allies' Dodge for 7 seconds. The dodge bonus is 11,625 (10% Physical Attack + 3,000). This skill is particularly valuable because dodge is central to Dante's entire kit – when allies dodge, it triggers his Retribution skill. Think of this as a skill that not only protects your team but also creates more opportunities for Dante to counterattack.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill deserves high priority because it enables Dante's reactive playstyle. More dodges mean more Retribution procs, which translates to more damage and control. It's especially effective when paired with other dodge-based heroes in your team composition.

3rd – Retribution

Retribution is Dante's reactive counterattack skill. Whenever Dante or any ally dodges damage, he automatically throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing 81,772 (90% Physical Attack + 4,150) damage and knocking them back. This skill has an incredibly short 0.1-second cooldown, meaning in dodge-heavy compositions, Dante can trigger this multiple times in rapid succession, creating a relentless barrage of counterattacks.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is arguably Dante's most important skill because it's the foundation of his damage output. Combined with high dodge rates from Foresight and his 6th skill, Retribution can proc continuously, making Dante deal massive amounts of damage while simultaneously controlling enemy positions with constant knockbacks.

4th – Shackles of Weakness

Every time one of Dante's spectral spears hits an enemy, this skill reduces that target's main stat for 7 seconds. The reduction is 7,575 (6% Physical Attack + 2,400). This debuff is particularly effective against enemy damage dealers, as it weakens their primary stat (Strength for warriors, Agility for marksmen, Intelligence for mages), reducing their overall effectiveness in battle.

Evolution Priority: Medium – While useful for reducing enemy damage output, this skill is less impactful than Dante's direct damage and control abilities. It provides utility but doesn't directly increase your team's damage or survival as much as his other skills.

4th – Shackles of Weakness (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 20

The legendary enhancement dramatically extends the duration of the main stat reduction from 7 seconds to a whopping 16 seconds. This means that once an enemy is hit by Dante's spears, they remain weakened for much longer, significantly reducing their combat effectiveness throughout extended battles. The longer duration makes it much easier to maintain the debuff on multiple enemies simultaneously.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – The extended duration makes this enhancement more valuable than the base skill. In longer fights, keeping enemies weakened for 16 seconds instead of 7 provides substantial value, especially against teams with multiple damage dealers. It's worth investing in after you've maxed out your damage-focused skills.

  • Duration: 16 seconds

5th – Piercing Throw (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

Piercing Throw is a brand new skill that unlocks with Dante's Legendary Relic. Every 9 seconds, Dante automatically throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing massive damage of 260,726 (225% Physical Attack) with a knockback effect. This skill essentially adds another layer of consistent damage and control to Dante's already impressive kit, activating regularly throughout the fight without requiring any trigger condition.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This new skill is incredibly powerful because it scales with 225% of Physical Attack, making it one of Dante's hardest-hitting abilities. The automatic nature means it provides guaranteed damage output every 9 seconds, and the knockback adds yet another control element. This skill significantly boosts Dante's overall DPS and should be prioritized heavily.

6th – Shifting Focus (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

Shifting Focus is Dante's ultimate legendary skill that adapts to the battle situation. When enemies are close to Dante, he gains 28,970 (25% Physical Attack) bonus Dodge. When no enemies are nearby, he instead gains 28,970 (25% Physical Attack) bonus Physical Attack. This adaptive mechanic makes Dante incredibly versatile – he becomes evasive when threatened and aggressive when safe, maximizing both his survivability and damage output.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This skill is the crown jewel of Dante's kit. The bonus Dodge when enemies are near synergizes perfectly with Retribution, creating even more counterattack opportunities. The bonus Physical Attack when enemies are far away allows Dante to deal even more devastating damage safely from range. This dual-mode adaptability makes Dante extremely effective in virtually any combat scenario.
