Kayla stands as one of the most fearless and explosive warriors in Hero Wars Alliance. This warrior of the Way of Chaos charges into the frontlines with unmatched agility and pure devastation. With her legendary relic abilities unleashed, she becomes an unstoppable force that can wipe out entire enemy squads with devastating area damage.

Whether you're building a pure damage composition with her brother Aidan or synergizing with Peech, Kayla's legendary relic skills transform her into a damage powerhouse. Even facing strong counters like Somna, Kayla remains the most feared pure area damage dealer in Hero Wars Alliance. Ready to unleash chaos? Let's dive into mastering the Warrior of Chaos!

Who Is Kayla in Hero Wars Alliance?

Kayla is a warrior of the Way of Chaos faction, positioned at the Front Line with pure area damage output. Her legendary relic abilities unlock devastating combos that make her one of the most feared heroes in both PVP and Frontier battles.

  • Faction: Way of Chaos
  • Glyph Class: Warrior
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Main Role: Pure Area Damage
  • Best Synergy: Aidan, Peech
  • Hydra Tier List: B Tier
  • How to get Soul Stones: Events, Heroic Chest

Kayla's true strength lies in her legendary relic abilities that grant her access to advanced mechanics unavailable to heroes without relic upgrades. These skills elevate her from a strong warrior to a legendary devastation machine capable of controlling entire battlefields with pure area damage.

Kayla's Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Master Kayla's relic abilities to unleash devastating damage across the battlefield. These legendary skills define her role as the most feared warrior in Hero Wars Alliance, transforming her into a pure area damage powerhouse with synergy and survivability that few can match.

1st - Phoenix's Fury (Suprema)

Kayla throws a chakram that strikes enemies around her with physical damage (109019 = 100% Phys.Atk. + 6000). Enemies hit lose maximum health equal to 20% of the damage dealt, and Kayla restores the same amount of Health. This creates a devastating sustain mechanic that makes her nearly unkillable in prolonged fights. Additionally, if Aidan is dead on your team, he gets resurrected with the same health value Kayla recovers creating incredible synergy with her brother and enabling powerful comeback mechanics.

Key Mechanics: Health steal healing + Aidan resurrection

Animation Delay: 0.25 seconds

Evolution Priority: VERY HIGH (dark green) – This is your foundation skill. The health steal makes Kayla unkillable, and Aidan resurrection is a game-changer. Always upgrade this first.


2nd - Acrobatics

This survival skill is crucial for any frontline warrior. When Kayla's health is above 40%, she performs an agile jump to the enemy team's center, positioning her for maximum damage output. But here's where it becomes brilliant: when her health drops below 25%, she immediately regenerates Health (111479 = 25% Health + 2400) over 5.0 seconds. She gains an additional 23016 healing (5% Health + 1200) for each allied Chaos Hero on your team, synergizing perfectly with heroes like Aidan and Peech. She then returns to her team when possible, ensuring she stays in the fight.

Key Mechanics: Positioning jump + Survival healing + Chaos Hero synergy

Cooldown: 8 seconds (initial) / 15 seconds (standard)

Evolution Priority: HIGH (green) – Your second upgrade priority. The survival healing keeps you in fights longer, and Chaos Hero synergy is essential for pure damage compositions.


2nd - Acrobatics Legendary (Relic Level 10)

At Relic Level 10, Acrobatics evolves into a legendary skill that transforms your survival into offensive power. Kayla now deals physical damage (depends on Physical Attack) to nearby enemies before leaping back to her allies. This means your escape becomes an attack turning defensive plays into offensive strikes. The upgrade essentially gives you additional damage while protecting your hero, creating a perfect balance between sustain and offense.

Enhancement Bonus: Physical damage before retreat

Evolution Priority: HIGH (green) – Prioritize this after Phoenix's Fury. The legendary version transforms Acrobatics from pure survival into a damage tool, making it essential for your PvP and Frontier battles.


3rd - Retaliatory Strike

A pure punishment skill for enemies who dare to damage your warrior. Retaliatory Strike causes physical damage to surrounding enemies based on the damage Kayla has taken since she last used this skill, with a maximum of 160528 (150% Phys.Atk. + 6000). The more damage your enemies deal to Kayla, the more she retaliates with. She also blinds enemies for 3.0 seconds, reducing their accuracy and making them less threatening. This skill rewards aggressive opponents and turns their offense into your counter-damage.

Key Mechanics: Damage scaling from taken damage + Enemy blinding

Cooldown: 12 seconds (initial) / 8 seconds (standard) | Animation Delay: 0.6 seconds

Evolution Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH (orange) – Upgrade after your core survivability skills. The blinding effect is valuable, but the damage scaling relies on incoming damage, making it less consistent than other skills.


4th - Inner Flame (Legendary Relic Level 20)

This is where Kayla becomes a devastation machine. After using her second skill (Acrobatics), she begins to burn, dealing pure damage (14478 depends on Health) every 0.5 seconds to herself and enemies around her. The effect continues until her health drops below 25%. This is the core of her pure area damage identity constant, relentless damage that targets everything around her.

Key Mechanics: Continuous pure AoE damage after Acrobatics

Scaling: Depends on Kayla's Health pool

Evolution Priority: VERY HIGH (dark green) – This is your damage core. The pure damage output is unmatched, and it scales with Health, synergizing with Phoenix's Fury's healing. Pair with heroes like Aidan or Byrna who increase ally health.


4th - Inner Flame Legendary (Relic Level 20 - Enhanced)

The legendary version of Inner Flame escalates the damage exponentially. While Kayla is burning, damage from the skill increases by 6% every 2.0 seconds, stacking indefinitely. This means the longer the battle lasts, the more devastating her damage becomes. In extended fights particularly in Eternal Frontier battles or long PvP matches Inner Flame becomes an unstoppable force.

Enhancement Bonus: Progressive 6% damage increase every 2.0 seconds while burning

Evolution Priority: VERY HIGH (dark green) – Upgrade this immediately after the base Inner Flame. The scaling damage makes this your most powerful skill in extended battles, especially in Frontier content.

Pro Tip: Combine Inner Flame Legendary with heroes like Aidan or Byrna who increase your maximum health, allowing Inner Flame to burn longer and deal more total damage.

5th - Chaotic Fracture (Legendary Relic Level 1)

A utility skill that opens weaknesses in enemy defenses. Chaotic Fracture reduces the Armor of all nearby enemies for 6.0 seconds with an armor reduction of 26491 (depends on Physical Attack). When Kayla's health drops below 50%, the effect becomes even more devastating with a bonus armor reduction of 52983 (depends on Physical Attack) essentially doubling the effect when you need it most. Lower enemy armor means your entire team deals more physical damage.

Key Mechanics: Team armor reduction + Enhanced reduction at low health

Duration: 6.0 seconds

Evolution Priority: MEDIUM (yellow) – Useful for team compositions heavy on physical damage, but doesn't directly increase Kayla's damage. Upgrade after your core skills are maxed.


6th - Frenzy (Legendary Relic Level 30)

The ultimate offensive skill that turns damage received into damage dealt. Frenzy causes Kayla's damage to increase by 15% each time she cumulatively loses 50% of her initial Health. Since Kayla takes significant damage in frontline positions, this skill naturally triggers multiple times throughout a battle, creating a stacking damage multiplier. Lose 50% health? +15% damage. Lose another 50%? Another +15% stacking increase. This synergizes perfectly with Phoenix's Fury healing, which keeps her alive while building damage stacks.

Key Mechanics: Progressive damage multiplier based on health loss

Damage Trigger: 50% of initial Health loss per stack

Evolution Priority: HIGH (green) – A powerful late-stage upgrade that transforms Kayla into an unstoppable damage dealer in extended battles. Prioritize after Inner Flame and its legendary version.


Kayla Relic Skill Priority Summary

  1. Phoenix's Fury (VERY HIGH) – Health steal + Aidan resurrection
  2. Inner Flame + Legendary (VERY HIGH) – Core pure area damage
  3. Acrobatics + Legendary (HIGH) – Survival + positioning + synergy
  4. Frenzy (HIGH) – Stacking damage multiplier for extended fights
  5. Retaliatory Strike (MEDIUM-HIGH) – Retaliation damage + blinding
  6. Chaotic Fracture (MEDIUM) – Team armor reduction utility

Note: Focus on upgrading Kayla's core damage and survivability skills first. Phoenix's Fury and Inner Flame are your top priorities, followed by Acrobatics for positioning and healing. Frenzy is a powerful late-game upgrade that enhances her damage output in prolonged battles.

Remember: Kayla thrives in long battles where her healing, stacking damage, and pure area effects can compound. Build teams around health-increasing heroes like Aidan, and Byrna to maximize her potential. The higher her health pool, the more damage Inner Flame deals, and the more powerful she becomes as the battle extends.
