Who Is Julius in Hero Wars Alliance?

Fluffiest defender with an impenetrable shield. He protects, he attacks, and most of all, he naps. Behind that playful personality is a front-line tank whose shields connect survival, healing, speed, and team damage.

  • Main Class: Tank
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Faction: Way of Progress
  • How to get Soul Stones: Events and Heroic Chest
  • Synergy: Judge, Isaac, Nebula, Eva, Lara Croft, Ginger, Polaris, Astrid and Lucas
  • Hydra Tier List: A+

Progress allies receive extra healing from his ultimate and a larger speed bonus while shielded. His Relic expands that support with protection for the most injured ally, healing from other shields, cleansing when shields appear, and Armor Penetration for every active shield.

In my tests, Judge matters because his shields protect Eva at the back and reinforce Julius at the front. That extra protection is particularly useful against Leonel. Julius without Judge can have a much harder time keeping the formation safe.

Julius Overall Rating in the Meta

My rating focuses on Julius with his Legendary Relic and a team that keeps shields active. His greatest contribution is making allies survive longer and deal more damage.

  • Crowd Control (CC): ★☆☆☆☆
  • Damage Output: ★★☆☆☆
  • Team Synergy: ★★★★★
  • Self-Sufficiency: ★★★★☆

He has no direct stun in this kit. His control value comes from helping allies keep acting through cleansing and speed, while most of the offensive payoff belongs to the damage dealers behind him.

Julius Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Multiple shields protect the team and its most vulnerable ally.

  • Level 20 adds cleansing when allies receive shields.

❌ Cons

Julius Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

See how Julius turns shields into healing, cleansing, speed, and Armor Penetration, with talisman calculations and clear priorities for each skill.

My base-skill upgrade order: Meowsterpiece of Defense, Copycat Device, Purrfect Reflexes, then Nine-Lives Engine. I keep the skills in their in-game order below. Nine-Lives Engine is still valuable, but its base defensive bonus lasts only 1.5 seconds.

Legendary Relic path: Pawsitive Influence at level 1, Meowsterpiece of Defense enhancement at level 10, Nine-Lives Engine enhancement at level 20, and Defensive Lunge at level 30. These unlock in a fixed sequence; the priorities explain their battle value, not an option to skip earlier levels. Julius's Relic also increases Health and Physical Attack.

Calculation basis: Examples use the two maximum-stat builds above, before temporary battle buffs and Copycat Device stacks. Relic stat gains are shown as cumulative milestone bonuses, not added a second time to these example builds. Actual values change with Julius's battle stats.

1st - Meowsterpiece of Defense (Base Skill, Ultimate)

In-game description: Protects the team with a shield that absorbs any damage. When the shield is destroyed or expires, Julius and all allies are healed. Way of Progress heroes receive twice as much healing.

Skill Explanation: His ultimate protects everyone first, then heals the team when that shield breaks or expires. It is the foundation of his protection cycle. Shield durability is 500% Physical Attack + 6000; ordinary healing is 75% Physical Attack + 9600, with twice that amount for Progress heroes. A shield disappearing can therefore be a recovery moment rather than the end of your defense.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Paw-Sitivity

  • Shield durability: 500% Physical Attack + 6000 (5 × 119,143 + 6,000 = 601,715)
  • Healing: 75% Physical Attack + 9600 (0.75 × 119,143 + 9,600 = 98,957.25)
  • Progress healing: 150% Physical Attack + 19200 (1.5 × 119,143 + 19,200 = 197,914.5)

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Purrfection

  • Shield durability: 500% Physical Attack + 6000 (5 × 125,143 + 6,000 = 631,715)
  • Healing: 75% Physical Attack + 9600 (0.75 × 125,143 + 9,600 = 103,457.25)
  • Progress healing: 150% Physical Attack + 19200 (1.5 × 125,143 + 19,200 = 206,914.5)

Skill Info: Shield, Healing; skill level: 120.

Evolution Priority: Very High — Teamwide protection and healing are his first job. Stronger ultimate shields buy time for every ally, with extra healing value for Progress heroes.

Meowsterpiece of Defense (Enhanced Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 10)

In-game description: When a shield is destroyed or expires, the hero protected by it recovers Health. Does not apply to the Meowsterpiece of Defense skill. Way of Progress heroes receive twice as much healing.

Skill Explanation: Other shields now contribute healing when they break or expire. If a shield protects one hero, that hero is healed; if it protects the whole team, everyone protected is healed. The ultimate already has its own healing, so this enhancement does not add a second copy of that effect to Meowsterpiece of Defense. Progress heroes receive twice the healing.

The enhancement changes which shields can trigger healing. A separate healing coefficient is not specified here, so the numerical examples above describe the base ultimate only.

Alexandre Tips: Breaking a shield no longer means losing all its value. A shield protecting one hero heals that hero, while a team shield heals the team. This is another reason I like surrounding Julius with allies who add shields.

2nd - Copycat Device (Base Skill)

In-game description: Passive: every time Julius gains a shield, his Physical Attack increases until the end of battle, based on his Physical Attack at the start of battle. Active: grants Julius a shield that absorbs any damage. If it survives for 5 seconds, its remaining durability is copied to each ally.

Skill Explanation: The active shield starts on Julius. If it lasts five seconds, each ally receives a copy of its remaining durability. Damage absorbed during those five seconds reduces what gets copied. The passive adds 8% of Physical Attack at the start of battle whenever Julius gains a shield, and that increase lasts until battle ends. Each stack uses the starting value, rather than recalculating 8% from the growing total.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Paw-Sitivity

  • Shield durability: 330% Physical Attack + 24000 (3.3 × 119,143 + 24,000 = 417,171.9)
  • Physical Attack per shield gained: 8% of Physical Attack at the start of battle (0.08 × 119,143 = 9,531.44)

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Purrfection

  • Shield durability: 330% Physical Attack + 24000 (3.3 × 125,143 + 24,000 = 436,971.9)
  • Physical Attack per shield gained: 8% of Physical Attack at the start of battle (0.08 × 125,143 = 10,011.44)

Skill Info: Shield, Buff; skill level: 120; shield copy delay: 5 seconds.

Evolution Priority: Very High — This combines a shield that can spread to the team with lasting Physical Attack growth, improving Julius’s other scaling abilities during the fight.

3rd - Nine-Lives Engine (Base Skill)

In-game description: When a shield on Julius or an ally is destroyed or expires, that hero gains Armor and Magic Defense for 1.5 seconds and all negative effects are removed.

Skill Explanation: When a shield disappears, the protected hero loses negative effects and receives a brief defensive boost. Both Armor and Magic Defense increase by 30% Physical Attack + 2400 for 1.5 seconds. Think of it as a short bridge between shields, not a permanent increase to the team’s defenses.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Paw-Sitivity

  • Armor increase: 30% Physical Attack + 2400 (0.3 × 119,143 + 2,400 = 38,142.9)
  • Magic Defense increase: 30% Physical Attack + 2400 (0.3 × 119,143 + 2,400 = 38,142.9)

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Purrfection

  • Armor increase: 30% Physical Attack + 2400 (0.3 × 125,143 + 2,400 = 39,942.9)
  • Magic Defense increase: 30% Physical Attack + 2400 (0.3 × 125,143 + 2,400 = 39,942.9)

Skill Info: Buff, Debuff Removal; skill level: 100; duration: 1.5 seconds.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High — Cleansing is valuable, but the base defensive window is short. Build your main shields first; the level-20 enhancement is a much stronger cleansing milestone.

Nine-Lives Engine (Enhanced Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 20)

In-game description: Whenever Julius or an ally gains a shield, all negative effects are removed from that hero.

Skill Explanation: Allies now lose negative effects when they receive a shield, instead of having to wait for a shield to break or expire. The original 1.5-second Armor and Magic Defense effect still belongs to the shield-ending trigger; this enhancement adds an earlier cleansing opportunity.

This is a cleansing trigger, not a new scaling formula. Both talismans receive the same added trigger; the base defensive calculations remain shown above.

Alexandre Tips: This is where Iris, Satori, Tempus, and Phobos become especially interesting matchups. Applying shields also clears negative effects, which gives your allies more opportunities to keep fighting. I value this milestone highly against teams that rely on control and debuffs.

Julius Realm Skills - Hero Wars Alliance

In the Realm, Julius protects troops with an armywide defense bonus and a shield chance on attacks. These troop effects are separate from his hero-battle skills.

1st - Cosmic Armor

In-game description: Julius shields the army with his technology, increasing the defense of all troops by 12%.

Skill Explanation: Every troop benefits from 12% increased defense. This is the more predictable part of Julius’s Realm protection because it does not depend on a chance to trigger.

  • Effect: All troops’ defense +12%
  • Type: Defensive troop buff
  • Keywords: Army, Defense, Protection

Evolution Priority: High — I would prioritize the consistent armywide defense benefit before the chance-based shield.

2nd - Star Wanderer's Shield

In-game description: All troops' attacks have a 30% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 8% damage for 2 turns.

Skill Explanation: Troop attacks have a 30% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 8% damage for 2 turns. It adds protection when it activates, but a 30% chance does not guarantee a shield every third attack.

  • Trigger Chance: 30% on troop attacks
  • Damage Absorption: 8%
  • Duration: 2 turns
  • Type: Troop shield
  • Keywords: Attacks, Shield, Damage Absorption

Evolution Priority: Medium-High — The shield adds another defensive layer, but its chance-based trigger makes it less consistent than Cosmic Armor.

Tank Class Skill: Protecting the Most Vulnerable Ally

At class-skill level 45, every 10 seconds Julius boosts the Armor and Magic Defense of the ally with the lowest Health percentage, including himself. The effect lasts 5 seconds.

Each defensive bonus uses 1% maximum Health + 24750. With the maximum-stat builds above, that gives 39,971.36 Armor and Magic Defense with Paw-Sitivity (1% × 1,522,136 + 24,750), or 39,171.36 with Purrfection (1% × 1,442,136 + 24,750). This is an additional reason to value Health alongside Physical Attack.

How to Counter Julius in Hero Wars Alliance

Julius becomes much less comfortable when opponents disrupt his shields. Look at what happens to the shield cycle, not just how much power each team has.

Leonel

Leonel destroys shields, which directly threatens the protection Julius needs. Losing a shield can also remove that hero’s speed bonus and reduce the Armor Penetration gained from Defensive Lunge. Julius still has healing and cleansing when shields end, so shield destruction alone does not tell the whole story.

My matchup advice: Judge’s extra shield on the frontmost ally reinforces Julius. I have had much better results with that extra layer of protection; Julius without Judge can struggle against Leonel.

Oya

Oya is another opponent I watch when using a defense built around shields. Julius needs those shields to preserve protection, speed, and his level-30 offensive support. Against her, check whether he can keep the shield cycle going before committing more resources to damage.

Alexandre Tips: With the level-20 Relic upgrade, I see Iris, Satori, Tempus, and Phobos as favorable targets for Julius’s added cleansing. Receiving shields creates more chances to remove negative effects, but the supporting heroes and timing still matter.

Julius Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Julius rewards allies who add shields or use the time and Armor Penetration those shields provide. These are the partners I would look at first.

Astrid and Lucas

Astrid and Lucas benefit from the protection and extra speed Julius gives Progress allies. At Relic level 30, each active shield also supplies Armor Penetration, helping their physical pressure reach through enemy Armor.

Crow

Crow is another partner I have tested around Julius, Judge, Nebula, and Eva. Julius contributes protection and cleansing while shield uptime supports the formation. I treat Crow as a matchup option rather than a reason to remove the core’s defensive support.

Eva

Eva has delivered exceptionally high damage in my Julius tests. Shields give her protection, and Defensive Lunge adds Armor Penetration while those shields stay active. I get the most from this pairing when Nebula supplies her attack buffs and Judge protects Eva at the back.

Ginger

Ginger is a Progress Marksman who benefits from stronger protection, the larger Progress speed bonus, and Armor Penetration for each active shield. Julius helps her stay in the fight while making the shield layers contribute to physical damage.

Isaac

Isaac adds shields and supports physical pressure. Those extra layers fit Julius’s healing and cleansing cycle, and at Relic level 30 they also become additional Armor Penetration on the heroes they protect.

Judge

Judge shields the frontmost and rearmost allies. In the core I use, that protects Julius at the front and Eva at the back. His extra shield is particularly important against Leonel because it reinforces the protection around Julius.

Lara Croft

Lara Croft can use Julius’s shields, extra speed, and level-30 Armor Penetration to support her physical damage. She is also one of the options I consider alongside the Julius, Judge, Nebula, and Eva core when adapting to an opponent.

Nebula

Nebula is essential to the results that impressed me. She increases attack, removes negative effects, and heals. Those contributions help the formation recover while Eva deals damage. Without Nebula, this particular combo performs much worse in my tests and can be disappointing.

Polaris

Polaris is another partner I consider when adapting a Julius formation. She benefits from his protection, cleansing, and shield-dependent speed. I value the survival support here; the Armor Penetration payoff matters most to the physical damage dealers sharing the lineup.

Julius Guide Conclusion

Julius’s Legendary Relic makes shield support more valuable at every stage: targeted protection at level 1, more healing at level 10, earlier cleansing at level 20, and an offensive payoff at level 30.

My upgrade focus is straightforward: give Julius enough Health to survive, then invest in the Physical Attack that strengthens his protection. Purrfection is my usual talisman choice for that job, while Paw-Sitivity offers more raw Health. Judge and Nebula make a major difference to the Eva pairing that has impressed me in PvP.

In my tests, the core at around 970,000 power, with strong Relics on Eva and Julius, beat opponents above one million power, including Cleaver, Byrna, Kendle, Dorian, and Aidan, and Leonel, Byrna, Guus, Polaris, and Aidan. Those wins show why I value the interaction between these heroes more than the power total alone.

⭐ Alexandre's Final Verdict: Julius is a strong investment for players building around shields and Progress damage dealers. I would prioritize his protection and Relic milestones, keep Judge’s shield support in mind against Leonel, and give Nebula the credit she deserves for making the Eva combo work. The damage increase is exciting, but the protection that keeps it running is what makes this tank special.
