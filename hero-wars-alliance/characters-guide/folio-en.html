Who Is Folio in Hero Wars Alliance?

Knowledge is everything for this living magical book. The power of spells hidden within it is devastating. Folio is a top-tier Middle Line mage from the Way of Mystery faction, built around massive area magic damage, Intelligence manipulation, and a unique Ink Copy mechanic that acts as both a tank and energy generator for allies.

  • Main Class: Mage
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Mystery
  • How to get Soul Stones: Events, Heroic Chest
  • Synergy: Somna, Byrna, Electra, Lian, Miu, Polaris
  • Hero Tier List: S+
  • Hydra Tier List: A

Folio excels in teams built around the Way of Mystery, where his Ink Copy gains additional bonuses per Mystery ally. His combination of devastating AoE damage from Inkblot, CC from Ancient Scriptures, Intelligence theft from Curse of Knowledge, and the sustained pressure of Echo Chant makes him one of the most versatile and dangerous mages in the game.

With legendary relic upgrades, Folio becomes even more terrifying: his Ink Copy absorbs all damage aimed at him, Echo Chant adds escalating periodic damage with increasing crit chance, and Know-It-All passively amplifies all his damage against enemies with lower Intelligence — which, thanks to Curse of Knowledge, is most of them.

Folio Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • ✔ S+ tier mage with devastating AoE damage: Inkblot scales up to 200% Magic Attack and hits the entire enemy team.
  • ✔ Ink Copy creates a tanking clone that absorbs damage (with legendary), generates energy for allies, and scales with Mystery team bonuses.
  • ✔ Curse of Knowledge steals Intelligence from the strongest enemy mage, weakening them while making Folio even stronger.
  • ✔ Legendary relics add escalating DPS (Echo Chant), damage amplification (Know-It-All), and enhanced survivability (Ink Copy legendary).
  • ✔ Exceptional synergy with Way of Mystery heroes like Somna, Byrna, and Electra for devastating team compositions.

❌ Cons

  • ✖ Vulnerable to anti-mage heroes like Cornelius (targets highest Intelligence) and Rufus (blocks magic damage).
  • ✖ Inkblot has a 5-second charge time, making it possible for enemies with CC to interrupt or reduce its effectiveness.
  • ✖ Ink Copy has a long cooldown (32 seconds), so if destroyed quickly, Folio loses his main survivability tool.
  • ✖ Requires significant investment in Magic Attack, Magic Penetration, and relics to reach his full S+ potential.

Folio Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Folio has two talismans, but you can equip only one in battle. This is different from skins: skin bonuses are always active once upgraded, while talisman bonuses only apply from the talisman currently equipped. Because Folio's key skills scale from Magic Attack and his role is pure magical damage, your talisman choice has a direct impact on his burst potential, survivability, and team contribution.

Talisman of Knowledge

Main Stat slot: Intelligence +2,000. Since Intelligence is Folio's main stat, each point grants 3 Magic Attack + 1 Magic Defense + 1 Physical Attack. This means: +6,000 Magic Attack, +2,000 Magic Defense, and +2,000 Physical Attack from the conversion alone.

Reroll Slot 1: Magic Attack +6,600. Reroll Slot 2: Magic Attack +6,600. Reroll Slot 3: Magic Attack +6,600. Treat these three slots as one combined value: Total Reroll Bonus = Magic Attack +19,800.

Main Stat slot: Armor +12,000. This is a pure defensive stat that significantly increases Folio's Physical Armor, improving his survivability against physical damage teams and marksmen.

Battle Recommendation

Alexandre Tips: In the early game, Talisman of Knowledge is the best choice, especially when Folio still lacks maxed glyphs (below level 80) or relics (below level 20). The bonus Magic Attack significantly boosts his overall damage output.

In the late game, however, Talisman of Bookworm becomes more valuable. Once Folio is fully upgraded, the additional Toughness provides much-needed survivability, allowing him to better withstand strong enemy teams and maintain consistent performance in both PvP and PvE.

Folio Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Relics empower Folio with stronger scaling and new effects. This guide explains each skill simply so you can upgrade the right ones first.

1st - Inkblot

Creates a Blot above the center of the enemy team which grows for 5.0 seconds and then crashes, dealing increasing area magical damage. This is Folio's ultimate skill and his single most powerful ability. The longer it charges, the more damage it deals, starting at 181,585.6 (80% of Magic Attack 207,482 + 15,600) and growing up to a devastating 474,964 (200% of Magic Attack 207,482 + 60,000) with Talisman of Knowledge. Think of it like a ticking bomb: the enemy team has 5 seconds before the sky falls on them.

Formula with Talisman of Knowledge

  • Formula 1 (Min Damage): Magical Damage: 181,585.6 (80% x 207,482 + 15,600)
  • Formula 2 (Max Damage): Magical Damage: 474,964 (200% x 207,482 + 60,000)

Formula with Talisman of Bookworm

  • Formula 1 (Min Damage): Magical Damage: 160,945.6 (80% x 181,682 + 15,600)
  • Formula 2 (Max Damage): Magical Damage: 423,364 (200% x 181,682 + 60,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Animation delay: 0.75 seconds
  • Duration: 5 seconds
  • Area: 120
  • Range: 3000

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Folio's main damage source with massive AoE scaling. Upgrading this maximizes his burst potential and team fight impact.


1st - Ancient Scriptures (Inkblot Enhancement) Legendary Relic: Level 10

This legendary enhancement makes Inkblot even more devastating. The skill damage now increases every 0.25 seconds while charging, and the final skill damage is further increased. This means the Blot doesn't just grow visually — its damage ramps up faster and hits harder at the end. In practice, if enemies fail to interrupt Folio's ultimate, the punishment is even more severe than before.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Amplifies Folio's strongest ability. Every second of charge time becomes more valuable, making this the most impactful legendary upgrade.

2nd - Ancient Scriptures

Covers a nearby enemy with Ancient Writings for 4.0 seconds dealing 49,896.4 (20% of Magic Attack 207,482 + 8,400) and preventing movement. When the effect ends, the target and nearby enemies take 219,482 (100% of Magic Attack 207,482 + 12,000) with Talisman of Knowledge. This is a CC+damage combo: the 4-second root locks down a key target, and the explosion at the end punishes anyone standing near them. It's incredibly effective for setting up Inkblot or disrupting enemy formations.

Evolution Priority: High – This CC+damage combo controls the battlefield and sets up devastating follow-ups. The 4-second root is extremely valuable for keeping enemies grouped for Inkblot.

3rd - Ink Copy

Creates an Ink Copy in front of allies. The Copy has 84% of Folio's Health, Magic Penetration, and Magic Attack, with bonuses per allied Mystery hero (+36% per Mystery ally). The Copy has 0 Armor and 0 Magic Defense but grants 10 Energy to nearby allies when attacked. With Talisman of Knowledge, the Copy has 697,441 Health (84% of 830,287), 174,285 Magic Attack (84% of 207,482), and 40,461 Magic Penetration (84% of 48,168). Think of the Ink Copy as a secondary tank that also fuels your team's energy generation — every time an enemy attacks it, your allies gain 10 Energy to charge their ultimates faster.

3rd - Curse of Knowledge (Ink Copy Enhancement) Legendary Relic: Level 20

This legendary enhancement transforms the Ink Copy from a disposable distraction into a true protective shield. Now the Ink Copy receives all damage instead of Folio and can be healed and buffed while alive. This means Folio becomes virtually untouchable as long as his copy stands, and healers like Miu can keep the copy alive, extending its protection indefinitely. This is a game-changing upgrade that dramatically increases Folio's survivability in prolonged team fights.

Evolution Priority: Insanely High 🔥 – This upgrade turns Folio into one of the most durable mages in the game. The ability to redirect all damage to a healable copy is an enormous defensive advantage.

4th - Curse of Knowledge

For 16.0 seconds, steals 7,784.46 (3% of Magic Attack 207,482 + 1,560) Intelligence from the enemy with the highest Intelligence. Once the effect ends, all enemies are drawn to the target. With Talisman of Knowledge this steals Intelligence worth 7,784.46 — which means Folio gains that Intelligence while the enemy mage loses it, creating a double swing in power. The displacement at the end groups enemies together, perfectly setting up Inkblot or Ancient Scriptures for maximum AoE impact.

5th - Echo Chant (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

Casts a spell every few seconds dealing damage to an enemy. With each cast, Critical Hit Chance and the skill's damage increase. The damage per cast starts at 72,618.7 (35% of Magic Attack 207,482) with Talisman of Knowledge and grows with each successive cast. Over 15 hits across 16 seconds, this skill deals escalating sustained damage while also boosting Folio's Magical Critical Hit Chance by 15% per cast. Think of it like a magical machine gun that gets deadlier with every shot: by the final hits, Folio is dealing massive crit-enhanced damage.

6th - Know-It-All (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

Passive: Increases damage from attacks and abilities against enemies with lower Intelligence than Folio. If Folio's Intelligence is higher than the enemy's, his attack and skills damage is increased by 10%. With Folio's naturally high Intelligence (18,123 with Talisman of Knowledge) and his Curse of Knowledge stealing even more Intelligence from enemies, this passive is almost always active. It means every single one of Folio's skills — Inkblot, Ancient Scriptures, Echo Chant — all deal 10% more damage in most battles. Combined with Curse of Knowledge's Intelligence theft, Folio practically guarantees this bonus against any team.

Passive Effect

  • Damage Increase: 10% against enemies with lower Intelligence
  • Trigger Condition: Folio's Intelligence is higher than the enemy's

Evolution Priority: Medium High – A flat 10% damage boost that applies to everything Folio does is very strong in practice, but it requires Folio to already have high Intelligence to trigger consistently. It becomes more powerful after Curse of Knowledge is active.

Best Skin for Folio Hero Wars Alliance

Discover the best skins to upgrade for Folio based on how each stat directly improves his magical damage, penetration, and survivability in battle.

Default Skin

Intelligence +1,365. Each point of Intelligence grants 3 Magic Attack + 1 Magic Defense + 1 Physical Attack (since Intelligence is Folio's main stat). This means: +4,095 Magic Attack, +1,365 Magic Defense, +1,365 Physical Attack. Since all of Folio's damage skills scale from Magic Attack, this skin boosts every offensive ability while also improving his magical defense.

Evolution Priority: High – Core main stat skin that improves both offense and defense. Should be one of the first skins upgraded.

Winter Skin+

Magic Penetration +21,300 (Skin+ doubles the bonus: 10,650 x2). Magic Penetration is absolutely crucial for mages — without it, enemy Magic Defense will absorb a huge portion of Folio's damage. This skin ensures that Inkblot, Ancient Scriptures, and Echo Chant actually reach their full damage potential by cutting through enemy defenses. The Skin+ version costs the same skin stones per level as a normal skin but provides double the stats.

Evolution Priority: Very High – The single most impactful skin for Folio. Magic Penetration is mandatory for any mage to deal real damage. Prioritize this first.

Masquerade Skin

Health +106,645. Increases Folio's survivability and also improves his Ink Copy's Health (since the copy inherits 84% of Folio's stats). More Health means the copy tanks longer and generates more energy for allies. Useful in prolonged fights, but does not directly boost damage.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Good for survivability and Ink Copy durability, but not as impactful as damage or penetration skins for Folio's primary role.

Solar Skin

Magic Attack +10,650. Direct boost to all damage formulas. Every skill — Inkblot, Ancient Scriptures, Ink Copy, Curse of Knowledge, and Echo Chant — scales with Magic Attack. This is a pure offensive upgrade that directly increases Folio's damage output across the board.

Evolution Priority: High – Essential offensive skin. Direct Magic Attack scaling means immediate damage improvement on every ability.

Demonic Skin

Magical Crit Hit Chance +2,960. Increases the chance of Folio's magic attacks dealing critical damage. This synergizes strongly with Echo Chant, which already increases Crit Hit Chance with each cast. The combination can lead to devastating crit chains in prolonged fights.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Excellent synergy with Echo Chant's escalating crit mechanic. Becomes more valuable as Folio's legendary relics are upgraded.

Astral Skin

Armor +10,650. Provides resistance against physical damage. Useful against marksmen and physical assassins, but does not improve any of Folio's skill scaling directly. Purely defensive.

Evolution Priority: Low – Defensive but situational. Folio's Ink Copy (with legendary) should be absorbing damage, making raw Armor less critical. Upgrade only after all offensive skins.

Folio Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Discover which artifacts to prioritize for Folio to maximize his devastating AoE magic damage and team-wide impact in battles.

1st – Weapon Artifact: Archmage's Feather

Magic Attack +32,040. This weapon activates every time Folio uses his ultimate skill, Inkblot, with a 100% activation chance. It grants a massive Magic Attack bonus to the entire team for 9 seconds. Because Inkblot is Folio's most important ability and this artifact has guaranteed activation, it's by far the most valuable artifact to upgrade.

Evolution Priority: Very High – 100% activation on his ultimate, team-wide Magic Attack buff, and the highest single-artifact stat boost. Core to Folio's performance.

2nd – Book Artifact: Manuscript of the Void

Magic Penetration +10,680, Magic Attack +5,340. This artifact provides both penetration and attack — two stats that are critical for Folio. Magic Penetration ensures his damage isn't absorbed by enemy Magic Defense, while the additional Magic Attack directly increases all skill formulas.

Evolution Priority: High – Dual offensive stats make this the second-best artifact. The combination of penetration and attack is exactly what Folio needs.

3rd – Ring Artifact: Ring of Intelligence

Intelligence +3,990. Each point of Intelligence grants 3 Magic Attack + 1 Magic Defense + 1 Physical Attack. Total conversion: +11,970 Magic Attack, +3,990 Magic Defense, +3,990 Physical Attack. While the converted stats are valuable, the indirect scaling is slower than the direct boosts from the weapon and book artifacts. This ring also strengthens Know-It-All's passive trigger by keeping Folio's Intelligence higher than enemies.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Important for overall stat scaling and Know-It-All synergy, but lower priority than the direct Magic Attack and Penetration from weapon and book.

Folio Glyph Evolution Priority

Find out which glyphs you should level first for Folio in Hero Wars Alliance to maximize his devastating magic damage and team synergy.

1st – Magic Attack Glyph

Every single one of Folio's offensive skills scales with Magic Attack — Inkblot (200%), Ancient Scriptures (100%), Curse of Knowledge (3%), Echo Chant (35%), and even his Ink Copy inherits 84% of it. Boosting this stat increases everything Folio does offensively.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Directly powers up every damaging skill. Core to Folio's role as an S+ tier mage.

2nd – Health Glyph

More Health improves Folio's survivability and also directly affects his Ink Copy, which inherits 84% of Folio's Health. A tankier copy means more energy generated for allies and more time for Folio to cast his devastating spells.

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Important for survivability and Ink Copy durability, but Folio's primary value comes from damage, not tanking.

3rd – Magic Defense Glyph

Helps Folio survive against enemy mages, which is important since Magic Defense is one of the stats Intelligence naturally provides (1 per point). In mage-heavy metas, this glyph becomes more valuable for keeping Folio alive through enemy magic burst.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Defense.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Defensive glyph useful against mage teams, but not urgent unless facing constant magic pressure.

4th – Magic Penetration Glyph

Magic Penetration is mandatory for any mage to deal effective damage. Without enough penetration, a large portion of Folio's damage is absorbed by enemy Magic Defense. This glyph ensures Inkblot, Ancient Scriptures, and Echo Chant actually hit for their full calculated values against armored targets.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Penetration.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Essential offensive glyph. Folio needs penetration to convert his massive Magic Attack into real damage against defended targets.

5th – Intelligence Glyph

Intelligence +2,110 grants: +6,330 Magic Attack, +2,110 Magic Defense, +2,110 Physical Attack. While the converted stats are useful, the per-point efficiency is lower than the direct Magic Attack or Magic Penetration glyphs. However, this glyph also helps trigger Know-It-All's passive by keeping Folio's Intelligence above enemies.

Level 80 Stats: +2,110 Intelligence.

Evolution Priority: High – Provides broad stat improvement and synergizes with Know-It-All passive. Upgrade after Magic Attack and Magic Penetration.

How to Counter Folio in Hero Wars Alliance

Folio is an S+ tier mage, but he has clear weaknesses that specific heroes can exploit to shut him down.

Cornelius

Cornelius targets and deals pure damage to the hero with the highest Intelligence on the enemy team. Since Folio has extremely high Intelligence (18,123 with Talisman of Knowledge) and his Curse of Knowledge steals even more, Cornelius will always lock onto Folio and deal devastating pure damage that bypasses all defenses.

Rufus

Rufus provides a magic immunity shield that completely blocks all of Folio's magical damage. Since every single one of Folio's offensive skills deals magical damage, Rufus's shield renders Folio temporarily useless, protecting the team during Inkblot's critical charge time.

Satori

Satori places Fox Fire marks on enemies when they gain energy. Folio's Ink Copy generates 10 Energy per hit for nearby allies, which means enemy Satori marks accumulate quickly. When Satori triggers his marks, the pure damage can be devastating against Folio's team, turning the Ink Copy's energy generation mechanic into a liability.

Folio Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Folio excels in teams built around the Way of Mystery faction, where his Ink Copy gains bonus stats per Mystery ally. These are the heroes that synergize best with him.

Byrna

Byrna is a Way of Nature hero, her frontline tanking presence protects Folio, and her capability of restoring health and energy abilities helps using his Ink Copy effectively while keeping enemies grouped for Inkblot's devastating AoE damage.

Electra

Electra amplifies absorbing allied damage and provides additional enemies magic defense reduction. Her abilities chain perfectly with Folio's Inkblot and Ancient Scriptures, creating overlapping damage zones that devastate enemy teams. Together, they create one of the most destructive magic combos in the game.

Lian

Lian, with Legendary Relics, can chain her Charm CC perfectly with Folio's Ancient Scriptures root. While Ancient Scriptures locks down one target, Lian charms others, creating a cascade of crowd control that prevents the enemy team from acting. This gives Folio's Inkblot enough time to fully charge.

Miu

Miu excels at blinding enemies, drastically reducing their accuracy and limiting incoming pressure. This disruption allows Folio to operate safely and deal massive damage. While enemies miss their attacks, Folio takes full control of the fight, giving Inkblot the time it needs to ramp up and destroy the enemy team.

Additionally, her Gift of the Storm buff significantly boosts Magic Penetration whenever Miu or her allies land Critical Hits. This effect can trigger every 5 seconds, further amplifying Folio's damage output and making his abilities even more devastating against high-resistance targets.

Polaris

Polaris provides stun CC and magic damage pressure. Her disruption keeps enemy heroes locked down and unable to dodge or interrupt Folio's Inkblot. The combination of Polaris's stuns with Folio's Ancient Scriptures root creates devastating CC chains that control the entire enemy team.

Somna

Somna's Sleep CC is one of the most powerful crowd control abilities in the game, and it pairs perfectly with Folio. While enemies are asleep, Folio's Inkblot can fully charge without interruption. Somna is also a Way of Mystery hero, increasing Folio's Ink Copy stats by another 36%. This combination creates one of the strongest magic teams in Hero Wars Alliance.

Folio Guide Conclusion

Folio is an S+ tier mage that combines devastating AoE damage, powerful crowd control, Intelligence theft, and a unique Ink Copy mechanic into one of the most complete kits in Hero Wars Alliance. His legendary relics transform him from a strong mage into an absolute powerhouse: Enhanced Inkblot with charging damage, a healable Ink Copy that absorbs all damage, escalating periodic DPS from Echo Chant, and a passive 10% damage boost from Know-It-All.

For maximum damage, equip the Talisman of Knowledge and prioritize upgrading Magic Attack Glyph, Magic Penetration Glyph, and the Winter Skin+ (Magic Penetration) first. Pair Folio with Way of Mystery heroes like Somna and Byrna to maximize his Ink Copy bonuses, and use Miu as a healer to keep the legendary Ink Copy alive for extended protection.

Folio rewards players who invest heavily into his relics and understand his timing. Master the 5-second Inkblot charge window, protect him with the Ink Copy, and watch as this living magical book erases entire enemy teams from the battlefield.
