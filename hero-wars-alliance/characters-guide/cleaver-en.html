Cleaver has always felt like a trophy hero: difficult to obtain, impossible to ignore, and instantly recognizable when that enormous hook tears the farthest enemy out of safety. But his new Legendary Relics change the question from "Can Cleaver still tank?" to "How much can one tank disrupt an entire battle?"

My answer is simple: this is the most interesting Cleaver has been in years. He still drags a vulnerable back-line hero into danger, but now he can spread pure damage, suppress healing, cleanse allies in Sacrifice teams, and punish enemies who surround him. Let us look at where the real value is before you spend all 2,880 Relic Shards.

Who Is Cleaver in Hero Wars Alliance?

Cleaver is a front-line Tank and Control hero from the Way of Chaos. He begins by protecting the team with an enormous Health pool, then turns enemy positioning against them with Rusty Hook. Once a fragile enemy is pulled forward, Cleaver's pure damage, stun, and nearby allies can erase the safety that hero expected to have.

  • Main Class: Tank
  • Roles: Tank, Control
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Faction: Way of Chaos
  • How to get Soul Stones: Heroic Chest
  • Best Synergy: Kendle, Dorian, Xe'sha, Aidan, Kayla
  • Hydra Tier: B

Cleaver arrives at Absolute Star when obtained. The first major reward after opening 400 Heroic Chests is Cleaver, which explains why unlocking him still feels like an account milestone rather than an ordinary summon.

Cleaver Overall Rating in the Meta

These ratings reflect his current value as a durable disruption tank, including the new Legendary Relic interactions.

  • Crowd Control: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Hook displacement and a nearby stun can dismantle enemy positioning.
  • Damage Output: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ - Pure damage, Miasma, and Carnage give him real pressure for a tank.
  • Team Synergy: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Heavyweight's Legendary cleanse is exceptional with Chaos Sacrifice allies.
  • Self-Sufficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ - Huge Health and 55% Vampirism help, but concentrated damage can still overwhelm him.

Cleaver Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

Pros

  • Pulls the farthest enemy out of position.
  • Deals pure damage and brings reliable nearby control.
  • Legendary Relics add healing reduction and team cleansing.
  • Excellent durability with Health, defenses, Toughness, and Vampirism.
  • Strong natural synergy with Way of Chaos Sacrifice teams.

Cons

  • Extremely difficult to obtain before the Heroic Chest milestone.
  • His best new utility requires a large Relic Shard investment.
  • Putrefaction also damages Cleaver, so timing and sustain matter.
  • The two talismans force a real choice between raw durability and low-Health mechanics.

Alexandre Tips - Relic skills synergy: Cleaver works especially well with heroes who damage themselves through their own or allied skills. Putrefaction Legendary - Relic Level 10

In-game description: Enemies within this skill's area of effect or those affected by Miasma restore 60% less Health to themselves and their allies. If an enemy is affected by both effects, the healing reduction is doubled.

Skill explanation: This is Cleaver's most important Relic enhancement. Putrefaction controls the enemies around him while Miasma carries the debuff into the central formation. When both effects overlap, the stated reduction doubles. A tank who can reposition, survive, and suppress recovery creates the kind of pressure that forces an enemy team to collapse.

Alexandre Tips - Putrefaction: Putrefaction and Miasma severely weaken enemy healing. When both effects overlap, the doubled reduction effectively prevents affected enemies from healing themselves or their allies.

Cleaver Talisman Guide - Hero Wars Alliance

Talismans are different from skins: only the equipped talisman applies its bonuses in battle. That makes this a genuine build decision, not a cosmetic preference.

1 - Talisman of Decomposition

At Level 50, this talisman gives +2,000 Strength. For Cleaver, that means +80,000 Health and +2,000 Physical Attack. Its three Health rerolls reach +60,500 each, but what matters is their combined +181,500 Health. Cleaver Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Cleaver Mechanics You Should Understand

  • Vampirism: Cleaver has 55% Vampirism, healing for a percentage of damage dealt by basic attacks or abilities.
  • Crushing: increases outgoing Physical, Magical, and Pure Damage against enemies below 50% Health.
  • Toughness: reduces incoming Physical, Magical, and Pure Damage while Cleaver is below 50% Health.
  • Sacrifice exception: Crushing and Toughness do not apply to Health loss caused by allied abilities.
  • Realm: Cleaver provides an Infantry Attack/Defense buff of 120% and currently has no Realm skills.

How to Counter Cleaver in Hero Wars Alliance

Cleaver's Rusty Hook is supposed to break the enemy formation, but the right counter can turn that pull against him. These four heroes either stop the displacement, protect its target, or become even more dangerous when Cleaver brings them into the front line.

Andvari

Andvari shuts down Cleaver's main positioning trick. Living Earth protects allies behind Andvari from displacement, so Rusty Hook cannot drag the protected back-line hero into Cleaver's Mutilation and Putrefaction range. His defensive shield also helps the team survive Cleaver's repeated pure damage while they focus him down.

Dorian

Dorian is one of the worst heroes for Cleaver to pull. When Rusty Hook brings Dorian forward, his Vampirism Aura moves with him and reaches the tank and nearby physical damage dealers. Instead of isolating a fragile support, Cleaver can create a brutally sustainable front line whose attacks constantly restore Health while they punish him at close range.

Fafnir

Fafnir protects the Agility damage dealer Cleaver wants to isolate. Runic Shield can prevent the protected ally from dying when it activates and then absorb incoming damage. Runeblade also grants twice its normal physical damage bonus when attacking a Strength hero such as Cleaver, helping the protected carry break through his enormous Health pool before he can win the long fight.

Martha

Hooking Martha can make Cleaver's enemy faster and harder to kill. Once pulled into the active front-line fight, Martha takes damage and gains Energy more quickly, allowing her to answer with Foremother's Oath and accelerate her entire team. Her persistent healing then keeps the front line fighting, so Cleaver's attempt to remove the back-line healer can instead turn her into the engine of the counterattack.

Alexandre's Final Verdict

Cleaver's new Relics do not replace what made him famous; they finally build a modern strategy around it. Miasma turns enemy positioning into area pressure, Putrefaction attacks sustain, Heavyweight gives Chaos Sacrifice teams an unusually natural cleanse, and Carnage rewards the exact crowd Cleaver creates with his hook.

For most players, Relic Level 10 is the best value target. Level 20 is the competitive destination for dedicated Chaos teams. Level 30 is the luxury finish: powerful, satisfying, and very expensive. Pair that path with the Talisman of Decomposition and Cleaver becomes more than a nostalgic chest prize. He becomes the reason the enemy back line never feels safe.
