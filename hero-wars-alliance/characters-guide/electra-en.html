Meet Electra, the mysterious new tank from the Way of Eternity in Hero Wars Alliance. She carries the souls of fallen warriors and transforms her sacrifice into protection for allies. But with every advantage comes a cost her growing Health comes at the expense of Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge.

This guide will uncover how Electra works, the teams she shines in, and why players are excited about her unique role. Whether you are curious about her synergies with Eternity heroes or want to maximize her potential in battle, this walkthrough is built to spark ideas and strategies for your gameplay.

Who Is Electra?

Electra is a frontline tank belonging to the Way of Eternity faction. Her design focuses on absorbing enemy damage while boosting her Health, making her an unusual yet resilient protector. She sacrifices part of her defensive stats for greater durability, which gives her a unique playstyle among tanks.

  • Glyph Class: Tank
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Faction: Way of Eternity
  • How to get Soul Stones: Only through special events
  • Synergy: Mages and Eternity Heroes
  • Hydra Tier List: To be determined as players test her

Electra brings an exciting balance of sacrifice and survival that makes her stand out. She is not the traditional tank that stacks defenses endlessly but instead leverages her immense Health pool to stay in the fight longer.

Players looking for creative team setups will find her particularly valuable alongside Eternity heroes, where her synergy helps unlock hidden potential. Her limited availability through events makes her a rare and desirable addition to any roster.

Electra Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Mobile

✅ Pros

  • Absorbs all incoming damage with Pain's Embrace, protecting herself and allies effectively.
  • Scales extremely well with Health and defensive stats due to her unique mechanic of converting Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Health.
  • Boosts allies’ Magic Attack after using her ultimate, increasing team damage potential.
  • Reduces enemies' Magic Defense with Field of Lightning, amplifying damage for mages and Eternity heroes.
  • Legendary Relics further enhance her damage output and survivability, making her a strong front-line tank with team utility.
  • Immune to control effects at low Health with Unbreakable Mind, making her difficult to disable.
  • Great synergy with Health and Toughness glyphs, plus her artifacts maximize both survivability and team impact.

❌ Cons

  • Limited mobility: cannot attack, move, or be displaced while Pain's Embrace is active, which can delay damage output.
  • Magic Attack scaling is moderate; her offensive potential without Relics or team buffs is lower compared to dedicated damage heroes.
  • Requires investment in glyphs, artifacts, and Relics to reach peak effectiveness, which can be resource-intensive.
  • Relies on team synergy (especially mages and Eternity heroes) to maximize her skill effects and Magic Damage bonuses.
  • Armor and Magic Defense are converted to Health, so she loses raw defensive stats in battle, making early survival dependent on Health scaling.

Electra Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Electra’s relics enhance her lightning power, turning her into a living storm that protects allies while burning enemies. Upgrading the right skills makes her both an unbreakable shield and a destructive mage.

This guide explains which of her skills to upgrade first, how each one works in battle, and why some upgrades bring more value than others helping you make the best decisions to maximize her potential.

Note: Skill Formulas Max Level Calculated with Talisman of Resurrection

1st – Pain's Embrace

Electra absorbs all incoming damage for 6 seconds. During this time, she cannot move, attack, or be displaced. Once the barrier fades, she blinds nearby enemies for 7 seconds and increases allies’ Magic Attack. The absorbed amount is limited to 625902.5 (25% Health + 300000), and the Magic Attack increase is 55995 (50% Magic Attack + 6000). This skill makes Electra an incredible tank for magical teams — she absorbs devastating damage, shields her allies indirectly, and boosts their offensive output right after.

4th – Immortal Shell

At the start of the battle, Electra converts her Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Health, increasing her survivability. The increase is 142361 (10% Health + 12000) per 1000 defensive stat points converted. However, once converted, those defensive stats become zero and cannot be altered in combat.

Formula: Increases Health by 142361 (10% of base Health + 12,000) for every 1,000 defensive points converted.

Example Conversion:

With Armor 10,560, Magic Defense 10,650, and Dodge 2,960, Electra converts a total of 24,170 defensive points, resulting in approximately +2,031,061 Health. Her final maximum Health becomes about 2,751,097 (including her base 720,036 Health).

Best Skins for Electra – Hero Wars Alliance

Find out which Electra skins to prioritize in Hero Wars Alliance. Learn how each skin improves her survivability and how they interact with her Immortal Shell ability that converts defenses into Health. Electra also received the Jade Flame Skin+, which boosts Magic Reflection — the key stat for her Talisman of Revenge build.

Demonic Skin – Armor +10,650

Gain: +10,650 Armor → converted into approximately +894,000 Health through Immortal Shell.

This skin provides the most immediate and massive boost to Electra’s Health, because her ability converts Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into bonus Health at the start of battle. The more Armor she has, the larger her effective HP pool becomes.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Best first pick for a tank-focused Electra. The Armor scaling results in far greater survivability than Strength alone.

Winter Skin – Magic Defense +10,650

Gain: +10,650 Magic Defense → converted into approximately +894,000 Health through Immortal Shell.

This skin provides a massive boost to Electra’s effective Health via Immortal Shell. Magic Defense converts into bonus Health the same way Armor does, making the Winter Skin as valuable as the Demonic (Armor) skin for pure survivability.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Top priority for tank-focused or hybrid builds; prioritize when available alongside the Demonic Skin.

Oblivion Skin – Toughness +2,960

Gain: +2,960 Toughness → increases damage reduction when Health is low, improving survivability against heavy-hitting enemies.

Notes: Toughness stacks with other defensive stats but functions differently than Armor/Magic Defense conversions — it directly reduces incoming damage when the hero's Health is low, making it highly valuable in prolonged fights.

Evolution Priority: High – Recommended after Demonic and Winter for pure tank builds; consider earlier if you face teams that rely on high single-hit damage.

Default Skin – Strength +1,365

Gain: +1,365 Strength → +54,600 Health (1 Strength = 40 HP) and +1,365 Physical Attack.

This skin enhances both her base Health and Physical Attack. The Health increase also strengthens the 10% bonus Health scaling from Immortal Shell, making it a solid complementary upgrade after her defensive skins.

Evolution Priority: High – Improves total Health and gives a small offensive bonus, but the defensive scaling from Armor provides much higher value early on.

Jade Flame Skin+ – Magic Reflection +5,920

Gain: +5,920 Magic Reflection → increases Electra’s chance to dodge incoming magical damage and reflect 20% of it back at the attacker.

Magic Reflection gives Electra a chance to dodge incoming magical damage and reflect 20% of it back. The reflect chance is calculated by the formula:

Formula: (Magic Reflection / (Magic Reflection + enemy’s main stat)) × 100%

  • 🔸 Only activates when receiving magical damage. Physical and pure damage is not reflected.
  • 🔸 Is activated under Stun but is not activated under Silence.
  • 🔸 The enemy’s stat used in the formula is their main stat.

How to Obtain the Jade Flame Skin+

The Skin+ is obtained during the Skin+ Event: first purchase the standard Jade Flame skin during the event, then complete the event missions to unlock the Skin+ upgrade. Outside the event, the Skin+ may drop from the Heroic Chest approximately every 400 boxes opened (up to 2 chests per day for free, or more by spending emeralds — which takes considerable time). New hero Skin+ events rotate monthly, so the best strategy is to get it during the event or wait for its return.

⚠️ DoT System Note

The game introduced a DoT (Damage over Time) system. Heroes like Iris and Yasmine can bypass Magic Reflection with DoT damage, making this skin weaker against them directly. However, Electra remains a strong counter to Yasmine: Yasmine targets the enemy with the lowest Armor, but Electra’s Immortal Shell converts all her Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Health at the start of battle — drawing Yasmine’s focus onto Electra, while Electra’s damage-dealing allies safely eliminate Yasmine. This counter strategy still works effectively even with the DoT update.

The Talisman of Revenge build with Magic Reflection remains strong against powerful enemies that have not received the DoT upgrade, such as Satori, Folio, Miu, and others.

Evolution Priority: High – Excellent for Talisman of Revenge builds, significantly boosting Magic Reflection. Situational against DoT-based heroes, but still very strong in most matchups.

Final Skin Evolution Order (Tank-focused):

  1. Demonic Skin (Armor) – Very High
  2. Winter Skin (Magic Defense) – Very High
  3. Jade Flame Skin+ (Magic Reflection) – High (priority if using Talisman of Revenge)
  4. Oblivion Skin (Toughness) – High
  5. Default Skin (Strength) – High

Note: +10,650 Armor or +10,650 Magic Defense ≈ +894,000 Health from conversion, while +1,365 Strength adds only +54,600 Health directly. That’s over 16× more effective Health from the defensive skins — which is why they are top priorities for tank builds. For Talisman of Revenge users, the Jade Flame Skin+ (+5,920 Magic Reflection) should be prioritized early to maximize reflection chance.

Electra Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Electra’s artifacts directly influence her performance in battle, enhancing survivability and boosting her team’s power. Her Weapon artifact takes the top priority thanks to its synergy with her ultimate, while the Book improves durability through Health and Toughness, and the Ring provides Strength for personal scaling.

1st - Weapon Artifact: Pain Cage

Stats: Toughness +8898

When Electra uses her ultimate ability, Pain Cage automatically activates, granting a powerful teamwide stat bonus for 9 seconds. Because her ultimate is central to her gameplay, this artifact directly amplifies her overall damage impact and team synergy.

Evolution Priority: High – Triggers alongside her ultimate, boosting the entire team during crucial moments.

About Toughness:
🔸Reduces all incoming damage from enemies when Hero's Health is low.
🔸Applies to all damage types (physical, magical, and pure) except Health loss from allied abilities.
🔸Damage reduction formula: (Toughness / (Toughness + enemy’s main stat)) × 100%

2nd - Book Artifact: Grimoire of Fortitude

Stats: Toughness +2967, Health +53394

This artifact significantly boosts survivability. The Health bonus keeps Electra alive longer, while Toughness reduces incoming damage when her health is low, covering all damage types (physical, magical, and pure) except health loss from allied abilities. Since Electra converts defenses into Health, these bonuses synergize perfectly with her kit.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Great for durability and scaling, but less impactful for teamwide boosts than the Weapon artifact.

3rd - Ring Artifact: Strength

Stats: Strength +3990 Each Strength point grants: +40 Health and +1 Physical Attack (since Strength is Electra’s main stat).

The Ring enhances Electra’s personal stats by giving more Health and Physical Attack. While valuable, this artifact only benefits her directly and does not scale as well for team effectiveness as her Weapon or Book artifacts.

Evolution Priority: Low – Good for individual growth, but the least important to prioritize compared to Weapon and Book.

Electra Glyph Evolution Priority

Electra’s glyphs play a key role in maximizing her survivability and offensive power. Since she transforms defensive stats into Health and relies heavily on durability to stay effective, prioritizing survivability glyphs over pure damage ensures the best long-term value in battles.

Magic Attack Glyph

At level 80, this glyph grants a solid boost to skill damage. However, Electra’s strength comes from tanking and scaling Health, so this glyph is less important compared to survivability ones.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Attack

Evolution Priority: Low – Helpful for damage, but far less important than survivability-focused glyphs.

Health Glyph

This glyph directly increases Electra’s survivability, making her much harder to take down in prolonged fights.

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health

Evolution Priority: High – Essential for maximizing her tanking potential and scaling with defensive conversions.

Armor Glyph

Armor is one of the best stats for Electra because it converts directly into Health through her defensive transformation mechanics. This makes it not only valuable for mitigating physical damage but also for amplifying her total Health pool.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Armor

Health Gain from Armor: ≈ +1,078,200 HP

Evolution Priority: Very High – A top priority glyph, as it strengthens both her mitigation and Health scaling.

Toughness Glyph

Toughness reduces all incoming damage when Electra’s Health is low, applying to physical, magical, and pure damage. This makes her harder to burst down during critical moments.

Level 80 Stats: +4170 Toughness

Evolution Priority: High – Provides universal damage reduction and greatly improves her clutch survivability.

Strength Glyph

Strength improves both offense and defense since it is Electra’s main stat, granting her more Health and Physical Attack to support her hybrid role.

Level 80 Stats: +2110 Strength (→ +84,000 Health, +2100 Physical Attack)

Evolution Priority: Medium – Balanced value, useful for scaling, but not as urgent as pure defensive glyphs like Armor or Health.

Talisman of Resurrection

  • Main Stat – Strength +2000: Every Strength point increases Electra’s Health by 40 (totaling +80,000 Health) and adds +1 Physical Attack if Strength were her main stat. Since Electra converts defensive stats into Health, this bonus provides massive survivability scaling.
  • Reroll Slots – Toughness +6600 (sum of 3 slots): All three reroll slots add up, giving Electra a total of +6600 Toughness. This significantly reduces incoming damage from all types (Physical, Magical, Pure), except Health loss from allied abilities. Toughness is especially valuable for Electra as she functions as a tank, protecting her team in extended battles.
  • Synergy: Strength improves her Health pool, while Toughness decreases the effective damage she receives, making her even harder to take down. The combination perfectly complements her skill kit, especially Pain’s Embrace and Oath of Protection.

Conclusion – Electra Hero Wars Alliance

Electra is a versatile front-line tank who excels at absorbing damage and protecting her allies while boosting team Magic Damage. Her unique ability to convert Armor, Magic Defense, and Dodge into Health makes her exceptionally durable, especially when paired with Health and Toughness glyphs and the right artifacts.

Legendary Relics further enhance both her survivability and offensive impact, making her a strong centerpiece for Eternity and mage-focused teams. While she requires investment and team synergy to reach full potential, players who prioritize defensive scaling and team utility will find Electra a highly rewarding hero to develop.
