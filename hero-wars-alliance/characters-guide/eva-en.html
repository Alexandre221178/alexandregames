Eva does not enter a battle to chip away at easy targets. She aims, waits, and turns the enemy's biggest Health bar into a weakness. This Way of Progress Marksman threatens both the tank in front and the vulnerable hero hiding at the back.

What makes her truly exciting is the pressure she shares with other Marksmen. Eva increases their damage, redirects part of it toward a distant marked enemy, and becomes even more dangerous as her Legendary Relic grows. In this guide, I will show you where that power comes from and how to build around it.

Who Is Eva in Hero Wars Alliance?

Eva is a back-line Marksman from the Way of Progress. Her identity is built around two extremes: she aims her ultimate at the nearest enemy, usually a tank, while Focusing Scope marks the farthest enemy and exposes that target to shared damage from allied Marksmen.

  • Main Class: Marksman
  • Role: Marksman
  • Position: Back Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Faction: Way of Progress
  • Realm Buff: Marksmen attack/defense +120%
  • How to get Soul Stones: A permanent source is not currently listed.
  • Synergy: Nebula, Isaac, Judge, Julius, and all Marksmen
  • Hydra Tier List: A

Her signature advantage is percentage-based pressure. Impossible Shot adds damage based on the nearest target's Max Health, and the level 30 Legendary skill Explosive Payload adds another Max Health component whenever Eva deals eligible damage. That makes high-Health tanks a natural target rather than a wall she must slowly overcome.

Hydra tests confirm that Eva deals excellent damage, especially alongside other Marksmen. She did not perform as well as Xe'Sha or Kendle, but her results make her a strong secondary Hydra-team option after a primary team built around Xe'Sha and Kendle.

Eva Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Percentage-based physical damage puts enormous pressure on high-Health tanks.
  • Targets both ends of the enemy formation: the nearest and the farthest heroes.
  • Raises the damage of every allied Marksman, especially against distant targets.
  • Focusing Scope lets allied Marksmen pressure a marked back-line target while attacking elsewhere.
  • Antivirus repeatedly cleanses control and grants short control immunity.
  • Delivers excellent Hydra damage with other Marksmen and earns an A-tier rating.

❌ Cons

  • Impossible Shot requires three seconds of aiming before the shot is fired.
  • Offers almost no crowd control of her own.
  • Needs protection, healing, or shields to survive long enough to deliver repeated shots.
  • Some of her strongest team value depends on bringing other Marksmen.
  • Against Hydras, she does not match the damage of a leading team built around Xe'Sha and Kendle.

Eva Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Eva currently has one listed talisman: Talisman of Candidate. Unlike skins, whose unlocked bonuses remain active, only the talisman equipped for battle contributes its stats. For now, the decision is straightforward, but the reason this talisman works is more interesting than the lack of competition.

1 - Talisman of Candidate

Main Stat Slot: Agility +2,000. Since Agility is Eva's main stat, this becomes Physical Attack +6,000 and Armor +2,000.

Reroll Slots: Armor Penetration +6,600 in each of the three slots, for a combined reroll bonus of Armor Penetration +19,800.

Eva Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Learn exactly how Eva's shots, Marksman buffs, and Legendary Relic upgrades work, then follow a clear priority from her first unlock to level 30.

Recommended base-skill priority: 1. Marksmen Unity, 2. Impossible Shot, 3. Focusing Scope, 4. Piercing Shot. The visual guide below keeps the skills in their in-game order.

Legendary Relic path: Antivirus at level 1, Impossible Shot enhancement at level 10, Piercing Shot enhancement at level 20, and Explosive Payload at level 30. These unlock in a fixed level order; levels 10 and 30 provide Eva's biggest offensive jumps.

1st - Impossible Shot (Base Skill)

In-game description: Aims for 3 seconds, then fires at the nearest enemy, dealing physical damage plus extra physical damage equal to 25% of the target's Max Health.

Skill Explanation: Eva spends three seconds preparing one devastating shot at the nearest enemy. That delay can feel risky, but the reward is 249,228 physical damage (depends on Physical Attack) plus 25% of the target's Max Health as extra physical damage. Because the second part grows with the enemy rather than Eva, tanks and other high-Health heroes become her most attractive targets.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage: 249,228 (depends on Physical Attack)
  • Formula 2: Extra Physical Damage: 25% of the target's Max Health

Skill Animation Info

  • Aim Duration: 3 seconds
  • Targeting: Nearest enemy
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Max Health Damage

Evolution Priority: Very High - This is Eva's main burst skill and her clearest answer to high-Health front-line heroes. Improve it early because it defines her single-target damage.

1st Legendary - Impossible Shot (Enhanced Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 10)

In-game description: Now deals more extra physical damage equal to 50% of the target's Max Health.

Skill Explanation: The Legendary enhancement raises the Max Health portion of Impossible Shot from 25% to 50% of the target's Max Health. It does not add another separate 50%; it improves the original percentage. This is the moment Eva's anti-tank identity becomes genuinely frightening.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Formula 1: Base Physical Damage: 249,228 (depends on Physical Attack)
  • Formula 2: Enhanced Extra Physical Damage: 50% of the target's Max Health

Skill Animation Info

  • Relic Level: 10
  • Aim Duration: 3 seconds
  • Targeting: Nearest enemy
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Max Health Damage, Legendary Enhancement

Evolution Priority: Very High - Make Relic level 10 your first major milestone. Doubling the target-Max-Health portion from 25% to 50% is Eva's most immediate anti-tank power spike.

2nd - Focusing Scope

In-game description: Attacks the farthest enemy and applies the Marked effect to them for 9.0 seconds. While Marked is active, the target additionally takes a portion of the damage dealt by allied Marksmen to other Heroes.

Skill Explanation: Eva reaches past the front line and shoots the farthest enemy for 74,768 physical damage (depends on Physical Attack). The target remains Marked for 9 seconds and receives shared damage equal to 50% of the damage allied Marksmen deal to other heroes. In a Marksman team, attacking the tank can therefore keep hurting a dangerous back-line target.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage: 74,768 (depends on Physical Attack)
  • Formula 2: Shared Damage: 50% of eligible allied Marksman damage

Skill Animation Info

  • Marked Duration: 9 seconds
  • Targeting: Farthest enemy
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Mark, Shared Damage, Marksman Synergy

Evolution Priority: High - Its direct hit is smaller than Eva's ultimate, but the nine-second Mark can multiply team pressure. Raise it early when Eva fights beside several Marksmen.

3rd - Piercing Shot (Base Skill)

In-game description: Aims and fires at all enemies in front of her.

Skill Explanation: Piercing Shot is Eva's straightforward area attack. She aims forward and deals 91,210 physical damage (depends on Physical Attack) to every enemy in front of her. Its value rises when multiple enemies line up because one cast can damage several targets.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage per Enemy Hit: 91,210 (depends on Physical Attack)

Skill Animation Info

  • Targeting: All enemies in front of Eva
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Area Damage

Evolution Priority: Medium High - Useful area damage, but it lacks the Max Health scaling of Impossible Shot and the teamwide value of Eva's Marksman buffs. Build those defining tools first.

3rd Legendary - Piercing Shot (Enhanced Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 20)

In-game description: Now restores 30 Energy for each enemy hit by this skill.

Skill Explanation: Every enemy caught by Piercing Shot returns 30 Energy to Eva. One hit gives 30 Energy, three hits give 90, and five hits give 150. This does not increase the shot's listed damage directly; it helps Eva reach Impossible Shot sooner, so good positioning turns an area attack into faster ultimate pressure.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Formula 1: Physical Damage per Enemy Hit: 91,210 (depends on Physical Attack)
  • Formula 2: Energy Restored: 30 x number of enemies hit

Skill Animation Info

  • Relic Level: 20
  • Targeting: All enemies in front of Eva
  • Energy: 30 per enemy hit
  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Area Damage, Energy Recovery

Evolution Priority: High - The energy gain feeds Eva's strongest shot and becomes better against crowded formations. It is a valuable second Relic milestone after level 10.

4th - Marksmen Unity

In-game description: Increases the damage dealt by Eva and all allied Marksmen. The bonus scales with the distance to the target and can reach up to 400% of the original damage.

Skill Explanation: This passive makes distance part of Eva's damage formula. Eva and every allied Marksman receive their own bonus based on how far each attacker is from the target, up to 400% of the original damage. A hero does not inherit Eva's distance calculation; the game checks the distance separately for every Marksman. The trigger chance is reduced when the Marksman's level is above 120.

Maximum Effect with Talisman 1: Talisman of Candidate

  • Maximum Damage Increase: Up to 400% of the original damage
  • Distance Calculation: Calculated separately for Eva and each allied Marksman.

Skill Animation Info

  • Type: Passive
  • Affected Allies: Eva and all allied Marksmen
  • Keywords: Damage Boost, Distance Scaling, Marksman Synergy

Evolution Priority: Very High - This is Eva's most important team skill. It improves her damage and every allied Marksman's damage, so it should be one of the first base skills you maximize.

5th - Antivirus (New Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 1)

In-game description: Every 8 seconds, Eva gains an effect that removes all control effects and prevents new control effects from being applied for 5.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: Antivirus gives Eva a repeating defensive rhythm. Every 8 seconds, it cleanses all control from her and blocks new control for 5 seconds. That is especially important while she is trying to finish the three-second aim for Impossible Shot, although it does not protect her from raw damage.

Skill Animation Info

  • Relic Level: 1
  • Activation Interval: Every 8 seconds
  • Control Immunity Duration: 5 seconds
  • Keywords: Cleanse, Control Immunity

Evolution Priority: High - Unlocking Antivirus immediately makes Eva more reliable against control teams. It is crucial protection, but her shields, healing, and damage mitigation must still come from allies.

6th - Explosive Payload (New Skill, Legendary Relic: Level 30)

In-game description: Whenever Eva deals damage to a target, it is increased by 10% of the target's Max Health. Does not affect damage over time or shared damage.

Skill Explanation: Explosive Payload adds 10% of the target's Max Health whenever Eva deals eligible damage. It does not activate from damage over time or shared damage, so the 50% damage transferred by Focusing Scope does not receive another Payload increase. Even with those exclusions, repeated direct hits make this Eva's most powerful late Relic upgrade against durable enemies.

  • Keywords: Physical Damage, Max Health Damage

Evolution Priority: Very High - Relic level 30 is expensive, but this upgrade rewards almost every direct hit Eva lands. It is the final goal for players who want her full anti-tank damage package.

Best Skin for Eva Hero Wars Alliance

Eva needs enough damage to punish tanks and enough Health to finish her long aim. Keep the visual skin order, but invest by real battle impact.

Default Skin

Stats: Agility +1,365. Since Agility is Eva's main stat, the full bonus gives Physical Attack +4,095 and Armor +1,365.

Skin Stone Cost: 30,825 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) - Agility directly strengthens Eva's listed physical-damage shots and also gives Armor, helping her survive while aiming Impossible Shot.

Singularity Skin

Stats: Health +106,645.

Skin Stone Cost: 50,410 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: High (2nd Overall) - Eva is vulnerable during her three-second aim. Extra Health helps her finish the cast and stay alive for another Antivirus cycle, but it does not improve her damage directly.

Eva Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Eva's artifacts sharpen her physical burst, critical hits, penetration, and Agility. Keep their visual order while prioritizing the strongest impact.

1st Artifact: Ruiner

Effect at Level 100: Critical Hit Chance +8,898 for the whole team for 9 seconds when Eva uses Impossible Shot. Activation chance: 100%.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) - Eva's ultimate starts her biggest damage window, and Ruiner gives the entire team more critical-hit pressure at that exact moment. Marksman-heavy teams benefit especially well.

2nd Artifact: Prime Access

Effect at Level 100: Critical Hit Chance +2,967 and Armor Penetration +5,340.

Evolution Priority: Very High (2nd Overall) - Both stats support Eva's job. Critical hits raise burst potential, while Armor Penetration helps her direct physical damage cut through the tanks she is designed to target.

3rd Artifact: Ring of Agility

Effect at Level 100: Agility +3,990. Since Agility is Eva's main stat, this gives Physical Attack +11,970 and Armor +3,990.

Evolution Priority: High (3rd Overall) - The Ring gives excellent personal scaling, but Ruiner's teamwide window and Prime Access's penetration usually change Eva's battles sooner.

Eva's glyphs balance damage and survival. Keep their in-game visual order, but invest first in the stats that make every direct shot more dangerous.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) - Eva deliberately fires at front-line tanks, so penetration is essential for converting her high physical-damage numbers into real damage against armored targets.

How to Counter Eva Hero Wars Alliance

Eva wants time, clean aim windows, and predictable targets. The best answers either make her physical shots unreliable or force her to defend the back line instead of calmly charging Impossible Shot.

Dante

Dante increases Dodge for all allies for 7 seconds, making Eva's direct physical shots less reliable during an important damage window. Each successful dodge can also trigger Retribution, adding physical counterpressure and knockback against Eva's team.

Jhu

Jhu can focus a ranged enemy and, after 15 seconds, Mark the farthest enemy so his basic attacks also hit that target. Eva normally stands in the Back Line, so this pressure can force her out before her repeated Max-Health shots decide the fight.

Eva Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Isaac

Isaac brings shields and Physical Attack support for Progress allies. Eva normally stands behind him, allowing her to benefit from his Armor Penetration support while she prepares high-impact physical shots.

Judge

Judge protects allies at both ends of the formation, which is ideal for a Back Line hero like Eva. His shields help her complete the three-second aim, while his stun buys the team another safe damage window.

Julius

Julius gives Eva the front-line protection she needs through shields and buffs. His Critical Hit Chance artifact also supports Eva's burst and the other Marksmen benefiting from Marksmen Unity.

Lara Croft

Lara Croft is another Marksman, so she receives Eva's distance-based damage increase. Her damage also feeds Focusing Scope, allowing the duo to attack one enemy while continuing to pressure Eva's marked back-line target.

Nebula

Nebula strengthens Eva's offensive tempo and helps remove harmful effects. Her support lets Eva attack faster and stay functional long enough to cycle Impossible Shot, Focusing Scope, and Piercing Shot.

Polaris

Polaris does not amplify Eva as directly as another Marksman, but her control can freeze the enemy's rhythm. Those pauses help Eva finish Impossible Shot and give the rest of the team time to protect the Back Line.

Eva Guide Conclusion

Eva turns the enemy formation into a two-sided problem. Impossible Shot punishes the nearest high-Health target, Focusing Scope exposes the farthest hero, and Marksmen Unity rewards teams that attack from range. Her three-second aim keeps her from being effortless to use, but that weakness also makes good shields, healing, and timing feel meaningful.

For the most reliable build, develop Armor Penetration and Physical Attack, equip Talisman of Candidate, and surround Eva with Progress protection or another Marksman. Relic level 10 delivers her first huge anti-tank spike; level 30 completes the plan by adding Max-Health damage to almost every eligible hit.

Alexandre's Final Verdict: Eva is a high-ceiling Marksman with exceptional anti-tank and back-line pressure. I would invest in her for Progress or Marksman teams. Her tested A-tier Hydra performance also makes her an excellent secondary option, although a leading team built around Xe'Sha and Kendle still deals more damage.
