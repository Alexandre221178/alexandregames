Who Is Byrna in Hero Wars Alliance?

Byrna is a dedicated Healer from the Way of Nature Faction, positioned in the Middle Line. Driven by a mission to free the orcs, she is a crucial ally who combines powerful healing and debuff cleansing with surprisingly high damage from her bear spirit.

  • Glyph Class: Healer
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Nature
  • How to get Soul Stones: (Special Event only)
  • Synergy: Mushy, Alvanor, Mojo, Soleil, Maya
  • Hydra Tier List: (To be filled with her Hydra rating/performance)

Byrna's primary function is to sustain her team by providing burst healing and granting much-needed Energy. Her signature ability, calling on the Guardian Spirit, not only delivers a punishing roar to enemies but also provides essential crowd-control immunity by purging Control effects from allies. This makes her invaluable against teams heavy on stuns, silences, and other disabling effects.

As an Intelligence hero, investing in her main stat boosts both her healing output and her magical damage, making her a dual-threat support hero that can keep your front line healthy while contributing to the overall damage count.

Byrna Skill Guide for Hero Wars: Alliance

Byrna is one of the strongest support healers in Hero Wars: Alliance, combining constant healing, energy recovery, and a powerful Guardian Spirit that deals damage while protecting the team. This guide explains every skill in detail so you can use Byrna at her full potential.

1st Roar of Nature

Byrna summons her Guardian Spirit to unleash a wave of energy that deals magic damage to enemies. At the same time, Byrna and nearby allies restore Health, making this skill excellent for sustained team survival during long battles.

2nd Guardian Punch

The Guardian Spirit rushes across the battlefield attacking enemies. It cannot be targeted and fully inherits Byrna’s Magic Attack and Magic Penetration. In addition, Byrna’s basic attacks restore Health and Energy to the ally with the lowest Health, giving your team constant sustain.

3rd Bear Cuddle

Byrna applies a protective effect to the ally with the lowest Health for 20 seconds. Every 2 seconds, this ally restores Health and gains Energy, providing reliable survivability that is especially effective for fragile heroes.

4th Living Heart

If an ally is fully healed, any additional healing from Byrna or other support heroes is converted into bonus Maximum Health. This allows your team to scale defensively throughout the fight, making them significantly harder to kill.

Byrna’s Artifacts in Hero Wars: Alliance

Byrna is the first hero in Hero Wars: Alliance to receive a complete set of four artifacts at launch, making her one of the most unique support heroes ever added to the game. Because she is brand new, it is still too early to define the best artifact priority or long-term optimization strategy—we must wait for more testing and future balance adjustments to understand her final meta role.

Talisman of the Orc Princess

Provides Intelligence and Magic Penetration, directly boosting both Byrna’s damage contribution through her Guardian Spirit and the effectiveness of her healing skills, which scale from Magic Attack.

Guardian’s Fang

Increases Magic Penetration. This artifact improves Byrna’s overall magical pressure by allowing her Guardian Spirit to bypass enemy magic defenses more effectively.

Tome of Arcane Knowledge

Grants Magic Attack and Health. The Magic Attack increases the impact of both her damage-dealing skills and her healing, while the bonus Health helps Byrna stay alive longer during extended battles.

Ring of Intelligence

Provides a direct boost to Intelligence, increasing Byrna’s Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and overall scaling. This artifact helps strengthen every aspect of her support role.

Conclusion

Byrna enters Hero Wars: Alliance as a uniquely powerful support hero, combining consistent healing, energy regeneration, and a versatile Guardian Spirit that reinforces both offense and survivability. Her kit scales extremely well with Magic Attack and Intelligence, making her a strong long-term investment for many team compositions.

Because she is the first hero to launch with four artifacts, her final meta role is still developing. As more players test her in different modes—Arena, Grand Arena, Guild Wars, and Adventures—we will gain a clearer understanding of her best builds, ideal partners, and artifact priorities.

For now, Byrna already shows tremendous potential as one of the most reliable sustain supports in the game. If you enjoy durable, healing-focused teams, she is absolutely a hero worth leveling and experimenting with as the meta evolves.
