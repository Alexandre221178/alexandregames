Welcome, Guardian! Are you ready to command a healer who's as fierce as she is compassionate? Who Is Byrna in Hero Wars Alliance?

Byrna is a dedicated Healer from the Way of Nature Faction, positioned in the Middle Line. Driven by a mission to free the orcs, she is a crucial ally who combines powerful healing and debuff cleansing with surprisingly high damage from her bear spirit.

  • Glyph Class: Healer
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Nature
  • How to get Soul Stones: (Special Event only)
  • Synergy: Mushy, Alvanor, Mojo, Soleil, Maya
  • Hydra Tier List: (To be filled with her Hydra rating/performance)

Byrna's primary function is to sustain her team by providing burst healing and granting much-needed Energy. Her signature ability, calling on the Guardian Spirit, not only delivers a punishing roar to enemies but also provides essential crowd-control immunity by purging Control effects from allies. This makes her invaluable against teams heavy on stuns, silences, and other disabling effects.

As an Intelligence hero, investing in her main stat boosts both her healing output and her magical damage, making her a dual-threat support hero that can keep your front line healthy while contributing to the overall damage count.

Byrna Pros and Cons - Hero Wars (Mobile)

✅ Pros

  • Unmatched Survivability Stacking: Her passive skill, Living Heart, provides permanent Maximum Health increases throughout the battle, making her entire team significantly tankier over time.
  • Dual Utility: She combines burst healing (Roar of Nature) with persistent damage output (Guardian Punch), ensuring she contributes offensively and defensively.
  • Crowd Control (CC) Cleansing: Her Legendary Relic enhancements (on Roar of Nature and Bear Cuddle) provide crucial team-wide and single-target cleansing of negative effects, highly counteracting control teams.
  • Scales Exceptionally Well: All her core skills scale with Magic Attack, meaning investments in her Intelligence skin and artifacts yield high returns across her entire kit (healing, damage, and Health stacking).
  • Energy Synergy: She provides Energy to allies via Bear Cuddle and her Living Heart Relic enhancement, accelerating her teammates' Ultimate abilities.

❌ Cons

  • Vulnerable to Burst Damage: Although she stacks health permanently, she can be susceptible to enemies that deal high, rapid burst damage early in the fight, preventing her from starting the Living Heart stack.
  • Healing is Magic Attack-Dependent: Her entire support efficacy hinges on her Magic Attack stat. If she is heavily debuffed (e.g., Magic Attack reduction), her healing output plummets drastically.
  • Midline Hero Positioning: As a mid-line hero, she is often placed in the line of fire for AoE (Area of Effect) attacks, making the Health Glyph/Skin absolutely essential for her survival.
  • Low Initial Impact: Her signature strength (permanent Health stacking) takes time to build up. She is less effective in extremely fast battles or against teams designed to win in the first 10-15 seconds.

Byrna's Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Ready to command the power of the wild? Byrna is a strategic force of nature who heals, deals damage, and boosts your team's survival! Knowing which skills to level up first is the key to making her bear spirit unstoppable. This guide breaks down every skill, explaining the 'why' behind each priority ranking.

1st – Roar of Nature

This is Byrna’s Ultimate Ability and her most impactful moment! The Guardian Spirit unleashes a massive roar, which acts as a powerful two-in-one move: it deals a huge chunk of Magic Damage to enemies AND restores a significant amount of Health to Byrna and her nearby allies.

This is your big 'panic button' for both damage and healing. The Magic Damage inflicted is 148,321, calculated as: (85% Mag. atk. + 6000). The Health regeneration is 50% of the total calculation.

Why upgrade? This skill is the single biggest burst of healing and damage Byrna provides. Maximizing it ensures her survivability and team contribution spike dramatically when the Guardian Spirit roars.

2nd – Guardian Punch

This skill defines Byrna’s basic combat routine. The Guardian Spirit zips across the battlefield, attacking enemies. The Spirit cannot be targeted and inherits Byrna's Magic Attack and Magic Penetration. Additionally, Byrna's normal basic attacks now restore Health and Energy to the ally with the lowest Health. The Magic Damage dealt by the Spirit is 334,872, calculated as: (200% Mag. atk.). The Health regeneration per attack is 176,436, calculated as: (Mag. atk. + 9000). The Energy gain is fixed at (100).

Why upgrade? This skill is a consistent damage contribution, but the healing is a lower flat amount compared to her other skills. Since Byrna's strength is utility and stacking stats, this damage-focused skill is a lower priority.

3rd – Bear Cuddle

Bear Cuddle applies a persistent effect (a "buff") on the ally with the lowest Health for 20 seconds. This ally restores Health and gains Energy every 2 seconds. Think of it as a protective, continuous hug from the bear spirit! This is a fantastic sustain tool that keeps a vulnerable hero alive and helps them charge their Ultimate. The Health restored per 2 seconds is 85,518, calculated as: (50% Mag. atk. + 1800). The Energy gain is fixed at (100).

Why upgrade? This is a continuous heal, excellent for sustain, but the fixed Energy gain and lower burst healing make it less impactful than the Ultimate and the passive health stack.

In Hero Wars Alliance, every hero has a 1,000-point Energy bar. During battles, any effect that shows “+10% Energy” corresponds to a flat gain of 100 Energy. That is why Byrna’s Bear Cuddle skill displays an Energy gain of 100 in the combat log — it is simply 10% of the total Energy bar.


3rd - Bear Cuddle Legendary: Relic Level 10

The Legendary enhancement makes the Bear Cuddle hug even better: when the skill restores an ally's Health, all negative effects are removed (purged) from them. This turns your continuous healing into a constant form of crowd-control protection for your most fragile ally. There is no damage/healing formula here, only the powerful utility of cleansing negative effects.

Why upgrade? This skill provides immense, permanent long-term tankiness for your whole team. It’s a core mechanic you want at maximum efficiency ASAP.

Byrna Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Talisman of the Orc Princess

The Talisman of the Orc Princess provides a powerful +2000 Intelligence as its main stat, greatly amplifying Byrna’s healing and magical damage. Each point of Intelligence increases Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and Physical Attack (since Intelligence is Byrna’s primary stat). This results in +6000 Magic Attack, +2000 Magic Defense, and +2000 Physical Attack from the main stat alone.

This talisman also contains three reroll slots, each capable of rolling up to +6600 Magic Penetration. However, these slots should be treated as a single combined value because the only thing that truly matters is the total Magic Penetration after rerolling. With maximum rolls, Byrna can gain an incredible +19,800 Magic Penetration from this talisman alone.

This works perfectly with her Ultimate, Roar of Nature, which deals Magic Damage scaling from her Magic Attack and activates her weapon artifact, granting an additional +32,040 Magic Penetration. Together, these bonuses allow Byrna’s Guardian Spirit to cut through enemy defenses with exceptional efficiency.

Bearocious Talisman

The Bearocious Talisman provides +12,000 Armor as its main stat, a purely defensive bonus that increases Byrna's physical damage resistance. Unlike the Orc Princess, this talisman does not boost Intelligence or Magic Attack, meaning it contributes nothing to Byrna's healing output or magical damage scaling.

Its three reroll slots each provide +2,200 Magic Reflection, for a total of +6,600 Magic Reflection. Magic Reflection returns a small portion of incoming magic damage back to the attacker, but the reflected amount is relatively modest and does not reduce the damage Byrna takes.

While the Armor bonus may seem appealing for survivability, Byrna is a support hero whose value comes from healing and buffing — not from tanking physical hits. The Bearocious Talisman does not improve any of her core skills: Roar of Nature (85% Mag.atk.), Guardian Punch (200% Mag.atk.), or Bear Cuddle (50% Mag.atk.) all scale exclusively with Magic Attack.

Best Talisman for PVP: Talisman of the Orc Princess — The Orc Princess is overwhelmingly superior for PVP because every single one of Byrna's skills scales with Magic Attack, and her Guardian Spirit inherits her Magic Penetration. Here's why:

  • +19,800 Magic Penetration — In PVP, enemies have high Magic Defense. The Orc Princess nearly doubles Byrna's total penetration (44,288 vs 24,488), allowing her Guardian Spirit to cut through defenses and deal meaningful damage.
  • +6,000 Magic Attack (from +2,000 Intelligence) — This directly amplifies all healing skills: Roar of Nature (+5,100 more heal), Guardian Punch (+6,000 more heal per hit), and Bear Cuddle (+3,000 more heal per tick). In fast PVP fights, this healing difference often decides who survives.
  • +2,000 Magic Defense — A useful bonus against enemy mages, superior to Armor in most PVP matchups where magic damage dominates.
  • Bearocious Talisman's Armor and Magic Reflection are wasted stats — Byrna is a support hero on the back/mid-line who rarely absorbs physical hits. Magic Reflection (+6,600) only reflects a minor amount of magic damage back without reducing the damage she takes — far less impactful than the Orc Princess's massive offensive and healing gains.

The Talisman of the Orc Princess transforms Byrna from a decent healer into a PVP powerhouse whose Guardian Spirit shreds through enemy defenses while her healing keeps the team alive. The Bearocious Talisman offers marginal survivability in exchange for a massive loss in her core role.

Best Skin for Byrna Hero Wars Alliance

Ready to truly unleash the Way of Nature's power? Byrna's skins are crucial for boosting her core stats—Intelligence and Health—which directly affect her healing, damage, and survivability! Learn which skins to upgrade first to make her unstoppable.

Default Skin

The Default Skin focuses on Byrna's primary attribute: Intelligence. Upgrading this skin boosts her healing output, her magic damage, and her magic defense all at once, making it the most important skin for her core function.

  • Intelligence: +1,365
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +4,095
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +1,365
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +1,365

Evolution Priority: Very High – As an Intelligence-based healer and damage dealer, maximizing this stat should always be the top priority to increase the effectiveness of nearly all her skills (Roar of Nature, Living Heart, Bear Cuddle).

Winter Skin

The Winter Skin grants a large Magic Penetration bonus, directly increasing Byrna's damage output and the effectiveness of her Magic-based heals and debuffs.

  • Magic Penetration: +10,650

Evolution Priority: Very High – After the Default (Intelligence) skin, this is the best choice to maximize Byrna's damage contribution and ensure her heals and abilities bypass enemy Magic Defense. Prioritize this before pure Health skins in most team builds.

Why this matters: Byrna's Guardian Spirit deals Magic Damage to enemies while Byrna and nearby allies restore Health. Increasing Magic Penetration makes that damage more effective, amplifying both Byrna's damage contribution and the overall healing/utility that triggers from the interaction.

Cybernetic Skin

The Cybernetic Skin provides a massive boost to Byrna's Health. While she is a middle-line hero, increasing her Health is vital for her survivability, ensuring she can withstand area damage and continue healing the team, especially against unexpected bursts of enemy aggression.

  • Health: +106,645

Evolution Priority: High – Survivability is the second most crucial factor for any support hero. If Byrna dies, the entire healing and health-stacking mechanism stops. Max this immediately after her Default (Intelligence) skin.

Tribal Skin+ (Skin+)

The Tribal Skin+ is Byrna's 4th skin and her most powerful offensive skin! This is a Skin+ variant, which means it combines the base Tribal Skin (Magic Attack +10,650) with the enhanced Tribal Skin+ upgrade, effectively doubling the stats once equipped — for a total of +21,300 Magic Attack.

  • Magic Attack (Tribal base): +10,650
  • Magic Attack (Tribal Skin+): +21,300 (doubled when Skin+ is equipped)

Evolution Priority: Extremely High (Best Skin) – With +21,300 Magic Attack, this is by far the most impactful skin for Byrna. Magic Attack directly scales ALL her healing and damage skills: Roar of Nature (85% MA), Guardian Punch (200% MA), Bear Cuddle (50% MA), and the permanent Health stacking from Living Heart. This single skin provides more raw offensive and healing power than any other skin.

Alexandre Tips: The base Tribal Skin can be purchased with Skin Certificates after 6 months of the hero's release — this part is free-to-play friendly. However, the Skin+ upgrade is much harder to obtain. The best opportunity is during the launch event when the Skin+ is first released. After the event ends, the Skin+ can only be obtained through luck in the Heroic Chest, making it significantly harder to acquire. If possible, prioritize getting the Skin+ during its launch event!

Ready to focus Byrna's raw natural power? Artifacts are key to boosting the stats that make her heals colossal and her team survivability unmatched. Prioritizing these will dramatically increase her overall battlefield utility!

1st - Weapon Artifact: Guardian's Fang

This artifact provides a massive boost to Magic Penetration, a crucial offensive stat for Intelligence heroes. Crucially, its effect is guaranteed (100% chance) to activate when Byrna uses her Ultimate skill, Roar of Nature, granting a temporary bonus to the whole team.

It ensures her bear spirit's damage (Roar of Nature and Guardian Punch) is not negated by enemy Magic Defense, and the 100% team buff activation is invaluable for team synergy.

2nd - Book Artifact: Tome of Arcane Knowledge

The Book artifact is essential as it provides a large mix of base stats that Byrna needs: Magic Attack and Health. Boosting Magic Attack directly amplifies her healing and overall utility, while Health provides the necessary survivability.

  • Health: +53,394

Evolution Priority: High – This provides a necessary base boost to her Magic Attack, directly increasing the strength of her heals and damage, and crucial Health for her survival. It's a foundational upgrade for her effectiveness.

3rd - Ring of Intelligence Artifact:

The Ring artifact directly boosts Byrna's primary stat, Intelligence. Increasing this stat raises her Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and even Physical Attack, enhancing the effectiveness of almost all her skills.

  • Intelligence: +3,990
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +11,970
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +3,990
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +3,990

Evolution Priority: Medium High – While the Ring provides the biggest direct boost to her Intelligence (the stat her skills scale with), the Weapon's team utility and Magic Penetration are prioritized first, followed by the raw base stats from the Book. The Ring is the third step to solidify her scaling.

Byrna Glyph Evolution Priority

Unlocking Byrna's true potential means focusing on the stats that power her unique healing and health-stacking mechanics! Glyphs provide raw statistical power, making them crucial for boosting her utility and ensuring she stays alive long enough to turn the tide of battle.

1st - Magic Attack Glyph:

Magic Attack (MA) is the most crucial scaling stat for Byrna. Every single one of her healing and damage skills (Roar of Nature, Guardian Punch, Bear Cuddle, and the permanent Health stack from Living Heart) scales directly off her MA. Maximizing this stat ensures all her core abilities hit their maximum potential.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) – This is her highest priority because it enhances both her primary role (healing/support) and her secondary role (damage) simultaneously.

2nd - Health Glyph:

Health is the foundation of survival. As a midline support hero, Byrna needs a large Health pool to withstand area-of-effect (AoE) damage and continue supporting her team. A dead healer is useless, no matter how powerful her healing stats are.

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health.

Evolution Priority: High (2nd Overall) – Survival comes immediately after her core scaling stat. The sheer amount of Health gained here is necessary to ensure she stays on the field to stack permanent health via Living Heart.

3rd - Magic Defense Glyph:

Magic Defense (MD) reduces the damage Byrna takes from enemy mages and Magic Attack-based heroes. While not as important as Health, protecting her from magic damage is crucial in the current meta, where many powerful damage dealers use MA.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Defense.

Evolution Priority: Medium (4th Overall) – Survivability (Health) is a higher priority, but MD is an excellent secondary defensive stat. It should be leveled after the primary attack and survival glyphs.

4th - Magic Penetration Glyph:

Magic Penetration (MPen) allows Byrna's damage-dealing skills (Roar of Nature, Guardian Punch) to bypass the enemy's Magic Defense. While she is primarily a healer, MPen ensures her burst damage is effective against tanks and utility heroes.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Penetration.

Evolution Priority: Medium High (3rd Overall) – Although Byrna is a support, her damage contributions are important, especially with the Magic Penetration buff from her Artifact Weapon. Because of this synergy, it's slightly more important than raw Magic Defense to ensure her damage contributes meaningfully.

5th - Intelligence Glyph:

Intelligence is Byrna's main stat. Leveling this glyph provides a significant multi-faceted boost, as every point grants her Magic Attack, Magic Defense, and Physical Attack.

  • Intelligence: +2,110
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +6,330
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +2,110
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +2,110

Evolution Priority: Medium (5th Overall) – While Intelligence is her main stat, the Magic Attack Glyph (1st priority) gives a higher raw Magic Attack bonus. This Intelligence Glyph should be leveled after the top three priorities to gain the secondary defensive (MD) and Physical Attack benefits.

Conclusion

Byrna enters Hero Wars: Alliance as a uniquely powerful support hero, combining consistent healing, energy regeneration, and a versatile Guardian Spirit that reinforces both offense and survivability. Her kit scales extremely well with Magic Attack and Intelligence, making her a strong long-term investment for many team compositions.

Her final meta role is still developing. As more players test her in different modes Arena, Grand Arena, Guild Wars, and Adventures we will gain a clearer understanding of her best builds, ideal partners, priorities.

For now, Byrna already shows tremendous potential as one of the most reliable sustain supports in the game. If you enjoy durable, healing-focused teams, she is absolutely a hero worth leveling and experimenting with as the meta evolves.
