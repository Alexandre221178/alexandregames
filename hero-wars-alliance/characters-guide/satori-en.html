Who Is Satori in Hero Wars Alliance?

Satori is a front-line Mage from the Way of Mystery. He wins by placing Fox Fire marks, punishing bonus energy, stealing Intelligence, and converting long fights into devastating magical burst.

His kit is simple to understand but very punishing for opponents: the longer enemies carry marks, the scarier Ravenous Pack becomes. Against teams that gain bonus energy, Looming Justice can quickly stack 9 Fox Fire marks and create a sudden knockout window.

  • Main Class: Mage
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Mystery
  • How to get Soul Stones: Events, Heroic Chest, Highwaymen Shop
  • Synergy: Byrna, Cascade, Celeste, Amira, Lian
  • Hydra Tier List: B
  • Hero Tier List: S+

Satori's strongest identity is anti-energy punishment. He is especially dangerous when protected long enough to stack marks, steal Intelligence, and trigger Ravenous Pack at the right moment.

Satori Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

Pros

  • Excellent burst when Fox Fire marks are stacked before Ravenous Pack.
  • Looming Justice punishes enemies that gain bonus energy.
  • Centurial Wisdom steals Intelligence and helps Satori scale in longer fights.
  • Strong Mystery synergy with Amira, Celeste, Cascade, Byrna, and Lian.

Cons

  • Needs protection because he fights from the front line.
  • Damage depends on marks staying on enemies until activation.
  • Heavy Legendary Relic investment is needed to unlock his full kit.
  • Can struggle against anti-magic and cleanse-heavy teams.

Satori Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Satori has two talismans, but only the equipped talisman gives its bonus in battle. For most fights, Talisman of Cunning is the stronger choice because it improves his damage scaling and magic penetration.

Talisman of Cunning

The main slot gives Intelligence +2,000. Because Intelligence is Satori's main stat, this means Magic Attack +6,000, Magic Defense +2,000, and Physical Attack +2,000. The three reroll slots give Magic Penetration +19,800 total.

Talisman of Prudence

The main slot gives Armor +12,000, and the three reroll slots give Health +181,500 total. Choose it when Satori is being eliminated too quickly, especially against physical pressure.

Alexandre Tips: Use Talisman 1, Talisman of Cunning, more often when Satori is protected in the middle line or behind a stronger front line, because the extra Magic Penetration helps him turn Fox Fire marks into real damage. Use Talisman 2, Talisman of Prudence, more often when Satori is forced to play on the front line, because Armor and Health give him more time to survive until his marks explode.

Satori Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Satori's best upgrades improve Fox Fire mark damage, mark generation, and survival, turning enemy bonus energy into a dangerous mistake.

1st - Ravenous Pack

In-game description: The hero's basic attacks deal magic damage and apply Fox Fire marks to enemies. When the skill is activated, the enemies take damage for each effect applied to them. Satori is immune to control effects while using this skill.

Skill Explanation: This is Satori's main burst button. Each Fox Fire mark deals magic damage, so the skill becomes stronger when Spirit Banishment, Looming Justice, and Searing Claws have already stacked marks. With Talisman of Cunning, each mark reaches 138,229 (70% Magic Attack + 18,000); with Talisman of Prudence, each mark reaches 134,029 (70% Magic Attack + 18,000).

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Prudence

Skill Animation Info

  • Range: 590

Evolution Priority: Very High - It is the payoff for every Fox Fire mark Satori applies, so upgrading it directly improves his main win condition.

1st - Ravenous Pack Enhancement - Legendary Relic Level 10

In-game description: Now, upon activating Ravenous Pack, Satori removes all negative effects from himself and becomes immune to them while the skill is active.

Skill Explanation: This upgrade protects the exact moment Satori needs most: the Ravenous Pack activation. Cleansing negative effects helps him complete the cast and detonate marks instead of being shut down.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Cunning

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Prudence

Evolution Priority: High - It does not add a new damage formula, but it makes Satori's most important skill much harder to interrupt.

2nd - Spirit Banishment

In-game description: Satori attacks all enemies, dealing damage and applying a Fox Fire mark. Applies an extra mark for each allied Mystery Hero.

Skill Explanation: This is Satori's broad mark setup. It damages all enemies and creates the mark base that Ravenous Pack later converts into burst damage. With Talisman of Cunning, the hit reaches 106,653 (60% Magic Attack + 3,600); with Talisman of Prudence, it reaches 103,053 (60% Magic Attack + 3,600).

2nd - Spirit Banishment Enhancement - Legendary Relic Level 20

In-game description: Spirit Banishment now applies 1 additional mark.

Skill Explanation: One extra mark may look small, but on Satori it matters because every extra mark becomes another Ravenous Pack damage instance.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Cunning

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Prudence

  • Formula: No separate damage formula is defined. The value comes from creating one more mark for Ravenous Pack to consume later.

Evolution Priority: High - It strengthens Satori's mark economy, which is the engine of his burst damage.

3rd - Centurial Wisdom

In-game description: Each instance of successfully damaging an enemy steals some Intelligence from them. Intelligence remains stolen until the end of the battle or the death of Satori. This cannot be overturned. Satori can't steal more Intelligence than an enemy has. Each successful basic attack restores Hero's health in the amount of 300% of damage dealt.

Skill Explanation: This passive helps Satori snowball in longer fights. Intelligence steal weakens enemy magic heroes while improving Satori's own scaling path through his main stat. The healing is based on damage dealt, so the equipped talisman still matters through Satori's final damage stats.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Cunning

Formula with Talisman 2: Talisman of Prudence

Evolution Priority: Medium High - It is excellent for scaling and sustain, but Satori's mark skills should usually come first.

4th - Looming Justice

In-game description: Satori applies 9 Fox Fire marks to enemies each time they gain bonus energy. The energy gained from damage dealt to a hero, using a base attack, using a skill, or killing an enemy is not considered bonus energy.

Skill Explanation: This is Satori's anti-energy trap. When the enemy team relies on bonus energy, he can quickly place enough marks to threaten a massive Ravenous Pack burst.

Formula with Talisman 1: Talisman of Cunning

5th - Searing Claws - New Legendary Skill Level 1

In-game description: Every 17.0 seconds, deals magic damage to all enemies with Fox Fire marks without removing them. If no enemies have marks, applies 6 marks to each enemy.

Skill Explanation: It gives Satori a passive damage cycle every 17 seconds against marked enemies, and if the enemy team has no marks yet, it creates 6 marks on each enemy instead. With Talisman of Cunning, the hit reaches 103,053 (60% Magic Attack); with Talisman of Prudence, it reaches 99,453 (60% Magic Attack).

6th - Cunning Fox - New Legendary Skill Level 30

In-game description: Enemies with 4 or more Fox Fire marks deal less damage to Satori.

Skill Explanation: Cunning Fox rewards Satori for doing what he already wants to do: spreading marks. Once enemies have 4 or more marks, Satori receives a major defensive benefit.

How to Counter Satori Hero Wars Alliance

Celeste

Celeste can pressure healing and support anti-magic control plans, making it harder for Satori to survive long enough to detonate marks.

Cornelius

Cornelius is dangerous because Satori is an Intelligence hero. If Satori becomes the highest Intelligence target, Cornelius can punish him heavily.

Iris

Iris can counter Satori's Fire Fox Marks and punish him, making it harder for Satori to focus on his primary role.

Julius

Julius plus Isaac and Judge are a natural shield counter against Satori Fox Marks. Julius can clean up the marks when an allied shield are destroyed.

Phobos

Phobos can paralyze enemies with highest magic attack, making it harder for Satori to apply his Fox Fire Marks.

Rufus

Rufus resists magical damage plans, which makes him one of the most direct defensive answers to Satori's Fox Fire burst.

Nebula

Nebula can clean up Fire Fox Marks from two closer allies with here equilibrium passive skill.

Satori Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Amira

Amira fits Mystery teams and adds strong magical pressure, helping Satori punish enemies while they deal with multiple magic threats.

Byrna

Byrna is listed as a top synergy because she supports with healing, energy gain and helps Satori play into longer, more controlled fights.

Cascade

Cascade adds Mystery synergy and helps create more layered pressure, making it harder for enemies to focus only on Satori.

Celeste

Celeste brings healing and counter healing support, giving Satori more time to stack Fox Fire marks and convert them into burst.

Lian

Lian adds control and Mystery synergy, slowing enemy rhythm while Satori builds toward Ravenous Pack.

Satori Guide Conclusion

Satori is a high-pressure magical damage dealer who becomes terrifying when enemies feed him bonus energy triggers. His best builds focus on Magic Penetration, Magic Attack, and enough survival to let Fox Fire marks reach their payoff.

For most battles, prioritize Talisman of Cunning, the Beach Skin, Magic Attack and Magic Penetration glyphs, and his Ravenous Pack upgrades. If he is falling too early, switch to Talisman of Prudence and invest more into Health and Armor before chasing maximum damage.
