Nebula has always been one of those Support heroes who can make a damage dealer look unstoppable—but the old version demanded perfect positioning, timing, and a little luck. Her rework changes that feeling. She attacks less often as she develops, yet her healing and buffs become far more dependable, turning her into a true battlefield conductor.

The new Legendary Relic skills solve several of Nebula's oldest problems: Serenity can cleanse the whole team, Equilibrium can empower two allies at once, Disharmony protects against Damage over Time, and Deep Meditation keeps buffs and first Artifact effects active longer. In this guide, I will show you what these changes mean in real battles, which Talisman I prefer, and where your resources create the biggest impact.

Who Is Nebula in Hero Wars Alliance?

Nebula is a middle-line Way of Progress Support whose value comes from making nearby allies better. She can heal and cleanse, accelerate allies behind her, block incoming Damage over Time, burn enemy Energy, and provide enormous Physical and Magic Attack buffs.

  • Main Class: Support
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Agility
  • Faction: Way of Progress
  • Realm Buff: Spearmen Attack/Defense +120%
  • Best Synergy: Eva, Isaac, Judge, and Julius

What makes the rework exciting is consistency. Before it, Equilibrium could land on the wrong ally or miss the exact moment a damage dealer used an ultimate. At Legendary Relic level 20, Nebula buffs both nearest allies simultaneously, so the team no longer needs a lucky timing window to receive her biggest offensive advantage.

Disharmony also gives Nebula a clear counter role. Its protection blocks incoming Damage over Time for Nebula and allies behind her, which is especially valuable against Iris and other heroes who build pressure through repeated damage effects.

Nebula Overall Rating in the Meta

This editorial rating reflects her reworked kit, Legendary Relic value, team impact, and reliance on correct positioning.

  • Crowd Control (CC): ★★☆☆☆ — Energy burn disrupts enemy tempo, but she has no hard stun or silence.
  • Damage Output: ★★☆☆☆ — Her own damage is secondary to the damage she enables.
  • Team Synergy: ★★★★★ — Healing, cleansing, speed, Main Stat, Attack, and Artifact extension benefit many lineups.
  • Self-Sufficiency: ★★★☆☆ — She can heal or buff herself in limited formations, but her greatest value still depends on allies.

Nebula Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Massive Physical and Magic Attack support through Equilibrium.
  • Full-team healing and cleansing after the level 10 Relic upgrade.
  • Blocks Damage over Time and helps counter Iris.
  • Extends allied buffs and first Artifact effects by 2 seconds.
  • Fits naturally into strong Way of Progress and Dodge cores.

❌ Cons

  • Much of her power is locked behind high Legendary Relic levels.
  • Positioning decides which allies receive several of her effects.
  • Provides limited personal damage and no hard crowd control.
  • Talisman of Consistency sacrifices Armor and Dodge for offense.

Nebula Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Nebula's rework turns precise timing into reliable teamwide momentum. Here is every skill in game order, with practical priorities for beginners and experienced players.

0 - Basic Attack

In-game description: Nebula's basic attack deals physical damage.

Skill Explanation: Before Equilibrium changes her attacks to Support mode, each basic hit deals 100% Physical Attack. At maximum stats, that is 145,933 with Talisman of Consistency or 126,733 with Talisman of Stars.

  • Cooldown: 5.2 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.66 seconds
  • Damage Type: Physical Damage

Evolution Priority: Low — Nebula is built to support, and her rework makes her attack less often as her buffs improve.

1st Skill – Astral Projection

In-game description: Launches a projectile that deals damage to enemies within its area of effect and burns their Energy upon activation. The damage and Energy burn are divided equally among affected enemies. If not activated manually, the projectile explodes automatically upon reaching the farthest enemy.

Skill Explanation: You can activate the projectile where it can hit the targets that matter most, or let it travel to the farthest enemy. Its total Physical Damage depends on Physical Attack, while the total Energy burn is 100. Because both values are shared among affected enemies, fewer targets mean stronger pressure on each one.

  • Energy burned: 100 total
  • Damage Type: Physical Damage
  • Activation: Manual, or automatic at the farthest enemy

Evolution Priority: Medium — useful for disrupting enemy ultimates, but Nebula's buffs and protection usually decide more battles.

2nd Skill – Serenity

In-game description: Restores Health to the two nearest allies and removes negative effects from them. If there is only one nearby ally, Nebula applies this skill to that ally and herself.

Skill Explanation: Serenity combines healing and cleansing in one action. The recovered Health depends on Nebula's Physical Attack, so Talisman of Consistency improves its scaling. The removal chance is reduced when the enemy that applied the negative effect is above level 120.

Evolution Priority: High — keeping two nearby allies active is valuable, especially when control or debuffs would otherwise interrupt your damage plan.

2nd Skill – Serenity Enhanced Legendary Relic: Level 10

In-game description: Heals Nebula and all allies, removing negative effects from them.

Skill Explanation: This upgrade changes Serenity from a local rescue into a full-team reset. Everyone receives the heal and cleanse, including Nebula, so a single activation can recover a formation that has been covered in negative effects.

Evolution Priority: Very High — expanding a two-target heal and cleanse to the entire team is one of her biggest reliability upgrades.

3rd Skill – Disharmony

In-game description: Increases Movement Speed and Skill Recharge Speed of herself and allies behind her by 25% for 5.0 seconds, and blocks incoming Damage Over Time.

Skill Explanation: For 5 seconds, Nebula and allies behind her gain 25% Movement Speed and 25% Skill Recharge Speed. More importantly, incoming Damage over Time is blocked. This directly answers repeated effects such as Iris's fourth-skill burn, Yasmine's poison, Heidi's toxin damage, and several other periodical damage sources.

  • Keywords: Buff, Damage over Time Protection
  • Duration: 5 seconds
  • Targets: Nebula and allies behind her

Evolution Priority: Very High — speed changes the rhythm of the fight, and DoT protection can completely disrupt teams built around Iris or other repeated-damage heroes.

Alexandre Tips: Use Disharmony at Progress Teams in Defense to create space and protect your team from Iris DoT effects, Iris used to be a major threat in that context.

4th Skill – Equilibrium

In-game description: Nebula's basic attacks now switch to Support mode. Whenever this skill triggers, it increases Physical and Magic Attack of one nearby ally for 5.0 seconds. If there is only one nearby ally, Nebula alternates this skill between that ally and herself.

Skill Explanation: This is the heart of Nebula's rework. Her attacks become support actions that grant one nearby ally a five-second Physical and Magic Attack increase. Both boosts depend on Nebula's Physical Attack; the recorded maximum temporary Magic Attack boost is 224,031.

Evolution Priority: Very High — Equilibrium defines why you place Nebula beside a carry, and Physical Attack investment improves its enormous offensive payoff.

4th Skill – Equilibrium Enhanced Legendary Relic: Level 20

In-game description: Increases both Physical and Magic Attack of the two nearest allies simultaneously. If there is only one nearby ally, Nebula applies this skill to that ally and herself.

Skill Explanation: The buff now reaches both nearest allies at the same time. This removes the old gamble of waiting for Equilibrium to select the correct damage dealer during an ultimate. A carefully positioned Nebula can continuously empower two carries, which is a monstrous increase in practical team damage.

Evolution Priority: Very High — this is the essential Relic breakpoint for anyone building Nebula as a serious offensive Support.

5th Skill – Power of Balance New Legendary Relic: Level 1

In-game description: Every 12.0 seconds, increases the Main Stat of the nearest allies for 4.0 seconds. If there are no nearby allies, Nebula buffs herself. Only affects allies who started the battle with Nebula.

Skill Explanation: The Main Stat increase depends on Nebula's Physical Attack. This is more powerful than it first appears: Main Stat can raise attack, Health, or defense according to the ally, while also improving Dodge accuracy and resistance to Critical Hits. The restriction to allies who began the fight with Nebula prevents summoned units from taking the intended buff.

  • Cooldown: 12 seconds
  • Duration: 4 seconds
  • Keywords: Buff, Alliance

Evolution Priority: High — it is the first affordable Relic breakpoint and adds a versatile stat advantage before the costlier enhancements.

Best Skin for Nebula - Hero Wars Alliance

Nebula's best skins build Physical Attack first, then add enough durability to keep her healing, cleansing, and empowering allies in battle.

The skins remain below in their in-game visual order. The Evolution Priority inside each entry shows the recommended investment order.

Default Skin — Agility +1,365

As Nebula's main stat, 1,365 Agility provides Physical Attack +4,095 and Armor +1,365. It combines useful scaling and defense, but the two direct Physical Attack skins provide larger offensive gains.

Skin Stone Cost to Level 60: 30,825 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: High — 3rd – a strong all-around upgrade after both Physical Attack skins.

Romantic Skin — Physical Attack +7,095

This is Nebula's second direct Physical Attack skin. It improves the scaling behind her healing and buffs and finishes only 25 points behind the Winter Skin.

Skin Stone Cost to Level 60: 50,410 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd – nearly equal to Winter and essential for maximizing her support output.

Spring Skin — Dodge +2,960

Dodge can prevent physical hits entirely and fits Dodge-centered teams, but it is chance-based and does not improve Nebula's skill scaling.

Skin Stone Cost to Level 60: 50,410 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: Low — 5th – valuable in specialized Dodge teams, but the least universal investment.

Winter Skin — Physical Attack +7,120

Winter supplies Nebula's largest direct Physical Attack skin bonus. That stat supports her damage, healing, Main Stat increase, and Equilibrium buffs.

Skin Stone Cost to Level 60: 50,410 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st – the best first skin for strengthening her entire reworked role.

Celestial Skin — Armor +10,650

Celestial is the reliable defensive option against physical teams. It helps Nebula remain active long enough to complete more healing, cleansing, and buff cycles.

Skin Stone Cost to Level 60: 50,410 Agility Skin Stones.

Evolution Priority: Medium High — 4th – move it earlier only if physical burst is consistently defeating Nebula.

Nebula Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Nebula's artifacts combine a teamwide Physical Attack buff with personal scaling and survival, keeping her strongest support cycles effective longer.

1st Artifact — Unborn Stars

At level 100, Unborn Stars grants the team Physical Attack +21,357 with a 100% activation chance when Nebula uses Astral Projection. Its normal nine-second effect becomes eleven seconds after Deep Meditation.

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st – it strengthens physical allies and creates the best teamwide return from Nebula's Artifact resources.

2nd Artifact — Book of Illusions

At level 100, the book provides Dodge +2,967 and Health +53,394. These stats improve survival but do not directly increase her Physical Attack scaling.

Evolution Priority: Medium — 3rd – complete it after the Weapon and Ring unless Nebula urgently needs durability.

3rd Artifact — Ring of Agility

At level 100, Agility +3,990 derives Physical Attack +11,970 and Armor +3,990 because Agility is Nebula's main stat.

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd – it improves both skill scaling and physical durability, giving better overall value than the Book early on.

Nebula Glyph Evolution Priority

Nebula's glyph path starts with Physical Attack and Agility, then adds Health and defenses so her reworked support cycle remains active in battle.

The glyphs remain in their in-game visual order; each Evolution Priority states the recommended upgrade rank.

1st Glyph — Physical Attack

Level 80 Stats: Physical Attack +8,340.

This is the most direct way to strengthen Nebula's damage and every reworked effect that depends on Physical Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High — 1st – maximum impact on her core support scaling.

2nd Glyph — Armor

Level 80 Stats: Armor +12,850.

Armor reduces physical pressure and can prevent Nebula from disappearing before her next support cycle.

Evolution Priority: Medium — 4th – useful after scaling and Health, or earlier against physical-heavy opponents.

3rd Glyph — Magic Defense

Level 80 Stats: Magic Defense +12,850.

Magic Defense is matchup-dependent and does not improve her skill values, so it normally waits until the core build is complete.

Evolution Priority: Low — 5th – raise it sooner only when magical burst is your main obstacle.

4th Glyph — Health

Level 80 Stats: Health +122,800.

Health protects against both physical and magical damage and gives Nebula time to activate more buffs and cleanses.

Evolution Priority: High — 3rd – the most universal survival investment.

5th Glyph — Agility

Level 80 Stats: Agility +2,110, deriving Physical Attack +6,330 and Armor +2,110.

Agility gives Nebula offensive scaling and a useful layer of physical defense at the same time.

Evolution Priority: High — 2nd – upgrade immediately after Physical Attack for the best mixed return.

Support Class Skill

At class level 45, each time Nebula damages an enemy or buffs allies, she applies a four-second effect to all enemies that reduces Armor and Magic Defense. It can stack up to five times. The effect uses whichever is higher between 1% Physical Attack + 2,925 and 1% Magic Attack + 2,925. This gives her still more reason to favor Physical Attack.

Nebula Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

These partners either protect Nebula, amplify the same team tempo, or benefit strongly from her position-based offensive buffs.

Isaac

Isaac adds shielding and Physical Attack support for Way of Progress allies. When allies stand behind him, his Armor Penetration support complements Nebula's huge Physical Attack buffs and helps physical damage convert into real pressure.

Judge

Judge shields allies at both ends of the formation and adds Stun control. His protection helps the team survive, while Nebula's Skill Equilibrium buff Judge skills kit.

Julius

Julius anchors the team with shields and buffs, while his Artifact contributes Critical Hit Chance. Nebula rewards that protected formation with stronger attacks, faster cycles, cleansing, and longer Artifact effects at Relic level 30.

Polaris

Polaris adds control through Aurora Borealis, while Nebula helps allies behind her recharge their skills faster. This gives control-centered teams more opportunities to chain their disruptive effects.

Conclusion — Is Nebula's Rework Worth Building?

Yes—especially if you already use Way of Progress, Dodge, or dual-carry teams. The rework gives Nebula something more valuable than a larger number: reliability. Full-team Serenity repairs debuff-heavy fights, two-target Equilibrium removes the old timing lottery, Disharmony punishes Damage over Time strategies, and Deep Meditation lets carefully built teams hold their strongest windows longer.

My practical path is Talisman of Consistency, Physical Attack investment, Relic level 10 as the first major checkpoint, and level 20 as the transformation point. If you can reach level 30, Nebula stops being merely a powerful buffer and becomes the hero who keeps an entire team's plan running for longer.
