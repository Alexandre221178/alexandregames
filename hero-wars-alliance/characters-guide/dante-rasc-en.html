Dante Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Dante's Legendary Relics transform him into a devastating control and damage dealer. Understanding which skills to prioritize will maximize your impact in battle!

1st – Instrument of Fate

Dante's primary attack launches 4 spectral spears at multiple enemies, dealing physical damage and knocking them back. This is your bread-and-butter ability that provides constant crowd control while dealing respectable damage. The formula scales with Physical Attack (95% Physical Attack + 10150), making it stronger as you level Dante's gear. The knockback effect is particularly valuable against heroes who rely on positioning, like enemy backline damage dealers or tanks trying to protect their team.

Evolution Priority: High – This skill provides consistent control and damage throughout the battle, making it a solid investment for all game modes.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Damage: 95% Physical Attack (116111.85) + 10150

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Damage: 95% Physical Attack (118011.85) + 10150

Skill Animation Info

  • Animation delay: 0.88 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

1st – Instrument of Fate (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 10

The Legendary upgrade transforms this skill into a powerhouse! Whenever Dante knocks an enemy back with his spears, he gains a massive bonus to Armor Penetration for 6 seconds. The formula 7.5% Physical Attack + 6000 means Dante can shred through even the toughest tank defenses. This synergizes perfectly with his frequent knockback effects from skills 1 and 3, keeping the armor penetration bonus active almost constantly in prolonged fights.

Evolution Priority: Very High – The armor penetration buff dramatically increases Dante's damage output against armored targets, making this one of his most impactful upgrades.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Armor Penetration Bonus: 7.5% Physical Attack (9166.725) + 6000

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Armor Penetration Bonus: 7.5% Physical Attack (9316.725) + 6000

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 6 seconds
  • Keywords: Armor Penetration Increase

2nd – Foresight

Foresight provides a team-wide Dodge buff that lasts 7 seconds. While it may seem like a purely defensive skill, it has an incredible synergy with Dante's 3rd skill, Retribution! Every time an ally dodges an attack thanks to this buff, Dante throws an extra spectral spear. The formula 10% Physical Attack (12222.3) + 3000 provides a decent dodge bonus. Think of this as both a defensive layer and a damage amplifier that turns enemy attacks into counter-attacks.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – While not a direct damage increase, the synergy with Retribution makes this valuable for increasing overall DPS through more spear throws.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Dodge Bonus: 10% Physical Attack (12222.3) + 3000

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Dodge Bonus: 10% Physical Attack (12422.3) + 3000

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 4 seconds
  • Cooldown: 14 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.5 seconds
  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Keywords: Buff

3rd – Retribution

This is where Dante truly shines! Retribution is a reactive ability that triggers whenever Dante or any ally dodges an attack. When triggered, Dante instantly throws a spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing 90% Physical Attack (110000.7) + 4150 damage and knocking them back. With a 0.1-second cooldown, this skill can fire rapidly in fights with high dodge rates. Combined with Foresight and Dante's natural dodge stat, this ability can turn the tide of battle by providing constant interruption and damage.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This skill defines Dante's playstyle and provides exponential value when combined with dodge-heavy teams or against fast-attacking enemies.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Damage: 90% Physical Attack (110000.7) + 4150

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Damage: 90% Physical Attack (111800.7) + 4150

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 0.1 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.72 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

4th – Shackles of Weakness

Every time one of Dante's spectral spears hits an enemy, that target's main stat is reduced for 7 seconds. The formula 6% Physical Attack (7333.38) + 2400 may not look impressive, but remember that Dante throws spears constantly! This debuff weakens enemy damage dealers (reducing their Agility/Intelligence/Strength) and makes them easier to kill. It's a passive effect that works in the background, gradually tilting teamfights in your favor.

Evolution Priority: Medium – A useful debuff, but the impact is less immediate than Dante's other abilities. Good to level after prioritizing damage and control skills.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 6% Physical Attack (7333.38) + 2400

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 6% Physical Attack (7453.38) + 2400

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 7 seconds
  • Keywords: Debuff

4th – Shackles of Weakness (Enhanced) Legendary Relic: Level 20

The Legendary upgrade more than doubles the duration of the main stat reduction from 7 seconds to 16 seconds! This extended debuff window means enemies will spend most of the fight weakened, especially in prolonged battles. While this doesn't increase the raw numbers, the extended duration ensures better uptime and more consistent debuff coverage across multiple targets.

Evolution Priority: Medium – The extended duration is nice but doesn't fundamentally change combat outcomes like damage or control upgrades do. Prioritize this after core offensive abilities.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 6% Physical Attack (7333.38) + 2400

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Main Stat Reduction: 6% Physical Attack (7453.38) + 2400

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 16 seconds
  • Keywords: Debuff

5th – Piercing Throw (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

This brand-new Legendary skill adds another tool to Dante's arsenal! Every 9 seconds, Dante automatically throws a powerful spectral spear at the nearest enemy, dealing massive damage with the formula 225% Physical Attack (275001.75). Unlike his reactive abilities, this is a proactive damage source that triggers on a timer, ensuring consistent pressure even when enemies aren't attacking. The lack of a base damage bonus means it scales purely with your Physical Attack stat, making gear investments even more valuable.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This adds a significant chunk of guaranteed damage and control to Dante's kit. Essential for maximizing his offensive output.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula: Damage: 225% Physical Attack (275001.75)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula: Damage: 225% Physical Attack (279501.75)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 9 seconds
  • Keywords: Knockback, Control

6th – Shifting Focus (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

Shifting Focus is Dante's ultimate adaptation ability! When enemies are close to Dante, he gains a 25% Physical Attack (30555.75) bonus to Dodge, making him incredibly evasive and triggering more Retribution counters. When no enemies are nearby (usually late in fights when tanks fall), Dante switches to offense mode, gaining 25% Physical Attack (30555.75) as a raw Physical Attack bonus instead. This passive ensures Dante is always optimized for the current battle situation!

Evolution Priority: High – This adaptive buff synergizes perfectly with Dante's entire kit, boosting both his defensive counterplay and his cleanup potential.

Formula with Talisman of Soul

  • Formula 1: Dodge Bonus (enemies nearby): 25% Physical Attack (30555.75)
  • Formula 2: Physical Attack Bonus (no enemies nearby): 25% Physical Attack (30555.75)

Formula with Talisman of Retaliation

  • Formula 1: Dodge Bonus (enemies nearby): 25% Physical Attack (31055.75)
  • Formula 2: Physical Attack Bonus (no enemies nearby): 25% Physical Attack (31055.75)

Skill Animation Info

  • Keywords: Buff
