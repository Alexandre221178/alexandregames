Oya is one of the most dangerous anti-shield warriors in Hero Wars Alliance. As a Front Line Strength hero from the Way of Nature, she punishes protected enemies with heavy Physical Attack scaling, critical-hit synergy, and legendary relic upgrades that make shield-based teams much easier to break.

In this guide, you'll learn how Oya's base kit and legendary skills work, when to use each talisman, which allies maximize her pressure, and how skills like Shieldbreaker, Predator Bite, and Huntress Fury turn her into a serious PvP threat.

Who Is Oya?

Oya is a powerful Front Line warrior from the Way of Nature faction. She is built around high Physical Attack scaling, shield punishment, and critical-hit synergy, and her legendary relic upgrades make her even more oppressive against teams that rely on barriers or defensive timing.

  • Class: Warrior
  • Position: Front Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Faction: Nature
  • How to get Soul Stones: Events, Heroic Chest
  • Hero Overall Tier List: A
  • Hydra Tier List: B+
  • Synergy: Yasmine, Chabba, Jhu, Ishmael, Guus

Oya works exceptionally well with critical damage dealers like Yasmine, Jhu, and Ishmael, forming high-pressure offensive teams. Her synergy with Guus enhances survivability and control, while Chabba adds frontline support and distraction, creating space for backline assassins to operate freely.

Thanks to her ability to ignore shields, Oya is particularly effective against tanky lineups and protector heroes. Although she’s less efficient against magic-heavy teams, she shines in physical compositions, especially in PvP battles where shield-breaking and strong frontline presence make a significant impact.

Oya Pros and Cons – Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • ✔ Outstanding anti-shield hero: Shieldbreaker punishes protected targets, and its legendary upgrade spreads even more pressure to all shielded enemies.
  • ✔ Strong synergy with crit-based allies like Guus, Yasmine, Jhu, and Ishmael, which improves both burst damage and healing triggers.
  • ✔ Legendary relic kit adds extra control and scaling: Overwhelming burns energy, Predator Bite adds repeated pressure, and Huntress Fury rewards every enemy shield applied.
  • ✔ Flexible talisman choices let you lean into raw Physical Attack burst or more survivability and crit consistency.
  • ✔ Useful in aggressive PvP teams because she pressures tanks, low-health targets, and shield-reliant backlines at the same time.

❌ Cons

  • ✖ Her full value is matchup-dependent: if the enemy team barely uses shields, part of her anti-shield advantage becomes less impactful.
  • ✖ Needs solid investment in Physical Attack, survivability, and crit support before her pressure feels truly threatening.
  • ✖ Relies on team synergy and target access, so poor positioning or the wrong lineup can reduce her overall impact.
  • ✖ Can struggle more against magic-heavy or control-oriented teams that survive her opening pressure and punish the front line.

Oya Talisman Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Oya has two talismans, but you can equip only one in battle. This is different from skins: skin bonuses are always active once upgraded, while talisman bonuses only apply from the talisman currently equipped. Because Oya's key skills scale from Physical Attack and she has shield-breaking mechanics, your talisman choice has a direct impact on her damage profile, sustain triggers, and kill pressure.

Talisman of Prophecy

Main Stat slot: Strength +2,000. Since Strength is Oya's main stat, this grants +80,000 Health and +2,000 Physical Attack from the base conversion. The talisman is focused on stabilizing Oya while improving crit consistency through rerolls.

Reroll Slot 1: Crit Hit Chance +2,200. Reroll Slot 2: Crit Hit Chance +2,200. Reroll Slot 3: Crit Hit Chance +2,200. Treat these three slots as one value in practice: Total Reroll Bonus = Crit Hit Chance +6,600.

Battle Recommendation: Equip Talisman of Warrioress in most battles. Oya's main damage skills are tied to Physical Attack: Shieldbreaker (500% Physical Attack + 2,400), Hunting (crit chance buff based on Physical Attack), and Overwhelming (25% Physical Attack + 9,600). The +8,000 Physical Attack gives stronger immediate scaling for all of them, and the +13,200 Crushing improves kill conversion in real fights. Use Talisman of Prophecy when your team plan needs extra crit consistency and survivability instead of raw burst.

Alexandre Tips: If your goal is PvP pressure and faster kills, run Talisman of Warrioress. The direct +8,000 Physical Attack plus +13,200 Crushing gives better real damage on Oya's core abilities and improves finisher potential. Switch to Talisman of Prophecy only when your composition needs extra Health and Crit Hit Chance consistency.

Oya Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Relics empower Oya with stronger scaling and new effects, and this guide explains each skill in a simple way so you can upgrade the right one first.

1st - Shieldbreaker

Shieldbreaker is Oya's most important damage skill. It hits the nearest enemy with a heavy strike, also splashes 30% of that damage to marked enemies, and makes shielded enemies take 100% more damage from all Oya skills. That practical effect is huge: when the enemy team depends on shields, Oya stops them from hiding safely. The damage formula is 584,655 (500% of Physical Attack 116,451 + 2,400) with Talisman of Prophecy, or 614,655 (500% of Physical Attack 122,451 + 2,400) with Talisman of Warrioress.

Formula with Talisman of Prophecy

  • Formula 1: Damage: 584,655 (500% x 116,451 + 2,400)
  • Formula 2: Marked Enemies Damage: 175,396.5 (30% of 584,655)

Formula with Talisman of Warrioress

  • Formula 1: Damage: 614,655 (500% x 122,451 + 2,400)
  • Formula 2: Marked Enemies Damage: 184,396.5 (30% of 614,655)

Skill Animation Info

  • Animation delay: 0.78 seconds
  • Range: 100
  • Get energy on kill: 700

Evolution Priority: Very High - This is the most important skill to evolve because it gives Oya her biggest direct DPS gain and her best anti-shield value at the same time.


1st - Shieldbreaker (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 10

This legendary enhancement makes Shieldbreaker much more dangerous in real battles. Now 75% of the skill's damage also hits all shielded enemies. That means Oya no longer punishes only one shielded target: she pressures the whole enemy line. The extra damage becomes 438,491.25 (75% of 584,655) with Talisman of Prophecy and 460,991.25 (75% of 614,655) with Talisman of Warrioress.

Formula with Talisman of Prophecy

  • Formula 1: Extra Damage to Shielded Enemies: 438,491.25 (75% of 584,655)

Formula with Talisman of Warrioress

  • Formula 1: Extra Damage to Shielded Enemies: 460,991.25 (75% of 614,655)

Evolution Priority: Very High - This upgrade is extremely valuable because it turns one of Oya's best skills into a much stronger anti-shield team punish tool.

2nd - Hunting

Hunting helps Oya control the fight and support allies at the same time. It marks the enemy with the lowest health, pulls that target closer, and makes attacks on that target burn energy. It also gives bonus crit chance to allies who heal themselves or receive healing from Oya. The bonus formula is 9,333.825 (7.5% of Physical Attack 116,451 + 600) with Talisman of Prophecy, or 9,783.825 (7.5% of Physical Attack 122,451 + 600) with Talisman of Warrioress. For Nature heroes, that becomes 18,667.65 and 19,567.65.

  • Formula 2: Nature Heroes Crit Hit Chance Bonus: 18,667.65 (15% x 116,451 + 1,200)

Formula with Talisman of Warrioress

  • Formula 1: Crit Hit Chance Bonus: 9,783.825 (7.5% x 122,451 + 600)
  • Formula 2: Nature Heroes Crit Hit Chance Bonus: 19,567.65 (15% x 122,451 + 1,200)

Skill Animation Info

  • Initial Cooldown: 10 seconds

Evolution Priority: High - This skill brings good control, energy pressure, and crit synergy, so it has strong practical value in many teams.

3rd - Overwhelming

Overwhelming is Oya's quick 3-hit skill. Each next hit grows stronger, and shielded enemies behind the main target still take part of the damage. This makes it useful for finishing targets and pressuring teams that hide behind shields. The growth per hit is 38,712.75 (25% of Physical Attack 116,451 + 9,600) with Talisman of Prophecy, or 40,212.75 (25% of Physical Attack 122,451 + 9,600) with Talisman of Warrioress.

Formula with Talisman of Prophecy

  • Formula 1: Damage Increase with Each Hit: 38,712.75 (25% x 116,451 + 9,600)
  • Formula 2: Damage to Shielded Enemies Behind Target: 19,356.375 (50% of 38,712.75)

Formula with Talisman of Warrioress

  • Formula 1: Damage Increase with Each Hit: 40,212.75 (25% x 122,451 + 9,600)
  • Formula 2: Damage to Shielded Enemies Behind Target: 20,106.375 (50% of 40,212.75)

Skill Animation Info

  • Cooldown: 15 seconds
  • Animation delay: 0.01 seconds
  • Range: 120
  • Hits: 3

Evolution Priority: Medium High - It adds solid damage and shield-line pressure, but it still matters less than Shieldbreaker and Hunting in most battles.


3rd - Overwhelming (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 20

This upgrade adds strong indirect control. Every hit burns 200 Energy from all shielded enemies, and because the skill hits 3 times, that means 600 total Energy burned per shielded enemy every time the skill is used.

Skill Animation Info

  • Hits: 3
  • Energy Burn per Hit: 200
  • Total Energy Burn: 600

Evolution Priority: High - Burning energy from all shielded enemies is very useful because it slows enemy ultimates and improves Oya's anti-shield control.

4th - Brutal Onslaught

Brutal Onslaught is Oya's sustain skill. When an ally deals critical damage or breaks an enemy shield, that ally restores health. This is easier for beginners to understand in practice: the more crits and shield breaks your team causes, the more healing the team gets. The health formula is 38,323.075 (2.5% of Health 1,508,923 + 600) with Talisman of Prophecy, or 36,323.075 (2.5% of Health 1,428,923 + 600) with Talisman of Warrioress. Nature heroes receive 76,646.15 and 72,646.15.

Formula with Talisman of Prophecy

  • Formula 1: Health Regen: 38,323.075 (2.5% x 1,508,923 + 600)
  • Formula 2: Nature Heroes Health Regen: 76,646.15 (5% x 1,508,923 + 1,200)

Formula with Talisman of Warrioress

  • Formula 1: Health Regen: 36,323.075 (2.5% x 1,428,923 + 600)
  • Formula 2: Nature Heroes Health Regen: 72,646.15 (5% x 1,428,923 + 1,200)

Skill Animation Info

  • Trigger: When an allied hero deals critical damage or breaks an enemy shield

Evolution Priority: Medium - This healing is helpful, but it is more supportive than decisive, so it should come after Oya's stronger damage and control upgrades.

5th - Predator Bite (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

Predator Bite is a new legendary skill that periodically attacks the nearest enemy and all shielded enemies. Its role is easy to understand: it adds more repeated pressure exactly where Oya already wants to fight. The oya_hwa.json file does not provide a numeric formula for this skill, so no exact damage total can be calculated without inventing values.

Evolution Priority: High - Even without a published coefficient in the JSON, the practical benefit is clear: more frequent anti-shield pressure and more damage opportunities.

6th - Huntress Fury (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

Huntress Fury is a passive legendary skill that temporarily increases Oya's current damage every time a shield is applied to an enemy. If the whole enemy team gets shields, the effect can trigger multiple times. The oya_hwa.json file does not include a numeric scaling formula for this skill, so exact totals are not available from the provided source data.

Evolution Priority: Very High - In shield-heavy fights this becomes a repeated damage amplifier, which gives Oya excellent scaling over the course of battle.

Best Skin for Oya Hero Wars Alliance

Find out the best skins to upgrade for Oya in Hero Wars Alliance based on how they improve her performance in battle and skill impact, incluindo a nova Stellar Skin.

Default Skin

Strength +1,365. Each point of Strength provides 40 Health and 1 bonus to Physical Attack if Strength is the main stat. Since Oya’s first three skills scale with Physical Attack and her fourth scales with Health, this skin boosts all her abilities, making it her most important skin.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Core stat that maximizes both damage and survivability. Always upgrade this one first.

Mythical Skin

+7,095 Physical Attack. Oya’s main offensive skin: boosts the damage of Shieldbreaker, Hunting, and Overwhelming. Since these three skills scale directly with Physical Attack, this skin has excellent synergy with her kit.

Evolution Priority: High – The best offensive choice after Strength/Penetration, especially for maximizing burst and sustained DPS.

Solar Skin+

Health +213,290. Increases Oya’s survivability and directly improves her fourth skill, Brutal Onslaught, which restores Health based on her max HP. Also enhances synergy with Guus, boosting sustain in critical-heavy teams.

Evolution Priority: High – Essential for balance between durability and healing support. Great in long fights or defensive PvP setups.

Winter Skin

Armor +10,650. Provides resistance against physical damage, useful against marksmen and warriors. However, it does not improve Oya’s skill scaling directly.

Evolution Priority: Low – Defensive but situational. Consider only if facing heavy physical damage compositions.

Sport Skin

Crushing +5,920. Crushing increases all outgoing damage against low-health enemies, applying to Physical, Magical, and Pure damage (but not allied health-loss effects). Formula: (Crushing / (Crushing + target’s main stat)) × 100%. This skin makes Oya much deadlier as a finisher, securing kills on weakened enemies.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Great for late-fight damage pressure, but less impactful on base skill scaling compared to Strength, Physical Attack, or Health.

Stellar Skin (New)

Armor Penetration +10,650. CRUCIAL STAT. Penetration is mandatory for Physical Attack heroes to ignore enemy Armor, significantly boosting the effective damage of all her offensive skills.

Evolution Priority: High – Essential offensive skin. Should be prioritized immediately after Strength to ensure maximum damage effectiveness against armored targets.

Tribal Skin (New)

Physical Attack +7,095. CRUCIAL STAT. This skin is now one of Oya's most important upgrades, as it directly boosts the damage of her main skills: Shieldbreaker, Hunting, and Overwhelming. Stacking Physical Attack with the Mythical Skin maximizes her offensive potential, especially for burst and sustained DPS.

Evolution Priority: High – Prioritize this skin right after Strength and Penetration (Stellar Skin). It is essential for maximizing Oya's damage output in both PvP and PvE.

Oya Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Discover which artifacts to prioritize for Oya in Hero Wars Alliance to enhance her Shieldbreaker and team synergy in battles.

1st – Weapon Artifact: Ancestor's Sword

Critical Hit Chance +8,898. This weapon activates every time Oya uses her ultimate skill, Shieldbreaker, which is her most important ability. The artifact grants a critical hit bonus to the entire team for 9 seconds, making it incredibly valuable in both offense and team synergy.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Activates with her main skill and benefits the entire team. Core to her performance in battle.

2nd – Book Artifact: Warrior's Code

Critical Hit Chance +2,967, Physical Attack +3,561. This artifact increases both her damage potential and the chance to trigger healing and bonus effects when landing critical hits. It's a solid boost to her offensive capabilities, especially useful when combined with Guus or other Nature allies.

Evolution Priority: High – Supports critical hit builds and overall damage output, complementing her skill synergy well.

3rd – Ring Artifact: Strength Ring

Strength +3,990. Each point of Strength gives Oya +40 Health and +1 Physical Attack. While this artifact boosts both survivability and power, its impact is slower and less direct than her weapon or book, especially in shorter PvP fights where fast bursts matter more than long-term stats.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Important for overall scaling, but lower priority compared to her weapon and book, which affect active combat more directly.

Oya Glyph Evolution Priority

Find out which glyphs you should level first for Oya in Hero Wars Alliance to maximize her shield-breaking power and synergy with Nature heroes.

1st – Physical Attack Glyph

+8,340 Physical Attack. All of Oya’s offensive skills scale with Physical Attack — from her triple strike to her ultimate. Boosting this stat increases her overall damage and helps her eliminate shielded enemies faster.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Directly powers up every damaging skill. Core to Oya’s role as a damage-dealing frontline hero.

2nd – Health Glyph

+122,800 Health. Oya fights on the front line, which means she takes a lot of incoming damage. More health means greater survivability and better synergy with healing effects from skills like Brutal Onslaught and allies like Guus.

Evolution Priority: High – Helps her stay alive long enough to keep applying damage and shield-break effects.

3rd – Critical Hit Chance Glyph

+4,170 Crit Hit Chance. Critical hits are important for triggering healing from Brutal Onslaught and maximizing damage. This becomes even more powerful when Oya is in a team with Guus or other crit-focused allies.

Evolution Priority: Medium-High – Great for synergy and healing triggers, but less important than pure Physical Attack.

4th – Magic Defense Glyph

+12,850 Magic Defense. While this glyph doesn’t boost Oya’s offensive power, it helps her survive longer against magic-heavy teams. Useful in PvP when facing mages, but not a main focus for most builds.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Good defensive boost, but not urgent unless you're constantly facing mages.

5th – Strength Glyph

+2,110 Strength. Each point of Strength grants +40 Health and +1 Physical Attack. While it helps with both survivability and damage, it gives less benefit per level compared to the direct glyphs for Health and Physical Attack.

Evolution Priority: Low – Offers mixed stats, but should be upgraded last for maximum efficiency.

Which Allies have Best Synergy with Oya?

Ally Skill Synergy

Yasmine
Yasmine is a character with a high chance of critical damage. Whenever an ally of Oya deals critical damage, they receive healing and a temporary critical hit bonus from Oya’s passive effects.

Chabba
Chabba is a durable tank who can devour and disable enemies in the front line. This creates space for Oya to strike unchallenged while reducing incoming pressure. His crowd control and survivability allow Oya to stay active longer and consistently break enemy shields.

Elmir
Elmir shifts to the back line after cloning himself, dealing ranged damage from a safe position. His weapon artifact increases Oya’s armor penetration. In teams with Dorian, he gains bonus critical damage, synergizing perfectly with Oya’s crit-based healing.

Guus
Guus boosts critical hit chance and heals allies with Golden Feathers. Oya benefits immensely from these buffs, triggering more critical hits and healing effects with Brutal Onslaught. A top synergy for sustained crit builds.

Jhu
Jhu deals massive damage the lower his health gets. Oya’s shield-breaking and energy-drain effects help soften targets, allowing Jhu to secure kills faster. They both benefit from critical-hit-centered compositions.

Ishmael
Ishmael thrives in crit-heavy teams. Oya’s passive bonus to critical hit chance supports his burst playstyle. In longer battles, their combined crit scaling can overwhelm enemy frontlines.

 How enemies defeat Oya

Astaroth
Once per battle, Astaroth can resurrect an ally and when Oya uses her Hunt skill, she eliminates enemies quickly, and Astaroth revives them with full energy.

Oya receives 700 extra energy when eliminating an enemy, so Satori applies his marks. But Satori has little health and can be the target with less health, so it's good to have another ally with less health or Astaroth to resurrect him with full energy and counterattack.

Conclusion – Oya Guide Hero Wars Alliance

Oya is a powerful and versatile frontline hero who can make a huge difference in battle thanks to her ability to ignore shields and boost allies’ critical hit potential. She excels in physical and crit-based team compositions, especially when paired with heroes like Guus, Yasmine, Ishmael, and Jhu.

Her synergy with support heroes that enhance critical damage and healing, such as Guus and Aidan, makes her a strong choice for both offensive and defensive strategies. Choosing the right Talismans, Glyphs, Skins, and Artifacts can greatly impact her effectiveness, so careful investment is key.

Whether on attack or defense, Oya brings valuable pressure against shield-heavy tanks and energy-dependent enemies. When used with the right allies, she becomes a central piece in winning team setups.
