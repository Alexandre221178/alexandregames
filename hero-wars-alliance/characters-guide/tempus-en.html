Who Is Tempus in Hero Wars Alliance?

Tempus is a middle-line Support hero from the Way of Eternity faction who harnesses the power of time to control entire battlefields. His toolkit combines crowd control, debuffs, and team buffs — making him a hero who elevates every ally while dismantling every enemy plan. His Legendary Relic adds new offensive dimensions and a defensive counter-control mechanic that makes him truly exceptional at higher investment.

  • Main Class: Support
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Eternity
  • Equipment Rank: 16
  • How to get Soul Stones: Special Events
  • Synergy: Iris, Corvus, Phobos, Kayla, Aidan
  • Hydra Tier List: To be determined

His unique ability to freeze enemy skill cooldowns with Chronostasis makes him one of the most disruptive heroes in competitive play. Combined with Withering — which reduces enemy Physical Attack and max Health — and Time Paradox — which speeds up allies while slowing enemies and boosting their max Health — Tempus simultaneously weakens the enemy team while empowering his own.

The synergy with Kayla and Aidan is especially notable: Tempus increases allies' max Health through Time Paradox, which directly amplifies Aidan's Phoenix Ritual. This creates a chain reaction of health boosts, team revivals, and sustained pressure that is extremely difficult for opponents to counter.

Tempus Pros & Cons — Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Chronostasis stops all enemies simultaneously and freezes their skill cooldowns — one of the strongest CC skills in the game.
  • Withering dramatically reduces enemy Physical Attack and max Health, crippling frontline heroes.
  • Time Paradox boosts allies' max Health and speed while slowing all enemies in range.
  • Outstanding synergy with the Way of Eternity faction, Iris, Corvus, Phobos, and the Kayla+Aidan combo.
  • Legendary Relic adds Magic Penetration aura (Chrono Field) and an auto-CC counter (Time Rebound), dramatically increasing his value at higher investment.

❌ Cons

  • Middle-line position leaves him exposed to AoE damage and burst attacks without a strong frontline.
  • Damage output is moderate — he is a controller and support, not a primary damage dealer.
  • Full potential requires Legendary Relic investment, which is resource-intensive.
  • Relies on team composition to maximize his control chain and health-scaling synergies.

Tempus Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority — Hero Wars Alliance

Master every timeline: learn Tempus's skills, their exact formulas, and which to prioritize to dominate the battlefield in Hero Wars Alliance.

Relics empower heroes by granting them new abilities while boosting existing ones. Beyond skill improvements, Relics also enhance two secondary stats of each hero. The stats boosted depend on how that particular hero's skills formula scale.

1st – Chronostasis

In-game description: Stops time for all enemy Heroes. For 2.5 seconds they cannot use skills, move, or dodge attacks, and their Armor and Magic Defence are reduced by 27,241.65 (13% Mag.atk. + 1800). For the duration of this skill, the cooldown of all enemy skills that have it will be frozen. This skill increases the duration of all Curses and Debuffs cast on the enemies.

Skill Explanation: Chronostasis is Tempus's ultimate and the heart of his entire kit. Imagine every enemy on the battlefield being frozen at once — they cannot move, use abilities, or dodge for 2.5 full seconds. During this window, your team attacks freely without any resistance. On top of that, frozen enemies also lose Armor and Magic Defence, making them take significantly more damage during those precious seconds. The icing on the cake: this skill also extends the duration of ALL debuffs and curses already on the enemies — meaning if Iris or Phobos has applied a curse, Chronostasis makes it last even longer. Every fight becomes a choreographed execution.

Evolution Priority: Very High – Chronostasis is the first skill you should upgrade every time you have resources. It is the most powerful crowd control ability in Tempus's kit, stopping the entire enemy team simultaneously while freezing their skill cooldowns — your allies get a free attack window with zero retaliation. The Armor and Magic Defence reduction during those 2.5 seconds amplifies every magic damage dealer on your team, making this upgrade the single highest impact investment for Tempus.

2nd – Withering

In-game description: Weakens distant opponents in front of him for 6.0 seconds. Weakened enemies lose 24,370.5 (10% Mag.atk. + 4800) Physical Attack and 126,423 (60% Mag.atk. + 9000) max Health.

Skill Explanation: Withering is a dual-purpose debuff that hits enemies where it hurts most. It simultaneously cuts their Physical Attack — reducing the damage your frontline receives — and slashes their maximum Health by a massive amount. A hero who appears to have 600,000 Health but loses 126,000 of it is far easier to finish off than it looks. The max Health reduction is particularly powerful because it stacks with the damage your team is already dealing. This skill fires every 12 seconds after the first 8, hitting all distant enemies in front of Tempus in a wide area. It is a reliable, frequent debuff that keeps the enemy team permanently weakened throughout the fight.

2nd – Withering (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 10

In-game description: Now also debuffs all enemies in front of him.

Skill Explanation: Before this upgrade, Withering only targeted "distant" enemies — heroes standing further away from Tempus. At Relic Level 10, the skill now hits ALL enemies in front of him, including those standing close. This is a significant tactical improvement: no enemy on the frontline or midline escapes the Physical Attack and max Health penalty. Against teams that cluster closely together or use short-range tanks, this enhancement ensures every opponent is weakened simultaneously every 12 seconds, not just a select few.

Skill Animation Info

  • Requirement: Legendary Relic Level 10
  • Keywords: Debuff
  • Area expanded: Now applies to all enemies in front (not only distant)

Evolution Priority: High – This is one of the first Relic upgrades to prioritize. Expanding Withering's coverage to all enemies in front turns a situational debuff into a reliable team-wide weakener. In competitive PvP where enemies are tightly packed, this upgrade ensures Tempus punishes the entire opposing team's frontline and midline every time Withering fires — not just whoever happens to be far enough away from him.

3rd – Temporal Anomaly

In-game description: Sends the enemy Hero with the highest Armor Penetration into the Temporal Anomaly for 5.0 seconds. The target is slowed down by 25%, losing 50 energy and receiving 31,755.75 (15% Mag.atk. + 2400) magic damage per second.

Skill Explanation: Temporal Anomaly is Tempus's targeted neutralizer. Instead of hitting all enemies, this skill surgically hunts down the single most dangerous attacker: the hero with the highest Armor Penetration. That is usually a heavy hitter built to tear through your tank — like a hard-hitting warrior or carry. Once caught, they are sent into a time loop for 5 seconds: movement is slowed by 25%, they lose 50 energy every second (enough to drain most heroes' entire energy bar and prevent their ultimate), and they take consistent magic damage throughout. The initial cooldown is just 1 second, meaning Tempus uses this almost immediately when battle starts — protecting your frontline from the very first seconds.

Level this skill consistently and your tank will notice the difference immediately: far less pressure from the enemy's hardest hitter.

4th – Time Paradox

In-game description: For 4.0 seconds, conjures a huge Clock in the center of the arena. Enemies in range of the Clock are slowed down, while allies are sped up by 10%. Affected allies' max Health is also increased by 146,593.5 (70% Mag.atk. + 9600).

Skill Explanation: Time Paradox is a battlefield-wide manipulation skill that affects both teams simultaneously. A giant clock appears in the center of the arena: your allies move and attack faster (+10% speed) while enemies are slowed (-10% speed). But the most impactful part is the massive max Health boost for all allies — 146,593.5 (70% Mag.atk. + 9600). This is the key synergy with Aidan: his Phoenix Ritual skill boosts allies based on their max Health, meaning the more Health your team has from Time Paradox, the harder Aidan's sacrificial buff burns the enemy. Combined with Kayla, the resurrection mechanic becomes even more reliable. Every time Time Paradox fires, your team becomes more durable and faster while the enemy team slows to a crawl.

4th – Time Paradox (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 20

In-game description: Now all enemies in the skill's area take magic damage every 1 second. Magic damage: 84,282 (40% Mag.atk. + 6000).

Skill Explanation: At Relic Level 20, Time Paradox becomes a complete multi-purpose ability. The clock still boosts your allies' speed and Health while slowing enemies — but now it also deals AoE magic damage to all enemies within its range every 1 second. Since the clock lasts 4 seconds, enemies caught inside take up to 4 hits of 84,282 (40% Mag.atk. + 6000) magic damage each. When combined with the Withering debuff (reduced max Health), Chronostasis (armor reduction), and Chrono Field (Magic Penetration), these hits become devastatingly efficient. An opponent who is already weakened, frozen, and slowed will crumble under this additional pressure.

5th – Chrono Field (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

In-game description: Every 15 seconds creates an aura around Tempus for 4.0 seconds, increasing Magic Penetration by 39,141 (20% Mag.atk.) for him and all allies within the area.

Skill Explanation: Chrono Field is one of the most exciting rewards of the Legendary Relic system — and it activates the moment you reach Relic Level 1, meaning you get it instantly with no additional investment. Every 15 seconds, an aura automatically appears around Tempus that boosts Magic Penetration for himself and every ally standing nearby. Magic Penetration is incredibly powerful in competitive play because it strips away a portion of the enemy's Magic Defence, causing your magic abilities to deal significantly more damage. If you run heroes like Iris, Phobos, or Xe'Sha, this aura makes all their skills hit noticeably harder during the 4-second window. Since it triggers passively throughout the entire battle without costing any energy, it functions as a constant free damage amplifier for your whole team.

Since it triggers automatically every 15 seconds without requiring any energy, this is essentially a free passive that multiplies the effectiveness of every mage on your team throughout the entire fight.


6th – Time Rebound (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

In-game description: Applies the Chronostasis skill for 2.5 seconds to the enemy that inflicts a Control effect on Tempus.

Skill Explanation: Time Rebound is the ultimate passive defense. Any enemy hero who tries to stun, silence, freeze, or otherwise control Tempus will immediately receive Chronostasis in return — meaning they cannot move, use skills, or dodge for 2.5 seconds. Think about what this means in practice: a hero like Judge who silences Tempus expecting to shut him down instead gets frozen and loses all skill access themselves. This completely inverts the counterplay dynamic. Instead of enemy controllers being a threat to Tempus, they become a liability for their own team. The more control-heavy your opponents are, the more devastating Time Rebound becomes. It turns one of the most common weaknesses of support heroes into a strength.

Skill Animation Info

  • Requirement: Legendary Relic Level 30
  • Keywords: Passive
  • Trigger: Any enemy inflicts a Control effect on Tempus
  • Duration of applied Chronostasis: 2.5 seconds

Evolution Priority: High – Time Rebound rewards the commitment to the full Legendary Relic path. At Relic Level 30, it makes Tempus nearly impossible to shut down — any enemy who tries to control him immediately gets his signature Chronostasis in return. In competitive PvP, Grand Arena, and control-heavy metas where heroes like Judge, Thea, or Octavia are common, this passive completely changes the flow of battle. The more control-heavy your opponents are, the more powerful Tempus becomes.

Angel Skin+

Synergies

Tempus works exceptionally well with Way of Eternity heroes, but he is particularly potent when paired with:

  • Iris: Iris’ debuff abilities complement Tempus’ time manipulation. When Tempus uses his skill Chronostasis, it enhances the effects of Iris' debuffs by extending their duration.
  • Phobos: Phobos’ Curse ability, which lowers enemy effectiveness, becomes even more deadly when combined with Tempus' skills that slow and weaken enemies.
  • Xe'Sha and Celeste: These powerful mages benefit from Tempus' time manipulation abilities, allowing them to unleash devastating magic while enemies are slowed and unable to defend.
  • Morrigan – Supports Tempus with healing and damage buffs, helping keep him alive in the front lines.
  • Nebula – Enhances Tempus’s magic attack with her boosts, making him more lethal.
  • Dante – Protects Tempus from high burst damage while helping control enemy movement.
  • Kayla and Aidan – Kayla and Aidan benefits from Tempus max health increase, control, and damage mitigation, allowing them to survive longer.

Tempus Guide Conclusion

Tempus is a versatile and powerful support hero in Hero Wars Alliance, excelling at controlling the battlefield and amplifying his team's damage output. His unique time manipulation abilities allow him to slow enemies, boost allies, and even turn enemy control attempts against them. With the right investment in skins, artifacts, and glyphs, Tempus can become an indispensable asset in both PvP and PvE content. Pairing him with synergistic heroes like Iris, Phobos, and Xe'Sha will maximize his potential and help you dominate your opponents.
