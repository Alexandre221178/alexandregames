Who Is Guus in Hero Wars Alliance?

Guus is a Middle Line Healer and Support from the Way of Honor. He uses Golden Feathers to restore Health, raise allied Critical Hit Chance, and protect allies who are close to defeat.

  • Main Class: Healer
  • Position: Middle Line
  • Main Stat: Strength
  • Faction: Way of Honor
  • How to get Soul Stones: Special Events and Heroic Chest
  • Synergy: Way of Honor and critical-hit teams, especially Artemis, Tristan, Galahad, Ishmael, and Qing Mao

Under the Wing provides team-wide healing and critical-hit support, while Feather Squad and Beak Peak help allies survive sustained pressure and dangerous low-Health moments.

Honkageddon turns Guus's critical hits into area healing and additional Golden Feathers. It also gives Honor Heroes extra damage based on their current Health, making Guus especially valuable in coordinated teams.

Guus Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Strong team-wide healing through Under the Wing.
  • Golden Feathers increase allied Critical Hit Chance.
  • Beak Peak can save allies before they fall below 25% Health.
  • Honkageddon rewards critical-hit teams and Honor Heroes.
  • Most healing scales well with Physical Attack.

❌ Cons

  • Has no crowd-control skill.
  • Before Relic level 20, his critical hits heal instead of dealing bonus damage.
  • Feather Squad only affects allies positioned in front of him.
  • Beak Peak consumes the Golden Feather after it triggers.
  • Needs the right allies to maximize Honkageddon.

Guus Skills Upgrade Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

The numerical examples below use the level-30 Relic and the equipped talisman. Healing values are shown before Critical Healing; at Relic level 10, critical heals restore 200% of the normal amount. The chance depends on Guus’s Critical Hit Chance.

Guus turns Golden Feathers into healing, critical support, and emergency protection. This order shows which base skills create the most battle value.

Recommended upgrade order: Under the Wing → Honkageddon → Beak Peak → Feather Squad → Basic Attack.

In-game description: Deals physical damage to a single target.

Skill Explanation: This is Guus's regular single-target hit. It deals 100% Phys.atk., which equals 101,464 with Talisman of Feathers or 112,664 with Talisman of the Grand Yolk.

1 - Under the Wing

In-game description: Restores Health and gives a Golden Feather to each of his allies. Passive effect: Golden Feather raises an ally's Critical hit chance for 8.0 seconds. If Golden Feather is applied to an ally again, the duration of this effect is extended by 8.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: This is Guus's main team-wide heal and the easiest way to place Golden Feathers on everyone. Health restoration uses 150% Phys.atk. + 3600, reaching 155,796 or 172,596 Health. The Critical Hit Chance bonus uses 5% Phys.atk. + 1200, giving 6,273.2 or 6,833.2 depending on the equipped talisman.

2 - Feather Squad

In-game description: Gives a Golden Feather to each ally in front of him. While an ally has a Golden Feather, their Health is restored each time they receive damage from enemies.

Skill Explanation: Feather Squad protects Guus's frontline for 5 seconds. Every enemy hit received during that window triggers healing based on 30% Phys.atk. + 1800, restoring 32,239.2 or 35,599.2 Health each time. Frequent enemy attacks can therefore turn this modest number into strong repeated sustain.

3 - Beak Peak

In-game description: If Health of an ally with a Golden Feather is supposed to drop below 25%, then before that happens, it is partially restored. The amount of Health restored is increased for each remaining second of the Golden Feather's duration. The Golden Feather disappears after triggering this effect.

Skill Explanation: Think of Beak Peak as an emergency rescue. The base heal is 15% Health + 6000, equal to 221,349.75 or 209,349.75 Health. Each second still remaining on the Golden Feather adds 10% Phys.atk. + 1200, or 11,346.4 or 12,466.4 more healing per second.

Best Skin for Guus Hero Wars Alliance

Discover the best skin upgrade order for Guus in Hero Wars Alliance to maximize his healing and support potential in battle.

Default Skin

Strength +1,365
Each Strength point gives Guus 40 Health and increases his physical attack since Strength is his main stat. This boosts all his healing abilities and Golden Feather effects.

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is the most important skin to level first. It directly increases Guus’s physical attack and health, powering up all his healing effects and buffs.

Angel Skin

Health +108,285
With a massive health boost, this skin greatly increases Guus’s durability in all fights. More health allows him to withstand more incoming damage and continue supporting the team.

Evolution Priority: High – A solid upgrade after Default and Harvest. While it doesn't increase his healing power directly, the survivability helps him stay active longer in fights, especially against burst teams.

Dapper Skin

Magic Defense +10,650
This skin helps Guus survive longer against strong magical damage dealers. More survivability means he can keep healing and buffing allies for a longer time.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Useful when fighting magic-heavy teams. Guus’s role is to keep allies alive, so extra magic defense ensures he stays longer on the battlefield.

Gentleman Skin

Toughness +2,960
Toughness reduces damage taken when Guus’s health is low, regardless of the damage type. This helps him survive when he's almost down, but only activates in critical moments.

Evolution Priority: Low – This is the least urgent skin to evolve. While helpful in dangerous fights, the effect is situational and less impactful overall compared to boosting healing output or regular survivability.

Mysterious Skin

Armor +10,650
This skin significantly increases Guus’s durability against heavy physical damage. The added survivability allows him to stay on the battlefield longer, continuously healing and supporting his allies.

Evolution Priority: Medium–High – A strong secondary choice. As a support hero, Guus benefits greatly from extra defense, especially when facing physical threats that target the backline.

Harvest Skin

Physical Attack +7,095
This skin is extremely valuable for Guus because all his healing skills and support abilities scale with physical attack. The stronger his attack, the better his healing output and critical buffs for allies.

Evolution Priority: High – Upgrade this skin right after the Default Skin. It has a direct impact on Guus’s healing and support efficiency in all battles.

Twilight Skin+

Critical Hit Chance +5,920
Guus’s critical hits activate Honkageddon, healing allies and granting Golden Feathers. At Relic level 20, he also deals critical damage while providing this support. This large Critical Hit Chance bonus makes that healing trigger more reliable and helps Guus maintain Golden Feather coverage during battle.

Evolution Priority: Very High – One of Guus’s strongest PvP upgrades. More consistent critical hits mean more frequent area healing and Golden Feathers, improving team sustain while enabling the extra critical damage granted to feathered allies. Prioritize it alongside his core Physical Attack skins.

Guus Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Discover which artifacts to prioritize for Guus to boost healing, survivability, and team-wide critical synergy in Hero Wars Alliance!

1st - Weapon: Golden Goose

This artifact boosts Toughness, reducing all types of damage received by the entire team for 9 seconds when their health is low. This team-wide damage reduction is crucial, as it helps keep Guus a mid-line healer and his allies alive, allowing him to consistently provide healing and apply Golden Feathers.

Evolution Priority: High – Keeping Guus alive longer means more healing and more critical boosts for your team. This makes the weapon the top priority for survival and team support.

2nd - Book: Warrior's Code

This artifact grants Critical Hit Chance and Physical Attack. These directly improve Guus’ skill "Honkageddon", allowing his critical hits to heal nearby allies and spread Golden Feathers more frequently.

Evolution Priority: Medium – While not his main role, Guus’ crit-based healing becomes more impactful in longer fights or with critical-focused teams. Level this second for added team synergy and healing support.

3rd - Ring: Ring of Strength

This artifact boosts Strength, which increases Guus’ health and physical attack. While both are helpful, this provides less immediate impact on his healing abilities compared to the other artifacts.

Evolution Priority: Low – Although it improves survivability and basic stats, it doesn't directly enhance Guus’ healing mechanics or critical utility. Upgrade this last after ensuring better team-wide impact through the other artifacts.

Guus Glyph Evolution Priority

Learn the best glyph upgrade path for Guus in Hero Wars Alliance to boost his healing and support effectiveness in battle.

1st - Physical Attack:

This is Guus’s most important glyph. His healing and Golden Feather effects scale directly with his physical attack. Upgrading this glyph significantly increases his support capabilities.

Evolution Priority: High – Directly powers all healing and buffing skills.

2nd - Health:

Guus’s healing with Beak Peak is based on a percentage of his own max health. This glyph increases that healing and improves his durability in longer fights.

Evolution Priority: Medium–High – Boosts survivability and enhances healing from skill 3.

3rd - Critical Hit Chance:

Guus’s critical hits activate extra effects like healing and Golden Feather sharing. While useful, his performance doesn’t fully depend on crits, making this a secondary priority.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Adds value, but not essential for his core utility.

4th - Armor:

Armor helps Guus resist physical damage, especially in the front line. However, since he’s primarily a support hero and usually positioned safely, this glyph isn’t a top priority.

Evolution Priority: Low – Defensive, but not crucial to his role.

5th - Strength:

While strength does provide a minor boost to both health and physical attack, its scaling is much less efficient than upgrading the direct Physical Attack and Health glyphs.

Evolution Priority: Very Low – Offers minor stats compared to more impactful glyphs.

Guus Talisman Guide – Hero Wars Alliance

  • Guus has two Talismans, and you must choose which one to equip for battle. Unlike skins, where all bonuses are applied no matter which skin is active, Talismans only grant the stats and effects of the one currently equipped. Choosing the right Talisman can make a big difference in battle.
  • The three Reroll Slots work together as one combined bonus — what matters most is the total value of all three rerolls. You can reroll each slot to maximize critical chance, armor, or physical attack depending on the Talisman and your build focus.

Talisman of Feathers

This Talisman increases Critical Damage, allowing Guus to land critical hits that enhance his healing through his passive. It strengthens his Strength stat, improving both health and physical attack, which are key to his healing and support abilities. It’s an excellent choice for offensive healing builds.

Guus SYNERGIES - Hero Wars Alliance

With her recent rework, Artemis deals even more critical damage. Guus enhances her potential, and the higher her allies' health, the more damage she deals.

⭐ Alexandre's Final Verdict about Guus in Hero Wars Alliance

Guus is one of the most flexible sustain supports in the current meta because Golden Feathers connect healing, critical-hit pressure, and emergency protection. He can support both physical and magical damage dealers, but his greatest value appears in teams that keep attacking while his healing layers remain active.

Best overall choice: Team #1 with Aidan, Polaris, Guus, Byrna, and Leonel. The three support layers give Leonel the time he needs to break tanks, especially shield-dependent frontlines such as Julius. Use Team #2 when you prefer Kayla's faster burst and Julius's shields over Byrna's additional healing and Leonel's specialized tank destruction.
