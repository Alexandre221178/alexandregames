Who Is Dorian in Hero Wars Alliance?

Dorian is a Blood Mage Healer from the Way of Chaos faction. After the rework, he became one of the most unique supports in the game: instead of simply restoring HP, he sacrifices his own health to permanently increase allies' Max Health, resurrects dead teammates, and empowers the entire team's Vampirism — even from beyond the grave.

  • Main Class: Healer
  • Position: Back Line
  • Main Stat: Intelligence
  • Faction: Way of Chaos
  • How to get Soul Stones: Campaign & various events
  • Synergy: Fafnir, Artemis, Tristan, Galahad, Martha, Lilith, Faceless, Xe'sha
  • Hydra Tier List: S-tier (Fire & Light Hydra), A-tier (Dark & Water)

His role is not just to heal — it's to reshape the battlefield by making every ally harder to kill, then refusing to let them stay dead. He is an Intelligence hero, meaning Magic Attack is his primary damage and healing multiplier.

Rework note (Nexters): "Dorian becomes much stronger as a Hero overall, and especially as a Healer! He sacrifices his own health to heal allies and increase their maximum health. He is ready to do anything for the sake of common victory!"

Dorian Overall Rating in the Meta

This quick rating summarizes how Dorian performs in the current Hero Wars Alliance meta based on healing output, team survivability, CC immunity, and self-sufficiency.

  • Crowd Control (CC): ⭐⭐ — Dorian himself doesn't control enemies, but with the Legendary relic, he grants CC immunity to allies.
  • Damage Output: ⭐⭐ — Not a damage dealer; his Wings of Night deals magic damage mainly to enable healing, not burst.
  • Team Synergy: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Exceptional. Vampirism aura, Max Health boosts, resurrection, and CC immunity all stack beautifully with physical DPS teams.
  • Self-Sufficiency: ⭐⭐⭐ — Decent survivability through his own vampirism and class passive, but he is a deliberate self-sacrifice hero — he needs allies to return the favor.

Dorian Pros & Cons — Hero Wars Alliance

✅ Pros

  • Permanently increases allies' Max Health — scales with every point of his own Health.
  • Vampirism aura stays active even after Dorian dies.
  • Legendary relic grants stun and sleep immunity to the whole team.
  • Skill 6 (Call of Blood) can resurrect a dead ally — a game-changing mechanic in long fights.
  • Excellent for Hydra — especially Fire, Light, and Dark Hydra teams.

❌ Cons

  • Very dependent on ally team having Vampirism heroes to unlock his full kit.
  • Sacrifices his own HP — can self-destruct in weak teams without support.
  • Resurrection (Skill 6) has strict conditions: allies must heal through Vampirism during Near Death window.
  • Soul Stones are not easy to farm in early game.

Dorian Talisman Guide — Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian has two talismans: Talisman of Blood and Talisman of Thirst. Unlike skins — where you keep all bonuses regardless of which you wear — only the equipped talisman grants its stats. Choosing the right one can make a significant difference in battle. The three Reroll Slots count as one combined bonus, so what matters is the total from all three slots added together.

Talisman of Blood

The Talisman of Blood main slot gives Intelligence +2,000, which is Dorian's primary stat. Since Intelligence is his main stat, each point grants: 3 Magic Attack, 1 Magic Defense, and 1 Physical Attack. That means this talisman delivers +6,000 Magic Attack, +2,000 Magic Defense, and +2,000 Physical Attack from the main stat alone. All three reroll slots are dedicated to Magic Penetration, totaling +19,800 Magic Penetration combined. This directly reduces enemy magic resistance — making Wings of Night deal more damage, which means more healing returned to allies.

Talisman of Thirst

The Talisman of Thirst main slot gives Armor +12,000, which is a purely defensive stat. All three reroll slots are dedicated to Health, totaling +181,500 Health combined. This massive Health pool boosts survivability and — crucially — scales Ancestors' Amulet: the formula (20% Health + 6,000) means every 1,000 extra Health adds +200 Max Health per cast to the lowest-Health ally. With +181,500 Health from rerolls, Ancestors' Amulet hands out an extra +36,300 Max Health per cast.

Magic Penetration from the reroll slots makes Wings of Night deal more damage, which directly amplifies the healing returned to all allies — a multiplier effect you don't get from Thirst's Armor bonus. Switch to Talisman of Thirst only if your team is already dying to physical burst and needs raw HP to survive long enough for Ancestors' Amulet to stack — for example, against aggressive physical compositions in Guild Wars.

Dorian Legendary Relic Skills Upgrade Priority — Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian's rework introduced two legendary enhancements and two brand-new skills. Here's exactly how each one works, what the formulas mean in practice, and which order to upgrade them to get the most out of every Relic you spend.

1st — Fountain of Blood

In-game description: Applies the Fountain of Blood effect to the nearest enemy for 7.0 seconds. Allies' attacks against that enemy restore their Health. If the target dies before the effect ends, it transfers to the nearest enemy for 7.0 seconds. While active, the effect cannot be reapplied to the same target.

Skill Explanation: Dorian marks one enemy with a vampiric aura. Every time an ally hits that marked target, they steal life back — calculated as (30% Magic Attack + 12,000) healing per hit. If the marked enemy dies, the effect jumps to the next target automatically, keeping the healing chain alive throughout the fight. Think of it as a living leech that refuses to stop until your team is topped off.

Formula with Talisman of Blood

  • Healing per hit: Healing: ~64,594 (30% × 175,312 + 12,000)

Formula with Talisman of Thirst

  • Healing per hit: Healing: ~62,794 (30% × 169,312 + 12,000)

Skill Animation Info

  • Duration: 7.0 seconds
  • Keywords: Upgradeable to Legendary

Evolution Priority: Very High — This is Dorian's primary healing engine. Upgrading it raises the 12,000 base, while the Legendary enhancement extends duration and changes the healing formula. Always prioritize first.


1st — Fountain of Blood (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 10

In-game description: The effect now lasts 10 seconds (up from 7). Allies restore more HP when attacking affected enemies — healing formula changes to 10% of the enemy's Max Health, capped at (20% of Dorian's Health).

Skill Explanation: At Legendary level, Fountain of Blood becomes a percentage-based life-steal instead of a flat formula. Each hit now drains 10% of the enemy's Max Health, but is capped at (20% of Dorian's own Health) per hit. This means his Romantic skin and Health glyph directly increase how much healing each ally gets per attack — a very powerful feedback loop. With Talisman of Blood stats (~968,232 Health), the cap per hit is approximately ~193,646. Against tankier enemies this can far exceed the base skill healing.

Formula with Talisman of Blood

  • Healing cap per hit: Cap: ~193,646 (20% × 968,232 Health)

Formula with Talisman of Thirst

  • Healing cap per hit: Cap: ~229,946 (20% × 1,149,732 Health)
  • Duration: 10.0 seconds

Evolution Priority: Very High — The percentage-based formula is dramatically stronger against high-HP enemies like Hydra bosses. This relic transforms Dorian from a mid-tier healer into a top-tier support. Unlock as soon as the base skill is maxed.

2nd — Ancestors' Amulet

In-game description: Dorian sacrifices a portion of his current Health to increase the Max Health of the ally with the lowest Health until the end of the battle. This skill only affects allies who started the battle with him.

Skill Explanation: This is Dorian's identity skill after the rework. He drains (20% of his current Health) from himself and adds (20% Health + 6,000) permanently to the Max Health of his most vulnerable ally. The key mechanic: the more Health Dorian has, the more Max Health he gives. With Romantic skin, Health glyph, and Talisman of Thirst, this cast can grant over +200,000 Max Health to an ally in a long fight. It is the reason his Romantic skin and Health-scaling items have "Very High" priority.

Formula with Talisman of Blood

  • Max Health granted per cast: Max Health: ~199,646 (20% × 968,232 + 6,000)
  • Health sacrificed per cast: ~193,646 (20% of current Health)

Formula with Talisman of Thirst

  • Max Health granted per cast: Max Health: ~235,946 (20% × 1,149,732 + 6,000)
  • Keywords: Sacrifice

Evolution Priority: High — Upgrading raises the 6,000 base of the Max Health formula. The percentage part scales automatically with Dorian's Health items and skins, so this skill benefits from both direct upgrades and investment in the Romantic skin and Health glyph.

3rd — Wings of Night

In-game description: Attacks enemies and heals allies for 100% of the damage dealt.

Skill Explanation: Dorian unleashes a magic strike that deals (50% Magic Attack + 9,000) magic damage to enemies — and simultaneously heals all allies for the same amount. This is his burst healing tool: one cast can top off the whole team in a single global heal. Because it is 100% damage-to-healing conversion, Magic Penetration from the Talisman of Blood also indirectly boosts how much is healed by reducing enemy magic defense and maximizing the damage dealt.

Formula with Talisman of Blood

  • Damage & Healing: Magic Damage / Healing: ~96,656 (50% × 175,312 + 9,000)

Formula with Talisman of Thirst

  • Damage & Healing: Magic Damage / Healing: ~93,656 (50% × 169,312 + 9,000)

Evolution Priority: High — Upgrading raises the 9,000 base, directly increasing both damage and team-wide healing per cast. Second skill to invest in after Fountain of Blood is maxed.

4th — Blood Seal (Passive)

In-game description: Increases the Vampirism of nearby allies. This skill only affects Heroes who started the battle with Dorian and remains active after his death. The chance to increase Vampirism is lower if the allies' level is higher than 120.

Skill Explanation: This is Dorian's aura — a passive vampirism amplifier that boosts all nearby allies' life-steal by 105%. The most important mechanic here: it persists even after Dorian is dead. So even when the enemy kills him, your team keeps stealing life on every hit. This is why Dorian is so effective in Hydra teams — the aura sustains the whole fight duration.

  • Vampirism increase: +105%
  • Keywords: Upgradeable to Legendary, persists after death

Evolution Priority: Medium High — Upgrading Blood Seal increases the vampirism bonus percentage, which compounds across the whole team. It doesn't scale with Dorian's stats directly, but higher vampirism means every ally heals more on every single attack throughout the entire battle — including after he's dead.


4th — Blood Seal (Enhanced Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 20

In-game description: After being healed by Vampirism or Fountain of Blood, Dorian's allies become immune to stun and sleep for 3.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: This Legendary enhancement adds a reactive CC immunity layer on top of the vampirism aura. Every time an ally gets healed by Vampirism or Fountain of Blood — which happens constantly in a well-built team — they gain 3 seconds of stun and sleep immunity. In practice, this means your team is almost permanently protected from two of the most dangerous CC types in the game. Heroes like Polaris, Arachne, Keira, and Jet lose much of their lockdown value against a Dorian team with this relic active.

  • Immunity Duration: 3.0 seconds
  • Immunity Types: Stun, Sleep
  • Trigger: After being healed by Vampirism or Fountain of Blood

Evolution Priority: Very High — Permanent CC immunity for your whole team is one of the most impactful mechanics in PvP. This relic completely shuts down stun-based strategies and forces opponents to build entirely different compositions to counter Dorian. Unlock this as a top priority after Fountain of Blood Legendary.


5th — Blood Pact (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 1

In-game description: Every 14 seconds, deals damage to allies with Vampirism. For 7.0 seconds, Vampirism restores more Health Points. Dorian's skills also heal allies more during this time.

Skill Explanation: Blood Pact is a double-edged sword by design. Every 14 seconds, Dorian deals (5% of each ally's current Health) as pure damage to his own allies who have Vampirism — a deliberate sacrifice. In exchange, for the next 7 seconds, Vampirism heals for +100% more HP and all of Dorian's healing skills output double healing. This creates a powerful 7-second window where your entire team is virtually unkillable. The 5% damage to allies sounds scary but it enables the burst healing window that more than compensates for it.

  • Ally damage per trigger: Pure Damage: 5% of ally's current Health
  • Vampirism healing bonus: +100% for 7.0 seconds
  • Cooldown: 14 seconds
  • Keywords: Sacrifice

Evolution Priority: High — Unlocking this skill (Relic Level 1) gives you the doubled vampirism window immediately. Upgrading it further increases the healing bonus percentage. Once unlocked, the 14-second healing burst changes the flow of every long fight — critical for Hydra and prolonged PvP battles.


6th — Call of Blood (New Skill) Legendary Relic: Level 30

In-game description: When an ally dies, Dorian resurrects them, sacrificing himself. After that, Dorian enters the Near Death state for 5.0 seconds. He cannot move, use skills, or be attacked. If allies restore HP through Vampirism 1 time during this period, Dorian is resurrected. This skill only affects Heroes who started the battle with Dorian.

Skill Explanation: When one of your allies falls in battle, Dorian immediately sacrifices himself to bring them back with 50% of their Max Health. He then enters a frozen "Near Death" state for 5 seconds — he cannot move, act, or be targeted. If any ally heals even once through Vampirism during those 5 seconds, Dorian returns from the dead too. In a Vampirism-heavy team, this cycle can happen multiple times per battle — making Dorian and his team effectively immortal as long as they keep attacking.

  • Resurrection Health: 50% of ally's Max Health
  • Near Death Duration: 5.0 seconds
  • Vampirism heals to self-resurrect: 1 instance
  • Near Death restrictions: Cannot move / cannot use skills / cannot be attacked

Evolution Priority: Medium High — This skill is a game-changer in long fights and PvP, but requires Relic Level 30 to unlock — a significant investment. In teams built around vampirism, the resurrection cycle can decide entire matches. Invest here after Blood Seal Legendary and Blood Pact are both active.

Best Skin for Dorian Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian's skin priority centers on Intelligence and Health — stats that directly scale his healing output and the Max Health boost he grants through Ancestors' Amulet.

Default Skin

The Default Skin boosts Dorian's primary attribute: Intelligence +1,365. As his main stat, every Intelligence point grants three points of Magic Attack, one point of Magic Defense, and one extra point of Physical Attack — directly scaling Fountain of Blood and Wings of Night healing formulas.

  • Intelligence: +1,365
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +4,095
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +1,365
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +1,365

Evolution Priority: Very High – Intelligence multiplies into Magic Attack (×3), scaling every formula-based skill Dorian has. Always prioritize first.

Champion Skin

The Champion Skin directly raises Magic Attack +10,687 — the stat that scales Fountain of Blood (30% MA + 12,000 per hit) and Wings of Night (50% MA + 9,000 per cast), boosting healing output every time those skills fire.

  • Magic Attack: +10,687
  • Fountain of Blood bonus per hit: +3,206
  • Wings of Night healing bonus per cast: +5,344

Evolution Priority: High – Flat Magic Attack is efficient for healing scaling, especially during mid-game when skill formulas are the main healing driver. Prioritize third, after Default and Romantic.

Masquerade Skin

The Masquerade Skin provides Armor +10,650, reducing physical damage received. For a Back Line mage-healer, armor is the least impactful of his five stats since most threats against Dorian are magical.

  • Armor: +10,650

Evolution Priority: Low – Armor is largely wasted for a Back Line Intelligence hero whose survival threats are predominantly magical. Evolve last when all other skins are maxed.

Romantic Skin

The Romantic Skin provides Health +106,645 — the best secondary stat for Dorian. Health directly scales Ancestors' Amulet (20% Health + 6,000 Max Health granted to the lowest-Health ally per cast), making every point of Health a direct buff to how much Max Health he hands out.

  • Health: +106,645
  • Ancestors' Amulet Max Health bonus per cast: +21,329

Evolution Priority: Very High – Every point of Health multiplies through Ancestors' Amulet (×20%), making this skin both an offensive support tool and a survivability boost. Prioritize right after the Default Skin.

Winter Skin

The Winter Skin grants Magic Defense +10,687, improving Dorian's resistance to magic damage — the most common threat vector for a Back Line support hero frequently targeted by enemy mages and AoE abilities.

  • Magic Defense: +10,687

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Magic Defense meaningfully extends Dorian's survival time against magic-heavy enemy compositions, helping him keep casting Blood Seal and Ancestors' Amulet throughout the fight. Prioritize fourth.

Dorian Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian's artifacts reward a double-scaling approach: the Book and Ring both amplify his healing through Magic Attack and Intelligence, while the Weapon adds team-wide Toughness for defensive coverage.

1st - Weapon Artifact: Bleeding Steel

When Dorian uses a skill in combat, Bleeding Steel triggers an effect granting Toughness to the whole team for 9 seconds. Toughness reduces the critical damage your team receives. With 100% activation chance, this fires reliably on every skill cast.

  • Activation Chance: 100%
  • Toughness (team): +8,898
  • Duration: 9 seconds

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Toughness is a reliable defensive buff for the whole team but does not scale Dorian's healing formulas. Upgrade after the Book and Ring to complete the artifact set.

2nd - Book Artifact: Tome of Arcane Knowledge

The Tome of Arcane Knowledge delivers both Magic Attack +10,680 and Health +53,394 — making it Dorian's best artifact. Magic Attack amplifies Fountain of Blood and Wings of Night directly, while the Health bonus multiplies through Ancestors' Amulet's formula (20% Health + 6,000 Max Health per cast).

  • Magic Attack: +10,680
  • Health: +53,394
  • Fountain of Blood bonus per hit: +3,204
  • Wings of Night healing bonus per cast: +5,340
  • Ancestors' Amulet Max Health bonus per cast: +10,679

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) – This artifact uniquely scales both Dorian's active healing formulas and Ancestors' Amulet simultaneously. Always upgrade first.

3rd - Ring Artifact: Ring of Intelligence

The Ring of Intelligence boosts Dorian's primary stat. Each Intelligence point grants three points of Magic Attack, one of Magic Defense, and one of Physical Attack — the most efficient multiplier in his kit for scaling healing output and defensive resilience simultaneously.

  • Intelligence: +3,990
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +11,970
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +3,990
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +3,990

Evolution Priority: Very High (2nd Overall) – The 3× Magic Attack multiplier from Intelligence makes this the most stat-efficient offensive artifact for Dorian. Upgrade immediately after the Book.

Dorian Glyph Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian's glyph priorities follow his dual scaling: Magic Attack powers his healing formulas, and Health multiplies through Ancestors' Amulet — both simultaneously increase offense and survivability.

1st Glyph - Magic Attack

Magic Attack scales Dorian's two active healing skills: Fountain of Blood (30% MA + 12,000 per hit) and Wings of Night (50% MA + 9,000, heals for 100% of damage dealt). Also raises the damage-to-healing cap of the Healer class passive (30% MA + 89,100).

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Attack.

Evolution Priority: Very High (1st Overall) – Highest-impact glyph for all of Dorian's healing formulas. Upgrade first.

2nd Glyph - Health

Health directly scales Ancestors' Amulet: the formula is 20% Health + 6,000 Max Health granted per cast. With +122,800 Health from this glyph, Ancestors' Amulet hands out an extra +24,560 Max Health to the target ally each time it fires. Also extends Dorian's own survival and synergizes with Blood Pact (5% ally current Health damage).

Level 80 Stats: +122,800 Health.

Evolution Priority: Very High (2nd Overall) – Uniquely serves both offense (Ancestors' Amulet scaling) and survivability. Upgrade right after Magic Attack.

3rd Glyph - Armor

Armor reduces physical damage received by Dorian. While most Back Line threats are magical, physical carries (Keira, Jhu, Yasmine) can reach the back row — especially in PvP. A baseline armor investment keeps Dorian casting under physical pressure.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Armor.

Evolution Priority: Medium – Defensive value is real but situational. Armor contributes less to Dorian's core output than Magic Attack or Health. Upgrade third.

4th Glyph - Magic Defense

Magic Defense reduces damage from enemy mages. As a primary support target, Dorian is frequently focused by heroes like Orion, Lars, and Helios. Higher Magic Defense extends his survival window and ensures Blood Seal's Vampirism aura and Ancestors' Amulet remain active longer.

Level 80 Stats: +12,850 Magic Defense.

Evolution Priority: Medium High – Against magic-heavy PvP teams and certain hydra configurations, Magic Defense significantly extends Dorian's usefulness. Upgrade fourth.

5th Glyph - Intelligence

Intelligence is Dorian's primary stat, providing a three-way multiplier: Magic Attack (×3), Magic Defense (×1), and Physical Attack (×1). It scales all healing formulas while also improving survivability through added Magic Defense.

  • Intelligence: +2,110
  • Magic Attack (from Int): +6,330
  • Magic Defense (from Int): +2,110
  • Physical Attack (from Int): +2,110

Evolution Priority: High – The three-way multiplier is valuable, but dedicated Magic Attack and Health glyphs offer higher individual returns for Dorian's specific scaling. Upgrade fifth.

How to Counter Dorian — Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian's strength comes from sustained vampiric healing and his team's near-permanent CC immunity. To beat him, you need heroes who bypass healing, punish self-sacrifice, or overwhelm his team before the resurrection cycle can activate.

Arachne

Arachne's web immobilizes and stuns the backline. Before Blood Seal Legendary is unlocked, Dorian and his allies have no stun immunity — Arachne can lock Dorian down and prevent him from using Ancestors' Amulet or Blood Pact during the opening seconds of the fight.

Cornelius

Cornelius applies magic defense and kill mages with a monolith. While this doesn't directly counter healing, it punishes Dorian's teams.

Celeste

Celeste blocks healing.

Dorian Synergy in Hero Wars Alliance

Dorian reaches his full potential when paired with heroes that have natural Vampirism or that amplify his sacrifice mechanics. The more Vampirism in the team, the stronger Blood Pact's healing burst and Call of Blood's resurrection chain become.

Artemis

Artemis has natural high attack speed and benefits massively from the vampirism boost from Blood Seal. Every rapid-fire arrow heals her back through Dorian's aura, making her nearly unkillable in long DPS fights like Hydra.

Fafnir

Fafnir boosts team attack speed, which directly increases the number of times allies trigger Dorian's Fountain of Blood vampirism per second. More hits = more healing = more Blood Pact cycles. An essential pairing for Hydra damage.

Galahad

Galahad is a frontline tank who already has built-in Vampirism on his skills. Dorian's Blood Seal amplifies Galahad's self-sustain, making him nearly unkillable as a tank — the frontline holds while Dorian's healing runs in the background.

Lilith

Lilith's Soul Collector skill gives Vampirism to the entire team, which perfectly stacks with Dorian's Blood Seal aura. Together, they create a dual-vampirism layer that makes the Blood Pact healing window extraordinarily powerful.

Martha

Martha provides additional healing and a resurrection passive of her own. Running Martha alongside Dorian means the team has two resurrection mechanics — an extremely difficult combination to kill in Guild Wars or Hydra of Light encounters.

Tristan

Tristan amplifies physical attack speed and has built-in Vampirism on his charge. Blood Seal's Vampirism amplification stacks directly onto Tristan's own drain, making him deal damage and self-heal simultaneously — an incredible Hydra DPS combination.

Conclusion — Dorian Guide Hero Wars Alliance

The Dorian rework did something rare in Hero Wars Alliance: it took a good hero and made him extraordinary. He is no longer just a healbot standing in the back — he is a strategic engine of sacrifice, resurrection, and vampiric sustain that reshapes how every fight unfolds.

To get the best out of Dorian, always prioritize the Talisman of Blood for its Magic Penetration synergy with Wings of Night. Build the Romantic Skin and Health Glyph early to maximize Ancestors' Amulet's Max Health grant. For relics, unlock Fountain of Blood Legendary first, then Blood Seal Legendary for the team-wide CC immunity, and work toward Call of Blood for the resurrection chain that can completely swing close fights.

In Hydra teams, Dorian is S-tier for Fire and Light Hydra, and a reliable A-tier pick across most other types. In PvP, his CC immunity legendary makes the entire team resistant to stun-based strategies — forcing opponents to rethink their entire approach.

Invest in Dorian. He will pay you back in blood — and in victories.
