Pallant's Ancient Awakening Event Shop has several tempting offers, but the best strategy is to spend your event coins on progress that may be difficult to obtain again. Pallant Fragments, his Primordial Skin, rare Elarite Artifact fragments, and the right Hero Relic Shards deserve attention before normal resources.

The short version is simple: build Pallant first, buy the Elarite artifacts you actually need, and choose Dante if you want a generally useful Hero Relic. Avoid turning rare event currency into Fairy Dust, Spheres, or Titan Potions that regular gameplay can provide.

Alexandre's Quick Verdict

Best overall plan: get all three Pallant Fragment purchases, decide early whether you will reserve 90,000 coins for the Primordial Skin, then invest in Elarite artifacts. Dante Relic Shards are the best general Hero Relic purchase. Maya is only for a committed high-level team; Kai and Arachne can be skipped.

Best Ancient Awakening Shop Priority

  1. Pallant Fragments: buy all 3/3 offers for 30 fragments at a total cost of 18,600 event coins.
  2. Pallant's Primordial Skin: a very expensive but rare Health skin for an important tank. Reserve 90,000 coins if you want to secure it now.
  3. Rare Elarite Artifact fragments: Zorval's Rite, Zorval's Wing, Elarite Crown, Chthonic Balance Seal, and Chthonic Attack Seal are much harder to collect than normal shop materials.
  4. Dante Relic Shards: the strongest general Relic recommendation in this shop because Dante is useful in both Realm PvP and Realm PvE.
  5. Orm and Alecto Fragments: valuable for players who are actively building Water/Elarite or Fire/Elarite teams, but do not let them consume the budget reserved for Pallant.
  6. Angus Biomech Skin: optional for accounts that use Angus heavily. Sylva and Avalon are easy skips here.
  7. Everything else: buy only with leftovers, if at all.

This order is a framework, not a reason to buy an upgrade for a Titan you never use. The rarest purchase still needs to fit your real team plan.

#1 Priority: Pallant Fragments

The shop allows 3/3 purchases of Pallant Fragments. Each purchase gives 10 fragments for 6,200 event coins. Buying the complete stock gives you 30 Pallant Fragments for 18,600 coins.

This is one of the first offers I would secure. Pallant is the featured Titan, and another convenient opportunity to collect his fragments may take a long time to appear. Evolution directly improves the stats he needs to perform as an Earth/Elarite tank, so these guaranteed fragments have lasting value.

For his skills, skins, artifacts, and teams, read the complete Pallant guide.

Should You Buy Pallant's Primordial Skin?

Pallant's Primordial Skin costs 90,000 event coins. That is a high price, so you need to plan for it from the beginning instead of spending freely and hoping to have enough coins at the end.

The skin increases Health, which is one of the most important stats for a tank. More Health helps Pallant stay on the front line longer, protect the Titans behind him, and get more value from his battle mechanics. The unlock is especially attractive for players building Pallant as a long-term main tank.

The tradeoff is timing. New Titan skins can take months to become available through the regular 15,000 Titan Skin Stone unlock path. Buying the skin during Ancient Awakening gives you the unlock now; waiting can save event coins, but you may not know when the free path will open. Remember that the 90,000-coin purchase unlocks the skin - you will still need Titan Skin Stones to level it.

Relic Shards: Dante Is the Best Choice

The shop offers Relic Shards for Dante, Kai, Maya, and Arachne. Each purchase gives 2 Relic Shards for 9,300 event coins, with a limit of 10/10 per Hero. Completing one Hero's full stock costs 93,000 coins and gives 20 Relic Shards, so buying without a plan becomes extremely expensive.

Dante Relic Shards - Recommended

Dante is the only general Relic recommendation in this shop. He is a strong Hero and is particularly useful in Realm, where his Realm skills give him value in both PvP and PvE. If you are choosing one Hero Relic from this selection, Dante offers the broadest return for most developed accounts.

Maya Relic Shards - Only for a Dedicated Team

Maya is very good, but she is usually a project for players competing at a higher level, such as Royal League. Her Relic Shards make sense when you are deliberately building a team around Maya and already understand where she fits. Otherwise, pass on them and keep your event coins focused on Titans.

Kai and Arachne Relic Shards - Skip

Kai and Arachne are currently far from the main meta, making their expensive shop bundles hard to justify. Hero Relic Shards can also appear for free in the Relic Reward Chest from Guild VS. Unless either Hero is part of a specific personal project, save your coins.

Orm, Alecto, and Pallant Elarite Artifacts

The Elarite Artifact fragments are among the rarest and most valuable long-term rewards in this shop. These newer artifact families can remain difficult to obtain for a long time, so they deserve much more attention than common upgrade materials.

The shop contains Zorval's Rite and the Chthonic Balance Seal for Orm, plus Zorval's Wing and the Chthonic Attack Seal for Alecto. The Elarite Crown is shared by Elarite Titans, including Orm, Alecto, and Pallant. Pallant's unique weapon and Defense Seal are not included in this shop listing.

Each artifact offer gives 50 fragments for 1,200 event coins and can be purchased 20/20 times. One complete artifact stock therefore costs 24,000 coins and gives 1,000 fragments. Buying the full stock of all five artifacts would cost 120,000 coins, so prioritize the Titans and artifact slots you actually use.

Avalon, Sylva, and Angus Biomech Skins

The Avalon, Sylva, and Angus Biomech Skins each cost 14,000 event coins. These skins are already available through the normal unlock path for 15,000 Titan Skin Stones each, which makes them less urgent than Pallant's new Primordial Skin and the rare Elarite artifacts.

  • Angus: the only one I would consider here. Angus remains useful, especially as an Earth tank and for Dungeon progression. Buy the skin only if he is important to your account and you prefer saving 15,000 Titan Skin Stones.
  • Sylva: skip the event-coin purchase and unlock it with Titan Skin Stones if you eventually need it.
  • Avalon: also safe to skip here because the normal 15,000-Skin-Stone unlock is already available.

Even Angus should come after your rare-event priorities. Fourteen thousand event coins can buy more than eleven artifact bundles, so the opportunity cost is significant.

Items to Avoid in This Shop

Fairy Dust, Summoning Spheres, Elemental Spheres, and Titan Potions should stay near the bottom of your list. All of them are useful, but usefulness is not the same as shop value. They are available from normal progression, while Pallant Fragments, the new skin, and Elarite artifacts may remain difficult to obtain.

  • Fairy Dust: avoid spending event coins on a resource that builds gradually through regular Titan activities.
  • Summoning Spheres: the result is less targeted than buying the exact Pallant, Orm, or Alecto fragments your team needs.
  • Elemental Spheres: useful for general Artifact progress, but direct Elarite Artifact bundles remove the randomness.
  • Titan Potions: convenient for levels but replaceable through normal progression.
  • Titan Skin Stones: more useful than the other filler items, but still secondary to rare unlocks and fragments unless a small purchase completes an important skin level.

Final Recommendation

Buy the 30 Pallant Fragments first and decide immediately whether the 90,000-coin Primordial Skin is part of your budget. If Pallant is a serious tank project, the Health skin has excellent long-term value and may save you months of waiting.

After that, prioritize the rare Elarite Artifact fragments for the Titans you use. Choose Dante Relic Shards if your developed Hero account needs a broadly useful Relic project. Maya is situational, Kai and Arachne are skips, and the regular Titan materials should receive only leftover coins.
