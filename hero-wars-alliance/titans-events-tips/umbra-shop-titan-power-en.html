If you've just started playing Hero Wars Alliance and you're wondering what to do when the Titan Power Event shows up, don't panic. I know it can feel overwhelming at first. There are plenty of tips out there, but most of them assume you already know a lot about the game. I want to talk to you like a real person, because I remember what it's like to be a beginner and not know where to spend your resources. Let me walk you through Umbra's event shop step by step.

What Is the Titan Power Event and Why Does It Matter?

The Titan Power Event lasts three days. During those three days, the game rewards you with a special currency called Champion Coins just for playing as you normally would. You'll also collect items like the Titan Weapon Artifact and the Crown of Darkness, two resources that are very important for upgrading Umbra directly.

What makes this event extra special is that it can run together with other titan-focused events and rewards. That means while you're playing, you can also progress your titan account, collect Soul Stones, improve artifacts, and prepare your Darkness team for stronger PvP battles. Two rewards, one effort. If you know how to take advantage of this window, you will save a lot of time in your progression.

Who Is Umbra? Why Should You Care About This Titan?

Umbra is the Darkness Summoner in Hero Wars Alliance. His strength is not simple damage. His real value comes from controlling the enemy team's Energy with Child of Darkness. When Umbra summons his shadow, nearby enemies lose Energy, and that stolen Energy is transferred to your allied titan with the highest current Energy.

Umbra becomes much stronger when you build around Darkness Titans such as Brustar, Mort, Tenebris, and Keros. His passive, Shadow Bond, rewards you for keeping more Darkness Titans alive on the battlefield. In the right team, Umbra can delay enemy ultimates, speed up your own ultimates, and make Darkness teams extremely difficult to break.

But here is the honest beginner note: Umbra is mainly a PvP and faction synergy titan. If your main goal is dungeon progression, you still need strong tanks and core dungeon titans. That is why this shop guide keeps the same beginner logic as the other Titan Power shops: first build the featured titan, then support the titan roster that keeps your account growing.

And speaking of dungeon progression, make sure to read my guide on how to earn infinite gold in the dungeon, because that gold is going to be essential for upgrading artifacts, titan levels, and skins.

Also, don't forget to check the full Umbra Titan Guide if you want to understand his Energy drain, Shadow Bond bonuses, artifacts, and best counters.

What to Buy in Umbra's Shop - Beginner Priority List

This is the most important part of this guide. I want to be honest with you: at the beginning it's very tempting to buy a little bit of everything. But if you do that, you end up with nothing powerful. Focus is everything in Hero Wars Alliance. Here is my recommended priority order for beginners:

  1. #1 - Umbra Soul Stones (Absolute Priority)

    Your number one goal in this shop is to collect as many Umbra Soul Stones as possible to push him toward Absolute Star (the maximum star level). Every star you add to Umbra increases his base power and makes him more reliable in Darkness teams.

    Alexandre's tip: Once you have your first titan at 6 stars, the game unlocks the Titan Soul Shop, where you can spend extra Soul Stones (that you can't use anymore) as coins to buy helpful items like Titan Potions and Gold. The more Soul Stones you collect, especially in the dungeon, the more coins you'll have in that shop. So getting Umbra to Absolute Star is useful not only for PvP, but also for long-term account economy.

  2. #2 - Light and Dark Titan Boxes

    After Umbra, focus on boxes that help you grow Light and Dark Titans. Umbra wants Darkness allies beside him, and Light Titans are also important because they are the main counter to Darkness teams. Building both sides gives you better options for attack, defense, and future titan events.

  3. #3 - Chest of Seals

    Seal items directly increase your titans' main stats and health. This makes them more durable in battles and especially helps summoners like Umbra stay alive long enough to use their key abilities. Each chest you open is extra survivability for every titan you field.

  4. #4 - Summoning Spheres & Elemental Spheres

    Summoning Spheres are great because they give you a chance to earn more Soul Stones, including rare titan stones that are valuable for long-term progress. The Elemental Sphere gives Artifacts and Fairy Dust, but since Artifacts need a lot of Gold to be upgraded, be careful not to rush resources you cannot use yet. At this stage, getting more Soul Stones is still more valuable.

  5. #5 - Fairy Dust (Use with Caution!)

    Fairy Dust is available in the shop and it's tempting to buy, but I want to warn you about something. To use Fairy Dust, you need a lot of Gold. And when I say a lot, I mean levels of gold you probably don't have in the early game. If you apply Fairy Dust too early without the gold to back it up, you're stuck with inventory items you can't use.

    My recommendation: first focus on raising your titans' star levels. Then advance as far as you can in the dungeon, because the dungeon rewards you with more gold as your progress grows. Once your gold income is steady, then start investing in Fairy Dust to level up your titans' artifacts.

  6. #6 - Titan Potions

    Titan Potions are used to level up your titans. They are useful, but they should not be your first priority in this shop. You can get them for free from daily dungeon progress, so spend Champion Coins on rarer progress first.

Items to Avoid in the Shop (Beginners)

This section can save you weeks of wasted progress. There are two items in Umbra's shop that seem useful but are usually poor investments for beginners:

  • Avoid - Spark of Power - These are the items you earn automatically every time you level up a titan. As you progress and level up your titans more, you'll naturally accumulate plenty of them. Spending Champion Coins on Sparks of Power means you're buying something that will show up for free later.
  • Avoid - Gold - I know. Gold is scarce in the early game and it's incredibly frustrating. I've been there. But buying Gold in the event shop is one of the least efficient uses of Champion Coins you can make. Gold becomes much more abundant as you advance in the dungeon. If you follow my infinite gold dungeon guide and focus on upgrading the right titans first, the gold problem becomes much easier to manage.

Final thought: Events like Umbra's Champion Coins shop are your biggest windows to make real progress without spending real money. Each time this event appears, you have three days to farm hard, make smart choices in the shop, and take a significant step forward with your titan team. If you want to play Darkness teams seriously, Umbra is the kind of titan you should not leave behind.
