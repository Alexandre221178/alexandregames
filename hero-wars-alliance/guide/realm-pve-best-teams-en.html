⚠️ Realm PvE Changed After the Fortress Update

The Realm battle system changed with the arrival of the Fortress. Before this update, strong Heroes could practically win PvE battles by themselves. Now, damage against Bosses and Monsters depends mainly on your troops, while Heroes are valuable for the Realm Skills they provide to those troops.

There is one major exception: Jhu is currently the essential Hero for dealing consistently high direct damage to Realm Bosses and Monsters in my tests. This is the detail many players are missing. Dante is the second offensive priority because his Realm Skills greatly increase Lancer troop damage, while Kayla, Iris, Folio, and other Heroes known for PvP damage do not produce the same direct damage against PvE targets.

This guide explains the new PvE priority, the two best lineups I am using, why Realm Skills matter more than normal Hero power, and how to avoid weakening your army through unnecessary troop losses.

What Changed in Realm PvE?

Hero power alone is no longer a reliable way to judge a Realm PvE team. After the Fortress update, troops became the real source of most Boss and Monster damage. A highly upgraded Hero without Realm Skills can look stronger on the selection screen but still contribute less than a weaker Hero who properly buffs or protects the army.

Every Hero with Realm abilities also grants the automatic class bonus to troops of that Hero's class. Their individual Realm Skills may then add critical chance, burst damage, enemy debuffs, health, defense, or shields. That is why filling a team with strong normal Heroes who have no Realm Skills is a costly mistake: they neither increase troop damage nor help keep those troops alive.

The new PvE rule: build around Jhu's direct damage, prioritize Dante to boost Lancer troop damage, then use Heroes with useful Realm Skills to strengthen and protect the army.

Best Teams for Boss Rally and Monsters

Main Team – More Protection

Aidan, Iris, Jhu, Dante, Julius

This is my preferred safe lineup. Jhu supplies the essential direct PvE damage; Dante is the second offensive priority and greatly boosts Lancer troop damage; Aidan and Julius improve troop survival; and Iris provides Realm debuffs and Curse support.

Alternative Team – Kayla Version

Aidan, Jhu, Dante, Kayla, Julius

Use this version when Kayla and her Realm Skills are better developed. Kayla adds Infantry critical chance and directional troop damage, while Aidan, Julius, and Jhu preserve the core balance of damage and survival.

Both lineups work for Boss Rally and Monster battles. The important part is not copying five names blindly: Jhu must be present, the supporting Heroes need developed Realm Skills, and your troops must be strong enough for the target level.

Why Jhu Is Essential and Dante Is the Second Offensive Priority

Jhu may not look like the best general Realm Hero when you read a PvP tier list, but PvE follows a different rule. In my current Boss and Monster tests, Jhu is the indispensable Hero whose personal attacks cause the highest meaningful damage to these targets. Without him, even Heroes known for huge PvP damage—such as Kayla, Iris, or Folio—barely damage the PvE target directly.

Dante is the second-best offensive investment for this PvE formation, but his role is different. Jhu contributes exceptional direct Hero damage, while Dante's Realm Skills boost troop damage by giving Lancers +25% critical hit chance and a guaranteed +150% burst every three turns. The battle report can therefore show Dante as the second major damage contributor because the Lancers linked to his bonuses hit much harder.

This does not make the other Heroes useless. Their job is to provide Realm bonuses to the troops, weaken enemy troop classes, and reduce casualties. The supporting lineup is what helps the army deal its damage and survive long enough to finish the fight.

This is also why the Realm Tier List and this PvE guide should be used together. The tier list evaluates Realm Skills mainly for PvP; this page explains their value against PvE Bosses and Monsters, where Jhu has a unique role.

What Each Recommended Hero Does for the Troops

  • Jhu: the essential source of direct Hero damage in current PvE tests. His Realm Skills also give Lancers +13% defense and make every third allied Marksman attack deal +40% damage to enemy Lancers and +20% to enemy Marksmen.
  • Aidan: increases the health of all troops by +12%. Every attack also has a 13% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 15% damage for one turn.
  • Julius: gives all troops +11% defense. Every attack has a 30% chance to grant an 8% shield for two turns, making him one of the best options for reducing troop damage.
  • Dante: the second offensive priority after Jhu. He gives Lancers +25% critical hit chance; every three turns, they deal +150% additional damage and reduce the target's defense by 25% for one turn.
  • Iris: passively reduces enemy Marksman damage by 20%. Allied Marksman attacks have a 20% chance to apply a two-turn Curse that makes the target take 18% more damage.
  • Kayla: gives Infantry +16% critical hit chance. Every third Infantry attack deals +20% damage to enemy Lancers and +10% to enemy Marksmen.

Do not select by normal Hero power alone. A stronger Hero without Realm Skills can be much less useful than one of these Heroes because the new battle system is centered on troop damage and troop survival.

How to Beat Boss Rally

A coordinated guild Rally can have up to six members combining their forces. With Jhu in the lineup, a strong and organized group can defeat Bosses up to Level 30 comfortably. However, this result depends on the whole Rally—not only on the player who started it.

If guild members bring weak troops or fill their teams with powerful-looking Heroes who have no Realm Skills, their formations contribute little damage and protection. One well-prepared player cannot always carry several uninformed formations, especially against the highest Boss levels.

Boss Rally Checklist

  • Every member who has Jhu available should use him.
  • Use one of the two recommended teams and develop their Realm Skills.
  • Coordinate all six formations instead of judging only the Rally leader's power.
  • Check the report after every attack for troop damage, wounded units, and permanent deaths.
  • If losses are too high, lower the Boss level and improve the group's troops before trying again.

Monster Battles Are Harder Because You Fight Alone

Monster battles use the same Jhu-centered teams, but they are much harder than a guild Rally at a similar stage. A Rally combines the troops and bonuses of up to six guild members; a Monster battle is solo, so your formation must supply all the power by itself.

Do not expect the same team to produce the same result against Monsters that it produces in a Rally. Even with Jhu, you need stronger and more numerous troops. If your power is not high enough, attack a lower-level Monster. Consistent victories with controlled losses are better than repeatedly attacking a level your army cannot safely handle.

Choose the Level by Your Battle Report

Watch what happens to your troops after each battle. If units are only wounded, send them to the Hospital and let them recover. If troops are being permanently eliminated, every new attempt makes your army weaker. Drop to a lower Monster level until you can fight without unacceptable permanent losses.

Troop Survival Is More Important Than Displayed Hero Power

The post-Fortress system rewards accounts that build a large, high-rank army and use Realm Skills intelligently. Hero stars and normal upgrades still matter, but they cannot replace the troops that now deliver most of the PvE damage.

There are two very different outcomes after a battle: wounded troops can be treated in the Hospital, while dead troops are lost permanently and must be trained again. Repeated deaths reduce your real fighting strength even if your Heroes have not changed.

How to Grow Without Weakening Your Army

  • Keep troop training active and promote troops to the highest rank your Camp allows.
  • Upgrade the Troop Camp so you can unlock and promote higher-rank units.
  • Use Heroes with Realm Skills instead of choosing only the five highest normal-power Heroes.
  • Heal wounded troops before the next difficult attack.
  • Lower the target level whenever permanent losses become excessive.
  • For Rally, teach the whole guild the same strategy; coordination is part of your team's real power.

Second offensive priority: Dante, because his Realm Skills greatly increase Lancer troop damage.
