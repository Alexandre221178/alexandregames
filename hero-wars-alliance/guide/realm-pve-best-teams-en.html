Best Team for Boss Rally – Wild Demon (Level 5)

Team: Julius, Isaac, QingMao, Mojo, Jhu
Power used in my tests: ~965K

Alright, let's start with the Boss Rally. The Wild Demon is one of the toughest bosses you'll face at Level 5, but this team lineup gets the job done. I know 965K sounds like a lot of power, but here's the thing — I've received reports from players who have beaten this boss with significantly less power. So don't be discouraged if your team isn't there yet. The hero combination itself is what makes this work.

The beauty of the Rally system is that you don't have to do it alone. You can rally with your guild mates, and here's what makes this even better: the more allies who use this same team composition in the rally together, the fewer troops each person loses and the more rewards everyone earns. So share this tip with your guild! When your whole guild coordinates and uses this lineup, the results are incredible.

One more important tip: make sure you select at least 1 Spearman troop before starting the rally. This small detail can make a big difference in your survivability and helps reduce troop losses during the fight.

Pro Tips for Boss Rally

If you want to solo the Rally, I honestly recommend starting at Level 4 against the Dire Werewolf instead of jumping straight to Level 5. Here's why: at Level 4, if you use only Spearmen at their highest tier (like Tier 8), you can achieve zero troop losses. Yes, you read that right — zero losses. And that matters more than you think.

Now, if you do want to try Level 5 solo, you absolutely can win with this team. But be prepared: in my tests, I was losing about 20 to 30 Spearmen per battle at Level 5 even with decent power. That might not sound like a lot, but those losses add up over time, especially when you consider that every single troop needs to be retrained from scratch.

Here's the smart approach: Start at the lowest Rally level you can handle with zero or minimal troop losses. As you train more Spearmen and your troop count increases, your overall power grows and your losses naturally decrease. Then you move up to the next level. This way, you're constantly getting stronger without bleeding troops along the way.

One more important detail — the rewards between Rally Level 4 and Level 5 are honestly not that different. Many reward drops are random, and sometimes Level 4 gives you almost the same loot as Level 5. So think about it: is it really worth losing 30 Spearmen per fight for a marginally better reward? In my opinion, protecting your troops is always the smarter long-term play.

Quick summary for Rally:
• Guild Rally (recommended): Use Julius, Isaac, QingMao, Mojo, Jhu with your guild. Coordinate so everyone uses the same lineup.
• Solo Rally LV4: Use only Spearmen (highest tier). Target the Dire Werewolf. Aim for zero troop losses.
• Solo Rally LV5: You can win, but expect 20–30 Spearmen losses per fight. Use all available troops to minimize losses. Only attempt this when your troop count is high enough that the losses don't hurt.
• Golden rule: Start low, build up, and never rush to a level where you bleed troops unnecessarily.

Best Team for Monster – Hell Night (Level 25)

Main Team: Iris, Soleil, Nebula, Byrna, Cleaver
Power used in my tests: ~934K
Troop: Only 1x Infantry (Tier 8)

Now let's talk about the Monster battles. Unlike the Rally, Monster fights are solo only, so you need to pay even more attention to how you approach them. The Hell Night at Level 25 is the highest-level monster you can fight, and this team beats it consistently.

I know 934K might seem like a lot, but I have a friend who beat this same monster with only 829K power, and many other players have reported winning with even less than that. The hero synergy in this lineup is what carries the fight — not raw power alone.

The Cleaver Secret — Why He's Essential

Let me explain something really important about this team: Cleaver is your tank, and he needs to be strong. If Cleaver dies too quickly, the entire team falls apart because there's nobody left to absorb the damage. So if you're using this lineup and losing, the first thing you should check is Cleaver's stats.

Focus on improving Cleaver's HP (health) and Armor above everything else. His defensive stats are what keep the rest of your heroes alive long enough to do their damage. If Cleaver gets crushed early, Iris, Soleil, and the others simply can't survive on their own. Think of Cleaver as the foundation of a building — if the foundation is weak, the whole thing collapses.

The 1-Troop Strategy — This Changes Everything

Here's the tip that will absolutely change your Realm experience: send only 1 Infantry unit (Tier 8) into the Monster battle. Yes, just one single troop. I know it sounds crazy, but let me explain the math behind it.

When I tested using a full army of troops against the Level 25 Monster, I won every single fight — but I lost approximately 60 troops total across those battles. That's 60 troops gone forever. Never coming back. Each one requiring real training time to replace.

Now, when I used only 1 Infantry (Tier 8), my win rate was about 19 wins out of 20 fights. So yes, I lost roughly 1 out of every 20 battles. But here's the key: when I lost, I only lost that 1 troop. Compare that to losing 60 troops with a full army. The difference is massive.

When you lose a battle with the 1-troop strategy, you only lose energy (which regenerates) and 1 single troop. You don't lose meaningful power. But when you send a full army and troops die, you're losing real permanent power that takes hours and hours of training to rebuild.

What If You Keep Losing?

Sometimes the Monster's power spikes randomly — I'm not sure if it's a bug or just the game's randomness, but there are moments where the Monster becomes unusually strong and you lose no matter what your power is. I've tested this myself, and here's what works: if you lose, switch to a different Monster on the map. Don't attack the same one again. Move to another Hell Night, and you'll probably win.

Also, if Level 25 is too tough for you right now, there's no shame in dropping down to Level 24 or even Level 23. The most important thing is that you're farming rewards without losing troops. The rewards vary from level to level and sometimes the difference isn't even worth the risk.

Power: ~829K
Troop: Only 1x Spearman (Tier 1)

If you don't have the heroes for the main team, this alternative lineup also works at Level 25 with even lower power. That's the lowest possible troop investment, meaning if you lose a battle, the loss is practically nothing.

Why Troop Management Is the Real Game in Realm

I want to take a moment to really explain this because I've seen so many players make this mistake — and I made it too when I started. In Realm mode, your troops die permanently. Let me say that again: permanently. There is no hospital like in other base-building games. Once a troop is dead, it's dead. You have to train a brand new one from scratch, and that takes real time.

Here's the trap that catches everyone: you look at your total power after a battle and think, "I only lost 179 power, that's nothing." But that number is deceiving you. The real cost isn't the power number — it's the training time you now need to spend to replace those troops. For example, 573 Tier 8 troops take about 8 hours and 46 minutes to train. Imagine losing 200 T8 troops — that's hours of your account's progress, gone.

And here's another sneaky problem: after you lose strong T8 troops and retrain, you end up with a mix of different tier troops. Your total power number might look almost the same, but your actual army is weaker. You replaced elite soldiers with recruits, and that difference shows up when it matters most.

The Biggest Beginner Mistake

Almost every new Realm player does this: they send all their strongest troops (T8) into every single fight, thinking they're getting stronger. But the truth is the opposite. They're bleeding expensive troops, delaying their progress, and falling behind players who fight smarter.

The correct mindset is simple: every battle costs time. Not just the time you spend fighting, but the time needed to replace what you lose. A smart player asks before every fight: "Is the reward worth the troops I might lose?" If the answer is no, you drop to a lower level or use fewer troops.

The Right Way to Play Realm

Here's what the top players do — and I'm sharing this with you so you don't have to learn it the hard way:

1. Protect your strongest troops. Never throw T8 units into a fight where you know you'll take heavy losses. Use lower-tier troops (T6 or T7) as your frontline instead. They die faster, sure, but they're much faster to retrain.

2. Keep your barracks training 24/7. This is non-negotiable. Every minute your barracks are idle, you're losing potential power. Set alarms if you have to. Train troops constantly, all day, every day.

3. Choose your battles wisely. Not every fight is worth taking. If the reward doesn't justify the potential troop loss, skip it or go for a lower level. Your kingdom power depends on long-term troop accumulation, not short-term loot grabs.

4. Think in terms of time, not power. Power is just a number. Time is your most valuable resource. The player who manages their time best — by training nonstop and losing minimal troops — will always overtake the player who fights recklessly.

Remember: the best Realm player isn't the one with the most power — it's the one who wastes the least time retraining troops.
