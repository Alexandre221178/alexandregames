If you have been playing Hero Wars Alliance for a while, you have probably heard other players talking about "books" — those items you can buy with Global Championship trophies. The official name is Boosters, and there are three types: EXP Booster, Skill Booster, and Evolution Booster. Each one does something different, and knowing when to use them (and when NOT to) can save you thousands of trophies.

In this guide, I will walk you through each Booster one by one. I will explain the refund formula in plain language, show you real scenarios with numbers, and share the strategy I personally use to squeeze the most value from every single book. If you are just starting out, do not worry — this guide was written for you.

Where to Buy Boosters

All three Boosters cost 1,500 Global Championship trophies each. You purchase them from the Guild Championship Shop, which becomes available after your guild participates in the Global Championship.

Here is the important part many beginners miss: if you already have partial progress on the hero you are boosting, the game will refund a portion of the trophies back to your inventory. The closer the hero is to being maxed out, the more trophies you get back. This is the core of the strategy I will teach you below.

Skill Booster – The Hidden MVP

What It Does

The Skill Booster upgrades all unlocked skills of a chosen hero up to their current level cap. In Hero Wars Alliance, the maximum levels for skills are:

  • 1st and 2nd skill: level 120
  • 3rd skill: level 100
  • 4th skill: level 80

You might wonder: "What if I already upgraded some of the hero's skills manually?" Great question — the game calculates how much gold you have already spent and returns a proportion of trophies based on the remaining gap.

The Trophy Refund Formula

The formula looks intimidating, but here is what it means in simple terms: the more gold you already spent upgrading skills, the more trophies you get back.

Formula:

Refund = (1,500 / 20,032,100) × (20,032,100 − (X − Y))

  • 1,500 = the Booster price in trophies
  • 20,032,100 = total gold needed to max every skill from scratch
  • X = gold needed to reach the hero's current level cap
  • Y = gold you already spent on that hero's skills

Practical Example 1 — Beginner (Low Level Hero)

Imagine your Galahad is only level 6, with two skills unlocked. You have spent 200 gold on his skills so far. To bring both skills to level 6, the game needs 10,000 gold total.

  • X = 10,000
  • Y = 200

Refund = (1,500 / 20,032,100) × (20,032,100 − 9,800) = ~1,499 trophies back

So at level 6, you basically get all 1,500 trophies back minus a single trophy. Great, right? But the Booster only raised your skills from level 1 to level 6 — almost no real impact on your team.

Practical Example 2 — Near Maximum (The Smart Way)

Now picture a level 120 hero with all four skills unlocked. You have already spent 19,800,000 gold out of the 20,032,100 maximum, bringing skills very close to max.

  • X = 20,032,100 (hero is at cap level 120)
  • Y = 19,800,000 (you already manually leveled most skills)

Refund = (1,500 / 20,032,100) × (20,032,100 − 232,100) = ~1,483 trophies back

You still get ~1,483 trophies back — spending only about 17 trophies net to finish the remaining skills to max level. This is the sweet spot: level up skills manually as far as you can, then use the Booster to finish the last stretch and recover nearly all your trophies.

Alexandre Tips: The takeaway here is simple — never use the Skill Booster on a low-level hero. Instead, take your hero to level 120 first, manually level up every skill as high as your gold allows, then use the Skill Booster to clean up the rest. You will get nearly 1,500 trophies back every single time, which you can immediately reinvest into an Evolution Booster (which is way harder to replace with free resources).

Free Skill Booster from Hub Store Guild Rewards

Something most players do not realize: the Skill Booster is also available in the Hub Store under the Guild Rewards section. Here is how it works:

  • When guild members make purchases through the Hub, the guild earns bonus points.
  • As the guild reaches point milestones, everyone in the guild receives the rewards — including a free Skill Booster.
  • The counter resets every 30 days, so if your guild is active and members contribute, you can earn one extra Skill Booster per month at no personal cost.
  • Points belong to the guild. If you leave, your accumulated progress stays behind with the old guild.

This is essentially a free book every month that you can feed into the trophy-recycling loop I explained above. Talk to your guild about it — the more members contribute, the faster everyone benefits.

Alexandre's Trophy Skill Booster Recycling Strategy

This is the strategy I personally use and recommend to every player who asks me about Boosters. Here it is, step by step:

  1. Pick a hero you already use daily (Arena, Guild War, Campaign).
  2. Manually level their skills as high as possible — spend your gold until every skill is close to the hero's current level cap.
  3. Use the Skill Booster. Since you did the manual work, the Booster only cleans up the leftover gap. You will get back around 1,470–1,499 trophies.
  4. Buy an Evolution Booster with those recovered trophies.
  5. Use the Evolution Booster on a meta hero who needs the star upgrade the most — ideally someone whose Soul Stones are difficult to farm.
  6. Repeat the cycle every time you accumulate 1,500 trophies again.

The magic of this loop: you are effectively converting gold (which is easy to get) into Soul Stones (which are hard to get) by passing through the Skill Booster's refund system. Over time, you will push several heroes to Absolute Star much faster than players who buy Boosters blindly.

Alexandre Tips: Keep in mind that this only works well if you invest the gold to level skills manually first. If you use the Skill Booster on a hero with base-level skills, you will waste most of your trophies. Patience pays off here — spend the gold, then use the Booster, then grab the Evolution book.

Evolution Booster – The Soul Stone Saver

What It Does

The Evolution Booster raises the evolution level of one hero by exactly one star. You cannot go beyond the Absolute Star. Each use consumes up to 300 Soul Stones worth of value, and if the star upgrade needs fewer than 300 stones, you get trophies back.

Important: This Booster does not work on certain recently released heroes. The game will tell you which heroes are excluded when you tap the Booster's description. After some time, Nexters usually removes the restriction for those heroes.

The Trophy Refund Formula

Refund = (1,500 / 300) × (300 − (X − Y))

  • 1,500 = price in trophies
  • 300 = maximum Soul Stones the Booster covers
  • X = Soul Stones required for the next star
  • Y = Soul Stones you already own for this hero

Practical Example 1 — Mid-Range (4★ to 5★, With Some Stones)

You want to evolve Galahad from 4 to 5 stars. That requires 150 Soul Stones, and you already collected 25 of them.

  • X = 150
  • Y = 25

Refund = 5 × (300 − 125) = 5 × 175 = 875 trophies back

You spent 1,500 trophies, got back 875, so the net cost was 625 trophies to jump an entire star. That is a solid deal, especially for meta heroes whose Soul Stones are hard to farm.

Practical Example 2 — Near Maximum (5★ to 6★, With 280 Stones)

Going from 5 to 6 stars costs 300 Soul Stones. Suppose you have been saving and already have 280 stones.

  • X = 300
  • Y = 280

Refund = 5 × (300 − 20) = 5 × 280 = 1,400 trophies back

Your net cost: only 100 trophies for the final, most expensive star upgrade in the game. And the best part is that those 280 Soul Stones you collected are consumed by the Booster instead of being wasted — the trophy refund accounts for them automatically.

EXP Booster – The Weakest Option

What It Does

The EXP Booster raises the level of one hero to match your current team level (capped at level 120). If the hero is already above level 1, or you have not reached team level 120 yet, you get some trophies back.

The Trophy Refund Formula

Refund = (1,500 / 2,948,945) × (2,948,945 − (X − Y))

  • 1,500 = Booster price
  • 2,948,945 = total EXP a hero needs to reach level 120
  • X = max EXP your team level currently allows
  • Y = EXP the hero already has

Practical Example 1 — Mid-Range (Level 30 → 60)

Your Galahad is level 30 with 6,675 EXP, and your team level is 60. At level 60, a hero can hold up to 174,094 EXP.

  • X = 174,094
  • Y = 6,675

Refund = (1,500 / 2,948,945) × (2,948,945 − 167,419) = ~1,414 trophies back

Net cost: about 86 trophies to jump from level 30 to 60. Sounds nice, but here is the catch...

Practical Example 2 — Near Maximum (Level 115 → 120)

Suppose the hero is already level 115 with 2,600,000 EXP. Your team is level 120.

  • X = 2,948,945
  • Y = 2,600,000

Refund = (1,500 / 2,948,945) × (2,948,945 − 348,945) = ~1,322 trophies back

Net cost: about 178 trophies for 5 hero levels. You got more levels but spent more net trophies than the mid-range scenario! That is because EXP costs grow exponentially.

Why the EXP Booster Is Usually Not Worth It

Here is the honest truth: you can level up heroes in many other ways, Daily missions, campaign battles, Tower rewards, and event tasks all give EXP potions. You can also spend emeralds on energy refills during events that already grant EXP alongside other loot.

Global Championship trophies, on the other hand, can only come from the Global Championship itself. That makes them far more valuable than EXP, which flows in from multiple sources every day. Spending 1,500 rare trophies to skip something you can get for free is almost never a good trade.

The only scenario where it makes any sense is if you urgently need a brand-new hero at max level for an upcoming Guild War or Arena push, and you have zero potions saved. Even then, think twice.

Alexandre Tips: I have been playing Hero Wars for years and I can count on one hand the number of times the EXP Booster was the right call. Unless you are in a critical emergency, skip this one entirely. Put those trophies into Skill Boosters or Evolution Boosters instead — you will thank yourself later when your meta hero jumps to 6 stars while other players are still farming Soul Stones.

Verdict: Which Booster Should You Buy First?

After years of playing and testing every combination, here is my honest ranking:

  1. Evolution Booster — The undisputed champion. Soul Stones are the hardest resource to farm in Hero Wars, especially for meta heroes like Iris, Phobos, Dante, Fafnir, and many others. There is no free alternative to Soul Stones — you either grind them slowly or use this book. The jump from 5★ to 6★ Absolute Star costs 300 stones and can take months of campaign farming. A single Booster solves it instantly.
  2. Skill Booster — Extremely useful, but mainly as a trophy-recycling tool. When you level skills manually first, this Booster gives you almost all your trophies back, which you can funnel into Evolution Boosters. Use it regularly.
  3. EXP Booster — The weakest by far. EXP is available from too many free sources (daily missions, campaign, Tower, events). Spending rare trophies on EXP is almost always a waste. Only consider it in extreme emergencies.

If you are a newer player, the best thing you can do is focus on your first five heroes: build a solid main team, use the Skill Booster loop to save trophies, and use Evolution Boosters to push your core damage dealer and tank to 6★ Absolute Star as fast as possible. This alone will rocket your Arena placement and daily emerald income.
