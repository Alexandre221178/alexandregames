How Byrna Changed the Hydra META in 2026

Before Byrna arrived, the dominant Hydra team was Martha, Aidan, Faceless, Xe'sha, and Mushy. Faceless would copy Aidan's powerful sacrificial skills, creating a devastating damage cycle. This team dominated Hydra content through most of 2024 and into 2025.

Everything changed when Byrna was introduced. Unlike Aidan, Byrna's ultimate skill cannot be copied by Faceless. But that is not a weakness, it is exactly what makes the new composition so explosive.

With Byrna in the team, Faceless can no longer copy Xe'sha's ultimate, Mushy's ultimate, or Byrna's ultimate. The only skill Faceless ends up copying is Martha's Foremother's Oath, her ultimate that increases all allies' speed by 240% for 6 seconds.

This means Faceless constantly duplicates a 240% speed buff across the entire team. The battle becomes extraordinarily fast, heroes are activating their skills in rapid succession, generating a cascade of damage and healing that obliterates Hydra heads, sometimes in just a single battle. Damage records exceeding millions per battle are now common with this team.

Key Insight — Why the Combo Works:

Because Byrna, Xe'sha, and Mushy all have uncopyable ultimates, Faceless defaults entirely to Martha's 240% speed buff. Every Faceless cast is a second 240% speed boost for the whole team. This creates an ultra-fast battle rhythm where Xe'sha's passive bonus compounds from continuous ally health loss and Mushy's Rotting Seeds deals exponentially more damage from all the accumulated healing.

Hero Synergy Breakdown: How Each Hero Contributes

Understanding why this team works requires looking at each hero's role and how their skills interact. Below is a detailed breakdown of the abilities that make this composition exceptional.

Martha — The Engine of the Team

Foremother's Oath (Ultimate) — Increases all allies' speed by 240% for 6 seconds.

Martha's ultimate is the cornerstone of the entire combo. Because Faceless cannot copy Byrna's, Xe'sha's, or Mushy's ultimates, he defaults entirely to copying Martha's speed buff. Every Faceless ultimate cast applies the same 240% speed increase to all allies. In practice, the team operates at near-maximum speed for almost the entire battle, dramatically increasing how many skills fire before the fight ends.

Martha is also an excellent healer throughout the fight, keeping the team alive long enough for the damage to compound.

Faceless — The Speed Multiplier

Ultimate (Copy Skill) — Copies the last used ultimate of an ally.

In this composition, Faceless ends up copying only Martha's ultimate. This is the key mechanic. With Byrna's, Xe'sha's, and Mushy's ultimates all marked as Can't Be Copied, Faceless will always replicate Martha's Foremother's Oath, giving the whole team a second 240% speed buff that chains with Martha's own casts.

The result is a battle that plays out at incredible speed — skill cycles that would normally take a full fight finish in a fraction of the time, multiplying total damage output enormously.

Xe'sha — The Damage Amplifier

Spiritual Power (2nd Skill) — Sacrifice / Buff

  • Magic attack bonus: (50% Mag. atk. + 3600)
  • Xe'sha fills allies with power, dealing 3% of their max health as damage if their current health is above 25%. Empowered heroes gain a magic attack bonus for 4 seconds. The bonus is doubled for Chaos Heroes.

Soaring Might (4th Skill) — Sacrifice / Passive

  • Whenever an ally loses health from allied skills, Xe'sha permanently gains a magic attack bonus equal to 25% of the health lost by the ally.

Xe'sha's passive stacks continuously throughout the battle. Because Martha heals allies and Xe'sha's 2nd skill constantly sacrifices ally health, Xe'sha's magic attack climbs to astronomical levels. Combined with the ultra-fast speed from the Martha + Faceless loop, these bonuses compound rapidly into record-breaking magic damage.

Byrna — The Sustain Core Ultimate: Can't Be Copied

Roar of Nature (Ultimate) — Can't Be Copied

  • Magic Damage: (85% Mag. atk. + 6000)
  • Health regen: 50% of damage dealt by the skill
  • ouardian Spirit deals magic damage to enemies while Byrna and nearby allies restore Health equal to 50% of the damage dealt.

Byrna's full kit revolves around healing and maximum health increases. At Legendary Relic level 20, she also increases the maximum health of allies whenever healing is received from any source — even when allies are already at full health. This means Byrna's presence continuously expands the entire team's health pool throughout the battle, which directly feeds Mushy's damage formula.

Byrna also has a secondary skill that grants extra energy to the ally with the lowest health, providing additional sustain and keeping the team's skill cycling active even when heroes take heavy damage.

Critical note: Because Byrna's ultimate cannot be copied by Faceless, she is the hero who unlocked the entire current META by "freeing" Faceless to copy only Martha. This single mechanic is the fundamental shift that makes the 2026 composition so powerful.

Mushy & Shroom — The Healing Converter

Rotting Seeds (Skill)

  • Basic damage: (40% Mag. atk. + 6000) + 42579 (10% Health)
  • Extra damage: 60% of all allied healing received since this skill was last activated.
  • Damages the nearest enemy. Damage increases for each point of healing received by allies since the last activation.

Mushy is the team's damage outlet for all the healing generated by Martha and Byrna. Every point of healing the team receives is converted into bonus damage on Mushy's next Rotting Seeds cast. With Martha and Byrna constantly healing, and the battle running at 240% speed, the healing accumulated between each cast becomes enormous — pushing this single skill into the tens of millions of damage per cast.

Types of Hydras in Hero Wars Alliance: Full Analysis

Hydras are formidable guild bosses in Hero Wars Alliance. There are six types, each with different elemental abilities and mechanics. Understanding each type is essential for choosing the right team composition and maximizing damage.

Earth Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals physical damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals physical damage and stuns the first hero affected plus nearby heroes in a small area.
  • Analysis: The most physically dangerous Hydra. Its high physical damage output is why Aidan replaces Xe'sha in the current META for this type. Heroes with high physical resistance or survivability buffs are critical here.

Water Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals physical damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals physical damage — the closer the hero, the more damage they take. Affected enemies are drastically slowed.
  • Analysis: The slowing effect can disrupt skill cycles, making Martha and Faceless's speed boosts even more critical to keep the damage chain running.

Fire Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals physical damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals physical damage and applies burns to affected heroes, reducing their armor and dealing physical damage over time.
  • Analysis: The burn reduces armor progressively, making sustained healing from Martha and Byrna essential to keep the team alive long enough for damage to maximize.

Light Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals magic damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals pure damage and blinds affected heroes, reducing their accuracy.
  • Analysis: Blindness reduces physical attack accuracy, but since the main damage sources in the current META are magical (Xe'sha, Mushy), the Light Hydra is one of the highest-damage fights for this team, reaching 190M.

Wind Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals physical damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals magic damage and knocks heroes back. The first affected hero is pushed further than others and slowed drastically.
  • Analysis: Knockback and slow can interrupt positioning, but the massive speed buffs from Martha and Faceless help the team recover quickly and maintain damage output.

Darkness Hydra

  • Basic Attack: Deals magic damage.
  • Elemental Breath: Deals magic damage. Affected heroes gain the Cursed Flame penalty — preventing any healing.
  • Analysis: The healing block is dangerous for a healing-dependent team. However, Byrna's maximum health increases remain active even when healing is blocked, and the team's speed advantage means they deal their damage before Cursed Flame becomes lethal.

Hydra Levels

  • Common: Suitable for beginners and lower-level teams.
  • Uncommon: Slightly stronger, moderate challenge.
  • Rare: Requires a well-rounded team and strategic approach.
  • Epic: Demands a highly skilled and coordinated team.
  • Legendary: The hardest level. All META teams in this guide are optimized for Legendary Hydra.

Important Tip:

Hydras are immune to all Crowd Control (CC) effects such as stuns, knockdowns, and charms. Focus on damage output, healing, and survivability rather than CC when selecting heroes.

Hydra Rewards

Rewards vary based on the Hydra's level and the number of heads defeated. Possible rewards include Fairy Dust (used to level up Titanic Artifacts) and Elemental Spheres (exchanged for artifact shards at the Altar of the Elements). Preview rewards from the Hydra selection menu by tapping the three stripes button.

The Golden Horn Crate

The Golden Horn Crate can be obtained from the Altar of Elements by summoning Titan Elemental Spheres. When you open a Golden Horn Box, every member of your guild receives a Golden Horn of Summoning, and a notification appears in the guild chat.

Extra Hydra Battles

Each Golden Horn provides an extra attack against the Hydra, even after exhausting your daily free Summoning Horns. Save at least 6 extra Golden Horns for special events — they do not expire.

How Crystal Fairies Work

Crystal Fairies become accessible only after all Hydra heads have been defeated. In end-game guilds where most heroes are at maximum level, all heads may be defeated quickly. The Crystal Fairies allow ouild members who could not attack the Hydra in time to still receive their daily summoning sphere rewards.

You can gift your Golden Horns to the fairies to receive summoning spheres from daily missions. However, if you have already used your three daily attacks and offer Golden Horns to the fairies without completing the daily mission, you will spend your Golden Horns without receiving anything. Complete the daily mission first.

Best Hydra Teams for Beginners: Jhu and Mojo

If you are just starting out in Hero Wars Alliance and do not yet have Byrna, Xe'sha, or Faceless fully developed, Jhu and Mojo form an excellent beginner duo. They are easy to acquire and can deal solid Hydra damage even at lower account levels.

Why Jhu and Mojo Work Well Together

  • Jhu's abilities benefit greatly from Mojo's Hex skill, which continues dealing damage even if Mojo is defeated in battle.
  • Jhu's first ability makes him temporarily invincible, boosts his attack speed, deals pure damage, and restores health — making him very durable in Hydra fights.
  • Focus on leveling Mojo's Hex first and Jhu's first skill second for maximum results.

Hydra ouide Conclusion

The arrival of Byrna in Hero Wars Alliance marked a turning point in Hydra content. By making Faceless a dedicated Martha speed-buff machine, the new META achieves damage numbers that previously seemed impossible. Teams that once peaked at 80–90M on Water and Earth Hydras now routinely exceed 180M+.

The key heroes to prioritize for Hydra content in 2026 are: Martha, Faceless, Xe'sha, Byrna, and Mushy. If you are building toward this composition, Martha and Faceless are the foundation. Add Xe'sha and Mushy next, then invest in Byrna to unlock the full power of the META.
