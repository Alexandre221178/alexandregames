The Ginger Soul Stone Trick

The first farm I believe every beginner should know is Heroic Mission 1-5, Merchant Camp. I have farmed Ginger there almost every day since I started playing Hero Wars. What makes this mission special is its reliability: it awards a Ginger Soul Stone on every run.

Your three normal attempts therefore produce three guaranteed Ginger Soul Stones. I also spend 20 emeralds to refresh the attempts, bringing my total to six Soul Stones per day. Six does not sound dramatic, but this is exactly the kind of farm whose value becomes obvious after weeks, months, and years.

Ginger is probably the hero for whom I have collected the most Soul Stones. You do not need to make her the center of your main team to benefit from the farm; the long-term objective is the currency those extra stones eventually generate.

Why Ginger Still Matters at Absolute Star

A common beginner question is: why keep farming Ginger after she reaches Absolute Star? Because her extra Soul Stones can be exchanged for Soul Coins in the Soul Stone Shop. Reaching your first six-star hero unlocks this shop and turns every surplus stone into useful account progression.

The shop can provide valuable resources such as:

  • equipment and crafting materials;
  • Artifact Chest Keys;
  • Titan Summoning Spheres; and
  • Jet Soul Stones.

My honest recommendation for beginners is not to rush Jet. He is much farther from the current meta than he was in earlier years. I would spend most Soul Coins on equipment and progression materials that improve the heroes you already use. A resource that helps your core team today is usually better than a long, expensive side project.

Realm Mode and Soul Coin Priorities

Realm Mode has become another excellent source of value for new accounts. It can provide strong hero Soul Stones, Titan resources, and useful progression materials every day. Several heroes available there are stronger investments than Ginger herself.

That does not make the Ginger route obsolete. I see the two systems as complementary: Realm Mode helps build the roster, while Ginger's guaranteed surplus keeps feeding the Soul Stone Shop and completing daily Heroic Missions. More extra Soul Stones mean more Soul Coins, and more Soul Coins mean less time waiting for the equipment your teams need.

The Best Beginner Energy Investment

One of the biggest mistakes I see beginners make is trying to farm many Heroic Missions without buying enough energy. My recommendation is to take the two daily energy purchases that cost 50 emeralds each. Each purchase grants 120 energy, so 100 emeralds buys 240 additional energy for farming.

In my opinion, this is one of the best routine emerald investments for beginners and free-to-play players. Extra energy means more Soul Stones, more equipment pieces, faster hero promotion, and more account experience. Early random summons may feel exciting, but energy gives you control over what you are building. In Hero Wars Alliance, energy is progression.

Aurora Mission 9-1: My Second-Best Farm

After Ginger's guaranteed route, Heroic Mission 9-1, Shore, is my second-favorite farm. This Aurora mission always gives your account a useful chance to progress: you may receive Aurora Soul Stones or important purple equipment components.

Its key drops include Minotaur's Head, Hand of Glory, and Desert Blade. These components appear repeatedly in crafting recipes, which makes the mission valuable even when Aurora's Soul Stone does not drop. You are working on a hero and your wider equipment inventory with the same energy.

Why Heroic Missions Are So Efficient

Heroic Missions give you two possible wins: hero Soul Stones and equipment materials. A missed Soul Stone is not necessarily wasted energy when the mission also supplies a component your team will soon need.

This is why I prefer missions whose equipment remains useful across many crafting recipes. Instead of farming only the next visible item, I gradually build a reserve of frequent bottlenecks. That reserve makes it much easier to promote new heroes or strengthen a second and third team later.

Conclusion: Consistency Beats Luck

Strong Hero Wars Alliance accounts are not built in one lucky day. They are built by repeating small, efficient choices. That is why I still farm many of these missions after years of playing: guaranteed Ginger Soul Stones create Soul Coins, high-value Heroic Missions fill equipment gaps, and daily energy purchases keep the entire system moving.

Start with a routine you can afford and sustain. If your main team needs a specific purple item, prioritize that bottleneck instead of copying every route blindly. The best farm is the one that moves your actual heroes and teams toward their next useful upgrade.
