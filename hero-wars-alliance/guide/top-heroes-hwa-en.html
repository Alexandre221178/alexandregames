Top 17 Meta Heroes in Hero Wars Alliance

What's up, everyone? I'm Alexandre from Alexandre Games. If I started a brand-new account today, these are the heroes I would want to build first. This is not a list based only on raw damage. I ranked them by Arena and Guild War performance, versatility, how often they appear in strong teams, and the long-term value they bring to an account.

The meta never stops moving, so treat this as my current opinion rather than an eternal rule. Some choices will be controversial, and my number two is the biggest surprise of the entire ranking.

Top 17 Heroes at a Glance

  1. Dorian
  2. Kendle
  3. Electra
  4. Julius

  6. Yasmine

  9. Guus

#17 Dorian

Dorian is back in the meta after receiving his relics. He fits powerful Chaos teams and becomes especially dangerous beside Iris: his Vampirism Aura can make her extremely difficult to bring down. The best part is the low entry cost. Level his vampirism skill and he can already produce results, because the aura remains on the battlefield even after Dorian is eliminated.

Read the complete Dorian guide

#16 Kendle

Kendle has excellent synergy with critical-damage and Chaos heroes. Her ability to let pure damage score critical hits creates frightening combinations with front-line pure-damage allies. She is the kind of hero who can turn an already threatening damage core into something opponents cannot prepare for with normal defenses.

Read the complete Kendle guide

#15 Electra

I still think many players underestimate Electra. She protects magic teams by taking damage for her allies, making the entire formation harder to kill. That protection becomes even more valuable in long fights, where fragile mages normally collapse before they can take control.

Read the complete Electra guide

#14 Julius

Julius remains one of the safest tanks in the game. He shields, heals, removes debuffs, and protects the heroes responsible for your damage. What makes his position even more interesting is his future potential: he already does all this without relic skills.

Read the complete Julius guide

#13 Drayne

Drayne is easy for beginners to overlook, but that changes at the highest levels. With Golden Relics, he becomes incredibly strong and shows his real value in Royal League battles. He is a late-game investment whose ceiling is much higher than his early performance suggests.

Read the complete Drayne guide

#12 Yasmine

Yes, Yasmine has more counters today. That does not make her harmless. If an opponent enters the fight without the right answer, she can still tear through the back line and end the battle in seconds. Her speed and explosive pressure continue to punish poorly protected teams.

Read the complete Yasmine guide

#11 Somna

I really like what Somna brings to the game. Sleep is one of the most annoying control effects to face, and her accessibility makes the choice even better: you can farm her through Realm. That mix of control and practical progression gives her excellent account value.

Read the complete Somna guide

#10 Byrna

Byrna is one of the best healers in the current meta, but healing is only part of her value. She also feeds energy to the team. More energy means more ultimates, and more ultimates often decide close battles before the opponent can recover.

Read the complete Byrna guide

#9 Guus

Guus is one of my favorite healers for physical teams. He works especially well in critical-damage formations and can support several of the strongest offensive cores on this list. The scary part is that he still does not have relic skills, so his future ceiling may be even higher.

Read the complete Guus guide

#8 Miu

The more I test Miu, the more I like her. She deals serious magic damage, applies Blindness, and keeps appearing in more top-level teams. She is no longer just an interesting experiment; she is becoming a reliable meta option.

Read the complete Miu guide

#7 Kayla and Aidan

Even after the arrival of many counters, Kayla and Aidan remain one of the game's strongest duos. Aidan also works in many other formations, so your investment does not depend entirely on keeping the pair together. You gain two valuable heroes and a core that can still overwhelm unprepared defenses.

Read the Kayla guide
See the best Kayla and Aidan teams

#6 Polaris

Some players may disagree with placing Polaris this high, but in my own tests she outperformed Miu in several battles. She also fits many strong team variations, giving her the kind of flexibility I always value when deciding where to spend scarce resources.

Read the complete Polaris guide

#5 Folio

Folio is a powerful mage who is unusually friendly to build. His Soul Stones are accessible, and Guild-versus-Guild rewards make his relic progression more practical. Combine that accessibility with his battle impact and you get one of the safest magic investments in the game.

Read the complete Folio guide

#4 Satori

Satori's damage is simply absurd. If the enemy allows him to place his marks and execute his plan, the fight can end before they understand what happened. He demands protection and timing, but the payoff is one of the most explosive win conditions in Hero Wars Alliance.

Read the complete Satori guide

#3 Leonel

I honestly believe every player should build Leonel. He destroys tanks, breaks shields, and is completely free to play through Realm. Very few heroes combine that kind of anti-meta power with such accessible progression, and that makes him outstanding value.

Read the complete Leonel guide

#2 Crow

Crow is the biggest surprise on this list and the hero whose position changed most after real-battle testing. He became one of my favorite answers to magic teams because his Silence can shut down dangerous mages before their skills take over. If your Arena is full of magic damage, Crow is one of the best heroes you can build right now.

Read the complete Crow guide

#1 Iris

Iris is still the queen of the current meta. She fits different team styles, deals overpowering damage, and wins battles that appear lost. Her combination of flexibility and fight-changing pressure is why she remains my number-one hero today.

Read the complete Iris guide

My Final Take on the 2026 Meta

The meta will change. New relics, skins, counters, and heroes can completely rearrange this ranking in only a few months. Today, however, these are the 17 heroes I trust most for account value and competitive performance.

Do not build a hero only because of a number beside their name. Start with the team you want to create, identify the role you are missing, and use this ranking to decide which flexible investments can strengthen that plan. Now I want to hear your list: who should be higher, and who should not be here at all?
