Mistake #1: Upgrading Random Buildings – Focus on Heroes' Hall Level 9

The first massive mistake most players make is upgrading buildings randomly without a clear plan. Your absolute priority should be reaching Level 9 of the Heroes' Hall. Why? Because that's where you unlock the Research Center — the most important building in the entire Realm Mode.

The Research Center is where you upgrade technologies that grant real stats to ALL of your Hero Wars Alliance heroes — not just in the Realm, but in Arena, Guild Wars, Campaign, and everywhere else. There are 3 tech trees to explore:

  • 🌱 Growth Tree: Boosts stats for ALL your heroes in the main game — HP, Attack, Armor, and other crucial kingdom improvements.
  • 💰 Economy Tree: Increases iron and food production — start here to avoid resource shortages.
  • ⚔️ Battle Tree: Enhances troop and hero stats for combat effectiveness and army performance in Realm battles.

Pro tip: Begin with the Economy tree to sustain your resource flow, then shift your focus entirely to the Battle tree for those game-changing stat boosts that will make your heroes significantly stronger across all game modes.

Why the Research Center is a Game-Changer

The Research Center is a game-changer because it allows you to upgrade technologies that provide real, tangible power benefits to all your heroes in Hero Wars Alliance. By focusing on the right tech trees, you can significantly boost all your heroes' stats, improve your resource production, and enhance your overall combat effectiveness. This makes the Research Center the most critical building in the Realm Mode.

To maximize your Realm power, prioritize upgrading the following buildings in this order:

  • Heroes' Hall: The main structure of your Realm. Upgrading it unlocks new buildings, additional features, and increases your overall progression.
  • Research Center: One of the most important buildings in the Realm. Research upgrades boost your heroes and buildings across multiple game modes, not just inside the Realm. Once your Heroes' Hall reaches Level 9, prioritizing the Research Center becomes extremely important.
  • Training Grounds: Increases the level cap of your heroes and troops, allowing them to grow stronger and improving your combat efficiency.
  • Resource Buildings (Farms, Mines, and Lumber Mills): These buildings generate the essential resources required for upgrades and research. Keeping them upgraded ensures a steady resource income.

Optimizing Resource Buildings with Elarite

In addition to the standard upgrades using basic resources, each resource building contains special furniture upgrades that require Elarite. These upgrades significantly increase the production output of your buildings.

While upgrading these furniture pieces is not required to level up the building itself, the production bonuses they provide are extremely valuable. Investing Elarite here is one of the most efficient ways to boost your long-term resource income.

The main buildings that benefit from Elarite upgrades include:

  • Mill
  • Sawmill
  • Stone Quarry
  • Iron Mine

Getting Resources from the World Map

Another important way to collect resources is by sending your heroes and troops to gather them directly on the World Map. Resource nodes appear near your Realm and can be farmed by sending marches to collect them.

There are six levels of resource nodes, and higher levels contain significantly larger amounts of resources. The amount your troops can carry and the gathering speed depend on your overall power and the Research Technologies you have unlocked.

Farming higher-level nodes can take more than 8 hours, making it a perfect activity to run while you are offline, sleeping, or working.

Mistake #2: Wasting Resources on Wrong Realm Skills

Here's a crucial detail that many players overlook: in the Realm, your characters gain 2 Exclusive Skills specifically for construction battles. You need to level up these skills to defeat the stronger demons on the world map. The fatal mistake? Leveling up random skills on random heroes without a plan. Since resources are extremely scarce, you need to know exactly which ones to prioritize.

🎯 Here's the smart strategy for Realm Skills:

  • To level up Realm Skills, you need to defeat camps on the world map. The more camps you clear, the more chests you earn containing random Realm skill points.
  • Focus on building a complete formation first: prioritize having 1 Archer, 1 Lancer, and 1 Infantry hero.
  • Separate your heroes into Attack Troops and Gathering Troops.
  • Attack Troops → Prioritize leveling their Realm Skills first so they can beat tougher monsters.
  • Gathering Troops → If a character doesn't have Realm Skills, assign them to gather wood, food, and iron on the world map instead.
  • Try to complete your strongest attack formation first to take on higher-level camps and earn better rewards!

🏆 Golden Tips: Speed-Up Warning & Resource Management

🔴 CRITICAL TIP — Don't Auto-Finish the Last Minute!

When speeding up constructions in the Realm, never leave it on automatic for the last minute. The final countdown gets broken into small fragments, and you'll keep losing precious seconds that add up over time. Those wasted minutes will come back to haunt you later when you desperately need speed-ups for important upgrades. Always manually manage your speed-ups and stop before the final minute to let the timer run out naturally.

⚔️ RAID: How to Activate Auto Join for Raid Boss Battles

If you want to gather resources while you are AFK, you can enable the Auto Join option in your Rally settings. This feature allows your heroes to automatically participate in rallies started by your guildmates.

After selecting your Auto Join Formation, simply press X to close the Auto Join screen. Your heroes will then automatically participate in rallies against Raid Bosses started by your guildmates for up to 8 hours.

This is a great way to help your guild succeed and earn rewards even while you are offline. Just make sure your formation includes strong heroes with good Realm Skills so your troops can perform better during these raids.

When a Raid Boss appears, your team will automatically join the rally. Make sure to check your formation regularly to ensure it contains the best heroes available for supporting your troops.

⚔️How to Choose the Best Realm Team for Raid and Monster Battles

When preparing your formation for Realm battles, it is important to choose heroes that have strong Realm Skills. While the overall power of your heroes helps, the final result of the battle is mostly determined by the strength of your troops.

In the Realm, heroes mainly act as support for your troops. Their abilities can provide powerful buffs that increase troop attack, defense, and survivability. Because of this, heroes with strong Realm Skills can greatly improve the effectiveness of your army.

Some heroes can deal very high damage, such as those often used in Hydra battles. However, if they do not have Realm Skills, their impact in Realm battles will be much lower. Without proper troop support, heroes alone usually cannot win fights against stronger enemies.

Before starting a battle, make sure to review your troop selection and levels. Stronger troops significantly increase your army's combat power, and their level often has a bigger impact on the battle than the individual strength of your heroes.

Before enabling Auto Join or starting a battle, make sure to set a strong team in the Formation area. Teams with good survivability and consistent damage perform better in most Realm activities, including raids and monster battles. Heroes that provide shields, healing, or damage reduction can help your troops survive longer and increase your chances of victory.

Alexandre Pro Tips: A useful strategy is to separate your heroes based on their roles. Heroes without Realm Skills can be assigned to resource gathering on the World Map, since their combat bonuses are not needed there. Meanwhile, heroes with strong Realm Skills should be reserved for fighting monsters and Raid Bosses, where their buffs can greatly improve the strength and survivability of your troops.
