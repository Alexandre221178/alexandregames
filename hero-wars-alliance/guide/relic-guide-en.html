Basics of Relics

At their core, Relics empower heroes by granting them new abilities while boosting existing ones. Beyond skill improvements, Relics also enhance two secondary stats of each hero. The stats boosted depend on how that particular hero’s skills scale:

You’ll gain access to the Relic screen early in the game at team level 15, but don’t expect to unlock Relics right away. They only become available once your hero reaches level 50 and has at least four evolution stars.

Heroes with Relics include Somna, Arachne, and the heroes listed in the magic and physical attack tables below. Always keep an eye on official news and events, as Relics are shaping up to become a cornerstone of endgame progression.

Exclusive Skills

Perhaps the most thrilling aspect of Relics is the introduction of Exclusive Skills. These skills dramatically enhance your gameplay and can be the deciding factor between victory and defeat. There are four milestones in Relic development:

  • Level 1: Unlocks a brand-new active skill unique to that hero.
  • Level 10: Enhances the ultimate ability, adding new effects and power. In Hero Wars, the ultimate is the skill a hero uses once their energy bar is fully charged.
  • Level 20: Upgrades another core skill (different depending on the hero it may enhance either the green or blue skill).
  • Level 30: Unlocks a powerful new passive skill.

Enhanced skills retain their original function but gain additional effects, making them stronger and more versatile. To highlight this, their icons also transform into golden versions. Keep in mind that new skills unlocked at levels 1 and 30 don’t automatically generate energy unless their description specifically states otherwise.

When a hero has all Relic skills unlocked, they receive one enhancement to their ultimate plus two entirely new abilities. This means a fully upgraded Relic hero can bring a total of six active/passive skills into battle, greatly expanding their tactical impact.

Upgrading Relics

Relics can be upgraded up to level 30. Each level boosts stats, improves abilities, and unlocks milestones. To level them up, you’ll need Relic Shards, a valuable resource tied directly to hero progression.

Unlike ordinary upgrades, Relic progression demands careful resource management. You’ll want to decide whether to invest in one hero heavily or spread your shards across multiple heroes. This choice can significantly impact your team synergy.

Relic Shards Types

Image Type Description
Universal Relic Shards Relic Shards are a universal resource that any hero can use. They are required to upgrade a Relic’s level.
Note: Universal Shards can only be used after you unlock a Relic level 1.
Individual Relic Shards Individual Relic Shards are unique to each hero and named after them (e.g., Arachne Relic Shards). They are required both to unlock a Relic and to upgrade its level.

Relic Shards Explained

Relic Shards come in two forms: individual shards (specific to each hero) and universal shards (usable for any hero). You can mix and match them, but only individual shards can be used to initially unlock a Relic.

Here’s the breakdown of shard requirements for leveling:

LevelShards Needed
1 (Unlock)50 individual shards
2–520 shards each
6–1040 shards each

Additional requirements apply at certain milestones:

  • Level 10: Requires Blue rank and Absolute evolution (6 stars).
  • Level 20: Requires Violet rank.

Visual Progression & Avatars

As your Relic evolves, your hero’s avatar also changes color to reflect their Relic’s level. This visual indicator makes it easy to spot powerful heroes at a glance:

  • Level 1: Green
  • Level 10: Blue
  • Level 20: Violet
  • Level 30: Orange

Somna Relic - Progression & Avatars

       

In battle, the Relic itself appears next to the hero with matching color and level, symbolizing their strength and progression.

Final Thoughts

Relics are far more than just another upgrade system they are a game-changing addition that redefines how we think about heroes in Hero Wars Alliance. With their ability to grant exclusive skills, boost stats, and unlock exciting new strategies, Relics open the door to endless team compositions and tactical possibilities.

Whether you’re a casual player curious about new content or a competitive warrior seeking every possible advantage, diving into the world of Relics is a journey worth taking. Start planning now which heroes you want to invest in, because the earlier you begin, the faster you’ll reap the rewards.

Somna and Arachne may be the first to wield these mystical artifacts, but rest assured more heroes will join them soon. And when they do, the battlefield of Dominion will never be the same.
