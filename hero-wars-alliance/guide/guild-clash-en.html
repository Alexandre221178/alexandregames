A concise walkthrough of the new cross‑server Guild Clash mode: how it runs, when to prepare, team placement tips and what you can win.

Guild Clash is a weeklong, cross‑server competition where six guilds face off in a sequence of encounters. Each guild fields multiple teams across three lanes; the goal is to outplay opponents, win as many matchups as possible and claim individual and guild rewards.

Event timeline (7 days)

  1. Days 1–3 — Registration. Guild members register their top teams and assign them to lanes.
  2. Short matchmaking pause. The system pairs guilds with comparable strength.
  3. Battles period — 5 consecutive matchups. Each matchup consists of lane clashes; between matches leaders can rearrange placements.
  4. Final scoring & rewards. Results are tallied and prizes are distributed to players and the guild.

Registration — what to do first

  • Pick your two strongest teams. These will carry most of your guild’s offensive and defensive weight, so choose lineups you trust.
  • Place teams into lanes. Consider counters, lane matchups and which heroes perform best in each lane.
  • Finish upgrades. Level up heroes, awakenings and gear before registration ends — small power gains can flip close fights.
  • Complete pre‑event tasks. Any special challenges that grant temporary boosts or items should be prioritized.

Clash Boosters in the Registration Phase

During the registration phase, you can activate and upgrade your personal boosters to strengthen your teams before battles begin. To access this feature, simply tap Boosters in the event’s main menu.

To unlock or upgrade boosters, you’ll need Booster Tokens , which can be earned through a parallel special event. Managing these tokens wisely can give your Guild a significant advantage once the battles start.

Important Note: Boosters remain active until the final results are announced and are reset automatically when the event concludes.

Which Booster Should You Choose?

Life Vigor – Health is far better for survivability compared to Rune Ward – Magic Defense and Aegis Shield – Armor. For offense, Arcana Staff – Magic Attack and Mighty Blade – Physical Attack are strong options; I recommend pairing them based on your team’s main stat — Intelligence for magic-based heroes.

When building your team, focus on using both defense and attack boosters of the same type — either all physical or all magical. This approach helps you save Booster Tokens and allows you to upgrade those boosters to higher levels more efficiently. If you still have spare tokens afterward, invest them into Armor or Magic Defense for additional balance.

Matchmaking & battle flow

After registration closes, the event runs a short matchmaking step to pair similar guilds. Then your guild will play five matches in a row against five different enemy guilds. Between each match, Guild Masters and Generals can change team placements — this is the core strategic layer of the event.

How lane control works

You will place offensive and defensive teams across three lanes. Matches are won by capturing objectives in lanes, usually by defeating opposing teams quickly. Timing, counters and correct placement are crucial.

Practical strategy tips

  • Coordinate as a guild. Decisions about placement should involve your officers and active players — group strategy beats solo decisions.
  • Save flexible teams. Keep at least one adaptable lineup that can be moved to counter unexpected opponents.
  • Exploit counters. Track which heroes enemy guilds commonly use and plan specific lane counters.
  • Use information between rounds. After each matchup, analyze results quickly and swap teams where needed.
  • Prioritize relic shard farming. Many event rewards include universal relic shards — focus battles where you can reliably win those bonuses.

Battle Phase

The Battle Phase is made up of five rounds, with each round pitting your Guild against a different opponent from your group. During each round, both Guilds strive to capture three flags located on separate battle lanes. To win the match, a Guild must secure at least two out of the three flags.

All battles occur automatically, without direct player control. Teams enter combat one after another, starting from the weakest and progressing to the strongest. The winner of a battle is determined by who triumphs in both attack and defense, or who achieves a faster victory in one of the duels.

Note: Your Guild’s attack serves as the rival Guild’s defense, and vice versa. When a team wins, it advances to face the next enemy squad in the queue, but keep in mind — the heroes’ health does not regenerate between fights.

A team is removed from the round if it is defeated, if it has already won twice, or if both sides fail to win on attack and only succeed in defense after time runs out — resulting in a draw that eliminates both teams. The first Guild to defeat every enemy team in a lane captures the flag. If the final battle ends in a draw, neither side claims the flag.

Each round lasts four hours. Once the timer expires, you can open the Journal to review the results and replay past battles. At the end of the round, Guilds earn Clash Points based on performance: the Guild that captures the most flags earns three points, while a draw grants one point to each side.

Rewards & progression

Both personal and guild prizes are awarded after results are calculated. Rewards typically include currency, upgrade materials and universal relic shards — useful for upgrading multiple heroes. The better your guild performs across the five matches, the bigger the payout.

Guild Clash Rewards

Guild Clash Shop

Each Guild Clash event features a different hero available for purchase using Guild Clash Trophies — the special currency you earn by participating in battles. These trophies can be spent in the event shop to acquire Hero Stones, as well as several other valuable resources.

The shop also offers rare and highly sought-after items such as Relic Shards and Rune Spheres, along with Booster Tokens that can be used for Clash Boosters during the Registration Phase. Although each batch of 500 Booster Tokens costs 500 Emeralds, it’s still a worthwhile investment for competitive guilds aiming to secure better results in the event.

Best practices before the event

  • Complete daily quests and resource runs to have upgrade materials ready.
  • Test different team compositions in friendly practice matches where possible.
  • Keep active communication channels open (guild chat, external apps) during the event.
  • Assign clear roles to officers: who makes final placement calls, who monitors opponent lineups, and who coordinates late swaps.

Teams Registration

How many teams can I register?
Typically you register your two main teams and place additional lineups for lane coverage — check the event UI for the exact limit.
Can I change my teams after registration?
During the registration window you can modify picks. After registration closes you’ll have the opportunity to rearrange placements between matches, but check the event rules for lock periods.
Are matches cross‑server?
Yes — Guild Clash pairs guilds from different servers so expect unfamiliar opponents.

F.A.Q. — Guild Clash

November 06, 2025

Q: How do I join the event?

A: Reach level 12 and belong to a Guild. For the Guild to participate, at least five members must register their teams. The more members who register, the stronger your Guild’s chances — everyone is welcome to join! 😉

Q: Will hero upgrades after registration count?

A: No. Once registration closes, hero power is locked for the event, so any upgrades applied afterwards will not affect your registered teams.

Q: How do you defeat an opposing Guild?

A: Guild battles are fought across three lanes. To win a round, your Guild must secure at least two of the three lanes. Teams in each lane fight automatically; winning the majority of lane fights secures that lane.

Q: What are these lanes and how do they work?

A: Each match has three lanes — top, middle and bottom. A lane can hold up to 10 players. Teams placed on the same lane will battle automatically, and the Guild that wins most fights on that lane captures it.

Q: How is the winner of the event determined?

A: Guilds earn points for each victory against other guilds. At the end of the event, total points determine the ranking. Higher ranks bring bigger rewards for both individuals and the guild as a whole.

Q: What kinds of rewards are there?

A: Rewards include both individual and guild prizes. Individuals collect rewards based on lane performance, while guild-wide payouts depend on your Guild’s final standing. Expect currencies, upgrade materials and universal relic shards.

Q: Will all Heroes be available for participation?

Important Note

A: Depending on the Guild League, there are restrictions on the use of heroes from certain factions: the higher the League, the more factions are banned.

Q: I still have questions — where can I find more details?

A: If this FAQ doesn’t answer everything, there will be an in-game tutorial and a detailed guide published on the event’s first day. Check the official channels and your Guild’s communication for updates.
