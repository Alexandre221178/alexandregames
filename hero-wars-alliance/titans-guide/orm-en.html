Hey friends, welcome back! Orm is one of the most interesting Titans added to Hero Wars Alliance because he does not simply bring more damage to Water teams. He changes how the team survives pressure. As an Elarite and Water support Titan, Orm links your allies to a central enemy, stores part of the damage your team receives, and then sends that stored power back as a punishing hit.

If you are building around Hyperion, Tydus, Mairi, Nova, or Sigurd, Orm gives Water teams a very different rhythm. He rewards teams that can survive long enough for Devastating Link to finish, and he becomes especially scary when enemies keep pouring damage into your allies during those 8 seconds.

Orm is called the Mystery Keeper, and that title fits his gameplay. He is a Back Line support Titan who strengthens Water compositions through damage redirection and delayed punishment. He does not replace Hyperion's healing, Sigurd's protection, or Nova's control. Instead, he connects those pieces into a more dangerous team plan.

His Elarite side also gives him special artifact interactions against Light and Dark Titans. That makes him valuable in matchups where classic elemental Water teams might need extra tools.

Who Is Orm in Hero Wars Alliance?

Orm is a Back Line support Titan with two elements: Elarite and Water. His main role is not to burst enemies instantly, but to make enemy damage work against them. When Orm activates Devastating Link, all allies become bound to the enemy in the center. During the effect, Orm accumulates part of the damage taken by allies and releases it when the effect ends.

  • Role: Support
  • Element: Elarite + Water
  • Position: Back Line
  • Synergy: Water Titans

Because his skill depends on damage taken by allies, Orm works best with teams that can protect themselves while the link is active. Sigurd's immunity, Hyperion's healing, Tydus's sustain, Mairi's attack reduction, and Nova's stun all help Orm reach the payoff moment.

Orm Skills Guide - Hero Wars Alliance

Orm has one signature skill in the available data, and it defines his whole identity as a Water support Titan.

Devastating Link (Ultimate)

In-game description: Binds all allies to the enemy in the center for 8.0 seconds. Accumulates a portion of damage taken by allies and deals it to the enemy when the effect ends.

Duration: 8.0 seconds

Target: Enemy in the center

Accumulated damage: 50% of damage taken by allies

Formula: Accumulated Damage = 50% of total damage taken by allies during 8.0 seconds.

Skill Explanation: When Orm uses Devastating Link, he binds all allies to the enemy in the center for 8 seconds. During that window, Orm accumulates a portion of the damage your allies take. When the effect ends, that stored damage is dealt to the linked enemy.

This means Orm is strongest when your team can take pressure without collapsing. The enemy may think they are winning by damaging your whole team, but Orm turns part of that pressure into a delayed counterattack.

If allies take 2,000,000 total damage during the link, Orm can store 1,000,000 damage for the final hit.

Alexandre's Strategy Note: Orm wants a team that can stay alive while the link is active. Sigurd's immunity, Hyperion's healing, Mairi's attack reduction, Tydus's healing support, and Nova's stun all help buy the time Orm needs. The more damage the enemy wastes into a protected Water team, the more threatening the final Devastating Link explosion becomes.

Best Skin for Titan Orm - Hero Wars Alliance

Orm has two skins in the JSON data: Default Skin and Primordial Skin. Both are useful, but they support different parts of his kit.

Default Skin

Physical Attack: +196,800

The Default Skin increases Orm's Physical Attack. This is useful because Orm still attacks and benefits from offensive scaling, but his role is not pure DPS. Prioritize this when you want more pressure and better finishing power from your Water team.

Evolution Priority for PVP: High - More Physical Attack keeps Orm's pressure relevant, but survival still matters more for his support role.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Medium - Useful for damage, but the Dungeon usually rewards Orm more for staying alive through longer fights.

Primordial Skin

Health: +2,627,975

The Primordial Skin is extremely important for Orm because Devastating Link needs time. Extra Health helps him survive long battles and makes the Water team more stable in pressure-heavy matchups.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Very High - For a support Titan who needs to stay alive until Devastating Link ends, this is the safest investment.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Very High - Extra Health improves consistency in long Dungeon battles and helps Orm survive repeated waves.

Skin Priority Summary:
1st: Primordial Skin - Health improves Orm's survival and consistency.
2nd: Default Skin - Physical Attack improves offensive pressure after durability is secured.

Orm Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Orm's artifacts follow the Elarite pattern: a unique weapon, the Elarite Crown, and a Chthonic Seal. His weapon is the most important because it directly supports the whole team when he uses Devastating Link.

Zorval's Rite (Weapon Artifact)

Activation chance: 100%

Team buff duration: 9 seconds

Extra Defense from Light and Dark Titans: +150,000

When Orm uses Devastating Link, Zorval's Rite can trigger a team-wide bonus for 9 seconds. At level 120, the activation chance is 100%, so every ultimate gives your whole team extra protection against Light and Dark Titans.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Very High - This is Orm's most important artifact because it gives the whole team a guaranteed defensive buff after his ultimate.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: High - Strong team protection is valuable, but Light and Dark Titan matchups are not present in every Dungeon fight.

Elarite Crown (Crown Artifact)

Extra Damage to Light and Dark Titans: +213,000

Extra Defense from Light and Dark Titans: +120,600

The Elarite Crown is excellent against Light and Dark Titans. It gives Orm both offensive and defensive bonuses in those matchups, but it is more situational than the weapon and seal because it does not help against Earth, Water, or Fire Titans.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Medium - There are five Titan element groups, and this Crown only helps against Light and Dark, so it should not rank above medium for general PVP.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Medium - Useful only in the right elemental matchups. Upgrade it after the weapon and seal.

Chthonic Balance Seal (Seal Artifact)

Physical Attack: +97,500

Health: +3,975,000

This is the most universally useful stat artifact for Orm. It gives him a huge Health boost and a solid Physical Attack increase, improving both survival and pressure in every matchup.

Evolution Priority for PVP: High - Prioritize it right after Zorval's Rite because Health and Physical Attack work in every matchup.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Very High - This is Orm's best Dungeon artifact because it improves survival and damage in every battle, regardless of enemy element.

Artifact Priority Summary:
1st: Zorval's Rite - guaranteed team defensive buff after Devastating Link.
2nd: Chthonic Balance Seal - Health plus Physical Attack for every matchup.
3rd: Elarite Crown - powerful against Light and Dark Titans, but situational.

How to Counter Titan Orm - Hero Wars Alliance

Orm is dangerous when your team keeps dealing damage into Devastating Link without finishing his allies. The best counters are teams that can pressure Water Titans quickly, disrupt the formation, or use the Earth element advantage against Water compositions.

Angus

Angus: Angus is one of the strongest answers to Orm because Earth Titans naturally pressure Water Titans. His sustained area damage can hit the whole Water lineup during Orm's setup window, forcing Hyperion, Tydus, and Sigurd to spend resources defensively before Devastating Link can pay off.

Avalon

Avalon: Avalon helps Earth teams survive the delayed punishment from Devastating Link. Her shielding gives the Earth lineup more time to keep attacking while reducing the danger of Orm's final stored-damage strike.

Eden

Eden: Eden is a key counter because his control can remove a random Titan from the fight and break the timing Orm wants. If Orm's team loses control before Devastating Link ends, the Water formation becomes much easier to defeat.

Sylva

Sylva: Sylva strengthens Earth Titan pressure and helps the team keep momentum against Water lineups. This matters because Orm is strongest when his allies survive calmly; Sylva helps Earth teams turn the fight into constant pressure instead.

Verdoc

Verdoc: Verdoc is especially dangerous for Orm because he improves Earth team performance against Water Titans. Extra pressure and stronger Earth synergy make it harder for Orm's Water team to survive the full 8-second Devastating Link duration.

Water Titans + Orm Versus Light Titans in Battles

Water Titans + Elarite Titan Orm in battles are very effective against Light Titans, Fire Titans, and Darkness Titans due to their synergy and strategic abilities.

Best Teams for Titan Orm - Hero Wars Alliance

Orm belongs naturally in Water Titan teams. The two formations below come directly from the Orm JSON notes and focus on different ways to protect the team while Orm prepares Devastating Link.

Team 1 - Water Team with Nova as Front Line

This is a very strong team using Nova as a tank-style frontliner. Even though Nova is a Marksman, when she stands in the front line she gains energy quickly and can use her ultimate to stun enemies. That control gives Orm more time to complete Devastating Link.

Team 2 - Water Team with Sigurd Tank

Team Member Roles

  • Hyperion: Super Titan, Marksman, healing and damage.
  • Mairi: Support Titan that decreases enemies' attack by 40%.
  • Tydus: Summoner Titan and healer.
  • Orm: Support Titan and Devastating Link specialist.
  • Nova: Marksman Titan with important stun control.
  • Sigurd: Tank Titan with shield and immunity during ultimate.

Team Weaknesses and Considerations

Weak Against: Earth Titans such as Eden, Sylva, Avalon, Verdoc, and Angus. These teams can pressure Water lineups with strong control, durability, and elemental advantage.

Strategic Considerations: Orm is strongest when your team survives the full 8-second Devastating Link duration. If your Water team collapses too quickly, Orm cannot convert incoming damage into a meaningful counterattack. Build enough healing, protection, and control around him before expecting the skill to carry fights by itself.

Conclusion - Is Orm Worth Building?

Orm is absolutely worth building if you are investing in Water Titans. He adds a support mechanic that Water teams did not have before: damage absorption converted into a delayed strike. That makes him especially valuable in battles where enemies rely on heavy team-wide pressure.

For skins, prioritize the Primordial Skin first if you want consistency, because Health helps Orm survive and keep supporting the team. Then build the Default Skin for more Physical Attack. For artifacts, start with Zorval's Rite, then Chthonic Balance Seal, and finish with Elarite Crown for Light and Dark matchups.

My favorite setups are Hyperion, Mairi, Tydus, Orm, Nova for control tempo, and Hyperion, Tydus, Orm, Nova, Sigurd for a safer tank version. Both teams understand the same idea: protect the team, let Orm complete Devastating Link, and punish enemies for attacking into your Water formation.
