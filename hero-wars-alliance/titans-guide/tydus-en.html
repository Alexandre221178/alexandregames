Meet Tydus, the Water Summoner who brings balance and strength to the battlefield. More than just a damage dealer, he’s a healer and cleanser who can turn the tide of any Titan fight.

Whether you’re a veteran or new to the Water lineup, this guide will reveal how Tydus can unlock the true potential of your Titan army and lead your team to triumph.

Who Is Tydus?

Tydus is a Water Summoner in Hero Wars Alliance, known for his exceptional versatility and team support. He can deal impressive damage, restore health to allies, and cleanse negative effects, making him a vital member of any Water team.

  • Role: Summoner
  • Element: Water
  • Position: Middle Line

His synergy shines when fighting alongside other Water Titans especially when six are present on the battlefield. Together, they create a powerful wave of healing and destruction.

Tydus is not only the cornerstone of the Water element but also a reliable ally in every Titan War formation. With the right strategy, he can transform your team into an unstoppable force.

Tydus Pros and Cons - Hero Wars Alliance: Mobile

✅ Pros

  • Excellent team support thanks to his ultimate, Child of Water, which heals allies with the lowest Health.
  • Physical Attack scaling allows strong healing and synergy with Water Titans like Mairi and Sigurd.
  • Effective in mixed-element teams due to his healing not being restricted by Water-only allies.
  • Very useful in the Dungeon mode for keeping the team alive through sustained fights.
  • Can significantly extend the survivability of the frontline Titans.

❌ Cons

  • Low direct damage output — relies heavily on team synergy rather than solo strength.
  • Weaker against Earth Titans, which deal bonus damage and have extra defense versus Water Titans.
  • The Child of Water can’t attack, reducing offensive potential during long battles.
  • Vulnerable if the healing is interrupted or if the team is burst down too quickly.
  • Less impactful in PvP when facing burst compositions with strong area damage.

Tydus Skills Guide - Hero Wars Alliance

Learn how Tydus’s abilities work and why they’re vital in PvP battles. His Water-based powers heal, protect, and dominate enemy Titans with teamwork and timing.

Child of Water

This skill lets Tydus summon the Child of Water for 7 seconds. It doesn’t attack or move but constantly helps your team by stealing Health from the strongest enemy and transferring it to the weakest ally once every second. This means that during battle, Tydus acts as a stabilizer keeping his team alive while draining the opponent’s front lines. In PvP, this ability is extremely valuable against strong burst teams because it softens their main attackers while healing your own.

Formula: Steals (40% ATK) Health.

💭 Strategy Note: Tydus performs much better when paired with at least two other Water Titans. This setup already unlocks good PvP combinations, even in mixed-element teams. In both Dungeon and PvP, he synergizes perfectly with all Water Titans, providing great sustain and control through his skills. However, a full Water team can struggle defensively it tends to be weak against Earth compositions, which counter their healing advantage with strong burst and durability.

Deep Current

Deep Current is a passive skill that boosts the power of every allied Water Titan, making the team grow stronger as more of them stay alive. Each Water Titan adds new bonuses:

  • 2 Water Titans: Extra Damage against Fire Titans.
  • 3 Water Titans: Extra Defense against Fire Titans.
  • 4 Water Titans: Tydus gains +400 Energy when using skills.
  • 5 Water Titans: Using the first skill heals all Water Titans and cleanses all negative effects.
  • 6 Water Titans: Reduces all incoming damage by 50%.

This skill shines in PvP, especially in full Water teams. It rewards players who keep their Water Titans alive, creating a snowball effect each surviving Titan makes the team tankier, stronger, and harder to kill. The healing and cleanse on the fifth tier can completely turn around a fight against Fire compositions.

Formula: Extra Damage and Defense: 125674 (25% ATK)
Health restored when using the first skill: 30% of Max Health.

💧 Note: The summoned Child of Water is treated as a real Water Titan while active. This means that even if your team includes only 3 Water Titans and 2 Light Titans, Tydus can still trigger the 4th synergy bonus of his Deep Current skill (+400 Energy). This interaction makes him an excellent choice for PvP battles, allowing flexible team compositions that still benefit from Water synergy effects.

Best Skin for Titan Tydus Hero Wars Alliance

Tydus’s ultimate Child of Water summons a Water spirit that steals Health from the enemy with the most HP and transfers it to the weakest ally. The healing amount equals (40% of Physical Attack), making his only skin extremely valuable in all team compositions.

Default Skin

Physical Attack: +196,800

Evolution Priority: Very High – This is Tydus’s only skin and a must-have upgrade. His Child of Water ability heals allies based on (40% of Physical Attack), so increasing this stat greatly boosts both healing and overall combat impact.

Tydus Artifact Evolution Priority Hero Wars Alliance

Tydus’s healing scales with (40% of Physical Attack), making artifacts that boost this stat the most valuable. Survivability and elemental defense have secondary importance, being more situational in PvP and Dungeon battles.

Tydus Weapon Artifact – Siungur’s Mace

Physical Attack: +80,000

Evolution Priority for PvP: Very High – The best artifact to level first. It increases Physical Attack for all Titans for 9 seconds after activation, enhancing Tydus’s healing power and team synergy in every PvP setup.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Very High – Essential in Dungeon runs as the constant Physical Attack boost helps sustain healing output, ensuring the Water team survives longer during consecutive battles.

Tydus Crown Artifact – Water Crown

Extra Damage to Fire Titans: +255,000
Extra Defense from Fire Titans: +100,500

Evolution Priority for PvP: Medium – Useful only when facing Fire Titans, which limits its PvP impact. The elemental bonus is situational and does not improve Tydus’s healing directly.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Medium – Provides protection against Fire teams in the Dungeon, but overall value remains moderate since other elements appear more frequently in mixed battles.

Tydus Seal Artifact – Summon Seal

Physical Attack: +90,000
Health: +4,275,000

Evolution Priority for PvP: High – Offers a solid mix of Physical Attack for better healing and Health for survivability. Slightly less impactful than the Weapon in PvP but still valuable in longer fights.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: High – The Health boost helps Tydus endure deeper Dungeon waves, while extra Physical Attack supports sustained healing over multiple battles.

How to Counter Titan Tydus – Hero Wars Alliance

Earth Titans naturally counter Water Titans due to their elemental advantage. In Hero Wars Alliance, Angus, Eden, and Verdoc stand out as the most effective counters against Tydus, while Avalon and Sylva provide valuable support to strengthen Earth teams even further.

Their combined Physical Damage output, Extra Defense, and Bonus Damage against Water Titans make them capable of overcoming Tydus’s defensive skills and constant healing. When used together especially Verdoc, Avalon, and Sylva they form a powerful synergy that enhances both offense and survivability in battle.

Below you’ll find detailed explanations of how each Earth Titan performs against Tydus.

Angus

Why he defeats Tydus: Angus’s Anger of the Earth skill deals Physical Damage to all enemies for 8 seconds ((90% ATK)), completely bypassing Tydus’s healing and wearing down the entire Water frontline. Since Tydus cannot mitigate this consistent area damage effectively, Angus becomes one of the strongest anti-Tydus options in both Dungeon and PvP.

Eden

Why he defeats Tydus: Eden’s Burden of Creation launches a massive rock dealing Physical Damage ((350% ATK)) to all nearby enemies. This burst can instantly break through the Water Titan frontline and disable Tydus’s ability to sustain his team. Additionally, Eden’s crowd-control effect can interrupt Tydus’s combo rotation, preventing him from unleashing his full healing and damage potential.

Verdoc

Why he defeats Tydus: Verdoc’s passive Earth’s Call significantly boosts his entire Earth Titan team against Water Titans. The bonuses include Extra Damage and Extra Defense when fighting Water Titans ((25% ATK)), giving a massive edge in both offense and durability. This makes Verdoc essential for countering full Water teams that include Tydus, ensuring that Earth Titans take reduced incoming damage while dealing more per strike.

In summary, Angus provides the best consistent damage over time, Eden offers strong burst and control, and Verdoc grants team-wide buffs that amplify overall performance. Together, they create the ultimate counter-setup against Tydus and other Water Titans in both PvP and Dungeon battles.

Conclusion – Tydus Guide Hero Wars Alliance: Mobile

Tydus stands as one of the most reliable support Titans in Hero Wars Alliance: Mobile, providing consistent healing and sustain for his allies through his Child of Water ability. His performance scales directly with Physical Attack, making offensive investment highly rewarding for both PvP and Dungeon battles.

While he lacks burst damage and struggles against Earth Titans, his value as a healer and stabilizer makes him indispensable in Water teams and versatile enough for mixed-element formations. Players who focus on team balance and endurance will find Tydus a cornerstone of their Titan lineup.
