What if one Titan could take an enemy out of the fight while helping your team heal, hit harder, and move faster? Metida brings that combination to Hero Wars Alliance. This Elarite Super Titan fights from the back line, trapping the central enemy with Crystal Vortex and empowering her allies with Supreme Harmony.

What interests me most about Metida is how much her value changes with her teammates. In my maxed-team tests, Hyperion, Metida, Tydus, Orm, and Sigurd won all 10 battles against the tested Light lineup and all 10 against the tested Dark lineup. Let me show you how her stats and skills work so you can understand what she brings to your own Titan teams.

Metida belongs to the Elarite element. Her synergy with Earth, Water, Fire, and Elarite Titans comes from Supreme Harmony; it does not mean that she belongs to all four elements.

My first impression is that her combination of control and flexible support makes her especially interesting for mixed teams. The battle results are encouraging, but they describe specific matchups, not guaranteed wins against every formation.

Who Is Metida in Hero Wars Alliance?

Metida is an Elarite Super Titan who combines single-target control with team support. Her whirlwind stops the central enemy from acting, while her second skill grants bonuses based on the elements of surviving allied Titans.

  • Role: Super Titan
  • Element: Elarite
  • Position: Back Line
  • Synergy: Earth, Water, Fire, and Elarite Titans

Think of her as a way to give each teammate an additional purpose. Fire allies enable healing, Water allies add attack, Earth allies add speed, and Elarite allies enable damage boosts for priority attackers. Keeping those allies alive helps preserve her support potential.

Metida Max Stats - Hero Wars Alliance

These maximum build values provide the baseline for the skill calculations below:

  • Health: 15,283,000
  • Physical Attack: 717,470
  • Extra Defense from Light and Dark Titans: 120,600
  • Extra Damage to Light and Dark Titans: 213,000

Physical Attack strengthens Crystal Vortex and three Supreme Harmony effects: healing, the Physical Attack buff, and the damage boost. The Earth speed bonus instead depends on the number of surviving Earth Titans.

Metida Skills Guide - Hero Wars Alliance

Learn how Metida traps a central enemy and turns surviving allies into healing, attack, speed, and damage bonuses, with formulas for PVP battles.

Skill 1 - Crystal Vortex (Ultimate)

In-game description: Summons a whirlwind for 5.0 seconds that traps the central enemy. The whirlwind deals damage every 1 second and moves the enemy around the battlefield. While trapped, the enemy cannot move, attack, or use skills.

Skill Explanation: Metida catches the enemy in the center of the opposing formation. For five seconds, that Titan is carried around by the whirlwind and cannot act independently. The whirlwind deals damage once per second while your allies continue fighting.

Damage per tick at max stats: 717,470, before battle modifiers.

Formula: (100% × Metida's Physical Attack).

Max-stat calculation: (100% × 717,470) = 717,470 damage per tick.

Why it matters in PVP: A trapped opponent cannot contribute attacks or skills during the control window. That can give your team time to recover or pressure the remaining enemies. The target is the central enemy, so do not assume the whirlwind will select the opponent with the highest Attack.

Crystal Vortex also activates Zorval's Amulet. At artifact level 120 with its listed 100% activation chance, the whole team gains +150,000 Extra Damage to Light and Dark Titans for nine seconds.

Alexandre's Strategy Note: Watch which enemy occupies the center when Metida casts. The damage is useful, but preventing that Titan from using a key skill can be just as valuable.

Skill 2 - Supreme Harmony

In-game description: Empowers allies for 9.0 seconds. The bonuses depend on the number of surviving Titans of each element: each Fire Titan provides healing to all allies every second; each Water Titan increases all allies' Physical Attack; each Earth Titan increases all allies' speed by 10; each Elarite Titan increases the damage of one ally, prioritizing Titans with the highest Attack.

Skill Explanation: Think of each element as a different bonus. Count your surviving Titans of that element to understand what the team receives during the nine-second effect. You do not need all four elements to benefit: each available element contributes its own effect.

Fire Titans: healing every second

Each surviving Fire Titan adds 71,747 healing per second to every ally at Metida's listed maximum Attack. Two surviving Fire Titans provide 143,494 healing per second to each ally.

Formula: (10% × Metida's Physical Attack × surviving Fire Titans).

Max-stat calculation: (10% × 717,470) = 71,747 healing per ally per second for one Fire Titan.

Water Titans: more Physical Attack for everyone

Each surviving Water Titan adds 143,494 Physical Attack to every ally at the listed baseline. Two surviving Water Titans add 286,988 Physical Attack to each ally for the effect's duration.

Formula: (20% × Metida's Physical Attack × surviving Water Titans).

Max-stat calculation: (20% × 717,470) = 143,494 bonus Physical Attack per ally for one Water Titan.

Earth Titans: extra speed

Each surviving Earth Titan gives all allies +10 speed. Two surviving Earth Titans give +20 speed. This bonus does not scale with Metida's Physical Attack.

Formula: (10 × surviving Earth Titans).

Elarite Titans: damage boosts for priority attackers

Each surviving Elarite Titan enables a damage increase for one ally, prioritizing Titans with the highest Attack. At the listed baseline, the damage increase is 143,494 for a selected ally.

Formula: (20% × Metida's Physical Attack).

Max-stat calculation: (20% × 717,470) = 143,494 damage increase.

The percentage uses Metida's Physical Attack as its basis. It is not a flat 20% increase to the selected ally's total damage.

Why it matters in PVP: Team composition determines which benefits you receive. Fire brings recovery, Water boosts attack, Earth adds speed, and Elarite directs extra damage toward priority attackers. Losing an ally reduces the surviving elemental count available to this skill.

All examples use Metida's listed 717,470 Physical Attack as a fixed baseline. Actual battle values can differ with buffs and other combat effects.

Alexandre's Strategy Note: Start with what your team needs. A lineup without Fire Titans receives no healing from Supreme Harmony, even if it benefits strongly from Water and Elarite bonuses. Check which elements your surviving team supplies before judging how much support Metida is giving you.

Best Skin for Titan Metida - Hero Wars Alliance

Choose Metida's skin by balancing stronger skill effects with survival, and see how Primordial Skin+ changes Health without raising leveling costs.

Default Skin

Physical Attack: +196,800

Level: 60/60

This is my first general investment because Physical Attack improves Crystal Vortex and Supreme Harmony's healing, attack buff, and damage increase. It benefits Metida even when the enemy team contains no Light or Dark Titans.

At maximum skin level, its contribution to each healing tick per surviving Fire Titan is (196,800 × 10%) = 19,680. Its contribution to each Water attack bonus and Elarite damage boost is (196,800 × 20%) = 39,360.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Very High - First for general skill value: the same stat strengthens her own damage and several allied benefits.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Very High - First when she survives reliably. Stronger healing with Fire allies and stronger damage can help a mixed team sustain repeated fights.

Primordial Skin+

Health: +5,255,950

Level: 60/60, with Skin+ activated

The base Primordial Skin gives +2,627,975 Health at maximum level. Skin+ doubles that bonus at the same skin level: (2,627,975 × 2) = 5,255,950 Health. These are two versions of the same skin, not two separate bonuses to add together.

Evolution Priority for PVP: High - Second by default. Move Health ahead when Metida dies before her control and support can influence the battle.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: High - A useful survival investment where Metida is available to your lineup. Prioritize it sooner if keeping her alive is the limiting factor.

How Skin+ works: Metida is the first Titan to receive Skin+ in Hero Wars Alliance. Activating it doubles the base skin's stats immediately at its current level. The Titan Skin Stone cost for leveling remains the same as the base skin, whether you activate Skin+ before or after upgrading. This refers to leveling costs; it does not mean the Skin+ unlock itself is free.

Metida skin order: Default Skin first for stronger skills, then Primordial Skin+ for durability. Reverse that practical priority if survival is the problem. The bonuses above are components of a maximum build, not extra stats to add again to the max-stat totals.

Dungeon priorities: These ratings follow her skill mechanics, not a recorded Dungeon test. Check room eligibility and your team's survival before investing specifically for Dungeon use.

Metida Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Plan Metida's artifact upgrades around universal skill scaling, then weigh her team damage buff and personal defenses against the enemies you face.

Zorval's Amulet (Weapon Artifact)

Level: 120

Extra Damage to Light and Dark Titans: +150,000 for the whole team

Activation chance: 100%

Buff duration: 9 seconds

When Metida uses Crystal Vortex, the weapon artifact grants this team bonus. The buff improves damage against Light and Dark targets; it does not increase general Physical Attack or the healing formula of Supreme Harmony.

Evolution Priority for PVP: High - Second for a build aimed at Light and Dark opponents: the benefit reaches the whole team. Lower its priority when your regular opponents use other elements.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Low - Its bonus requires Light or Dark targets. In rooms without those elements, it does not help damage or recovery.

Elarite Crown (Crown Artifact)

Level: 120

Extra Damage to Light and Dark Titans: +213,000

Extra Defense from Light and Dark Titans: +120,600

The Crown improves Metida's personal offense and defense against Light and Dark Titans. Across the six element groups, its listed bonuses target those two; they do not provide a general defense bonus against Earth, Water, Fire, or Elarite.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Medium - Third for a general build. Raise it above the Weapon when Light or Dark damage is killing Metida before she can support her team.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Low - For rooms without Light or Dark enemies, the listed bonuses do not apply. If you face those enemies, its defensive benefit can make it more useful than the Weapon for survival.

Chthonic Balance Seal (Seal Artifact)

Level: 120

Physical Attack: +97,500

Health: +3,975,000

The Seal is my first artifact choice. Health helps Metida survive, while Physical Attack improves Crystal Vortex and three Supreme Harmony effects regardless of the opposing element.

Its contribution to healing per tick per Fire Titan is (97,500 × 10%) = 9,750. Its contribution to each Water attack bonus and Elarite damage boost is (97,500 × 20%) = 19,500.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Very High - First: universal survival and skill scaling are useful against every enemy element.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Very High - First where you use Metida: Health and Physical Attack help across eligible matchups instead of relying on a specific enemy element.

Artifact priority: Chthonic Balance Seal first, Zorval's Amulet second for Light and Dark matchups, then Elarite Crown. If personal survival against those elements is the issue, move the Crown ahead of the Weapon. For Dungeon rooms without Light or Dark targets, focus on the Seal.

How to Counter Titan Metida - Hero Wars Alliance

The recorded win against an opposing Metida used Hyperion, Metida, Araji, Orm, and Angus. It won 10 out of 10 against Brustar, Araji, Mort, Umbra, and Metida with both teams maxed. This supports that complete lineup for this matchup; it does not establish a solo counter or a universal answer to every Metida team.

My approach is to pressure her surviving allies while keeping your own support alive. Each lost elemental ally reduces the support available from Supreme Harmony. Do not assume Earth Titans automatically counter Metida: she is Elarite, and the tested full Earth team lost to a different Metida composition.

Key members of the winning lineup

The following contributors are listed alphabetically. Their roles explain the lineup's approach; the test does not isolate which skill decided the result.

Angus

Angus supplies a durable front line and area damage that can pressure multiple enemies while Crystal Vortex controls only one. His Earth element also enables Supreme Harmony's speed bonus on your side. His anti-Water bonuses are not a direct elemental advantage against Metida.

Araji

Araji brings sustained beam damage against the front line and allied speed support. His Fire element enables Metida's healing bonus, helping this lineup combine pressure with recovery. Treat him as part of the tested team, not as a guaranteed answer to an opposing Metida.

Metida

Your own Metida contributes central-target control and elemental support. In the tested winning lineup, she can work with Fire, Water, Earth, and Elarite allies, giving Supreme Harmony access to all four bonus types.

Orm

Devastating Link accumulates a portion of allied damage taken and later deals it to a central enemy. That adds a way to return pressure during an extended fight. Orm also belongs to Water and Elarite, contributing to both corresponding Supreme Harmony bonuses.

⭐ Metida - Alexandre's Final Verdict

What I like most about Metida is that she makes team composition matter in a very visible way. Crystal Vortex creates a five-second control window, while Supreme Harmony rewards the allies you keep alive. Her value comes from how those two skills work with the rest of your formation.

My starting upgrade order is Default Skin before Primordial Skin+, with the Chthonic Balance Seal first among artifacts. Physical Attack strengthens several effects at once. If Metida dies too early, move Health up the list; against Light and Dark opponents, develop the Weapon and Crown according to whether your team needs more damage or Metida needs more protection.

The four 10-0 results make her a promising addition to the tested teams. My advice is to choose the composition that matches your available Titans and the opponent you need to beat, then test it at your own investment level. The mixed team with Angus is particularly interesting because it brings every Supreme Harmony bonus into the same lineup.
