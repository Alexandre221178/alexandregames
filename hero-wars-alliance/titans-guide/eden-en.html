Who Is Eden in Hero Wars Alliance?

Eden is the Earth Super Titan — a devastating Marksman and Control unit who combines massive area damage with terrifying crowd control. His most famous ability, Subterranean Prison, pulls a random enemy underground for 6 full seconds and then stuns them for 2 more. That's 8 seconds where one of your opponent's Titans is completely removed from the fight. Think of him as a force of nature — unstoppable, earth-shattering, and impossible to ignore.

  • Role: Super Titan / Marksman / Control
  • Element: Earth
  • Position: Front Line
  • Synergy: Earth Titans (Angus, Avalon, Verdoc, Sylva)

Eden is the cornerstone of the Full Terra composition, currently one of the strongest Titan teams in the meta. His destructive rock-hurling skill clears groups of enemies, while Subterranean Prison's unpredictable targeting keeps opponents guessing and unable to plan their defense properly. With Verdoc amplifying the entire Earth team's damage, Eden becomes an almost unstoppable force in both attack and defense.

Eden Skills Guide - Hero Wars Alliance

Understanding Eden's skills is the key to using him effectively and to countering him when he's on the enemy team. Let me break down each ability in full detail.

Burden of Creation (Area Damage Skill)

In-game description: Raises a humongous chunk of rock from the ground and hurls it at the center of the enemy team. All enemies next to the impact receive damage.

Skill Explanation: Burden of Creation is Eden's primary offensive skill. Here's exactly what happens when he activates it:

The Attack: Eden raises a humongous chunk of rock from the ground and hurls it directly at the center of the enemy team. The impact creates a massive shockwave, and all enemies near the impact zone take damage. This is pure, devastating area damage that can hit multiple Titans simultaneously.

Damage: 2,709,002

Formula: (350% of Physical Attack) - calculated as (3.50 Ã— 774,001 = 2,709,003).

2,709,002 Physical damage to all enemies near the center

To put that into perspective: most Titans have between 5 and 11 million Health. A single Burden of Creation hit can wipe out 25% to 50% of a Titan's health bar in one blow - and it hits several enemies at once. If your entire enemy team is clumped together, every single Titan takes this enormous hit simultaneously.

Why does this matter in team fights? Many Titan compositions rely on keeping their team alive long enough to use their skills. Eden's Burden of Creation applies extreme early pressure on the entire enemy lineup, reducing everyone's Health before their abilities even activate. This synergizes perfectly with Verdoc's buffs, which amplify the damage even further.

Subterranean Prison (Control Skill)

In-game description: Binds a random enemy with roots and pulls the victim under the ground for 6.0 seconds. Once the enemy is released, they stay stunned for 2 more seconds. If the target is the last enemy on the battlefield, they won't get pulled underground, but the stun effect will still affect them.

Skill Explanation: This is Eden's most powerful and feared ability. Let me explain it step by step so you understand exactly what it does:

Step 1 - Target Selection: Eden activates Subterranean Prison and selects a random enemy Titan. This is crucial - the targeting is completely random, which makes the outcome of battles involving Eden inherently unpredictable. You can never be sure which enemy will be taken out of the fight.

Step 2 - The Binding: The chosen enemy is bound with roots and then pulled underground. While underground, that Titan is completely removed from the battle - they cannot attack, use skills, or be targeted by either team.

Step 3 - Underground Duration: The buried Titan stays underground for 6.0 full seconds. During this time, your team has a 5 vs 4 numerical advantage. This is enormous and can completely swing the battle in your favor.

Step 4 - Release and Stun: When the enemy emerges from underground after 6 seconds, they are immediately hit with an additional 2-second stun. So the total time that Titan is incapacitated is 8 seconds - 6 underground plus 2 stunned.

Special Case: If the target is the last surviving enemy on the battlefield, they won't be pulled underground (since there's no point). However, the 2-second stun effect still applies to them.

Cooldown: Initial cooldown: 8 seconds - then every 19 seconds thereafter.

Random enemy pulled underground for 6s + 2s stun on release = 8 seconds of total incapacitation

Why is random targeting so important? It makes Eden incredibly hard to play against mentally. Your opponent can never plan around which Titan will be removed. If Eden buries the enemy's main damage dealer early in the fight, the whole enemy team collapses. Even if he buries a less critical target, your team still has a 5v4 advantage for 8 seconds. The randomness creates constant pressure and uncertainty for the opponent - which is exactly what you want.

Alexandre's Strategy Note: Eden's Subterranean Prison buries a random enemy for 6 seconds plus 2 more seconds of stun. This randomness is both his greatest strength and his one unpredictable element. In the current meta, the Full Terra team is built to take advantage of this: while one enemy is buried, Verdoc's buffs amplify the damage your other Earth Titans deal to the remaining four enemies. The opponent literally cannot defend against this combination - they never know who will be buried, and when one Titan disappears underground, the math just doesn't work in their favor anymore.

Eden Artifact Evolution Priority - Hero Wars Alliance

Eden's artifacts follow the standard Earth Titan pattern. His Weapon (Andvari's Soul) is unique to Eden, while the Earth Crown (Book) and Defense Seal (Ring) are shared with other Earth class Titans. Upgrading these artifacts is essential to reaching Eden's full potential - let me walk you through each one and tell you exactly which to prioritize.

Andvari's Soul (Weapon Artifact)

Activation Chance: 100%

Effect: Extra Defense from Water Titans +150,000

Andvari's Soul is Eden's unique Weapon artifact. With a guaranteed 100% activation chance, it always triggers and provides Eden and the whole team with +150,000 Extra Defense from Water Titans. Since Earth naturally counters Water in the elemental matchup system, this artifact reinforces that advantage by making Eden and his allies even harder for Water Titans to damage.

Think of it this way: when your Earth team goes up against a Water team (which is a very favorable matchup to begin with), Andvari's Soul adds another layer of protection for Eden and his allies specifically. He becomes even more durable in these fights, surviving longer to use more Subterranean Prison casts and Burden of Creation hits.

Evolution Priority for PVP: Very High The guaranteed 100% activation with a substantial defense bonus makes this a reliable first priority. Upgrade this first as it directly improves Eden's survivability in the most common Earth vs Water matchup.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: High In dungeon encounters involving Water Titans, this artifact significantly increases Eden's longevity and overall performance.

Earth Crown (Book Artifact)

Extra Damage to Water Titans: +255,000

Extra Defense from Water Titans: +100,500

The Earth Crown is the standard Crown artifact for all Earth class Titans. It provides both offensive and defensive bonuses specifically against Water Titans - +255,000 extra damage and +100,500 extra defense. This is enormous in Water matchups: your Eden hits harder AND takes less damage simultaneously.

In practice, the Earth Crown effectively means that when Eden fights against Water Titans, every single hit deals significantly more damage while Eden absorbs significantly less. Combined with Andvari's Soul, Eden becomes practically impenetrable to Water Titans at max level.

Keep in mind that this Crown only benefits Water matchups. Against Fire, Earth, Light, or Dark Titans, it provides zero benefit. That's why it ranks second - the Defense Seal (below) provides universal value across all matchups.

Evolution Priority for Dungeon: Medium â€“ Valuable when facing Water sections of the dungeon, but the Defense Seal provides more consistent universal value across all dungeon encounters.

The Defense Seal is Eden's Ring artifact and one of the most powerful universal artifacts in the game. It provides a combination of both offense (Physical Attack +82,500) and survivability (Health +4,575,000) that works against every single opponent, regardless of element.

This artifact works against Fire teams, Water teams, Light teams, Dark teams, and even mirror Earth matchups. There is no situation where it doesn't help. That universal value makes it arguably the most important artifact for overall performance.

How to Counter Titan Eden - Hero Wars Alliance

Eden is incredibly powerful, but he has clear elemental weaknesses. Here's how to beat him effectively:

Araji - The Hard Counter

Araji is the most effective counter to Eden and the entire Full Terra composition. As a Fire Titan, Araji has elemental advantage over all Earth Titans. His Incinerating Ray deals sustained damage at 1,422 ATK per second for 7 seconds, and it locks onto the nearest enemy - making it devastating against Eden's large hitbox. More importantly, Araji's Herald of Flame speeds up all allies by 30% for 8 seconds, giving the entire Fire team a massive activation speed boost that allows them to use their skills before the Earth team can respond. The combination of elemental advantage, sustained damage, and the speed buff makes Araji the single best counter to Full Terra compositions.

Full Fire Team with Alecto

A full Fire Titan team including Alecto (Ignis, Asherona, Araji, Alecto, Moloch) completely dominates Full Terra compositions. Alecto, the Elarite/Fire hybrid, deals massive burst damage to the highest-Attack enemy while her Zorval's Wing artifact buffs the entire Fire team with every ultimate. Fire's elemental advantage over Earth, combined with Alecto's individual burst power, makes this the most dangerous composition for Eden's team to face.

Eden in the Dungeon - Hero Wars Alliance

Eden is S-tier in the dungeon - both as an individual Titan and as part of the Full Terra team. Here's everything you need to know about using him effectively in dungeon content:

Eden's Dungeon Strengths

Overwhelming Area Damage: Eden's strength in the dungeon is undeniable. His ability to deal massive area damage with Burden of Creation and control the battlefield with Subterranean Prison can turn the tide in any encounter. In every fight, Eden ensures that your team not only survives but thrives, maximizing your chances of success and progression through harder floors.

Marksman + Tank Synergy with Angus: While Eden excels as an offensive Super Titan Marksman, it's important to pair him with a dedicated tank. Elemental Advantage in the Dungeon: Eden's elemental strength against Water Titans aligns perfectly with many dungeon encounters where Earth Titans reign supreme.
