Before you invest in a healer, ask yourself one question: do you need the strongest healer for your team, or the one that gives your account the most value? The answer changes with your roster, your resources and the opponents you face.

In this guide, I rank my top seven healers in Hero Wars Alliance based on team synergy, Legendary Relics and how difficult they are to build. Guus takes first place with the right team, while Byrna is my safer starting recommendation for free-to-play players.

This article follows the ranking and observations in the video below. References to the meta, available Relics and Soul Stone sources reflect the video rather than a promise about future updates.

7. Maya: An Accessible Healer for Beginners

Maya is no longer my top choice for competitive teams. She was impressive when her Relics arrived, but the meta described in the video has moved on. That puts her seventh in this ranking.

She still offers useful value during early progression, especially for free-to-play accounts. Her Soul Stones are available in the Tower Shop, and you can work on her Relics without spending money. Her combination of healing and damage can help while you develop your first roster.

My recommendation: consider Maya if you are a beginner who needs an accessible healer. I would not make her a priority specifically for top endgame teams. See the Maya guide for more about her kit.

6. Martha: Hydra Utility and Back-Line Protection

Martha earns sixth place because of the practical jobs she can perform. She helps in Hydra battles, and her back-line position can be useful when an opponent targets the rear of your team.

One example from the video is an enemy Jhu with Relics repeatedly attacking your back line. Martha can help hold him off even without Relics of her own. That utility is why I place her above Maya.

Her main drawback is accessibility. The video lists her Soul Stones in the VIP Shop at VIP level 14; free-to-play players mostly rely on events or the Heroic Chest. Build her when her Hydra role or back-line durability solves a specific problem for your account. For related lineups, see the Hydra teams guide.

5. Morrigan: Eternity Support and Resurrection Control

Morrigan brings more than healing. She is tough, works especially well with Eternity allies, and can improve your team's physical and magic defense. These defensive benefits can keep allies alive when healing alone is not enough.

Her resurrection utility is another reason to consider her. As discussed in the video, she can revive an ally and stop enemies from coming back to life. Preventing an enemy resurrection can decide a close fight.

Her Soul Stones come from the Hydra Shop. She had no Relics at the time of the video, so my recommendation rests on the support she already provides. Invest if her Eternity synergy and resurrection control fit your team; do not base that decision only on a possible future update.

4. Aidan: Emergency Protection with Flame Shield

Aidan is already strong without Legendary Relics in the video's comparison. His purple skill, Flame Shield, is a major reason: it gives an ally a chance to survive a dangerous burst of damage.

  • Trigger: an ally who started the battle with Aidan drops below 30% Health.
  • Duration: the shield lasts four seconds.
  • Protection: it blocks physical and magic damage.
  • Scaling: shield strength depends on your team's total maximum Health.
  • Cooldown: it can trigger once every 15 seconds per ally.

His Soul Stones mostly come from events and the Heroic Chest, which can make him harder to develop. Future Legendary Relics could change his position, but their actual effects would need to be assessed first. His existing protection is the reason he ranks fourth here.

For more lineup ideas, explore the Kayla and Aidan teams guide.

3. Dorian: Vampirism for Sustained Damage Dealers

Dorian shows how much Relics can change a hero's value. His vampirism helps allies recover Health by dealing damage, making him an appealing partner for heroes who can keep attacking.

I especially like pairing Dorian with Iris. That sustain can make her much harder to kill while she continues dealing damage. The video also highlights a Chaos lineup I have been using successfully in Wars and Arena:

Cleaver + Byrna + Kendle + Dorian + Aidan.

The investment cost deserves attention. The video lists Dorian's Soul Stones at 750,000 Gold in the Town Shop, a substantial expense for a beginner. I value Dorian highly, but I would not neglect other important upgrades just to rush him. Read the Dorian guide before committing resources.

2. Byrna: My Safer Free-to-Play Starting Point

Byrna is one of my first recommendations for free-to-play players. Her Soul Stones come from Realm, and daily tasks make her easier to develop steadily.

Her appeal goes beyond accessibility. She increases maximum Health, removes negative effects and provides extra Energy. She can fit physical or magic teams, giving her flexibility across different rosters. That combination of useful support and manageable progression is why she ranks second.

Watch out for Satori. Byrna's bonus Energy can trigger his marks. A benefit that normally helps your team can become a disadvantage in that matchup, so check the enemy lineup before choosing her.

If you want a practical healer to start building without depending mainly on events or chest drops, Byrna is my safer starting point. See the Byrna guide for a closer look at her abilities.

1. Guus: My Top Healer for the Right Team

Guus was already good before Relics. With the Relic effects discussed in the video, he takes first place in my ranking, particularly when paired with a team that benefits from critical hits.

Beak Peak: Critical Healing at Relic Level 10

At Relic level 10, Beak Peak allows Guus's skill healing to critically heal for 200% of the normal amount. The chance depends on his Critical Hit Chance: increasing that stat creates more opportunities for stronger healing. For example, a normal heal of 100,000 becomes 200,000 when it critically heals.

Honkageddon and Golden Feathers

Honkageddon turns Guus's critical hits into healing for nearby allies and grants Golden Feathers. These boost allied critical damage, with additional benefits for Honor heroes.

That is why I prefer Guus in physical teams that use critical hits. You can use him with mages, but those teams will not benefit from every part of his kit. His first-place position is most useful when your allies can take advantage of that synergy.

What Holds Guus Back?

His Soul Stones are difficult to obtain, mostly coming from events and the Heroic Chest. Even if he is your preferred healer, that availability can limit how quickly you build him.

In the video, I do not identify a strong direct counter to him. My usual approach is to defeat his allies first, because Guus becomes much easier to finish when he is alone. Treat this as an observation from the video's matchups, rather than a guarantee for every fight.

Which Healer Should You Build First?

If you can build Guus and support his critical-hit synergy, he is my number one. For free-to-play players looking for a more accessible starting investment, Byrna is the safer recommendation.

Dorian is worth considering when vampirism supports your damage dealers, provided you can afford his Gold cost. Aidan offers emergency shields, Morrigan brings Eternity and resurrection utility, Martha helps with Hydra and back-line pressure, and Maya remains an accessible early progression option.

Choose the healer who solves your team's actual problem. Team synergy, Soul Stone access and the resources needed to develop the hero matter alongside their place in a ranking.
