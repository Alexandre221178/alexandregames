In the Realm mode of Hero Wars Alliance, resources for upgrading hero skills are limited. Leveling the wrong skill means losing strategic value, especially as the mode shifts from PvE into real PvP competition.

This guide analyzes all 21 heroes with Realm skills and ranks them by tier, separated by class, using objective technical criteria.

How the Realm Class System Works

Before looking at the tier list, you need to understand the core rule that defines everything: every hero automatically grants +120% attack and +120% defense to troops of their own class. This bonus exists regardless of Realm skills. It is fixed and guaranteed just by placing that hero in the lineup.

That means Realm skills should be judged by what they add beyond that 120%. A hero whose Realm skill only increases their own class attack adds limited extra value, because that troop type already receives the class bonus. A hero whose skill benefits all troops delivers something truly additional.

Golden Rule of This Tier List: A skill that buffs all troops is more valuable than a skill that only buffs its own class, because the class bonus (+120% attack/defense) is already granted automatically to every hero.

Evaluation Criteria

The heroes were evaluated using the following criteria, in priority order:

  1. Buff scope — a skill that affects all troops is worth more than a skill tied to only one class
  2. Effect reliability — passive guaranteed buffs are worth more than chance-based effects
  3. Damage magnitude — high multipliers (150%, 75%) carry more weight in PvP
  4. Defensive impact — enemy debuffs and shields improve troop survival
  5. Redundancy — heroes whose Realm skills duplicate the automatic class bonus are penalized

Tier List - Infantry

🛡
INFANTRY
Front line - absorbs damage and protects Lancers and Marksmen

Infantry is the wall of your army. It stands in front, absorbs enemy attacks, and protects your Lancers and Marksmen while they deal damage. If your Infantry collapses too fast, the enemy reaches your DPS immediately and the battle ends. The best Infantry heroes in PvP are the ones that add extra survival or unexpected offensive pressure beyond the class bonus.

S Tier S — Kayla
Kayla Infantry
Demon's Blood

Increases Infantry critical hit chance by +16%.

Oath to Aidan

Every 2 Infantry attacks, the next one deals +20% damage to enemy Lancers and +10% to enemy Marksmen. Guaranteed, with no RNG.

Why Tier S: Kayla turns Infantry from a passive tank line into a real offensive threat. The +20% bonus against enemy Lancers triggers every two attacks, so it is frequent, guaranteed, and aimed at one of the most dangerous enemy troop types. Combined with Aidan, the named synergy in Oath to Aidan suggests future game mechanics may strengthen this pair even further. In PvP, a frontline that hits hard while staying alive is difficult to counter.

S Tier S — Electra
Electra Infantry
Necrotic Seal

Increases the health of all troops - Infantry, Lancers, and Marksmen all start the battle with more HP.

Choir of Souls

Reduces damage taken by Infantry and increases damage dealt by Marksmen at the same time. Both effects are passive.

Why Tier S: Electra is the best defensive support option in the mode. Her first skill buffs the health of all troops, which is a real upgrade beyond the class bonus. Her second skill delivers two effects at once: less damage on Infantry, so your frontline survives longer, and more damage on Marksmen, a different allied class. That kind of dual passive impact is rare and extremely valuable in PvP where every turn matters.

A Tier A — Julius
Julius Infantry
Cosmic Armor

Increases the defense of all troops by +11% - a passive permanent effect from turn 1.

Star Wanderer's Shield

All attacks have a 30% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 8% damage for 2 turns.

Why Tier A: Cosmic Armor is one of the best passive buffs in the mode because it gives defense to all troops with no condition attached. The 30% shield chance is the highest shield proc rate available, and the 2-turn duration increases the real value of each activation. Julius stays in Tier A instead of S because the 8% shield value is still relatively low, and the defense buff, while global, does not create the same offensive impact as Kayla or Electra.

B Tier B — Astaroth
Astaroth Infantry
Wings of the Fallen

Increases Infantry attack by +15%.

Heavenly Grace

All attacks have an 18% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 5% damage for 3 turns.

Why Tier B: Wings of the Fallen is partially redundant because Infantry already gets +120% attack from the class bonus. The extra +15% is not worthless, but it is not a major differentiator either. Heavenly Grace has decent duration at 3 turns, but its shield value is low at 5% and the proc chance is only moderate. Astaroth is functional, but not a priority resource investment.

B Tier B — Leonel
Leonel Infantry
Double Blade

Increases Infantry critical hit chance by +13%.

Predator's Shadow

Every 2 Infantry attacks, the next one deals +15% damage to enemy Lancers and +15% to enemy Marksmen.

Why Tier B and not S: Leonel follows the same pattern as Kayla, with crit plus directional damage every 2 attacks. The problem is that his numbers are lower: +13% instead of +16% crit chance, and +15% instead of Kayla's stronger directional damage profile. Kayla is strictly better. Leonel is a workable alternative if Kayla is unavailable.

C Tier C — Galahad
Galahad Infantry
Iron Whirlwind

Increases Infantry attack by +15%.

Knight of Harun

Infantry attacks have a 13% chance to stun the enemy for 1 turn.

Why Tier C: Iron Whirlwind is almost entirely redundant with the class bonus. A 13% 1-turn stun can matter when it triggers, but it depends too heavily on luck to be trusted in high-level PvP. Not a priority investment.

Alexandre's note: In my video about the best Realm heroes, I ranked Galahad as the third-best Infantry option. I still stand by that recommendation for beginners because Galahad's stun is easy to notice in battle and produces visible results quickly. But after going deeper into the technical analysis for this guide, I concluded that Leonel delivers better numbers: slightly stronger crit support and the same directional damage pattern every 2 attacks. For long-term resource investment, Leonel is the more efficient choice. Galahad is still a solid option, especially if you already have him developed.

D Tier D — Yasmine
Yasmine Infantry
Poisoned Blade

Infantry attacks have a 20% chance to poison the enemy, dealing 12% damage for 3 turns.

Dance of Shadows

Gives Lancers a 14% chance to evade enemy attacks.

Why Tier D: Yasmine has two serious issues. Her 12% poison over 3 turns is the weakest damage-over-time multiplier in the mode, and it still depends on a 20% chance. The second problem is structural: Dance of Shadows buffs Lancers, not Infantry, which is Yasmine's own class. That creates a strategic mismatch where her skill supports a troop type she does not reinforce with the class bonus. Not recommended for investment.

Tier List - Lancers

⚔️
LANCERS
Main DPS - the highest damage potential on the field

Lancers are the offensive engine of your Realm army. Protected by Infantry in front, they are responsible for the highest raw damage output. The Lancer class includes the strongest damage multipliers in the mode and some of the best global support heroes, such as Folio and Xe'sha.

S Tier S — Dante
Dante Lancer
Blood Price

Increases Lancer critical hit chance by +25% - the highest single-troop crit bonus available in the Realm.

Soul Collector

Every 3 turns, Lancers deal +150% additional damage and reduce the target's defense by -25% for 1 turn. Guaranteed, with no RNG.

Why Tier S: Soul Collector is the largest guaranteed burst effect in the mode. +150% bonus damage every 3 turns, with no RNG, stacked with a passive +25% crit bonus, creates devastating damage spikes in PvP. The target's -25% defense reduction also amplifies the damage of every other allied troop attacking in the same turn. Its only limitation is scope, since it only affects Lancers, but the magnitude more than makes up for that. In a team with Folio, the Folio curse plus Dante burst combo is the strongest offensive pattern currently available.

S Tier S — Folio
Folio Lancer
Clumsy Blast

Reduces damage dealt by enemy Lancers by -12% from the start of battle - passive and permanent.

Ancient Runes

All attacks from all troops have a 50% chance to apply Curse - cursed targets take +13% damage for 1 turn.

Why Tier S: Ancient Runes has the highest proc rate in the game: 50% on every attack made by your entire army. Statistically, one out of every two attacks makes a target vulnerable. In full-army multi-turn battles, Curse will be active almost every round on some enemy troop. That amplifies the damage of Dante, Kayla, Octavia, and everyone else at the same time. The passive -12% debuff on enemy Lancers is an extra layer of value that works with no chance requirement.

A Tier A — Morrigan
Morrigan Lancer
Father's Shadow

Increases the health of all troops by +10% - a global passive effect.

Vengeful Whisper

Every 3 turns, Lancers deal +75% additional damage and reduce the target's defense by -30% for 1 turn. Guaranteed.

Why Tier A: Morrigan offers the best mix of defensive support and burst among Lancers after Dante. The global +10% health avoids class-bonus redundancy, and the -30% defense reduction from Vengeful Whisper is the largest in the mode, stronger than Dante's -25%. Her +75% burst is lower than Dante's +150%, which keeps her in Tier A. In teams without Dante, Morrigan is the best Lancer option.

A Tier A — Xe'sha
Xe'sha Lancer
Abyssal Shackles

Increases the attack of all troops by +14% - passive, with no condition.

Pulse of Sorrow

Increases the critical hit chance of all troops by +13% - passive, with no condition.

Why Tier A: Xe'sha is the only hero who combines a global attack buff with a global crit buff, both passive and always on. No RNG involved. That means every troop in your army enters battle stronger from turn 1 regardless of composition. She is not Tier S because the values themselves, +14% attack and +13% crit chance, are still moderate compared with the burst multipliers offered by Dante and Folio.

A Tier A — Iris
Iris Lancer
Fangs of Bitey

Reduces damage dealt by enemy Marksmen by -20% - passive and permanent from turn 1.

Demonic Tome

Marksman attacks have a 20% chance to apply Curse - cursed targets take +18% damage for 2 turns.

Why Tier A: Iris is the only hero with a direct passive debuff aimed at the enemy army. Reducing enemy Marksman attack by 20% is guaranteed value regardless of what the opponent does. In PvP, where you do not control the enemy lineup, a skill that automatically weakens the opponent's backline has strong strategic value. Her +18% Curse for 2 turns is also one of the strongest and longest curse effects in the mode.

B Tier B — Somna
Somna Lancer
Hunter's Instinct

Reduces damage taken by Marksmen by -20% and increases damage dealt by Lancers by +10%.

She-Wolf's Aim

Lancer attacks have a 13% chance to stun the enemy for 1 turn.

Why Tier B: Somna is a cross-support hero. She protects Marksmen while buffing Lancers, which gives her value in teams with fragile backline damage dealers. The 13% stun is inconsistent in PvP, and the +10% Lancer damage is partially redundant with the class bonus. Good in specific situations, but not a priority.

B Tier B — Jhu
Jhu Lancer
Pain Denied

Increases Lancer defense by +13%.

Wrath of Zarakkar

Every 2 attacks by allied Marksmen, the next one deals +40% damage to enemy Lancers and +20% to enemy Marksmen.

Why Tier B: Jhu has an interesting cross-support skill that empowers allied Marksmen to hit enemy Lancers harder. That makes him useful as a specific counter pick. However, Pain Denied is partially redundant because Lancers already get defense from the class bonus, and his strategic value depends on the enemy actually bringing Lancers.

C Tier C — Judge
Judge Lancer
Echo of Judgment

Lancer attacks have a 10% chance to apply Shock, dealing 16% damage per turn for 2 turns.

Blade of Justice

Increases Lancer attack by +15% and defense by +10%.

Why Tier C: Blade of Justice is almost completely redundant with the class bonus, since the automatic +120% already covers Lancer attack and defense. The 10% Shock chance is one of the weakest proc rates among status effects, making it far too inconsistent for PvP. Not recommended for investment.

Tier List - Marksmen

🏹
MARKSMEN
Ranged DPS - attacks from the backline protected by Infantry

Marksmen attack from the back of the battlefield, protected by Infantry. They are the army's secondary damage source and, in advanced compositions, can deal as much damage as Lancers with the right heroes. The Marksman class contains the highest number of heroes with global all-troop buffs.

S Tier S — Octavia
Octavia Marksman
Empress's Will

Increases the attack of all troops by +15% and the defense of all troops by +10%. Both effects are passive and permanent.

Shards of the Empire

Gives all troops an 11% chance to evade any enemy attack.

Why Tier S: Octavia is the best universal buffer in the mode. Empress's Will gives both attack and defense to the entire army at once, with no RNG and no setup, from the first second of battle. No other passive skill combines those two global stats in a single package. The 11% global evade chance can literally save a troop on a deciding late turn in PvP. Octavia fits into any composition and raises the ceiling of the whole team.

A Tier A — Ginger
Ginger Marksman
Duchess's Command

All attacks have a 20% chance to mark the target - marked enemies take +25% damage for 1 turn.

Flame of Strongford

All attacks have a 10% chance to burn the target - dealing 24% damage per turn for 2 turns.

Why Tier A: Ginger has the highest mark proc rate in the mode at 20%, and it affects all troops. Her burn damage, 24% per turn for 2 turns, totals 48%, making it one of the strongest DoT effects available. When mark and burn stack on the same target, the combined damage becomes very threatening. The limitation is that the mark only lasts 1 turn, which creates a short exploitation window in PvP, and both effects still depend on RNG.

A Tier A — Aidan
Aidan Marksman
Heart of the Phoenix

Increases the health of all troops by +12% - passive and permanent.

Heat of Eternity

All attacks have a 13% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 15% damage for 1 turn.

Why Tier A: Aidan is the best global survival hero among Marksmen. His +12% health for all troops is the largest global health bonus available, and his 15% shield has the highest raw absorption value in the mode. His synergy with Kayla through Oath to Aidan adds future strategic value as well. He stays in Tier A because he does not create direct offensive pressure. He improves resilience, not damage.

B Tier B — Polaris
Polaris Marksman
Chains of the North Wind

Marksman attacks have a 10% chance to mark the target - marked enemies take +25% damage for 2 turns.

Eternal Permafrost

All attacks have a 15% chance to apply Frostbite - dealing 16% damage per turn for 2 turns.

Why Tier B: Polaris has interesting effects. Her mark lasts 2 turns, which is better than Ginger's, and Frostbite affects all troops. The problem is that both skills have low proc rates, 10% and 15%. In short PvP fights, you can go through an entire battle without seeing them matter enough. Too inconsistent for priority investment.

C Tier C — Fafnir
Fafnir Marksman
Master's Hand

Increases Marksman attack by +10% and defense by +5%.

Anvil of War

Reduces damage taken by Marksmen by -15% and increases Lancer damage by +10%.

Why Tier C: Fafnir has limited scope. His skills only affect Marksmen and Lancers, never the entire army. The +10% Marksman attack buff is redundant with the class bonus. The -15% damage reduction on Marksmen has some situational value, but it is still weaker than the kind of global protection Electra provides. Not a priority.

C Tier C — Thea
Thea Marksman
Ingrid's Blessing

Increases the health of all troops by +11%.

Breath of the Grove

All attacks have a 13% chance to grant a shield that absorbs 15% damage for 1 turn.

Why Tier C: Thea is functionally almost identical to Aidan, with the same shield chance, the same absorption value, and the same duration. The only difference is +11% health instead of Aidan's +12%. She loses on every number and has no Kayla synergy. If you already have Aidan, Thea adds almost nothing. If you do not have Aidan, she is an acceptable fallback, but not an ideal one.

Recommended PvP Team

Based on this tier list, the most balanced and efficient PvP team in the Realm combines Tier S heroes with at least one representative from each class to guarantee Infantry as tank, Lancers as core DPS, and Marksmen as ranged DPS.

Main Team - Maximum PvP Synergy

Infantry Kayla
Marksman Aidan
Lancer Dante
Lancer Folio
Marksman Octavia

Why this team works: Kayla and Aidan form the direct Oath to Aidan synergy across Infantry and Marksmen. Dante brings +150% burst damage for Lancers every 3 turns. Folio amplifies the entire army with a 50% Curse chance on all attacks, including Dante's burst turns. Octavia passively raises everyone's attack and defense. This lineup hits the hardest, survives longer, and has the highest overall PvP ceiling.

Investment Priority Order

With limited resources, this is the recommended order for upgrading Realm skills when immediate PvP impact matters most:

  • Dante - the largest guaranteed burst in the mode. Immediate and consistent payoff in any Lancer battle.
  • Octavia - universal passive buff. Raises your whole army from turn 1 regardless of the enemy composition.
  • Folio - amplifies the damage of the entire team. Strengthens Dante, Kayla, and Octavia at the same time.
  • Kayla - turns Infantry into an offensive threat. Essential for the Aidan combo.
  • Aidan - global survival plus Kayla synergy. Best upgraded after Kayla is already developed.
  • Electra - the best defensive Infantry support. Prioritize her if your frontline is collapsing too fast.
  • Xe'sha / Morrigan / Iris - strong situational Tier A choices. Invest as your team composition evolves.

Warning: Do not invest resources into Tier C or D heroes such as Galahad, Judge, Fafnir, Thea, or Yasmine. Their skills are either redundant with the automatic class bonus or too dependent on low proc rates to be reliable in PvP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Shouldn't Dante be better than Folio because he has +150% damage?
Dante's +150% burst is huge, but it only affects Lancers. Folio buffs the entire army with a 50% Curse chance on every attack, which amplifies Dante, Kayla, and Octavia at the same time. Together they are stronger than either one alone. In teams where you can only pick one, Dante gets priority because his direct impact is larger.
Can I use Jhu to counter enemy Lancers?
Yes. Jhu's Wrath of Zarakkar makes your allied Marksmen deal +40% damage to enemy Lancers every 2 attacks. If you know the opponent relies heavily on Lancers, Jhu becomes an effective situational counter. He is not a top general investment, but he can be worth it in specific metagames.
Why is Thea Tier C if her skills are almost the same as Aidan's?
That is exactly why. Thea is worse than Aidan in every number, with +11% health instead of +12%, and she has no Kayla synergy through Oath to Aidan. If you already have Aidan, Thea adds almost nothing. If you do not have Aidan, she is a functional substitute, but not an ideal one.
Do I need one hero from each class on my team?
It is not mandatory, but it is strongly recommended. Each class occupies a different battlefield position - Infantry in front, Lancers in the middle, Marksmen in the back. Without Infantry, the enemy reaches your DPS too fast. Without Marksmen, you lose ranged pressure. The most balanced team usually has at least one hero from each class to guarantee full field coverage.
