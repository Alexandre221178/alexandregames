Why Wodryn and Mushy work together

Wodryn helps protect allies behind him. He can remove harmful effects, provide critical hit chance, and help the team take less damage. Mushy adds another body to the front line and becomes a damage threat when she has enough time and healing.

Healing helps her mushrooms become active and also increases the damage of one of her skills. This is why I like pairing her with healers: the team needs to stay alive long enough for the mushrooms to join the fight and turn it around.

1. Yasmine: my first Wodryn and Mushy team

  • Guus

  • Yasmine
  • Mushy
  • Wodryn

Yasmine provides physical damage, while Mushy adds magic damage and more pressure as the battle develops. Yasmine does not need to do all the work alone. Her critical hit chance also helps her benefit from Wodryn's damage reduction. Guus and Byrna supply healing, which matters for Mushy's mushrooms too.

This lineup was very strong against Cleaver, Byrna, Kendle, Dorian, and Aidan. It also gave me good results against Julius, Byrna, Crow, Guus, and Peech. These defenses had different investments, so they were not equal tests. What stood out was that Mushy could help finish fights even when Yasmine did not survive.

I would keep this team ready for attacks against those tested defenses. It is also the first defense team from this group I would try, but the attack wins do not establish how well it will hold on defense.

2. Eva: use the damage dealer you already have

  • Eva
  • Guus
  • Byrna
  • Mushy
  • Wodryn

I would choose Eva when she is already one of my main heroes. This version had excellent runs against both defenses listed above, including before I unlocked Wodryn's Relic. It also had weaker runs as I changed his build.

That makes it a team I would use for a planned attack after checking the opponent. The idea is still to survive, bring Mushy's mushrooms into the fight, and give the damage dealers time to work.

If Eva is already strong and Yasmine is weak, I would try this team before spending resources on Yasmine. A useful lineup with heroes I own can be a better investment for my account than starting a new damage dealer.

3. Miu and Crow: an attack option that frees Byrna

  • Miu
  • Crow
  • Guus
  • Mushy
  • Wodryn

I like this lineup as another attack choice against the Cleaver and Kendle defense. Miu also worked well with Mushy in my separate tests: with Miu, Blindness gives allies more magic critical hit chance, helping Mushy deal more damage while contributing at the front.

This team also leaves Byrna available for another Grand Arena lineup. I would still pick the Yasmine version first for my account, but I would try this option if Miu and Crow were already strong.

4. Luther and Tristan: Mushy against Chaos

  • Guus
  • Byrna
  • Tristan
  • Mushy
  • Luther

This lineup uses Mushy without Wodryn. In my Arena tests, it beat Chaos teams with golden Relics and more than 150,000 extra power. It also worked against a Chaos team with Electra. Those are reported results, not a promise against every Chaos lineup.

Luther jumps into the enemy team while Mushy helps hold our front line. I would keep this option ready for Arena or a planned Guild War attack when I see a similar defense, and check the exact enemy lineup before choosing it.

5. Kayla and Aidan: Wodryn without Mushy

  • Aidan
  • Peech
  • Guus
  • Kayla
  • Wodryn

If Kayla and Aidan are already in your main team, this is another Wodryn lineup worth testing before building Mushy from scratch. Against the Cleaver and Kendle team, it won all ten fights in that test.

The result applies to that test setup. Use it as a reason to try the lineup with your existing heroes, rather than assuming it will repeat against every opponent or investment level.

6. Crow and Tristan: another option for both modes

Another team I like is Guus, Crow, Tristan, Mushy, and Wodryn. In my experience, it works very well on both attack and defense.

The video does not give a fight count or a named opponent for this version. I would treat it as another option to test with an already developed Crow and Tristan, while keeping the Yasmine lineup as my first choice for building Wodryn and Mushy together.

How to read these PvP results

The results here come from the battles described in my video. The enemy defenses had different investments, and Wodryn's build changed between some runs. The video does not provide complete builds, exact Relic levels for every lineup, or a common test setup for all six teams.

My first choice to try on defense is the Yasmine version. Eva is primarily my alternative for planned attacks. The Crow and Tristan team has a positive attack and defense assessment from my experience, with fewer test details in this video. Keep those distinctions in mind when choosing what to build.

What I would build first

  1. With Wodryn and Mushy together: start with the Yasmine team if those heroes are already developed.
  2. With a stronger Eva: try her version before investing in Yasmine.
  3. Against similar Chaos defenses: keep the Luther and Tristan team with Mushy as an attack option.
  4. With Kayla and Aidan already built: test their Wodryn team before starting Mushy from scratch.
  5. For free-to-play and low-spending accounts: build one team well before starting another.
