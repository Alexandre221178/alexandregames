Special Reward Events in Hero Wars: Alliance

🌌 Abyss Splendor

Explore the Outland and the Tower, and collect Titanite to get skin stone chests, rune spheres, and other rewards. Realm players will also gather food and train Marksmen for a variety of useful resources and speedups.

Highlights:


Skin Stone Chests


Rune Spheres


Army training and Realm resources

📿 Shards of the Past

Power up your Heroes with rune stones and prove your newfound strength against Hydras and in the Arena to get relic shards, artifact and talisman resources, and other rewards. Realm players will also defeat regular monsters and train army units for a variety of useful resources and speedups.

During this event, you'll also get to unlock Talismans with Talisman Coins obtained in the event or Reward Boxes in the sidebar on the right. Also check out the offers on Talismans in that same sidebar. Only available to level 120 players. No new Talismans are coming this time.

Highlights:


Relic Shards


Talisman Coins


Talisman resources and offers

🌪️ Rune Storm

Power up your Heroes with skin stones, send Expeditions, and spend energy to get red gear recipe fragments, rune stones, rune spheres, and other rewards. Realm players will also defeat bosses and train Infantry for a variety of useful resources and speedups.

Highlights:


Rune Stones


Rune Spheres


Energy spending rewards and Expedition progress

✨ Path to Perfection:

Complete different sets of quests to gain coins that you can spend on a specific Hero’s evolution levels and various resources and items. This time we’re featuring Miu.

🔰 Guild Clash

Compete with 5 other Guilds in this weekly event to earn special trophies that can be exchanged for valuable resources.

Guild Clash will, as per usual, run every week of the next Season too. The rotation of banned Factions is changing once again:

  • 🔹 Guilds in Stone & Bronze will not be able to pick Honor heroes;
  • 🔹 Guilds in Silver & Gold won’t have heroes from Honor and Nature;
  • 🔹 Guilds in Diamond and above will be left without Honor, Nature, and Mystery heroes.

Highlights:


Special trophies and exchange currency


Relic Shards for victory and exchange


Valuable guild shop resources

🆚 Guild Versus

Compete with other Guilds from Monday to Saturday, complete tasks that make your team stronger, and earn relics and other rewards.

Highlights:


Relics and relic-related rewards


Extra guild progression rewards
