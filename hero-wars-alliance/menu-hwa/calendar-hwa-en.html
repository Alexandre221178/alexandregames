Summer Glory Season Summary Events in Hero Wars: Alliance

🌌Trade Routes Event Group

The Trade Routes event group is dedicated to Talismans. You can purchase them in the Caravan Hall with Event Coins or obtain them from Reward Boxes. In addition to Talismans, the Event Shop will also feature Byrna's Relic Shards and plenty of other valuable resources.

⚡️ Tip: Take advantage of the discounts during the event to complete quests even more efficiently!

The event group includes three events:

➡️ Trade Routes Event 1: Roads Unlocked

Rewards: Large amounts of Event Coins, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Titan Soul Stones, and many other valuable resources.

Complete quests by logging in, spending Energy, Skin Stones, Summoning Spheres, and Titan Potions, earning VIP Points and Titanite, and defeating Bosses as the Rally Leader.

➡️ Trade Routes Event 2: Grand Caravan

Rewards: Talismans, Skin, Talisman Coins, Heroes Soul Stones, Titan Soul Stones, (red item fragments), Rune Spheres, and many more valuable rewards.

Complete quests by logging in, spending Emeralds, sending Expeditions, battling in the Arena, earning VIP Points, and opening Tower, Artifact, and Outland Chests.

➡️ Trade Routes Event 3: Bountiful Roads

Rewards: Talisman Coins, Mastery Crystals, Rune Stones, Soul Stones, Titan Soul Stones, and many other valuable resources.

Complete quests by logging in, rerolling Talismans, spending Mastery Crystals, defeating the Hydra, fighting in Arena battles with a Talisman equipped, and completing quests from the two previous events.

🪙Balance of Power Event Group

The Balance of Power event group focuses on upgrading Honor Heroes while earning a large amount of valuable resources for your entire roster. Throughout the event, you'll collect Event Coins that can be spent in the Event Shop on exclusive rewards.

The Event Shop features Heroes Soul Stones, Artifact Fragments, Relic Shards, along with many other essential upgrade materials.

Tip: Try to save your resources before the event begins. Completing quests across all three events allows you to earn more Event Coins and maximize the value of the Event Shop.

➡️ Balance of Power Event: Defiant Edge

Rewards: Event Coins, Titan Soul Stones, Artifact resources, Recurve Bow (red item fragments), and many other valuable rewards.

Complete quests by logging in, spending Energy, Summoning Spheres, and Skin Stones, fighting in the Arena, earning VIP Points, and defeating Bosses as the Rally Leader.

➡️ Balance of Power Event: Unbroken Bond

Rewards: Titan Soul Stones, Heroes Soul Stones, (red item fragments), Artifact resources, and many more valuable items.

Complete quests by logging in, earning VIP Points, spending Emeralds, Rune Stones, and Elemental Spheres, opening Tower Chests, earning Titanite, and collecting Hero Soul Stones.

➡️ Balance of Power Event: Gear & Glory

Rewards: Heroes Soul Stones, Artifacts, his unique red item, Skin+, Skin, Talisman, and many additional rewards.

Complete quests by upgrading Heroes's Level, Rank, Gift of the Elements, Glyphs, Artifact Level, and Artifact Star Rank, while also completing quests from the Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond events.

🕳️Titan Rift Event

Rewards: Titan Soul Stones, Heroes Soul Stones, Titan Potions, Metacubes, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, and (red item fragments), plus many other valuable resources.

👑Legacy of Power Event

🪽Ascendant Glory Event Group (July 23–29)

The Ascendant Glory event group is centered around Guus, offering one of the best opportunities to strengthen this hero and collect valuable upgrade materials. During the event, you'll earn Event Coins to spend in the Event Shop on exclusive rewards.

The Event Shop includes Heroes's Skin, Heroes's Artifact, and Heroes Soul Stones, their Artifact Fragments, and Relic Shards.

⚡ Special Discounts: You'll also be able to take advantage of 4× Emerald Offers and the Outland Chest Discount, making it a great time to stock up on resources and complete event quests.

The event group consists of the following three events:

➡️ 🪽Ascendant Glory Event: Rising Legend

Rewards: Heroes Soul Stones, Orm Soul Stones, Titans Soul Stones, Skin Stone Chests, Skin Stones, Titan Potions, (red item fragments), plus many other valuable resources.

Complete quests by logging in, spending Chaos Cores and Fairy Dust, using the Summoning Circle, opening Outland Chests, fighting in the Arena, earning VIP Points, and defeating Bosses as the Rally Leader.

➡️ 🪽Ascendant Glory Event: Trial of Legends

Rewards: Heroes Soul Stones, Titans Soul Stones, Titan Skin Stones, Mastery Crystals, (red item fragments), and many more rewards.

Complete quests by logging in, earning VIP Points, spending Emeralds, Titan Potions, and Rune Stones, earning Activity Points, starting Expeditions, and opening the Heroic Chest.

➡️ 🪽Ascendant Glory Event: Spark of Glory

Rewards: Heroes Soul Stones, Heroes Artifact Fragments, the Skins, Heroes's Talisman, his unique red item scroll, Large Skin Stone Chests, and many additional rewards to make Heroes significantly stronger.

Complete quests by upgrading Heroes's Level, Rank, Glyphs, Gift of the Elements, Artifact Level, and Artifact Star Rank, while also completing quests from the Rising Legend and Trial of Legends events.

🟡 Summoning Sphere Discount

The Summoning Sphere Discount is one of the best opportunities to strengthen your Titan team. During this event, you can open more Summoning Spheres while completing event quests and collecting valuable rewards.

Every Summoning Sphere gives you a chance to obtain Titan Soul Stones, helping you evolve your Titans, increase their power, and improve your performance in the Dungeon, Guild War, and other game modes. If you've been saving Summoning Spheres, this is the perfect time to use them.

Tip: The stronger your Titans become, the deeper you can progress in the Dungeon and the more daily rewards you'll earn. To get the most out of your Titans, follow our Hero Wars Alliance Dungeon Guide, where you'll learn the best strategies for building a powerful Titan team and clearing more Dungeon floors every day.

📦 Artifact Chest Discount

The Artifact Chest Discount is the best time to open Artifact Chests. During this event, you can obtain more Artifact resources while spending fewer Emeralds, making it one of the most valuable discounts for improving your heroes.

Opening Artifact Chests rewards you with Artifact Weapons, Artifact Books, and Artifact Rings, all of which provide significant stat bonuses and increase your heroes' overall power.

You'll also earn Artifact Coins, which can be exchanged for the specific Artifact fragments you need, giving you greater control over your heroes' progression instead of relying entirely on luck.

Another valuable reward is the Chaos Core, one of the rarest Artifact resources in Hero Wars Alliance. Chaos Cores are required to increase the Star Level of your Artifacts, making them essential for maximizing your heroes' potential.

Tip: Save your Emeralds for the Artifact Chest Discount whenever possible. You'll complete event quests while collecting powerful Artifact resources that are difficult to obtain outside of special events.

🟣 Elemental Sphere Discount

The Elemental Sphere Discount is one of the best opportunities to open Elemental Spheres. During this event, you can collect valuable Titan resources while spending fewer Emeralds, making it an excellent time to strengthen your Titan team.

Opening Elemental Spheres rewards you with Titan Artifacts for all elements, allowing you to increase your Titans' combat power. You'll also obtain Fairy Dust, an essential resource used to level up Titan Artifacts, along with Hydra Coins, which can be spent in the Hydra Shop on valuable items.

The grand prize is the Golden Horn, one of the most valuable rewards in Hero Wars Alliance. When used, it grants one additional Hydra attack to every member of your guild, making it extremely useful for defeating stronger Hydras and earning even better daily rewards.

Tip: If your guild is actively fighting the Hydra, saving your Emeralds for the Elemental Sphere Discount is highly recommended. The extra Titan Artifact resources and the chance to obtain a Golden Horn make this one of the most rewarding discounts in the game.

Want to deal more damage to the Hydra? Check out our Hero Wars Alliance Hydra Best Teams Guide to build stronger teams and maximize your daily rewards.

🎁 Heroic Chest Discount

The Heroic Chest Discount is the best time to spend Emeralds on Heroic Chests. During this event, you can open more chests for less, complete event quests, and work toward some of the rarest rewards in Hero Wars Alliance.

Every Heroic Chest contains valuable resources to strengthen your heroes, including Hero Soul Stones, evolution materials, and other upgrade items. In addition, every 25th Heroic Chest guarantees a new Hero if there are still heroes available for you to unlock from the Heroic Chest pool.

The biggest attraction is the chance to obtain a Grand Prize. A Grand Prize is guaranteed at least once every 400 Heroic Chest openings. Your first Grand Prize will always be Cleaver, while future Grand Prizes can include some of the most valuable rewards in the game.

One of the most sought-after Grand Prizes is a Skin+. These upgraded skins provide even greater stat bonuses than regular skins, making them one of the best long-term investments for increasing a hero's power. Keep in mind that you must already own the corresponding hero before you can receive its Skin+ as a Grand Prize.

Tip: Save your Emeralds for the Heroic Chest Discount whenever possible. Besides progressing toward the guaranteed Grand Prize, you'll also complete event quests while collecting valuable heroes and upgrade resources.
