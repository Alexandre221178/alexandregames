What Is Damage over Time (DoT)?

Let’s start from the beginning.

In Hero Wars, there are different types of damage. Some attacks deal damage instantly. You press a skill, and the damage happens right away.

But there is another type of damage that happens slowly over time. This is what we call Damage over Time, or simply DoT.

Examples of DoT in the game include:

  • Poison
  • Burn (fire damage)
  • Toxin effects

Instead of dealing one big hit, these effects deal small amounts of damage repeatedly, for example every 0.5 seconds.

If this is your first game, think of it like this:

Instead of losing 1000 health all at once, you lose small pieces of that damage over a few seconds.

This is easier to understand and also easier to see during the fight.

What Changed in the New DoT System?

This is the most important part of the article.

Before the update, DoT effects were not consistent. Sometimes they could miss. Sometimes they interacted with other mechanics like dodge or reflection.

Now, everything is different.

The new DoT system has three very important rules:

  • It always hits
  • It cannot be dodged
  • It cannot be reflected

Let’s simplify this.

If a DoT effect is applied to a hero:

  • It will deal damage, no matter what
  • The enemy cannot avoid it
  • The enemy cannot send the damage back

This makes the game more predictable and easier to understand — especially for new players.

Which Heroes Are Affected by the New DoT?

The developers confirmed which heroes now deal DoT under the new system. Here is the full official list:

  • Kai — pure damage from the third skill
  • Maya — damage from the second, third, and fourth skills
  • Qing Mao — burn damage from the first skill
  • Yasmine — poison from the third skill
  • Rufus — damage from the second skill
  • Arachne — damage from the fourth skill
  • Iris — burn damage from the fourth skill
  • Tempus — damage from the third skill
  • Polaris — damage from the first skill
  • Heidi — toxin damage
  • Alvanor — damage from the third skill enhanced by a Relic

Beyond hero skills, the new DoT rules also apply to:

  • Adventure effect that deals a percentage of Heroes' maximum Health as pure damage
  • Pure damage from the Outland Boss Ilyssa the Weaver
  • Burning effect from the Fire Head of the Hydra

This is a long list. If you use any of these heroes, the update directly affects how your team performs in battle.

Why This Change Is So Important

You might be thinking: “Okay, but why does this matter?”

It matters because many heroes in Hero Wars depend on mechanics like dodge and reflection to survive.

Before the update:

  • A hero could avoid damage using dodge
  • A hero could reflect damage back to the attacker

Now, against DoT:

  • Dodge does not work
  • Reflection does not work

This means that DoT became much stronger — and heroes that rely on it became more powerful.

Understanding Iris: Why She Became Stronger

Now let’s talk about one of the most important heroes affected by this change: Iris.

Iris uses a type of damage that includes burn effects. These are DoT effects.

Before the update, her damage had a weakness.

If the enemy could dodge or reflect, Iris could lose the fight very quickly.

After the update, this weakness is mostly gone.

Why?

Because her damage now follows the new DoT rules:

  • It always hits
  • It cannot be dodged
  • It cannot be reflected

This makes her much more consistent and much more dangerous.

What Happened Between Iris and Octavia?

This is the part that many players are asking about.

Before the update, Iris often lost to Octavia.

Let’s understand why.

Octavia has a skill that creates a mirror. This mirror works in a very specific way:

  • When an ally dodges an attack
  • The damage is reflected back to the attacker

So in simple terms:

If you attack and the enemy dodges, your own damage can come back to you.

This was very strong against heroes like Iris.

Why This No Longer Works

Now let’s connect everything.

Octavia’s skill depends on one key condition:

The attack must be dodged.

But with the new DoT system:

  • DoT cannot be dodged

So what happens now?

  • No dodge → no reflection
  • No reflection → no damage returned to Iris

This completely changes the interaction.

Now Iris can apply her damage safely, without triggering Octavia’s mirror.

This is why many players feel like Iris suddenly became “too strong”.

A Simple Example for Beginners

If you are new to games, let me explain with a simple example.

Imagine this situation before the update:

  • Iris attacks
  • The enemy dodges
  • The mirror reflects the damage
  • Iris takes damage and loses

Now after the update:

  • Iris applies DoT
  • The enemy cannot dodge
  • The mirror does not activate
  • Iris keeps dealing damage safely

This is the core reason behind the change.

What This Means for Your Team

This update affects everyone, not just Iris.

If your team relies on:

  • Dodge
  • Magic reflection

You may notice that your team is weaker now in some situations.

On the other hand, heroes that use DoT are now more reliable and consistent.

This doesn’t mean your team is “bad”. It just means the game changed, and you may need to adapt.

Minor Changes to Tempus' Clock

Along with the DoT update, the developers also made two small but important adjustments to Tempus and how his clock works.

First change: unavailable heroes.

Before, the effects from Tempus' clock — such as increasing maximum Health and applying slow or speed-up — could affect heroes who were unavailable during the battle (for example, heroes that had not yet entered the fight).

Now, those effects will not apply while a hero is unavailable. However, once the hero becomes available again, the effects will apply automatically — as long as the hero is still inside the clock's area.

Second change: effect removal.

Before, if a hero's buff from the clock was removed (by a dispel effect, for example), it was gone permanently during that fight.

Now, even if the effects are removed, they will be reapplied as long as the hero stays inside the clock's area.

In short: Tempus' clock became more reliable for heroes that stay inside its range.

How to Counter Iris After the DoT Buff

Iris is clearly stronger after the update. But she is not unbeatable. Here are the heroes that can still challenge or shut her down.

Phobos (Reworked)

The new Phobos is very powerful. He targets the enemy with the highest damage output, paralyzes them, and drains their energy. This means Iris can be stopped before she even activates her burn skills.

The catch: Phobos may not be enough on his own if Iris has a strong support team around her. His effectiveness depends a lot on the full team composition on both sides.

Amira

Amira protects allies against magic damage, which helps reduce the impact of Iris' burn effects. At maximum level, she already offers solid resistance — but she may still struggle against a fully built Iris.

The key detail here: Amira has not yet received a Relic. Once she does, her magic protection should scale even higher, potentially making her a reliable long-term answer to Iris.

Corvus (Altar Mechanic)

Corvus' Altar is one of the most interesting counters to Iris right now. Here is how it works:

  • Every time an enemy deals damage to your allies, the Altar deals damage back to that enemy
  • This directly punishes Iris for applying her burn effects
  • The damage from the Altar scales with Corvus' maximum Health

A well-built Corvus can eliminate Iris quickly if she keeps targeting his team.

Here is the exciting part: Corvus has not received a Relic yet. His Relic is expected to increase maximum Health, which means the Altar damage will grow significantly. When that happens, Corvus could become one of the best answers to any DoT hero, not just Iris.

Lian and Somna

Both Lian and Somna can put enemies to sleep. Sleep fully neutralizes a hero for a period of time — they stop acting, stop using skills, and stop dealing damage.

If Iris is put to sleep before she applies her burn, she becomes harmless for that window. This gives your team time to deal with her supports or set up a stronger counterattack.

Sleep-based control is a reliable option because it does not depend on dodge or reflection — two mechanics that no longer work against DoT.

Final Thoughts

I know this can feel like a lot, especially if this is your first game.

But the good news is that the new system is actually simpler once you understand it.

Just remember these key points:

  • DoT always hits
  • DoT cannot be dodged
  • DoT cannot be reflected

And because of that:

  • Iris became stronger
  • Octavia no longer counters her the same way

If your battles feel different, now you know why.

Take your time, test your heroes, and don’t be afraid to learn step by step.

You are improving every day just by trying to understand the game better.
