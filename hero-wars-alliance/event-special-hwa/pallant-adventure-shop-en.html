The Shop Looks Tempting—but What Is Actually Worth It?

Hey everyone, Alexandre here! The Elarite Heat Season has opened an Adventure Shop built around Pallant, Cleaver and Peech, and it is exactly the kind of shop that can make a good account decision feel strangely difficult. There are rare Titan resources, hero equipment, skins, gambling coins and progression materials all competing for currencies that take time—or boss victories—to earn.

So let me save you from the purchase I would hate to see you regret. My rule for this shop is simple: secure the account-changing rewards first, then buy the rare resources, and leave random rewards for last. Once you look at the shop through that lens, the priority becomes much clearer.

If you do not own Pallant yet, this season can change your Dungeon account. He is not merely another Titan to collect; he can become the missing piece that keeps your tanks healthy and your run alive. That is why my first recommendation is also my strongest one.

Understand the Three Resources Before You Spend

Adventure Coins

Adventure Coins are the regular shop currency. You earn them through the season's activities and missions, so this is where most players will have the freedom to choose between Pallant resources, Fairy Spark, Skin Stones, equipment and Coin of Luck.

Valor Coins

Valor Coins are much harder to replace because you receive one for each victory over the final Adventure boss. Reaching that boss requires Adventure Energy, and your energy comes back over time, from event missions or through purchases. Every Valor Coin represents a successful boss cycle, so I would never spend one casually.

⚡ Adventure Energy

Adventure Energy is both your ticket to progress through the Adventure and a currency for a separate set of progression offers. This creates a real tradeoff: spending energy in the shop may slow your next boss victory and therefore your next Valor Coin. Buy the strongest energy offer first, but do not empty your energy without considering how close you are to another final-boss clear.

Why Pallant Soul Stones Are My First Priority

If you still do not have Pallant, buy his Soul Stones before anything else. Pallant is the Elarite and Earth tank, excellent in PvP and extremely valuable in the Dungeon. His weapon artifact heals the entire team, and that single mechanic makes him feel less like a normal tank and more like the engine of a sustainable run.

In Earth-versus-Water rooms, Pallant's healing can make the Earth lineup dramatically safer. I also use him in mixed rooms as a replacement for Angus. My lineup is Tydus, Hyperion, Moloch, Pallant and Sigurd. The idea is not complicated, but it is powerful: Pallant, Hyperion and Sigurd create a cycle of healing that repeatedly brings the tanks back toward full Health. Instead of watching each room slowly drain your account, you enter the next fight ready to keep going.

My point of view: Dungeon progress is not only about winning the room in front of you. It is about how much Health you still have for the next ten rooms. Pallant solves that second problem, which is why I consider him indispensable for players who want longer, more stable runs.

Adventure Coin Purchases: What I Would Buy

1. Pallant's Artifacts: Weapon, Crown, Then Seal

After unlocking Pallant, my order is weapon artifact first, crown second and seal third. The shop offers each Pallant artifact purchase at a limit of 1/1, awarding 15 fragments for 50 Adventure Coins. The weapon deserves the first investment because its team healing is the reason Pallant changes Dungeon consistency so much.

Pallant ArtifactRewardCostPriority
Weapon15 fragments50 Adventure CoinsFirst
Crown15 fragments50 Adventure CoinsSecond
Seal15 fragments50 Adventure CoinsThird

2. Fairy Spark: The Purchase I Would Not Overthink

The shop offers Fairy Spark 30/30 for 1,500 Adventure Coins each. This item is important and difficult to obtain outside events or paid offers. It grants +870 Magical Crit Hit Chance and +1,358 Crushing.

What makes Fairy Spark exciting is the list of heroes who use it: Iris, Polaris, Kendle, Satori, Orion and Xe'Sha. That is not a list of forgotten bench heroes; those are characters many competitive accounts actively build. If one or more of them are part of your plans, buy Fairy Spark without fear. It is the kind of resource players usually wish they had collected when an event offered it.

3. Skin Stones, Then Equipment

Once Pallant's essential resources, Fairy Spark and your other rare targets are secured, I would move to Skin Stones. They are flexible, permanent progression and never depend on luck. Regular equipment comes last because it is useful but more replaceable through normal gameplay.

Cleaver's Weapon Artifact Fragments

The shop also offers Cleaver's weapon artifact at a 5/5 purchase limit. Each purchase gives 2 fragments for 300 Adventure Coins. This is not a bad reward, but it is not rare enough to outrank Pallant or Fairy Spark because Cleaver's artifact fragments can be obtained through Valkyrie's Favor more easily. I would only move this upward if Cleaver is a central project on your account and you need the fragments immediately.

Valor Coin Purchases: Every Boss Victory Must Count

After the initial Adventure Coin artifact offers, the shop presents additional Pallant artifact offers with a higher reward value at a cost of 1 Valor Coin per purchase. These can accelerate Pallant, but remember what the currency represents: one final-boss defeat. I would prioritize the weapon fragments again, then the crown and seal, based on your weakest Pallant artifact.

Universal Relic Shards — Limit 20/20

The Relic Shard offer has a 20/20 limit, provides 3 shards per purchase and costs 1 Valor Coin. Relics are difficult long-term progression, so this is one of the strongest flexible purchases once your essential Pallant artifact goals are covered. Unlike a random chest, you know exactly what kind of progress you are buying.

Ripper Gauntlet for Cleaver — Limit 20/20

The Ripper Gauntlet recipe costs 1 Valor Coin and is available 20/20. It grants Cleaver:

Ripper Gauntlet StatBonus
Health+22,320
Toughness+930

If you are seriously investing in Cleaver, you should think carefully about this gauntlet because event equipment can become the annoying missing piece later. If Cleaver is not in your active teams, however, I would not let a specialized item consume Valor Coins that could strengthen Pallant or buy flexible Relic Shards.

Why I Would Skip the Skins and Coin of Luck

Morrigan and Guus Skins for 10,000 Emeralds

You can buy Morrigan and Guus skins for 10,000 Emeralds each. Personally, I think that is unnecessary. I would rather use those Emeralds during a discounted Outland Chest event. You gain Outland rewards, Skin Stones and a chance to obtain skins or Skin Shop Certificates at the same time. If the exact skin does not drop, a certificate can still let you unlock it later.

Spending 20,000 Emeralds on the two direct offers removes all that extra value. With discounted Outland openings, you have a realistic chance to collect certificates while progressing several skin resources at once. The direct offer gives certainty, but in my opinion the price is too high for something that is not permanently missable.

Coin of Luck: A Fun Button, Not a Smart Priority

The shop offers Coin of Luck 5/5 for 2,500 Adventure Coins each, plus a rather funny 200/200 Valor Coin limit. Two hundred purchases? I would like to meet the player clearing enough final bosses to make that limit feel restrictive!

Jokes aside, I do not recommend Coin of Luck. The reward is completely random. You might receive a skin you never wanted—or something with low account value, such as Titan Soul Stones that are easy to obtain from Summoning Spheres. Event currency should buy progress you can control. Gambling it away is exciting for a moment, but disappointment lasts much longer when you later discover you are missing Fairy Spark or Pallant fragments.

  • Buy it only if all meaningful priorities are already complete.
  • Do not treat the possibility of a skin as guaranteed value.
  • Never sacrifice Pallant, Fairy Spark or Relic Shard progress for random rewards.

Adventure Energy Shop: Rune Spheres Come First

Rune Spheres — Maximum Priority

The Rune Sphere offer is available 3/3, gives 1,000 Rune Spheres and costs 37,000 Adventure Energy per purchase. I consider this the maximum-priority offer in the Adventure Energy section because Rune Spheres become a major bottleneck in advanced glyph progression.

There is one important catch: Adventure Energy also moves you toward the final boss and more Valor Coins. Check your event route before buying. If spending 37,000 Energy prevents a boss clear you could otherwise reach, compare the value of that lost Valor Coin with your immediate need for Rune Spheres.

Secondary Energy Priorities

After Rune Spheres, I rank Rune Stones, Meta Cubes and Mastery Crystals as secondary purchases. They are all important progression resources, but they do not beat the Rune Sphere offer for accounts facing the high-level glyph bottleneck.

My Final Shopping Plan

If I were shopping on an account without Pallant, I would not hesitate: Pallant Soul Stones first, then his weapon, crown and seal. That sequence unlocks the Titan and builds the healing engine that makes him so valuable in the Dungeon. Next I would secure Fairy Spark and the best Relic Shard opportunities before moving to Skin Stones and ordinary equipment.

For Valor Coins, I would ask one question before every purchase: “Was this reward worth defeating the final boss for?” Pallant's key artifacts, flexible Relic Shards and—on a dedicated Cleaver account—the Ripper Gauntlet can pass that test. Coin of Luck usually does not.

And that is the real lesson of this Adventure Shop: do not let variety create confusion. Build the account you actually play. Pallant can extend your Dungeon runs, Fairy Spark can finish several elite mage builds, and Relic Shards can unlock permanent power. Those rewards create progress you will still feel after the Elarite Heat Season disappears.

Alexandre's final verdict: Pallant first, rarity second, certainty before gambling. Follow that order and this shop can leave your account stronger instead of simply leaving your currencies empty.
