The Astral Reflections event is a Skin+ event focused on Lian. It is basically a two-in-one deal: you need to acquire the base Astral Skin first, and then you unlock the ability to upgrade it to the Skin+ version. This event also features Folio's Astral Skin, which provides +10,650 Armor — a solid defensive boost if you use Folio in your lineup.

Lian's Astral Skin+ grants Magic Reflection, a stat that gives her a chance to completely dodge incoming magical damage and reflect 20% of it back to the attacker. The base Astral Skin provides +2,960 Magic Reflection, while the Skin+ upgrade doubles that to +5,920 Magic Reflection — using the exact same skin stone investment.

What Is Magic Reflection and How Does It Work?

Magic Reflection is a defensive stat that gives the hero a chance to completely avoid an incoming magical attack and reflect 20% of that damage back onto the enemy. When it triggers, Lian takes absolutely zero damage from that hit — it is not a reduction, it is a full dodge.

Here is a step-by-step breakdown so there is no confusion:

  • Only activates against magical damage. Physical damage and Pure damage cannot be reflected at all. This means the stat is specifically useful against mage-heavy enemy teams.
  • Can activate while Lian is Stunned, which is great because it means she still has a layer of protection even when she cannot act.
  • Does NOT activate while Lian is Silenced. If an enemy silences your team, Magic Reflection will not trigger during that silence window.
  • The activation chance is calculated using a formula:

(Magic Reflection / (Magic Reflection + enemy's main stat)) × 100%

The "enemy's main stat" means the attacker's primary attribute — Strength, Agility, or Intelligence — whichever one is their highest and defines their hero class. So if a mage with 30,000 Intelligence attacks Lian and she has 5,920 Magic Reflection, the chance to dodge and reflect would be: 5,920 / (5,920 + 30,000) = roughly 16.5%. That might not sound huge, but across an entire battle with multiple magical attacks, those dodges add up and can save Lian's life multiple times.

Is the Astral Skin+ Worth It for Lian?

Let me be very clear about something: Lian's primary job in your team is to apply Charm and put enemies to sleep. She is an outstanding crowd control support hero. The longer she stays alive, the more Enchantments she casts, and the longer the entire enemy team stays locked down doing nothing. That is her value.

Because of that, the most cost-effective way to build Lian is to focus on her survivability first. Honestly, even a low-power Lian that stays alive long enough to cast two or three Enchantments is already incredibly useful for your team.

That said, if you want to take your Lian to the next level, the Astral Skin+ is still worth getting. Magic Reflection adds an extra layer of magical damage avoidance that directly helps her stay alive longer against mage teams. It is not going to make a huge difference against physical teams or Pure damage dealers, but against mage-heavy lineups it can be the difference between Lian surviving one more critical second to cast another Enchantment or not.

The reflected damage is a nice bonus too — 20% of a big magical hit going back to the enemy adds up over time.

How to Get the Astral Skin+ During the Event

This is the part where I need to explain very carefully, because a lot of people get confused about the process. There are multiple steps, and if you skip one, you will not be able to buy the Skin+.

  1. Step 1 — Buy the base Astral Skin: The base Astral Skin can only be purchased through in-game real-money offers during this event. There is no free way to get it right now. You must own the base skin before the Skin+ becomes available to you in the Perfection Shop.
  2. Step 2 — Complete event missions to earn Charisma Coins: The Astral Reflections event has a set of missions you need to complete. Each completed mission gives you Charisma Coins, which are the event currency. For a detailed list of all missions, check the Skin+ Event Mission Guide.
  3. Step 3 — Buy the Skin+ in Lian's Perfection Shop: Once you own the base Astral Skin and have accumulated enough Charisma Coins, go to Lian's Perfection Shop. The Skin+ costs 85,000 Charisma Coins. If you did not purchase the base skin, the Skin+ option will not be available, and you will need to spend your coins on other items in the shop instead.

Why You Should Not Wait to Get the Skin+ Later

I want to explain this in detail because a lot of people ask me "Alexandre, can I just get the Skin+ later for free?" — and technically yes, but let me tell you exactly how painful that path is.

The base Astral Skin is not that bad to wait for. After about three months, it becomes available in Outland free chests, and after roughly six months, you can buy it with Skin Certificates. That is a reasonable free-to-play timeline.

The Skin+ is a completely different story. After the event ends, the only way to obtain a Skin+ is through the Heroic Chest. Here is the reality of the Heroic Chest:

  • You need to open 400 Heroic Chests to guarantee a chance at a random Skin+. And it is random — you might not even get Lian's.
  • The main prize from the Heroic Chest is Cleaver at 6 stars. If you already have Cleaver, you get 50,000 Cleaver Soul Stones instead, which are useful for the Soul Stone Shop but probably not what you were hoping for.
  • You get 1 free chest per day. You can unlock 1 more by watching an ad every day. That is 2 per day maximum for free.
  • At 2 chests per day, that is 200 days minimum to reach the 400-chest guarantee. But here is the catch: there is a 24-hour timer after each chest opening that can slowly drift, and if you forget a single day, it extends even further.
  • Realistically, for most free-to-play or low spenders, it can take over a year to reach the Skin+ through the Heroic Chest — and even then you might get a Skin+ for a hero you do not even use.

So the bottom line is this: the best time to get any hero's Skin+ is during their Skin+ event. Waiting means months or even more than a year of slow progress with no guarantee. For advanced players, the Heroic Chest grind is especially frustrating — just 1 free chest per day plus 1 from watching a daily ad, and if you miss a day or the 24-hour timer drifts, it gets even worse.

After the Skin+, What Should You Buy in the Shop?

Once you have secured the Skin+ (or if you could not buy the base skin and have leftover Charisma Coins), here is the best order of priority for the remaining items in the Perfection Shop:

  1. Folio and Lian Soul Stones: Both heroes have soul stones available in the shop. Folio's soul stones can also be obtained from the Arena Shop, and Lian's from the Grand Arena Shop. However, Lian's soul stones are harder to justify buying from the Grand Arena Shop because that shop also sells orange equipment pieces that are essential for crafting red gear in the endgame. So the event shop is actually a smart place to pick up Lian soul stones instead. Remember: aim for at least 4 stars, then use the Evolution Booster to finish the job — do not overspend event coins on soul stones past that point. You need at minimum level 50 and 4 evolution stars to activate a hero's Legendary Relic, which is extremely important for progression.
  2. Rune Stones: Not the hardest resource to get day-to-day, but you need a massive amount of them and you will never have enough. Every extra rune stone from the event helps speed up your team's progress.
  3. Chaotic Cores and Artifact Coins: These are some of the worst bottlenecks in the game, especially for free-to-play players who do not subscribe to the Valkyrie's Favor. You need enormous amounts of both, and they take a very long time to accumulate naturally. So if you see them in the shop, they are solid investments.
  4. Equipment items: Avoid buying equipment from the event shop if possible. Here is why: you can farm equipment by spending energy in the campaign, and doing so gives you experience, gold, and helps you complete other event missions at the same time. That means energy farming is far more efficient than buying gear directly. Only buy equipment here if you desperately need one specific piece for your main hero right now.
