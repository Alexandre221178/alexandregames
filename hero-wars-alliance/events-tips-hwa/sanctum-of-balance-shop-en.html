The Sanctum of Balance Shop is one of those event shops where a player can either make a beautiful long-term decision or burn valuable coins on things that only feel useful for one day. My view is simple: during the Balance of Power event group, your Balance Sigils should chase rare progress first, not easy farmable gear.

This shop opens with the Balance of Power event group, usually in the second week of the season. The event runs from Monday to Sunday, and Sunday is the last event day to earn and spend with the event active. The shop normally stays open for another 24 hours after the event ends, but I do not like leaving decisions for the last minute. Spend with a plan, because Balance Sigils disappear faster than they look.

The Shop Lineup Rotates

The featured Hero, Relic Shards, Soul Stones, Artifacts, skins, and other premium rewards can change each time Balance of Power returns. The priorities below describe the supplied season; always compare them with the live Sanctum of Balance Shop and your current teams.

How to Earn Balance Sigils

You spend in this shop with Balance Sigils, the event coins earned across the Balance of Power event group. The good part is that you are not limited to one source. You can earn a strong amount by completing missions in the event group and also through the brawl-style event, where attacks and defenses can both give coins.

This is why I always say: prepare before the event begins. Energy, Emeralds, Summoning Circle summons, Skin Stones, Rune Stones, Elemental Spheres, Tower Chests, Arena and Grand Arena battles, Titanite, VIP Points, and Realm boss defeats can all matter depending on the event mission. If you arrive empty, the shop feels expensive. If you arrive prepared, the shop becomes an opportunity.

Best Sanctum of Balance Shop Priorities

This rotation rewards a simple rule: buy the hardest resources to obtain first. The best items in the shop have purchase limits, so secure the limited rare rewards before spending Balance Sigils on resources you can farm every week.

  1. Crow and Kendle Soul Stones: these are my first priority because both heroes' Soul Stones are rare at the moment and very difficult to obtain. Each offer has an 8/8 purchase limit and gives 5 Soul Stones. If you are developing either hero, do not miss this limited opportunity.
  2. Crow and Kendle artifacts: buy Crow's and Kendle's weapon artifact fragments first, followed by Crow's Laws Codex and Kendle's Torture Secrets book artifact fragments.
  3. Xe'Sha's weapon artifact: this comes after the Crow and Kendle artifact priorities.
  4. Xe'Sha's skins: all three are useful and can save resources later. My order is Dark Depths, Solar, and then Cybernetic.
  5. Xe'Sha Soul Stones: buy these after the skins. They are useful, but Xe'Sha Soul Stones can also be purchased week after week in the Hero's Way Shop.
  6. Arachne, Maya, Dante, and Kai Relic Shards: valuable, but not the maximum priority because their shards are also available from the weekly Guild VS Relic Chest.

Quick reminder: almost everything truly attractive in this shop has a purchase cap. Check every limit before using your remaining Balance Sigils on repeatable materials.

Are Xe'Sha's Skins Worth 25,000 Balance Sigils?

Each Xe'Sha skin costs 25,000 Balance Sigils. You should eventually be able to unlock these skins with 1 Skin Certificate or 5,000 Skin Stones, so they are not permanently exclusive. Even so, they are good purchases for a developed Xe'Sha because buying them here saves those resources later.

Xe'Sha Soul Stones and the Missing Peech Reward

After the skins, move to Xe'Sha Soul Stones. Xe'Sha can also be developed through the Hero's Way Shop, where you can buy her Soul Stones every week. If you purchase the pass, you can earn more shop currency and accelerate her evolution, which is why I would not place her stones above the rarer Crow and Kendle offers.

Honestly, I think the developers made a mistake by not putting Peech Soul Stones in this shop. This event focuses on Peech's Relic, so leaving her Soul Stones out of the rotation makes the progression feel unnecessarily difficult.

When to Buy the Relic Shards

The Relic Shards for Arachne, Maya, Dante, and Kai are good rewards. Remember that a hero must have at least 4 stars before you can unlock that hero's Relic, so do not buy shards you cannot use yet unless you already have a clear evolution plan.

I do not rank these shards at the very top because they are available in the weekly Guild VS Relic Chest. You can accumulate them for free every week, while Crow and Kendle Soul Stones and the best artifact offers are harder to replace.

What I Would Leave for Last

Chaotic Cores and Artifact Coins are useful for evolving artifacts, but buy them only after the rare Soul Stones, priority artifact fragments, Xe'Sha skins, and desired Relic Shards.

Equipment items should normally be avoided. It is better to farm equipment with Energy during events, earning mission progress at the same time. The only favorable exception is a beginner who needs gear for a first team of five heroes and wants to accelerate that team's development.

After your first team is established, resist the temptation. Equipment is not the hardest end-game resource to obtain, so I consistently rank it as a low-priority use of rare event currency.
