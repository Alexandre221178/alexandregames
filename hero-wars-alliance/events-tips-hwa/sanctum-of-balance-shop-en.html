Before you spend your Balance Sigils, there is one big decision in the Sanctum of Balance Shop: do you chase Nebula's new Singularity Skin+, or use those coins on rare Relic Shards?

Alexandre here from Alexandre Games. My number one priority is Nebula's new Singularity Skin. Here is how I would compare its immediate value with Relic Shards and decide what to buy during Balance of Power.

The Shop Lineup Rotates

The featured Hero, Relic Shards, Soul Stones, Artifacts, skins, and other premium rewards can change each time Balance of Power returns. The priorities below describe the supplied season; always compare them with the live Sanctum of Balance Shop and your current teams.

Nebula's Singularity Skin: My First Priority

The base Singularity Skin costs 90,000 Balance Sigils and gives Nebula 106,645 Health. That extra Health helps her stay alive longer and keep healing, cleansing, and buffing the team.

Buying the base skin unlocks the Singularity Skin+ offer. It costs more than 300,000 Balance Sigils and gives 213,290 Health, double the normal skin bonus.

How to Earn Balance Sigils and Unlock Event Rewards

The event lasts one week, with new missions opening every day. Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond also unlock Gear & Glory missions, so completing more missions gives you access to better rewards.

  • Defiant Edge: 260 completed quests can give Nebula's Romantic Skin, and 420 can give Harmony Gloves.
  • Unbroken Bond: 68 completed missions can give Nebula's Spring Skin, and 70 can give her second Talisman, Talisman of Stars.
  • Hero Brawls: during the first three days, attack and defend to earn extra Balance Sigils while testing teams at maximum level.

Should You Buy Nebula's Skin+ or Focus on Relics?

If you complete enough missions, you may still have Sigils left for Relic Shards after buying the skins. You can also skip the Skin+ and focus on Relics.

However, Skin+ upgrades are rare and can take months to return after an event. The base skin is easier to get later, while obtaining the exact Skin+ you want from the Heroic Chest can be very difficult. If Nebula is important for your team, think carefully before skipping it.

For Free-to-Play players: I would not spend everything just because the Skin+ is rare. If Nebula is not in your main team, saving resources for stronger account upgrades can still be the better choice.

Relic Shard Costs: 93,000 Sigils Still Does Not Unlock a Relic

You need 50 Relic Shards to unlock a hero's Level 1 Relic, and the hero must be at least four stars.

In this shop, each hero has a limit of ten purchases. Each purchase gives two shards for 9,300 Balance Sigils. Spending the full 93,000 Sigils gives only 20 Relic Shards.

That is almost the same price as Nebula's base skin at 90,000 Sigils, but if you start with no shards, you still need another 30 shards from somewhere else, such as a Hub Shop offer. This is another reason why I think Nebula's base skin gives better immediate value during this event.

Which Relic Shards Would I Prioritize?

  1. Kendle: my first priority. I consider Kendle one of the strongest heroes in the meta, with excellent synergy alongside Chaos heroes that use Sacrifice.
  2. Miu: my second choice. She is very strong, deals high Magic Damage, and can blind enemies.
  3. Drayne: an excellent hero with a very strong Level 30 Golden Relic. However, his shards can also be earned through Relic Caskets from Guild versus Guild daily missions.
  4. Somna: a strong counter to area damage heroes, especially Kayla. Her shards can also be collected through those Relic Caskets each week.

Because Drayne and Somna have this alternative source, I would keep them below Kendle and Miu in this shop. If you can collect shards for free every week, think carefully before spending rare event currency on them.

Soul Stones, Artifacts, and Lower-Priority Purchases

After Relics, I would look at Kendle and Crow Soul Stones. They are still rare and difficult to get, so they have good value if you have not evolved these heroes yet.

Next, I would consider Crow and Kendle weapon Artifacts, followed by their Book Artifacts.

Skin Stones, Realm items, and normal equipment are lower priorities for me. Realm items can be earned from daily missions, and normal equipment can usually be farmed with Energy.

For beginners, equipment can make sense for a first team of five heroes. For intermediate and end-game players, I would avoid spending rare event currency there.

My Final Shop Order

  1. Nebula's Singularity Skin: my first choice for a team that uses Nebula.
  2. Decide whether to buy the Skin+: weigh its huge cost against Nebula's importance on your account.
  3. Kendle Relic Shards, then Miu Relic Shards: my main Relic targets. Drayne and Somna come below these two because of their alternative shard source.
  4. Kendle and Crow Soul Stones: rare resources for heroes you still need to evolve.
  5. Crow and Kendle Artifacts: weapon Artifacts first, then Book Artifacts.

Skin Stones, Realm items, and normal equipment remain lower priorities. Choose the upgrades that matter for your main team and check purchase limits before spending your remaining Sigils.
