The Elemental Forge is the Lords of the Elements shop for the Summer Glory season featuring Crow. It combines Hero Relic Shards, Titan Soul Stones, rare Titan Artifact fragments, Primordial Skins, and normal progression materials in one place. That makes it look generous, but not every offer produces the same improvement in battle.

My goal in this guide is to help you spend Eternal Marks according to your account stage. A beginner who still needs a functional Titan roster should not follow exactly the same order as an advanced Royal League player chasing rare Hero Relics.

How to Earn Eternal Marks

Eternal Marks are earned from the three connected Lords of the Elements events: Titan's Roar, Elemental Tempest, and United by Power.

Use the mission guides above to match your actions with the correct Guild VS days. The same Rune Stones, Titan Skin Stones, Elemental Spheres, Summoning Spheres, Tower Chests, Arena battles, and other saved resources can advance event missions and Guild VS together. Better timing means more Eternal Marks without wasting account resources.

The Elemental Forge remains available for approximately 24 hours after the event ends. I still recommend deciding your priorities before the final day, then checking the live shop one last time before spending.

Is Iyari Worth Buying?

Iyari is a very good Light support Titan. She is useful in the Dungeon and in Guild Titan PvP because her healing helps the team survive repeated pressure. Her Primordial Skin increases Physical Attack, improving the strength of the healing produced by her skill.

However, I would normally collect Iyari Soul Stones from Elemental Spheres instead of spending rare Eternal Marks on her fragments. The shop becomes attractive only when a small purchase immediately completes an evolution level you need for a full Light team. Do not keep buying Iyari just because she is featured if a rare Relic or artifact would improve your account more.

Read the complete Iyari Titan guide, the Iyari Primordial Skin analysis, and my Dungeon guide before deciding whether she solves a real weakness in your roster.

Best Priority for Intermediate and Advanced Players: Relic Shards

For intermediate and advanced accounts, the heart of this shop is the Relic Shards for Oya, Lian, Jhu, and Alvanor.

In this Elemental Forge lineup, each purchase gives 2 Relic Shards for 5,250 Eternal Marks. Each hero has a 25/25 purchase limit, so you can obtain up to 50 Relic Shards for one hero at a total cost of 131,250 Eternal Marks. Buying every shard for all four heroes would cost 525,000 Eternal Marks, so almost every player must choose.

  1. Lian - my safest first recommendation: Lian is versatile, disruptive in PvP, and fits many defensive or control teams. Even if she is not in your main five, she can work with relatively low investment when you prioritize Health and defenses. Her Relic adds Health and Magic Attack together, giving her both survival and useful scaling. This is efficient progress for Grand Arena, Guild War, Guild Championship, and other multi-team modes.
  2. Oya - number one if you are truly building around her: Oya is powerful and can define strong PvP compositions, but unlike Lian, she normally needs high investment to become reliably dangerous. If Oya is already part of your main plan, move her above Lian. If not, do not start an expensive new project only because her shards are visible in the shop.
  3. Jhu - the priority many players underestimate: I could honestly put Jhu at number one for a PvE-focused account. In Realm, my Jhu can defeat level 27 bosses and monsters with a single hit, preventing troop losses; with more development, level 30 Rally Boss one-shots become a realistic target. He is also excellent against Hydra, in Campaign, and in Eternal Frontier. Jhu can still work in PvP, but he is less universal there and can be stalled or countered by heroes such as Martha, Jorgen, Somna, or another Jhu depending on the complete lineup.
  4. Alvanor - specialized Nature value: Alvanor is not bad, but he is not a universal top-tier project either. His best value appears in Nature teams and in deeper Royal League or multi-team competition where roster width matters. If you are not building Nature and do not need another developed team, those Eternal Marks may produce more impact on Lian, Oya, or Jhu.

Best Titan Priorities for Beginners

If your Hero Relics are not yet the correct account priority, I would invest in the rarest Titan development paths. Start with the Elarite artifacts used by Orm, followed by the Summoner artifacts used by Umbra. These Titans and artifact families are newer and harder to collect than Iyari's normal progression.

  1. Orm and Elarite artifacts: Orm is an Elarite/Water support who absorbs part of the damage received by allies and turns that pressure into a delayed attack. Zorval's Rite gives the team a guaranteed defensive buff after his ultimate, Chthonic Balance Seal improves Health and Physical Attack in every matchup, and Elarite Crown helps against Light and Darkness Titans. These upgrades can make Water teams survive burst damage and maintain control longer.
  2. Umbra and Summoner artifacts: Umbra is a Darkness Summoner who drains enemy Energy and transfers momentum to an ally. Summon Seal supplies the Health Umbra needs to stay active, while Nephtis's Horn provides a team-wide defensive buff. This directly improves PvP tempo because opponents cast later while your Darkness team accelerates.
  3. Mort fragments: Mort weakens the enemy with the highest Physical Attack. More evolution gives him the stats to survive and keep suppressing dangerous physical carries, but his fragments are less urgent if your Darkness team is not being built.
  4. Other crowns, seals, and weapon fragments: buy them only for Titans you actively use. A weapon artifact can create a powerful team buff when its Titan uses an ultimate, but fragments sitting on an unused Titan have zero battle impact.

For detailed build orders, see the Orm guide and Umbra guide.

Iyari and Mort Primordial Skins

The Iyari Primordial Skin and Mort Primordial Skin each cost 15,000 Eternal Marks in the shop. Both skins can normally be unlocked for 15,000 Titan Skin Stones, so buying one here saves that amount of Skin Stones for leveling other Titan skins.

Iyari's Primordial Skin increases Physical Attack, strengthening her healing. Mort's Primordial Skin increases Health, helping him survive longer and continue weakening physical attackers. Both offer real battle value, but the decision depends on opportunity cost: Relic Shards are currently among the rarest items in the game, while Titan Skin Stones continue to arrive through regular progression and events.

My verdict: buy a Primordial Skin with Eternal Marks only if that Titan is already important to your main team and you have enough event currency after your irreplaceable priorities. Otherwise, be patient and unlock it later with Titan Skin Stones.

Farmable Items and the Spark of Power Trap

Elemental Spheres, Summoning Spheres, Titan Potions, Fairy Dust, Titan Skin Stones, and Sparks of Power are all useful at the right moment. The problem is not that they have no value; the problem is that they can be collected gradually through normal play while this shop also sells resources that are far harder to obtain.

  • Elemental and Summoning Spheres: take the free and regular sources first. Buy them here only with leftovers or when a small purchase completes a meaningful event or evolution breakpoint.
  • Titan Potions and Fairy Dust: these improve levels, but patient Dungeon, Guild, and daily progression continues producing them.
  • Titan Skin Stones: valuable, especially for a main tank or carry, but compare them with the permanent scarcity of Relic Shards and newer artifact fragments.
  • Spark of Power: this is the classic shop trap. Every type of Titan upgrade produces Sparks over time. Be patient; in the endgame you can have millions sitting unused. Spending Eternal Marks on them trades rare event currency for a resource your account will eventually overflow.

Final Recommendation

My final order is simple: rare progress before convenient progress. Intermediate and advanced players should prioritize the Relic Shards that match a real Hero project: Lian for flexible low-cost PvP value, Oya for a committed high-investment team, Jhu for outstanding PvE power, and Alvanor for a developed Nature roster.

Beginners should build the rare Titan path they will actually use, starting with Orm's Elarite artifacts and then Umbra's Summoner artifacts. Iyari is excellent, but Elemental Spheres give you another path to her Soul Stones. The Primordial Skins can save 15,000 Titan Skin Stones, yet they should not push truly rare Relic Shards out of your budget. Whatever you do, leave Spark of Power at the bottom.
